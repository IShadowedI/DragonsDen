[40K] 11th Edition Points Review: Space Marines

June 17th, 2026 via Goonhammer
The final piece of the 11th Edition puzzle is revealed today, with the Munitorum Field Manual landing and providing points costs and Force Dispositions for all factions. Our team have dug in to analyse what this means for the early 11th Edition metagame in our massive review. This part focuses on the Space Marine factions.

Thanks to Games Workshop for providing us with early access to these rules. Any details we mention are subject to change until published on Warhammer Community or the Warhammer 40K App.

Welcome to our review of Space Marine points for 11th Edition. As it's a whole new world out there, most factions in the game get lots of changes, so we're not going to try and list every single one. For each faction, we're focusing on the changes that we think will have the biggest impact on listbuilding, and covering off where we think the army lands in the new edition.

You can find the rest of our review, covering other factions and a general overview, via the links below:

  • Overview
  • Imperium Factions
  • Chaos Factions
  • Xenos Factions

Before we start, there’s an important thing to note here. Each Codex Supplement now has its own set of points, separately to the others. While a lot of costs are the same, there’s some that are different, depending on the utility a unit has in a particular book. This is a good balance lever to have, and it’s important to be aware of it. Where costs are significantly different between books, we’ll highlight this in the supplement’s focus.

Codex Space Marine

Codex Space Marines, especially Ultramarines, have been great since they were handed super Oaths by a Balance Dataslate. They’ve had a bit of a dropoff right at the end as Defilers have outclassed them, but 11th resets the board, and should give them (plus other choices like Salamanders) a chance to thrive again.

Key Points Changes

Buffs

  • Desolation Squad: -20pts. Their built-in ability to ignore cover is a lot less useful now than it was, because if they’re firing indirect they’re hitting on 4s or 6s anyway and ignoring cover does nothing in either case, so they get cheaper as a result.
  • Judiciar: -15pts. As trailed by Warhammer Community, Fights First is way less effective than it was in the past, so the Judiciar gets a hefty drop as a result.
  • Land Raider Redeemer: -20pts. Look. We do understand what happened here. The flamestorm cannons have both TORRENT and IGNORES COVER, which effectively double count the same buff, so to save it paying twice the points have been cut. Sometimes it’s ok to leave things alone, though.
  • Outrider Squad: -10pts. There’s a lot of -10pts in here, so we haven’t picked them all out. Making 3 of these 70pts is pretty wild, though - they’re fast, tough, and reasonably killy for that price.
  • Redemptor Dreadnought: -10pts, but +10 for each macro plasma incinerator, so if you want to take the good gun it stands still.
  • Repulsor: -10pts. Similarly, these things are pretty crazy at 170.
  • Suboden Khan: -15pts. This guy gets cheaper as do the Outriders he attaches to, for a potentially spicy combo.
  • Terminator Assault Squad: -25/-50pts. A healthy drop for these, though they have wargear costs - 5pts per thunder hammer. So really you’re paying the same as you were before for the hammer and shield versions, but get a healthy discount on a unit with lightning claws. Curie: Also pretty interesting to run a mixed unit of 5/5 THSS/LC for an averaged-out price with some multi-wound shenanigans for the new allocation group rules.
  • Vulkan He’stan: -15pts. Similarly to the Redeemer, his Gauntlet of the Forge has the IGNORES COVER/TORRENT nonbo. That said, that seems pretty thin for making him 85pts.
  • Whirlwind: -15pts. Again, indirect is a lot worse than it was, especially without being able to re-roll anything. Amusingly the second one is 5pts more than it was before.

Nerfs

  • Chief Librarian Tigurius: +20pts. A big hike, justified by his built-in -1 to hit for his unit, which stacks with cover to make something really sticky.
  • Repulsor Executioner: +10pts. A slight bump here, opposite the regular Repulsor’s 10pt drop.

There’s a lot more 10pt buffs than we’ve highlighted, and one or two 5pt taps on good units - very much in the Marines’ favour here.

Force Dispositions

3 DP Detachments
  • Armoured Speartip - Take and Hold
  • Blade of Ultramar - Priority Assets
  • Ceramite Sentinels - Take and Hold
  • Gladius Task Force - Priority Assets
  • Stormlance Task Force - Disruption
2 DP Detachments
  • 1st Company Task Force - Priority Assets
  • Anvil Siege Force - Take and Hold
  • Bastion Task Force - Take and Hold
  • Emperor’s Shield - Priority Assets
  • Firestorm Assault Force - Purge the Foe
  • Forgefather’s Seekers - Purge the Foe
  • Hammer of Avernii - Priority Assets
  • Headhunter Task Force - Priority Assets
  • Ironstorm Spearhead - Purge the Foe
  • Orbital Assault Force - Take and Hold
  • Reclamation Force - Take and Hold
  • Shadowmark Talon - Disruption
  • Spearpoint Task Force - Disruption
  • Vanguard Spearhead - Reconnaissance
1 DP Detachments
  • Fulguris Task Force - Disruption
  • Librarius Conclave - Reconnaissance
  • Subversion Assets - Reconnaissance

Outlook

Winner

Thoughts

Corrode: Marines were already a strong faction, and they get some eyebrow-raising points drops to go with it and thanks to their millions of detachments, easy ways to build for pretty much any mission set. Ironstorm Spearhead giving you Purge the Foe seems like a particularly good baseline.

Black Templars

Templars landed warm-ish in 10th with solid all-rounder Gladius builds, but were dialed back points-wise, which left them outclassed as all-rounders. They got a strong second wind from the addition of the Bastion Task Force detachment, as they could build for this with either large Crusader units or Assault Intercessor squads with their various herohammer pieces. Notably, their own detachments have largely done nothing at all, and happily the DP costs reflect that.

Key Points Changes

Buffs

  • Crusade Ancient: -10pts. At 45pts and Support, you might run one of these ever, but probably not.
  • Crusader Squad: -10pts for the big unit. As mentioned on Warhammer Community, big units take a hit now from the 9” coherency, so these get a reduction to reflect that.

There’s no other drops here which aren’t already in the vanilla Codex.

Nerfs

  • Marshall: +10pts for units 2+. This is a bit of a head-scratcher, as yes you saw lists with lots of Marshalls and Castellans in units, but the Marshall is the more replaceable of the two.

Force Dispositions

2 DP Detachments
  • Companions of Vehemence - Purge the Foe
  • Godhammer Assault Force - Disruption
  • Vindication Task Force - Priority Assets
1 DP Detachments
  • Marshal’s Household - Priority Assets
  • The Living Miracle - Purge the Foe
  • Wrathful Procession - Take and Hold

Outlook

Neutral

Thoughts

Corrode: Black Templars weren’t doing fantastically before, and they basically stand still on points here on any of their unique stuff. That said, cheaper Land Raider Redeemers may do something for them as platforms for melee to come out of, and their good stuff standing still (plus riding the coattails of Marine improvements) ends up as a net boost for them.

Blood Angels

Blood Angels have been perpetually solid in 10th on the back of the Liberator Assault Group detachment, one of the best to ever do it. Later on, Rage-Cursed Onslaught provided a second powerful option, and these two have ebbed back-and-forth in popularity since depending on the rest of the metagame.

Key Points Changes

Buffs

  • Chief Librarian Mephiston: -10pts. Down to 110, he’s an attractive option.

The remaining buffs are in line with the vanilla book.

Nerfs

  • Assault Intercessors with jump packs: +5pts. A unit that does very well in Blood Angels with its focus on fast melee, especially in Liberator Assault Group where they punch much harder than on baseline. They get a 5pt drop in vanilla Space Marines and a 5pt bump here, which is an overall 10pt difference between the two.
  • Assault Intercessors: +5pts. Like their jump-pack brethren, these go up here while standing still in the vanilla book.
  • Bladeguard Veteran Squad: +5pts. Again, catching a nerf here, and standing still on the 6 model version, whereas the vanilla ones are unchanged at 3 models and go down for 6.
  • Chaplain with jump pack: +5pts. Another which is a jump from the vanilla book.

Force Dispositions

3 DP Detachments
  • Angelic Inheritors - Priority Assets (Unique: Grace)
  • Liberator Assault Group - Take and Hold
  • Rage-cursed Onslaught - Purge the Foe (Unique: Doomed)
2 DP Detachments
  • The Angelic Host - Disruption
  • The Lost Brethren - Purge the Foe (Unique: Doomed)
1 DP Detachments
  • Encarmine Spearhead - Disruption
  • Legacy of Grace - Priority Assets (Unique: Grace)
  • Wrath of the Doomed - Purge the Foe (Unique: Doomed)

For some reason Stormlance Task Force is 2DP rather than 3DP for Blood Angels. We’re not sure if this is an error or not.

Outlook

Neutral

Thoughts

Corrode: In line with other Marines, there’s a lot of buffs here. That said, many of the units you want to use to play Blood Angels get pricier. We’ll see how that shakes out.

Dark Angels

Dark Angels had a bumpy ride through 10th - their Codex was (bluntly) bad, but they were massively improved by Dataslate changes and digital detachments, and ended up with very good options. They did have a real dropoff in the full arms race that was the end of the Edition though, not quite able to contest Defilers.

Key Points Changes

Buffs

Similar to the other codex non-compliant chapters, they now have their own costs for all units. Cost changes are in line with the codex-compliant marine changes.

  • Lion El’Jonson: -30pts. This change makes sense - the Lion’s [FIGHTS FIRST] ability is less good than before, so he got a reduction. That being said, he’s still a primarch with some really good buffs for your army and can hold his own in combat quite well.
  • Deathwing Terminators: -15/-30pts. At 33pts a model the Deathwing Terminator now seems priced to move; while they lost some durability by being unable to stack cover and AoC, they still are quite resilient and have decent output. Could be interesting to look more closely at these!
  • Deathwing Knights: -10pts (20pt tax on unit 2+). This is an odd change - if you take two units of Deathwing Knights, then this is a zero-point change. If you were running three units of them, it’s a 10pt nerf. Most popular lists ran two units so this doesn’t change anything for them but does make splashing one unit into other armies more easy.
  • Ravenwing Darkshroud: -20pts. This is more funny than anything else - the Darkshroud’s ability confers [STEALTH] on friendly units within 6”. With how easy it is to get cover in the new book, this is less valuable but still useful.
  • Sammael: -10pts. Quite nice - he slots in very well into most Dark Angels armies, especially if you’re making use of anything that targets FLY or RAVENWING.
  • Inner Circle Companions: -10pts (both 3 & 6 model squad sizes). ICC kept their -1 to hit for being lead, which makes their durability considerably better with the changes to cover.
  • Belial: -10pts. Not sure if this is an actual meaningful buff, but at 75pts he’s getting close to worth his points just as two Terminator bodies stapled together.
  • Too many to list but will change lists up slightly: Lots of point tweaks to Ravenwing units, most small 5pt changes.

Nerfs

  • Azrael: +15pts. This poor chapter master has gone up 35pts so far this year - leave him alone! That being said, a 4+ invulnerable save on any unit he leads is really good and extra CP will be very precious in this upcoming edition.

Force Dispositions

3 DP Detachments
  • Wrath of the Rock - Priority Assets (Curie: GW pls)
2 DP Detachments
  • 1st Company Task Force - Priority Assets
  • Company of Hunters - Disruption
  • Inner Circle Task Force - Priority Assets
  • Lion’s Blade Task Force - Purge the Foe
  • Unforgiven Task Force - Take and Hold
1 DP Detachments
  • Dark Age Arsenal - Priority Assets
  • Darkflight Pursuit - Reconnaissance
  • Interrogation Conclave - Purge the Foe

Outlook

Slight Winner

Thoughts

Curie: Dark Angels are coming out of 10th edition as one of the worst-performing armies on aggregate. That being said, they never really sat at the bottom - just failed to break out against the top meta armies. The changes to the edition leave them in an interesting position. They definitely benefit from the point changes above, especially since they only had one meaningful increase in Azrael. Their spread of options for force dispositions and flexibility of play in the Deathwing/Ravenwing/Greenwing options leave them with a strong outlook. Where they’ll suffer is they still have marine bodies in a meta that seems very lethal to marines.

One thing to keep an eye out for is triple 1 DP armies such as Fulguris Task Force + Darkflight Pursuit + Dark Age Arsenal. This combination taking maximum speeders and plasma can be very potent due to its ability to ignore cover practically army-wide and have very good mobility. Similarly Ironstorm Spearhead with Darkflight Pursuit enables options very similar to the old “Lionstorm” builds that centered around Azrael, Stormraven Gunships, and Land Speeder Vengeances.

Deathwatch

Deathwatch briefly stopped existing as an army in 10th, but re-appeared deadlier than ever, and essentially ratcheted up in success all the way until Defiler time after that. Even with the rise of the crabs, they’ve stayed in contention for a top spot, but a major part of that has been their uniquely efficient access to indirect fire, which takes a major hit as we enter 11th.

Key Points Changes

Buffs

  • Talonstrike Kill Team: -10 points on the first 2 units. This team has always been on the edge points wise, since you were really taking it for AP-3/-4 Plasma “inceptors”, the rest of the unit was kind of dead weight. Buttttttt, with the new changes to Hazardous this squad is way more effective longer since they only take 1 mortal on a fail now and that can go on any model.
  • Decimus Kill Team: -10 points for 10 model units. This makes the primaris-ified equivalent to the Deathwatch Veterans unit exactly the same cost, which is cool. This squad is slightly shootier than Vets, so if you want a shootier battleline unit, this is a decent contender now.
  • Spectrus Kill Team: -10 points for the first 2 units. Well the good news is it is the cheapest 10 model unit kill team, and thats about it. It still is the lowest output of any of the KTs.
  • Deathwatch Terminator Squad: -10/-20 points. ***+5 points per thunder hammer / storm shield model *** So if you want to run a 5 man with 3 Cyclone Missile launchers and 2 bolter/fist or lightning claw models, this unit is cheaper. Otherwise it's just a sidegrade
  • Assault Intercessors with Jump Packs: Yes yes I know this isn’t a bespoke DW unit, buttttt it is a very good skirmish unit, and the first 2 units are -5/-10 points, so taking 2 5 mans is a very real option.

Nerfs

  • Fortis Kill Team: +15 points for the first 2 and +30 for the 3rd. With this points cost increase, combined with the nerfs to indirect, this unit is looking kind of dead. You could try and take this unit as a direct fire shooty competitor to the Decimus/DW Termies of similar costs, so maybe?
  • Indomitor Kill Team: +10 points for the first 2 and +25 for the 3rd. This has been the highest ranged damage kill team you could take, so honestly not super surprised.
  • Storm Speeder Hammerstrike: +5 points for the first 2 and +15 for the 3rd. These are a good source of stripping cover, so valid increase.
  • Infiltrator Squad: +10 points for 5 model units for the first 2. Not a super popular choice, but a 5 model screen in the backfield was always a nice option, now a little more expensive.

Force Dispositions

3 DP Detachments
  • Black Spear Task Force - 3DP - Priority Assets

Outlook

Slight Loser

Thoughts

Shane: Fortis and Indomitor Kill teams eating points hikes will make the meta builds have to change up some. Terminators will probably pop back up in lists. Black Spear being 3DP kind of stinks, since no synergy with the 1DP detachments. So all in all it seems like at best a side grade to the army, and a nerf to the current list style. Black Spear being Priority Assets will also require some list tooling I imagine, since it is a detachment that wants to do an action for all 5 of its missions. That said I think Priority Assets is poised to be a Purge the Foe killer, but time will tell.

Space Wolves

Poor Space Wolves. Briefly above average, but then over-nerfed, and have generally been on a low to middling ebb since. They do have some solid Characters and some devoted fans, so have shown up at the top occasionally, but really struggle to stack up against other Chapters.

Key Points Changes

Buffs

  • Wolf Scouts: -10/20 pts. Wolf Scouts are great. They are a super versatile, fast, and hard hitting skirmish unit, and with -10 points for 6 and -20 points for 12, bringing them to 95/190, they are even more enticing. Realistically, you will never take the 12 man because it has big diminishing returns, but with Incursors going up, 6 man Wolf Scouts are even more tasty. Ask yourself, for 10 more points over Incursors, would you take +1M, a bunch of plasma, +1 to wound roll access, better melee, and an extra model with 10”M? I think resoundingly yes. They also don’t increase in points until the third unit, so I fully expect to see plenty of lists with two squads.
  • Blood Claws: -15 pts for 20 man. Is this good? I don’t know. It at least makes the big blobs a bit more palatable, if you were wanting to take them anyway. I think they probably still don’t make the cut, but maybe in Bastion they have some play. Honestly, I just needed to write more in the buff section for my own sanity…
  • Wolf Guard Terminators: -20pts/-40pts. On paper, this is a huge buff. Wolf Guard Terminators are probably the best unit in the Wolves rolodex, so more of them is better. However, this comes with the HUGE caveat that each Storm shield is +5 points. This is by far the best loadout, as an extra wound is a massive buff. This means that if you take one special weapon and four shields in a five man, it’s a wash. If you take the fist’assault cannon guy, you save 5 more points. Basically this just shifts the needle from “windmill slam shields” to “windmill slam shields unless you desperately need a few points.” Still, bringing back a bit of granularity to list building will be nice. However, I hate to break it to you, that Wolf Guard Terminators, my most special boys, are also subject to…

Nerfs

  • Wolf Guard Terminators: Only -10/20pts starting at your second unit. With shields, this comes out to a nerf of 5-10 points on a small unit, and maybe more if you’re taking a big. This is a bad change, as Wolves are already in a rough spot and this is cutting into points that you desperately need, especially since 2-3 units of Wolf Guard Terminators is very common in lists, and likely to be more so with the inclusion of the new Terminator buffing Legends of Saga and Song Detachment.
  • Fenrisian Wolves: +5pts/-5pts for 10. This sucks. Fenrisian Wolves are bad, and their only value is to take up space. While the 10 man is only 75 points now, the whole point of these is to take a unit that can do some stuff for as few points as possible. The changes to coherency further damage the viability of the 10 man. With Space Marine Scouts down to 65, for me personally this change, while small, is enough to put the doggos back on the shelf unless I have 45 and exactly 45 points to spend.
  • Wolf Guard Headtakers: +5pts/+10 points for the 3 man and 6 man variants with Hunting Wolves only. Hunting Wolves are Fenrisian Wolves but somehow worse. You only take them if they are sufficiently cheap, and in this context 5-10 more points is very likely not worth it. While Headtakers are still good, especially with the buffed math of mass precision, this change sucks for our access to cheap little units.

Force Dispositions

2 DP Detachments
  • Saga of the Beastslayer - Purge the Foe
  • Saga of the Bold - Priority Assets
  • Saga of the Hunter - Disruption
  • Saga of the Great Wolf - Take and Hold
1 DP Detachments
  • Legends of Saga and Song - Take and Hold
  • Veterans of the Fang - Disruption
  • Champions of Fenris - Purge the Foe

Outlook

Loser

Thoughts

Garrett: Wolves are already in a bad spot, and GW continues to ignore them and give what are honestly quite lazy rules passes. No units were given Support, no character units had points adjusted, Thunderwolves and Wolf Guard Terminators were “buffed” but with costs on what is basically mandatory shields that either negate the buff or functionally create a nerf, and little trash units that are a unique tool for Wolves were nerfed. Many units that either rarely or never see play and don’t create problems if brought in multiples, like Venerable Dreadnoughts or Wulfen Dreadnoughts, are taxed on multiples, and Detachments that synergize often have the same disposition, locking you into one style of play. While I try not to be negative, I will admit that I am very frustrated that Wolves have now been through multiple dataslates in 10th edition and now the 11th edition launch pass without any buffs to speak of, while catching unwarranted nerf and also sitting in the middle-low end of the meta. I hope I’m wrong, but I really don’t see Wolves making much of a splash in the 11th launch meta.

Wrap Up

That's it for Marines, but there's plenty more to unpack across the game's other factions, so use the links at the top to check those out!
