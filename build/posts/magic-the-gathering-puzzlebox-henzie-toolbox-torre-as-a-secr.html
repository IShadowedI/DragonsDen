Magic: The Gathering Puzzlebox – Henzie "Toolbox" Torre as a Secret Commander

July 21st, 2026 via Goonhammer
Henzie "Toolbox" Torre has been a staple of Commander Nights since his printing in New Capenna Commander, boasting a substantial ~18,000 decks on EDHREC, and standing (or squatting) as the 80th most popular Commander of all time. Despite this, to me the devil is in the details: Henzie is clunky. While the first cast feels good, being a way to break even on advantage and spread haste across big beaters, paying 5 has less appeal, and 7+ less still. Even if you wanted to jam a free Blitzed Solemn Simulacrum, you'd need to cast a Commander 4+ times, with that final cast of Henzie being a laborious 11 mana. Being in Black & Green though, means Henzie is exceptionally findable by way of omnitutors & Creature tutors, and there's a convenient way to spread the cost of ramping up his Blitz discount: Partners. I'd like to present a new way to look at Henzie, for all the blitzed-out Riveteers in the audience, that makes an earnest attempt to de-clunk the Devil by placing him in the 99, rather than at the forefront.

This is part of a shortform Magic series where I’m covering rapid-fire concepts about Commander that I couldn’t cover in a full Commander Focus, so if you like what you see, certainly let us know!

Uncivil Union

Looking at our Partner options, there's a pair of standouts: Rograkh, Son of Rohgahh & Dargo, the Shipwrecker. Each pushes Henzie in a different direction, with Rog being a free-cast Commander that automatically stacks a Henzie discount, and which we can spin off for mana or tutors via cards like Culling the Weak or Diabolic Intent. Dargo meanwhile flexes Henzie's capacity to be used as a combo enabler, as alongside a Phyrexian Altar or similar (more than a few in Jund) he can be cast an infinite number of times from the Command Zone, drawing a card if Blitzed each time. Of course, Henzie makes this easier, and allows Dargo to draw through the Library, but the caveat is of course than these options cannot both be used, for we still require a Partner to add BG to our identity. Reyhan, Last of the Abzan or Ikra Shidiqi, the Usurper are our options, the former being as cheap as a normal Henzie (but cannot benefit from his Blitz), and the latter being a correct cost to snowball Henzie's discount. The really cool thing here isn't just that these options implicitly benefit Henzie from there just being two ways to stack the discount, but also in using his ability differently. A Rog/Ikra deck is there to give Henzie explosive discount turns early on, usually getting a 3-4 mana discount on Blitz with relative ease, while Dargo/Reyhan is about seeing Henzie as a finisher, with some added upside for midgame value plays.

Credit: Wizards of the Coast

Whether you're playing a more fair (if it can be called such) Henzie list with Rog, or a combo win with Dargo, there's more tools than just Henzie to benefit from having two Commanders we're casting often. The big one is Myth Unbound, which halves your Commander Tax, and provides an additional draw when things die to Blitz or a sacrifice. With Rog, it means we effectively pay {1} to get a compounding discount for Henzie...and you can imagine how silly that gets. Better still, once we stack our discount enough via Rog, we can even cast Ikra for just {B}{G} each time, as her consecutive casts also discount...herself. That's right, between Henzie & Myth Unbound—and a little help from Rograkh—we can turn Ikra into her very own Dargo-lite. Beyond that, Jund also provides access to things like Skyfire Phoenix, a Blitz-able, recursive body that loves to repeat itself, and Flamekin Herald, which while not benefiting from Rog does give the Dargo/Reyhan build plenty of Cascade. Don't forget, we also have Jeska Reborn, another potential Red option, who I find better-used in the 99 to dunk targets after the game goes on long enough.

The Great Henz

Credit: Wizards of the Coast.

On the topic of Henzie ideas, a semi-recent change to CR601.4 opens up more possibilities to enjoy the Commander, by clarifying the choice window for {X} costs. Put simply, if you would pay X=N, where N brings the total mana value of the spell to 4+, you may Blitz instead of any alternate cost, with Henzie on the board. Coupled with our capacity to cast Commanders with considerable consistency, this makes cards like Endless One, Ugin's Conjurant, Omarthis, Ghostfire Initiate and similar free cantrips with haste, so long as they eventually die. If the fact these enter with +1/+1 counters excites you, consider that Reyhan is an option instead of Ikra, if you're looking to dunk people from orbit in exchange for losing a Blitz-able Commander; as stated, there's interesting opportunities with every permutation of R+BG in the Partner choices. Affinity, Convoke, etc all also assist in offsetting the colorless cost of our Creatures, meaning Henzie enjoys cycling through a Frogmite just fine, and at worst we can treat it like a Memnite or Rog, and pitch it to our copious sac effects.

This brings to mind the fact that we do need to find Henzie, and while that isn't difficult, it isn't instantaneous either. There's your fairly obvious options that also Blitz once he's out—Sidisi, Undead Vizier, Grim Servant, Emeritus of Woe—but I must stress that Black & Green can find a Creature more readily than any other color combination. You're in prime Secret Commander colors, and as an added bonus, I want to bring up another card you should include: Panglacial Wurm. We're already doing all this tutoring, playing fetchlands, and more, so why not Blitz for ~{G}{G} a hasty 9/5 Trampler from your library? The look on your opponents' faces will be utterly unmatched. You have a moral obligation to include Panglacial Wurm in any Henzie-adjacent list running fetchlands; while traditional Henzie has issues getting the necessary discount to really make it sing, having two Commanders sure does help. We can also continually cycle through Sabin, Master Monk & Tenacious Underdog for extra draws as well, and if that's the plan, doubling up on a Passionate Archaeologist (which also sees Panglacial) is great, with two Commanders. With all this talk of casting cards from odd spaces, it's worth refreshing what Henzie can and can't Blitz, which comes down to the difference between alternate casting costs versus casting permissions. Chainer, Nightmare Adept & Ebondeath, Dracolich are both great cards for Henzie, because they allow you the permission to cast a Spell from a different zone, even though you're given the onus to choose which casting cost to use (normal, Blitz, etc). Bolas' Citadel, Bloodbraid Challenger, Tlincalli Hunter, etc instead provide their own alternate ways to cast Spells, whether via paying life, an Escape cost, or {0}, none of which can be coupled with Blitz. Look for ways to gain permissions to cast spells from odd spaces, such as our beloved Panglacial friend, rather than alternate costs.

Decklists are kept updated, and may change with set releases.

I've always had a fondness for 'Secret Commanders', with one of my longest-held paper decks being a Gorex, the Tombshell Ancient Cellarspawn-centric list (which you can read about here). The feeling of Henzie going from a clunky enabler to a powerful midgame engine is night & day, and as you've probably reckoned with already, Rog is just a busted Commander in most contexts. Even if all you're doing is chucking him away for a ritual or Village Rites, that's still a potent opener. Finding Henzie is a cinch, with all the aforementioned tutors, as well as things like Eldritch Evolution on any of our 1-mana dorks, but the fact that we can get established with our Partners makes the early game less of a hassle to manage. Indeed, whether you're aiming to slam aggressive fair 4+ mana bodies, or treat it like an odd cantrip machine via X-Spells, there's a ton to love from any Partner pairing in this strategy. While I do favor having a free Commander, and one we can Blitz, that's ultimately personal preference, and I encourage you, dear reader, to use this article as a springboard to dive into your own souped-up Henzie list!
