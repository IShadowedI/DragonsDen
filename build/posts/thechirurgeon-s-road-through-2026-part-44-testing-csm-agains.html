TheChirurgeon's Road Through 2026, Part 44: Testing CSM against Space Wolves

October 22nd, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive play progress through 2026. Last week I talked about my plans for the 2026 Grand Narrative and World Championships of Warhammer, how I'm planning to paint and build armies for both, and how I might tackle those hobby challenges. This week I'm setting that plan in motion, starting with some test games and more hobby progress on my Red Corsairs, who kind of became my key hobby project for 2026.

Getting in Some Practice

Over the last few weeks I've been kind of snowed in writing content updates in the wake of the September 2026 Balance Update, which released alongside the new Codex: Space Marines and more or less upended the entire game with stats and points updates to every marine faction. I've been looking through results over the last few weeks, updating Detachment Focus articles (like Cabal of Chaos), and thinking about how I might want to tackle list building in the latter half of the year. While my operating plan is currently to work on Emperor's Children for worlds, I have Red Corsairs in front of me right now for the Grand Narrative.

For the most part, the toughness changes don't seem to matter that much. They matter in the context of being shot/attacked by other marines, but a lot of weapons went to S5 to compensate for this change, making some (power weapons) comparatively worse, and keeping wound values fairly similar. Terminators being at T6 is interesting, but it's not a particularly common breakpoint. one of the funniest parts of this is honestly that there are now very few T4 units in the game.

What does matter however is having S5, AP-1 bolters. Specifically combi-bolters. I made entire Top cuts happen on the back of Scarab Occult Terminators having massed quantities of AP-2 shooting, and that's replicable on the cheap with CSM terminators now - you can combine terminators with S5 AP-1 bolters with Sorcerers in Terminator Armour to give a target +1 AP on incoming attacks, and when the Terminators do their Dark Pact for Sustained or Lethal Hits (a key part of the Tsons sorcerer), they automatically get to re-roll their to hit rolls. As an extra bonus, they get +1 Strength on their bolters (to S6) when shooting in Cabal of Chaos thanks to being a [PSYKER] unit.

That's a solid base. Using that, I put together this list:

It's a little wonky, and not likely to be what I actually take to the Grand Narrative. For one, I'm unlikely to bring Huron to the event. I may have the model on hand but I tend to think having named characters at the Grand Narrative is kind of lame. Huron's got his own shit going on, he wouldn't be slumming it with my guys. I may still bring the Masters of the Maelstrom, though - they're a bit more instrumental to building playable lists in 11th edition and they're painted very well. Fabius is a bit more of a wild card here as well in that regard - he's a named character but again, I don't think you can build great lists without him so I'm on the fence there. May also depend on my ability to get more guys painted. We'll see.

The general idea here is the Terminators drop in and shoot things to death. The unit with Huron is OC +1, which makes them good Take and Hold objective holders, plus they have a reactive move (and VotLW gives me a second to play with). The Bikes and Red Corsairs Raiders are my forward action units/objective holders, and the Chosen are my third big threat in the army. There's also a Nemesis Claw. I painted them. And they're a [PSYKER] unit, which is pretty funny. It lets them do fun stuff with the Detachment. That's more a bad unit I'm taking because they're painted well, but they have some solid utility.

I need to start getting in some good WCW practice as well, so I called up my buddy Garrett Moore, who you can find occasionally writing some stellar content for our Subscriber-only service. He's also going to Worlds, having secured his ticket at a teams event this year. And he's running Space Marines, an army I am dreading and need to play against. Here's his war crime list:

The Wolf-Touched Bike Captain is the real capital-N Nasty Shit in this list, joining a large unit of Wulfen to give them extra movement (thanks to the MOUNTED keyword) on top of the ability to Advance and Charge but the Thunderwolves and Outriders are also cracked here, moving 14" in Stormlance before they advance and charge, and all of the units I just mentioned can auto-advance 6" before they charge. This list has me terrified, partly because it just seems like what I'm doing but better in every way, and partly because it has just way too many units for what it seems like it should have - you have four massive threats: Wulfen, Grimnar + Wolf Guard, and two big units of Thunderwolves, and then you still have eight units on top of that, giving you lots of fast ability to spread out and dominate the table. I am legitimately worried I won't be able to deep strike if I don't go first, and even then it's a crap shoot.

The Mission: Take and Hold Mirror - Battlefield Dominance

I'm going first. Which is good here, because even though I want to react to Garrett, I need to get deep strikers down before he eats the entire fucking table. My plan is to try and drop after him as much as possible - I need my units to come in and kill key threats, not get charged. I put the Nemesis Claw in reserves by accident (forgot to deploy them), along with both units of Terminators. Terminators being 370 points in CSM is a neat benefit.

I draw Outflank and Burden on turn 1 and pass the turn. I'll score 4 for Burden at the end of his turn. Garrett plays cagey on his turn as well, trying to avoid getting alpha struck. He can play aggressively with his Eradicators and Impulsors, because the idea is to drop the Eradicators and bring the Impulsors up to try and move block me aggressively, pinning me in my Deployment Zone early. On my second turn I draw Secure No Man's Land and Behind Enemy Lines. I'm not able to steal the middle objective for Secure, but I kill Garrett's Impulsor to stop it from gumming up my end of the table and score Outflank with my Nemesis Claw.

It's around this point that I notice we aren't really playing the same game when it comes to Command points. It's the bottom of turn 2, and I've spent one Command point, and I look over at the totals:

What the fuck are we even doing here? Garrett is getting extra command points essentially for free and getting them at the start of the battle round. I cannot stress enough that making this "if your warlord is on the table" rule should just be a game rule and not something rolled out in codexes. It is incredibly tedious to play against if you're not a codex haver and feels completely busted. In part because it meant Garrett never had to even think about discarding a secondary for CP - why would he, when doing so provides no value?

I end up struggling to drop second against Garrett. My big plan - dropping the Terminators via Ingress to hit is Thunderwolves after a charge - gets ruined when he re-rolls his charge roll for them into an 11 and just smashes into my Terminators, no problem. I'll lose half of them to the now 3 damage Thunderwolf melee attacks before clapping back, and Huron will just roll incredibly disappointingly to ensure one of the cavalry lives. I'll fall back and kill him with other units.

On the other side of the table I get a fun little reminder that no, we aren't playing the same game when my three bikes go up against Garrett's. For the same points cost (80), marine bikes get 4 wounds apiece instead of 3, have BS 2+ bolters with [ASSAULT] and [TWIN-LINKED] and they get [SUSTAINED HITS] and either +1 to hit on their thunder hammers or +1 damage on their other melee weapons when they charge. Again: What the fuck are we doing here?

The real brutal game-ender of a turn happens when I fail a 3" charge with my raiders into the scouts at mid-table (seen above), then on the following turn attempt to take out the Thunderwolves with Terminator Shooting. My Terminator Sorcerer fails his psychic test roll and takes 3 mortal wounds on the unit, killing a model and getting no AP debuff. Ok, fine it happens. Then my other Terminator Sorcerer attempts the same thing and also fails, letting Garrett drop Armour of Contempt to just shrug off the damage and keep his TWC unit alive.

Ultimately I'm out of units before Grimnar even hits the table in a meaningful fashion. I've managed to take out the Wulfen and the TWCs but there's still a ton on the table and I was only ever able to deep strike in my own Deployment zone - a brutal combo for an army that needs to drop in and shoot. Having attempted this with Veterans, I think the smart play is to switch to Warpstrike Champions. There's more value there, and being able to pick back up is a big benefit I'm just kind of missing.

Result: 95-58, Loss

This was a brutal loss because it felt unwinnable at the outset and I'm not sure what my counterplay is. I think I need to retool completely and work on a Warpstrike Champions list that runs different units - it may be time for Mutilators, though I don't love the idea of putting them in here. That said, I could definitely do a set that are based on Assault Centurions, and that seems really cool conceptually, now that I think about it. Stay tuned.

Hobby Progresss

My big focus right now is on my Red Corsairs. I need to get them done in time for the Grand Narrative, which is about a month away. They're in good shape already, and I'm thinking about how I might want to do some of these units and hit on bigger centerpiece models. My Dallas lads are all still around, and I need to finish the following at least:

  • 2 Red Corsairs Raiders (I have 8/10 painted)
  • 5 Red Corsairs Chosen (converted using Raiders models)

On top of that, I have some stretch goals:

  • 5 Red Corsairs Terminators
  • 5 Red Corsairs Warp Talons
  • 2 Red Corsairs Predators
  • Red Corsairs Multilators

Those stretch goals I'll take as I can. On the topic of the two Raiders, I got those painted over the weekend:

This guy's done, but I also had another I was doing, and it turned out I'd forgotten to do a second meltagun in the unit, so I had to model him holding one, and did a strap for it out of green stuff. I think it turned out great:

Then there's this guy, the first of my Red Corsairs Chosen. The idea here is to use Raiders bodies with the arms and heads of Chosen, to give them the right wargear and to give them a bit more of a "Chaos touch."

I'm really happy with this guy and working on the next four, as well as the Warp Talons. They're converted from Mk. VI jump units, using Warp Talon bits and Vanguard Veteran lightning claws. I like how they look so far- very much in line with the aesthetic I'm going for here.

The Tone Pro paints are really nice on these, giving me a nice, saturated red that blends easily with layering. I've been enjoying using them for my newer models. I'm going to finish painting this guy, see how I like him, then figure out my plan for the rest.

Final Thoughts

That's it for this week. Next week it'll be more painting and hopefully another game in but I have my work cut out for me. I'm really not sure what I can do at the moment to compete in the new marine/Ork meta but I'm sure some things will emerge soon and I'll focus on copying what works in those areas. Only poorly, and tailored to my ability to paint units in time for the World Championships.

See you Thursday.
