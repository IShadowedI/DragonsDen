Thoughts

White Scars claim the first trophy today, going all-in on a very thematically appropriate biker and speeder build. Proper lightning warfare here, congratulations Marcus.

Archetype

Delve Hearthguard Ingress

Thoughts

A novel Votann combo takes the second undefeated slot here, combining melee brutality from Beserks with one big Hearthguard brick. That combination creates one of the most vicious Rapid Ingress menus out there, and the speed at which this list can carve through foes that disrespect it is staggering. It also makes the interesting choice to run without forts, which reduces the threat projection of the big Beserk squads a bit, but makes the list much more adaptable to terrain, and allows it to fully leverage Hidden. It feels like a far more valid option to exclude them in 11th compared to 10th. The list is rounded off with a fairly high number of small utility pieces, ensuring that it can reliably push buttons for Priority Assets. That Disposition feels very strong with this setup, probably better than either Purge or Recon (the other easily accessible options for Delve), so another great reason to try this combo. Congratulations to Ben.

Archetype

Big Robot

Thoughts

Robot big. The Porphyrion is very much one of those remaining Forge World datasheets that's strong enough to pop up and make a splash sometimes, and changes in how specialist anti-tank units are handled in both the Ork and Marine books add an incentive to give one a go. T13 on a big hull is now a much more impactful breakpoint when the anti-tank you're worrying about is S12 Hunter profiles rather than S8-10 stuff with Wound modifiers and re-rolls. You just gotta hope and pray you don't run into an Eldar player still defiantly running Fuegan with 10 Fire Dragons. Alongside the big guy you've got a bunch of Cerastus Knights here, and it's worth highlighting that the combination of the magna lascannons and double Atrapos makes this unusually solid at taking down spammed 4W Terminators, also obviously a good place to be right now. Congratulations Lionel for going big and going undefeated!

Thoughts

Thoughts

Big Bugs vs. Big Boyz here - are there enough Boyz to overcome triple Norn in a take and hold mirror?

It definitely seems plausible that there could be. The bugs have decent damage output, but not really the right tools for killing lots of Boyz fast, unless they roll hot on the Norn blast gun. They will also need to commit two Norns to the central objective to reliably hold onto it, and the Orks really only need to take down one of those for things to start tilting in their direction. With access to Ghaz and a full Flash Gitz unit, I'd expect that to happen and the Orks to take it. However, there are a couple of curveballs that could upend this. Both a good Shadow drop on the Tyranids turn, or the Orks flubbing their roll on a well-timed Savage Roar in theirs, could completely change the game here, as in both those cases it's a while before the Warbosses can remove the shock from their units. Given the bafflingly low scores here on a mission where I'd expect at least a middling floor, my guess is that something like that happpened and it was so catastrophic that the game got called for the Tyranids rather than playing on.
