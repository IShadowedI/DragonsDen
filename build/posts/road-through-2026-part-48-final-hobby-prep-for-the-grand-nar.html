Road Through 2026, Part 48: Final Hobby Prep for the Grand Narrative

November 20th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive play progress through 2026. Last week I got in a trio of practice games by attending the Ettin November RTT at my friendly local game store. That was a great warm-up for some of the work I’m doing this year and with those games under my belt, it was time to buckle in and work on the hobby progress I need to get done. This post should have been out yesterday but well, I had hoped to use the plane wifi to finish this article and publish it and United Airlines had other ideas about whether they should have working internet or inflight entertainment.

In that regard, there are two sets of hobby projects on my plate: Things I wanted to finish in time for the Grand Narrative, and work that needs to be done in time for the World Championships. The two have a lot of overlap, but aren’t necessarily fully synched. There are things I need for the WCW that I don’t necessarily need for the Grand Narrative; I could run a ten-man Terminator unit but that’s going pretty hard in the paint for the soft lads of the Grand Narrative and I like running a wider variety of units at those anyways.

By my reckoning, here’s what I need to get painted before worlds:

  • 1 Terminator Sorcerer
  • 5 Terminators
  • 3 Mutliators
  • Kravek Morne
  • 3 Chosen

That’s the WCW list. For the Grand Narrative I generally wanted to get those painted, plus if I could get some of my Legionaries updated, that’d be a strong added bonus. I can eat a lot of the points in my Grand Narrative list with a Land Raider; I’m not planning to take one to the WCW. I may still take a Vindicator, though - haven’t decided yet. Vindicators are certainly playable in a Veterans of the Long War list, anyways.

Kravek Morne

This was the big project. I already painted up a Kravek Morne back when the model released, but he’s painted in traditional Iron Warriors colors. Instead what I needed was a Red Corsairs version and that’s where I figured I’d do something special as a proxy - and convert/paint Armenneus Valthex, the Forgemaster of the Astral Claws/Red Corsairs. He had a Forge World model back in the day, and his deal is having a conversion beamer and electro whips. I decided to build him from a Warpsmith - it’s a cool model and fits the unit - adding a conversion beamer and an Escher whip to complete the look. I decided to only do a single whip and opt for the hammer, in part because it looks cool and in part because it makes it more clear that he represents Kravek, who also has a hammer.

The conversion itself wasn’t too bad; the hardest part was sourcing a conversion beamer. Originally I had though to use the one from a plastic Contemptor - thanks to “Contemptor” Kevin Stillman for sending me one - but that ended up too big so I just ended up snagging an old resin Valthex off eBay and using the original bit. The whip is from an Escher model and provided by a regular at my local store named Justin (thanks again!). The mount on the backpack is part of a Plague Marine plague spitter that I cut apart after seeing the idea on another Valthex conversion on Reddit. This was a lot of chopping and testing but in the end I’m really happy with the result.

This was also my first time playing with Tone Pro and OSL and I’m pretty happy with the result. Doing OSL is always a ton of fun and it’ always the little things that sell it - in this case I think painting the grass on the base made a huge difference.

Mutilators

I’ve been working on this one for a few weeks but with the Grand Narrative closing in I had to finish the project. This was an idea I had a while back and while I’d have loved to do a little more converting on them I think the end concept - Mutilators as former Centurions - really comes through.

The fists in particular look great and I love how the drills look on the final models. The poses are dynamic and the helmets look great. I think these make a great centerpiece unit in the army and I’m excited to be taking them to the event.

Chosen and Legionaries

The next big thing on my list was finishing my five Chosen and updating the Legionaries. I had originally painted 15 Red Corsairs Legionaries back in 2019 for NOVA, planning to take them as part of a “Spiky 17” Battalion Detachment in my army. They’re still around and I use them from time to time, but they could use an update, both in terms of generally not being painted well enough and having bad loadouts - they all have just a boltgun.

Now, having just a bolter isn’t terrible now that they’re all S5 AP-1, but it’s not ideal for the entire unit. I want a mix of bolt pistol + chainsword and I want some heavy weapons in there. So I spent some time breaking the arms off a few painted guys, updating their paint schemes using the new brighter Tone Pro reds, and changing out some of their weapons.

Updating older models always feels really good - it’s a quick way to score some painting “wins” without having to paint an entirely new model, and in this case gave me an entire new unit in about a day’s worth of painting.

Terminator Sorcerer

This one’s still in its infancy, but I’m happy with it so far. I stopped by my local store last weekend and Max hooked me up with a Terminator LIbrary and after a bit of chopping and gluing, I have a conversion I really like that fits that “half loyalist” aesthetic I”m going for. He’ll make a good lone Sorcerer option in the army.

That wraps up my progress - I’m one Chosen short of finishing the set (and he’s about half done), and I need to start on those Terminators but I’m in a very solid place with two weeks to go before the WCW.

Next: The Grand Narrative

That wraps things up for this week but I’ll be back next week with a full update on the 2026 Grand Narrative. As I write this I’m at IAH waiting on my flight to board, so by the time you read this I’ll be well on my way. My goal remains as always to win best painted, so wish me luck. And if you’re at the event, stop by and say “hi” - I love meeting readers and I’ve got assloads of Tabletop Battles dice to hand out this year.

See you next Thursday.
