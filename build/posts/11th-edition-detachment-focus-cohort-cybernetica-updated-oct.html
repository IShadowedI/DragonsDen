11th Edition Detachment Focus: Cohort Cybernetica (Updated October 5, 2026)

October 5th, 2026 via Goonhammer
In our Detachment Focus series we take a deep dive into an army’s Detachments, covering what’s in them, how they play, and how they’ll fit into the broader meta and your games. In this Detachment Focus we’re looking at the Cohort Cybernetica Detachment for the Adeptus Mechanicus.

Do you like medium-size robots? Do you tire of the rare bits of flesh remaining in your standard Adeptus Mechanicus infantry? Well, the Cohort Cybernetica is your solution, giving you an army focused on Kastelan robots and vehicle units. Through the transition into the new edition, the Cohort has found itself as one of the premiere 1 point Detachments the Omnissiah has access to and there is a lot of strength in here for those of us trying to play a more vehicle-heavy list.

Changelog

  • 2026-10-05 (Current): Updated for 11th Edition Codex.
  • 2025-02-10: Initial publication

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

Force Disposition: Take and Hold

The Cohort Cybernetica is a unique Detachment. With all of the stratagems only being able to activate in your command phase, this army is for those with a plan. However, as we are 40k players that is a rare find in this hobby. NONETHELESS, the support here is VERY strong and overall I think a super solid starting point in the army.

Take and Hold is good here for the army as the army list you’ll generally want to be building towards is aiming for a very unshiftable defensive gunline that wants to slowly bully its way up the board. Being able to sit around on points with Heavy and access to Ignores Cover is where a lot of your tanks want to be as they fire away at your opponent’s army, and they’re happy with that approach here. This is one of the first 2 point Detachments to come down to just 1 point, and man does it have a lot of tools for a 1 point Detachment. Having access to possibly 12 stratagems and 4 different enhancements could be overwhelming, but for now it’s great!

Detachment Rule: Cyber-Psalm Programming

Add 2” to the Move characteristic of models in LEGIO CYBERNETICA units from your army. In addition, unless that unit is battle-shocked, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in that unit.

Overall, not the flashiest ability as it only affects one unit, but with the most recent cost of Kastelan Robots hitting sub-300, I think that you’re suddenly fine with that as lists pop up running 4-12 of the big oafs. With access to Mobile enhancements from other Detachments and a big movement buff we’ll see later, these robots can find themselves moving double the speed on their datasheet to reach the opponent's lines. The additional OC bringing each robot to 3 a pop isn’t nothing either, as most units after getting rock 'em sock' em roboted aren’t gonna have the models left around to hold their current point. Overall a clean rule (and much improved from its Codex launch).

Adeptus Mechanicus - Tech-Priest Dominus
Credit: Pendulin

Enhancements

All of the Enhancements for this Detachment are for TECH-PRIEST models only, and three of the four affect VEHICLE units, though Lord of Machines affects enemy vehicles.

  • NECROMECHANIC (20 Points) - Once per battle round, when a saving throw made for a friendly LEGIO CYBERNETCA or ADEPTUS MECHANICUS VEHICLE model within 12” of the bearer is failed, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If they do, change the Damage characteristic of that attack to 0. This is the best enhancement of the bunch and as close to an auto-take as you will see here. Usually this is given to a Tech-Priest Enginseer who sits comfortably within all of your nice tanks and hands them out a 5+ FNP. That extra defensive stat along with a big damage blank makes landing damage onto your units very difficult and this enhancement a mainstay of the build.
  • LORD OF MACHINES (15 Points) - Once per turn, at the start of your opponents shooting phase, select one enemy vehicle unit within 12” and visible to the bearer. That unit has to take a Leadership Test: If they pass then until the end of the phase they get -1 to their Hit rolls; if they fail, that unit is not eligible to shoot this phase. The “I had 15 points and didn’t really have anything else to grab” enhancement, it’s not necessarily bad but it’s not something you should comfortably plan your turn around. I’ve seen it brought on the 2nd (or 3rd) datasmith to make gunline armies sweat a little once the robots have made their way into combat, but it’s not consistent enough for me to recommend it over other things.
  • EMOTIONLESS CLARITY (10 Points) - Once per turn, when a friendly LEGIO CYBERNETICA or ADEPTUS MECHANICS VEHICLE model with the Deadly Demise ability that is within 12” of the bearer is destroyed, you can use this enhancement. If you do, skip the roll for Deadly Demise - you automatically do damage. There’s tech here and I’ve always enjoyed something like this on a model nearby my little distraction Dunecrawler as it blows up on the melee army I’m going into. However, with how niche the case is going to be for you to use it I think this also falls into the “I had 10 extra points and Lord of Machines costs 15” category
  • ARCH-NEGATOR  (5 Points) - Ranged weapons equipped by the bearer have the [ANTI-VEHICLE 4+] ability. “I had 5 extra points and Emotionless Clarity costs 10”

In sum, a fairly mediocre selection of enhancements with Necromechanic being the standout choice and usually taken. The rest are quite forgettable and largely there are better things to spend points on.

Kastelan Robots with Cybernetica Datasmith. Credit: Rockfish

Stratagems

The six Stratagems here can be used on either LEGIO CYBERNETICA or VEHICLE units. One of the unique things about the Cohort Cybernetica Stratagems is that every single one of them has to be used in your Command phase. This is a double-edged sword: On the one hand, you’ll never have to think about using them reactively or during turns. On the other, all of your Stratagem moves will have to be thought out in advance and you can’t double up on stratagems as you’re still only restricted to one per unit per phase.

  • Motiver Imperative (Battle Tactic, 1 CP) - Used in your Command Phase on an Adpetus Mechanicus Vehicle unit. Until the start of your next Command phase, increase its movement by 3” and add 1 to its advance and charge rolls. Kastelan Robots, a fairly slow unit overall, benefit tremendously from this stratagem on the turn you plan to commit them to combat. The other unit to benefit the most from this Stratagem is Sydonian Dragoons with Taser Lances if you decide to use them but I’m more interested in 11” moving robots with +1 to charge.
  • Machine Superiority (Epic Deed, 1 CP) - Used in your Command phase. One Legio Cybernetica or Adeptus Mechanicus Vehicle can Fall Back and Shoot; also it can ignore any and all modifiers applied to its characteristics and any rolls or tests it must make until the end of your turn. This is a great Stratagem and often utilized on your damage turn on Kastelan Robots, or to allow a Skorpius tank to gain its full value if one is tagged in combat. There are many powerful modifiers in the game (minuses to hit, -1 damage, halving damage, -1 to wound, OC manipulation, and much more), and being able to ignore all of them is fantastic.
  • Auto-Divinatory Targeting (Battle Tactic, 1 CP) - Used in your Command phase.  Pick a Legio Cybernetica or Adeptus Mechanicus Vehicle and an objective marker. Until the start of your next Command phase, that unit's ranged weapons become BS 3+ and gain the Ignores Cover rule, however they can only target enemies within range of your selected objective marker. The qualifier of being in range of a specific objective marker is the main limiting factor here. This is useful on a Skorpius tank or Onager, but is less useful on Kastelan Robots as the combat variant is superior to the Phosphor variant.
  • Transcendent Cogitation (Strategic Ploy, 1 CP) - Used in your Command phase on a Legio Cybernetica or Adeptus Mechanicus Vehicle unit. Until your next Command phase, both the Conqueror Imperative and the Protector Imperative are considered active for your unit. This is another great stratagem, especially for Kastelan Robots, allowing them to benefit from the +1WS and +1AP from Conqueror while also gaining the -1 to hit in melee if near battleline units to try to keep them alive. It’s also great to allow your tanks to have access to heavy shooting with an AP buff or Assault while keeping the increased Ballistic Skill. (For a fun judge call with your opponent, consider advancing fewer than 3" and seeing if either of you know if that qualifies you for the benefits Heavy.)
  • Machine Spirit Resurgent (Epic Deed, 1 CP) - Used in your Command phase on a Legio Cybernetica or Adeptus Mechanicus vehicle unit below its Starting Strength. Until the start of your next Command phase, that unit can re-roll Hit rolls. If it's below half Strength, it can also re-roll wound rolls. The second half of this stratagem will rarely come up, but the first part is outstanding and means that you can run the Kastelan Robots without needing Cawl to boost their efficiency with their quality attacks. 
  • Benevolence of the Omnissiah (Battle Tactic, 1 CP) - Used in your Command phase. For one battle round, one Legio Cybernetica or Adeptus Mechanicus Vehicle unit gains a Feel No Pain 6+ ability, which is improved to Feel No Pain 5+ vs Mortal Wounds.

    This is okay and useful in certain matchups, but usually the Enginseer placing a 5+FNP on one model is enough to tank mortals and you can instead spend CP to make sure the Robots get to where they are needed and kill what you need them to kill.

Six very strong stratagems here that are all going to help you in situations commonly in the game make this detachment really shine. As well the ability to basically have 2 ways to pseudo ignore cover in this is amazing as it makes your gunline builds much more viable. I think anyone looking to compete with AdMech will find this to be a simple and good place to start out in the army.

Sicarian Ruststalkers. Credit: Pendulin

Playing This Detachment

The way I would focus this detachment comes in the form of pairing it with Lords of the Forge to give your Kastellans access to stratagems like AoC while on a point (remember we’re in T&H or Prio) while your tanks follow them up with overwhelming firepower. As well with access to the new Mobile keyword and just the core rules changes to vehicles moving through enemy models, the Kastellans really can find their range to be quite threatening in this detachment.

Whether you go Priority Assets or lock down the board with Take and Hold I think you’re going to be in a strong spot but with access to shoot and do actions from Battleclade and Lords, you do have a super strong and easy pivot into Prio if you want it or your team needs it. As well, Priority tends to make the other dispositions walk towards it which all of your guns really enjoy the thought of as they’re more easily able to get the +1 to hit from heavy to help put damage down range.

Kastelan Robots and a Cybernetica Datasmith. Credit: Pendulin

Strengths

  • Let's be honest: One of the main reasons you started a Mechanicus army was the dream of running ancient robots. This detachment does allow and encourage you to run more than one unit of them if desired.
  • Great suite of stratagems for a wide variety of common situations/problems. 
  • Admech has the cheap units to surround an expensive core of 300+ point units.
  • Gun go boom.

Weaknesses

  • With a very restrictive range of units buffed from this Detachment, there isn’t much wiggle room in the kinds of units you want to bring.
  • Having all of your Stratagems being used in the Command phase in an army without guaranteed additional CP from a rule (like a Calgar, Azreal or Imotekh) makes the Detachment very difficult to play. Even players with extreme CP discipline have struggled with this problem when playing Cybernetica.
  • Kastelan Robots have continued to go down in cost, but their base cost isn't the entire problem, as they are effectively saddled with the cost of each Robot, the Datasmith for the unit, and usually an Enginseer; possibly Necromechanic as well on one of the characters. That's a much more expensive unit to justify.

A Sample List

Editor’s Note: The old editors note talked about how AdMech hadn’t won anything with Cohort in 10th. But in 11th that’s changed, so I just wanna take this time to say hi :3 -Floof

Recently we’ve seen a Cyberlords list doing very well and being talked about at the top end of the scene that I would like to highlight here. A strong core of 4 robots, the unkillable crab, and good tough shooting from Cawl, Thulia, and the Dissies makes a very durable list that plays the primary game well while having just enough good trash to get solid secondary scoring. With this specific list doing well in some European events, I would expect this to start popping up as the default way to build AdMech right now.

Final Thoughts

Overall, Cohort Cybernetica, even with its very limiting Detachment rule, is one of the better Mechanicus Detachments in my opinion. If you love running Kastelan Robots, there is enough power here to justify running one to two large units of them. However, spamming them will run into difficult matchups as you start running low on resources to play the mission, and because your damage is combat oriented on units that can’t go through walls or advance and charge in this Detachment, it can be hard to cripple the enemy in a single turn.  With that being said, the support offered in this does look rewarding enough for those who are willing to put in the reps. Thank you for reading and have a lovely day!
