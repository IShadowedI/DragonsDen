Tabletop Battles Reviews- Ogor Mawtribes Gutbusters and Beastclaw Raiders

August 15th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Alrighty Ogor Fans! A few weeks ago we devoured the new Ogor Mawtribes Army Box and now we’re back with the second course of tasty gristle and throbbing meat. Games Workshop were extremely generous with their portions of review models for us to gobble up! So the team has been working hard on building and painting all these new culinary delights to report back to you here. Let's tuck in!

Ogor Mawtribes.
Warhammer Community

What's New

  • Morga the Mighty/Tyrant on Glutthorn (Multi-build kit, Hero or Unique hero, 120mm Oval)
  • Grell Firefist (Unique Hero, 50mm base)
  • 5 Hunters with Sabrefangs (40mm and 60mm oval bases)

For all the hotness in the Mawseekers go HERE.

Morga the Mighty/Tyrant on Glutthorn

Theresa:

Building

Building Morga the Mighty was a seemingly small task: There were a surprisingly few amount of sprues for such a large lady! Most of the bits fit together well, yet some seams were impossible to hide without sprue goo, especially when they were running down the front of the face of Grutta, her mount. One of my biggest gripes of the kit was where Morga herself sits on the back of Grutta, with the knees-down portion of Morga's legs attaching to Grutta's mount. At this junction, the pieces seemingly can't be put together without a massive gap above the knee. Thankfully, the gap isn't terribly noticeable, but some green stuff to fill the gap would go a long way! The kit is super easy to sub-assemble. I kept Morga separate, as well as the totem pole to airbrush separately. There aren't a ton of options for the model, but if you're building the generic Tyrant the rider is significantly different. It would be easy enough to magnetize the rider to swap between Morga and the Tyrant, though. If you decide to put LEDs in Grutta's eyes (why would anyone ever do that, definitely not something I'm looking to do), the head is assembled to make your life a million times easier, and the torso is hollow!

Painting

I'd like to talk about the textures on this model. She's a giant blank canvas, with a bunch of lovely crackle texture, but there are so many surfaces to have fun with!! The metals have enough layers to make them interesting and pop against the model. I honestly think Grutta looks a bit naked without the thigh panels, but you could absolutely do some fun freehand on her rough skin to make it pop. Morga has a good amount of fun fabrics and textures on her, with teeny details like a pinky ring! She's a little small for the base, so I recommend building up the base with some rocks or foliage to really show off how cute she is.
Morga Credit: Theresa (Mawqueen Minis) Badusernametag: Morga is a cunning and thoughtful general indeed, observing the battlefield with a wry smile that tells of the tactical mind beneeath the embellished helmet.

The other option you can make from this box is a Tyrant on Glutthorn, or GLUTTRANT! the new generic mounted Tyrant for Gutbusters. This big-boned hero is a little more bombastic than his Overtyrant, with slashing choppa raised high and below issuing from maw.

You get alternate:

  • Rider (duh), who has bare head and helmeted options.
  • Back icon-mount, trophy rack thing
  • Head for the Glutthorn

As I’ve alluded above, the Gluttrant is less subtle but maybe a little more dynamic than Morga, and his Glutthorn is also angrier. Big mouth open, teeth on display. His weapon is a little generic, being honest and doesn’t have the impressive heft or savage look of those the foot Tyrant can wield. I think Morga is much cooler, but the monster head here is better, much more interesting. My main criticism of this model generally is that the big cow isn’t very menacing or impressive really. Whilst the skin is sculpted really nicely, I’m not terrified by the whole ‘docile rhino’ vibe. It’s a shame the reins are tied to the rider’s torso build, as Morga riding the more aggro Glutthorn would be nice. I feel like this is a bit of a miss in terms of a Monster mount; unlike the Maulbeast, which are mean looking, demonstrably new and a ‘fantasy’ beast, the Glutthorn is a little too ‘real’ for me. Could it have had six legs? An enormous maw? It just needs something to make it stand out, its enemies fear it and Ogors go WOW!

This kit is pretty cool for bits, which ever version you make. The whole body and arms of each build are unique, so whilst you don’t get more rider legs, you get the whole torso, arms, head (rider) and head of the Glutthorn. I for one am excited to see what a Stornehorn looks like with a Glutthorn head. This could be an easy way to make this kit stretch to TWO Gluttrants? OR just make a fancy ass Forstlord for your new Mawtribe?

I’m excited to play with this kit, but mainly for kitbashing purposes. I’ve got aspirations of leading a mighty horde of Monstrous beasts, and with these two fine riders I may have just found the bits I need and conversion impetuous to make it happen. Watch this space…

GLUTTRANT!!!

Grell Firefist

YAARRR!!!

Grell Firefist- Unique Maneater Hero- SPRUE

RIP Leadbelchers, long live Grell Firefist; wandering Ogor Maneater Pirate Captain and badass of the Mawtribe seas! This is the last of the three new Unique Heroes added to the new range, and this time we have a support foot hero with a massive mortar.

It is a little odd that they drop the cannon unit (Leadbelchers) and the Mercenary unit (Maneaters) just as they introduce a unique Hero that is both, but I am not going to argue! There is cannon (pun intended) reasons for this, as the fanatic Redd the Maw convinces the meatiest to give up blackpowder for his support. Sure. This is a great model and a nice concept for what amounts to a special character Tyrant. Here we continue the strong Ogor tradition of Guns (and cannons) for hire! Roaming the realms in search of good scraps, exotic foods and plenty of booty! As someone with a full mercenary blackpowder company I am not sad to add her to my collection.

This model comes on the stock 50mm base you get with big heroes, but unlike the Tyrant, she is actually quite diminutive. Not bigger than a Cleaver, certainly. What Grell lacks in stature she makes up in Firepower however, as that mortar is certainly girthy! 

Grell Firefist- Unique Maneater Hero
Credit: @badusernametag

This is a fun and easy build; no options, but goes together smoothly. Grell has an interesting and quite thoughtful pose for an Ogor; she is looking down at her cannon and has her hand at her side. This is fine, but I think I like something a little more dynamic and aggressive in my fantasy pirate weirdos.

I ended up taking off the stock head and giving her a thick-necked head from the Cleavers kit; added in a knife wielding arm also and I’m happier. Can’t wait to get her painted now and add her to the throng! She will have very grey skin, like my other cave dwellers.

Grell Firefist.
Credit: @badusernametag

Hunters with Sabrefangs

Beastclaw Hunters and Sabrefangs.
Credit: @badusernametag

You get crossbow, they get crossbow, EVERYBODY get crossbow?!

The Beastclaw Raiders have had a smallest portion of the feast in this new range refresh, but that isn’t to say they have been cut out of the pie altogether. By some logic, fighting infantry being covered by the Gutbusters (and now Mawseekers) and having plenty of Monster units, someone at GW decided that the Beastclaw Raiders needed a speciality in ranged units, specifically those with crossbows! The Bloodpelt Hunter shifted over to the Beastclaw sub-faction and gives a nice little boost to all the crossbow toting, infiltrating hunters in the army. I really like the fluff of the big, brutal Ogres also being capable of stealth, that they know the land and stalk prey. It’s nice bit of development that takes us beyond the basics of a brutal and terrifying Ogor horde, only bent on destruction and feasting. So in the new cannon of the Mawtribes the Beastclaw are the subtle ones, attuned with the winter and wild places and on the hunt…

The unit is made up of three Ogor Hunters with crossbows and javelins, and two Sabrefangs. The Sabrefang is a reimagining of the various hunting cats that have accompanied the Beastclaw since the World that Was. Back with a modern kit and a new look.

Beastclaw Hunters and Sabrefangs- Sprue

This is a really great kit with some top notch bits. All the new Ogor kits have some high end food and feasting bits, but these are probs my faves of the bunch. Aside from the cool leather gorget they’re wearing, the Blood Vulture eating from the champions hand and the brutal weapons they’re all carrying theres some stand out snack options I need to highlight.

The first is this gruesome Severed trolls head! Complete with horrified death grimace, this is just casually hung from a Hunters belt, one assumes for a snack later!? Baller.

trolls head!?

The second is this MASSIVE rack of ribs (Carnavlax?) that the other goon is dragging for later. Really great bit.

Amazing bits! Rack of ribs!!!

The Champion is just SO COOL and I am totally enraptured with this bad ass Ogor huntress. So many nice details on her face/head, Blood Vulture, weapons etc. This is my fave of the new range and I just loved every moment of building and painting it!

These models are the usual amazing CAD designed multi-part plastics we are all now privileged to be used to. They go together nicely, often the outer bit of the body forming an interlocking shell that works just so. This does have the advantage for making really beautiful, dynamic models, with very few visible joins or seams. The downsides can be that some joins are fiddly and not obvious to find (instructions help) and that customisation requires you to fully assemble the thing and then hack it up to do a conversion. It’s not like you can take the head, arms etc from one model and easily move to another, as the joins are all unique to each model.

In this instance, one of these is a bit of a fiddle. The one with the crossbow slung over their shoulder has some bits that don’t seem to go instinctively where they should. In all cases such as this I recommend prepping ALL the bits for the model, cleaning the mould lines etc (I use 400 git sand paper to clean all mould lines and joins) and then laying them out in order before starting. This means once you’ve started you can get them all in, before the joins dry. I find that you need to get all of the “shell” together in one go, else you get misalignment.

The crossbow over shoulder guy gets weird on the right hand side, when you get to getting the sword arm on. Take your time and it’s fine.

Hunters- Tricky bit!
Credit: @badusernametag

So here you have an option of BloodVulture or not (spear instead) on the champion and hats or no hats on each model. This isn’t enough variance for me on a unit that you might well want two of. This was true of the Ironguts and it’s a design decision that I’m not really impressed by. These are units I want to repeat without having to make onerous conversions if I don’t want to.

The Sabrefangs are a bit of a ‘meh’ to me. They’re very big plain cats in three parts each. They go together nicely enough and if you’re into sabretoothed tigers sure. But are these especially High Fantasy or AoS? Not really. I know the Ogor Mawtribes are the ‘serious’ Destruction faction, but these don’t do it for me. Could Ogres not hunt with giant mountain worms, toads, or some other unique and interesting beast!? 

In all these are Lovely models, probably my favourite to paint of the new range. A real pleasure of detail, texture and interesting features. They speak to the more subtle and quietly terrifying facet of the Ogor race, the one that creeps up on you silently in a blizzard and you never know you were taken!!

Beastclaw Hunters and Sabrefangs.
Credit: @badusernametag

Ogor Mawtribes Hunters.
Credit: @badusernametag

These droogs follow my usual style for Ogors, red/grey/brown skin, drab colours all over, rusty weapons and armour with layers of texture. I’ve gone for a really dark (Atramant black, with Naples yellow highlight) colour for the draped skin cloak/gorgets and I’m planning to carry this through to a repaint on my Beastclaw monsters and then on into Glutthorns, Maulbeasts and the rest. Pale Ogors, dark and brooding beasts; hopefully it’s a nice contrast and brings a new element to a collection, without starting from scratch. Also in this instance, some dark colours adds to the air of stealth, is my idea…

Ogor Mawtribes Hunters.
Credit: @badusernametag

Ogor Mawtribes Hunters.
Credit: @badusernametag

Final Thoughts

Badusernametag: This range release has been really impressive for the whole Ogor Mawtribes army. No sub-faction has been neglected and some of the nicest models (IMHO) sit in the Beastclaw Raiders. You can now assemble an army of nice models in any of the sub-factions without compromising on the pedigree of your meat. The Battletome even facilities this with the Armies of Renown and these are not to shabby. There is no reason not to mix all of them together however and I am excited to get moar Thicc bodies on the table and make a messy dinner…
