TheChirurgeon's Road Through 2026, Part 28: Back Half Prep

July 9th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive play progress through 2026. Last week I talked about testing against and Emperor's Children list with double Defilers. This week I'm prepping for our annual Baltimore event, the 2026 Goonhammer Open, and planning for the rest of the year. I've got a little bit of paint progress, but this is one of those weeks where I've been too busy to formally get much done; my one game I planned didn't pan out for work reasons and most of the stuff I'm painting I can't talk about until Saturday. So it's a good time to talk about the rest of the year.

My Schedule

Right now I'm in an odd holding pattern: I won best painted in Dallas and I don't really want to play in the 2026 World Championships. If I win a ticket, I'll go, but getting a ticket and going last year more or less checked that box for me and put a nice capstone on my five-year competitive journey toward some form of legitimacy. I've won a golden ticket and I've won a GT; I don't need that validation any more.

On the other hand, I do need to stay sharp and I want to keep playing events. Here's the current sched:

  • July: 2026 Baltimore Goonhammer Open. I'm going to play some games in the Narrative as a ringer, helping fill spots. I'll be taking either Death Guard or Thousand Sons for this, whatever seems easier to fit in a case.
  • July: The GW US Open in Tacoma. This is a big event (500+ players now I think), and one I've been to every year. I want to go, and I'm almost certainly taking Thousand Sons to it just because they're the army I've been practicing with and they seem really good right now.
  • August: The Frontline Gaming Lone Star Open. This is another event I've been to several times before, and where I first met Goatboy. It's a great event and I'm excited to go back.
  • September: The 2026 Challenger's Cup in Utah. I had a blast at this event last year. Green Banner put on some great events and I'm excited to go back with my friends and play at this.
  • November: The 2026 Grand Narrative. I missed this last year, and I'm going back as a narrative player this time around. I'm going to try for best painted again, and I may start back up on my Emperor's Children soon. We'll see.

That's the current schedule, but there's certainly room to fit one or two more events in there. That would give me five more for the year, and help me hit my goal of 100 games, but isn't quite enough. Right now I think I'm going to take Thousand Sons with me this weekend in order to get the extra reps in for Tacoma. I have no expectations for Tacoma; I want to make top cut but I have no interest in playing in the Shadow Round; mark me down for a round 1 loss if that happens. That said, if the numbers I've seen are true, top cut may only be 4-0 players, which would be pretty crazy.

Hobby Progress

I'm trying to get more Space Wolves done for my friend Bidwell. Currently I'm rushing to finish this guy to get him a Rune Priest, converted from the new Armageddon boxed set and an upgrade kit. I like how he's coming along.

There's still a bit of work to be done on him but I'm confident I'll finish before my flight tomorrow.

On that note, the GHO this year is the first in the new edition and there's a lot of work going into it. We've been hard at work assembling new terrain kits from Bandua for the event - these look great and have some awesome details on them. Bandua have been great about working with us on events and the detail on the new kits is something else.

We've also been working on custom terrain and concepts for the narrative. In particular, we've put together a set of custom secondary missions just for doubles play that we'll be handing out to players at the event. I had a lot of fun making these and getting them printed off as custom decks for our players is a great piece of event swag.

These, along with the shirts we've printed, make for a good setup swag-wise for this year's event. The GHO is always a fun time but physically exhausting; this year we're doing our largest 40k GT in a while with 48 players, and our narrative is currently at 8 full teams and a couple of solo players we'll help gap fill to get to a full team. That's largely how I'm going to end up playing - filling in for the missing team - but we have a few different people who may take those spots.

Beyond that it's secret hobby stuff and working with the tech team on improvements to the Tabletop Battles App; we're planning to launch a new build tonight and have it ready for this weekend so if you've been waiting on faction trackers or terrain measurements or fixes to scoring, this weekend is going to be a good weekend to update the app.

Next Time: Tacoma Prep

That's it for this week. Check back next week when I'll be recapping my experiences at the Baltimore GHO event and prepping frantically for Tacoma. It looks to be a great time and I'm excited to get back up there to see friends and run the event. Tacoma prep will be a lot less fraught when it comes to last minute rules prep; My major focus with Tacoma is to just show up, play some games in the new edition and have some good meals.

See you next week.
