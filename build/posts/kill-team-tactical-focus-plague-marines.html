[Kill Team] Tactical Focus: Plague Marines

September 24th, 2026 via Goonhammer
In this series of Kill Team articles, we take a deep dive into one of the available teams, analyzing each team's strengths and weaknesses, how to set up important plays, and ultimately what to consider to pilot the team to victory. Note that this article assumes you already have knowledge of the basic rules for each team, and will reference abilities and ploys from that team throughout. If you want to learn more about the rules for each team you can find the most up to date version of the rules on the Warhammer Community Website here. In this article, we’re covering the Grandfather’s chosen disciples, Plague Marines.

An overwhelming stench fills your nostrils. A cloud of decay so thick it blots your eyes. Pestilence and death march forward implacably, and so too do the Plague Marines. Fallen Astartes who have given their souls and their pus-filled bodies wholly to Nurgle, the Plague Marines are a slow moving but highly resilient team who bring the gift of rot to all they touch.

Basic Overview

Wallowing forward unrelentingly, Plague Marines spread their Poison, which causes a mortal wound when an operative who has it activates. A reliable piece of chip damage that’s very easy to apply, as most of your operatives' ranged weapons have it innately on their profile. Use abilities like the Malignant Plaguecaster’s Poisonous Miasma action to spread this liberally, as the more enemy operatives are Poisoned early, the more bonuses you’ll be getting later (as well as applying damage sooner.) 

Disgustingly Resilient, your second faction rule, is the Plague Marines primary source of damage reduction. When your operatives meet the enemy in battle, this rule will let you trade up favorably and stage aggressively in combination with ploys like Cloud of Flies and Sickening Resilience to let you stage in locations that would otherwise be unsafe. Any damage applied to key operatives can then be healed with Malignant Plaguecaster’s Putrescent Vitality, potentially healing a whopping 7 wounds to an operative. 

This toughness exceeds beyond just keeping Wounds high. With the Plague Bells equipment, your team will never suffer movement or hit stat penalties from being injured, so should they fall below that threshold, you will continue to enjoy your 3+ Hit stat, which will mean bad news for anybody who rushed forward to take shots at you. 

Team Strengths and Weaknesses

In keeping with their motif of slow, methodical warfare, Plague Marines excel at playing a defensive game. You’ll rarely want to overextend, instead forcing your opponent to come to you and play your game. Lumbering Death keeps your rolls consistent if you can plan to move just a bit during your activations, but even if you’re only planning to take two or three Shoot actions, investing a command point for the rerolls can pay big dividends. Importantly, it is always on when you’re retaliating, so your Champion and Fighter gain even more bonuses in combat should your opponent dare to venture into the Plaguelands.

Your other big strength comes from punishing those operatives who get too close to take shots. Your flagship strategy ploy, Contagion, is something you want to use nearly every turning point. Reducing opponents' hit stats can be important to make them waste resources like Purity Seals or command points, and further increase the strain on your enemies economy. The Icon Bearer is an important combo with Contagion as well. If he is within your opponents territory (note: not wholly within) Contagion will cost you 0CP. Just as important, while other operatives require the enemy to be poisoned before Contagion reduces their stats, the Icon Bearers malignant presence is enough to worsen them (as long he can see them or vice versa.) This makes him a particularly tough operative to deal with, as anybody who charges or gets within 2” to Shoot will suffer the consequences.

Your firefight ploy Sickening Resilience is a standout performer that effectively guarantees the reduction of incoming opponent damage, keeping someone alive from a spiked opponent shoot, or slowing down damage from incoming an Fight. This ploy stays active the entire activation once you turn it on, which can be very easily used to turn an enemies guaranteed kill into death on their own activation. Always try to keep a Command Point around for this. Between Contagion and Sickening Resilience, the team can be very difficult to dislodge once their in position without a significant expenditure of enemy resources.

The tricky part can be getting them where you want them, as Plague Marines are slow. With only a 5” movement value, careful consideration is required to squeeze the juice out of the rotting fruit. You won’t be able to adjust as quickly if things start to go badly in a certain area of the Killzone, and this problem is exacerbated on Close Quarters Killzones where moving through doors will further reduce this movement value. Plan carefully where you wish to commit your forces, as you will have difficulty reallocating your forces to a different sector.

Besides the movement impediment, Plague Marines will suffer from the usual action economy limitations any elite team suffer from. The movement simply makes it worse as you won’t be able to quickly shore up a defense that starts to break on an objective marker. Certain operatives really want to be performing two Shoot actions or otherwise taking aggressive actions to Poison the enemy. This can be difficult if you drop operatives too soon as you might not have the actions to do everything you want to do later to score on Crit Op.

Standout Tactics

Once you’ve navigated your operatives into the position, the hard part is over. Plague Marines have been gifted by the Grandfather with many nefarious Strategy and Firefight ploys to ruin the day of anybody who dares enter the Garden of Rot they sow. Once you’ve begun allocating your Poison tokens to enemy operatives, which you should be doing as often as possible with the Malignant Plaguecaster, your Toxic operatives can begin to shine. Your Warrior has this rule on his boltgun, meaning anybody he shoots will be suffering the wrath of a 4/5 ranged weapon. Combine this with your Plague Rounds equipment option, a mandatory selection, and you will be guaranteeing critical success with the Severe rule. With Plague Rounds also applying Poison to your bolt weapons, your Warrior can also Shoot once, Poison the enemy, and then shoot again with a boosted damage profile. 

Plague Rounds will also apply to your Bombardier operative, who gains the Toxic rule on his Blight Grenades. This is another operative who can Poison an enemy with his boltgun, and then take advantage of his boosted 3/5 profile to deal massive damage to a group of enemies. Combine this with Virulent Poison to maximize damage against a group of enemies who have foolishly clustered together, and don’t forget Blight Grenades also have the Severe rule to guarantee crits against foes. 

Your Champion also has Toxic on his Plague Sword, turning it into something that will take down any 15W model in 3 hits, a very nice breakpoint for most operatives in the game. You can use Virulent Poison here before the Fight action if your target isn’t Poisoned to help guarantee that damage, as the Champion also comes stock with a Plasma Pistol. Charging, Fighting, and then Shooting some unfortunate soul is a very nice way to pick up double kills and even the activation odds. The Champion will also restore up to 3 wounds every turning point when Poisoned operatives suffer damage, giving him even more survivability. This is an operative who wants to be up at the frontlines, so don’t be afraid to toss him into the fray, as he has the tools he needs to survive, or otherwise force your opponent to spend a lot of resources to bring him down.

Your Icon Bearer is worth a special mention here. Since this operative always reduces your opponent hit stat with 3”, you can combine your Cloud of Flies with where he intends to end up, and force your opponent to pick a Poison. Either they stay outside 3” where there shots will be obscured, or they move within 3” to hit worse. Use this with his enhanced marker control to secure a key point and swing the game your way. This operative will be a lynchpin of many of your strategies, as well giving you a key Strategy Ploy for free, so do your best to utilize him appropriately.   

Tac Ops and Crit Ops

Occupying the fantastic archetypes of Seek and Destroy and Security, Plague Marines have some great choices to choose from. In the Seek and Destroy category, Dominant remains the best choice. Rout can be unreliable as it requires you to do the thing Plague Marines are bad at: move. Sweep and Clear strains your action economy, and if your opponent chooses to not interact with Objectives can leave you a bit anemic on points. With their resilience and refusal to die, as well giving you extra time to position if you play a bit a cagey on Turning Point 2.

In Security, your best choice is Plant Banner. Your Icon Bearer is an excellent choice for securing this marker, using the aforementioned Contagion/Cloud of Flies combination to lock down the marker, and keeping him covered with other operatives. Position him in Turning Point 1 to move where you intend to place the banner and keep the Plaguecaster nearby to provide healing if he needs it.

Like other elite teams, Plague Marines enjoy Crit Ops that minimize the effort they need to put into it, especially into those teams that out activate them. Clever planning with the Icon Bearer can allow him to secure your Banner and the central objective marker, and lets you focus on a single sector to commit your forces to. This will also ease the strain of repositioning if operatives begin to falter. Teams with many operatives may move a lot of them up trying to secure kills, making them ripe for a grim harvest with either Torrent profile on the Plaguecaster or the Heavy Gunner.  

Matchup Considerations

As befits what Games Workshop considers a starter team, Plague Marines don’t have any match ups that are exceptionally bad, but conversely, they don’t have any that are exceptionally good either. Your skill as a pilot will determine a lot of your success, and your ability to plan ahead will behoove you more than usual here. Plague Marines will require patience to master as a couple small mistakes can cost you more than you gain, and put you on the back foot quickly.  

As always, beware the melta Gunner operatives your opponents can bring to bear, as they can still tear apart a Plague Marine with a good roll. Outside the melta, very few weapons are going to be taking you down in a single shoot, giving you at least an activation to figure out how you want to adjust. Teams with abilities to cleanse your Poison need to be given careful consideration, since you may want to reapply it somehow, straining your limited resources. 

Your Killzone can determine a lot of your success as well. Tomb World and Gallowdark, with their plethora of Accessible doors, can begin to bog you down and leave operatives unduly exposed. Be careful of where you’re staging and consider bringing Barricades for operatives to stage behind if you’re not going to be able to make it to cover moving through these access points. While your equipment selection is moderately flexible, never leave behind Plague Bells or Toxic Rounds. These two selections are simply too powerful for what they give you to take anything else over them. 

Finally, though they have seven operatives to choose from, only six can be taken with the team. Who you drop is going to be a big factor in how you pilot the team. Generally speaking, it's going to be a "Pick 2" choice between your Heavy Gunner, your Fighter, and your Warrior, as the utility other operatives provide is very strong. Fighter is great into teams where the enemy will be running up into your face, like Angels of Death or Goremongers. The Warrior gives you an extra boltgun and better saving throws when fire is returned into teams that might play from a distance, like Tempestus Aquilons or Kasrkin. The Heavy Gunner gives you a very reliable shooting weapon into teams with bad saving throws or that want to clump up. Pick the right tool for the right job, as each of these operatives fill their niche very well.

Piloting the Team to Victory

Like an insidious disease spreading across the land, so too must your Plague Marines proceed across the Killzone. Your Champion and your Fighter should be your point men, followed closely by the Malignant Plaguecaster. You need the Plaguecaster close-ish to the front so you can maximize value from his Poisonous Miasma ability. The more you use this early, the more mortals you can dish out later with it. 

Plan where the Icon Bearer is going as far ahead as during deployment. If you’re getting counteractions and can move another couple inches with him, check (ie, measure) if you can make it into your opponent’s territory safely with him, as he only needs a portion of his base inside to get you Contagion for free. That ploy costing 0CP will free up your resources for other ploys going forward. 

You shouldn’t expend too much effort chasing down enemy operatives, instead relying on the Poison to force your opponent to start coming to you. Once they go on Engage, take advantage of Lumbering Death to have highly reliable Shooting into them. Bear in mind this Strategy Ploy does not work during your counteractions, but it will work during a Guard shot on Close Quarters Killzones. Plan accordingly.   

Final Thoughts

Plague Marines can feel like they start off slow, both literally and figuratively, but once they start winding up, they swiftly begin bringing death. Apply Poison to key enemy operatives early, boosting your damage output from the Plaguecaster and Toxic operatives into them, then reap the benefits of having so much Severe access across your force. These operatives are the vectors of disease and victory, and with a bit of early planning, you’ll find your opponent withering beneath the miasma of rot all too soon.
