[40k] Ben Jurek's Road to LVO 2026, Part 1: A New Road

May 30th, 2026 via Goonhammer

The Road to LVO is a series sponsored by Frontline Gaming.

Welcome back to my Road to LVO series, 2026 edition! I have once again been recruited to bring to you all my long and winding journey to the largest major on this side of the planet. It is currently May, 2026 and as I write this on a plane to Omaha, I have a solid four months to get prepped and ready for this spectacle of an event. Frontline Gaming knocked it out of the park last year with the new venue and I am excited for my hopefully triumphant return.  

What happened last time ?

Not Pictured: One (1) Atrapos. Credit: Ben Jurek

When we last saw our hero (at least I hope I am the hero in this story), I'd had a great performance at LVO. I kicked off the event 6-0 and broke into the top cut, taking down some very talented opponents. A win against Kyle Grundy's T'au in the shadow round playing deep into the knight kept the dream alive, and my Chaos Knights were in a great place and positioned well to take down the top 8, with another win into Matthew Geyer's Adeptus Mechanicus.

Our journey ultimately ended in the top 4, narrowly out of reach of the gold and the opportunity to take down this white whale of an event. I was taken down by a Deathwatch army and fell short of the podium, leaving the event battered and beaten. It's a treasure of a memory however, and I had a great time - I love super majors and being able to attend one close to home affords me the opportunity to host friends in my home and cook for them.

What have I been up to?

I’ve been a busy boy since then. My 40K hobby road has taken me all over the place, including the World Championships of Warhammer the month after LVO. I closed out that event with a respectable 5-3 performance with my Chaos Knights and I finished out the ITC season ranked #9 globally.

Which brings us to the new season. Since the start of the 2026 season I've rotated through a few armies and attended in a number of events. I participated in the Team USA Academy process, which is an intense couple of months filled with practice and scrimmages. While I didn't make the starting roster this year, I always love the incredible competition and experience the process provides. Here's a quick rundown of my recent events and the results:
  • Nov 2025 California Cup - Chaos Knights - 4th place 
  • Jan 2026 Las Vegas Team Tournament - Astra Militarum -1st place 
  • Jan 2026 Warhammer Open Palm Springs - Astra Militarum - 5th place 
  • Jan 2026 Asia Teams Warhammer GT - Necrons - 2nd place 
  • Feb 2026 Scorched Earth Open - Necrons - 1st place
  • March 2026 California Team Championship- Necrons - 5th place
  • May 2026 Rocky Mountain Open - Chaos Space Marines - 5th place
  • May 2026 Warhammer Open Dallas- Chaos Space Marines - 2nd place 
As far as what I’ve been playing, I went from Chaos Knights to Astra Militarum after Grizzled Company dropped. While prepping for Asia teams in Thailand at the end of January, I was slotted into Necrons since we did not have a Necron pilot and I was the most flexible man to do it. If you’ve read my previous articles you will see I am often taking the role of the flexible player to jump on the new broken thing for a team. In this case, we needed a C’tan pilot and I was the man for the job. After a few events with the silver legion I jumped into a new army with hobby goals in mind: I became a Chaos Space Marines pilot and have been terrorizing events with triple defilers, most recently at the Warhammer Open event in Dallas. I made the podium there with my only loss being in the Finals to Liam VSL.

That's a pretty solid path of success behind me, and I'm well positioned right now in the ITC Rankings. Once the Dallas scores are submitted I should wind up firmly in the top 3 for the global rankings and in good company with some great players. That said, it's not first place and while I'm not necessarily saying that I'm pushing to win the ITC this year, it certainly would be nice if it were to happen.

Though honestly I just want to keep Innes Wilson on his toes and force him into a position where he has to come out to LVO and see me and attempt to score more ITC points.

The Road Ahead

As I write this I am on a plane headed to the Bug Eater 5-man teams event in Omaha, Nebraska. I am attending as a coach for Team Sanctum and am in a non-playing role. It’s a solid team and I am excited to flex my coaching skills. That being said I do not have any more plans to play additional games of 10th edition. My remaining journey to LVO will take me to many of the events I attended last year, but also some new ones.

First on the itinerary is the Heroes Cup in Scottsdale Arizona, where I will be playing on a 5-man team with John Lennon, Tyler Bortel, Floof Cox, and John Garza. This will be one of the two Team USA teams in attendance for the event and we have our eyes set on the gold. This will likely be my first real crack at 11th edition and without all the rules out I am not even entirely sure what I’ll be playing at this point. I am hoping to stick to playing my Chaos Space Marines, as I have done something I’ve never done before with them: Being proud of my hobby progress with the army and being excited to do more. I really made a push for the hobby side of things at Dallas and got a lot of great feedback. I easily eclipsed my practice play time by 10-15x with the amount of hours I put into getting this army painted. Heroes Cup will be an exciting test bed for... well, everything! We have to learn how to tackle not only the new game but new pairings and how the teams format is bound to change in the new edition as well.

After the heroes cup I will be headed back to Tacoma where I snagged a podium placing at last year's event. This event is likely to be huge in many ways, from attendance to understanding the meta for the edition. It’s also an event I'll get to hang out with the other Tabletop battles folks at and we always have a grand time of it. I think by this event I will be solidly locked in to what I’ll be playing before the next balance update or set of codexes. I'll be making a serious push to try and make the Army Showcase and really up my hobby game, so I am hoping I will have the time available to do that but also do well at the gameplay side of things like we did in Dallas. We will get to fun experience of finding out together. 

This one's up in the air, but I am also likely attending the Lone Star Open in Dallas, Texas. This is a major I missed the last two years after taking home the gold in 2023. Making a return here would be hype and I am hoping I can make it happen. I’ve always enjoyed the venue and the event so it will feel good to be back at one of my favorite frontline gaming events.

Then we head overseas to Mechlen, Belgium for the World Team Championships. I am once again teaming up with Wargames Live and will be on the stream team bringing you live updates and info as the games get played. The WTC is really the 40K equivalent to the World Cup or the Olympics and being there for it is always so damn hype. USA is sending one banger of a team and I am confident we can take home the gold with these boys. This team is currently undefeated in International competition at both Pyra Cup and Home Nations and I believe can carry on that momementum. As for me,  I don’t intend on playing any games at the event but I’ve been wrong before.

Once I am back stateside I will make my return to the Smite Club open in Phoenix, AZ. This is probably my longest tenured event in the list and its ran by the team I play with locally when I am sporting the art of war or USA jersey. It’s a major usually in the 100 person range and I get to often pair into some long time friends. By this time we might be in the latest version of 11th with balance updates and/or codexes. So brand new territory to explore here. 

Then my last event before the big show happens to be the biggest 8-man teams event on the planet, the challengers cup is Salt Lake City, Utah, I will be competing with 7 other Art of War Allumni to try and take down an event we failed to get gold at last year. We took a narrow loss to the Australian National team in a very intense round and here’s hoping for a rematch. I feel confident in the team and love the 8-man teams experience. 

Then we are back at LVO. I always try to set my goal to simply do better and the only way to do that this time around is make the podium. So that’s exactly what we are going to try and do. It’s a whole lot of pressure but sure does make for a pretty cool narrative that I might just win the whole dang thing and cap off a multi-year journey.

So we know our goal and we know what we have to do. It’s time to hone our skills to put us in position to get there. 

A whole new world (edition)

The elephant in the room is obviously the edition change. There is so much to tackle and learn and I am chomping at the bit to get some games in as soon as all the rules become available. The last time I went through an edition change I was able to capitalize very quickly into the new game and new meta. I am hoping to do the same thing this time around. I am hopeful since it is not a fully indexed edition that much of my 10th knowledge will be useful. 

Unlearning 10th rules and the mistakes I am bound to make does give me some anxiety though. I can just invision myself getting absolutely bodied by making some incorrect decisions due to edition bleed. I really do think the new primary missions system is likely to have the biggest impact into just how I will be playing my games going forward. I think once I figure that out the rest is hopefully easy going.

One portion of 11th I am excited about is there are more balance levers available in GW's hands to balance armies and make adjustments when a meta gets too stale or too wild. Whether it be through points, dispositions, or mission changes, having more places to make changes is just better and the game is gonna look familiar but also be quite a bit different. 

Once the Meta has been figured out and I’m out of the mines, I really do wonder what it will look like and just exactly what I will be doing. Will I go back to Knights? Will the call of the Astra Militarum be what I need in my life. Will my hobby project of CSM keep me from army hopping, or will I return to my long lost love of the orks. Well reader, the cool part is you will be able to follow along in the road to LVO series and we can live through the highs, the lows together.

What's Next?

Good question! I am in this weird holding pattern until 11th fully drops. Check out my other content available to Warhound Tier subscribers for tactical advice and my upcoming article on how I will approach 11th edition dispositions. We'll have a ton of it this upcoming week. In the meantime, I'll be back toward the end of the month with another update.
