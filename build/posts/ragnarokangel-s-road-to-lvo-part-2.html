RagnarokAngel's Road to LVO - Part 2

June 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer
This is part of our Road to LVO series, sponsored by Frontline Gaming, where Tabletop Battles contributors and shareholders discuss their journey to the Las Vegas Open later this year, from October 1st to 4th. Get your tickets today!

Welcome back to my Road to LVO. Last time I covered a local RTT that I did experimenting with a hammer list that I liked quite a bit, but didn't love the lack of versatility that it had (Charge forth and hope you kill stuff). There has been some popularity around a different sort of Deathriders centric list for Ossiarch Bonereapers so going to another RTT I decided to give that a shot:

The core unit here is the Kavalos Deathriders. They have an ability, Deathrider Wedge, to charge and instead of fighting, deal D3 mortals and leave. This has cropped up in a few armies this edition but the one that makes the Ossiarch's version so special is it is guaranteed to work (as it isn't a 2+ on a D3) and isn't one per turn army. Thats the big one, since it means you can send multiple units in and then leave. The damage on its own doesn't seem like a lot, but it adds up.

There's a couple of nifty combos you can do with it, too. The Heroic Trait Imperious Will lets a unit do D3 mortals on a 2+ whenever a unit within 12" uses a FIGHT ability, which Deathrider Wedge has the matching keyword. The Liege-Kavalos also has the Lord trick of fighting then handing off combat to another unit. So they can charge, swing, and then order a Deathrider unit to do Deathrider Wedge and both get to leave.

There's also a few other tricks here, the ranged fire is a lot better than other stuff ive run. Crawlers do pretty respectable damage but more importantly, on a 4+ it inflicts strike last. Which pairs great into the Deathrider Wedge spam listed above. The Triaxes are a very good shooting unit, with the Liege-Mortek getting to charge in, swing, then order the Triaxes to shoot again in the combat phase. So even getting close enough to try and stop them won't save you.

The Reapers and Teratic Cohort are mostly objective pieces. They're cheap and can steal objectives from the opponent with little cost, the Teratic Cohort gets a free 3" move in both players hero phases, even into combat, so you can lock them into baby jail.

Off the Wall Games - Spring AOS Tournament

There haven't been a some AOS events I can attend at the moment. I started a new job which eats up my Sundays so that really only leaves the occasional Saturday RTT right now, as I'd have to take time off for Sunday.

But there was an option for an RTT in Springfield, MA on June 6th that I could pop into so I signed up and headed over. Was a nice little store that nonetheless was economic with its space and had a ton of tables to play off of.

Unfortunately I was really bad at taking picture this time so you'll have to trust me, sorry!

My first round opponent was Aidan Tardy who admitted he was pretty new at the game. His list was pretty solid, given how many models I had to wade through and my army does not like having to wade through chaff.

Luckily I got Bonetither Shrieker off early and he just was having zero luck trying to unbind it. It's a -1 to Ward aura, which is pretty much Nurgle's whole deal. While he had the Purple Sun he tended to put it near his own guys, which hurt him as much as it hurt me (Though me a bit more, Nurgle saves are pretty trash anyway so whats another -1).

The fight took a long time, we both had anvil armies it was just very grindy dismantling the army piece by piece. We only got to about round 3 before we timed out and he gave me the win, talking out the rest.

Ossiarch Bonereapers Victory - 40 - 34

Round 2 I faced Nick Robert and I was dreading this matchup more than any other, mostly because I was not sure what the new COS book really played like. Nick was very polite to explain to me what his army could do any how many gotchas there were, but until I see it in practice I am very poor at judging danger until I see it in person.

I gave him first movement, he shuffled around a bit but mostly kept his formation together. I got greedy and pushed my Deathriders up to the Southwest Objective. I noticed he kept his Cavalier Marshall exposed in the middle of the field, within charge range. I tried to charge his Cavalier Marshal with my Mortis Reapers, thinking with their anti-HERO they might have a shot at putting him down early.

...He then proceeded to charge right away from them and go after my Deathriders and the Liege-Kavalos, killing the hero outright. While Cavaliers cannot charge out of combat, 10 horses is a lot of wiggle room to charge to another unit and still remain within 3" of your original unit.

With that I pretty much lost my trump card. We played another round but I conceded, kicking myself for my own stupidity. At least I know what to expect next time and not to go after a Cavalier-Marshal that has been left out in the open. Like I said, he did kindly explain this before hand, I just didn't listen.

Ossiarch Bonereapers Loss 25 - 75

My final round opponent was Matthew Rowbotham. Matthew had a bit of a hodge podge list, kept most of it back and a lot in deep strike. He pushed his Cogfort up to the center objective, making an honestly pretty imposing target since it counts as 20 on an objective. Also until errata hits, the Cogfort can make itself unable to be seen aganist Ranged Fire, so I had to aim my ranged stuff at other targets, mostly his crypt horrors.

As he got up real close in the middle objective, I kept hidden in woods planted conveniently around the field until he got close enough for my Teratic Cohort to just move 3" right into combat on his turn, shutting off his shooting. I then took 2 rounds to charge and retreat to keep pinging it for D3 mortals until it was weak enough to take it down with some of my other hammers. Once that was killed, it opened up my entire army to push up the field.

My Triaxes and Crawlers mostly kept the Crypt Horrors busy long enough to thin them out, and I avoided taking serious damage. He ended up conceding around Turn 3, not having enough stuff left over to win as I was now pushing up to his side of the field.

Ossiarch Bonereapers Victory 78 - 36

I ended up placing third in the event, not bad overall. That'll be my entry fee for the next time they hold an event.

Wake Me Up When Ghyran Ends

So I do like the list. I feel like I have to get more practice using it correctly and how to allocate units in different places, since the damage individually isn't a lot, you have to chip away over time or go for one solid swing with the ranged hammers. Finding that balance based on the durability of a unit is crucial and it's going to take a bit more practice to feel that out.

It's getting difficult to find events around I can be flexible for due to time constraints, but I think the problem is that Ghyran just doesn't have people very happy right now. I'm hoping to get a chance to goto Ironweld next month, a local gaming event in Massachusetts, though I may have to settle for some RTTs I signed up for locally as there is currently a waitlist.

Aqshy is coming up soon which should reinvigorate the game big time, and it'll drop this weekend so we'll see how that goes. Along with the mission changes it'll also bring some points changes and rumored "Scourge of Aqshy" Warscrolls with new tools that might shake up a lot of armies.

No matter how things hash out, I'll check in next month on my Road to LVO.
