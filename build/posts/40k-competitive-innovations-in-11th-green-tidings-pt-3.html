Archetype

Crisis Cadrew

Thoughts

T'au continue to rock their way through the metagame with a Crisis-heavy build, eschewing Riptides in favour of mostly cheap and cheerful Crisis/Commander setups, plus the chunkier (and near mandatory) Starflare Missile unit. Lots of killing power for pretty much any matchup, and the ability to play more cagey in a Purge mirror via Torchstar. Great work from Max.

Thoughts

This list is so violent, good grief. It also has so much stuff - loads of deadly volume firepower from the Infernus and Sternguard units, a great melee anchor from the Bladeguard, and somehow enough points left to buy a Redeemer and two Vindicators. Not sure how I feel about the fact that we might genuinely be in a world where the best Codex Space Marines list isn't Ultramarines, truly wonders never cease. I don't have much more to say about this list, as you might be able to tell - it's just a wildly efficient killing machine with tools for every matchup. Congratulations to Jake.

Thoughts

The Salamander menace tries to make it a double here, facing off against an AdMech army that's brought a mix of old and new. Rad-Zone Corps harkens back to their 10th Index Detachment, while the recent Lords of the Forge hotness turbo-charges Thulia and Cawl into proper monsters. Thulia's presence also helps breath some life into Rad-Zone - her ability to hand out Advance/Charge means you can run Ruststalkers in a Detachment that doesn't get that via Stratagem without hating yourself, and Rad-Zone synerises with that via the auto-Advance option. Elsewhere in the list being able to switch on Lethal Hits for Skystalkers is also good, the high number of threats is solid against so deadly a foe, and Scriptural Prognosis is also very useful here, as it means that only the Sternguard brick can drop Thulia or Cawl out of the volume units, not the Infernus.

The Salamander build needs no real introduction, the only notable difference here being that they've picked Reconnaissance rather than Purge. That feels like it's hurting them in this matchup - Priority Assets will pull ahead of Recon gradually unless Recon pushes a bit, and the Salamander threats are pretty short ranged, which puts a cap on how impactful an alpha strike turn would be. The AdMech list is also pretty solid at counter-attacking, and has quite a deep bench of toys, and I think the bounce potential for the Salamanders is quite high. Thulia/Cawl with Lone Operative active is also a very solid bait piece to perform an Action with, as it will force a serious commitment to try and counter, which the robots can then exploit.

I don't think that makes this a wash, by any means, because the sandblasting builds are incredibly nasty (as above). There's definitely a world where the Salamanders just deck this off the table, deep bench be damned, and two Infernus units gives them a reasonable amount of agency to shut out some of the Skitarii, as they simply cannot afford to get Overwatched. The AdMech found a way despite this, managing to rack up a very commanding victory.

Adeptus Mechanicus (Lords of the Forge/Rad-Zone Corps) Victory - 100 - 84
