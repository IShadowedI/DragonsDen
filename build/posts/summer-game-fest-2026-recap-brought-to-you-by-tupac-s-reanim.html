Summer Game Fest 2026 Recap: Brought to You by Tupac's Reanimated Corpse

June 6th, 2026 via Goonhammer
After the fairly entertaining and exciting State of Play earlier this week, Summer Game Fest's big flagship show that celebrates Geoff Keighly and also occasionally shows off video games arrived with a two hour slog that, frankly, kind of sucked all of the energy out of the room from the State of Play. Perhaps it is the length of the show itself, or just the things that were shown, but this author was left pretty down about things shown off here, barely walking away with five things in total to be excited about or look forward to. If you happened to miss the stream for any reason, though, we've got a recap here to help highlight the big things you might have missed!

Resident Evil Veronica



Publisher: Capcom

Release Date: 2027

Platform(s): Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X, Nintendo Switch 2

Resident Evil's weirdest non-mainline iteration is back, bringing the remakes of previous games up to date with Resident Evil Code Veronica finally making its way to a modern platform and given the RE: Engine treatment. The trailer was pure teaser, but at this point following the successive hits of previous RE Remakes and new iterations, it isn't hard to guess that Veronica will play and look amazing. What really remains to be seen is what Capcom does with Code Veronica's somewhat trainwreck of a story, and if it they make attempts to modernize and update the game in ways that help make it into a better overall remake. I'm excited for this being the case, because I love Code Veronica, even if the game is kind of a mess and the story has some unfortunate elements, but the previous remakes give us some mixed signals: RE4 Remake and RE2 Remake feel like almost totally new games, while RE3 Remake felt very stale in comparison.

Bernhardt: They've already indicated they'll deal with the hanging nonsense about Claire going to look for her brother Chris -- this isn't a spoiler, it's the premise of the above trailer; it even has an RE5 boulder joke in it -- but all us real heads care about is if Steve will carry over correctly. That is to say, like a shithead you're still a little bit protective of.

Cuphead



Publisher: MDHR

Release Date: TBA

Platform(s): Probably Everything

MDHR had two announcements today, namely a new Cuphead game featuring the same hand-drawn animation of the first game, and another game, an 8 bit title called "Mighty Cuphead Adventure", which was promised to be in the near future. Neither announcement had a date, but Mighty Cuphead Adventure had a trailer, and seems to be nailing the 8 bit aesthetic honestly and earnestly, as a Gematsu article revealed that Mighty Cuphead Adventures is being programmed in the Sega Master Systems Assembly Language, and will be actually playable on a Master System.

Yeah. I don't know what's going on anymore either.

Alien Isolation 2



 

Publisher: SEGA

Release Date: TBA

Platform(s): Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X, Nintendo Switch 2

Thanks to a teaser some months ago, Creative Assembly's surprise announcement here didn't really surprise, even if the trailer being entirely in subtitled Japanese for most of it certainly seemed to throw some people off for a few seconds. the scariest part of this trailer was the note that Alien Isolation came out 12 years ago, so just remember that no one can hear you scream in space, if you need to do so at the revelation of how old you have gotten.

Bernhardt: I look forward to playing like 90 minutes of this and watching one of my favorite streamers completing the rest.

gen ATLAS



Publisher: Epic Games

Release Date: TBA

Platform(s): Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X

When Geoff began glazing Fumito Ueda, I will admit that my expectation for his new game previously known as Project Robot would not turn out to be an action shooter game featuring a giant robot for puzzle solving? I know this might sound blasphemous but I never truly enjoyed most of Ueda's games other than Ico, so I'm perhaps not the right person to ask about this title, but this trailer left me particularly cold.

Blood Message



Publisher: NetEase

Release Date: TBA

Platform(s): PC, Others TBA

NetEase showed off a narrative trailer for Blood Message, and the game has some cool trappings that certainly play into the Chinese historical action game. There wasn't a lot else to go off of here, but the trailer did at least build an interesting concept and give some sense of the scope and style the game wanted to evoke, so now it just remains to be seen if the gameplay can match up to that when we get more information.

Stranger Than Heaven



Publisher: RGG

Release Date: January 15, 2027

Platform(s): Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X|S

I have to provide a preamble to this one: I used to be a huge fan of RGG Studios games. My household deeply loved the Yakuza series games, and up until recently RGG was a studio that could announce just about anything and have me opening my wallet. Unfortunately, once Nagoshi left, I had some fears for the future of the studio, and Stranger Than Heaven's weird limbo state certainly played into that. Then, RGG's disastrous handling of Yakuza Kiwami 3 burned any remaining good will I had for the studio, between the removal of and alteration of the game's story and missions (including the pro-trans and anti-racist storylines), but also RGG's support for convicted sexual harasser Teruyuki Kagawa and insisting he remain in the game while other projects had dropped him. So, am I excited for Stranger Than Heaven? No. Do I think it will be worth playing? Who can say. Do I think resurrecting Tupac to include him in the game alongside Snoop Dogg is in bad taste? Absolutely!

Bernhardt: There were some cautious good vibes around this one going into this show; glad they put paid to that to bring it in line with the rest of what they've been up to recently.

Haex



Publisher: Dead Astronauts

Release Date: 2027

Platform(s): PC

HAEX, a name that has a deeply weird mouthfeel to me, was announced as a "1-4 player open world survival shooter", which probably says everything it needs to say in order to make you really excited or really, really not excited. This is apparently the first game from Dead Astronauts studios, made up of Epic and Massive games employees, and the trailer didn't exactly show off a lot to give us much context about whatever the main thrust of the game is other than "shoot things".

Bernhardt: This looks like a big-budget take on Lethal Company and the like, honing in especially on the Three Player Vibe. I might watch some of this but I don't think I have much interest in playing it.

Gundam Rogue Orbit



Publisher: Bandai Namco Entertainment

Release Date: 2027

Platform(s): Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X

One of the more controversial trailers from today's show based off of online chatter, most viewers seemed to be debating whether or not the gameplay shown in Rogue Orbit "fit" the Gundam name and universe. Frankly, I don't really care about that, and I really just wanted more information on what the game was actually about and where, perhaps, it fits in various Gundam universes. My initial assumption is the game is some form of action game that is either like a mech character action game, or is "Gundam Monster Hunter", both of which sound pretty cool to me.

Control Resonant



Bernhardt: Bringing this up here because even though I grabbed this from the PlayStation channel for convenience (Remedy has restricted their own posting of it), this is the Summer Game Fest trailer for this, as opposed to the one that Sony got for their State of Play. And it is some three thousand times better. Just needed to register a mea culpa on that, because they gave Sony the bad one about Dylan, and they gave the Dread Lord Keighley the one that bangs. Here's the song.

Guild Wars 3



Publisher: Arenanet

Release Date: Fall 2027

Platform(s): PC, PS5

Arenanet surprised people with an announcement for Guild Wars 3 today, perhaps the most surprising detail of which was that it will be on both PC and PS5, but not Xbox. The trailer noted that it was alpha footage and showed almost nothing, but the big news that the game even existed seemed to be enough to excite most people.

Virtua Fighter Crossroads



Publisher: RGG

Release Date: 2027

Platform(s): TBA (Likely PC, PS5, Xbox)

Excluding my RGG rant above, this trailer was another that I've seen fairly divided opinions on. When I heard "Virtua Fighter", I expected a new iteration of the fighting game, but instead, we were shown a trailer for a third person action game very much in the line of games RGG already is known for making (down to the inclusion of some form of organized crime). SEGA ran an extended trailer later in the evening that went a bit more into detail about the game, and while I'm still not very on board with it due to my reservations about RGG, I'm slightly curious about how they merge the world of Virtua Fighter, which never really sold on its narrative, and RGG's focus on narrative action games. I've seen equal amounts of people excited for this, though, so we'll have to see later next year.

Bernhardt: This is not the most surprising single-player campaign-forward game of the show (that was Clutch), but it's up there. The stuff they showed looked good; too bad about the studio.

Stellar Blade Blood Rain



Publisher: Shift Up

Release Date: 2027

Platform(s): PC (others TBA)

The sequel to Stellar Blade, Stellar Blade Blood Rain stars "Evie", who notably does not prominently have a bladed weapon and instead relies on a gauntlet like weapon and martial arts for much of the combat shown in this trailer. Stellar Blade was a pretty fun action game with a big emphasis on dodging, parrying, and timing, and so far the trailer here seems to be following in those footsteps. There are a few curious things about this trailer, though: Sony's name is missing (Stellar Blade was initially a Sony exclusive), and there is some current debate about how much of Stellar Blade Blood Rain is using generative AI. The key image released for the game has received some scrutiny for AI usage, and Shift Up themselves have often talked about using AI as a tool in various interviews. Personally I'm disappointed if that turns out to be the case and it is being used to develop the game, because I enjoyed the gameplay in the first game, and would happily have more of it.

Bernhardt: In the immortal words of Chris Person, "gotta age that protag up playboy."

The Wolf Among Us Remastered & 2



Publisher: Telltale Games

Release Date: Remake 2026, TWAU2 2027

Platform(s): TBA (Likely PC, PS5, Xbox)

The surprisingly solid and somewhat cult hit The Wolf Among Us is finally receiving a sequel from the revived Telltale Games. The new game got a spiffy new trailer showing off some new details and hints about what's up with Bigby Wolf and some new characters to round out the new adventure. The trailer also ended with news that the original Wolf Among Us will be getting a remake that will release later this year in 2026. Although I have a lot of reservations about the source material that Wolf Among Us comes from, I really enjoyed the game when it first released, so I'm hopeful the 2nd one will continue with the solid noir detective concept, and I'll certainly check out the remake.

Bernhardt: Glad that moron dipshit Bill Willingham will be getting some more passive income. I dunno. People like these games, but fuck Fables.

Star Wars: Zero Company



Publisher: EA Star Wars

Release Date: August 27, 2026

Platform(s): PC, PS5, Xbox Series X

The most surprising thing in this announcement was the release date, which is a mere 2 months away! Zero Company is basically Star Wars Xcom, and the trailer here did a decent job of showing off the various customization and other concepts of the game and some of the gameplay flow. It also established a bit of the narrative of the game, which is firmly set in the prequel universe, thanks especially to the end of trailer stinger cameo of General Anakin Skywalker.

Bernhardt: Very interested in this, but not the best Star Wars game showed here.

1666 Amsterdam



Publisher: Panache Digital Games

Release Date: TBA

Platform(s): PC

I have to take the piss out of this announcement a bit: Geoff led into this game's trailer by talking up how producer Patrice Desilets spent 16 years working on this project after leaving Assassin's Creed behind, and then the trailer was the most Assassin's Creed looking game I've ever seen. Anyway, the game has you playing as a woman who is looking to hunt down the corrupt and evil in Amsterdam, with a heavy focus on the supernatural and witchcraft. The game does look quite interesting, and there is a demo you can play if you want to.

Bernhardt: Patrice spent the end of his piece pushing you to go play the Steam demo, and I've done so, and I recommend you do. It's wild. So like. The end state you get to, that explains the game, is that you're gonna be controlling two characters: The Dutch witch Noa and her cat familiar, Aaron. Noa does character action stuff; Aaron does stealth and exploration. Why's the cat named Aaron? Well. In 2026, a young woman learns that in 1999, her father got hot and heavy with a new girlfriend and she did a blood witch sex ritual on him that isekai'd him back to 1665, where he became a cat, and then assumed the place of the familiar of the most powerful Dutch witch in existence. Consequences. They were absolutely correct to go, "We've put out a demo because you need to walk with us a little bit."

Marcy: Okay so I went to play the demo while Bernhardt was editing this at his insistence and holy shit this game is off the rails. I'm fully on board. Game of the show for me now. Go play the demo. I am sorry I joked that your game looked too much like Assassin's Creed, Patrice.

Star Wars Galactic Racer



Publisher: Fuse, Secret Mode

Release Date: October 6, 2026

Platform(s): PC, PS5, Xbox Series X

Pod Racing is back, baby! We're getting closer to the game's release in October, and this trailer focused on the narrative trappings that will be accompanying the game and giving you some reason to play through it as opposed to existing solely on the merits of multiplayer pod racing (which really is probably enough anyway). Hopefully we'll start learning more gameplay details as we get closer to the  game's release, including how many players it includes and what types of racing can be done, but it sure was... something to see Sebulba again! Two prequel cameos in one day, wow!

Bernhardt: This is my game of the show. It looks like Xtreme G with the "car" physics of Burnout. I would be there day one on this even without the Star Wars license.

Saw Genesis



Publisher: Bloober Team

Release Date: TBA

Platform(s): PC

In the Mandatory Geoff Talks to Puppet Segment, Billy from Saw wheeled his way onto the stage to introduce a trailer voiced by Tobin Bell, Jigsaw himself, that actually showed almost nothing about what the game is or what would be going on, and then dropped a website for alpha sign up and the note that this is a 3v1 asymmetrical horror game. The trailer was gory and kind of alluring in the way Saw can be, and the note that this is a 3v1 style game is really curious, so hopefully the alpha will reveal more about what players will be doing in the Saw universe soon.

Bernhardt: Glad Bloober Team is doing dumb licensed crap I don't care about instead of dumb licensed crap I do.

Swords of Legends



Publisher: Aurogon Shanghai

Release Date: TBA

Platform(s): PC, PS5, Xbox

I appreciate games that show me gameplay, and Swords of Legends came out with a strong debut teaser showing the protagonist up against a Chinese Theater styled villain in a supernatural action-adventure game, immediately getting me on board. There isn't a ton of information about this game yet, but what was shown in the trailer looked really intriguing and I love the setting and trappings, so I'll be following this one. The publisher, Aurogon Shanghai, hasn't published a game in English either, so this seems to be their first, and frankly it seems like a strong first title if the gameplay matches the cool factor of the trailer.

Final Fantasy 7 Revelation



Publisher: Square Enix

Release Date: Spring 2027

Platform(s): PC, PS5, Xbox Series X, Nintendo Switch 2

The final trailer of the day was the reveal of Final Fantasy Remake Part 3, Revelation, which will be coming in Spring of 2027. The wrap-up to the remake of FF7 showed off some of the usual crew and Sephiroth, and plans to finish off the remake's version of the FF7 storyline. What types of changes and updates will be made to the story as it comes to a close remains to be seen, but the Square team noted that players will see Wutai and Rocket Town in this game after it was expected in the second, as well as noting that this is, indeed, the finale of the trilogy, so there won't be much waiting left as the new series comes to a close.

Bernhardt: This trailer spent a lot of time going, "Look. We hear you. You only care about this version of Cloud when he's up at the Honey Bee Inn. You can play as everyone else on the roster instead of him," and they're correct and I respect that; and I do appreciate that for Highwind fast travel, they took their inspiration from the PUBG deployment mechanic. It's the third game in a trilogy that's had some fall off, so it just being here on all platforms makes a lot of sense.

Wrap Up

There were some smaller announcements too that weren't exactly worth as much space as some of the other projects. Control Resonant showed off more gameplay and gave some extra details about what players can expect, while Paramount+ re-announced the TMNT Last Ronin game with a teaser trailer from Platinum Games. Ubisoft also showed off a trailer for a remaster of Assassin's Creed: Black Flag, while Capcom debuted Monster Hunter Wilds revealed a new expansion, Ascendance, teasing Elder Dragons and some other returning monsters to hunt. Crossfire was announced, based on the Korean multiplayer game of the same name, although this iteration is a single player, narrative focused game. Attack on Titan 3  was announced, if you care, while two racing games were shown off: Maverick Games revealed an open world racing game called Clutch, while Milestone games revealed Hot Wheels: Infinite Rush.



Street Fighter 6 revealed their Year 4 roster, with new characters like Arjun, Bosch, and Yasmine, and guest collaboration character Tifa Lockheart from Final Fantasy 7, a deeply funny announcement following the Tekken 8 collaboration announcement with Yujiro Hanma last week; what a time to be alive and into fighting games. Except Virtua Fighter, because that's not a fighting game anymore, apparently. Speaking of crossovers, Sonic Racers announced the most hilarious crossovers ever: Godzilla and Neon Genesis Evangelion.

That about does it for Summer Game Fest, though! There are some smaller showcases this weekend, and depending on what is shown off there, we may be back with a recap of some of the notable titles from The Wholesome Direct or PC Gaming Show, otherwise, we hope you've enjoyed these recaps and perhaps saved you having to have watched the 2 hour spectacle of Summer Game Fest. Until next time!
