[40K] Weekend Update: October 19, 2026

October 19th, 2026 via Goonhammer

In our Weekend update series, we cover the big events and stories that are happening each week in wargames, so you can stay updated and informed every week. The 40k side will be mostly covered by me, Isaac Terada, while Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson covers the non-40k side of event coverage and news. 

Mid-size Singles Events Dominate the Weekend as Paris Wargames Expo Finishes Today

After a slow weekend last week, singles events for Warhammer 40k had a return to form this week with six events boasting more than 70 players. The biggest event of the weekend was the Paris Wargame Expo 2, a 40k singles event with 212 players, marking it as a proper major. As of this writing the event is still ongoing, and will conclude today. It’s currently difficult to tell which players are in the event’s top cut (8 players), as the results do not appear to be displaying correctly in Best coats Pairings It appears that the event’s top 8 includes a surprising number of Necrons players, along with Leagues of Votann, Dark Angels, Astra Militarum, and Death Guard.

Damien Cooper Wins the Alba GT with Orks

Moving on to events which actually completed, Damien Cooper won the 2026 Alba GT in Székesfehérvár, Hungary - the weekend’s second largest event, with 99 players. Damien scored a perfect 500 battle points over the weekend running an Orks list running Reconnaissance with Brute Bosses, Bully Boyz, and Da Big Hunt. There were four other undefeated players at the five round event. Damien has been running Orks since May after switch from Death Guard and placed third with Orks at the Leoben Autumn Spring event in late September. He’s currently 186th in the ITC with this event.

Ben Greenlay Takes Gold with Grey Knights at Bloodshed Bay

Ben Greenlay won the weekend’s third largest event - Bloodshed Bay Two, a 77-player singles event held in Ontario over the weekend, defeating Marco Zecconi’s Orks in the final round en route to a 589/600 score for the event. Ben’s Warpbane Task Force list, which he ran with Purge the Foe as his Disposition, featured two Grand Master Nemesis Dreadknights, a single unit of five Terminators with a Grandmaster, a pair of Razorbacks, and an Eversor Assassin. Ben’s win at the event moves him to 101st in the ITC global rankings.

Matt Bell Wins the That 6++ Grand Tournament: South

Matt Bell took home gold at the 74-player That 6++ GT over the weekend, winning the weekend’s fourth largest event with his Necrons (Skyshroud Spearhead + Cursed Legion).

Another Killer Teams Weekend

It was a big weekend for teams events as two countries ran their national team championship events. The largest of these was the Italian teams national championship, a six-man teams event held in Italy, followed by the five-man Scottish Open Championship (won by Stat Check), and the 8-man Tennessee Elite Open (won by the Warp Dust Crusaders).

Altrimenti ci Arrabbiamo Win the Italian Team National Championships

Altrimenti ci Arrabbiamo (which loosely translates to something like “Alternatively, we’ll get angry”) won the largest teams event of the weekend - the 2026 Italian Team National Championship, featuring 264 players competing over five rounds with teams of six. Congratulations to Andrea Manfroni, Francesco Di Bonaventura, Ludovico Mazza, Nicolo Gili, Pietro Riccio, and Simone Caroselli for winning the event with a 5-0 record.

Stat Check Wins the Scottish Open Team Championship

Stat Check took an absolutely loaded team to the 140-player event this year, and Innes Wilson, Liam Vsl, Nathan RobertsGustavo José Branco Varandas de Sousa, Maciej ‘Pumba’ Guziec, and Nassim Fouchane took gold at the event with a 100-0 win in the final round over Black Crow Gaming. Ironically the team’s toughest matchup was in round 4 against team “Stat Check Uncs,” featuring a number of their teammates.

Deathwatch Terminators by Craig "MasterSlowPoke" Sniffen

OH GOD DEATHWATCH OH MY GOD

Codex Marines is officially here, and some of its biggest problems have become clear. Deathwatch-flavored Marines (armies with Deathwatch Detachments) put up a staggering 69% true win rate this weekend, capturing 8% of all TiWP (Tournament in Winning Position) spots. Comparatively, the next highest results were for the Adeptus Mechanicus, whose true win % was only 55%.

It’s clear that the Deathwatch are a problem in the current meta; Marine armies without Deathwatch support posted only a 42% win rate over the weekend, though this is in part due to the game’s best players opting to run Deathwatch as the obvious best choice from a Codex packed with solid options. Among non-Deathwatch Marine armies, Space Wolves appear to be the best flavor, as the speedy, melee-heavy army loves the Assault Brethren and Terminator Storm Force Detachments.

Beyond the overall dominance of Deathwatch, we saw a surprisingly good spread of even winners this weekend, with standout showings from Tyranids, Necrons, and Grey Knights, each of which had two event wins. They were joined by the Adeptus Mechanicus and World Eaters, the latter of which boasted the weekend’s best Event Results as the changes to Berzerker Warband opened things up for the faction.

The Death Guard also saw a large jump in the results this weekend with four players achieving TiWP results, primarily on the back of Shamblerot + Paragons lists. Keep an eye on them and World Eaters next week - there may be a new build coming for them from Innes or Folger.

The Grand Narrative Announces Its Lords of War 

Games Workshop announced four of the Lords of War for their annual Grand Narrative Event this weekend, showcasing what appears to be two of the Lords of War for Chaos and two for the Imperium faction. This year’s event looks to take place in the Maelstrom, where players will be fighting over the planet of Ferrum’s Wake, a world currently under the iron-fisted rule of Kaelen Voss, a former Red Corsair who preaches a kind of radical anarchy. He’s currently being hunted by both the Imperium and Huron’s own forces.

Player Focus: Folger Pyles

Folger is one of the players that everyone knows. In comparison to some of the other top threats he is much newer to the game but he has left an impact. Winning the ITC last year and grabbing first at Warmasters and WTC this year he is one of the most acclaimed players of the modern 40k era. He hasn’t pushed quite as hard at ITC this year, only currently ranking in at 26th, probably pushing him out of this year's winning position, but with a good enough WCW run he may have a shot. He is pushing up his rankings in other places however with a 6th on Stat Check elo’s ranking, where he has been holding in to the top for a long time.

Folger is not quite a faction expert but not quite a faction hopper. He tends to stick with a faction for a pretty long time even when afterward he moves to a different one. He became basically the faction spokesman for Adeptus Custodes for a year, but before then he was an Aeldari specialist, and now just this weekend he is taking a crack with Death Guard. Maybe we can convince him to bring Agents to WCW if we ask hard enough.

Alongside our coverage of the what's-what of 40k each week, Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson joins us to provide some quick updates on the movers and shakers in the competitive scene of some of the other games we cover here at Tabletop Battles!

Tomb Kings on the Horizon for Old World

This week’s Sunday preview takes us to the shifting sands of the Old World, as the Sunday preview over on Warhammer Community revealed that the Tomb Kings are next on the release docket. This second wave of Tomb Kings includes new Skeleton Warrior, Skeleton Archer, and Skeleton Chariot kits, first available in the Host of Nehekhara Army Reinforcement Set. Alongside this release will see a new Arcane Journal for Tomb Kings, The River of Bones, which expands upon both the Tomb Kings and Wood Elves factions, including Armies of Infamy for both factions.

Oh, also a new Red Gobbo is going on preorder this week, and he has a little sled new, which is cute and fun. Glad the Red Gobbo is just out here having a grand ol’ time.

Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Two European Events Dominate Age of Sigmar Headlines

While there were multiple reasonably large events this weekend for Age of Sigmar, such as the Through the Breach invitational in Canada and the Sparkle Party Death Match in Seattle, the European scene made the biggest splash in the Sigmar world this weekend with both the Paris Expo in France and Trials GX in Italy.

The Paris Expo event was a singles event that consisted of 83 players, with five rounds played between Saturday and Sunday. Two players emerged with undefeated records, with battle points as the tie breaker. In second place we have Tristan Darde with Sylvaneth, piloting a list consisting of Allarielle, Drycha, Belthanos, and a Spirit of Druthu with some Dryads and a Warsong Revenant to support. In first place undefeated we go from rampant life to rampant undeath with Nicolas Eisenberg’s Soulblight Gravelords. Nicolas’s Soulblight list was just as big on the big monsters as Tristan’s was, bringing Prince Vhordrai, Lauka Vai, Radukar the Beast, and a Vengorian Lord, supported by three units of the Warcry monster hunter vampires, the Askurgan Trueblades. Congratulations to both Tristan and Nicolas for their outstanding performances here!

Even larger than the Paris Expo though was the Trials GX 2026 event held in Italy, a massive teams event consisting of 193 players. The teams format has been on a hot streak in Age of Sigmar over the past few weeks, and this event certainly takes the cake as far as the sheer scope of players involved. The event consisted of teams of six players competing over five rounds, with only one team going undefeated over the course of the weekend. That team, Flamingo Weapon, consisted of players piloting Soulblight, Nighthaunt, Hedonism, Ogors, Daughters of Khaine, and Sylvaneth. Another huge congratulations to Flamingo Weapon’s team members for the huge showing at such a massive event!

SPIEL Essen Kicks off this Week

One of the largest tabletop gaming expos in the world, Germany’s SPIEL Essen event, kicks off this week and promises to bring not only a huge wave of reveals and games from across the gaming space, but the convention is the current home of Europe’s Golden Daemon challenge.

The convention attracts over 200,000 attendees internationally across over 1,000 vendors, and will run from October 22nd to October 25th. The weekend consists of vendor booths, organized and casual play, panels, and hobby classes for attendees to partake in. Golden Daemon, which moved its home to SPIEL last year, is one of the biggest draws at the event, with hobbyists competing across a multitude of categories to create stunning miniatures of the highest level for a chance to win the coveted Slayer Sword award.

We will be sure to update here next week with some of the big reveals from this marquee event, as well as the winners from Golden Daemon.

Fliers Are Swooping in to Star Wars: Legion

One of Star Wars’s most iconic bits are the starfighters, with ships like the X-Wing and TIE Fighter speeding and soaring through the imagination of folks for fifty years. Now, Star Wars fans can bring these famous craft to tabletops near them, as Fliers were announced late last week for Star Wars: Legion.

This first wave of craft, set to launch in February of next year, will allow players to use these airborne predators as support for their ground forces during games of Legion, and currently one ship is planned for each of Legion’s four factions. These ships include X-wings, TIE fighters, Vulture Droids, V-Wings, and Fang Fighters, with additional fighters possible in the future.

No rules have been revealed yet, but the AMG team was eager to talk about bringing these flyers into the game in the announcement video, giving us a taste of what we can potentially expect when they release in full next year. Until then, we will have to wait patiently for more news and keep a sharp eye for any other teasers AMG might offer.
