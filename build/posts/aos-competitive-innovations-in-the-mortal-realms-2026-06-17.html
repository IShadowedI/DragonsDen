[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-06-17

June 17th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Welcome back everyone. After AOS Worlds and going into 40k's 11th edition, I do expect things to slow down a bit as people check out the new hotness. We have 3 events of smaller size this time. This week we'll be covering:

  • GT solo AoS Mootland Clan - France
  • III PUÑO DE SIGMAR - Spain
  • Sigmar Summerfest '26 GT - United States

Thoughts

While the favored is definitely Daughters of Khaine, this Stormcast list actually has some pretty good tools for dealing with it. The Longstrikes are exactly the kind of kit you want to bring against Morathi and the Shadow Queen, to avoid having to confront her directly. While Annihilators, being 3 wounds a piece are a dicey proposition into Blood Sisters, it's still reasonable and you thin their numbers with the drop in from the start.

That all said, it's a very tight game, but the Daughters of Khaine walk away with the victory.

Archetype

Morathi and Krethusa

Thoughts

We pretty much see some variant of this list every week, so you probably got the jist now if you're a regular reader. Morathi is the Wizard Supreme of the army, while Shadow Queen is a terrifying beat stick that inflicts some pretty gnarly debuffs if you try and fight her in melee, so without a solid ranged option to deal with her first you'll probably struggle with killing her. Backing her up is the Blood Sisters, gnarly foot soldiers who get to inflict strike last on enemy units with high wounds, and smack them down with Crit Mortals.

Krethusa is an option piece but as both a Priest and privy to her own excellent Warscroll Prayer that gives a free move action or shuts off criticals on enemies. Everything else more or less acts to support the army by being very mobile and cheap objective grabbers.

Thoughts

Ah, tree vs tree. Basically, Jota's list favors a more elite approach to fighting. Kurnoths are probably the best melee unit in the book, but are expensive so you have to time their use properly because if they get jumped at an inopportune time, without proper support, they will roll over quite quickly and that's almost a quarter of your army.

Meanwhile Andres's Twistwields are the opposite approach. They're super cheap so you can spam them, they punch above their weight class if they charge first, and as a perk they can reduce enemy movement to just 6", even if they run. Allarielle and Drycha are for punching stuff they can't handle, and Alarielle can even return for another round.

It's a close fight, but Jota narrowly wins by 3 whole points.

Archetype

Belthanos and Drycha with Kurnoths

Thoughts

Belthanos and Drycha are both very strong hammers with tech-y teleport abilities. Drycha in the Charge phase, and Belthanos at End of Turn. Backing them up are the Kurnoths, the greatswords are better on the charge while the Scythes are better being charged, but really they're good enough to switch hit basically everything as long as they're near Overgrowth and have proper support.

Dual Warsong Revenants give access to two priests (which is a very good prayer lore overall) and 2 mobile bubbles of a 6+ ward, which pays off well in a list with so many high wound units.

The List

Archetype

Cavaliers and Fusiliers

Thoughts

I'll say it again, man this list sucks to deal with. The general thrust of the list is the Cavaliers, which will try and bait you into charging them. If they are in combat, they actually get a chance to charge back out of combat with fairly good reliability. Since the Cities of Sigmar have an ability to change the lowest die on a charge roll to match the highest one, they can pretty easily get 8, 10 or 12s, so don't leave them an opening to move or any nearby targets to charge.

Even if you successful do do that, they have options. Steelhelms can redirect your attacks to force you to Fight them, protecting the Cavaliers from harder hitting attacks. Even once they're gone, there are so many debuffs to reduce your combat efficiency while they bide their time and wait for Tahlia to show up and assist.

Even if you try and stay away from their charge range, they have decent ranged fire supporting them, and then if you try and screen them out with chaff, they'll just charge around it and into the thing that matters!

Thoughts

Glutos obviously heads this list, granting stacking buffs over the course of the game. This is in addition to be a solid fighting piece on his own and a lot of wounds to chew through. The Blissbarb Archers are ok on their own, but become pretty frightening with Euphoric giving Run and Shoot, Crit (2 Hits) and then another -1 to saves thanks to the Shardspeaker.

Once the Archers successfully soften them, the Slickblade Seekers and Twinsouls make their way up the field to clean up. Slickblades are pretty decent Cavalry at 4 wounds a piece, while Twinsouls are great Objective holders.
