[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: LVO 2026

October 8th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Sorry for running a tad late this week folks. I was at the Las Vegas Open myself this weekend so it was a bit of a long trip getting back and I am exhausted.

So this week, as expected, we will cover the Las Vegas Open, one of the biggest events of the year. We also have the similarly sized Sydney Grand Tournament down in Australia, and right next door is the Auckland AOS GT in New Zealand.

This Week's Events

  • LVO 2026 - Age of Sigmar Championships - United States
  • Sydney Grand Tournament 2026 - Australia
  • No Ones Home! Auckland AoS GT2 - New Zealand

Thoughts

Ogor Mawtribes were probably expected to show up in the finals, if not take the whole thing. It's a very strong army with a simple play pattern and even with Redd's uptick in cost, he still makes the army extremely difficult to move and take objectives back from.

Meanwhile, it is interesting to see the Skaven pop up here. After a pretty dismal win rate going into the event, seeing Skaven make it to the finals against one of the best performing armies is no small feat. A pretty novel list, using a shielded Vizzik to force fire away from the Nightrunners which do the bulk of the damage with multiple crit mortals, a pretty clever trick.

Unfortunately though this does go for the most predictable contestant, the Ogor Mawtribes win the event. Congrats to Christopher!

Archetype

Redd The Maw and Gorgers

Thoughts

With the 6+ Ward granted by Redd the Maw, Gluttons were the best point per wound value you could get in the game. Since both Redd and Gluttons took a points hike in the Battlescroll right before this event (alongside the new Hunters unit), that wasn't quite as true. Make no mistake, they're still very good but since all the units people were using got a hike, that adds up.

So instead, Christopher switched to Gorgers. Far worse save (6+ instead of 4+ when charged) but more wounds, and Redd gives them a 5+ Ward, so still incredibly dangerous to deal with. The Huskard gives another priest to work with, and a control piece to reduce opponent's fury if necessary.

Archetype

Morathi and Blood Sisters

Thoughts

Points keep going up but the lists really don't change. Morathi is still one of the strongest hammers in the game right now and heavily favors the Scourge of Aqshy mechanic to get some free swings with her tail at the end of turn. Blood Sisters are an incredibly strong hammer unit, especially into Monsters or just higher wound units, granting them Strike Last and then bopping them with Crit Mortal swings.

The Witch Aelves with Tenebrous Aura is a cute trick, creating a unit that can pin something dangerous in place long enough for Morathi to set up the kill

Thoughts

This is a fascinating matchup. Herohammer Lumineth continues to do quite well for itself (and including Gotrek for the Lord of the Rings reference is a cute touch) while Helsmiths have been having a very middle of the road run that was only marginally helped in the last updated.

Both armies have access to some solid shooting, but of course the Helsmiths have a lot more. It's going to come down to the ability for Helsmiths to lay down serious fire early on to thin out the Lumineth numbers before they can bridge the gap and get close.

It's an extremely narrow game, a real nailbiter at only one point difference!

Archetype

Taar's Grand Forgehost

Thoughts

This remains the premier way to play the army, at least for now. We have to see how the changes to the Battlescroll might affect future builds, but this is a solid build nonetheless.

The Spears sit out on objectives, essentially breaking the rules of the game (They can harvest Demonic Points despite that not being a mechanic for the core battle traits, then have Taar reassign them). Once handed off to the Dominator Enginers they can charge and swing real hard, with a real hardy 2+ Save that makes them very difficult to bring down without a lot of Mortal Wounds.

Sitting in the back are the artillery, offering fire from afar, alongside Urak Taar's casting.

  • 4th - Dave Ward - Lumineth Realm-Lords: See Showdown
  • 5th - Harry West - Maggotkin of Nurgle: Glottkin and Sorcerers with Rotswords and a Daemon Prince RoR.
