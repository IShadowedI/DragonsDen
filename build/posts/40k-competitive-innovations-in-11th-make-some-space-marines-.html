November 20th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Remember the sunny optimism of Wednesday, when it looked like other factions were pushing back against the Marine menace? Yeah not so much today. Such is life.

Archetype

Darkwatch

Thoughts

First up today we have Brad Chester unleashing another spin on the Dark Angels theme. His variant uses the powerful Speeders and Outrider units you can set up, then adds in Deathwatch Terminators for good measure. They're not quite as crunchy as Deathwing Knights, but they're cheaper, and the pile on the firepower to support the Speeders. It's also not like foes are going to reach them quickly as they're going to be frantically trying to deal with all the Outrider nonsense that's slamming into their lines. Yet another very scary way to run Dark Angels, congratulations Brad.

Archetype

Phobultrawatch

Thoughts

It's Ultrawatch Jim, but not as we know it.

We've already seen a few people trying out triple Eliminators with Guilliman/Shadow Force, and here Joseph has gone all-in on the double plus version of that plan, using a couple of Spectrus Kill Teams with Leaders to cram the absolute maximum quantity of evasive firepower into the list. The result is an army that can brutally grind down any foe that can't press forward with Advance/Charge on lots of fronts, and has some aces in the hole even when it does get tagged.

The Spectrus teams can also zap around the board as required, and provide a deep pool of Precision (especially with the Detachment rule added) that can pick apart enemy plans. This list really does feel like the absolute worst nightmare for some armies, while needing to play extremely cautiously into others, but it's super cool and completely different to anything else I've seen, so big congratulations to Joseph on the undefeated second place.

The damage on big Berzerker units (Kharn's especially) is monstrous, so that could work in some circumstances, but there being Crusade Ancient's in two of the big bricks is killer to that - always on Fight on Death makes the cost of clearing a squad out very high, and also minimises the overrun potential of a big multi-charge by the Khornate forces. Especially because the Templars also have Eradipulsors as speedy ways to get angles on Rhinos or Kharn, the World Eaters are going to have to start a fight on the Templars terms at some point, and while things could just about go their way when that happens, the odds are firmly against them. Give it a good old college try for the skull throne, but expect blood-soaked defeat, which is what happened here.

Thoughts

This whips, three big Crusader units, two shooting threats, some utility, done. As highlighted in the Showdown, the Crusade Ancients are a really sharp choice here - this list by definition has lots of eggs in a few baskets, so moderating how bad it is when enemy melee threats rock through one of them is genuinely important.
