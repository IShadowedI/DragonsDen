November 21st, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Thoughts

A skewed dispo match up here that pushes the agro requirements of Purge the Foe into the defensive passive scoring of Priority Assets. It's the Drukhari who need to go and kill and dispossess here, with the Tyranids seeking to hold the line and push buttons. Armies wise this game can often be drawish - Dark Lances and anti-infantry blending gets the Dark Kin through Nid units reasonably quickly, but they dislike the battle-shocking and die to any connects the bugs manage to make in return.

I think the disposition is what settles is here- Spite is just not a natural Purge army unless the match up is very suitable, and the Tyranids will have a good stodgy midboard presence that should make wracking up a score easy. The Drukhari will have to push  early- bounce, and they lose big. And so it went.
