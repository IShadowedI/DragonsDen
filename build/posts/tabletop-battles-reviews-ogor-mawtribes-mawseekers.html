Tabletop Battles Reviews: Ogor Mawtribes Mawseekers

August 15th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Howdy hungry Goons! The Ogor Mawtribes have been starving for an update for years, now they swim in a delicious soup of new releases. Games Workshop were generous with a bounty of review models for us to chow down on! Read on to see what our team think of the delectable delicacies of the new Mawseekers sub-faction.

For the other Ogor meat on the menu, go here.

With this range refresh and new fluff for the Ogor Mawtribes a new sub-faction has emerged; The Mawseekers. The Butcher shamans and their howling Gorger packs have been bolstered by raving, fanatical Cleavers and grim, mystical Gutseers. Last but far from least at the feast the High Slaughtermaster, Redd the Maw has replaced the Slaughtermaster of old. 

The Ogor Mawtribes have finally had a fluff update and some story progression; this is long awaited and is driven by the new model line. The Mawseekers are a core part of this, a fanatical cult of GurgMagurg, the Gulping God. Expanded and refined, the Butchers are no longer simple shamans of the Gutbusters, they lead, "The Mawseekers, crazed fanatics in thrall to their race’s Gastro-Shamans and addicted to the spray of blood at Close quarters.” Brutal fare indeed...

What's New

  • Redd the Maw (Unique Hero, 160mm base)
  • Butcher (Hero, 50mm base)
  • 3 Cleavers/Gutseers (Multi-purpose kit, 40mm bases)
Mawseekers
Credit: Warhammer Community

Redd the Maw

Badusernametag: Redd the Maw is a gross and bombastic prophet of the Gulping God. Returned from a long wandering pilgrimage in Shyish, he preaches the purity of raw meat and the rise of the Ogor race. This model replaces the generic Slaughtermaster with a unique hero. This is kinda ironic, as the Slaughtermaster model was originally in fact a unique hero (Special Character) in the world that was. We spin in circles to the tune of the hungry realms…

Redd the Maw
Credit: Warhammer Community

Redd is one of two centrepiece models the banquet of Mawtribes have been graced with in this range refresh release, the other being Overtyrant Morga the Mighty. Whilst Morga is a massive smash monster hero, Redd follows the new tradition of slow Warmachine/Infantry centrepieces, featuring a gaggle of followers around them. Redd rides a massive chariot that bears his cauldron into battle, pulled by a slavering Gorger and flanked by two fanatical Gutseers. 

Redd is one of the biggest and meanest of Ogors. His cauldron, though reminiscent of the old Mawpot, is decorated with the new metal ‘coiled entrails’ design that features in the Mawseekers range on Gutseers Gutplates and the like. It’s a really nice visual motif. You also have the ‘toothed maw’ icon on the top of his ladle and at the front of the pot. This range has coined new iconography for the faction, as well as a solid and clearly recognisable design aesthetic. It’s a strong visual language and we’re eating it up!

I have mixed feelings about this kit; it's amazing to see new and developed sculpts for the Ogor Mawtribes and I love the new design direction, visual cues, and motifs. On the other hand, this model feels a little lazy in composition, and lacks the depth and interesting composition of other large centrepiece ensembles in the Age of Sigmar range.

More of that later; let's get it built!

Redd the Maw- Sprues

Redd the Maw- Sprues

This is quite a straightforward build: You put together the chariot/war-cauldron, add Red to the top, then add in the followers. The chariot is a little flimsy during assembly, however, and I would suggest a slightly different order of operations to build it.

  • Before starting the chassis as suggested, turn the page and assemble the cauldron.
  • Then go back and do the basic chassis.
  • Once you’ve got the first bit together (pic below) add the cauldron in, which locks it all together.
  • Then flip and do the wheels and hanging corpses.
  • Get the wheels on and LET THEM DRY.
  • Then come back and carry on.
Redd the Maw- basic chassis build
Credit: @badusernametag

Redd the Maw- Add the pot, to LOCK it all together.
Credit: @badusernametag

This assembly order allows the cauldron to lock the structure together and get it straight before you fiddle about with the details. Also, leaving the wheels to dry fully before moving on gives them the best strength and chance of being straight; too much movement whilst wet could bend them easily, as the connections are a little flimsy.

Redd the Maw- Upside down for wheels and corpse meat. Let it DRY
Credit: @badusernametag

Shout out to the amazing hanging corpses (cow-ish thing and human) the hang down behind the chariot. Very cool.

Best damn bits.
Credit: @badusernametag

Also a shout out to this absolute beast of a Gutseer weirdo, just casually offering forth a steaming handful of glistening entrails and looking out with the most menacing ever mien from under his Goth-Ogor hood.

Top goth Ogor!!
Redd the Maw
Credit: @badusernametag

Redd the Maw
Credit: @badusernametag

Having assembled this model I realise that whilst it has the same intention as other large models in AoS with hangers-on, it does not seem to have the considered composition of other comparable models. Unlike Kakakros (OBR), this is not a ‘scene’ that draws us in and tells a story; the characters are not connected and there isn’t a physical scenic element connecting them. I guess the nearest good companion would be Glutos Orscollion (Hedonites) on his Excess wagon. That model has many hangers-on, monstrous servants and characters that are all mounted onto the chariot and are clearly all connected in one scene. It’s also on an oval base, which the chariot, its many riders and the mutants pulling it fill nicely.

In this case, the scene doesn’t seem well composed; the Gutseers are just wandering alongside and I am left wondering why this couldn’t have just been on a small oval (the chariot itself would fit) and you could put it amongst your units. It’s also on a massive 160mm base and really seems a little lost on there. I think I would have like to have seen a chariot with more levels that brought the hangers-on into the scene more. Or perhaps some scenic elements that tied them all together. 

Over all this is a great model concept that I think could have had some more love put into it in terms of composition and delivery.

Redd the Maw
Credit: @badusernametag

For painting I went for fast and loose here, using only contrast paints, some washes and a bible highlight colour (pallid witch flesh) to get this to a tabletop standard quickly.

Everything was Zenthinel highlighted up to Wraithbone together on the base, then I took the goons (Gutseers) off to paint them separately.

The base I sprayed loosely with two Montana browns over the Wraithbone, for depth. I used a range of Contrast and washes, highlighting them all when wet only, this blending unifies the tone and makes for a better final cohesion. But is pretty fast and fun to do.

List of paints:

  • Pallid Witch Flesh
  • Nuln Oil
  • Agrax Earthshade
  • Ratling Grime
  • Black Templar
  • Apothecary White
  • Gilliuman Flesh
  • Militarum Green
  • Shyish Purple
  • Gore-Grunta Fur
  • Blood Angels Red

I don’t really do metallics anymore, but this would of course love some Warlock bronze and maybe some dirty verdigris over the top.

Redd the Maw
Credit: @badusernametag

Redd the Maw
Credit: @badusernametag

Butcher

Badusernametag: Okay stop the clock. This is it. The best and most saliva tingly gross Hero kit ever for AoS, and I’ll hang my apron on the statement. It’s dripping with character and for me, it sums up everything that is gruesome, brutal and delicious about this army! New Butcher
Credit: Warhammer Community

There are three new infantry heroes for the Mawtribes: The Tyrant, Grell Firefist and this Butcher. Whilst the Tyrant has a slew of amazing weapons, heads and adornments, this kit is just so characterful and charming I cannot help but love it more!

Butcher Sprues

You get two head options here, one bare and one hooded. Interestingly, I think the hooded one just crosses the line here from grotesquely dark to a bit-too-silly. It’s a fine balance, which this model otherwise captures so well! She is so pleased with herself and clearly so full of joy to be tearing that corpse apart, whilst happily swinging the cleaver for the next victim. It has an almost Nurgle-esque jolliness to it, that is rendered dark and terrifying by the gruesome corpse in her grip and the swinging cauldron full of blood and corpses at her waist. 

Ogor Mawtribes Butcher.
Credit: @badusernametag

The movement in the model, from the cauldron sloshing, to the rippling oozing flesh of her body is spot on. Gives a real dynamism to this monster of flesh and hungry menace. Her wild-eyed expression of feral joy speaks to her fanaticism and the folds of flesh and just right, best I’ve seen on any Ogor for how the fluff and art speak for the army. This model is bigger than the other heroes and dominates its 50mm base. This will really stand out amongst the other Mawseekers.

It’s a clever kit to assemble, but unlike some others, the pieces slot together just so and this is a really fast build. Not a niggle or complaint. When I first saw this I really wanted to convert it into my mounted Overtyrant (Bolga Braindrinker), but now I must buy another, as it is just too damn cool not to paint up and join the Mawseekers throng!

Ogor Mawtribes Butcher.
Credit: @badusernametag

Cleavers/Gutseers

Cleavers on the block
Credit: @badusernametag

Norman: These went together relatively easily, but there was a good amount of bit hunting that I needed to do to find the pieces I needed. The poses themselves are great, they're very open and are pretty simple to customize with the rest of the range if that's something you're looking to do. Aesthetically, they do a great job of repeating design language from the Butcher and Redd, which helps differentiate them from their non-Mawseeker friends.

Badusernametag: Much as I love the new Butcher, this really is the infantry kit for me. Of the new infantry kits, this is the only one to feature multiple build options (more than one unit) and I quite love that the differences are subtle, as it allows for a bit of wiggle and customisation in your build. You could build this kit 3-4 times and have some mixed and interesting units to show for it.

Cleaver Champion and Gutseer Champion- new BFFs!!!
Redd the Maw
Credit: @badusernametag

Broadly, the Cleavers are like mini-Butchers, with ragged apron gut plates and two chopping weapons apiece (a mix of cleavers, knives, hooks) and the champion carries a swinging cauldron of gore, much like their Butcher masters. The Gutseers have hoods, one weapon and a gristly totem in the other hand made from an icon and part of a corpse. The Gutseers also have the ‘coiled entrails’ metalwork motif on their gut plate, which is super cool.

Cleaver Champion
Credit: @badusernametag

Interesting that the two unit champions are made from different base models, which means that you can build both out of one kit. Why would you? Not sure! But I did, as I wanted to see what the thickest and meanest of the Mawseekers had to offer. Both the builds have real appeal and I’m sure that we will see many of both on the tabletop. I have used the ‘coiled entrails’ gut plates on all my models here though, as its a really great motif I just can’t resist! 

Can’t wait to get the rest of them painted and on the table.

Ogor Mawtribes Cleaver.
Credit: @badusernametag

Norman:

Gutseer Credit: MildNorman

These paint up pretty fast! not too many doodads on them nor too much complexity to them. The new skin textures on these guys do a lot to helping washes push your shadows a bit more and adds some visual interest to the huge swathes of skin. The materials are easily differentiated and the poses are wide open which makes painting a breeze. This one guy took me about 3 hours primer to done not counting drying times for washes and basing but a lot of that time was spent figuring out a scheme. If you're going in with a plan, I expect folks can bang out a reinforced unit of these in a couple of painting sessions.

Final Thoughts

Badusernametag: This is a great release for those into the meaty and bloody end of things in the Ogor Mawtribes army. With these new kits and the gorgeous Gorgers (which are only a couple of years old) you can make the core of a gruesome army with all new models and a strong new theme. Throw in some Gluttons and a Tyrant and it’s going to look great and smash hard. 

All these new kits fit together so well, go together easily and are a joy to paint. This is a release I had such high hopes for and it really has not disappointed me.
