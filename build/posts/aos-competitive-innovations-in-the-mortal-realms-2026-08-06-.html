[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-08-06 (Part 2)

August 6th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Welcome back to part 2! As promised yesterday we are returning to this weeks events to follow up with some larger events that happened in Europe. As the new Aqshy book continues unabated, we'll continue to see some surprises as some of the stronger factions develop new metas, and even some underappreciated armies finally get some time to shine,

Thoughts

Cities of Sigmar is (one of) the hottest armies right now and while Sylvaneth also occupied this slot, they took a pretty bad beating with the nerf to Drycha and some points hikes for the new season so things do lean a fair bit in Cities favor.

The bonus to Cities is they are a lot more flexible in their ability to play aggro or stay on the defensive, while Sylvaneth are a bit more limited in the early game (before Overgrowth has really spread). So if Cities can get aggressive early with Cavaliers and Tahlia they can prune the numbers early and retain control of the board a lot easier than Sylvaneth, who play a bit more of the long con.

Thoughts

The meat and potatoes here is the basic template for a Cities list. Cavalier Lord with 2 Reinforced units of Cavaliers makes a strong hammer that can be difficult to lock down, since they can charge even in combat, preventing the enemy from reducing their damage output. The Gate Gargants are a nice little bonus that also allow retreat and charge, so they are never truly stuck.

The Fusil Major holds back to shoot targets and grant a +1 to hit, while the Great cannon lays down covering fire. Steelhelms hold the undignified but vital role of soaking up hits to protect your stuff that actually matters.

Thoughts

Your Lord of Change leads the party, giving a -1 to be hit Aura for all daemons and a +1 to cast for your wizards. He can also bring back dead daemons at half strength, making the Flamers and Horrors way more of a pain to deal with than they seem at first blush.

The Tzaangors Enlightened are your hammer for dealing with stuff that requires a real strong hand, otherwise youre bogging your enemy down and hitting them with Mortal Wounds over time from Flamers and Chariots.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Morathi and Blood Sisters

Thoughts

Even with the heavy points hikes they took, the best stuff still rises to the tops.

Morathi is still an oppressive beat stick who is very difficult to confront directly, forcing opponents to get more clever with how they combat her. Either staying at range or avoiding traditional means of doing damage. Spear snakes have a Strike Last effect that makers charging in with high wound models a dangerous proposition, and are pretty scary on the charge themselves thanks to crit mortals.

Khinerai help round things out by giving some objective grabbers, while the remaining Priests offer strong support spells.

The Best of the Rest

There were 7 more players on 4-1 records. They were:

  • 2nd - Lukas Herbst - Sons of Behemat: King Brodd and Gatecrusher with Mancrushers.
  • 3rd - hugo jonquiere - Sylvaneth: Herohammer with Alarielle, Drycha, Spirit of Durthu and Belthanos.
  • 4th - Tobias Dreger - Sylvaneth: Belthanos, Drycha and Spirit of Durthu with Kurnoth.
  • 5th - Robert Bock - Ossiarch Bonereapers: Kavalos Deathrider spam
  • 6th - Oliver Pupkes - Stormcast Eternals: See Showdown.
  • 7th - Maurice Heitzer - Maggotkin of Nurgle: Rotigus and the Glottkin, with Rotswords.
  • 8th - Florian Rechel - Lumineth Realm-Lords: Vanari good stuff and Severith.
