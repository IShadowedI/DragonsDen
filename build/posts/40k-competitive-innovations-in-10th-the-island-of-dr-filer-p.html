[40k] Competitive Innovations in 10th: The Island of Dr. Filer pt.3

May 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Time for our final crop of events for the week, featuring (you guessed it) more crabs - but also some credible challenges from other factions as well.

Archetype

Slaanesh Monster Mash

Thoughts

Slaanesh demands more claws, more. This is achieved by the mechanism of taking Carnival of Excess and adding some Keepers and some Daemonettes alongside Fulgrim, Maulerfiends and Defilers. This will fully ruin the day of anyone who isn't absolutely loaded for bear, and has some pretty brutal proactive combos as well. Sustained Hits right out of the gate is great for Defilers and Keepers alike, and I can't really imagine any opponent managing to win a game if the Slaaneshi ever trigger Ecstatic Slaughter to slingshot a Defiler extra deep into the enemy's lines. Deeply vicious stuff going on here, and a whole bunch of extra D3 melee won't go amiss in some matchups too. Nice to get some variety amidst The Horrors, even if it's still very much part of The Horrors, well done Lachlan.

Archetype

Crab-hunting Clowns

Thoughts

Serpent's Brood bursts onto the podium here, deploying a whole bunch of angry, fragile clowns to try and murder the vehicle-heavy metagame. Three haywire bike units will certainly do vicious things to hard-to-hide hulls like Defilers, and in combination with the rest of what's here they're enough of a full gut punch that opponents facing this have to be careful not to open themselves up to getting fully bodied. Small units in boats can surgically pick off enemies, the big Scouts 6" unit can also pile on the pressure, and while everything here dies in a stiff breeze, it'll do a lot of damage on the way out. This detachment also feels like one to watch for 11th Edition, because bikes with Weaver's Coils are going to be super good with the new FLY rules. Great to see Aeldari clowning on their foes (though tragically the forces of Slaanesh put a stop to it in round 5), good job Callum.

Thoughts

Deathwatch round out the top four here, going for the standard Kill Team setup, but dialing back a bit on Characters and normal flex support to add a couple of Terminator units. This adds more durable bodies to the table, and the Terminators are a little bit of a pain to pick off with D3 shooting thanks to there being a couple of shields in each unit. Otherwise, it's business as usual - Kill Teams teleport around the table flensing stuff to death, and any cheap /mid-weight stuff that tries to hide gets dunked on by triple vengor launcher. Good job Jordan.

Archetype

Spectacle

Thoughts

Spectacle continues to pirouette gracefully through the metagame taking trophies, continuing that trend here with a build that tinker with the formula ever-so-slightly. The break from the norm is adding an Archon to join to the other half of the Kabalite unit (Malys gets the first half), giving them just enough melee punch to contribute a little in a pinch, and providing a free use of Grenades to play with. It's a brutal metagame out there, so definitely no harm in squeezing every little bit of value out of units in your list, though I'd be interested in whether it felt worth it over (e.g.) another Venom. Good to see builds not completely static either way, and well done Tom on the win!
