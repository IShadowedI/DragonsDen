Thoughts

T'au as an army are tailor-made for Purge the Foe and this list plays directly into the army's strengths, running units which can hit hard and move quickly. The biggest challenge for Retaliation Cadre lists is weapon teching for the current meta: Starscythe units with Burst Cannons are great into Ork hordes but nearly worthless into big knights, and the natural advantage of Purge vs. Take and Hold - that you score for killing while your opponent has to bring units to the middle of the table to get killed - is mitigated by playing into a list of big knights, where all your energy has to be spent taking down massive threats. Those threats also double as having a lot of OC, making it easy for them to sit and hold objectives. T'au lists don't love punching up, but can usually make up for having to roll 5+ to wound by throwing out a ton of volume with high-damage shots. But a few choice 5+ invulnerable saves from the knights can make that all moot - a single knight living on 1 wound can easily decide a game, and that Knight Lancer's small number of attacks plays very well into targets like Crisis Suits and the Ghostkeel.

The plan for the knights player here is to just dominate the board's central objectives, pushing forward with the Lancer while the Atropos, Canis, and Crusader hang back and hold objectives. The Knights player will want to close the gap early - even losing two or three units can make a huge difference in the matchup, as the T'au player needs to combine shooting from the Hammerhead, Ghostkeel, and Broadsides to make something happen.

Result

The T'au player held on for an extremely narrow victory here, winning 90-89.

Archetype

Rad-Zone Electro Priests

Thoughts

You don't see Rad Zone Admech too often. The Detachment rule is fairly underpowered, or at least it was in 10th edition. It's become a much more interesting threat in 11th, where starting the game battle-shocked can mean you just stay shocked all game long. That's going to make this list pretty dice-reliant; sometimes your opponent will just pass all their tests, or taking D3 wounds across their army won't matter, or you'll just roll a 1/2 for every unit. That said, when it does come together, I can imagine few things more frustrating than just going "well, I can't score all game with these units."
