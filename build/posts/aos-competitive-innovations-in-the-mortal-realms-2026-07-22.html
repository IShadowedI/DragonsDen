[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-07-22

July 22nd, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

This is a big CI this week with 6 total events, including the rather large Warhammer Open in Tacoma. The first event with the new GHB (and first event in a while even) to enter triple digit players, there seems to be a lot of excitement for the new adventures in Aqshy. This makes it a great time to find out where the meta is drifting, so let's take a look.

Thoughts

This has been one of the biggest events of the year for Sigmar, and the first big event since the GHB dropped so this is a great opportunity to see where things are at. Daughters of Khaine have been a top tier contender in the game, where Slaanesh is the new kid on the block, desperate to prove themselves.

Daughters are a strong offensive army, wanting to push early and not relent. Slaanesh has a lot of tools to control combat, waiting to anticipate charge and then making getting a clean hit in extremely difficult before they clap back. This puts Daughters in a difficult position, as they don't want to press too early lest the Painbringers cut them down, but wait too long and Shalaxi (with her strong anti-Monster/Hero ability) will show up and bring the pain.

Thoughts

If there was going to be a place to see where the new Slaanesh book was headed, it would be at the Tacoma Open piloted by Tom Guan. If there's one thing to take away from this, it's that Painbringers are very good. 3+ Save, 5+ Ward and punish opponents for trying to swing into them, they are difficult to remove. Combine that with the powerful Temptation abilities they can be given strong offensive and defensive buffs, and ways to make charges less optimal, reducing damage even further.

Ideally, Painbringers want to sit on the point and wait for a charge. So you Shalaxai and Blissbarb Seekers to help push and put a little pressure on the enemy.

Thoughts

Mostly uninteresting due to being the typical Cities good stuff. Cavaliers are strong, hard to pin down. Cavalier Marshal, Tahlia and Warforger all exist to make them better at their job, while Steelhelms force opponents to attack them instead of the Horses, increasing their survival even further.

Archetype

Mannfred with Deathrattle

Thoughts

Mannfred hasn't really gotten enough love since Prince Vhordrai showed up and absolutely demolished the book, but he has a ton of cool tricks. He gets a chance at Strike First when he charges, and then if he kills any models he can give a bonus attack to the nearby Grave guard or Zombies, making for some pretty strong hammers for their price point.

The Vengorian lord is an assist, reducing rend of the enemy to make the rest of your list a lot hardier while also being able to clap back pretty well. Both him and Mannfred can move units right into combat without needing to charge, which helps get around nasty anti-charge abilities.

Thoughts

Hey look, more Fyreslayers! With a Fyreslayers mirror match it's going to come down to whether or not Magmadroths are worth their weight (spoilers: they are). Infantry Fyreslayers have struggled this edition to keep up with their mounted cousins, and are mostly seen as filler once you've run all the Magmadroths you got (or just want some objective holders). Gotrek also isn't much to talk about now, so it's a valiant effort to make it to the Showdown, but the meta pick is gonna win here.

Thoughts

Mercifully Runesons on Magmadroths survived the switchover to Aqshy. With this season being the realm of Fire, it'd be a damned shame if they started slipping now.

Magmadroth slayers aren't too complicated. The Runesons can pick a Runefather  (and they can all share the same dad). If he is in combat, injured or dead, all their mounts get +1 to damage. It's a lot, and they can be rough to take down with 14 wounds on a 4+ save and 6+ ward (plus mortals back to you on 6s to save). While it can lack board coverage, it creates immediate pressure that can be difficult to deal with.
