[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Lean, Green and Mean pt.1

July 29th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Our first 11th edition balance update lands in a big way this week, with an unprecedentedly high level of immediate uptake. Over three-quarters of GT+ events used the new rules, so we get a first look at what the impact has been.

The good news is that the cliff-edge at the bottom of the metagame looks a lot less scary now, with many more factions being able to make a credible challenge. A few more people are also giving Disruption a serious go, as it looks like the improvement to the Purge game really helped.

Of more concern, at least from initial results, is that Orks and Salamanders seem to have gotten away too soft, with Orks still miles out in front among factions with an appreciable player count (though the mighty seven Deathwatch players do technically pip them on win rate if you count everyone). The new Equatorial Hordes detachment also lands hot for the Orks with two tournament wins alongside Freebooter Krew. The other thing to keep an eye on is a shift in disposition representation - Purge has dropped off by a meaningful amount among X-1+ builds, with the spoils largely going to Take and Hold and Priority Assets.

It's early days, however, so let's focus on the good bits for now - many weaker factions have plenty of viable toys to play with, and I'm even looking at my Aeldari with some interest for the first time since the edition launched. Let's hit it.

Thoughts

A fiery showdown here as re-tooled Salamanders face off against newly powered up Bringers of Flame (and their Sacresant friends). The Salamanders build looks a fair bit different to what we've seen previously, with the Sternguard and big Infernus units gone in favour of a full Terminator unit with Celerity (a great rolling threat) and an Immolator Aggressor unit. Still lots of volume and also still two Land Raiders because the world is a brutal, brutal place.

The Sisters aren't slouches in the killing department though, bringing plenty of melta of their own, and powerful Castigators that are a pretty decent counter to the terminators. Sacresants also support this hull-heavy setup really effectively, providing either a defensive line or a counter-charge threat as required.

In terms of how they face off, I think the big issue for the Sororitas here is that the Salamander overwatch bullies their Primary plans way more effectively than the inverse. The Land Raiders will toast the Dominions, while the Aggressors can have a real go at lifting a Sacresant block, leaving the Sisters without great options to play for consistent mid-table scoring. That's only exacerbated by the two Infernus units having Action/Shoot, providing a bit of extra push for the Marines without having to sacrifice as much output. The speed of the Terminators is also a headache, because although the Castigators can chew through them, they're not really going to have much time. This feels firmly in the Marines favour, and I'm not super sure what I'd do from the Sisters side to try and flip it - maybe a really ambitious play with Vahl's unit? Anyway, the Marines did, in fact, win.

Archetype

Jungle Pirate Horde

Thoughts

Our first sighting through the foliage of the new Greenskin menace here, with Equatorial Hordes combining with access to extra Waaagh activations from Freebooterz to create a blisteringly fast and brutal horde setup with a fair bit of extra depth trick/damage-dealing wise. Maybe go ahead and keep your anti-horde stuff in your builds for now. Particularly helpful for this setup is that it wants to cap out at three big units, as that's the limit that can Scout, and also doesn't mind using Snaggas, so it gets hit less by taxes than pure Green Tide does. Krumpin' season continues unabated, congratulations Jordan.
