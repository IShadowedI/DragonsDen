Archetype

Alban Comes Home

Thoughts

Death Guard devotee Alban returns to his beloved after a regrettable flirtation with Slaanesh. He cuts a lot of what was central to the Shamblerot builds, with Mortarion and the Defilers making way for a Knight and lots more melee MSU in Plague Marines and Deathshroud. It's far wider, and he is playing Prio to force the push into him from enterprising Take and Hold players. Awesome to see him back cooking with his true Legion - well done mon ami.

Archetype

Immortal Blades

Thoughts

Necron master Arnaut cooks another new madness, with a million Immortal and Tomb Blade units flying about. My understanding is that this can apply massive amounts of mortal and dev wounds - exactly the sort of thing some of our current meta menaces don't enjoy, bypassing the defences they're investing in. Late stage Necrons have really become a sort of metal Aeldari, with speed, guile, and tricks propping up a book that has had most of it's pure stat checks nerfed over time. Don't worry though, there's still a C'tan! Well done fella.

Thoughts

A glorious showdown which highlights some thoughts I had a couple of weeks ago when the changes for Ad Mech first game through. I've not commented on them on  here yet - WHAT THE HELL, balance team. Shoving 5 dps into one list from very competitive and dangerous detachments whilst doing nothing remotely similar for any other faction isn't a good look. Ad Mech were already performing very consistently.

That said, the resulting Eradication builds have been, how do I put this? Greedy. Greedy as hell. All damage, no finesse, and grabbing the Take and Hold disposition. Ad Mech historically aren't the top dogs in this department - many other more brutal pressure type armies prefer that landscape. I have watched new Ad Mech tearing up the competitive world behind the scenes, and it's starting to filter out elsewhere now too. If there is a saving grace, it is probably in the slight weakness as a Take and Hold army Ad Mech have, which is being run down.

Enter the Blood Angels. Historically a really tough match up for Ad Mech because of their speed and ability to simply run through almost every defensive profile they have. This list packs in Dreadnoughts and Sang Guard, as well as forward pressure pieces to skirmish and tag. The combination here is enough to actually cleave through, then tie up the Ad Mech lines. Clustered Ad Mech units are vulnerable to massed overrun connects too. These are the kinds of list I expect to give the new hotness pause, and so it proved here.

There were 4 more players on 4-1 records. They were:

  • 2nd - Anthony LoGrande - Chaos Space Marines (Cabal of Chaos/Veterans of the Long War) - Take and Hold: Vindicator / Defiler / Double Oblits Vets leaning hard on ranged output.
  • 3rd - Tyler Hayes - Chaos Space Marines (Cabal of Chaos/Chaos Cult) - Disruption: Old school agro Chaos Cult played in Disruption.
  • 4th - Hank Bell - Orks (Wreckas/Green Tide/Kult of Speed) - Reconnaissance: Massed Tankbustas, Trukks, and Boyz.
  • 5th - Jason Flanzer - Necrons (Hand of the Dynasty/Canoptek Court) - Take and Hold: Canoptek Goodstuff with Wraiths, Doomstalkers and the like.
