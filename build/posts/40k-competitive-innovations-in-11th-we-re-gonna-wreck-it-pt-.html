Archetype

Dogs

Thoughts

Who let the dogs out? That would be GW, with a series of targeted buffs to bring the whoops all War Dogs play back to life. It loves Recon, there are plenty of armies that can't chew through it in time, and it is deceptively good at punching up and through low unit count elite armies - of which there are plenty in the meta. I love the individually named War Dogs, I've voted for my top three here for you Kenneth:

  1. Barbaghast - this has 10/10 real horror movie villain vibes. Some lines you can shout whilst attacking with this unit: "FEAR THE BARBAGHAST!" "BARBAGHAST IS HERE!" "You'll be BARBAGHAST AT WHAT THIS GUYS ABOUT TO DO TO YOU!", etc
  2. Kostirr the Hollow - an appropriate name for a plastic model with air inside
  3. Charmalio Voidrider - this guy stands out like a flashy European Aeldari that got lost and fell in with the wrong crew

Terrific work on the top placing!
