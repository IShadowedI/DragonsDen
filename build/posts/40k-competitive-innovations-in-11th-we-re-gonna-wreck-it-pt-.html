Archetype

Mercenary Goodstuff

Thoughts

Votann continue on their surprisingly strong surge, here sticking plenty of the popular goodstuff tools into Mercenary Oathband instead of Needgaard. This provides a bit more sticky, all-purpose melee from the big Steeljack units via Optimal Expenditure, while still preserving good all-round flexibility via the detachment rule and other Stratagems. One advantage of looking at this after the weekend is that it makes me think Votann are one of the better answers to the Lion (still a monster at his lower price), because all they do is unleash reliable, high-volume AP-1 damage, which is exactly what you need. If that proves true, look for them to continue to thrive! Congratulations Immanuel on the success with this slightly unusual spin on dorfs.

Archetype

War Horde Goodstuff

Thoughts

A first major win for the new Orks here, going the very down-the-line route of War Horde with a grab-bag of the book's best units, skewed towards herohammer (especially now Dakkarigs count as that). It's not super complicated, but War Horde is pretty appealing as 3DP detachments go, providing key tools to punch up in melee (the Detachment rule and Lethals) and at range (via a Stratagem for Rapid Fire 1). Key, I think, to the success are Meganobz, who are really good now thanks to having real combi profiles and way better killsaws, rocketing them to being multi-phase all-stars, and tough to kill. Ghaz and Wazdakka also remain great additions to any all-rounder list that can fit them. Dreads and Wreckas have been leading the charge for Orks, but this shows they can thrive on the fundamentals as well, congratulations Jonathan.

Thoughts

To close, we've got more Orks, this time going for one of the shooty setups that's proving popular. Either Gitz or Tankbustas inside a Targetin' Gizmos Battlewagon is incredibly vicious damage-wise, and Dakkarigs provide great all-rounder shooting support on top of that. Grots still fill out lists on the dirt cheap, and a big Kans squad to buff and unleash is a tremendous tool to complete the setup. Ork shooting is real again, as it somehow always is for a few months of every edition, so enjoy it, dakka fans. Congratulations Travis.
