Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. An excellent Coterie list that highlights the strength in the detachment at the moment - loads of Flawless and Infractors for trading, Defilers as an anchor and a cheeky Spawn unit to bop back and forth scoring the mission. It's a Prio army in name only, using the disposition as bait to start getting its teeth into the opponent. Great work on the top placing.

The little dreadnoughts that could make an appearance here in the style of Folger's list from WTC and Warmasters. Archetype

KFC Tsons

Thoughts

A run out for the Changehost here, which let's you spread the big boy threats a bit wider than running Magnus by having both Kairos and a Lord of Change in play. Between these, the Horrors, AND the MVBs, there is a lot of stat check potential here to make the opponent play and draw them onto the objectives so the flamers can get 'em. I love this build! Great work on the top placing and the unusual list.

Archetype

Legion of Excess Pressure

Thoughts

The classic Excess pressure list returns and takes a GT here- being able to fob off enemy damage onto absorbent Contorted Epitome squads is very nice in a world of relatively few activations. Excess can get stuck into the foe and really shut down their ability to score their own primary, and this list opts for Priority Assets to give it the option of playing a slower game if it wants. Fantastic work on the event win.
