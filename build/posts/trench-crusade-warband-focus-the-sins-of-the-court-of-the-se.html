Trench Crusade Warband Focus: The Sins of the Court of the Seven-Headed Serpent

September 25th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome everyone to another Trench Crusade Warband focus! This week is a little different, as we’ll be exploring the Sins from the Court of the Seven-Headed Serpent. We’ll be covering all the hangry folk from the Court of Gluttony, to the slow but steady demons of Sloth. 

Credit: darius_minis

The Lore

Behind any invading force lies a council that commands from the rear. In this instance, it is the Court of the Seven-Headed Serpent, a group of Archdevils representing the seven deadly sins. The Covenant of Heaven and Hell ensures that no true demon may walk the Earth, but the heads of the Serpent are nothing if not ingenious. Mixing demons with the creatures that walk the earth, both humans and animals, they have found ways to invade the material plane without incurring His retribution. 

The Seven Heads of the Serpent are masters of the Sin they are a part of, each one notorious and ruthless, obtaining their power through deceit, treachery, or outright brutality. First amongst all is Eligos, the Duke of Petals, who sits at the head of the Council as the High Cathedra of Pride. Next is Mammon, the Prince of Greed, whose seat is adorned with gold and marble. At the seat of Lust sits Asmodeus, the Monarch of Lechery. For Envy sits Moloch, the President of the Sacrificial Sun, whose idols are grander than Pride's. Next is Gluttony, which has seen stability once again since Beelzebub’s abdication in the form of Bael, the Uncrowned King of Stolen Land. The seat of Wrath, practical for one only concerned with destruction, is filled by Apollyon, the Lady of Locusts. Lastly, there is the Silk Bed of Sloth, where Belphagor, Duchess of the Gap, slumbers within. 

Should the Court unite under a common goal for too long, then the fall of Creation would surely be guaranteed.

The Sins

Unlike our usual Warband Focuses, we’ll go over each Sin individually, give a quick overview of the specific rule changes, and explain how to plan your warband with this Sin in mind. The abilities we’re about to cover have their own restrictions, limits, and Ducat costs, so do check those out when making your Warband. 

Wrath

  • Blind Rage: Add +1 Dice to Risky Success Rolls for Dash Actions.
  • Charge of Hatred: Has a Move Characteristic of 12” when the model Charges. If the target is not the closest target, you must make a Risky Success Roll first. If it fails, no charge is made at all. 
  • Less Mark of Cain: -1 Injury Dice to all incoming attacks. 
  • Desecrated Saint Aura: +1 Dash for Dash rolls for units within 8”.

If it wasn’t clear enough, this Sin is all about getting into Melee as quickly as possible, and ensuring your key pieces survive long enough to get where they need to be. This Sin makes great use of the Desecrated Saint, whose aura helps get not only all your other models into combat quickly, but also the Saint themselves. Due to the lack of Spells in this Sin, and the fact that your models can easily get into Melee, a Sorcerer isn’t necessary to include. 

Unit & Battkit Setup: 
  • Praetor: Less Mark of Cain, Serpent Assault Gun, and a Malebranche Sword. 
  • Desecrated Saint: Great Hammer / Hellblade, Sword x2.
  • Hell Knight: Blind Rage and Hell Blade and Trench Shield.
Credit: darius_minis

Envy

  • Coveted Position: This spell lets the spellcaster swap their position with another that is within 12”, as long as both models can fit (their base centres are swapped) and the model chosen is outside of 1” away from any enemy model. 
  • Envious Eyes: You can purchase 1 piece of Battlekit from New Antioch, Trench Pilgrims, or Iron Sultanate. This is only for your Praetors and Sorcerers, but at the end of the day, they are who you really need these on. 
  • What is Yours is Mine: After making a Spell Action, you can remove 1 Blood or Blessing Marker from a model in Line of Sight, and place it next to the Spellcaster. 
  • Saint Aura: Enemy models within 12” of the Saint cannot Charge it if it is within 1” of another enemy model. 

Envy is another great Sin to make use of the Desecrated Saint, as you can pull off some nice shenanigans to quickly propel it up the board, thanks to Coveted Position. When it comes to Envious Eyes, grabbing Machine Armour on your Praetor will turn it into a truly formidable foe, allowing you to hit the much-beloved -3 Armour stat, whilst still retaining both hands for your Serpent Assault Gun. The problem here is if you come up against a lot of Anti-Armour, in which case your Praetor falls way too quickly. You can also go for Martyrdom Pills on either your Sorcerer or Praetor model, having some more universal survivability. 

Unit & Battkit Setup: 
  • Sorcerer: Burning Inferno, Coveted Position, Envious Eyes, Martyrdom Pills, Burning Inferno.
  • Praetor: Coveted Position, Envious Eyes, Serpent Assault Gun, Hellblade, Machine Armour (if you don’t take Envious Eyes on the Sorcerer). 
  • Desecrated Saint: Hellblade, Sword x2.

Lust

  • Call of the Flesh: After successfully casting a Spell, the Spellcaster’s turn ends, and the next model the Opponent activates must Move, Charge or Retreat, it must be do so towards the Spellcaster. It may then carry out other Actions as usual. 
  • Exquisite Pain: After Casting a Spell, pay an amount of Blood Markers and pick a model in Line of Sight with the Spellcaster, and place those Blood Markers on the model you picked. 
  • Forbidden Pleasures: At the Start of each game, pick a model in your Warband that does not have the Demonic Keyword and place 3 Blood Markers next to that model. 
  • Saint Aura: Enemy Armour cannot reduce the Injury Dice roll, unless the Armour has the Impervious Armour rule. 

Lust is all about your Blood Marker economy, able to start each game with a plethora of them already on the board. Having such an abundance of Blood Markers compliments the Hunter of the Left-Handed Path, whose very Blood Marker hungry. The use of the Hunter, coupled with Hell Knights equipped with Ophidian Rifless and Exquisite Pain make for a formidable ranged Warband, able to reliably hit from afar. 

Unit & Battkit Setup: 
  • Hunter of the Left-Handed Path: Headtaker, Reinforced Armour.
  • Hell Knight: Exquisite Pain and Ophidian Rifle, Incendiary Ammunition. 
Credit: Josh Tedd (@thepaintingtedd)

Pride

  • Light of Samael: After a successful Spellcast, pick a visible enemy within 24” and make an Injury Roll for that model. If the enemy is on a 32mm or smaller base, it is driven back D6” if not taken out of Action. 
  • Proud Defiance: AS long as you have a model with this ability on the battlefield, you do not need to take Morale Checks. 
  • Too Proud to Fall: After a model with this Spell is Downed, you may make a Spell Action. If successful, you ignore the Down result. However, you still take the Blood Marker and Tough is still broken. 
  • Saint Aura: After the Saint has Activated, place 1 Blood Marker on each enemy model within 8” of the Desecrated Saint. 

Pride is all about defying the regular rules of the game, be it by making injury rolls without having to attack regularly, or by refusing to be Downed and standing tall.

Unit & Battkit Setup: 
  • Praetor: Too Proud to Fall, Light of Samael, Serpent Assault Gun, Greatsword.
  • Sorcerer: Burning Inferno, Proud Defiance, Light of Samael.
  • Hunter of the Left-Handed Path: Standard / Reinforced Armour, Headtaker.

Sloth

  • Charm of Acedia: After successfully casting, the next Action the Spellcaster takes that requires a Success / Risky Success Roll, it automatically succeeds. 
  • Daemonium Meridianum: Enemy models treat Open or Dangerous Terrain with 6” of this model as Difficult Terrain. 
  • Morphean Mind: Your opponent may not spend more than 1 Blood Marker to add -1 Dice to a Success Roll for a model with this ability. 
  • Saint Aura: Enemy models treat Minor Hits as Down results when within 8” of the Saint. Works even on models wearing Machine Armour, or other abilities that usually convert Down results to Minor Hits. 

Sloth wants to ensure success, through methodical and strategic play. Charm of Acedia ensure that your important rolls always succeed, and is a must to ensure your Elites do what they need to do. Morphean Mind is ok, but with the way that the math works, even a -1 to a standard roll can be very impactful. 

Unit & Battkit Setup: 
  • Sorcerer: Charm of Acedia, Burning Inferno, Slavemaster.
Credit: Josh Tedd (@thepaintingtedd)

Gluttony

  • Belly of the Beast: If a Melee Attack that targets this model causes a Blood Marker, place 1 Blood Marker on the attacking enemy model. 
  • Eater of the Flesh: Remove 1 Blood Marker from this model for each Blood Marker that is caued to an enemy model as the result of a Melee Attack. Does not work on models with the Black Grail or Demonic Keywords. 
  • Uncaring Gluttony: After successfully casting a Spell, pick one enemy piece of Equipment, and that is rendered unusable for the rest of the game. 
  • Saint Aura: - 1 Dice for enemy models when within 8” of the Saint, unless they have the Black Grail or Artificial Keyword. 

Gluttony wants to weaken your opponent by harming key equipment pieces that may rely on, why punishing them for punishing you. Through Belly of the Beast and Eater of Flesh, you’re playing a back and forth with your Blood Markers, creating an uphill battle for your opponent to take down your key pieces. 

Unit & Battkit Setup
  • Sorcerer: Burning Inferno, reinforced Armour.
  • Praetor:  Eater of Flesh, Belly of the Beast, Gas Grenades, Hellblade, Trench Shield.
  • Hunter of the Left-hand Path:  Headtaker, Armour of your choice. 

Greed

  • Black Heart: Before making a Success or Risky Success Roll, you can attempt to cast this Spell. If successful, you can add +1 Dice to the roll for the other Action.
  • Body of Gold: A Model with this ability has the Golem Keyword. It loses Tough if it already had that Keyword. 
  • Greedy Hearts: At the start of the Game, place 1 Blessing Marker next to a model with this ability for each enemy model that is worth a total of 150 Ducats or more. 
  • Saint Aura: Enemy models must charge the Saint if it is within 12”, in Line of Sight and can be done without having to cross Dangerous Terrain, or Climb or Jump, Down Down or make a Diving Charge. 

Greed offers it all, with defence, offence and utility. Black Heart will be great to add a simple +1 Dice to Success Roll. The standout is Body of Gold, which fits very well on a Hell Knight, who doesn’t have Tough, and their in-built -2 Impervious Armour Combines really well with the Golem keyword, making them a menace for your opponent. Greedy Hearts can go on something later in a campaign, where it can really push a single model into overdrive with all the Blessing Markers. 

Unit & Battkit Setup
  • Praetor: Black Heart or Greedy Hearts, Hellblade.
  • Hell Knight: Body of Gold, Weapons of your choice.

Final Thoughts

The Court are a unique faction in their own right, and incredibly nuanced in how they play. In many ways, they play similarly to New Antioch, in that every unit needs to make use of every part of their activation, except with Court, it's on a small selection of truly powerful models, rather than many weaker ones.
