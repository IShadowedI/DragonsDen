BattleTech Mech Overview: Gladiator

July 15th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Calen’s Icarus disappeared in a pillar of roiling explosions, scrap and shrapnel peeling away from the machine in giant chunks and spiraling bits of glowing metal that withered in the building blizzard like fireflies in a field. The stream of rockets walked off the battered ‘Mech, leaving a trail of corkscrewing smoke screaming off into the snow-filled night. The smoking hulk of the Icarus II stumbled from the plume, dragging its right arm through the snow by a ruined string of myomer

Boyce turned his Gladiator, tracking the grinding movement of the Archer for a moment as the snow around it flashed into steam. The battle had turned, quickly, with the entrance of Kagan’s Lance. More Pirates were hitting the ground with every passing second, chopped, shot, and blown apart by the withering deluge of fire that the massed guns of the Marshal ‘Mechs and their supporting tanks were bringing down on them. 

“Boyce,” the rumbling voice of Marshal Romer called over the antiquated Fujika Multiband mounted to the roof of the old Gladiator’s cockpit, “how’re you lookin’?” 

He glanced down, assessing the wireframe readout for a moment before reporting. “Armor’s holding for the most part. Down one of my medium lasers though.”

“Good enough, you’re on me, we’re ending this, now.” The lights of his Mackie’s spotlights cutting through the night like a knife as its bulk pushed past on Boyce’s left. “There’s a Coyotl out there that needs breaking.”

— Marshal Boyce Yultman, Fronc Reaches, Colonial Marshal Gladiator GLD-1R Pilot. Rockwellawan, Fronc Reaches, April 4th 3131.  

—

Howdy ‘Mechwarriors and welcome back to the ‘Mech Overview! This week we’re going to be stepping into the arena of ‘Mech ratings with a true Gladitorial spirit, with the Gladiator! A real weirdo of a 55 tonner that does some different things from its various weight-class sharing brethren.

Originally conceived of in 2468 as the first homegrown ‘Mech to ever come out of Draconis Combine the Gladiator is a real old horse that has seen some significant movement throughout its lifespan. With several variants, even the original Primitive version, still kicking around in the IlClan era there’s a lot of design space being worked with this chassis. From Primitive to Clan-tech the Gladiator has seen it all and been… interesting just about the entire time.

Now, without further ado, let’s get into it; and see if we’re entertained.

—

Gladiator. Credit: Rockfish

Chassis

The Gladiator shares just about one thing and one thing only across all of its variants: it’s a 55 tonner, and it’s got good armor. With only 2 of them ever dipping below 80% armor, and they’ve got gimmicks to compensate, the Gladiator is almost universally a very survivable ‘Mech for what you’re paying for, which is pretty cool for a weirdo line-’Mech that’s usually rather cheap to field.

With a BV range from 1,027 to 2,119 we’ve got a whole lot of motion in the deal-making ocean to play with here which is either a really good sign or a REALLY BAD ONE.

—

Variants

These mechs have all been reviewed based on a standard F through S scale, which you can find described on our landing page here (along with all of our other ‘mech reviews, the name of the box you can buy to get any of the mechs we have covered, and our general methodology).

—

Marik Militia Gladiator. Credit: Lynn B

GLD-1R

I am a man, in a dragon-shaped box. God has given me Fire. This is now everyone’s problem.

This is a primitive. It’s 1,027 BV. It has a Large Laser, a pair of Medium Lasers, and 14 single heat sinks. It builds movement heat firing everything. It goes 4/6 and has 95% armor. There’s 1 heat sink in either side torso, and that’s it… that’s the ‘Mech.

That’s all he needs to be, he was made in the year of our Lord 24-fucking-68. What do you want from him? Cool shit? He’s doing his best, leave him alone. Also his best is honestly pretty cool This is a brick. A device meant to be handed to a Kodiak to chew on for two-to-four turns, if it’s lucky, and that’s it. It’s not going to do much damage. It’s not going to win you the game off the back of killing things.

It’s just gonna be a pin-cushion and soak fire like it was meant to do and like you paid it to do.

And that’s fine. We need more shit-buckets like this that know their place and do not buck to be higher than their station. This is a machine meant to be flogged for its hubris and take it like a champ before asking for you to do it again. The Men and Women who drive this machine know this. They know that this is truly their lot in life and they accept it with stalwart grace and the acknowledgement that they will do that which the Coordinator asks not because it is good and right but because they must.

And no one will remember them, and that’s ok…

I guess.

Liberty’s Rating: C. Look man, you’re that cheap and that tough and you’re getting a lot of love from me. This thing walks along with your line force and either eats shit off the enemy’s big hitters or gets to throw kicks and laser beams all day like it’s the best day they’ve ever had. Love it.

GLD-2R

I’m a ghost. An Enigma. I was never real and neither were you.

The 2R stands in the funny spot of ‘being a record sheet we have, but an availability that never exists’. We usually don’t talk about the MUL but the 2R is the direct ‘oh shit, we can have non-primitive stuff?’ version that straight up is never on anyone’s availability.

Which is fucking hilarious… Because honestly I fuck with it, heavy.

This is a 1R that now costs 1,184 BV to carry the same armament, 19 heat sinks, and a full 100% armor load. This is more durable, due to the extra crit slots taken up by the heat sinks, and has no heat problem at all. Extra damage? No. Capable of being played in a tournament setting that says primitives are lame? Yes!

Cool. Friend-shaped.

Liberty’s Rating: C. Honestly just a 1R but cooler, literally.

GLD-3R

I’m a sell-sword. I’ve got them rain-makers. Them big ol’ sky-darkeners. Massive LRM Racks. Great big ol’ bombardaramas… What do you mean ‘a pair of LRM-10s isn’t that many missiles’? I’ll have you know that you’re a bitch.

The 3R is a 2R that ditches the Large Laser, 18% of its armor, and 7 heat sinks to get a pair of LRM-10s, a ton of ammo for each of them, and 5 jump jets. This is 1,282 BV and if you want a pair of LRM-10s on an OK chassis… Why do you want that? Go get a trebuchet.

Liberty’s Rating: D+. This is fine, and the extra armor over a trebuchet is cool but I don’t know that the platform’s well suited for it, considering the only backup guns to the LRMs you’ve got are the Medium Lasers.

GLD-4R

Oh boy, trooper time!

The 4R is where we’ve always been heading with the concept of the Gladiator. A 5/8/5 Trooper with a PPC, a pair of MLs, and an SRM 6 supported by 13 Single Heat Sinks makes a ‘Mech that goes up 2 heat on a jump firing off either band of guns, which is aggressively fine for a ‘Mech that only costs 1,291 BV. It’s got 82% armor like the 3R before it but that’s still fine, only one ton of SRM-6 ammo that’s padded out by 2 jump jets isn’t the most secure thing in the world but it’s enough longevity to keep you in the fight for a while while also just being a nuisance to support the rest of your force with.

Just uh… hope that you’re luckier than 33%?

Liberty’s Rating: C-. A world with some updated guns would be very kind to this design ethos. 

GLD-5R

OH MY GOD! UPDATED GUNS FOR MY DESIGN ETHOS!!!

Force Marshal, we have to make nice with the Foxes, or the Marians, I don’t care which but we need this ‘Mech. We’re back up to 100% armor coverage, kept mobile by a Light Engine, an XL gyro, and a 4/6/6 movement profile the 5R will be annoyingly tough to put down in the right hands.

Armed with my beloved Snub-Nosed PPC, an Artemis IV equipped MML-5 with 2 tons of ammo, a pair of ERMLs, a C3s, and a Guardian ECM while being kept dead cool by 13 Double Heat Sinks the 5R jumps its full distance and fires everything to remain heat neutral while sicking the hounds of war in its C3-net on whatever fool manages to stand in the wrong spot.

For 1,425 BV I quite like this. ECM is nice, ECM on mobile packages with functional guns and good utility options is very nice. I may not use C3 very often but this seems like a competent, robust, self-protected spotter which is always a good thing to have around for the job.

I dig it, good guns, good armor, good operations. Cash.

Liberty’s Rating: C+. Very usable, very cool, probably rather fun. There are better spends of this money out there but I think this isn’t a bad one for it.

GLD-7R

I’m Heavy. I’ve been eating my Wheaties. I’ve been hitting myself with hammers. I’m a real hard-head, I tell you what.

The 7R is… a real weird dude rolling off the lines of Star-Corps’ Son-Hoa facility. It’s doing some familiar things, namely being a trooper, but the manner in which it is doing it is rather odd.

For one, its armor value is down to 71% coverage, a mere 132 pips. Two, it’s got TSM while having a load of weapons that on a running alpha will send it up the requisite 9 heat so at least that works. We’ve got a Plasma Rifle with 2 tons of ammo, a pair of ER Medium Lasers, a pair of ER Small Lasers, and an SRM 4 with 1 ton of ammo. Unfortunately one of these is a plasma rifle and another is an SRM which means there is a timer on that TSM clock which is… inconvenient. 

Six improved jump jets get this thing moving quite well, while the TSM gives it a fair bit more mobility than the 5R before it which makes for a rather cool sounding ‘Mech so I’ve gotta ask:

Why is this thing 1,890 BV?

Simple: That low armor percentage? That’s hardened armor. In actuality this is a 142% armored 55 tonner which is fucking scary. A Light Engine and an XL Gyro is buying us weight here while propelling us to the somewhat inconvenient movement brackets of 4(5)/5(7)/6 which is… not great for BV calculations if we’re honest.

Liberty Rating: C. Very cool build and I like the idea but it feels a touch short of the capacity it could be achieving.

GLD-7R/SF

Very nice, Son Hoa, very nice. Let’s see Paul Sea Foxes’ ‘Mech.

Ok, take a 7R, now make it have a touch less armor, 128 total pips of hardened, keep the same 10 double heat sinks, engine, and gyro, and now give it clan grade guns. Namely an LB-5 with 2 tons of ammo, an SRM-6 with 2 tons of ammo, and a pair of Improved Heavy Medium Lasers.

Cool!

Note: That’s 19 heat on a ‘Mech that sinks 20. Also note that to maintain the TSM once you’ve got it spun up you’ve gotta jump and drop the LB-5 to maintain the heat. It’s… weird and strange. Leave it to the Clanners to fuck up using TSM, I suppose?

For a moderately more affordable price of 1,748 BV this isn’t that bad if you’re just looking for a tough brick with CASE II on its ammo to keep it trucking then honestly you could do worse. Jump around, try to break shins with your 22 damage kicks, and get to vibin’ I suppose!

Liberty’s Rating: C+. The guns are better than its predecessor so it’s a touch better on the scale. I could see this being a pain in the ass to put down, especially with the coming explosion changes, and would honestly hate to have to deal with more than one of these things.

Kell Hounds Gladiator. Credit: Jack Hunter

GLD-9SF

I’m a dozer. A real piece of work equipment. A man meant to work, suffer, and kill. Anyways… You wanna buy some mufflers?

This is a further development of the Foxes version of the 7R that is meant for Solhama troops to be able to use and defend their ships or ports with. It loses the Hardened armor and the IJJs while retaining its TSM, moving its movement profile up to 4(5)/6(8)/4.

Armament wise we’ve got a Clan ERPPC with a Capacitor, a Small Pulse Laser, an SRM 4 with 2 tons of ammo, a pair of Flamers, and a fluid gun with two tons of ammo. The real guns are made more accurate via the application of a T-Comp and CASE II is protecting the SRM 4 ammo.

Also it has a pair of Lift Hoists. Ya know, for doing work! Not for stealing Salvage! Never for stealing Salvage! No Sir!

So, what do you pay for this work ‘Mech? Oh? The most of any Gladiator, you say? 2,119 BV, you say?

To the little flower patch out back behind the shed, you say?

Liberty’s Rating: D-. I ain’t payin’, all that.

Peri Quote of the day: PPC with Capacitor you say? F. F. None of you are free from F related sins. Straight to the F zone.

GLD-1R(Keller)

Have you considered that I’ve got a friend in your zipcode? They see you and that means I see you.

The Keller is a 5R. It’s a 5R that existed 4 years before the 5R did. Instead of an ECM, Artemis for the MML, and a C3s it has a C3i, a capacitor on the SPPC, an armored cockpit, and a supercharger… that’s it. Also its missing 4 heat sinks, ya know, because it definitely didn’t need those.

Also its 1,553 BV.

Just take a 5R.

Liberty’s Rating: D-. I don’t like Capacitors, I don’t like ‘is more expensive’, and I don’t want ‘devices that break my ‘Mech’ like Superchargers and MASC. Stop doing it on slow ‘Mechs, its bad for their health.

Final Thoughts

Gladiators are… interesting. They’re the most explored of the ‘first ‘Mechs’ and its rather interesting that some of the primary explorators of that chassis exist in the Sea Foxes and the Marian Hegemony. I’m surprised we don’t have something for light hunting, or a later era take on the LRM one that uses weight saving tech to make itself into a good body guard for a bombardment lance or something.

Hell, considering the Marian’s interest in the thing I’m surprised we don’t have a hell-boat Rocket Launcher carrier.

Until next time! Fair weather, good seas and happy fighting-for-the-entertainment-of-fat-emperors-and-noble-assholes!
