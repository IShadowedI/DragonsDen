Prepare For Any Kind of Adventure With These D&D Campaign Guides

Meaghan Colleran
5 Minute Read
Jun 4 2026

These D&D adventure guides will help you take your players on quests without having to prep for every possibility yourself.

Even if you don’t use them yourself, adventure guides are a massively helpful part of D&D. These adventures have all of the twists, turns, NPCs, and locations plotted out for you, as well as ways to react to a variety of player reactions. Sometimes, future write-everything-themselves DMs will get their DMing legs with pre-written adventures. Others may use one of these adventures as a jumping-off point. But even on their own, adventure books can have your next fulfilling and fun quest standalone quest.

Curse of Strahd

One of the most famous (or infamous) adventures, Curse of Strahd, brings your players to Barovia, where everything is a little spooky. Count Strahd von Zarovich resides in Castle Ravenloft, and rumor has it that he’s a vampire. This module and setting are known for having all of the classic horror-movie aspects you could hope for. From monsters to haunted castles, and an endless string of foggy nights, it’s all in Barovia.

Under raging storm clouds, the vampire Count Strahd von Zarovich stands silhouetted against the ancient walls of Castle Ravenloft. Rumbling thunder pounds the castle spires. The wind’s howling increases as he turns his gaze down toward the village of Barovia. Far below, yet not beyond his keen eyesight, a party of adventurers has just entered his domain. Strahd’s face forms the barest hint of a smile as his dark plan unfolds. He knew they were coming, and he knows why they came — all according to his plan. A lightning flash rips through the darkness, but Strahd is gone. Only the howling of the wind fills the midnight air. The master of Castle Ravenloft is having guests for dinner. And you are invited.

Dragon Delves

For players of almost any adventuring level, this is a collection of ten different dragon-themed adventures. Throughout, you’ll meet many different kinds of dragons, learn their secrets, and see what kind of hoard they prefer to keep. If you think that your average Dungeons & Dragons campaign could use a little more dragon, this is the adventure book for you.

Meet the dragons of D&D in Dragon Delves, a collection of 10 dragon-themed adventures for characters level 1 through 12. Featuring artwork rendered in 10 unique styles, these ready-to-play adventures include streamlined prep and quick start guides for Dungeon Masters. Delve into mysterious dungeons, explore captivating fantasy locations, and come face to face with all the chromatic and metallic dragons, each with unique temperaments, alignments, and magic.

Eve of Ruin

Unlike some of the other adventures, this one is specifically meant for higher-level adventures with a minimum recommended level of 10. And that makes sense, because in this adventure, you’re trying to stop the infamous and terrifying Vecna. In this multiverse-spanning adventure, Vecna is trying to get rid of the gods and control everything that remains in all of reality. But no pressure.

The notorious lich Vecna is weaving a ritual to eliminate good, obliterate the gods, and subjugate all worlds. To stop Vecna before he remakes the universe, the heroes work with three of the multiverse’s most famous archmages, travel to far-flung locales, and rebuild the legendary Rod of Seven Parts. Vecna: Eve of Ruin is a high-stakes Dungeons & Dragons adventure in which the fate of the multiverse hangs in the balance. The heroes begin in the Forgotten Realms and travel to Planescape, Spelljammer, Eberron, Ravenloft, Dragonlance, and Greyhawk as they race to save existence from obliteration.

The Wild Beyond the Witchlight

Every few years, the carnival comes to town. Only, this carnival is a gateway to the Feywild, where nothing is as you’d expect it to be on the Material Plane. This adventure is easy to drop into any existing campaign, as long as your players have happened upon one of the carnival’s stops.

Once every eight years, the fantastic Witchlight Carnival touches down on your world, bringing joy to one settlement after the next. Its owners, Mister Witch and Mister Light, know how to put on a good show. But there’s more to this magical extravaganza than meets the eye!

The carnival is a gateway to a fantastic Feywild domain unlike anything found on the Material Plane. Time has not been kind to this realm, however, and dark days lie ahead unless someone can thwart the dastardly schemes of the Hourglass Coven.

Journeys Through the Radiant Citadel

Another anthology book, this one has thirteen standalone adventures for any players up to level fourteen. Travelers come from everywhere to visit to Radiant Citadel. So you never know who you’ll meet, where they’ll be from, and what new information or adventure they’ll have for you.

An anthology of thirteen stand-alone adventures set in wondrous lands for the world’s greatest roleplaying game. Journeys through the Radiant Citadel is a collection of thirteen short, stand-alone D&D adventures featuring challenges for character levels 1-14. Each adventure has ties to the Radiant Citadel, a magical city with connections to lands rich with excitement and danger, and each can be run by itself or as part of an ongoing campaign. Explore this rich and varied collection of adventures in magical lands.

Through the mists of the Ethereal Plane shines the Radiant Citadel. Travelers from across the multiverse flock to this mysterious bastion to share their traditions, stories, and calls for heroes. A crossroads of wonders and adventures, the Radiant Citadel is the first step on the path to legend. Where will your journeys take you?

Quests from the Infinite Staircase

Climb up the Infinite Staircase where the impossible becomes possible, and doorways lead to entirely other worlds. This adventure can be played by a huge variety of players with level recommendations spanning from first to thirteenth. But the stairs are long, and you’ll never know what you’ll find on the next level, so make sure your party is prepared for anything.

Quests from the Infinite Staircase embraces the magic of the most beloved Dungeons & Dragons adventures of all time. Your journey begins in the Infinite Staircase, a dreamlike expanse with doors leading to fantastical worlds, where you’ll meet Nafas, a noble genie who fulfills wishes with the help of valiant heroes—like you! As you explore what lies beyond each door, embark on timeless adventures and make memorable moments with your party. This handpicked collection contains 6 remastered first edition adventures, complete with striking settings and a lovable cast of characters. These unforgettable quests can be run individually or as a delightful campaign that takes characters from level 1 to 13.
