Trench Crusade Warband Focus: Black Klismos of Envy

October 30th, 2026 via Goonhammer

With the Court of the Seven Headed Serpent getting a massive October rules rework, Tabletop Battles is writing new subfaction focuses on each of the Sins, as they all dramatically change the way the warband plays. Perhaps the most dramatic changes are found in the Black Klismos of Envy. Envy focuses on Hell Knights, board control, positioning, and unique wargear options. Let's dig in!

The Lore

Moloch is the current Serpent Head of Envy, sitting on the Hellish council that dictates the course of the war against Creation for the Infernal forces. Jealousy and bitterness drive the forces sworn to his service, and now the upstart usurper Leviathan has been unleashed by the actions of the Scaled Saint Bartolomeo, and some of Moloch’s forces have betrayed him for a new master.

Unable to countenance any who might challenge their power, both Moloch and Leviathan spurn the more powerful lieutenants that serve the other Serpent Heads of the Court, instead relying on legions of bickering Hell Knights and serried ranks of Desecrated Saints.

Defining Traits of the Black Klismos of Envy

The first thing you’ll notice is that there are no Praetors or Sorcerers allowed in this list. Instead, we must take at least two Hell Knights and can take up to five. This can be disconcerting as, on its face, it seems like you’re losing your most powerful pieces. However, getting more access to Hell Knights allows for a lot of tactical and battlekit flexibility.

Losing access to the LEADER keyword (outside of taking a Troop Flag) can be a bit of a blow, and you’ll need to husband your resources and models carefully to make sure you aren’t taking morale checks at a significant disadvantage.

Two of your Hell Knights can also take battlekit stolen from a different warband! This opens up a few different attractive options, which we’ll cover in a bit.

We also get access to up to two Desecrated Saints! This is a huge boon as the specialized Saint aura for Envy, Maw of Comparison, is incredibly strong and allows you to lock down lanes on a battlefield exceptionally well. Desecrated Saints are powerful centerpieces in any Court warband, and getting two of them is amazing.

Uniquely, Desecrated Saints of Envy can take Iron Maiden Flails for 40 ducats, instead of the Glory cost (and limitation to Hell Knights). Their Iron Maiden Flails gain the INNATE keyword and the FIRE keyword, adding some utility to these powerful SIN marker-generating weapons. With the Saints’ NEGATE CUMBERSOME they also can be wielded without sacrificing an arm for additional weapons.

Envy’s SIN generation mechanics are a lot of fun and very characterful. At the top of every round, your Hell Knight with the lowest number of SIN markers gets a SIN marker, a sort of rolling version of Beacon of Corruption, for free. Additionally, every time one of your models with the DEMON keyword knocks down or takes out of action an enemy model, every DEMON within 6” of the DEMON who did the deed gets a SIN marker. This incentivizes you to keep your minis in small groups together so that you can piñata SIN markers whenever anything gets done.

Having two Desecrated Saints also helps with SIN generation, as Saints give any DEMON that activates within 1” of them a SIN marker (including themselves). Deploy your warband in a pair of tight groups, each around a Saint, to maximize initial SIN markers.

Additionally, we get our very own very special Glorious Deed every game, Petty Noble: the first time one of our Hell Knights gets to six SIN markers, we get a Glory. There is honestly no reason you should not be able to get this every game with some careful positioning.

Battlekit Highlights

One of the first Battlekit decisions you’ll make is what to snatch from other warbands for 2 of your Hell Knights. You have to choose a different item for each knight, so there are some interesting choices to make.

Your best bet is the Punt Gun. Its keywords fit well with a melee focused stat line that will want to be moving up the field. Hell Knights have a +0 ranged stat, so a SHOTGUN keyword with +1 DICE is attractive to make sure you hit. The ability to add BLAST is huge as well, as this synergizes extremely well with the Blood Magic Goetic spell all your Knights get for free, adding  +1 INJURY DICE to everything hit by the blast. There’s not really anything else available that has all the pieces the Punt Gun does to be a great fit for a Hell Knight of Envy.

A good melee option is an Anti-Tank Hammer. Court isn’t exactly lacking for good melee weapons, but you’re going to have a lot of Hell Knights and Saints and the AT Hammer is a great option that gets good use out of Blood Magic to one-hit KO enemies.

There are other decent choices out there, but many ranged weapons will struggle with the +0 ranged stat of the Hell Knight. I think an argument can be made for a Grenade Launcher or Satchel Charge to help even the odds against warbands with higher model counts. You could also invest in a consumable item like Martyrdom Pills to make an extremely hardy Knight, but keep in mind you’ll need to rebuy those every game in a campaign.

As for our own kit, we don’t go in for fancy laser rifles or terrain destroying lances or giant cleavers or guns shooting money here in the Black Klismos. Instead our special Battlekit is a stone knife, a barbed wire garrote, and a shield made of Fool’s Gold.

The Lying Knife is an intriguing melee weapon. It's RISKY, and gives +1 INJURY MODIFIER, but it also can borrow a keyword from any melee weapon held by an enemy you’re within 1” of. This means that you’ll need to put it on a model that can dictate who you’re going into combat with, which is why I like to put it on a Hunter of the Left Hand Path. At 12 ducats it's only two ducats more than a great hammer, for functionally the same base abilities in a one-handed package. A good deal!

The Usurper’s Saw Wire is a fun bit of kit you can use to swap out one of your normal attacks for a +2 INJURY DICE attack if the enemy model has the ELITE keyword (or is on a base the same size or bigger than yours). Put it on a Desecrated Saint and whomp on an enemy who you’ve set up for a Blood Bath attack to seal the deal. In addition, if you take the enemy out you generate two SIN markers.

Pyrite Mirror Pavaise is a difficult item to use. It’s an expensive Trench Shield at 30 ducats that lets you use a special ability of a model you’re within 1” of. With some of Envy’s movement shenanigans (specifically Coveted Position) it’s great if you can work your way into combat with something like an Observer or a War Prophet or Lieutenant, or another Court model with a cool Goetic Spell, but it's very situational for the same price as three Trench Shields.

Brand of Moloch lets you kill a friendly Wretched to power up a Goetic Spell instead of spending SIN Markers. Depending on your sin generation game and what powers you’ve unlocked for your warband this can be useful if you feel like spending Wretched instead of SIN.

Craven Vassalage is the go-to Glory item. It lets one of your Hell Knight FLY, and turn into a little bit of a Sorcerer or Praetor (depending on whether you align with Moloch or Leviathan). I’d go for the Sorcerer upgrade, as Burning Inferno is a great spell.

The Magic of Charlatans

Coveted Position

Our number one spell. Coveted Position lets you swap models’ physical positions on the battlefield for two SIN (within 12”). If you’ve ever played any MOBA ever you know this is the most bonkers ability for a pick. You can put your Hell Knight where you want them to set up Punt Gun shots or long range charges, and stick an enemy right where you want them. There’s also no restriction on yeeting yourself or an enemy model straight into combat range.

Meat Shield

If there’s one thing Envy loves doing, it's killing Wretched. Stick 'em in an Iron Maiden, kill them to cast a spell, or, with Meat Shield, make them take a hit for you.

What’s Yours Is Mine

Another very situational spell. If you’re playing against someone who generates a lot of BLESSING Markers it might be nice, or if you're playing another Court player you can zero-sum away some SIN. I’m not a huge fan of this spell.

Playing the Warband

We've found Envy lists to be fairly tall. Focus on getting a few Hell Knights, definitely take both Saints right off the rip, then fill in with a Hunter of the Left Hand Path and Pit Locusts.

For your Hell Knights, lean into what they’re good at. Give them a decent melee weapon like an Anti-Tank Hammer or a Hellblade (save your Malebranche Sword for a Saint) and give them ranged weapons that won’t penalize them for shooting as well as a half-blind New Antioch conscript, like a Punt Gun or Ophidian Rifle.

Your Saints are your big heavy hitters. Give one of them the Malebranche Sword and the other a Iron Maiden Flail to whale on your enemies. If you can afford to sacrifice a Wretched when you run into a heavily armored list, cram one into the flail to ignore armor and generate SIN. With their charge limiting aura, I also recommend taking a Tormentor Chain on a Saint to dictate the pace of the battle and positioning of enemy units.

You don’t have a LEADER and your Elites don’t have TOUGH, so be careful with your Hell Knights. They get an INNATE, IMPERVIOUS -2 armor stat which is great, but they’re still vulnerable to a random bolt action rifle shot, so pick your engagements and don’t rush in.

Keep your forces in small groups within 6” of each other so that any time one model knocks an enemy down or takes them out, you give multiple models SIN.  With decent positioning and play you should end up with more than enough SIN throughout the game.

In objective based missions you’ll suffer from the same problems as other Court warbands, getting out activated and out captured by the enemy, so you need to focus on killing enemies to force them off objectives and hopefully break their morale. Bully enemy models and don’t let yourself get spread thin. Envy has a lot of tools to dictate positioning, and it's our strength.

Troops

Battlekit: Tormentor Chain, Malebranche Sword, Trench Club

Desecrated Saint - 158D | 3G

Battlekit: Tormentor Chain, Hellblade, Trench Club

Battlekit: Reinforced Armour

Battlekit: Reinforced Armour

Final Thoughts

Playing Envy is a huge departure from how most Court lists will play. Your list will be incredibly brittle, with limited TOUGH, no LEADER, and expensive mid tier elites that will do all your heavy lifting. But the list is very strong, focusing on board control and tools that are hard for your opponent to anticipate. Envy has one of the trickier SIN marker generation mechanics, but by exploiting your positioning and wargear advantages they can generate more SIN than they can reasonably spend. Envy is one of the most characterful and fun subfactions to play, especially when you lean into your theme.

And remember, when in doubt, if you’re losing, it’s clearly the fault of how underpowered and unfair your warband is in comparison to everyone else, who gets cooler toys and models than you ever will. Complain about this loudly and at length, as Moloch would want you to.
