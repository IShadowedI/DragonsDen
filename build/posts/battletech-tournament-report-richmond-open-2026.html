BattleTech Tournament Report: Richmond Open 2026

June 12th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Howdy ‘Mechwarriors and welcome back!

To the Richmond Open.

That’s right we’re right back where we started a year ago and looking to get to it once again! Myself, Peri, Lynn, and our buddies Connor and Tentacle, went on back to the strange world of Richmond to compete in the yearly games to see what all we could drum up! It’s been a full year of tournament reports now, with 7 in the books and this one marking the 8th. I’ve made a good run of it so far and am quite pleased with how its all gone!

Now, this year we didn’t participate in any of the shenaniganary around the tournament, no we just got straight into it! In keeping with that sentiment let’s get this show on the road!

The Richmond Open

The Richmond open is, apparently, not an ‘alright’ sized event as I had said last year. The past year of tournaments and events has shown me that it’s actually quite large for a BT tourney and pretty damned big for everything else! We’re back once again with a good clip of players, many old familiar faces, and plenty of new ones!

So, what were the rules?

Well, as Lynn so aptly put it in her report, equal parts refreshing and perplexing. Thiel is a damned good TO and gives up his missions in advance which allows you to prep well in advance and build a force suited for the tasking. Ya know, like ya should.

That said, our shenanigans over the past year may have taught our good TO a few things and as such there’s some real odd ball constraints here. The Rule of Two stands as one of them, knocking you to only two ‘mechs with any amount of -2 modifiers to fire, be it a singular small pulse laser, Medium Re-engineered with a TC, or a Dire Wolf E romping onto the field full of hate and malice. Or only one thing with a -3, be it a singular small pulse laser on something with a TC for its other items or an Iron Cheetah B. As well we had some movement limitations, only allowing two instances of  anything capable of a +4 TMM and only one of +5.

With 7K BV these are some… interesting constraints to work around but I’m sure we can make something functional and applicable to really get our money’s worth out of it.

—

The List

So I’ll be honest at the get: I did it again.

I was working for quite a while to try for a return to form. A real book-end on the year’s tournaments by bringing along the GMGM again. I had a fun list but it lacked some things I wanted. With the rule of two taking away any real consideration for the MAD-5T, or the Nightstar due to its ancillary pulse lasers, I had something that worked but it didn’t have the funny that my soul craved.

Now, this would be fine, and my list of a Warlord 2G, Templar III OA- with attached Grenadier II As again -, Rakshasa 1Ar, and Stealth 1D would probably have been quite a time and a lot of meat for someone to chew on…

But in all honesty I was failing to find a good name. ‘The Union Cards’ was about the best I could come up with and I couldn’t really lodge it in my heart as something I was happy with.

Then it struck, inspiration in the stupidest of margins as I sat there priming and painting my Ravens for the Viking Funeral: The Two Shoe Beating.

Do you like ATMs, reader?

Do you like stupid 4/6 heavy ‘Mechs loaded to the gills with ATMs, reader?

I do. I think they’re very very funny.

So let’s meet some!

It's them Bird Cowboys, Credit: Liberty

Behold, the Two Shoe Beating.

Star Commander Abilene ‘Iron-Hand’ McKenna rides out in her Nova Cat E at 4/5. An old staple of ‘fuck you, die’ machines the Nova Cat E is a bad bitch mounting 4 ATM 9s, with 8 fucking tons of ammo, and a large pulse laser. While some mild heat concerns keeps you from taping a brick to the firing studs the steady ‘3 ATMs and an LPL’ firing pattern still hits like a damned truck.

Mechwarrior Daniel ‘Sundance’ stumbles to the field in his Small Cockpit equipped Loki MK II C fielding 4 ATM-12s and a significant heat debt of 8+movement if it fires them all off. Honestly I’ve been wanting to throw this thing at people’s heads for so long it may have just wormed its way into my fucking dreams and demanded to be here.

Mechwarrior Cassidy ‘Blackwood’ zips to field in her Phantom C. Yes I used one last tournament. No I’m not apologizing. I needed a fast boy to bring friends to field.

The Friends: A point of Salamanders, affectionately named the Honey Bees, at 4/3. Stand in the wrong spot, shit head, I fucking dare you. They roll in on the Phantom so they can be dumped on a point and vibrate aggressively at anything that has the poor mental faculty to even think that they can come near it.

Mechwarrior Vinson ‘Head Check’ barrels into the competition with unlimited shoulder checks in his Dasher II. A 6/3 pilot his real job is ‘I had the BV to make you better at doing objectives and not eating shit… if you can throw hands or shoulder check someone off a point we’ll take it’.

And of course, Star Colonel Liberty McKenna stands just off the field observing this Star’s performance so that he may better organize his Battle Cluster.

Let’s see how they do!

—

The Fit

I’m a creative man, don’t put in the Tournament Report that I’m not! Ok, I wore the same outfit again. I think it’s kinda just the standard for my Ravens and Marshals at this point. That said I do intend to put together a better dress-uniform kinda thing for my Alliance forces, once I get the chance… and some information on what their fuckin’ dress uniform actually looks like.

Starting off we’ve got the familiar pair of boots, handmade leather belt, a good pair of gray canvas-y jeans, and properly fitted black Stetson Bozeman, top it off with a slick handlebar mustache and my white bloused button up with the sleeves rolled up and you’ve got a good look! Toss on my Marty McFly poncho and we’ve got a good fit!

—

Round 1: Bird Cowboy’s Come ‘Round!

Round 1 and we’re playing King of the Hill on a very obstructed desert map. This year Thiel has put KoTH as a short edge deployment which is interesting but largely fine! My first round opponent was a wonderful player by the name of Ranger! Ranger is a reader. How do I know that Ranger is a reader? Well, I asked him.

But what made me think he was a reader?

THAT FUCKING TENSHI IS BACK AGAIN.

God those are fuckin' beautiful, Credit: Liberty

Ranger here is playing a Draconis Combine list, and did a fucking astounding paint job. Look at that shit, good god. This won best painted by the way; Christ alive am I jealous of that brush work.

We’ve got a Tenshi OA at 4/4, an Archer 9K at 4/4,an Avatar OH at 4/4, a Tarantula ZPH-1 at 4/4, a Locust 7V2 at 4/4, and a squad of IS Standard Mags at 3/4. This is a good list with some nasty weight and firepower in it. That said not all of it wants to be close to things, barring the Tenshi, so this mission forcing us into a tight engagement for the point will likely do me some good in trying to get the ATMs to bare.

Round 1 there are literally no shots and Ranger begins dumping excess ammo from several ‘Mechs as I am unable to actually do anything to keep him from doing anything. Round 2 the Phantom manages a good grab at the Tarantula, opening up some spots but doing little more than that as he misses just about everything fired back at me.

Round 3 things go a little worse. I lose initiative and end up getting the Phantom leg attacked, which blows its hip, slowing it considerably. The rest of the fray is developing as the fight closes in hard and fast.

Oh we in it now, Credit: Liberty

Then things start happening. The Dasher slams itself into the back for the Tenshi, knocking the bigger ‘Mech on its face as the Phantom gets to work on the opposing Locust. The Loki, for its part… Fires 4 ATMs at the Archer, it lands all of them and brutalizes armor off the machine while the Nova Cat finishes it off.

But uh… well the 9K is the MML one, and he has SRM-Is loaded. I go up 10 heat from my normal firing course on the Loki MK II, seeing the tantalizing chance to do that much damage on 5s will often get my ass, and it certainly paid off. But it also netted me a total of 25 heat into the red.

Uh-Oh.

The Loki has two checks of import here, a shutdown on an 8+ and an explosion on a 6+... and the golden bastard passes them both. While it won’t be doing anything next turn its still there, and more importantly it’s not immobile so it’ll still take some degree of luck to hit it!

Moving forward we have the Phantom cut the Locust down the rest of the way, sawing its CT in half while the Loki has a very bad no-good day. Let’s detail what happens to the man this turn:

  • A leg attack
  • 2 LVSPs
  • An HPPC
  • Two MML-3 LRM hits

The Right torso is open, the arm got 12d off, and the ‘Mech is in shambles. But it’s still upright, and it no longer has the heat problem now that the 4th ATM is gone.

Time to get back into it.

The Nova Cat elects to obliterate the opposing BA, wiping them out in one turn while the rest of the list that isn’t a Loki begins beating on the Tenshi and the Avatar starts making steam towards the Archer across the water.

The Loki takes another pair of LVSPs and just keeps going. Small Cockpits ain't shit.

The Phantom, with his busted hip takes an HPPC and two MML 3 hits which tops a PSR… a PSR that he fails. A PSR that results in him TACing his own leg to break the upper leg actuator alongside the hip in the right leg. The armor isn’t even broken yet, this is some bullshit!

The Dasher, somehow, misses a charge into the Archer’s back on a 4 and as such does not slam it forward into the water. He then gets juked next turn because my opponent wins initiative and as such holds the avatar until I’m forced to move the Dasher.

In the final two rounds the Tenshi receives the full Two Shoe Beating and is made into scrap. Alongside this the Phantom falls over… and lands on its CT… which crits out the last engine slot. What a bad and long way to die. The Loki isn’t even crippled and he’s been getting his ass kicked all game. Get it together Mechwarrior Cassidy, God.

I win the round having done a significant amount of capture points and direct fire damage. The final score is 114-6. Unfortunately this is over before I manage to put down the Tarantula for the meme.

Round 2: Birdy Didn’t Mind No Sideways Talk

Well, this one’s gonna fuckin’ suck.

Round 2 is against Peri. Peri’s list was a known factor, we’d tested it enough to know just about exactly what it did to my list: It shuts it down. They’re faster than me, have better big boys than I do, and most importantly one of those big boys has ECM. So the ATMs are not doing nearly as much as they should be.

Their list, for brevity so they can explain it further in their own article, consists of an Iron Cheetah C, a Blood Asp C, and a pair of Fireballs. I feel great hate for this list, I tell you what.

That Place Peri Shot Me Dead, Credit: Liberty

We were originally going to end up on the same map I had just been on but to avoid this Thiel just had us flip the table marker around and shift over to the next map. The mission is the one we had taken to calling ‘blow up buildings’. This year that mission has a twist we hadn’t noticed until the night before: The buildings explode now. For 20 damage, specifically called out as AE damage. Which means that if my BA are near it when it goes off they just die.

I deemed this an acceptable loss.

I’ll just cut the chase on the objectives: The Fireballs, Phantom, BA, and Dasher II did nothing for the fight here for quite some time. The Fireballs both went to go gang up on one of my buildings while the Dasher just started circle charging his head into one of the other ones for a few rounds, slowly but surely ablating his own ‘Mech away around him. The Phantom blitzed the Salamanders across the Map where they were dropped off and summarily obliterated the building on their own in one turn… and turned to dust along with it, while the Phantom moved on to go break the other one.

Stop standing still you coward! Credit: Liberty

I blow up 3 buildings to Peri’s 2.

Unfortunately this comes down to the fight.

On turn 1 the Nova Cat eats shit and falls after taking a withering amount of fire to my one ATM and LPL connecting on the way out. This bodes well. It then fails to properly get up and eats it a second time. Second turn comes around and it takes a truly savage beating being out of position, in no good range bands for its ATMs, and with no TMM.

It dies on turn 2 to an ammo explosion which shears the side torso off and finishes the engine destruction started by significant CT trauma.

The Loki MK II advances in its stead and takes way longer than it had any right to to go down. It does not die until something like round 5, doing all that it could the entire way through. The Dasher II, after having sufficiently beaten itself half to death and the building fully to death, screams in and tries to body check the Blood Asp… This does not work. He explodes into confetti that bounces off the beaten Blood Asp and

It suffers an ammo explosion and only dies when the CT is finally driven out of it. It manages to, barely, take the Blood Asp with it. A Blood Asp that did not go unconscious when in one turn it went to a 10+ consciousness check.

Yea, sure.

Eventually the Loki does indeed succumb and leaves me with not but the Phantom. Against a pair of Fireballs, that have yet still not busted down their own target building, and a full health Iron Cheetah C… Well, fuck. It’s 99-54 right now. I just need to kill something.

In my… greed is not the right word, perhaps restraint(?), I make a mistake here: I send the Phantom after the Fireballs instead of the Cheetah. The Fireballs are too far to get to in one turn, and I can get behind the Cheetah, and hopefully do some damage that might cripple it and put me over. Instead I waste a turn running after the Fireballs and doing nothing due to range. I also misunderstood what Peri meant about how much longer their target buildings had left in them and as such watch as the Cheetah and the fireballs all pop their buildings in the same turn while I kill one of the Fireballs.

The score ends 104-114, Peri.

I am beaten. Defeated. Laid low. Killed by, as Peri put it, ‘Comrade D6’. Damnation.

Round 3: A Worm on the Rocks

Busted down for my sins I find myself against… Lynn? Ah! Time for a round two from Southern Assault V!

Our mission is Karnov Down, on a map that I only described as ‘Stack Hell on one side, and it’s mine.’ This is a short edge mission that, as Lynn described in her report, can be made and broken on side decisions. She is running a Clan Sea Fox Trading Group consisting of a nasty cohort of ‘Mechs, one of which I can only blame myself for. The dreaded Tiburon 2 sallies forth alongside a Mad Cat III 2, a Phantom E, a squad of 4/3 Salamanders, and a big old Gargoyle G.

I also pushed myself harder than I'd ever pushed before on my paint scheme, doing the Hard Mode version of Spina Khanate! Credit: Lynn B.

I’ve got the damage here but she has some real movement advantage.

Certainly that plus the map won’t come back to bite me in the ass. Or the fact that my only real choice of where I could put the objective- which I definitely won’t blame on just straight up not really being able to put anything anywhere else that Lynn couldn't possibly get to first -and thereby taking the first spot that looked serviceable with a 2 turn path in… and the misunderstanding about how picking up and extracting the VIPs worked…

This is probably fine, Credit: Liberty

Really just a whole thing.

By turn 3 Lynn has acquired all 3 of her VIPs and is getting the extra points for extracting them. I meanwhile still only have one of them in my hands, the hands of my Dasher II… A Dasher II who’s leg is about to fly off from a double tap by the damnable Tiburon’s Improved Heavy Medium Lasers. This is fine, I hammer it in return and I’ll just have the Phantom go ahead and move to take the VIP off of him and pick one up off the Karnov as well. This is fine, I can work with thi- GOD DAMMIT.

Can you guys please get the governor faster? I think those Foxes over the hill are a bit upset we didn't buy any warranties. Credit: Liberty

The Phantom’s leg takes another hip crit from a TAC and it takes significant overall damage.

OK how about we give them to the Nova Cat instead.

Cue the Nova Cat fighting a hasty, very stupid, retreat for the next several turns while the Gargoyle chases after it and the busted Phantom, Salamanders, and Loki MK II do everything in their power to keep things from getting after them. This several round exchange results in the Dasher, the Tiburon, the Mad Cat MK III, the Gargoyle, and my Phantom dying. The Gargoyle, dying in the worst fashion as it gets no TMM next to a Loki MK II C that stood still and decides that it is indeed its time to die. It eats all 4 boxes and gets the arm drilled open which sets off a full tilt chain detonation that blows all 3 APGRs, turning the warrior within into jelly.

Free in the Harbor, Credit: Liberty

This leaves the last remaining Sea Fox Unit as the Salamanders.

The Nova Cat starts taking big steps and on the last turn it barely makes it off the table.

With one movement point to spare. I planned this. This was intentional. I definitely 100 percent meant for this to be exactly that tight… Totally.

I win, 121 - 88, successfully preventing Lynn from, as she put it on the car ride home, ‘Crab Bucketing me into the losers bracket’.

The Question

So, dear reader, you might be thinking ‘yea but Liberty, that’s round 3 and you lost to Peri! You’ve already lost!’

Fear not, Warrior, I am not yet beaten. Richmond Open this year is a FIVE round tournament. Originally meant to make it so that the 4 undefeated players would be able to determine a real winner this 4 man space of undefeated players was expanded to 5 due to the ever present inability of BattleTech TOs to say no to people who want to play. But… you can’t make a square bracket with 5 players, so a 6th slot must be made. A slot for the person who went 2/1 with the highest points.

Guess who that was.

It was me, coming in 26 points ahead of the guy with the most points in the undefeated bracket. Scoring over 100 in all 3 games will do that for you.

Well, that means I’m not out yet. Despite Peri’s best efforts I’m moving on, and they ain’t.

It’s Hog Killin’ Time.

Round 4: A Badman West of the Rio Grande

So we move on to Round 4 on Day Two. Our records have been reset and our Edge, which I had forgotten to use the day before, that may have saved my game against Peri, given back. But now we’re here! So it’s all in the past, black eye or not I’ve got lead in the holster and a need to draw like lightning.

Our opponent this round is Alex playing with a Taurian list full of AC/20s, infernos, and heavy armor. A Charger 1A5, A Cyclops 11H, A Sunder O, a Longbow 13C at 4/6, a Locust 7V2 at 6/4, and a squad of Marauder BA that are riding the Locust. Alex, if it’s not obvious, is also a reader… a Reader inspired by my own actions in Task Force Stampede.

Damnable Taurians, get off my lawn! Credit: Liberty

Why do I keep doing this to myself?

The Loki has to be very attentive to that heat scale and making sure it doesn’t get to close to the issues that can beat me down. Luckily, if I so please, I should be able to keep everything at arms reach with my Standard flights.

So, the opening to this sees my Phantom dumping his BA in cover on the right hand point as the enemy locust does the same on the center point. This elicits a reaction in two parts:

  • 1: The Nova Cat moves up into a position to put a heavy salvo into the BA
  • 2: The Dasher II moves full distance with 11 hexes traveled and charges the Locust in the face.
A Locust gets body checked clean into oblivion, Credit: Liberty

This results in the obliteration of both opposing assets as all fire directed at the Dasher goes wide and the Nova Cat obliterates the entire squad of Marauders in one volley. The Dasher connects its charge and rips the Locust clean in half in conjunction with the ground.

I score both of those points as the Dasher II does not fall over.

Let the fun begin.

The Loki and Nova Cat begin working over the Sunder in earnest while the Phantom and Dasher go hard in on the Sunder’s rear and side arc, resulting in the ‘Mech falling over and avoiding the charge. The Loki takes some licks in return, forcing it to back off behind some partial cover, hoping to bait the Charger out and off the left most point.

It doesn’t work, with the Charger happy to get up and stare intently at the Loki as the Longbow starts dumping its ammo. The sound of un-spent missiles hitting the ground triggers the Dasher II’s homing beacon like he’s a sidewinder staring at the sun but unfortunately it has no path for a rear charge and as such bypasses the Longbow and takes a gamble on 9s from an AC/20 in its rear from the Cyclops as it goes for a rear charge on the Charger.

It takes the AC/20 square in the CT and stops existing instantly.

You're not gonna kill me... OH N-, Credit: Liberty

The Charger continues to receive its beating, slowly but surely coming apart with one turn even recording EIGHT FUCKING CRITS to its AC/20. This is one, the most crits I’ve ever done in one turn, and two, the most crits I think an AC/20 has ever taken. It didn’t matter past a certain point but at another certain point I kinda just wanted to see if I could break the whole thing.

The turn after that the Charger suffers an ammo explosion and is sent into Low Earth Orbit while the Sunder and Cyclops begin aggressing the Nova Cat on its perch. I move the Loki up, score full on primaries and then in the last round as the Nova Cat is about to be blown away it resolves to do all it can to take the Sunder with it. I don't have it in my notes that either of them died so I'm pretty sure they made it out.

Oh lawd they comin', Credit: Liberty

I win 97-68.

Round 5: You Ought to Fear the Day El Patito Feo Flies By

Round 5, the final round, is against James and his Nova Cats! James here is also ATM maxxing by running another Nova Cat E, a Super Nova 2, an Adder E, and a Firemoth H. We are on one of the Savannah maps with a large river side on one end and some open area on the other. This is the ye-olde pickup boxes mission.

Oh, so this is the new and improved Savannah Hell! Credit: Liberty

I win initiative and choose the side that forces the big obstacle in his way rather than mine so I can drop a box over there for the Dasher to go grab and fiddle with for a few rounds while I get to work trying to pick things apart with the rest of the group.

Damnable Nova Cats AGAIN, Credit: Liberty

Round 1 his Fire Moth zips in on the box and immediately finds a Phantom C standing next to it with significant malice and hate in its eyes as it dumps a shitload of hate into it, immediately blowing a leg crit and knocking it over while it does little to nothing back at its better. While it doesn’t get kicked it does end up rather rapidly in a bad position as it does not end up with the box and needs to get moving.

I, unfortunately, suffer something far worse: A CT TAC to my Gyro on my Nova Cat. Something I should have burned Edge about but after having several near miss, and actual, ammo explosions over the last few games I decided to retain it and just hope. This pays off as the Nova Cat resists the concept of gravity and manages to stay upright.

Next turn the Firemoth, and the Salamanders, die together while the Loki begins to be picked apart by the Supernova and the enemy Nova Cat. The arm flies off with a 12, which sparks a mild argument about crits and limbs, and the armor suffers near breaches in several locations. For its part it shoots up the heat scale to absolutely bathe the enemy NC in fire from all 4 boxes. This also results in a double 5 point head hit on the NC, blowing out one of its sensors…

Next turn I allow the Nova Cat to get behind my own, discounting it on the back of its messed up sensors, as my Phantom screams in with the box and a hankering to try and put the offending heavy down. This turn has a lot of violence all at once. The Loki dies to a pelting of ATM HE fire from the Supernova taking out its engine. The enemy Nova Cat explodes, falling unconscious. The enemy Puma takes a gyro hit from over the shoulder fire from my Nova Cat’s ATMs and eats it to the floor. Both it, and my Nova Cat are having a bad time with busted Gyros and open CTs.

Oh god help, I've been discombobulated! Credit: Liberty

I have full scored primaries, I just need to do something to secure the path forward to a win.

The last turn comes upon us as my Nova Cat gutters out and dies at the same time as the enemy Puma while my Dasher II slams itself into the Super Nova’s chest.

This game ends with me winning 113-80. Of note: These two Nova Cats died in the exact same way. Both were salvageable but unconscious with gutted engines and gyros with ammo explosion damage. Shit was genuinely funny.

The Awards

So.

I lost a round.

But I didn’t lose the tournament.

That’s right, I won. I won over our buddy Connor by ONE FUCKING POINT. Peri shot me dead in the street and that shit didn’t stop me. I had the biggest shit eating grin you’ve ever seen on my face when we went to go get steaks on the first night, due to time constraints of travel plans unfortunately we could not do so after the second night.

I won a few fun things, for one my raffle tickets netted me both an Alpha Strike Missions card deck, which I traded to Connor because he likes them and wanted to adapt some to Classic, and something else… what was it?

Oh yeah, a fucking Boat.

bote, Credit: Liberty

Christ look at that thing. I love it.

As well I picked up another trophy for my shelf, one that I had mistakenly thought was a White Raven of some Raven Gamma Galaxy pilot, it’s not… it’s Brett Andrews’ Black Python, ya know, The Bloody IlKhan… possibly one of the worst men to ever visit his opinions on the Clans.

I STAND IN THIS TRIUMPH! ME! HERE AND NOW! Credit: Liberty

Uh, oops?

I also picked up an Eridani Light Horse box and a metal Thresher!

Good toys and things I fully intend to make use of!

Dinner

We ended up at a local place for dinner that was pretty good, if not a bit expensive, named Fire Birds! The steaks were pretty good as were the sides and apps! Not the best steak we’ve had but I wouldn’t be upset having one again! I uh- I forgot to take a picture...

Final Thoughts

So, that’s another year in the books. A year of games, competition, making friends, having fun, and going to fun new places.

If I’ve learned one thing over the past year it’s this: if you’re part of a community then be part of it. Go out there and find your people. Play games, go to tournaments, have fun and get to playing! Even if you don’t terribly care about the wins the act of going is still a great time! If you don’t have something like that local to you then be the catalyst for it! Spin up a small tournament and set it loose on your locals! Just get out there and do it.

After all, if no one else is doing it then I guess that just means that you have to!

Oh yeah, Connor and I decided to have it out as the top 2 from the tournament, I won that one too, and not by a little. In an unofficial way the two dudes at the top ended up even on our W/L so... funny!

Remember, even with this copious talk of tournaments, my adoration of competition, and gunning for a win, what matters is having a good time and doing it all with a smile on your face. All the ‘mech reviews, the pretty plastic and the shiny mathrocks aren’t worth a damn if you don’t use them to have fun with friends! So go grab a drink, a good pack of snacks, your favorite game partner(s), a box of funny ‘mechs, a cozy spot, and a fun map then get to making some good memories, at home or at a tournament!

Peri and I will be doing a ‘year in review’ here in a little while so keep an eye out for that!

Until next time! Fair weather, good seas and happy gamin’! Go tear shit up out there and have fun!
