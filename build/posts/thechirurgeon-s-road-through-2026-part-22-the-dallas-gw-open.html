TheChirurgeon's Road Through 2026, Part 22: The Dallas GW Open, Part 2

May 29th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive progress through 2026. Last time around I talked about the first day of the Dallas Open. Today I'm running through the rest of the games and the EXCITING FINISH to the event, and showing off my Red Corsairs with some new photos.

Day 2: Saturday

A touch of jet lag kept me from getting as much sleep as I wanted but I've had worse nights on the road. The big thing is that after a 9am start on Friday we get to be a little more relaxed, starting at 9:30 on Saturday. Dan and I grab bagels in the hotel lobby and head over. The Kill Team schedule is staggered from the 40k schedule; it's 11 games total, with four each on Friday and Saturday before a top 8 cut on Sunday.

Round 4: vs. David Spates' Tyranids



Round 4 sees me squaring off against Tyranids again. This time it's Crusher Stampede, and I'm a bit more worried about screamer killers, and it's a monster-heavy list that can give me real trouble.

It's a lot harder to ignore Norn Emissaries when there are two of them and only three objectives in the middle of the table. The good news is there are fewer targets. The bad news is there are more Tyrannofexes.

The Mission: Scorched Earth + Tipping Point



I'm going first. Not ideal but I can make it work and it'll get my deep strike units on the board early. My goal here is to use the Nurglings to block early movement and tag the Exocrines or Maleceptors with the Nurglings, where I can hopefully use their aura to stay alive and buy time to take down other targets. My goal is to control the upper left objective and the middle while forcing David off the third point. The Terminators here will be huge for getting me extra AP against the big bugs.



That ends up happening again here and I'm able to keep off primary the first three rounds of the game while scoring 10-15 of my own. I'm able to take down the first Tervigon pretty early and for the rest it's a game of careful selection and focusing. I'm only able to take down one of the Norn Emissaries, and the result is that I start to run out of steam late. The good is that by then it's too late to press forward and do much, so I take this one. David was a fun guy to play and I think he mostly suffered from not getting to aggressive in the middle of the game with his Norn and his Swarmlord, freeing me up to spend a turn whittling them down. This mission also gave 0 points for home objective control, so I brought the Cultists out early and used them along with the bikes and a Rhino to put 15 OC on the middle objective to stop him from scoring.

Result: 80-72, Win

Well, winning that game puts me at 3-1 and potentially in the top bracket, but a quick investigation of player scores shows that I'm stuck around 58th thanks to my opponents' win rates, meaning that I'm going to drop to the 3-1 bracket. The good news is that I make showcase, and that means 'm still in the running for Best Overall.



I have a quick sandwich lunch - turkey, cheese, lettuce, tomato, and mustard on a potato roll - outside, and then head back in. It's not the world's most filling lunch but it's solid and I don't feel like shit after eating it. The weather in Dallas was pretty great until Sunday and getting out during lunch was a very pleasant experience. May in Texas is usually pretty nice.

Gabe's list is just filled with all manner of bullshit. This thing does so much damage, but has some major issues in that it doesn't have a ton of units, and if they die he can be in some real trouble. So he needs to play a bit cagey. The big threat here is the Judiciar and his team, since there's very little I can do about a rapid ingress threat with Fights First - just have to hope I survive the encounter.

The Mission: Terraform + Crucible



Gabe gets first turn, which isn't so bad on Terraform, but he can't really afford to put his units out there in the open and actually do the action so he ends up largely standing still. I don't really have a choice here except to be aggressive so I spend my first turn moving up and terraforming all three midtable objectives, then I charge the combi-Lieutenant and Incursors with my Nurglings, hoping to tie them up for multiple turns. That works out OK.



On turn 2 Gabe takes shots at me but mostly ends up staging since he can't get anything in melta range of my Land Raider. I go full send and throw Bile and his brick at his Veterans, where they run into the Ingress Judiciar. I lose all but Bile and 3 Chosen to their attacks, but then they have a very solid clapback into the veterans and Bile manages to heroically hang on for like three turns, blanking damage and standing back up when I needed him to. That effort pays off huge as Huron and his crew in the middle of the table are able to slowly pick off more of Gabe's units.

This would end up being catastrophic for Gabe as he just couldn't score action secondaries when he drew them late game and getting triple Terraform on turn 1 largely meant he was never going to flip those, so I ended up maxing out primary by round 4.

Result: 89-51, Win

This wasn't a great matchup for me on the whole but the mission absolutely tipped it in my favor, letting me take advantage of the fact that my army is very good at scoring and denying primary. I take a quick break and then it's on to round 6.

Jeff's a mid-Atlantic guy who's been to a number of the Goonhammer Open events and over the last few years has really improved as a competitive player. He's a regular name at the top of GTs now and a tough opponent.

This is a nightmare list for me, full of things that can just rip apart my vehicles and cause me headaches in melee. I never enjoy playing into Sisters, where everything always feels like it balances on a knife's edge because they're such a weird glass cannon army.

The Mission: Take and Hold + Hammer and Anvil



I'm going second, which kind of helps but I'd have rather used the Nurglings as a tarpit on a turn 1 charge. Layout 8 should be good for me normally but against sisters I actually want longer firing lanes so I can throw lascannons out at multi-meltas. I don't have a great game plan into Jeff, but it's the same as usual: Dominate the objectives and try and drop him in melee early.



Jeff does a great job of blocking me out of the southern ruin at the start of the game and I'm unable to get off a good charge to go around and tag him, which leaves Bile's unit extremely vulnerable. I end up losing the bikes after they down a canoness at mid table, and the Nurglings die early. Jeff rambles forward with his Paragon warsuits - those are the key threats I need to down as quickly as possible.

In that regard, I do OK, throwing Huron's unit after them and a mortifier and Intranzia Fraye. I kill the Paragons and Vahl - who stands back up - but Fraye and the Mortifier hang on with 2 wounds - and would continue to do so for two more turns. My Sorcerer and his Terminators drop and kill the Predator in Jeff's backfield, but the Chosen go down quickly. Cypher shoots the Callidus to death when she pops up on top of the ruin in a nice little thematic quick draw shoot-out.



Things just went incredibly wrong for me after that, however. Bile was killed in melee and then just didn't stand back up, while a unit of Retributors popped out and shot the lander raider, scored 3 wounds, and casually did 17 damage to it - no miracle dice required. The Terminators failed all their saves, Huron and his Chosen were wiped by a revived Vahl, and when turn 3 rolled around all I had left on the table were three terminators and a unit of Cultists. I did the scoring as best I could from there but it was over.

Result: 46-75, loss

This one hurt, mostly because it cost me Best Overall. It's certainly possible to win best overall at 4-2 on day 1, but pretty unlikely unless I'm so far ahead on paint score that the next 5-1 person can't get there. I probably played too aggressively this game, but the flip side is that if Bile stands back up and the Land Raider gets an average damage roll (10-11), it survives on a few wounds and I'm still in the game long enough to turn things around. Or if Jeff wasn't spiking his 5+ feel no pain rolls and I could push through that damage. I don't know if I win in that situation, but it becomes a 5-round game instead of a 3-round game.



I set up for showcase with my Red Corsairs and we head to dinner. There's a new BBQ place up the hill across from the Courtyard and we're joined by Skari and Siegler as we grab dinner there. Ben Jurek and Liam VSL are sequestered away in the shadow round, which means they don't get to eat a good dinner.

Afterward we hang out for a bit and Garrett breaks out a card game he's got called Sea Salt & Paper, which is a kind of push-your-luck card game about collecting pairs of cards to score points. I'm terrible at it but it's good fun and we have a good few rounds of it before we turn in.

Day 3: Sunday

I almost left early on Sunday. At 4-2 I wasn't competing for any prizes, and I knew Garrett was heading back early. After a week away from home I was also itching to go back and see the family. The "problem" is that Dan has made the top cut for Kill Team, and is playing in the top 8 today, so he's not going home early. I take a look at the hobby scores, which had been posted in BCP and I saw that I'd done pretty well - 83 out of 85 - so I decided to stick around and get a ride back with Dan as planned.

That's a lot of Land Raiders! The big threat here are the Land Raider Redeemer - which will overwatch me to death in a heartbeat - and the Repulsor Executioner, which can tear up my vehicles pretty easily. The big downside is that the list is super elite, which makes it difficult to screen. Before the game begins I put the bikes into reserves.

The Mission: Linchpin + Tipping Point



Jake gets first turn and that's where I realize I haven't staged far enough back - he scouts a land raider forward, moves it up 12", gets out, moves D3+3", and then charges me. Yikes! The good news is that it's just a Marshal and some Sword Brethren, and Fabius and his Chosen brick are able to make short work of them. I push toward the middle and kill one Land Raider while trying to stay out of range of the other and the RepEx.



I'm able to kill the occupants and on turn 2 the other Land Raider makes its way out, where I'm able to down that one and the Aggressors inside. The backbreaker here is that Jake can't really screen his backfield, so I Ingress my terminators up there and wipe him off his home objective, dropping his primary score. He'll eventually do the same to me with his Suppressors, and I realize after the game I forgot about my bikes.



Ultimately however I won the Land Raider battle despite being outgunned 3-1 by Land Raider-esque threats and that ensures I win the primary battle. Jake gets off some good late-game blows with his Templars but it's not enough to overcome the first three rounds of primary deficit so we end pretty close on secondary - if he could have cleansed round 5 we'd have tied - but I stay ahead on primary.

Result: 83-56, Win

Jake was an absolute treat to play and I enjoyed our game tremendously. He had great energy which I struggled to match on day 3, but we had a good time. I grab venue lunch (we'd exhausted our sandwich supplies) and then prep for my next game.

Which, funny story...

Round 8: vs. Gabe's Deathwatch



This fuckin' guy. I knew the name looked familiar when I saw it, and sure enough, we'd been paired into each other again. I liked Gabe, we had a great game, but I'm tired. I offer him a draw. Gabe tells me he's playing for best in faction Deathwatch and that the guy two tables over is also playing for it. There's no award for best in faction here, but I get what Gabe wants to do. We ask the judges if we can switch opponents and get a resounding "no" on that one. I don't want to cheat that other guy out of best in faction (they had tied records but Gabe was ahead on points so that guy needed to win his game to have a shot) so I tell Gabe let's play the game, and if it looks like the other Deathwatch player had lost his game, we can just call it wherever we're at, and if it looks like that guy would win, we'd play it out. Otherwise, nothing else was at stake in this game.

The Mission: Hidden Supplies + Search and Destroy



The deployment helps me more, but the mission doesn't do me as many favors. I'm going first, which isn't what I want at all here - it gives Gabe free turn 1 uppy-downy and better end of game scoring, and forces me to be aggressive. That said, it's also day three, I'm tired, and I'm not looking to play a long game of Warhammer. I go full-send to the middle of the table, hoping to pop off some turn 1 charges and cause problems. That doesn't pay off, as I don't make my turn 1 charges with Huron and co and am forced to sit at mid-table and take lumps. The Land Raider also exploded and did a number to my units, which wasn't great.



I make the charges on turn 2 at least, but that's after I've lost the Land Raider to a group of deep striking Eradicators who do insane damage and can drop in melta range (thanks, GW, real fun interaction there). Huron and his unit eat shit but I make my charges into the side of Gabe's army with Bile and Co and Bile proceeds to be a menace again... but then doesn't stand up when killed this time. The big difference here was losing Huron early after failing an 8" charge with the re-roll - I needed that to go off and instead I don't have the power I need to get the job done.



I am still winning the primary battle pretty handily at this point at least, and making good work of the aggressor units, but I'm running short on units and I need to punch out more of Gabe's stuff. He still has plenty of up/down resources and he's able to take out the Vindicator at mid table. Some bad saves and failed hazardous/pact rolls later on my Terminators and they biff it too.



This one was surprisingly close. Gabe was close to tabling me by the end of round 4 but behind enough that it was going to come down to draws - and he was likely to just win the game. Fortunately we checked in two tables over and the other Deathwatch  player has lost to Blood Angels. We're sitting tied at score with 62-62, and call it a tie without finishing the game.

Result: 62-62, Tie

A great game against a fun opponent. Gabe was fun to play and we had a good time getting into it but now all I want in life is to pack away my minis and sit the fuck down.

We hang out for a bit and I talk to dan, who went 1-2 and finished in 7th place for the event, which is a hell of a showing. He's really been doing well since moving from 40k to Kill Team and while he doesn't have as much time to play as he'd like, Kill Team lets him do a lot more with limited time than 40k does. He wasn't even going to attend the event so I'm glad he was able to make it out. Not just because he was my ride but also because...



That son of a bitch won Best Overall and a ticket to Barcelona!

Dan's lovely Murderwing gave him a good paint + play score and he was able to walk away with a coveted trophy for his efforts. Now he's wondering how he's going to make it to Barcelona in December, which is a hell of an accomplishment. Great job, Dan.

Oh, on that note, while I didn't win Best Overall - it went to the person with the second highest paint score and a 4-1-1 record - I did win Best Painted:



Considering I chose the units for this army based on what I had painted, I'm ecstatic with this result. I'd have rather won Best Overall, sure - that one keeps eluding me - but I'll take Best Painted all day long. I cannot express adequately with words how satisfying it is to put in the amount of work I do into these armies over and over and be rewarded for the effort, especially at an event this size. It's a labor of love and while I don't go to events expecting to win, when I do it fills me with both validation and the sense of a job well done which lingers for weeks.



In particular this is my third Best Painted Trophy at a major, and I've won each with three different armies: My Thousand Sons at Kansas City in 2022, my Night Lords at the 2024 Grand Narrative, and now my Red Corsairs. I think there's a place for me to win with my Black Legion again at some point but I'm very proud of the work I've done to get here and ready to start working on the next Best Painted army in my collection.

Dan and I head out immediately after collecting awards. I grab Garrett's silver trophy for 2nd best painted (which I'm enjoying immensely after losing to him on painting at Clutch City - the display board did it for him there), and we run to the car. It's full on flash-flooding raining in Dallas when we leave but by the time we hit the first Buccee's on the road to Houston we've cleared the storm. The trip back is a mix of road-trip singalongs and the occasional high five followed by a "FUCK YEAH WE DID IT" from the two of us, triumphant heroes who came, saw, and conquered.

I make it back to the house around 10pm, give Dan his new Tabletop Battles jersey, and he heads on his way. I'm glad to be home, and I set the trophy up before crashing about ten minutes later.

I'm looking forward to swapping out the Land Raider for Warp Talons and adjusting the list a bit.
