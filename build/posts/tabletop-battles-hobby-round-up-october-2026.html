Tabletop Battles Hobby Round-Up October 2026

November 3rd, 2026 via Goonhammer

There's a chill on the wind as October comes to a close; the autumnal air every bit as crisp as the edge highlights on our crew's models. Scroll down to see what tricks and treats await!

Rockfish

I have unfortunately had a bit of a cursed month between being busy at work and not great health things, so only a handful of things from me this time.

Trukk. Credit: Rockfish
Trukk. Credit: Rockfish
Redemptor. Credit: Rockfish
Hippogriff AFV. Credit: Rockfish
Warboss. Credit: Rockfish

Jefferson Powers

I got back to Shatterpoint this month, painting the two new boxes featuring the villains from the Ahsoka streaming series. I was especially excited about the first one, as it features Baylan Skoll and his apprentice Shin Hati, two of the most interesting Star Wars characters to come along in years.

Image credit: Jefferson Powers

The zombie Stormtroopers were probably the most fun to paint out of this batch. It's not often you get to use that much color on Imperial models.

Image credit: Jefferson Powers

"Contemptor" Kevin Stillman

The 2026 Grand Narrative is almost upon us, so I spent most of October plugging away at my Ultramarines.  The first model, of course, is a new Repulsor Executioner.  I picked this up awhile ago, so I was going to have the Space Marines' flagship tank.

Ultramarines Repulsor Executioner. Credit: Contemptor Kevin Stillman

With the new Marine Codex, the detachment I loved playing in 10th Edition, Ironstorm Spearhead, became the Iron Hands' exclusive detachment Medusa's Wrath.  However, everything I have worked on for the past year or so is in the blue and gold of Ultramar.  How was I to square this circle?  Well, several years ago I purchased Iron Father Ferrios but never got around to painting him.  Until now, when I painted him blue. I cut off the Iron Hands' specific iconography or replaced it with Ultramarines iconography, and changed his head from the pulped meat of the standard model and into the much more handsome Ultramarines upgrade sprue Terminator bare head.  And viola, we now have Master of the Forge Fennias Maxim.

The list I'm planning also requires two additional Techmarines.  While one of those will be the usual Primaris Techmarine (whom I need to build and paint or repaint an older one, to be determined), I wanted a second, different Techmarine.  While I could use the Forge World Mk. IV marine, like I did my Dark Angels, I wanted to do something different.  Then I looked in my cabinet of painted miniatures at stuff I painted during the pandemic and found the solution: the Thunderfire Cannon Techmarine.  I swiftly rebased him onto a 40, and punched up his paintjob to a modern standard:

The last full unit I painted in October was a squad of Heavy Bolter Eradicators.  These big boys were painted when I picked up my TonePro paints, and I admit I had to take the yellows out for a spin.  I used Khemri Sands 4 as a base and Hawkshroud Yellow 3 as a highlight, and the two paints blended wonderfully for purposes of making hazard stripes:

Ultramarines Eradicators with Heavy Bolters. Credit: Contemptor Kevin Stillman

November will undoubtedly consist, at least from me, of more blue Space Marines and vehicles.

SRM

Hope you aren't tired of seeing blue Space Marines in this post because by gum you're getting more.

ULtramarines Terminator Squad. Credit: SRM

Terminators are the new hotness, if you haven't heard, so it was about time I added some classic "Tactical" Terminators to my Ultramarines, replete with a 2nd edition box art-inspired scheme. These are just the crew from 10th edition's Leviathan starter set, with some light kitbashing to give Sarge a power fist and a Cyclone Missile Launcher in place of the Assault Cannon. I was kind of holding out for the Tone Pro paintline to see if I could get better coverage on my yellow for hazard stripes, but I wanted paint on them sooner.

Ultramarines Ancient in Terminator Armor. Repost. Credit: SRM

Of course, these guys need an emotional support flagbearer to make them harder to kill, and that's where the Ancient comes in. This is the fourth giant detailed banner I've painted this year, and I would like to be done painting giant detailed banners now. I'm very happy with how it came out, don't get me wrong, they're just a lot of work. The rhythms of this model were otherwise about the same as those on the Terminators before him, though I wish I'd been able to fit the sheathed Ultramarines power sword from the upgrade kit on him instead of the generic one. Guess I'll need to find some other blue guy in big armor to carry a big sword, what a challenge.

Autumn is firmly Bigg Boi Season as I'm currently taking a momentary break from the stack of six Heavy Bolter Eradicators to write this. Will they be all I can finish next month? I sure hope not!

Micah "dirtboi"

I managed to get 105 models done this month, an absolute record for myself. First up I finished 50 Shoretroopers for Star Wars legion, using mostly contrast paints and taking my time with individual rank markings. Had a ton of fun with these guys, going to field them on the table in a week for a charity event.

[gallery td_select_gallery_slide="slide" columns="2" size="full" ids="259160,259159"]

As a light little afternoon project, I speedpainted General Veers for my Blizzard Force portion of my empire army.

General Veers. Credit: dirtboi

I got quickly sidetracked from Legion models for a Saturday, having a buddy print off some models for the game Planet Shark that is absolutely adorable. He was an absolute blast to paint, and I cannot wait to paint more. I was super excited as he was an excuse to try AK Interactives plethora of water/beach basing materials, and I'm really happy with the outcome.

[gallery size="full" td_select_gallery_slide="slide" ids="259168,259169,259170"]

After my cute shark friend, I finished 44 Stormtroopers, 6 Probe Droids, a customizable Officer and Agent, and General Tagge. Super excited to play General Tagge, only so I can shout about excel spreadsheets whenever I play his command cards.

[gallery size="full" td_select_gallery_slide="slide" ids="259176,259174,259172"]

As a young child, Star Wars: The Force Unleashed was one of my favorite games, and I felt super inspired to paint my Stormtroopers as Triton Squad from the first few missions of the game.

[gallery size="full" td_select_gallery_slide="slide" ids="259178,259179,259177"]

Credit: dirtboi

Super happy with my progress this month, partly because of my participation in the Paintober Painting Challenge. 105 models completed from start to finish in the month of October. My brushes need a cigarette. See yall next month!
