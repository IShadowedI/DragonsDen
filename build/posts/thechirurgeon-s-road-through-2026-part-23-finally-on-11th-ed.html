TheChirurgeon's Road Through 2026, Part 23: Finally on 11th Edition

June 4th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive progress through 2026. Last time around I talked about the rest of the Dallas Open, winning Best Painted, and my final games of tenth edition. This week the embargo has lifted and I'm free to talk fully about 11th Edition and playing games of it. If you missed our coverage of the new edition and its rules, I'd direct you to this post, which is a good central hub for all of it:

Loading...


That said, while I'd been doing some test games of 11th edition before, I hadn't actually been playing the actual full game - we hadn't yet received the boxed sets for Armageddon with the mission cards, nor had we received the layouts. Having those makes a huge difference, as it means I can play missions that aren't just Take and Hold vs. Disruption. So I had some other NDA people over last weekend to do some test games. For these games, I was running Veterans of the Long War Chaos Space Marines using the Purge the Foe Disposition. I don't know for certain that Veterans will get that Disposition, and I'm operating under the assumption that it'll be 3 DP. That said, because we don't know the Detachment Point costs for things, when we played our games, we took a 1 DP detachment in addition to our primary detachment, just to test new rules.

Here's the list I took:

The general idea here is to use Oath of Moment to make up for the -1 BS penalty against units in cover with things like the Predators. The rest of the infantry is there to eviscerate targets and hold objectives, with a modified and (hopefully) more effective version of what I was running in Dallas. I also wanted to test a few things here, like the Chaos Lord giving Raptors a 5+ invulnerable save, which seems neat but I worry won't matter much given how common AP-1 and AP-2 attacks are, and the Detachment ability for Murdertalon Raiders.

I'm running Purge the Foe for these missions, because I needed to write about that Disposition for our Force Disposition series this week.

Game 1: vs. Space Wolves



For my first game I tested into Garrett's Space Wolves, running a Space Wolves Detachment I can't talk about this week but which let him get a turn 1 deep strike with a unit of ten Terminators. His list was the ten terminators with a captain, Arjac + five terminators, Grimnar + five terminators, 10 Wulfen, a unit of six Headtakers, and some chaff guys.

Garrett was going first, and decided to just go full send and try and put me in Terminator jail. Because of what he was running, his big play was +1 to charges with built-in re-rolls out of deep strike, giving him an 8" re-rollable turn 1 deep strike charge into my lines. That's... pretty nasty. He dropped in and made his charge, using the new charge rules to multi-charge my Predator and a Rhino. Then he promptly bounced off the Predator after I dropped Armour of Contempt on it and failed to kill the Rhino, which heroically passed 5/6 5+ saves to stay alive on 1 wound. Then Fabius Bile and his crew got out, and I killed all ten of those Terminators, plus the Captain. Nice.

This one was pretty neck-and neck early on, and the cover rules didn't matter that much, mostly because we were just in each other's faces immediately and early in the game. Garrett initially used his Wulfen to screen out my Warp Talons, but then shot them across the table to try and kill some vehicles which would stop him from holding the center objective.



Key Finding: The massed [PRECISION] on the Headtakers is absolutely devastating with 11th edition wound allocation rules. They shot in and just immediately murked Huron Blackheart, though interestingly being forced to take all of the 1s/2s/3s on him meant that after he died, the remaining wounds were all saved.

In a hilarious twist, those Wulfen fought their way across the table to my home objective, where the couldn't flip it - the Chaos Cultists had stickied it at the start of the game, and the Wulfen couldn't undo that at OC 0. Meanwhile I used my Warp Talons to clear off Garrett's home and keep him from holding 3 objectives. I cleared out Arjac's band of Terminators, and by round 3 we were both pretty sparse on units.



That said, I'd consistently outscored Garrett on Primary, and while his Wulfen were an issue, I was in a good place to clear out Grimnar's unit with Chosen, the Predator, and Warp Talons. We called it.

This was a good game but another example of a game that was just really deadly. I've come to understand that maps with the large central objective have this problem much more than maps which split the objective, as they just feel much, much more open. Hidden didn't matter at all in this game, as you might expect.

Game 2: vs. Firestorm Assault Force



For my second game I played against Space Marines running Firestorm Assault Force and the Subversion Assets Detachment. My opponent was running a Land Raider full of Vulkan and Heroes, plus Adrax and Bladeguard, with a pair of Ballistus Dreadnoughts, Vindicators, and a lot of the usual suspects. We played Purge vs. Purge - that's Meatgrinder, which basically just gives both players Kill one/hold one/kill more/hold more for scoring.

KC got the first turn and ambled up, putting me in position to counter-punch. Veterans was pretty clutch in this matchup, letting me get in good conversions on my hit rolls for things like the Predator lascannon sponsons. My Red Corsairs Raiders were a particularly annoying unit this game, spending most of it fighting those two dreadnoughts and killing a unit of Incursors. The new charge rules are very nice - being able to decide after the fact where and how you want to multi-charge (or just not charge at all) gives you a lot more freedom.



The big goof on my end was getting too aggressive with Huron and co early - I lost everyone but Huron to Land Raider shooting, forcing him to fall back and regroup, though he did survive. The Warp Talons put in some work here on Vulkan and his unit, but KC nailed his saves and despite dropping 45 wounds on his remaining 4 guys he only took 12 wounds, leaving Vulkan alive on 2. I was able to recoup some of my losses by taking out both Vindicators with a mix of lascannon shooting and Raptor melee and I gotta say: Warp Talons and Raptors having Grenades is such a game changer.

Key Finding: The 5+ invulnerable save on the Raptors was super helpful against the Vindicator, but that Detachment rule never mattered. So basically the same as with Nightmare Hunt, hilariously.



I finally took the Land Raider and the second Ballistus down on turn 4, more or less sealing the game. This one did go to late 4th round, and I really think having split middle ruins was a big part of that. This layout felt much less open so it was ironic that, in a mission where the main primary goal was just "kill your opponent," the game felt less deadly than it did when I was playing into Take and Hold Space Wolves.

On the whole these were fun games, and it was good to have actual mission cards, layouts, and secondary missions to work with. The new secondaries are fine, but will take a bit of getting used to. They largely play the same as before, though there are definitely fewer actions in the deck now, and if you draw Cleanse and Plunder (the new Sabotage), you can redraw one of them. I think this is likely because three of the mission sets rely heavily on actions, and so it relieves armies playing those of having to do too many actions in one round, but it also largely absolves Purge/Take and Hold players of having to worry about actions in their games since only two secondaries (instead of four) require actions now.

Hobby

I've mostly been working on marines from the new Armageddon box. Specifically, I'm painting them as Space Wolves, doing some light converting work using bits from the Headtakers and Wolf Scouts sprues I still have lying around from the preview kits sent out last year.

Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

I'm painting these for a friend - SD47 - who does a ton of the work on the Tabletop Battles app right now and plays Space Wolves but isn't bit on painting. So these are kind of like a thank you to him for the amazing work he does. I'm not going all out with them, but they do look very nice when I'm finished with them.

Next Week: More 11th Edition and Space Wolves

That wraps up this week's updates but I'll be back again next week with more on my games of 11th edition, hopefully able to say more about some of the missing pieces and rules. I'll also be painting more Space Wolves, and trying to get more done on my Red Corsairs Warp Talons project. And at some point I'm going to pick up my Emperor's Children again for the Grand Narrative, I swear.

See you next week.
