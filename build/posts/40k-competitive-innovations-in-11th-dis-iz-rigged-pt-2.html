[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Dis iz Rigged pt.2

September 25th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Wings: A quick event spotlight from me up front before I hand you over to Lowest of Men for the event writeups!

Event Spotlight - Paris Wargame Expo

On 17th and 18th October, the French 40K Federation are running the Paris Wargame Expo for the second time, featuring a massive array of events for Games Workshop systems.

Corrode and I went out to the first of these last year, and had an absolute blast, which you can read more about in my post here. This year, the organisers are going even bigger (and at an even larger venue), and the following events are available:

  • 512-player 40K supermajor (running on official GW terrain).
  • 200-player 5v5 40k team tournament.
  • 600-player 2v2 40K
  • A massive 40K Grand Narrative
  • Age of Sigmar singles major
  • Age of Sigmar 2v2
  • Age of Sigmar Narrative.
  • Events for:
    • Underworlds
    • The Old World
    • Blood Bowl
    • Kill Team
    • Horus Heresy
Last year's event in full swing

This year, Games Workshop are also bringing the Hobby Challenge to the PWE. For those who want to focus on the building and painting rather than competitive play, the Hobby Challenge is a really cool way to spend a weekend that I don't think Games Workshop does nearly enough to spotlight, but luckily Cronch has a trip report from the run at the Newport GW open here, so take a look if you're curious.

GW will also be running their event store at the PWE, so for those on the continent who have less frequent access to event exclusives, this is an opportunity to grab them.

There's also goody bags for all participants, and breakfast/lunch included with tournament tickets.

We had a phenomenal time last year, and the event was extremely well run, so if you're looking for a great tournament next month, I highly recommend looking at this, particularly if you're in Europe or the UK (as travel on the Eurostar is dead easy). I am legitimately crushed that I can't go this year due to some unavoidable real-life clashes, so go have fun in my stead!

Tickets and full details are all available on the French Federation Website.

Disclosure: I was offered free tickets/press passes to this event for a promotional post. As above, I am unable to actually go due to some real-life clashes, so we are not receiving any payment for this post, but it is still important to disclose this.

Thoughts

A showdown that throws the old enemies, Elves and Dwarves, against one another. The Aeldari definitely have speed on their side here- Eldritch is FAST, with advance and charge everywhere, and certain key pieces in the Votann lines will melt (looking at you ALL the vehicles). Kharseth gives them some scope for managing the Votann drop turn - but is it enough?

Aeldari have screening issues atm at their high points cost, and they don't really have weapons outside the Yriel brick that want to dance with full bricks of Hearthguard. It'll often play out that the elves get up good early pressure before the Hearthguard arrive and turn them into a smoking crater- if the Votann go first, this happens sooner and the elf game is far more at risk of collapse. In the end the Kyn took this one! I'd have loved to have watched this game.

Eldritch Raiders

Thoughts

A beautiful speedy Aeldari list here that goes in on the sucker punch that is Eldritch Raiders. Giving everything advance and charge (and doubling down with the+1 to advance and charge aura from the Avatar) let's you get the first hit in real good into lots of armies.
