TheChirurgeon’s Road Through 2026, Part 36: Challenger's Cup Prep

September 3rd, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive play progress through 2026. Last week I talked about my trip to Paris and some hobby work I needed to get done. Said trip pretty much ate up my whole weekend, especially after my return flight got delayed, forcing me to re-book my connecting flight through EWR; I didn't get home until about 9:30pm on Saturday.

Despite some jet lag, I was up and ready to run HeroQuest with the kids on Sunday morning. I've mentioned before that this is typically three kids - my son and his two friends (a boy and a girl), and one adult - their dad. This works out well but recently we've swapped him out for another girl in my son's class. She started last time around and opted to play the monk, which is really cool - it's a powerful class with some neat abilities, but it's also complicated.

I'm happy to say that this time around she showed up with a whole page of strategies she'd put together, having looked up HeroQuest and how to play the monk online. This immediately made her my favorite player at the table. She was really into it, and we started on the first quest of Kellar's Keep, the expansion which immediately follows the base game. This actually starts with a downloadable quest - The Forsaken Tunnels of Xor-Xel, written to bridge the gap between the main Quest and Kellar's Keep.

The big hook in this quest is that it's very dark in the tunnels, so you only see three square ahead. It's an interesting hook, but I don't think it *quite* gets there, in part because most rooms are at most 4x4 and it's not clear how you do diagonal distances with that guidance.

Still, the quest was a hit and more challenging than some of these standard quests tend to be. It ran a bit long, but that's as much due to the fact that having four children instead of three kids and an adult makes things take way longer. Keeping them on task was a struggle but we eventually got there. Also, kids are just not great at searching for traps and hidden doors. They always remember to look for treasure; they seldom remember to look for doors and they never look for traps.

Challengers Cup Prep

The other big, looming event is Challenger's Cup - next week I'm shipping off to Utah with seven of my Tabletop Battles colleagues to play in the 8-man Challengers Cup team tournament. This was a blast last year and I'm eager to go back, though I am once again on the fence this year given I had to fill in for another dropped player on short notice. This seems to be a recurring theme with 8man events and I'm kind of sick of it.

I'm bringing Thousand Sons. After last week's balance update I was strongly considering Death Guard, but my teammate Wallace is already on them. And I'm still not sold on a Death Guard vehicles list, even if I already have the Defilers for it. So instead I'm sticking to Thousand Sons, even if I haven't loved how they play outside of Purge.

On that note, here's the thing about teams in 11th edition: Your Dispositions are part of the fun. Specifically, you have to have one of each Disposition on your team, and you can only double up once per disposition. That means our team needs to have one each of Purge, Take and Hold, Priority Assets, Disruption, and Recon, and we can double up on a few. Having been unimpressed by the play for Thousand Sons in Priority Assets and Take and Hold, I've decided to go pervert mode and run Purge the Foe Thousand Sons, taking Warpmeld Pact as my Detachment and choosing to take very little from it. This is an interesting gamble, as I typically did not remember to use the detachment ability on Hexwarp Thrallband when I played that one.

It's basically the Tacoma list, swapping a few things like the 10man Rubric unit and character for a second Forgefiend and a Mutalith Vortex beast. I like the triple mediums play here, and the double fiends can support the Terminators nicely. I still have a disc Shaman to zip around, only now he has the annoying trick of getting back up on death, which seems like a lot of fun.

The big loss here is not having the ability to fall back and shoot - I need to be really careful about not letting the Scarabs get tagged. Mutagenic Magicks and Doombolt are going to be key here to avoiding being stuck in combat when I don't want to be but my plan is to drop in and kill anything nearby and prevent that from being an issue. Otherwise the Mutalith is a concession toward the new Detachment, and a valuable piece for scrapping if I need some melee support. It also gets some extra value here out of the stratagems. Otherwise the Detachment does almost nothing for me, though I'm bringing some extra Chaos Spawn for Gift of Change.

Hobby Progress

The big thing now is finishing my unfinished Forgefiend for the event, then getting back to work on my Emperor's Children. I need to assemble that Defiler for the army, but in the meantime, a lot of what I'm doing is just final clean-up and detail work on the Forgefiend.

Magnetization helps here, but I still have tons of work to do on the body, painting rivets and edge highlighting the gold trim, painting the flesh parts, adding shading and burnishing the gold.

This is a simple but time-consuming process that involves building up the color on the washed flesh parts, then washing the gold trim with Agrax and edge highlighting it with Runefang Steel. The flesh I paint with Bugman's Glow, then a mix of Bugman's and Wraithbone, working up a couple of layers. I want the lightest parts of the flesh to be the ones stretched most taut over the metal endoskeleton.

After this I'll need to go back and fix that base, and I'll probably add rocks and tufts to it to make it look more complete and match the rest of the army. This model's far enough along that I really want to finish it, and I can see Forgefiends staying useful for a while in the army, even after marines move to T5.

Final Thoughts

That's it for this week. I'll be back next week with more hobby progress and potentially a practice game, depending on everyone's Labor day availability. Then it's off to Challenger's Cup next week, where we'll stay in a a big house, drink, and have fun before being forced to go play six games of Warhammer somewhere.

See you next Thursday.
