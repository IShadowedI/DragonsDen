Marvel Crisis Protocol Affiliation Spotlight: Storm, Uncanny X-Men

August 20th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Right after the 2026 crisis rotation came into play, I won 3 competitive events with my Storm X-Men roster. A couple things had changed that really benefitted mutants:

  1. Source extracts make up for their lack of steals as Extract holders drop them every round.
  2. Most activations in this roster are some combination of Move > Move > Interact, which also works fine on Source extracts.
  3. The removal of really fighty crises such as M’Kraan & Mayor Fisk means I’m rarely forced into having to fight.

Things were feeling real good. Then the 2026 errata dropped…and my roster got a combination of 11 additional health. Now feels like the right time to shine a spotlight on X-Men Gold and, more specifically, what they’re benefitting from most in the new world of MCP.

Storm

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

The only thing that happened to Storm in the errata was gaining +1 health on both sides of her card.

For a character that mostly stands at the back, throwing Lightning Bolts at people outside of her Stealth (Goddess of Storms) range…having the ability to survive an attack and then double move to a point and throw a Size 2 character off with Tempest is fantastic.

I usually do this at the end of Round 2 or 3, meaning she’s now very unlikely to be Dazed and KO’d in the following round. 

I play her like a coiled spring. She stands at the back in safety until the perfect time to strike, then she rotates, flips some points and throws some characters.

I basically never use Ice Blizzard or Hurricane. The best way to spend power on Storm is with Tempest. If I have more than 3 power (or 4-5 power if I’m on pay to flips…as flipping Secures is Storm’s #1 win condition), so I also very rarely use Eye Of The Storm. I think people value that too highly. A 6 dice Lightning Bolt can definitely still whiff - and if that leaves Storm with not enough power to Tempest, that’s a disaster.

Iceman

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

The absolute sleeper hit that is Iceman got a huge glow up in the errata.

His entire gameplan is to deploy on a flank and dish out Slow & Stagger to the enemy team while the rest of your team rotates to the opposite flank with all the Extracts. His primary way of doing this is Cold Snap, a superpower that requires him to be alive after being hit from Range 2.

With the health bump up to 6 / 6, he is now a lot more likely to do that.

Iceman single handedly won me games with the above play pattern when he was on 5 / 5 health, so this is a huge change for him. I take him on basically any game that requires people to double move around the board.

Emma Frost

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

I wasn’t quite done celebrating the recent thematic glow up Emma Frost got to her kit before they went ahead and gave her more health! Now sitting at 7 / 7, Emma is even less of a model worth going after.

90% of the time she transforms to her diamond form where she has 5 - 5 - 4 defences and can reposition herself into cover and outside of Range 2 to benefit from the Storm leadership, making her even harder to take down.

Her Normal Form side offers a ton of tools that pick apart annoying meta tricks like Re-Roll bubbles, Heroes For Hire/Bodyguards & Clapbacks and the like thanks to Power Inhibition (not to mention this also turns off cards like Fall Back & Sacrifice).

Not to mention that she can pull off a clutch double move > double pay-to-flip while also potentially throwing a Size 3 to end the game while in Diamond Form thanks to her 5 - 5 defences.

Emma Frost is great in any X-Men roster because she’s just plain good. She also brings an X-Men specific Team Tactics Card in the form of Headmistress which brings even more movement tech which they love.

Beast

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

Beast didn’t get a single thing in the errata this year - and I’m absolutely fine with that. You can see some of my thoughts on Beast in the X-Men Starter Set write-up here.

He was a top tier model before the errata and he remains a top tier model now. There’s a bit of discourse in the online MCP community that Beast is now unplayable because of not getting any additional health? Well I’m here to tell you that is categorically incorrect.

Nobody's damage output was increased in the errata, meaning Beast is still as tanky as ever. He’s just surrounded by X-Men that now also have a chance to survive attacks, meaning his job of move > move > throw a character is still some of the best value in the game.

Gambit

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

Gambit is great. Wilds counting as 2 thanks to The Cards Always Be In My Favor can make him a little unpredictable defensively, but it brings his ceiling up into the stratosphere when he does roll those sweet Wilds.

A Little Something Extra makes his attacks way more predictable, meaning he can reliably displace a Size 3 character with Bo Staff or ping some damage onto a character that survived on 1 by using Kinetic Ace on a target within Range 2 of them.

All of this paired with Enhanced Agility which is not once per turn and is not towards the attacker makes Gambit one of my most versatile and reactive models to play.

He got +1 health on his injured side in the errata, which mostly opens up more opportunities for a double 52 Card Pickup turn to turn the game in my favour when needed.

Angel

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

Well…I never thought I’d see the day where I include Angel in a write-up like this.

It’s still early days for me to be trying him out on the table, but moving to 6 / 6 health means he doesn’t instantly get deleted from the game anymore. But he is still 3 - 3 - 3 defences, so it can certainly happen.

His kit feels super awkward. Most of his activations you want him to double move and pull off an Air Lift and a Hostile Takeover. He can’t be paired with Beast because that guy is too big to be air lifted apparently. He needs to start his move action within Range 2 of the people he’s trying to move around, which plays a lot more awkward than it reads.

In short, I’m trying Warren Worthington III out here. Another medium base for using the Storm leadership doesn’t hurt. Healing Factor is cute but he tends to take more damage than he can heal off.

I bring Ultimate Sacrifice because I can see that being pretty game winning if timed literally perfectly (he has to pay for it, then you want him to Daze to get the effect). Awkward awkward awkward is the vibe I get from this guy.

I think he belongs in the roster for sure, because a guaranteed Size 3 throw on Avenging Angel is no joke in later rounds when you have a scary Dracula or Mephisto stomping towards you. But I need more play time with him for sure.

X-23

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

This one breaks my heart, but I’m currently experimenting with taking X-23 out of my Storm roster. In a world where everybody and their mom got more health, X-23 stayed where she is. Which is fine, she was already a top tier model (you can read all about my love for her here). But 5 health means she can easily have her activation removed by other models.

She’s now also trying to take out characters with more health than before. Which she can certainly do reliably…just a bit less reliably than before.

If Angel doesn’t make the cut after my experimentation, it’ll be Laura who takes his place for sure.

Valkyrie & Elendil

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

I’ve been really loving Valkyrie & Elendil under Storm X-Men. 

On the surface, she offers a large base for using the leadership with and acts as a model that can chase Extract holders around the board more effectively than the rest of my roster.

But if you look closer at their kit, this deadly duo bring a lot more to the table. 

Take Wing lets them trigger the extra dice on Geirr without the need of a Storm leadership hop. 

Need A Lift helps get X-tract holders (see what I did there?!) out of harm's way while Valkyrie & Elendil rotates.

Guardian of Valhalla works really well in a roster full of X-Men that have a tendency to Daze, meaning my usual play pattern with Valkyrie is to chase down an Extract, run away to safety and slowly heal up over the rest of the game while the rest of the X-Men are dazing.

Speaking of healing, these 2 went up to 7 / 7 health in the errata which is a big deal as it gives them more chance to survive and benefit from Guardian of Valhalla.

Another use I love having Valkyrie & Elendil for is playing as goalkeeper. Once the X-Men are loaded up on Extracts and running away from the enemy team, Valkyrie can jump in and hit some people with Fury Of The Valkryor with a pretty reliable stagger, making it harder for them to chase down your VPs.

There are likely better models for this spot in the roster such as Omega Sentinel & Prowler. But I’ve had a ton of fun playing Valkyrie as she’s just a big old toolbox filled with utility. Plus she’s easily the best model sculpt they’ve added to the game!

Toad

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

A lot of people are down on Toad now that he only has 4 health and cannot displace enemy models. I agree with that; I can see him being a liability in most teams these days.

However, I now consider him to have passed his entry exams at Xavier’s School For Gifted Youngsters making him an X-Men affiliated character (in my head cannon only…he’s still only affiliated to Brotherhood of Mutants and Servants of the Apocalypse).

The main reasoning here is;

  1. He’s still good on pay-to-flips, which Uncanny X-Men use a lot of.
  2. He can use Finders Keepers to take an Extract off Valkyrie & Elendil or Angel, allowing them to dive towards the enemy team and get on with their usual jobs.
  3. Once Toad is holding an Extract, if he survives an attack and can use Slippery, you’ll likely never see him again and he’ll reliably score that VP for a couple more rounds. This makes him a prime target for Xavier’s Dream.

My opponent often laughs when I choose to save Toad with this card, but my whole gameplan is to reach 16 VPs before we ever see Round 4. Keeping Toad alive while holding an Extract long enough is a key part of that, so I’m never sad to have to do that if it puts a Slippery online (remember that he has to suffer some damage to trigger this, so don’t Xavier Dream too hard).

Cable

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

Cable got more health in the errata. I don’t think he needed it. His best defence is killing everyone around him, which he’s great at.

More details on my thoughts on how to play Cable here.

Characters to Consider

Here is a list of characters that aren’t in my roster, but I am absolutely considering bringing in.

Gwenom

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

I played Gwenom at the World Team Championship in 2025 and bloody loved it.

With a Storm hop from the leadership, she can attack twice and move twice when using Symbiote Spider Technique.

She got additional health in the errata making the healing available from Maximum Gwenom a lot more likely to be useful.

She has Size 3 character replacement with Dark Rage, something X-Men sorely miss.

Pick On Someone Your Own Size is also cute as she’ll likely be surrounded by X-Men holding Extracts, so she can punish your opponent for having a swipe at them.

She’s very dice reliant as she really needs power to get her kit to go off. But with more health, she’s certainly likely to have an easier time generating it.

Wolverine

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

Wolverine got a lovely subtle glow up in the errata moving to a whopping 8 health. 

You can read about some of the tricks this opens up here.

Nightcrawler

Image courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

Kurt’s job is generally to wait until the end of a round and then BAMF! & Puff Of Smoke around flipping points and hopefully stunning people.

Him moving to 7 / 7 health makes this easier to pull off as he won’t have his activation removed as easily as it used to be previously.

His spender is a lot of fun and tends to be what people talk about with him. I find it a little unreliable for my playstyle (I prefer guaranteed triggers / less painful gambles where possible), so I personally think the moving/placing around to flip points at the end of the round is a better play pattern for Nightcrawler.

Crisis Cards

In terms of Crisis Cards, I currently take:

Extract Crisis

Mutant Extremists Target U.S. Senators! (19)

Skrulls Infiltrate World Leadership (20)

Unexpected Guests Crash Royal Wedding (17)

Scientific Samples Found in Discovered Universe (17)

Salvaged Supplies Fuel Resistance Efforts (18)

Secure Crisis

Mutant Madman Turns City Into Lethal Amusement Park (18)

Guardians Save Shi‘ar Empress in Style (17)

Strike Team Secures Shield Relay! (17)

X-Men Infiltrate Secret Weapons Facility (17)

Power Overload! Factory Goes Up in Flames (19)

The main thing to note here is that I’m taking every pay-to-flip that I can, besides Deadly Meteors because it’s too close and despite the additional health, Storm X-Men still really don’t want to fight if they can help it.

There is some Round 1 jank you can pull off with priority, Emma Frost & Headmistress on Salvaged Supplies that is a few too many hoops to jump through to make it reliable. So I’ll likely take this crisis out eventually in favour for something like Inhumans Deploy Advanced Weaponry or Spider-Infected Invade Manhattan.

At the start of this article I mentioned that Uncanny X-Men are good on Source Extracts. I do believe this, but I don’t think they’re high scoring enough to include in my roster. I may be wrong about this, but I’ve just found Source Extracts are fine for us…not necessarily great enough to include them in the roster.

I also bring Power Overload! Factory Goes Up in Flames because with the inclusion of Angel & Emma Frost, I now feel like I have some answers to the Kingpin Criminal Syndicate types of roster that you may encounter here.

Final Thoughts

I’ve had a ton of fun playing with Storm Uncanny X-Men these past few months - and have been surprised at the success I’ve had with it!

With the additional health to almost the entire X-Men roster in this update (finally confirming my propaganda of AMG standing for Anti-Mutant-Gang was actually just straight FACTS), now is a great time to pick up some mutants and try them on the table.
