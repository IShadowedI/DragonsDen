[40K] Weekend Update: November 16, 2026

November 16th, 2026 via Goonhammer

In our Weekend Update series, we cover the big events and stories that are happening each week in wargames, so you can stay updated and informed every week.

Another week another update, with more events around the country and some which were undoubtedly upended or affected by the widespread FAA grounding of flights on Friday afternoon. Pour one out for anyone who couldn't make it to their event or hasn't made it home yet.

Let’s dive into the weekend's big events.

Clement Tournade Upsets at Kempton Park with Aeldari

The biggest singles GT of the weekend was Games Workshop's National Tournament Series event in Kempton Park, a 121 player event with five rounds of play. Clement Tournade surprised everyone by winning the event with Aeldari (a faction which has struggled so far in 11th edition). Tournade's build employs a combination of the Seer Council and Armoured Warhost Detachments and its highlights include two Storm Guardian bricks with five attached Psykers which can take advantage of the stratagems in Seer Council. These are supported by a 10-man Fire Dragon unit with Fuegan and rounded out by the Avatar of Khaine and Jain Zar. They're supported by a ton of cheap trash, which helps the list run Priority Assets as its primary mission. It's a great place to start if you're looking for inspiration on how to play Aeldari in the new meta.

Playing Priority Assets support by a ton of cheap trash this list like a great place to start if you are an Aeldari playing looking for guidance in the new meta.

Also undefeated at the event were Malik Amin Rubio (Adeptus Mechanicus), Clement - Immortus Riu (Astra Militarum), and Tom Godfrey (Orks), who finished with an astonishing 500 battle points over five rounds. Stay tuned for more on their lists in Competitive Innovations this week.

Matt Evans Wins Springs Thundercluckers with Tyranids

Thundercluckers was comparatively a smaller event - just 61 players - with five rounds of play. Matt Evans won the event with a Tyranids Invasion Fleet list that's just sporting a very cool brew. Playing Invasion Fleet in Priority Assets he was able to eat the completion with a surprisingly large amount of Tyrant Guard and Warriors. Rounded out of a ton of Gaunts, some support pieces, and a Tyranofex, this list really has a unique build when compared to the more popular Norn spam. Also shout out to Trae Burnett for also finishing 5-0 with a Take and Hold CSM build sport Veterans of the Long War and Cabal of Chaos.

Brad Chester Wins Da Boyz GT

The weekend's mandatory Marines winner, Brad Chester won the five round, 59-player Da Boyz with a Take and Hold list using Assault Brethren and Stormlance with Deathwatch Support sprinkled in. Congratulations as well to Joseph Sault, who went undefeated with a Space Marines list running Deathwatch Support, Assault Brethren, and Phobos Shadow Force.

Team Spain Wins Autumn International Team Tournament 

The massive - 534 players! - International Team Tournament is the highlight teams event of the quarter for the UK, a UKTC event featuring six rounds of play. Team Spain absolutely dominated their competition at the event, scoring a huge win over team Ignite in the final round with a 67-33 victory that included blowout wins over some of the game's biggest names.

Congratulations to:

  • Alejandro Mancuso Serrano (Aeldari - TAH)
  • Borja FM (Space Wolves - PTF)
  • Falco (Death Guard - DIS)
  • Gonzalo "Raven" de La Moneda (Emperor's Children - RCN)
  • Terroxer El Rojo (Thousand Sons - PAS)

Danmarksmesterskaberne i 40K for Hold 2026 Won by Grumpy Old Bastards

The weekend's second largest teams event, this was a 240-player, five round event which took place in Denmark and had three teams finish 4-0-1, including Grumpy old bastards, Drekar, and Øst for Kasakhstan. Grumpy old bastards managed to barely squeak out a win on tiebreakers. Congrats to:

  • Martin Bræmer Nielsen
  • Michael Roune
  • Paul Bridge
  • Simon Darre
  • Thomas Donslund

Prague GT - Fall 2026 Crushed by Moravian Eagles

It was a big weekend for European teams events, and the Prague Fall GT was a 175-player, five round tournament held in Prague. The Moravian Eagles won the event with a 5-0 record and were the only undefeated team. Congrats to:

  • Antonin Man
  • Dan Sochor
  • Jan Tlustý
  • Luis Stiemerling
  • Lukáš Tölg

The NTL 8 Player Championship

The biggest teams event in the US was the NTL 8-Player Championship, a 112-player 8-man teams event taking place over five rounds. The even was the final tournament of the 2026 American Teams Championship season and took place in Cleveland, Tennessee. The top two teams each received qualifier tickets to the 2027 World Championships of Warhammer.

The mid-Atlantic-based team Nemesis won the event as the only undefeated team, going 4-0-1 with a narrow draw to ProTabletop in the final round. Congrats to:

  • Ari C
  • Dan Meloro
  • Jack Murphy
  • Jeffrey Kolodner
  • Joshua Campbell
  • Noah Keeth
  • TJ Lanigan
  • Zach Point
Marneus Calgar in the Armour of Antilochus. Credit: SRM

Space Marines Still Dominate Without Deathwatch; Custodes Finding Their Place

Turns out that Space Marines are still very good. Some events have begun to use the WCW FAQ, resulting in a general dropping of Deathwatch as an ally option, though they are still very solid. Space Wolves and Dark Angels take up the banner as the Marines of choice for the refined competitive player, while generic Marines still follows with an above 50% win rate.

Custodes are not one of top armies this week but they pulled both an above 50% win rate but also snagged two undefeated results! No event win this week but they are on pace to sang one soon and as the codex gets more fully explored I won’t be surprised if they are a faction of interest once their main predator, Space Marines, hopefully gets nerfed.

Thousand Sons and T’au left the dumps with huge win rates, but a tiny player size means this was most likely a fluke. Blood Angels, CSM, Leagues of Votann, Emperors Children, and Chaos Knights round up the bottom. A lot of these I expected but I am surprised to see Blood Angels so low after their decent results in the past few weeks. Hopefully the slate early next month can salvage some of these lower performers who have been stuck here for a long time, like Chaos Knights

40k Christmas Battleforces Up for Preorder  

At a retail value of $255 dollars the new Christmas battleforces are live and ready to purchase. As expected the CSM box sold out within the first few minutes - turns out Defilers are really hard to get still. The Chaos Knight box also sold out quickly, but the rest are still live and kicking if you want to jump into one of the factions, though I may steer you away from the Blood Angels box, it simply looks kinda sad. If you missed our review of the new battleforces, you can find them here.

Innes Wilson (white shirt) and Jack Murphy (USA shirt). Credit: Rob "TheChirurgeon" Jones.

Player Focus: Innes Wilson 

The current #1 player in the ITC/WGR, Innes is on a dominant streak this year, also holding the #1 rank in Stat Check's Elo scoring. He's currently the odds-on favorite coming into this year's World Championships and looking to break out this year and take home gold after failing to make it out of the group stage in 2025. Innes is an incredibly talented player who's won a number of majors and teams events this year and the current captain of Team Scotland. While often known for playing Space Marines or Genestealer Cults, he's known as one of the best faction hoppers in the game and has recently been tearing it up with Death Guard - he can perform well with pretty much any faction in the game. Innes is also a writer for Tabletop Battles, so look up some of his Warhound-exclusive articles here or the Stat Check podcast if you want to hear from him more directly.

Alongside our coverage of the whats-what of 40k each week, Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson joins us to provide some quick updates on the movers and shakers in the competitive scene of some of the other games we cover here at Tabletop Battles!

Tomb Kings and Blood Bowl Goblins Up for Preview

Big news for scary skeletons and silly little guys, as this week’s pre-order preview on Warhammer Community shows that we can expect new Old World Tomb Kings and Blood Bowl Goblins very soon.

The Tomb King release makes available separately most of the items that were released alongside the recent Tomb Kings reinforcements box, including resculpts of a wide array of the Skeleton models that makes up the bulk of the Tomb Kings line infantry and cavalry options. Alongside this wave of releases comes a new Arcane Journal, The Doom of Lord Carrard, which adds new options for both Bretonnians and Tomb Kings, as well as a new Army box for Tomb Kings featuring a new Skeleton sculpts and an all new Royal Herald sculpt, both mounted and on foot.

Meanwhile, in a very different part of the world-that-was, the Night Goblins are bouncing onto the Blood Bowl pitch with their new team, the Eight Peaks Loonatics. Consisting of Night Goblins, Squigs, and even several Night Goblins riding Squigs, the Loonatics lean on bending the rules and wacky play to bounce around the other team to victory. Alongside the new team and their accessories, Games Workshop is also releasing two new Star Players, the hulking Troll with a mind for ball Ripper Bogrot and the craziest, dizziest Night Goblin player of them all, Fungus the Loon.

All of these items will go on pre-order this Saturday, with a full release on December 5th.

The US Drives the Weekend’s Biggest AoS Events

We had not one, but two large Age of Sigmar GTs over the weekend, one singles and one teams, both of them coming to you hot from the North American region.

For singles, the Oklahoma Open (OKO) was easily the largest singles event of the entire weekend, drawing 76 players over five rounds. The final results is another win for the elves of the Mortal Realms, but this time with way less trees like we saw with last weekend’s results and this time with way more murder. While there were two undefeated players at this event, Wyatt Moran’s Daughters of Khaine took 1st place from battle points, with Moran leaning on Morathi, a big block of Blood Sisters and a big block of Witch Aelves to get work done. The other undefeated was Justin Foster on Khazalite Holds, bringing in the newest faction with a bang by bringing in a tough brick of Gholems supported by a wide range of goodstuff and even Gotrek for some Fyreslayers flair.

Meanwhile, the Old Town Throwdown was a 100 player teams event over the weekend held in California. After the dust settled, out of 20 teams represented only 1 team emerged undefeated, TSD Horde Sucks, Alliance FTW. While their taste in Horde versus Alliance is objectively wrong, their team composition wasn’t at all, featuring Flesh Eater Courts, Stormcast, Idoneth Deepkin, Soulblight, and Maggotkin. Congratulations to the TSD team for the win, as well as to Wyatt and Justin for their undefeated performances at OKO!

Poland Celebrates Da Red Gobbo with a Kill Team Teams GT

The folks over in the Polish Kill Team Metagame broke what was a quiet few weeks for Kill Team while we wait for new Approved Ops with one of the largest teams events we’ve seen for Kill Team in a while, Da Red Gobbo Tournament - Drużynowe Mistrzostwa Polski. This event had 72 players all divided up into 3 man teams, who played over 6 rounds for the top spot. Competition at the event was fierce, with no team ending a true undefeated record. The 1st place team did get pretty close though, with Lubikon I taking the top spot with a 5-0-1 record. The team composition featured Canoptek, Raveners, and Spectre Squad, which gives Lubikon a healthy variety of maps and teams that they can play into well no matter the matchup. Canoptek are generally good no matter the map or matchup, while Raveners remain a terror on Tomb World even after the nerfs and Spectre Squad when played well are extremely hard to pin down on Volkus. Congratulations to the Lubikon I crew for the big win for Da Red Gobbo!

Protoss Starter by Blake Law

StarCraft Breaks Into GT World with Huge Polish Event

We’re right back to Poland with our next event to look at, this time leaving the world of Warhammer 40,000 and into the world of StarCraft.

Likely ranking as the largest starCraft Miniatures Game event to date with 93 players, the Oficjalne Mistrzostwa Polski StarCraft Tabletop Miniatures Game took place over five rounds and saw players bring two 2k point lists from the same faction, one of the two lists to be selected each match up. Despite a slow start out of the gate, the recent buffs to Zerg were enough to push them into a competitive spot with Klusek piloting them to the top spot and an undefeated record. While Klusek changed around his upgrades used in each list, his main army core remained the same between both, leaning on plenty of vilelings, a bile launcher and hydralisks for ranged support, and the two hero dream team of both Kerrigan and Zagara for plenty of mayhem. Congratulations to Klusek on the win!

Rebel Forces Take Star Wars Legion Gold at OKO

We’re ending the same place we started today over in Oklahoma for the Oklahoma Open Legion GT, one of the largest events for the recently revitalized Star Wars Legion from Atomic Mass Games (AMG).

The event, which is a part of the official tournament circuit for AMG, took place over six rounds, with players bringing 1000 point army lists to the competition for first prize. While there was representation from all of the games factions, the Rebels in particular did extremely well at OKO alongside the Mandalorians, with both factions each taking 3 of the top 8 spots. The Rebels edged out the Mandalorians in the end though, with Colton Evans winning first place as the sole undefeated of the event. His list leaned heavily on some of the most iconic heroes of Star Wars, like Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, and Lando Carlrissian, with some specialist infantry and an AT-RT to support, which was enough to beat out Aidan Cone’s Republic forces in game six to take home the gold. Congratulations to Colton for taking home the top spot!
