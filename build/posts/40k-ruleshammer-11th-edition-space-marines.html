[40k] Ruleshammer 11th Edition: Space Marines

October 16th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Ruleshammer 11th Edition: Space Marines, a tour of some of their odder rules and interactions that I’ve been asked about! If you have a Space Marines questions you’d like to see answered in a future update to this article, please do send them via this form! 

Changelog

Published: 2026-10-16

Current Q&A

How much does a Black Templar’s Gladiator Valiant cost?

The base cost is 165. The MFM is pretty clear on this, but the App confuses things. In the Black Templars MFM the cost of your first Gladiator Valiant is 145 and then +10 per Multi-melta. Two Multi-meltas are mandatory according to the datasheet, bringing the base cost up to 165, same as a Space Marine Gladiator Valiant. The Templars variant can then optionally take a third Multi-melta for another 10 points.

However the Warhammer 40k App doesn’t reflect this accurately; if you add a Gladiator Valiant to a Black Templars list as of writing, it will suggest the cost is 145 including two free Multi-meltas. You might hear many players suggest that the App is the single source of truth in 11th edition 40k, but I would suggest that’s only the case when there’s no other source for the rule, which for many rules in 11th there simply isn’t a lot of the game is now solely in the app. However in this instance there is, the MFM, and it clearly states that the cost is +10 per Multi-melta and two are mandatory.

Black Templars Gladiator Lancer. Credit: SRM (We don't have photos of every variant of model in every scheme, sorry)

Can I rapid ingress in a Drop Pod and then use the Overwatch Stratagem on the unit that just disembarked?

Rainbow Warriors Captain with Intercessors in a Drop Pod by Craig "MasterSlowPoke" Sniffen

Yes if the unit assault disembarks. Drop Pods remain a special transport in 40k, being one of the few (if not only) that requires its embarked unit to disembark as soon as it’s deployed. This disembark can be a rapid disembark (one of the variants of the normal disembark move) or an assault disembark move but the outcome differs based on which one you choose. If the unit uses rapid disembark (which it would be required to if it doesn’t use assault disembark) then the unit would be unable to move further. This is because units that disembark this way are required to follow all the rules the transport had to follow.

RAPID DISEMBARK When a unit uses the rapid disembark mode after its TRANSPORT makes an ingress move, the models must follow the same rules and restrictions as that TRANSPORT did. For example, if that TRANSPORT had to be set up more than 8" from all enemy units and not within your opponent’s deployment zone, the same applies to the disembarking unit.

And one of those rules is the inability to move again after making an ingress move.

AFTER MOVING: Unless otherwise stated, until the start of the next Charge phase, your unit is not eligible to make any other type of move.

However, the assault disembark move doesn’t have this restriction.

It’s probable that this restriction should be included in this disembark move, but as of writing it does not so for now at least this combination works.

Does this mean that PHOBOS or SCOUT SQUAD units can disembark, overwatch, and move whilst remaining hidden?

Krakenguard Phobos Marines. Credits: That Gobbo

Yes. Unlike the Execute and Redeploy enhancement, the Phobos Shadow Force Detachment rule is triggered by having shot generally without a phase restriction.

PHOBOS SHADOW FORCE. When a friendly PHOBOS or SCOUT SQUAD unit has shot:

    • Those attacks do not prevent that unit from being hidden.
    • Or: That unit can make a normal move of up to D6". That unit is not eligible to declare a charge until the end of the turn.

Note that the charge restriction only lasts until the end of the turn, which in this case would be your opponent’s turn. The enhancement however is limited to having shot “in your shooting phase” so this combination doesn’t work with it as Rapid Ingress is limited to your opponents movement phase.

Ironstorm Spearheard gives you one re-roll for hit and wound rolls made as part of Ranged Attacks. Is that one per weapon, unit, phase, game?

It’s one of each per activation for the unit.

IRONSTORM SPEARHEARD. Friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES VEHICLE units’ (excluding WALKER units) ranged attacks can:

    • Re-roll one hit roll.
    • Re-roll one wound roll.

So if the unit has 10 attacks to resolve, it can re-roll one of those attack’s hit rolls and one of the subsequent wound rolls.

Does this stack with other re-rolls?

Yes, but a re-roll can not be re-rolled.

01.05.02 Re-rolls

...

    • You can never re-roll a dice more than once, and re-rolls happen before modifiers (if any) are applied.

So abilities such as the Gladiator Lancer’s would increase the number of dice you can re-roll, but not allow you to re-roll the same dice more than once.

Aquilon Optics: This unit’s ranged attacks that target a MONSTER/VEHICLE unit can:

    • Re-roll one hit roll.
    • Re-roll one wound roll.
    • Re-roll one damage roll.

Some players might suggest that the ONE element of these rules means they don’t stack; I think this interpretation stems from a misapplication of a ruling regarding mission objectives.

One

When a card states ‘one’, underlined, it means exactly one, not one or more.

This only applies to mission cards, it’s not a general rule about “one” in all instances throughout the game, at least as written. Until this ruling is included in the core rules and made broader in scope, though, I’m confident saying it doesn’t apply to these abilities stacking.

Can a model with the Wolf-Touched enhancement be attached to a Wulfen Dreadnought?

Space Wolves - Credit: keewa

No. Leader lists are not keyword based; they are unit based, except for some inquisitors who have "BATTLELINE IMPERIUM INFANTRY" in their list on the MFM. Every other entry in these lists is a unit name. Whilst unit names are also keywords, that doesn't change the outcome of this question. The Wolf-Touched enhancement allows the equipped model to be attached to Wulfen or Wulfen with Storm Shield units.

LEADER: WULFEN, WULFEN WITH STORM SHIELDS

ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only. This unit has:

    • WULFEN.
    • +2" M.

If this list was keyword based, the second entry wouldn't be necessary, as Wulfen with Storm Shields units have the WULFEN keyword.
