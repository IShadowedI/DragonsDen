[40k] Tabletop Battles Hot Take: Rules Updates for the 2026 World Championships of Warhammer

November 5th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Games Workshop published a new site today for their Warhammer Events, and with it, the long-awaited rules updates for Warhammer 40,000 ahead of the 2026 World Championships. This rules update, intended for use at the 2026 World Championships, act as a sort of “stopgap” between the September Balance update and the next, outlining a number of rulings and common rules problems ahead of the event, giving players some much-needed guidance on a few things.

In this article, we’ll be covering the changes, what they mean, and just as importantly - what they missed.

Core Rules Updates

Most of the rulings and updates here concern characters, specifically characters attached to units, though there are a few things here about terrain and movement.

Attached Characters

  • Revived characters which come back as a new unit lose their ‘while leading abilities’ benefits
  • If an attached character’s BODYGUARD unit had an enhancement and is destroyed, the character loses the benefit of the enhancement
  • If you split a unit with an attached character into multiple units, only the part of the unit which retains the attached character benefits from the leader’s benefits
  • If you gave an upgrade enhancement to a CHARACTER leader/support unit and attached it to another unit, when your attached character is destroyed, the attached unit loses the effects of the leader’s upgrade enhancement.

Desperate Escape Tests and Units Which Can Move Through Models 

If you fall back with a unit that can move through all types of model, you don’t have to activate Desperate Escape mode when making your Fall Back move. Makes sense, but this serves as a good catchall for abilities that work like fly but aren’t, such as Necrons phasing through walls.

Fighting on Death: Act Like You Have 1 Wound Left

This clears up questions around bracketing on vehicles and other big units.

INFANTRY/BEASTS/SWARM models don’t move vertically when going over dense terrain features that are less than 2” in height.

Not sure I’d ever seen this come up but sure.

Units selected to shoot but which make no attacks remain hidden.

This is a fun quirk, in that you can select a unit to shoot to get some benefit, then opt to make no attacks, and not lose your hidden status. This also applies if you are selected to shoot, but for some reason don't get to (e.g. your opponent activates a Stratagem that stops a unit being targeted. Good for the shoot-and-scoot crowd. Edit: Not quite - our Discord has points out that rule 04.03.04 specifically says that to qualify as having "shot" a unit needs to make at least one attack. Since most shoot-and-scoots happen after a unit has shot, if you make no attacks you won't get to do them. Some of the other things below such as opportunistically deciding not to shoot as AdMech do still work!

TheChirurgeon: This one caused a powerful stir in our competitive discussion channel, because the question of “can you activate a unit to shoot and make zero attacks?” is a touch controversial. As written we believe this to be the case, given the sequence for ranged attacks has you nominate a unit to shoot, then for each model in the unit you can pick one or more ranged weapons that model has to make attacks, giving you the option of picking zero. This also matters for the Adeptus Mechanicus, where the Baffling Data Screed ability on Tech-Priests lets them roll a D6 when their unit is selected and on a 2+, they can stay hidden after shooting. With this particular ruling you can now select them to shoot, roll, and if they fail the role, opt to shoot no weapons and stay hidden, risking nothing.

This also has big implications for the Phobos Strike Force, where the detachment rule lets you choose to stay hidden or move D6” after shooting - now you can opt to shoot and make no attacks, staying hidden and also moving D6”. 

Stratagems with Multiple Targets

You can’t use a Stratagem which requires a stated number of units if you don’t have that many eligible units, i.e. if you need to target two friendly units and you only have one, you’re out of luck.

Devastating Wounds and Damage Modifiers

They’ve added an FAQ stating that abilities that reduce damage work against [DEVASTATING WOUNDS]. One would hope that this comes with an actual rules addendum in the formal update, as it is currently just a “because we said so” FAQ rather than an actual rules entry. Previously whether these abilities worked on Devastating Wounds depended entirely on the specific wording of the effect.

Strategic Reserves and Ingress Moves

  • If you have an ability which allows a unit to to treat the battle round number as one higher than it is for a unit in Strategic Reserves, you can use it to make an ingress move in your first turn.
  • If a unit goes back into Strategic Reserves with an ability that says it has to make an ingress move in the/your next Movement phase, it can do so on the first turn.

These were both pretty clear and used in tenth but somewhere along the way in the transition to 11th wording changed and we needed new clarification for them.

Surge Moves don’t have to go the full distance, apparently.

A model making a surge move doesn’t have to move the maximum distance permitted by the move. What are we even doing here?

Mission Updates

There are four FAQs covered here, three of them relating to Lions of the Emperor and the Unleash the Lions stratagem. The first two cover the unit splitting and persisting effects - for both the Consecration primary mission as well as the Beacon secondary mission they are no longer eligible units for scoring. The third Lions interaction is that if the unit being targeted is condemned, then it does count as leaving the battlefield. All reasonable answers here, nothing too surprising - but good to see clear answers on these.

The last entry is a bit of a head-scratcher - it states that if you are being set up within 6” of the battlefield edge and there is a terrain feature at 6” from the battlefield edge you can perform actions on this terrain feature (e.g. Plunder) or contest it if the terrain feature is an objective.

TheChirurgeon: I don’t understand this at all. If the visible distance between a terrain feature and the edge of the table is 6”, then isn’t it implied that the terrain feature is more than 6” from the edge of the battlefield? How does deploying within 6” put you on the terrain? This suggests that a terrain feature 6” away from the battlefield edge is now considered to be within 6” of the battlefield edge, which OK makes some kind of sense but has really bad implications for terrain features that are on the line of your deployment zone - they’re now “in’ your deployment zone. Thankfully I don’t think this puts any new terrain features/objectives into deployment zones on the current layouts, but that feels more like a bullet dodged.

Faction Updates

Most of these updates cover outstanding FAQs, with some small tweaks to profiles for Space Marines and Chaos Space Marines.

Adepta Sororitas: You can use Miracle Dice Generated from a Character’s Death when they stand back up with Divine Intervention

This resolves an interesting timing question around when those dice are generated and used for the purposes of standing up with more wounds.

Adeptus Custodes: Shield-Captains can use their Master of of Ka’tahs ability to activate both the Dacatarai and Kaptaris abilities in the same phase

Jag the Power Armor Enthusiast: Custodes Shield Captains can in fact activate their stance re-up in the same phase! While this was assumed, the confirmation is very welcomed and makes shield captains even better than they already were.

Unleash the Lions

There were a number of rulings and changes here to a very complicated Stratagem:

  • Using this Stratagem does not count as destroying the unit.
  • The unit does count as having left the battlefield.
  • Using the Stratagem does not spread persisting effects to the split-off models.
  • Using the Stratagem does reload once per Battle Abilities on the split units.
  • Splitting a unit effectively breaks focused abilities like the Blade Champion's +1 to wound as the unit no longer exists but wasn’t destroyed, and a new unit cannot be targeted.

Jag the Power Armor Enthusiast: Lions of The Emperor… GW, Jesus, you had to say this much? To Summate: No, using Lions doesn't count as destroying them; they do count as leaving the battlefield; no, persisting effects do NOT stay on all the models once you use Unleash the Lions. (Failgrim catches another nerf indirectly from this, for the love of Slaanesh GW why do you hate him so much?!) You also now reload an Allarus Captain's once Per Battle abilities if he's part of the Unit that splits off! This is a very good buff for Allarus Shield Captains. Because here, let me teleport him over to push a button or hold an objective late game! Or reload a stance if you used it last round, for just the SC, Dread Host stonks go UP with this little note! We’re still not done! Here, want to make AdMech players and anyone with a targeted +1 to wound or similar ability in general lose the last bit of hope they didn’t even know they still had? Just wait for them to target the unit, and then use unleash the Lions next turn and break it! This certainly won't be a bad idea and cause feels bad moments, nope, not at all!

Aeldari: Dragon Knights can use multiple Dracolithe tokens against the same unit that ended a move within 8”.

Stunt on those hoes.

Astra Militarum: When using Summary Execution or Brutal Disciplinarian to remove battle-shock, you don’t regain the effects of issued orders.

Those soldiers apparently forgot what you were talking about earlier. They’re cool now, though.

Astra Militarum: A battle-shocked Kasrkin unit can use its Warrior Elite ability to pick an order.

This apparently doesn’t count as issuing an order, and you don’t “become” battle-shocked. So they can have an order applied to them, as a treat. You can’t improve your OC with this - it becomes ‘-’ when Battle-shocked - but you can use it to get Take Aim on your last two meltagunners, which is nice.

Blood Angels: Weapons and Base Tweaks

Mephiston’s Plasma Pistol ganed [HAZARDOUS], the plasma pistol on Death Company Captains gained [CLOSE QUARTERS], and the Twin Heavy Bolter on the Death Company Dreadnought has [RAPID FIRE 2]. They did not fix the thunder hammer on the Blood Angels Captain lacking Dev Wounds. Dante is on a 50mm base now in the Tournament Companion.

Chaos Daemons: Pink Horrors can still split if the attached CHARACTER survives.

You’ll have to kill absolutely everything in one activation if you want to avoid splitting.

Chaos Space Marines: Weapon Tweaks

Chaos Bikers get S5 AP-1 combi-bolters and Nemesis Claw get S5 close combat weapons. Sure, now make Bikers 4W like Outriders.

The Deathwatch Terminators Thing

Deathwatch Terminators saw one major change - their plasma cannon was reduced from Strength 9/10 to 7/8, bringing them in line with the plasma cannon wielded by the Deathwing Terminator Squad. They also added a caveat to Dark Angels army rule entry stating that Deathwatch Terminator Squad units do not gain the DEATHWING keyword despite being Terminator units.

Curie: This was needed; for anyone not aware you could run Deathwatch Terminators using the Deathwatch Support detachment and they’d benefit from the Inner Circle Task Force detachment as well, giving them +1 to wound against units on the vowed objective marker. It gave them access to an additional fight on death stratagem, as well as a stratagem for setting up from Deep Strike outside of 6” instead of 8”. As sad as this makes me as someone who was running the army and abusing this interaction, it’s for the better.

Jag the Power Armor Enthusiast: I really don't know if I agree with the decision to punish Deathwatch players’ units for this. Yes it's a needed nerf, but there were better ways to nerf this, especially as this doesn't solve the other major combo of Ultramarines taking DW Terms and using Guilliman and the Terminator Detachment to supercharge two squads. The easier solution would have been to say that Deathwatch Terms do not benefit from other Chapter abilities, so that way the UM/DW Castle of Death doesn't instantly delete armies. Or, hike the points on Plasma Cannons or limit them to one per 5, which would cut down on this problem immediately.

TheChirurgeon: None of these people are “Deathwatch Players,” only people running “Deathwatch Terminators,” and I’ll bet all the money in my pockets that when they do they’re typically painted as some other chapter. But yeah, generally the Deathwatch Terminator situation sucks and the Deathwatch Support Detachment needs to be violently uprooted from the game.

Jag: You are of course right, this is meta chasers abusing what was likely meant as a fluffy detachment but now anyone who wants to play Deathwatch itself catching a stray because of the Competitive Crowd abusing GW's oddball writing choices with Deathwatch on the whole. There are better ways to fix this in my opinion.

TheChirurgeon: I am once again banging a drum labeled “LEGENDS DETACHMENTS” to cover stuff that looks cool and lets armies mix in ways that can break the game.

Jag: Or, you know, add a specific thing, like a mode for narrative play that is long-form, and has consequences and rewards for fluff centric play. It could even have special rules for each army… oh well, such a thing is probably too complex for a small company like Games Workshop.

Death Guard: Shambling Wall Nerfed/Fixed

A set of changes to Shambling Wall make it work as intended in 11th edition, so now you no longer get to roll saves prior to picking up your Poxwalkers. Also note that Devastating Wounds from critical wounds fall outside of this sequence and so can’t be in the pool of wounds that go to your Poxwalkers when you absorb attacks via the Stratagem.

Drukhari: The Double-cross Stratagem

All of these rulings are about the Double-cross Stratagem from the Kabalite Cartel Detachment. This is another ability where you’re passing wounds off to another unit, and now this has been updated to reflect 11th edition sequencing, having your opponent pick a wound in their pool, end the attack sequence, and do mortal wounds equal to the attack’s D characteristic. They then repeat these steps until there are none left. Devastating Wounds don’t add to this pool and can’t be absorbed, and you get to roll your Feel No Pain rolls.

Imperial Agents: Detachment Tweaks

You can theoretically pick Detachments from outside the book now.

Imperial Knights: Sir Hekhtur and Oaths

Canis Rex no longer counts as having left the battlefield until Hekhtur is destroyed, so it won’t trigger key effects like being Condemned. When you make your Lay Low the Tyrant oath as an additional oath for Heroes of Legend, that’s also when you pick the character in your opponent’s army.

Iron Hands: Their Detachments can’t have other Chapters.

A tweak they made across the board here on some Chapter Detachments which were clearly missing some intended wording and allowing for a lot of potential misuse.

Necrons: Triggering Reanimations and Reactive Subroutines

Now when your Warriors get a re-roll on their protocols from being near a Canoptek Reanimator, they also get to re-roll the D3 additional wounds from the Nanoscarab reanimation aura. And if you use the Reactive Subroutines Stratagem on Wraiths, it will trigger the unit’s Wraith Form ability.

Orks: Grot Shields Changes

Both of the Ork items here relate to the Grot Shields Stratagem in the Runt Swarm Detachment. The wording on this has been adjusted and cleaned up so after the hit rolls step, your opponent picks one hit, ends the sequence for it, and does a mortal wound to your GRETCHIN unit. They repeat this steps until they’re out of hits or the Gretchin unit is destroyed. This gives the opponent substantially more power and control over the sequencing of attacks and how they’re allocated, and could make a big difference if they have a lot of different weapon types to throw in. There’s also clarification on how this works with [LETHAL HITS] and [SUSTAINED HITS] - they work as you’d expect.

Raven Guard: Shadowmark can’t have other Chapters.

Thus ends our long national nightmare of Guilliman Shadowmark.

Space Marines: Boltstorm Gauntlets lose [Close-Quarters]; selecting doctrines for a unit doesn’t count for the army doctrines.

Two quick clarifications here and Boltstorm Aggressors take another hit.

Space Wolves: Their Detachments can’t have other Chapters.

Similar to tweaks elsewhere. No, they didn’t fix the stupid Wulfen enhancement. No, we don’t know why they didn’t, either.

Ultramarines: Blade of Ultramar can’t have other Chapters.

Just a common sense move.

World Eaters: Defiler and Rhino Tweaks

The Defiler gets rules specifying the model it shoots with the Unleash Wrath ability had to be set up this phase, while the Rhino’s disembark ability specifies it’s a Rapid Disembark. This means that if it arrived from reserves this turn, the disembarking units cannot be within 8” of any enemy units.

Final Thoughts

This is… well, some of these are fine. The Shamblerot fix needed to happen, as did the Deathwatch and chapter-specific updates. But this document causes a few new problems - the notion that terrain listed as 6” away from a table edge is within 6” of that table edge is… certainly something, and has bigger implications we’ll feel later, I’m sure. The rules on leaving the table are also strange, and not having to move maximum distance with surge moves which say “as close as possible” feels wrong and strange.
