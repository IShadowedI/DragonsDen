[Warmachine] Tabletop Battles Reviews: Southern Kriels - Kithguard Fortress King

July 31st, 2026 via Goonhammer

The Kithguard Fortress King is the latest in a venerated line of mega Dire Trolls. For those unaware, the Trollkin and Dire Trolls of Warmachine are like humans and apes; same root ancestor but divergent paths in evolution, which is why Trollkin can more easily tap into these beasts on the battlefield. King class Dire Trolls are those who simply haven't stopped eating or aging. Constantly and ravenously growing becoming more primal and humongous in scale, taking the appearance and traits of their environment. Suffice to say that usually does not include spontaneously growing guns or axes larger than buildings. Good ol’ Kithguard recycling and ingenuity has partnered with a group of particularly agreeable Mountain Kings to form a new heavily armed coalition of death.

Credit: Steamforged Games

Thanks to Steamforged Games for providing us with a preview copy of these rules.

General Overview

This is probably one of the best support pieces for the 120mm scale that Warmachine has ever seen. Regardless of the loadout across its 27 to 31 pts of options, you are granted some incredibly powerful effects that will pair well with every single caster in Kithguard! That is very exciting for a class of model often relegated to whatever lists can make its numbers work.

Credit: Steamforged Games

On top of the standard Dire Troll rules of Snacking and Regeneration, alongside the standard colossal rule of Bulldoze, you also get:

Over the Top!

The number one reason to bring this mountain of multitudinous meaty muscle, Over The Top! lets you ambush, "Within 1 inch of the base of the Fortress King," then base to base with the first model for units. Savvy Kithguard players and smart melee players who have had to get around anti-charge mechanics will tell you that base sizes themselves are a threat extension. You can reasonably get at least two models in a unit of Throat Cutters or Kithguard Infantry into melee with anything within 5” of the Fortress King, let alone what that gives you during Stormcraw’s Feat turn where that is extra range extension for models that can move/charge after this ambush! The ability unfortunately does not allow you to deploy Chomper or Tarja's Jungle Trolls as Steamforged has expressly called out its exclusively for Units (this also rules out a plethora of nasty Solo options as well). The only other rule this expressly doesn’t work with is Extended Ambush, as that ability replaces the normal ambush distance, so no deploying 3” from the King to start. It is also not a trapdoor, so Craghorn’s feat can’t trigger off of it.

Moving Fortress

Just ambush enabling alone would at best tie the Fortress King with my claim of Best 120mm Support Piece. What puts it over the top is the list of optional abilities it can pick from turn over turn.

Reinforce is not your standard recursion ability. Most abilities return grunts; Reinforce returns small or medium based trooper models. Both weapon attachments and command attachments are defined as troopers - you can return a slain Kithguard Sergeant or Scrappers! The main limiter here is it leaves out Steelbacks, who are the army’s premier models to be volunteered (voluntold?) as Shield Guards by Logan Grindstone.

Vantage is crazy, letting any warrior models within 3” ignore enemy cover when shooting. That is often game winning on its own, and definitely a welcome tool for Gunnbjorn’s gunline builds. Gunny ignoring cover will terrify a lot of leaders.

The last rule, Armory, is a fun option that brings flexibility post-list writing. The concept is great; your Kithguard Infantry have options locked in at list construction, and sometimes they wind up not having the best tools for the job. This lets you swap out those weapon options for the better ones for your situation. Undoubtedly excellent, especially late game.

Credit: Steamforged Games

Build Options

That is all the built-in effects. What can you spend points on?

Alongside a default RNG 14 AOE 2 14/8 Heavy Gun and a RNG 2 POW 19 Fist, you can pick from different Heads, Left Arm, Embankment Guns, and Guns for your right arm. To break down every one of these insane options would double the size of the article, but it's worth highlighting which casters want what options very specifically.

Bagadibawm loves the cheap ROF 2 POW 14 Flamethrower and access to the Undergrowth Animus from the Rockmaw. Multiple base POW 14 Flamethrowers (with a bonus die due to Fuel for the Flames) is a major upgrade for his list’s output. Access to a DEF reduction animus that he can cast for free against cagier low ARM targets greatly expands avenues for assassination and scalpeling out key targets.

Wroughtmorne and Gunnbjorn love the Optical Array head for the Keen Eye Animus (Eyeless Sight) and the Targeting Flare from the Heavy Grenade Launcher. Gaining Eyeless Sight helps their ability to land spells and gunshots as needed. Targeting flare helps fix the fundamental issue in the faction with denying Stealth and Concealment outside of sprays. Prior to the Fortress King Kithguard's main anti-stealth tech was Spyglass from the guard tower; savvy opponents would simply ensure the static guard tower did not have line of sight to key models. Having both the melee arms be RNG 3 meaning you don't have to pick a sub-optimal choice to get better melee threat. That is a sigh of relief for Kithguard players who often have to make that choice on their Mistborn Dire Trolls. By default the Fortress King will threaten 11” in melee, or 13” with Bagadibawm, Craghorn or Wroughtmourn.

Stormcraw, Tarja and Craghorn just love access to the default package even though Tarja and Craghorn’s Feats expressly do not work on it (Colossals not being allowed to be placed and Craghorn explicitly calling out trapdoors respectively). Overall, Stormcraw is the biggest winner in all this. His feat is heavily telegraphed, so smart players could easily blunt his ability to ambush his unrestricted allies - that gets a lot harder in the face of a Colossal with a LOT of space to cover.

Credit: Steam Forge Games

Stormcraw is the obvious first spot for the King. He is fine with the cheapest option since it lets you fit in more stuff for the Feat turn. The Vorogger and Jungle troll are High DEF especially with the Guard Tower next to the high Wound/ARM ratio of the Fortress King forces your opponent to line up the right tools for the right target. All 3 take Deceleration very well to make them hard to disrupt early then they're ready to go on your big push turn where their best use is being the targets of Positive Charge for all your charging models. A lot of variety, a lot of fire power, and backed by a beat-stick Warlock to clean up the late game. The big thing the King brings is a way to force your opponent closer to stop ambushing from him or scare them back from objectives.

Credit: Steamforged Games

Final Thoughts

MasterBall: There is no other way to put it but the Fortress King is the missing piece to the Kithguard puzzle, where before there were issues with high ARM targets and anti-stealth you now have a one stop shop to sure up a variety of list holes. Competitively you could have two lists with two completely different loadouts supporting two entirely different strategies and that versatility will serve this model greatly through future yearly balance updates. Not even close to the highest damage potential colossal in the game, the Fortress King will nevertheless embed itself into Kithguard lists for years to come. Trish: I view the Fortress King through a different lens than MasterBall. (It doesn’t help my main Kithguard opponent goes for the assassination every game - he doesn't believe turn 3 exists.) I love the armory and reinforce rules. If I were putting a Fortress King on the table I would run it with minimal other beasts and lean towards lots of infantry. A unit of throat cutters popping out of ambush, shooting at RAT 7 and POW 16 (Sneak attack is amazing) and ignoring cover will put some serious hurt into your opponent. Access to Eyeless Sight also unlocks some terrifying ranged tech. With Eyeless Sight, Rune Mark, and Vantage, Gunny ignores all the most common anti-ranged tech in the game.
