11th Edition Detachment Focus: Shadowkeepers

October 3rd, 2026 via Goonhammer
Following hot on the heels of the Emperor’s Angels come his Praetorian Guard. The Adeptus Custodes get an early 11th edition Codex, and with it a slew of new rules and Detachments. In this article, we’re taking a look at the Shadowkeepers Detachment.

Even amongst the Adeptus Custodes, some duties are uniquely grim, and for those dark tasks the Shadowkeepers are unleashed. Let's find out what that looks like on the tabletop.

Thanks to Games Workshop for providing us with a review copy of this Codex and a provisional version of the Munitorum Field Manual. Any rules or points costs we reference are subject to change until they are published on the Warhammer 40K App.

Shadowkeepers

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

Force Disposition: Purge the Foe

Unique: SHIELD HOST

The Shadowkeepers are the Shield Host devoted to tracking down and imprisoning all the scariest things the Imperium doesn’t want you to know about. They’re tasked with being the SCP unit in a universe where we think the horrors are already out and about and this Detachment emphasizes this with a suite of abilities and strats that are aligned with holding objectives, fear, and pinning things down.

Shadowkeeper Allarus Custodes. Credit: Pendulin

Detachment Rule: Wardens of Darkness

Melee attacks that target friendly non-vehicle/monster Adeptus Custodes units within the range of an objective with Strength greater than their Toughness have -1 to wound rolls. Custodes units are already hard to move and adding -1 to wound without having to field Sentinel Sodalities is a sweet treat when playing the objective game while giving a ‘defend the prison’ vibe.

Favoured Ka’tah: Kaptaris

When readying the Kaptaris Ka’tah, Shadowkeepers units can select one enemy unit engaged with them and force them to make a battle shock roll at -1. The same unit cannot be selected more than once for this ability (boooo). As we’ve begun to learn in the early days of 11th edition, battle-shock is a far more powerful effect than it has ever been. This is a nice-to-have effect for sure when you need it, though perhaps less effective than in other armies where you’re not often planning on just straight killing what is touching you.

Enhancements

  • Genalchemic Warding (30pts): Usable on any Adeptus Custodes model, this grants them a 5+ Feel No Pain. Pricey but so are your characters and keeping them alive seems good. 
  • Unstoppable Destroyer (25pts): Adeptus Custodes Infantry model only, this upgrade allows the unit to move up to 4 inches for their pile-in/consolidation moves and, more importantly, they can choose any consolidation mode when making a consolidation move regardless of the before moving restrictions. This is an insanely powerful ability for sneaking onto objectives or slipping into/out of combats you find yourself staring into the face of and is reason enough to take this Detachment on its own. While most things a Custodes’ player touches in close combat will evaporate, in the times where it doesn’t this will win you the game. Always remember that they’re not stuck in with you, you’re stuck in with them. 

Stratagems

  • Grim Responsibility (1CP): During the Fight phase when an Adeptus Custodes Infantry unit is selected to fight, that unit’s melee attacks gain Lethal Hits: Character/Monster. Decent stratagem to have when surrounded by giant bugs and daemons given the weight of attacks an Adeptus Custodes unit can put out.
  • No Escape (2CP): During your opponent’s Movement phase when an enemy unit completes a Fall-Back move, one friendly unengaged Adeptus Custodes unit that is within 6 inches of said unit that has fallen back may declare a charge. When selecting charge targets you can only select enemy units that have made a fall-back move this phase and are within maximum charge distance. Now THIS is a spicy meatball and well worth the 2CP. Being able to force ongoing combats and halt cheeky fallback maneuvers is huge. Also, note the timing on this one as there are situations where you’ll be able to wait out all of your opponent’s fallbacks if you find yourself in a real cluster and then pick the juiciest target when everything is said and done, though you do open your opponent up to being able to sidestep your best targets if you wait too long.
  • Indomitable Guardians (1CP): During your Fight Phase, when an enemy unit has fought, you can select an Adeptus Custodes unit that is within range of an objective and that is eligible to fight with this stratagem. That unit gains the Fights First special rule and must be the next unit selected to fight. We’ve seen this stratagem for Custodes before and it has always been great as 1 CP interrupts are hard to find.

Detachment Combos

As with most of the Custodes Shield Host Detachments their power is pretty-self contained. If you believe that battle-shocking is your favorite way to play this is a great Detachment to team up with the Null-Maiden Vigil. Otherwise I kind of like Honoured Companions here. Honoured gives you a second reactive move in your arsenal and the potential to have two units ignoring consolidation rules to really mess with your opponent’s control of the board AND furthers your gameplan of being extra good at standing on objectives with Companion’s Watch. 

Is It Good?

Custodian Sentinels. Credit: Pendulin

Shadowkeepers has some great stratagem support and one helluva enhancement but suffers from the fact that there are Shield Hosts that are just objectively better from a straightforward power perspective. Still, it isn’t necessarily a bad choice as it opens up some interesting play patterns and allows for some incredible objective play.

Final Thoughts

Aesthetically, Shadowkeepers is my favorite looking Custodes army with its dark colors and gold trim and I do see some really fun play lines for an army that wants to lean more into the Custodian Guard and less into the other toys Custodes can bring to the table. It’s just neat.
