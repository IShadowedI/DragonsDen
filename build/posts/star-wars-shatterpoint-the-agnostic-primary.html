Star Wars Shatterpoint: The Agnostic Primary

August 20th, 2026 via Goonhammer

List building is often one of the most exciting parts of playing a war game.  It’s for the same reason drafting in card games is often so impactful.  Players love to design and build out strategies that adhere to their playstyle or synergize with a concept they enjoy.  I personally love to play high synergy lists and will often find myself building my Shatterpoint squads around keywords.

When it comes to synergy, the first thing a player should look into are primaries.  This isn’t some revolutionary idea, primaries are often the first thing you decide on anyways but why is that?  Primaries are objectively some of the strongest individual models in the game, but it’s their identities that often set them apart.  As an example, I want to look quickly at General Obi-Wan Kenobi and his Knowledge and Defense.

Obi has a fantastic identity that allows clones a massive defensive buff.  In fact, his ability is so powerful that it reshapes the way support design is handled for Galactic Republic.  You can’t put anything too defensively strong because this ability will simply push it over the threshold of what is fair.  AMG has worked diligently to keep Obi a unique model to play with and against without dominating the competitive scene.  He is simply something people have to know how to fight against and is a natural balance to the meta. 

However, General Obi-Wan isn’t the type of primary we want to focus on today.  His synergy encourages players to build a very specific style of list, one that includes Galactic Republic and the ability to easily acquire hunkers.  Instead, I want to show off the world of list building by covering some of the most versatile primaries in the game.  These are the Agnostic Primaries, as I like to call them.

What determines if a primary is Agnostic or not?  I look at these key features.  

  1. Neutral Identities - Does the model have an identity that anyone, or at least most models, can benefit from?  Are the prerequisites to its use not dependent on tags or other restricting factors?
  2. Multi Era - Can this primary take a wide variety of models underneath it?  Or is it stuck in an era with only a handful of options?
  3. Tag Count - Will this model have the opportunity to synergize with several models thanks to their wide spread tag allocation or are they limited to just helping Droids?  

Using these factors, I’ve made this list of Agnostic Primaries that we can start brainstorming lists with.

  • Ahsoka Tano, Fulcrum
  • Ahsoka Tano, Jedi No More
  • Baylan Skoll
  • Cad Bane
  • Cassian Andor
  • Hondo
  • Kit Fisto
  • Luminara Unduli
  • Lando Calrissian
  • Lord Maul
  • Princess Leia, Charming to the Last
  • The Mandolorian

The nature of being agnostic in this game means you can pair these models with most any secondary and support combo without much struggle.  There are some that will be clearly better at universal synergy than others.  Vader 1, for example, was a top tier neutral primary that could play with anything that favored a melee attack. I didn’t include him in this list now as his identity has now been limited to just include Galactic Republic and Empire.  These are decent era options but limit your list building if you want to actually use that identity. 

Compare him to Princess Leia who can dash any model plus revenge trigger off any wound inflicted to her team.  It’s clear she will excel with spies and Rebels yet she can realistically play with any model in the game and be competitive.  Let's demonstrate a couple of these characters with some list building!

The bogeyman from last season was Captain Cassian Andor and a massive reason for that was because of this agnostic topic.  We saw Cassian everywhere at worlds however the lists he was paired in weren’t always the same outside of… well… Baze and Chirrut.  Why?

This model can offer a ton of flexibility to nearly any team with an identity that says, “Any wound will trigger a dash from my team.”  An unconditional revenge trigger is fairly strong which is why Mother Talzin almost made it to my list.  Unfortunately for her, she lacks what Cassian can offer.  He brings 9sp which is currently the highest available in the game giving him access to nearly all models from his era.  He is limited to 2 in Reign of the Empire and Galactic Civil War, but at least those eras are flush with options.  Finally, “this town is ready to blow” was a brutal ability that plays fairly now yet is still a strong neutral option to help out the team.  Applying near global damage to your opponents models is a fantastic catch up mechanic and damage is universally helpful in Shatterpoint.

Cassian does suffer slightly from needing some synergy meaning you will always want to build SOME Rebel units in his list but fortunately, there are plenty of good options.

Strong pairings could be: 

  • Spy Revenge with Princess Leia, Charming
  • Rebel Strong with Leia Freedom Fighter, Han Solo, or Kanan
  • Adaptable Scoundrels with The Mandalorian, Hondo, and Cad Bane

  • Surprise Attack with Baylan Skoll

I feel that last combination is what interests me the most and also helps us tackle two agnostic primaries so lets build something there.  We now have two primaries to build under, Cassian and Baylan.

The intention with this list is to abuse Rebel hunker synergy with Baylan to hit hard yet still be defensive.  Now most of our options are going to come from Cassian here as Baylan’s big downside as an agnostic primary is his era restriction.  While he has a fantastic neutral identity, he can’t really provide many units to abuse it which honestly might be a good thing in terms of balance.

So we can build out his list in one of two ways.

Option 1 is to just play his box as is.  It’s a solid box that synergizes well with itself even if it is melee focused and can be punished for that.  I wouldn’t run this box into Obi 2 for example! 

Option 2 is funny and while I do think Covert Mandalorians LOVE to be with Baylan, it’s probably not great as Greef has no synergy with his squad.  We should consider that Cassian does enjoy dashing around tie breaking models though when someone is wounded!

To keep things simple, we are going to build with list 1.

Now it’s time for Cassian!  This list will be fun because we have plenty of options that love to hunker.  I’m going to start with the supports as I do think it’s a little more narrow there.  Rebel Pathfinders and Commandos are actually the only ones that can hunker on their own turn as an ability.  While Commandos are genuinely the stronger choice, I want to see what Pathfinders can do here.  Pathfinders are often shot off the table thanks to their 4 die roll vs ranged so getting to keep our hunkers will go a long way.  They also don’t roll many dice offensively but have a decent expertise so I expect that will come up often. 

For the secondary, we have a few good options actually.  K-2S0 is the standout choice as he can hunker rebel models but that will only be Cassian and Pathfinders.  Interestingly enough, Lobot can tactic dash anyone but the supports as neither are scoundrels.  We could potentially build a little better to put Lobot in the game, and I do love the idea of using Security Network on Shin Hati, but I think we should shelf our other 5pt secondary for now.  Chewie actually has SOME synergy with hunker in that he can dash towards any primary and hunker them as a tactic.  Is that good here?  Not sure!  But a hunkered 12 health wookie is pretty tough to take down!

There are some interesting options with Ezra, Kallus, Obi-Wan, Sabine Wren and Boushh but none of them give the obvious buff that K-2SO does.  Sure this list does struggle with force management but the synergy and power is there.  Oh wow, we ended up just building our two base boxes and combining them together which shows that AMG really knows how to synergize the box releases!

How about we build one more list to show that not everything will end up being just the base box.  Let's pick a model that has been consistently used with every faction since Shatterpoint’s initial release.  Please welcome: Luminara Unduli!

It’s no secret that Lumi has one of the most powerful identities in the game.  Any wound your team provides will dash a model on your team.  The obvious benefit here is that selfish powerhouse models that don’t support the team now have an outlet to do so.  Secondary crosshair has been an example of that since he often kills most targets but has no innate ability to move other models. 

Lumi can be played with a plethora of models if only because of the identity but her main focus does seem to be with aggressive lists or Galactic Republic to abuse tag synergy.  Her weakness is being locked in an era that won’t have certain models but if we look at most competitive lists, she can actually slot into any of them without much concern.

All of these lists function on their own and often have a different primary selected then Lumi to maximize their synergy yet Lumi still holds her own and even brings a unique competitive edge.  Consider Iden who would normally work with Grand Inquisitor in the Lumi spot because he offers force refresh and movement to the inquisitors.  Is it worth taking that out for the mobility bonus and healing factor that Lumi offers?  In some cases, yes… especially when you consider how much protection is in the list to mitigate chip damage.

I even placed a Mandalorian list in there to showcase how sometimes being in a single era isn’t always a detriment.  The Armorer has no access to Gar Saxon and his Commandos without using Lord Maul or Luminara.  It just happens to be a huge bonus that both of those agnostic primaries are also quite good for the list!  

Hopefully all of these examples and details on what makes a primary versatile will encourage you to find your own unique brews.  The re-balance has opened up list building in Shatterpoint which means these primaries are going to flourish.  Be sure to share some of your list builds with the team and comment below!
