Detachment Focus: Berzerker Warband (Updated October 9, 2026)

October 9th, 2026 via Goonhammer
In this series of articles we take a deep dive into a specific Detachment for a faction, covering the faction’s rules and upgrades and talking about how to build around that faction for competitive play. In this article, we’re covering the Berzerker Warband Detachment for World Eaters.

The "main" Detachment for the World Eaters is the Berzerker Warband, an oft-updated version of their tenth edition Index Detachment. In many ways it's their most basic offering, giving a strong, persistent boost to the army and a number of solid stratagems, though these are primarily focused on buffing Berzerkers.

Changelog

  • 10-09-2026 (Latest): Updated for 11th Edition and the September Balance Dataslate
  • 4-26-2025: Updated for the 10th edition Codex
  • 6-1-2024: Initial Publication

Detachment Cost: 2 DP

Disposition: Purge the Foe

The Berzerker Warband is all about running Berzerkers. While there are a lot of rules in this Detachment which benefit other units, two key Stratagems - Apoplectic Frenzy and Berzerker’s Wrath - can only be used on Berzerker units. And given one of those lets you Advance and Charge, this is the best place to run your a 20-man unit of Berzerkers led by a Lord on Juggernaut. Otherwise, there are some solid utility pieces here, some powerful effects, and enough value for other units that this Detachment works well as a combined arms Detachment for World Eaters. The +1 Attack bonus is just generically good all the time.

The cost of this Detachment dropped from 3 DP to 2 DP in the September balance update, making it much better as an option and justifying the change in Detachment rule.

Detachment Rule: Relentless Rage

Friendly WORLD EATERS units' melee attacks have +1 A.

This used to be +2S and +1A on the charge, but was changed in the September Dataslate to just be +1 Attack all the time. That's about the same for Berzerkers, who went to S5 on their chainaxes and now have +1 Strength on the charge, and a little worse for everyone else - you no longer get S10 Power Fists with Terminators, for example. The upside to the change was the Detachment's DP cost dropped, letting you combine this with powerful effects in other Detachments, such as Wrath of Khorne in Vessels of Wrath.

Despite the loss of a Strength bonus on the charge, this is still a powerful army-wide rule to have, just making every unit you're fielding between 20% and 50% more effective in melee. It's still plenty good on Terminators, but generally speaking this rule is better the fewer attacks you started with. You especially want it for your Khornate Eviscerators, as those only get 3 Attacks, and will still hit at S9 on the charge.

Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Enhancements

The four Enhancements here are pretty solid, and can go on any Character.

  • Berzerker Glaive – The bearer gets +1 Attacks and +1 Damage for their melee weapons, excluding Extra Attacks weapons. This goes just fine on a Master of Executions but looks great on a Daemon Prince, where the combination of D4 Dev wounds and 10 attacks can do a lot of damage.
  • Helm of Brazen Ire – Reduces the damage characteristic of incoming attacks by 1. This is fine. It's best suited to a Daemon Prince, where the high toughness and wound counts give it the most mileage. If you really need to put it on a Lord o Juggernaut, that's fine too.
  • Favoured of Khorne – Each time you make a Blessings of Khorne roll, you can re-roll up to two of the dice as long as the bearer is on the battlefield. With fewer easy ways to re-roll dice in the Codex this is still an important ability… but not crucial unless you desperately need to get Angron back.
  • Battle-Lust – You can re-roll Charge rolls for the bearer’s unit, and if the Unbridled Bloodlust Blessing of Khorne is active, you get +1 to Charge rolls as well. This is a useful one for your key game plan of getting guys into melee, and note that it is all Charge rolls, so if you choose to make a Heroic Intervention you have a little less chance of an embarrassing failure there as well.
Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Stratagems

There are some solid Stratagems in this Detachment, and only two of them are locked to Berzerkers. Frenzied Resilience is the only 2 CP Stratagem in the bunch but they’re all pretty solid.

  • Blood Offering (Epic Deed, 1 CP) – Use in any phase on a WORLD EATERS unit from your army that was just destroyed while it was within range of one or more objective markers that you controlled at the end of the previous phase. Pick one of those objective markers to stay under your control until your opponent controls it at the end of a phase. This is great for forcing an opponent to come to you - use it for those times an opponent shoots you off an objective to sticky it and hold it, preserving your "Hold 1 outside your deployment zone" primary objective. The classic play here is to throw it on a solo lord on juggernaut, then park him on an objective as a durable holder and if an opponent kills him, boom - use this to sticky. These days I think you'd be better off with a unit which can use the HIDDEN rules.
  • Hack and Slash (Battle Tactic, 1 CP) – Use in the Fight phase on one WORLD EATERS unit that hasn’t fought yet and that made a Charge move this turn. Until the end of the phase, improve your AP by 1. A really useful Stratagem in an army where a lot of your common melee weapons are AP-1. Only getting +1 AP on the charge means this won’t be useful all the time (it won’t be triggered by a blood surge), but remember that it works after Heroic Intervention.
  • Frenzied Resilience (Battle Tactic, 2 CP) – Use in the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets. One WORLD EATERS unit targeted by those attacks gets -1 Damage. Identical to the Index version except for the name, and just as good - while also being potentially easier to pull off as your foot Daemon Princes can now make it cheaper. That’s huge if you plan on going into something like a unit of Bladeguard with a Justiciar to give them Fights First. Dropping this against Power fists or some other 2-damage threat to turn 10 dead Berzerkers into 5 is massive. It's also huge on a unit of Terminators, and that's something you might actually do now that you can combine this Detachment with Butchers of Khorne.
  • Skulls for the Skull Throne! (Strategic Ploy, 1 CP) – Fight phase, just after a WORLD EATERS unit from your army destroys a CHARACTER or MONSTER model. Make a Blessings of Khorne roll and activate one Blessing of Khorne to be active until the end of the battle round in addition to your existing ones. Helpful, but be aware that you can’t use this to resurrect Angron any more, as his Reborn in Blood ability only works at the start of the battle round. This is fine, and what you really want this for most of the time is to activate 6” Consolidate moves. It can also be helpful if you’re going second and can pop this in your opponent’s turn.
  • Apoplectic Frenzy (Strategic Ploy, 1 CP) – Use in your Movement phase, just after a Khorne Berzerkers unit is selected to Advance. That unit can Advance and charge. This is super important now that you don’t have access to this through the Blessings of Khorne, and one of the only ways to advance and charge in the army. Paired with 10” movement on Berzerkers led by a Lord on Juggernaut, this is how you get those nasty turn 1 charges.
  • Berzerker’s Wrath (Strategic Ploy, 1 CP) – Use in your opponent’s Shooting phase when a Khorne Berzerkers unit would make a Blood Surge move as a result of those attacks. Instead of rolling a D6, your unit can automatically move up to 8”. A smart opponent will be playing around Blood Surge, but being able to get a guaranteed 8” off will make that a lot harder to do. This essentially makes it impossible for them to keep you out of range while being on an objective with you.
Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Playing This Detachment

If there’s one Detachment where you want to take the 20-model unit of Berzerkers, it’s this one. If you're doing that, the recipe here is easy: 20 Berzerkers with four Khornate Eviscerators, led by a Lord on Juggernaut. They move 10”, through walls, with the ability to Advance and Charge. You’ll want to keep a Daemon Prince nearby to pop Stratagems like Frenzied Resilience and Hack and Slash on them.

Now, with that said: Do you actually want to run one of these? Or two? That’s a tougher question. You definitely want to build around Berzerkers in this Detachment, but some of those will naturally want to be in Transports. Most of the time you're going to find it more convenient to run two or three 10-man units, led by Kharn and a Master of Executions with the Berzerker Glaive.  The Master of Executions is quite the beast he used to be, but he’s much cheaper now and worth adding to your Berzerker units for the ability to re-roll blood surges and to just get another model with a 2-damage attack in the unit. Kharn's a great way to get re-rolls and I’d strongly consider looking at a single unit of 3 Eightbound for their +1 to hit and wound aura.

Because the +1 Attacks benefit works on everything in your army, it’s worth pointint out that a ton of other units - Daemon Princes and melee vehicles like the Helbrute and Maulerfiend - really benefit from this upgrade, and can do their best work here. That makes the Berzerker Warband your best combined arms Detachment, even if you get some niftier buffs from the Goretrack Onslaught. [LANCE] after disembarking won’t do anything for a Defiler. The benefit is also great on Jakhals, who need the extra boost to be legitimate threats - and having them at S5 is a huge improvement that lets them punch way up.

When building lists with Berzerker Warband there are really two options to consider for your second Detachment:

  • Vessels of Wrath. This version gives you better Characters, and you'll likely want to take a third unit of Berzerkers led by a Master of Executions or a Lord on Juggernaut, maxing out on your Character counts for the army - that extra AP is huge, especially when stacked with the Hack and Slash Stratagem - going to AP-4 is a big deal for your units.
  • Butchers of Khorne. Terminators love getting +1 Attack and -1 Damage from the Frenzied Resilience Stratagem. They also love having +1 OC or +1 WS from the Enhancements in Butchers of Khorne, and getting an extra +1A from Focused Ferocity or +1 to Wound from A Trophy for the Throne. If you're going Terminator-heavy, this is your pick.

Strengths

  • Advance and Charge. This is the only place you’ll find it, and only on Berzerkers. But it’s still huge.
  • Melee buffs. That +1 Attack is a solid buff, and something that every fighty unit in your army wants. Even Land Raiders suddenly look scary with 10 attacks hitting on a 3+ and lethal/sustained hits.
  • DP Cost. At only 2 Detachment Points, this one's a steal. It's also a good way to get Purge the Foe as your Disposition, if you want it. The +1 Attack combos well with everything.

Weaknesses

  • Berzerker-focused. A couple of key Stratagems here only work on Berzerkers. And that’s a problem because…
  • Berzerkers. As a unit, berzerkers are kind of underwhelming. Imagine Assault Intercessors if they didn’t have re-rolls to wound. Yeah. They can do damage in volume, but you need stacked buffs to make them work. And they die quickly. 

Final Thoughts

The Berzerker Warband offers an exciting and balanced way to play World Eaters. Thanks to the September balance update, it's also the best way to play the army, combining in solid ways with the army's other Detachment options to buff key units across the army.
