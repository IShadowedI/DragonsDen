Archetype

Vanguard Recon

Thoughts

Victory here for the excellent pressure / scoring combination of Vanguard Onslaught and Reconnaissance. Vanguard has always wanted to get in your grill and cover the board, and it can wrack up a huge score whilst doing so. Excellent game into Priority Assets and Recon sees it in a good spot in singles, and it's less worried about chucking itself into table quarters than some Recon lists are against Take and Hold. The Red Terror is a dream here, capable of shoring up any necessary scoring every turn played right. Tremendous work on the top placing!

Archetype

Imperial Agents Bucket of Stuff

Thoughts

A glorious top placing run for the Imperial Agents. This list is basically a bucket of stuff in Recon, utilising Kroyle to handout rerolls and help it bunch up into one hard target every turn. It can block and score with the best of them but pushing damage through is a white-knuckle ride. Incredible work getting through to 4th place!

Wings: Congratulations to Derrick for taking up the gauntlet I threw down at the IA playerbase in the balance update review. This list rocks!

The Best of the Rest

There were 5 more players on 4-1 records. They were:

  • 5th - Hunter Boyd - Adeptus Custodes (Lions of the Emperor) - Take and Hold: Custodes Allarus spam.
  • 6th - Nicholas "Nickron" Henry - Chaos Knights (Hunting Warpack/Houndpack Lance) - Reconnaissance: War Dog spam.
  • 7th - Christopher Thomson - Death Guard (Contagion Engines/Mortarion's Hammer) - Purge the Foe: Lots of Daemon Engines and a big brick of Deathshroud.
  • 8th - David Harris - Grey Knights (Warpbane Task Force) - Take and Hold: Triple Terminator and triple Dreadknights.
  • 9th - Quaid Harlan - World Eaters (Brazen Engines/Possessed Slaughterband) - Purge the Foe: A Defiler, two Forgefiends, and loads of Eightbound.
