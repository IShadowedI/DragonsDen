[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Up To Eleven pt.2

July 10th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Archetype

Ret Cadre Purge

Thoughts

T'au continue to march in early 11th, and this Ret Cadre list builds hard into suits to exploit Purge the Foe. Ranged armies are seeing the most joy with this disposition early doors - it helps them secure early kills, they can achieve less risky consistent kill mores across a game, and the opponent typically has to push them anyway or risk being blown apart. Ret Cadre is singing because you can tool up your crisis for every occasion which makes it extremely flexible in an emerging meta. Great work on the event win!

Archetype

KFC

Thoughts

The Chickens of Tzeentch are also enjoying the edition start, ignoring new cover with their psychic weapons, teleporting around catching excellent angles on the new boards, and benefitting greatly from missions which often score big in the late game if the opponent is dead, which is a board state they are very capable of generating. I like this list that mixes in Calvacade so you can push out some cheap Skullcannons early on, forcing out anti-big damage that the chickens can then retaliate against. An excellent template for victory!

The Best of the Rest

There were 9 more players on 4-1 records. They were:

  • 5th - Luke Cook - Deathwatch (Bastion Task Force/Librarius Conclave): Deathwatch Kill Teams with a Repulsor.
  • 6th - Stephen Brain - Orks (Rollin' Deff/Bully Boyz): Bully Boyz Nobz in Battlewagon action, exploiting the new Rollin' Deff detachment.
  • 7th - Eddie Chater - Leagues of Votann (Hearthguard Covenant/Mercenary Oathband): Near maxed out Hearthguard and Thunderkyn, tasty.
  • 8th - Brett Smith - T'au Empire (Advanced Acquisition Cadre/Retaliation Cadre): Crisis Suit spam with a couple of Riptides.
  • 9th - James Greensmith - Orks (Blitz Brigade/More Dakka): Meganobz with lots of Gitz and Lootas behind.
  • 10th - Jacob Whitehouse - Imperial Knights (Freeblade Company): A Castellan, Errant and Destrier leading some Armigers.
  • 11th - Adam Watson - Adeptus Mechanicus (Lords of the Forge/Cohort Cybernetica): Kastelan Robots, gun chickens and lots of trash.
  • 12th - Chris Cuerden - Adeptus Custodes (Might of the Moritoi/Lions of the Emperor): Bike and Venatari spam.
  • 13th - Jakob Stokke Grande - Chaos Space Marines (Huron’s Marauders): Terminator / Huron brick CSM with a single Defiler.

Thoughts

Another run out for the Crisis Suits / two Riptide package. This list seems to have emerged as a firm favourite for the Greater Good! Not a tonne else to say on it - it'll shoot you if you hide, and it'll shoot you if you don't. The challenge is going to be shutting it down! Great work on the event win.

The Best of the Rest

There were 3 more players on 4-1 records. They were:

  • 2nd - mido Alturki - Emperor's Children (Coterie of the Conceited): Two Daemon Prince, two Defilers, and Lucius leading some Flawless Blades.
  • 3rd - Remco Hentzepeter - Necrons (Awakened Dynasty): Two Wraith bricks and a big Skorpekh brick in Awakened.
  • 4th - Adam Charlesworth - Grey Knights (Warpbane Task Force): Purifier and Dreadknight spam.

Wrap Up

There you have it folks! T'au on the rise, but a wide range of other options giving them a run for their money. Loads of fun to be had out there now, get out and purge your foes!

Lowest of Men, out.
