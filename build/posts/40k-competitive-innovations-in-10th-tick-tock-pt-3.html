[40k] Competitive Innovations in 10th: Tick Tock pt.3

June 6th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

🎵Crabs, crabs will tear us apart, again🎵.

Event News

My local TO is running their first major later in the year, and the looming edition transition seems to have everyone holding fire on buying tickets. I cannot stress enough how much I am in favour of majors happening within 20 minutes of my house. You can find more details on the Facebook page here.

Archetype

Brandfast Thunderkyn

Thoughts

Some proper novelty to round out the top four, which we love to see - Brandfast Votann. This build uses lots and lots of Hearthkyn to control the table (with Grimnyrs to make the big units extra tough), designed to pull foes into a position where big Thunderkyn plays can obliterate them. The full squad with the Trivärg Iron-master is obviously the key play there, and has extremely high reach if you use Secure Positions to launch them out in your Command Phase. Once battle is joined, the threat of an Uthar-powered Vengeance Flare off the fortresses is also going to give lots of foes pause - sure, it probably doesn't one-shot their key threat, but what if it does? Love to see these options used so effectively together, and I think Grimnyr/Hearthkyn units are probably a sleeper for 11th as well, since the Grimnyr's ability to remove Battle-shock gets a lot better in the new world. Congratulations to Eliot for hitting the top four with something unique!

Archetype

Double Victrix Hulltramarines

Thoughts

This list looks hauntingly familiar, because it's very close to what I got smashed off the table with in the Windsor GT top eight. This list is just good at everything, with strong anti-heavy firepower from the Ballistus Dreads and big Repulsor guns, anti-horde from the smaller weapons of the latter, and solid brawling capability from two units of Victrix. Defilers outclass it a bit in pure value terms, but it's close enough behind that the players who've mastered it (as it's been around for longer) can still triumph. Congratulations Jonathan.

Thoughts

A high-damage spin on Invasion Fleet to close us out for the week. This build has Neurolictors, Hormagaunts and Primes to bait and disrupt the enemy, then full Ravener stacks to rip stuff to shreds in melee, or Tyrannofexes to blow them to bits. Although their armour save is mediocre, big Ravener units with access to a 5+ Feel No Pain are actually meaningfully annoying for the game's big threats to handle, and with repositioning and free Ingress they can reliably ensure they sweep something good before they're even taking damage. The bait and disruption package also seems really cheap here, and Neurolictors are on my list of units to watch in 11th because of the Battle-shock improvement. Congratulations to John, our final winner of the week!
