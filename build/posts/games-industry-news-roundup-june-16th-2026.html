Games Industry News Roundup- June 16th, 2026

June 16th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Here at Tabletop Battles, we know that it’s hard to keep track of all the news happening all the time in the games industry. So much is always going on, all over the place, all the time. With games of all sorts, and their related media, being an informed gamer can feel impossible some days. 

That's why every week, we help you stay in the know by rounding up five of the biggest recent stories in the gaming and gaming-related pop culture sphere in the Games Industry News Roundup. Our trusty news boy, Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson, is right here at the very real, and cool TTB newsdesk with the scoop.

Oh boy. Oh God. buckle up this week, news fans. This one’s a doozy.

Games Workshop Denies AI Use in Horus Heresy Box Art

Credit: Games Workshop

Last Monday, Games Workshop unveiled an updated box of MK IV Marines coming to Horus Heresy, giving fans a sneak preview of the new kits set to release with the upcoming Maximus Battle Group box. While many were excited to see this fan favorite armor mark updated, a few eagle eyed readers noticed something alarming about the art. One of the Space Marines had too many fingers, a common indicator of the use of AI in generative art. 

Games Workshop previously stated in its 2026 half year report to investors that the company takes hard stance against the use of generative AI, with CEO Kevin Rountree writing “We have agreed an internal policy to guide us all, which is currently very cautious e.g. we do not allow AI generated content or AI to be used in our design processes or its unauthorized use outside of GW including in any of our competitions.” 

The six fingered marine on the Maximus box art raised alarms in the community, with many criticizing Games Workshop for going back on their word on AI use. GW responded to these claims in a social media post denying the use of AI in the art, writing “Does that Space Marine have an extra finger? Yes, but it’s not AI or early signs of mutation among the Traitor Legions! The Horus Heresy art style, since its first edition in 2012, involves an artist blending miniature photography with art to create a dramatic scene. In this case, adding a little unexpected drama. So please, go easy on our artists. They are only (and completely) human.”

While GW states that the art is not produced with generative AI tools, the company hasn’t released any further information regarding the art, such as the artist behind the box art or the methods used to blend miniature photography and art, as the company wrote in the statement. 

EA Announces EA Advertising Program for Live In-Game Advertisements

Credit: EA

You best start believing in Cyberpunk dystopias, dear reader, because you’re in one. 

Yesterday, EA announced its new EA Advertising initiative, a program designed to allow for live in-game advertisements integrated directly into gameplay. 

“EA Advertising is expanding EA’s ecosystem by enabling brands to integrate directly into gameplay through dynamic, real-time placements, from stadium signage to custom in-game content, designed to enhance, not disrupt, the player experience,” EA writes in the announcement. “In these interactive gameplay environments, brands become part of the game itself, reflecting how players engage with advertising in real-world contexts. Brands can activate across live environments, tailoring placements to meet campaign objectives, and update campaigns with ongoing optimization informed by aggregated engagement insights.”

This is not EA’s first attempt to bring more advertising and brand campaigns into the forefront of its games. EA has been partnering with other companies for ad space in its EA sports titles for decades, and has only increased the presence of ads in their games since then. Most notably was the company’s inclusion of unskippable ads in UFC 4 in 2020, which was met with such a severe backlash that EA took the ads out of the game. Other less intrusive examples include EA’s partnership with Red Bull for a series of challenges in Battlefield 6. 

EA Advertising’s initial focus will, unsurprisingly, be in EA Sports titles, where dedicated ad space such as billboards and scoreboards can be used by members of EA Advertising’s partnership program to participate in “a new model for how brands participate in sports culture—moving beyond traditional sponsorship into co-created fan experiences built in, around, and beyond the game.”

Brands already confirmed to have partnership deals with EA Advertising for these live service ad campaigns include VISA, Lowes, Red Bull, Xfinity/Peacock, and Mountain Dew.

Paizo Forced to Restructure Following Distributor Bankruptcy

Credit: Paizo

While at first blush, Paizo has been thriving off of a number of successes, including the launch of its Paizo 3D service earlier this year, things have been difficult behind the scenes for the tabletop RPG publisher. Last week, the company decided to make those troubles public with the announcement that the company is laying off several employees as part of a restructuring process caused by the closure of Diamond Distributors last year.

Paizo CEO Jim Butler explained the company's financial troubles in a statement, writing that while the company’s sales of product at hobby retailers and online directly have been on the rise, the closure of Diamond Distributors put Paizo in a financial crisis. According to Butler, the closure of Diamond put over $10 million in Paizo stock in limbo, as Diamond was the main distributor for books to major retailers such as Amazon and Barnes & Nobles. Following Diamond’s bankruptcy, JP Morgan placed a lien on the company’s assets, which included Paizo’s large stock held in Diamond warehouses. Paizo and several other companies were forced into costly legal battles to reclaim this product. Additionally, Paizo was forced to deal with litigation from Diamond, who appealed a judge’s decision from earlier this year to terminate Paizo’s contract with Diamond for exclusive distribution rights. 

With the dust settled, Paizo’s been able to resolve the Diamond crisis to stay solvent and has partnered with a new distributor, but doing so caused Paizo to report a $2 million loss for last year while major retailer book sales are far below target. 

Butler reports that this loss has forced major restructuring at Paizo, which includes the layoff of 12 employees across several departments. Butler states that Paizo is working carefully with the Paizo Workers Union to conduct these layoffs, and affected employees will be given several protections, including a 20 notice for proposed layoffs in each department for the union to propose alternatives, and guaranteeing a week of severance pay for every year worked with the company with a minimum of two weeks. Employees may also volunteer for layoffs in affected departments, otherwise the least senior employee will be cut. 

Most affected by the restructuring is Paizo’s organized play department, which will see a decrease in published scenarios starting later this year, as “the needs of retailers, conventions, and home groups have evolved over time, and the cost of producing scenarios has grown significantly.” Paizo does plan to attempt to offset this with more initiatives designed to encourage player created scenarios and campaigns, and will launch these programs later this year. Paizo also plans to pause official Virtual Tabletop Support through Foundry for the foreseeable future. 

Xbox Studio Head Resigns, Multiple Studios Fighting to Avoid Closure

Credit: Xbox Game Studios/Microsoft

Meanwhile, things are tremendously, stunningly bad over at Microsoft and Xbox, as despite some recent good press, the company is in dire straits. 

On June 10th, the new Xbox CEO, Asha Sharma and CCO Matt Booty announced that the company would be undergoing another round of mass layoffs over the next 100 days as part of a “hard reset”, stating that the current state of the company “cannot continue”. The pair of executives cite several factors in this decision, including a dismal 3% profit margin, high operating costs, hardware shortages, and being spread too thin across too many studios.

There was little time for speculation as to who may be affected by this announcement, as multiple reports on Monday painted a bleak picture at what’s happening at Xbox. Firstly, Craig Duncan, the longtime head of Xbox Games Studios, abruptly resigned from his position. Duncan has been at Xbox since 2011, and was previously the head at Rare. Duncan took on the role as Xbox Studio Head in 2024, where he would be in charge of overseeing the plethora of smaller game studios owned by Xbox. 

In his announcement email to employees, Duncan wrote, “When I stepped into the role of leading XGS 20 months ago, my purpose was to serve our studios, our teams, and the people making our games. Together, we set out to deliver high-quality games, strengthen the cultural fabric across our studios, and help shape the future of the business. I’m proud to say we delivered many flawless launches that drove business success for the company.”

Shortly after this news broke, Jason Schrier reported at Bloomberg that several prominent studios, Ninja Theory, Double Fine, and Compulsion Games, are in active negotiations to avoid closure. According to Schrier, all three studios are attempting to decide on terms with Microsoft leadership to potentially buy themselves out and go independent. Should an agreement not be reached, Microsoft plans to go through with closing these studios, affecting hundreds of workers. Even if any of these three developers manage to spin off from Microsoft and save their studio, it's almost certain that doing so will still force the studio to fire members of its staff to stay afloat. 

While all three studios are on the smaller side, each of them has produced notable crucially and commercially successful games. Double Fine saw major success with the release of Psychonauts 2 a few years ago, and Compulsion Game’s latest game, South of Midnight, won a Peabody Award for its writing, which Booty had publicly praised in the past. Ninja Theory’s upcoming Hellblade game, simply titled Senua, was featured as a part of Xbox’s Games Showcase 2026 as a part of Summer Games fest.

Currently, there are no updates regarding the fate of these studios, or any other potentially affected studios, but all of the companies under the Xbox Games Studios banner are advising their employees to prepare for the worst.

Dead By Daylight Brings Iconic Terrifier Character to its Roster

Credit: Behaviour Interactive

It’s been a hard roundup this week. Save us from this nightmare, Art the Clown. 

The inclusion of the terrifying Terrifier Icon was one of several announcements made during Dead by Daylight’s 10th anniversary stream last week. Marcy joins us this week for more on these exciting reveals.

Marcy: Dead by Daylight developer and publisher Behaviour Interactive celebrated 10 Years in the Fog on Sunday Night with an at times winding stream that hit the usual gaming entertainment stream highlights: Awkward crowd interactions, bad jokes, weirdly mistimed speaking from teleprompters, obligatory movie segments, and some actual announcements for the beloved 4v1 asymmetrical horror hide and seek game. Dead by Daylight remains the unchallenged champion of asymmetrical horror games, having outlived and outlasted just about every competitor. Following a 13 hour stream a few weeks ago to announce Jason Voorhees, Sunday's stream included a ton of new information. 

First and foremost, Jason will be the next killer added to the game officially this week following the conclusion of the PTB period. A new survivor, an indigenous man named Shane, will be joining the game shortly, likely by the end of June or early July. The community voted on Grimoire chapter will follow in August with a new original Killer and Survivor, with a new map/realm combo coming in December, "The Mall". The game is also getting a major graphical facelift, including redone character models, dynamic weather, and much more, along with auditory updates to the game. 

A slew of surprising collabs were also included, such as more Iron Maiden (with Huntress receiving an Eddie cosmetic), an Ice Nine Kills cosmetic collab (which somehow involved Tony Hawk), and more Walking Dead cosmetics, bringing Glen and Negan (as a survivor) to the game. The most surprising collab announced was Scooby Doo, which has been explained to only be survivor cosmetics and will not involve new characters or killers; they teased Dwight wearing a Scooby kigurumi during the stream.

Silent Hill is getting a collab skin featuring Hinako from Silent Hill f as a legendary skin for Cherly, and The Nurse from Silent Hill will become a Spirit skin, called "I Take Your Meds". Finally, the developers closed the show with one of the most desired but also surprising collaboration announcements: Terrifier will be joining DBD in November, bringing Art the Clown to the game, with the team promising that players will be surprised by the level of gore for his moris and how he plays to keep him true to character. 

The stream also included some indie announcements, with BHVR wanting to still publish other titles, and also that DBD will get some new game modes, including a 1v1 "Competitive" mode, a Zombie Scenario mode that looked to be very early alpha, and an upcoming anniversary mode called "The Black Banquet" that will feature survivors hunting for food while killers sabotage it. The somewhat infamous Casting of Frank Stone game to DBD pipeline is also confirmed, with Frank Stone coming to the game officially next year.
