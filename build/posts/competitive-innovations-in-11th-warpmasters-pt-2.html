Thoughts

It's a 9th Edition throwback final here, as two iconic 'loads of stuff' armies have at it for the crown. The Drukhari recon is a list we have seen a lot of now - probably the standout recon list at edition start, it has slowly watched as other faster, punchier armies gradually steal the mantle from it. That said, Drukhari have tonnes of assets in their favour and plenty of damage that lines up well into the Ad Mech. They don't love -1 to hit, and they don't love surprise battle shocks and feel no pains, but they can put the pressure on and rack up a big score as they do so.

The Mechanicus have the slightly more defensive scoring plan of Priority Assets to call upon, and a huge barrage of guns and small trading units to hit back with. They'll kill whatever Drukhari they see, it's about setting up carefully to connect into transports and the like (perhaps some tasteful wrapping) to frustrate Drukhari staging plans. I can see Cawl and Thule being pushed quite agressively here- they're good bait and scourge won't be able to interact with them much, so that will draw the other heavy hitting Druks units out for elimination.

In the end this one ran extremely close - with a score like that it could have been as simple as end game swing taking it home! Very well done Richard.

Thoughts

Richard demonstrates the cagey power of Ad Mech here with a magnificent win on British shores. We saw similar lists at WTC, including Jesper of Sweden's list. Richard goes wider on MSU infantry here, building tonnes of blocking and frustrating potential into the list whilst ensuring a very easy run to max points on Priority Assets. It's on the opponent to stop this thing hitting 100 points, and Ad Mech are at their best retaliating against pressure where their speed limitations are a bit less pronounced. The lack of melee here makes me a bit apprehensive, but Richard has guns and mission for days instead, and the results speak for themselves! Great work.
