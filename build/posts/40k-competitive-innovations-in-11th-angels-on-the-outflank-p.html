[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Angels on the Outflank pt.1

November 11th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Back to normal this week wth a healthy crop of events, and there's a bit of movement at the top. Dark Angels loom ominously over the metagame, which is not much of a surprise, but we've had huge surges for the Adepta Sororitas and Iron Hands in pursuit, as both factions find solid ways to adapt to the metagame. The World Championships of Warhammer loom ever closer too, and with the release of the FAQ for that last week, the stage should be set for people to conduct final testing. Let's see what's going on out there.

Archetype

Melee Wolves

Thoughts

Space Wolves lead the charge this week with a brutal pressure build, assembling an exciting array of elite Infantry pieces to crush the enemy. Furious Assault hammer Wulfen are extremely lit, as the Sustained gives them the flexibility to bonk most targets to death, while the stacked buffs on the Bladeguard squad also makes them versatile blenders. Logan, meanwhile, teams up with regular Terminators, who are extremely nasty with the combination of always-on Doctrines and the Great Wolf detachment rule. Their shooting is really strong, and their melee plus Logan's is obviously also a serious threat. The accumulated power here is tremendous, allowing the build to carve through even the most daunting foes (including a triple Norn builds we'll see in a second), and I'm sure Fenris has a lot of fancy new trophies adorning the fang. Congratulations Alex!

Archetype

Norn Bugzilla

Thoughts

Falling to the Space Wolves in a closely-fought final, we have the magnificent healing Norns. Guaranteed to ruin the day of any Take and Hold foe who hasn't brought overwhelming killing power. The setup here is pretty obvious - with three Norns you can dedicate at least two of them to just locking down the central objective forever, and the opponent will be on a rapid clock to clear them before being terminally outscored. That's non-trivial when they're healing, and also a Shadow turn will land at some point and disrupt their efforts. Cheap and cheerful support also gives the list plenty of board presence despite the tall investments, and I'll say that triple Pyrovore squads are way cheaper than I thought, and seem kinda interesting. Well done Mike.

Archetype

Crusader sand Eradipulsors

Thoughts

Black Templars also had a bit of a breakout week, albeit at fairly low player counts, and Celeste does a sterling job here of leading the charge. The "nice" thing about Eradipulsors is that they're so cheap you can have two of them and still build hard in another direction, which here is usd to bring along two big Crusaders squads. These, obviously, benefit significantly from the improved Marine toughness, and I'd particularly highlight the value of the squad with Helbrect and a Judiciar. Fight First is significantly less good in 11th than it was in 10th, but goes back up in value significantly when there's lots of melee threats in the metagame, and you can put it on a squad that's extremely hard to one-tap. Crusaders assuredly fit that bill, and this brick can be really tough for some armies to handle. Cool to see the Templars get some momentum, well done Celeste!

The Best of the Rest

There were 2 more players on 7-1 records. They were:

  • 5th - Camden Holt - Imperial Fists (Deathwatch Support/Ceramite Sentinels) - Take and Hold: Heavy Infantry galore wity a big Lysander squad to seize key objectives, Garadon with Eradicators in a Repulsor for big stuff, and Deathwatch Terminators as all-round quality pieces. Imaginging trying to kill that Lysander brick with -1 to Hit and the option for -1 to Wound from a Stratagem is pretty horrifying.
  • 6th - Durante "Glynisir" Bozzini - T'au Empire (Retaliation Cadre) - Purge the Foe: Fireknives and Starscythes supported by Hammerheads, going for longer ranged anti-tank rather than gambling on Sunforges.

Thoughts

Well done Brodie.

Thoughts

The crowd is chanting "Quad-o-Saur, Quad-o-Saur", and you better believe that one of the most brutal Ork builds we've yet seen is stepping onto the table. God it must have been so viscerally satisfying to realise fit in 2000pts. It's a total nightmare for anyone else, but you have to admire the beauty of it.

The Sisters are going to try and do more than that, of coruse, aiming to turn the hunt back on the greenskins. This...is not going to go super well. Normally you'd fancy Sisters to have a decent go at a matchup like this, as multi-meltas and Vahl are ideal tools for it, but the Sisters are light on those pieces (including no Vahl, very rare) in favour of more balanced board control. Good for some matchups, absolutely the last thing you want here - the Orks can bully the Sororitas something fierce, and have the speed to make that extremely problematic very quickly. I imagine if you're in the Sisters shoes here you look at the split flamer/melta Retributor squad in your army and just start crying. The fact that almost all the big threats have a Feel No Pain just adds insult to injury, as even if you do push some melta shots through, they can still spike through it.

Anything the Sororitas can do? The Triumph squad can be somewhat annoying, and maybe grab a point for longer than you might think, though has to watch out for Ghaz coming and ripping them apart with Precision. Really and truly, to win here the Sisters need both Repentia squads to fully pop off and cleanly lift a priority target each, and then maybe this is a game (albeit still very uphill). Odds of that feel nowhere near high enough, and a brutal trampling is the expected (and actual) outcome here.
