Archetype

RepEx Iron Hands

Thoughts

It's a breakout week for the Iron Hands, surging towards the top of the metagame with builds that combine their unique Characters with Repulsor Executioners and Dreadnoughts.

Please do not be alarmed. You have not time travelled back to 8th Edition. You're safe.

In terms of how it works, there's really not that much to explain - with Feirros and Var around, Medusa's Wrath provides some of the best re-roll support available to Marines outside Guilliman, and has some extremely powerful Stratagems, most notably Methodical Brutality and the one-two punch of Ancient Fury into Vengeful Animus to turn a Brutalis into a terrifying missile. Power of the Machine Spirit also creates some extreme failure cases for opponents who try and fail to kill the tanks.

In terms of exact components, most builds have at least one powerful Dreadnought and one RepEx, and you can slide stuff back and forth from there. Here, Arne has gone for a second RepEx, so Tactical Doctrine makes strong sense to keep them fully operational.

I, for one, welcome our new machine overlords. Congratulations Arne.

Thoughts

Our top four here is completed by Ultrawatch, albeit with a few shakeups from the norm. Florian's version cuts Calgar, removing the need for Terminator Storm Force, and instead builds a central brawler stack with Victrix/Cato/Caius, and augments the list's shooting with triple Eliminators and Shadow Force. With Guilliman's re-rolls, these are in fact a nightmare for their price tag, and provide a deep well of board presence to keep the build active even when the foe strikes back hard. Cool to see some experimentation with the formula, and well done to Florian.

C'tanless Cursed

Thoughts

Necrons wave their blood-stained banners as one of the few non-Marine/Imperium factions that are really thriving right now, deploying a high threat Destroyer build to crush the competition. Double Lokhusts gives you reliable damage into everything, and here rather than a C'tan, they're rolling with a Wrath squad to tarpit foes, and some powerful Characters in support.  These choices free up some points for a Skorpekh unit, which is a really solid trade piece to have, and also for a big Ophydian unit, a surprisingly lethal Ingress threat when they're S6 that can flex to scoring as required. With the metagame so lethal at the moment Wraiths really are the only thing that can reliably tarpit in the Necron roster (even more so than C'tan), so I'm not surprised to see this doing well, and it's going onto the list of builds I might audible into if my testing of other WCW armies crashes out. Do I want to paint three more Ophydians? No. But I might. Congratulations Kalle.
