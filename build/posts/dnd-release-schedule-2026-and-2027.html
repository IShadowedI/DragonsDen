DnD release schedule 2026 and 2027

October 2nd, 2026 via Wargamer

The DnD release schedule for 2026 has almost wrapped up, with just one season of launches left. We've seen horrors, and we've seen arcane marvels. Now, though, our eyes our looking forward to the upcoming DnD books of 2027. You can learn all about Dungeons and Dragons' latest books below, with handy summaries of what's just come out - and what's next.

If you're after more evergreen info, check out our guides to DnD classes and DnD races. These offer every bit of info you might need to start building your next character. For now, though, let's talk releases.

Here's the current DnD release schedule:

  • World of Warcraft
  • Dark Sun
  • Greyhawk: Crown of the Witch Queen
  • Star Wars
  • Untitled Greyhawk book
  • D&D Beyond Partnered Content
  • Most recent D&D books
  • Arcana Unleashed

D&D: World of Warcraft

Release date: Tuesday, November 17, 2026

Our mystery winter product is, in fact, the start of D&D's new Universes Beyond line. D&D: World of Warcraft takes us to the famed world of Azeroth. We're getting a treasure trove of rules here, with 13 WoW-specific classes, nine subclasses, and 40 specializations.

Wizards has also promised 16 new species, 60+ spells, and 25+ magic items. This might be one of the most ambitious D&D books of the year, but will the crossover be any good? Only time will tell.

Dark Sun

Release date: 2027

It's the one reboot we never thought they'd do! D&D returns to Dark Sun in early 2027, and it's not shying away from the apocalyptic's settings grit and gore. The two sourcebooks, Edge of Oblivion and Blood & Power, are D&D's first R-rated books, with so much edgy art that they'll come shrink-wrapped. They'll also come with an all-new Psion class, a guide to the setting, and a bunch of new character options.

Greyhawk: Crown of the Witch Queen

Release date: 2027

We return to D&D's oldest setting in Greyhawk: Crown of the Witch Queen. The big bad of this new campaign is Drelzna, the vampire daughter of Igwilv. You may also know Igwilv as Tasha, archfey, witch queen, and all around badass. We don't expect her daughter will go down easy…

D&D Star Wars

Release date: 2027

When Darth Vader walked across the stage of D&D's Gen Con keynote, one thing became clear: D&D Star Wars is making an official comeback. We don't know much about what the Season of Rebellion looks like yet. We do know, though, that it'll continue Wizards' new Universes Beyond strategy trialed with World of Warcraft. May the Force be with anyone who gives this unusual crossover a go.

Untitled Greyhawk book

Release date: 2027?

Wizards of the Coast has confirmed that one more official D&D book is in the works. We just don't know what it's called or when it's coming out.

What we do know is it's being developed by Luke Gygax, son of D&D's original creator. Luke's original character, Melf the Elf, is now a key part of D&D canon, and it looks like he'll be our starring guide for this Greyhawk tour. Art has been shared of Melf on the cover of this as-yet-untitled book, but that's as much detail as we're getting right now.

D&D Beyond Partnered Content

We're only getting a handful of official D&D books this year, but D&DBeyond remains a busy marketplace. That's thanks to the horde of partnered content coming to the platform. Here's a rundown of all the new, third-party books, and when they become available:

  • Exploring Eberron - January 27
  • The Pugilist Class - February 3
  • Faster, Purple Worm! Everybody Dies, Vol 1 - February 24
  • Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting: Part 2 - Early March
  • The Griffon's Saddlebag: Book One - Late March
  • Dr Dhrolin's Dictionary of Dinosaurs - April
  • Tom Cartos Sticker Packs - April
  • Northlands Worldbook - May
  • Northlands Sagas - May
  • Steinhardt's Guide to the Eldritch Hunt Player Pack - June
  • Valda's Spire of Secrets: Player Pack 2 - July
  • Vampire: The Masquerade - Bound by Blood - July
  • Legends of Greyhawk: Saviors of Safeton - July
  • Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone - August
  • The Field Guide to Floral Dragons - August
  • Legends of Greyhawk: Dreams of the Chained God - August
  • Legends of Greyhawk: The Tear-Stained Idol - September
  • The Magus Class - September 29
  • Legends of Greyhawk: Heralds of the Old One - October
  • Obojima: Tales from Yatamon - October 27
  • Legends of Greyhawk: The Ruins of Castle Greyhawk - November
  • Legends of Greyhawk: Beasts of Johrase - December
  • Warlord Player Pack - December 1
  • Herbarium - December 15

Most recent DnD books

Arcana Unleashed

Release date: Tuesday, September 15, 2026

Releasing in September, Arcana Unleashed is all about new magic options. That means a heap more subclasses, spells, magical feats, and unique items for your party inventory. It also means new guidance on working for magical patrons and factions, plus a little bit of adventure content to boot.

Personally, I'd call this the best 5.5e book so far. The ideas are original and exciting. The new character options are generally strong, with a few really interesting standouts. Not every concept Wizards introduces flies flawlessly, but there's far more good than bad.

Read our full 9/10 Arcana Unleashed review here.

Arcana Unleashed Deadfall

Release date: Tuesday, September 15, 2026

Arcana Unleashed Deadfall is a companion adventure for the previous book on the release schedule. Starting at level 11, this high-stakes espionage campaign takes you all the way to level 20. The goal? Infiltrate the Red Wizards of Thay in order to stop the necromantic machinations of one Szass Tam.

It's a solid concept, but Wizards sadly doesn't deliver. The narrative is shallow, the gameplay gets repetitive, and the high challenge level will either delight your masochistic party or drive them away altogether.

Learn more in my Arcana Unleashed: Deadfall review.

Ravenloft: The Horrors Within

Release date: Tuesday, June 16, 2026

First off the blocks is Ravenloft: The Horrors Within - a "Gameplay Expansion" (a.k.a. rulebook) that returns us to the Domains of Dread, featuring everyone's favorite romantically challenged immortal maniac, Count Strahd von Zarovich. Oh, and also Cthulhu is here!

Several horror-themed subclasses appear alongside revised 'lineage' species options and a heap of tempting yet terrifying Dark Power feats. Many of these feel familiar to longtime 5e players, but the remastered rules feel significantly more streamlined. Plus, the all-new Hollow Warden Ranger is seriously exciting.

Beyond player-facing options, we get stat blocks for a heap of Darklords, plus a whistlestop tour of the most famous dread demiplanes - including the Lovecraftian Innsmouth. There's some great campaign fodder here, though the stripped-back lore feels less rich than when we last saw it in Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft.

Find out why we gave the book a 7/10 in our full Ravenloft: The Horrors Within review.

That's all for now! Though, if you'd like to keep talking about Dungeons and Dragons, join us for a chat in the Wargamer Discord.
