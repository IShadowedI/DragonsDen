New Tabletop Battles App and Administratum Updates: August 29, 2026

August 29th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back to more app updates! We talked last time around about adding support for StarCraft in the Tabletop Battles app, plus some additional updates for Kill Team, Warhammer 40k, and Age of Sigmar. This time around we have some more new and exciting updates to share - not just around Tabletop Battles, but also Administratum.

If you haven't downloaded the app yet, you can get it through one of the links below:

Today's update is live on Android and iOS.

Tabletop Battles: The August 2026 Balance Update

We've updated the Tabletop Battles app to include the new layouts for missions in the 2026-27 missions pack, reflecting the layout changes from the latest balance update. We've also updated the Dispositions on a few Detachments which changed, and fixed a few lingering bugs.

As a quick aside, I'd like to take a moment to remind people that you can compare scores to opponents in the Tabletop Battles app using our Game Link feature - this has been around for years, and lets you view your opponent's score in app, and will alert you whenever your scores line up. It's a pretty solid feature that lets you square up whenever you need.

So why don't more people use it? Well the answer is because it's one of the few features which requires you to log into the app, and most people don't. We've always prioritized ease of use and speed with the app - our philosophy is that you should be able to show up at an event, download the app for the first time, and immediately play, no subscription or bullshit required. But that means some more complicated features can get missed. So if you're wondering why Game Link and "generate code" aren't showing up on your create game screen, that's the most likely reason.

Administratum 4.0

We've been hard at work on a new version of Administratum, and the first version of it has finally released. This is a big step for us for two reasons: The first is that we're finally decoupling Administratum from Crusade. This isn't just because Crusade seems to be gone in tenth Edition 40k; it's also because we wanted to open up Administratum to work with other game systems, so you could use it to run Age of Sigmar, Trench Crusade, StarCraft, Necromunda, or anything else.

To activate this version of the app, just click on the handy "V4" button on the upper left. NOTE: Your current rosters and campaigns won't transfer to V4. We're going to leave the old version alive for quite a some time while we build out new features and support in the app, so don't worry too much about losing this old data.

This first version has some cool new features for managing your roster, letting you create templates for common units and set some rules for how much things cost. It's not a full roster builder in that we aren't putting all the unit profiles in there or GW rules, but if enterprising players can certainly treat it as such if they want to put those values in. We've always thought Administratum could use some kind of roster builder, but one built around "what units do I own" and not "what list do I want to make." There will be more work to be done on this.

Of course, we also just support Crusade in the new Administratum, and will be working on updates to that framework as well. We have some plans for what we want to do around Crusade in the platform - stay tuned for those.

If you haven't used Administratum yet, give it a look - it's pretty great, and doesn't need Crusade now.

What's Next?

We're working on a few more features for StarCraft support, plus ongoing development on Administratum and some new Crusade-like things we're supporting. We'll also be updating support for Age of Sigmar to add the new Ogors rules and rules for the Sons of Behemat when those rules release. The work never stops here on our end but if we miss anything, let us know.

Why Use Our App?

We've seen this question pop up once or twice from players who aren't familiar with our app, or whose introduction is the War Journal in the Warhammer 40,000 app. Well, there are a few reasons:

  • Runs faster and better. Tabletop Battles is built in Flutter and not React. Flutter is less common but removes a lot of runtime jank that can bog down React Native apps. If you've noticed that TTBA runs faster, this is a primary reason why. Flutter generally outperforms React Native and uses less CPU and as a result, less battery.
  • Built-in stats screens. If you want more than just a list of your games, we've got you covered, and we're working on more personal stats you can access via our Edge web app.
  • Faction trackers. If you want to track things like Pain tokens or Saga progress, our app has that.
  • Other game modes. Do you want to play asymmetric missions? track custom games? Do you need a different disposition than the one you're allowed to select? The Tabletop Battles interface is much more flexible, and allows for custom score input.
  • Direct feedback. We read every bug report and work to implement changes, and we respond to inquiries on Discord. The Tabletop Battles app is very much what you help make it.

Also it's free - we don't get any money from you using our app. If anything, it costs us money. We're just in it to make the best possible app for tracking games of tabletop wargames.

So if you haven't already, give the app a download and check out our newest features. Everything shown today is available in our apps, right now.

Have any questions or feedback? Drop us a note in the comments below or email us at contact@tabletopbattles.com. Want articles like this linked in your inbox every Monday morning? Sign up for our newsletter. And don't forget that you can support us on Patreon for backer rewards like early video content, Administratum access, an ad-free experience on our website, and subscriber-only content covering competitive Warhammer 40K!
