September 4th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Archetype

Infantry Templars

Thoughts

Don't let the single Redeemer fool you, this is an infantry horde Templars list in the truest fashion. In an out and out melee scrap this thing will do horrific damage, and the Redeemer can bully the middle early game before it's time to commit the Crusader bricks. A really cool list from a lesser seen chapter, great work.

The Best of the Rest

The rest of the top eight was as follows:

  • 5th - Will Paul - Orks (Freebooter Krew, More Dakka) - Take and Hold: Lootas, Boyz, Trukks, Squigz - all the good stuff.
  • 6th - Atal Walia - Blood Angels (Wrath of the Doomed, Headhunter Task Force) - Purge the Foe: Full vehicle spam in Blood Angels?! Mental!
  • 7th - Tim Deetlefs - Adepta Sororitas (Chorus of Condemnation, Bringers of Flame) - Priority Assets: Car Park Bringers of Flame with loads of Immolators and Castigators.
  • 8th - Ridvan Martinez - Drukhari (Exhibition of Slaughter, Kabalite Cartel) - Reconnaissance: Cartel combined with Exhibition Wyches for brutal punch at range and in melee.

Archetype

Space Wolf Purge Bricks

Thoughts

Space Wolves appear to have something of a build emerging around big slabs of Wold Guard Terminators and accompanying lackeys. This list also packs a few Venerable Dreadnoughts in. Game plan wise this looks like an encroach then full send sort of deal, hoping to absorb the clap back and then roll to victory. It won't be easy to out kill it in several turns of a game which suits the Purge scoring nicely. Great work on the event win!

The Best of the Rest

There were 4 more players on 5-1 records. They were:

  • 2nd - Peter Yap - Space Marines (Astartes) (Forgefather's Seekers/Librarius Conclave) - Reconnaissance: Double Redeemer Salamanders.
  • 3rd - Gareth Hunt - Chaos Daemons (Cavalcade of Chaos/Blood Legion) - Purge the Foe: A Crusher spam list that also shoves several Great Unclean Ones in - deliciously weird.
  • 4th - Dustin Zonnenberg - Aeldari (Seer Council/Warhost/Armoured Warhost) - Priority Assets: A very go-wide Seer list with tonnes of units and the full three Warlock Conclaves, glorious.
  • 5th - Andrew Edwards - Orks (Equatorial Hordes/Da Big Hunt) - Purge the Foe: Loads of scouting Squigs and lads, ready to brutalise.

Thoughts

The voters have once again picked an absolute shellacking as their showdown choice! This is a match up I know intimately. Unfortunately Daemons is utterly cursed for my beloved Drukhari - let me tell you why...

Drukhari have excellent high AP weapons for bullying traditional tanks and elite infantry. They greatly dislike massed invulnerable saves, especially on big monsters. Units like Scourge really dislike Lone Op auras (like the one Belakor is handing out), and their fragile, one and done melee struggles a lot with tricks and traps like surge moves and charge mortals - both of which the Khorne Daemons have in abundance here.

To put the nail in the coffin, the charging Crusher bricks can kill multiple Drukhari units a turn for free and the massed elf unit count makes Purge scoring very straightforward. Couple that with the fact that the Drukhari are going to struggle to get a meaningful stash of pain tokens going, and you have a recipe for a blowout defeat. And that, reader, is how that went.

Thoughts

T'au have quietened down somewhat since their start of edition dominance, helped along by fairly substantial nerfs. Ret Cadre remains a very potent option though - ranged, snide lists can play the Purge game in a slower and more subtle fashion, using their relocation and fire and fade to force the opponent into a bad situation where they can no longer screen out the real damage. Darkstrider sneaks in as excellent home screening tech, freeing the rest to bomb forwards when the time is right. Great work on the event win!

  • 3rd - Zach Parker - Necrons (Skyshroud Spearhead/Cursed Legion) - Purge the Foe: Wraith brick and Lokhust brick in Cursed.
  • 4th - Rodrigo Chaves - Space Marines (Astartes) (Librarius Conclave/1st Company Task Force) - Purge the Foe: An absolute grab bag of marine units anchored by a big Terminator brick. Cool to see 1st Company being used!
  • 5th - Justin Weinapple - Necrons (Skyshroud Spearhead/Cursed Legion) - Purge the Foe: Massed Lokhust and a couple of C'tan.

Wrap Up

There you have it folks! It's pretty green out there right now, but we have a reasonably wide range of other armies having a go beneath the Orkish plateau. A new codex beckons for them, and hopefully further balance changes for the rest too before long! Let's see what fresh horrors await us eh?

Lowest of Men, out.
