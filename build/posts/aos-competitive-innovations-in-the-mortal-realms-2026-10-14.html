[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-10-14

October 14th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Now that LVO has come and gone we move into the cooler fall and winter seasons. Since a Battlescroll dropped right before LVO, we're starting to see some major shifts in how the game's meta works. So let's dive in and see the fruits of those changes.

This Week's Events

  • OMG Open 3 - AoS Championship - United States
  • Bendigo's Bush Bash Bonanza '26 - Australia
  • Trash Panda GT II - United States

Thoughts

This ones a real shocker. Ossiarchs were at the bottom of the charts right before the Battlescroll drop, and with some radical changes to how the army functioned it looked like we'd see the accepted meta change in some big ways but this goes beyond expectations.

This is a variant on some of the top Soulblight lists, with Prince Vhordrai trying to buff Askurgan Trueblades by increasing their stats with stackings buffs, snowballing as they only get stronger. Likely the clutch for the OBR here was very strong shooting, which can reduce Wards and healing and cripple the army early. If they took a shot at Vhodrai early and were lucky enough to kill him, the army would collapse fairly quickly.

Archetype

Ossiarch Midrange

Thoughts

After the change to Relentless Discipline so it no longer scaled to larger units, it seemed that reinforced hammers were on the table in a way they weren't before. The Immortis Guard, the Necropolis Stalkers and even the Mortis Reapers help as front line support, with the two wizards able to spread out Empower Nadirite Weapons to multiple units. Supported by the Mortek Guard, who are basically a free All Out Defence each round, these units can be a lot tankier than you'd expect.

Hanging in the back is the Triaxes, which can be pretty swingy damage wise with their 4+ to wound, but their -1 to Wards (and throw in the Bone Tithe Shrieker if you need to deal with a 5+ or better Ward) and ability to shut off healing can weaken the enemy before they close the gap, where you respond by sending in the Liege-Mortek to get another round of shooting in.

Thoughts

This is a really interesting matchup since current Kharadrons can be quite aggressive, using the Frigate to get the Strongest melee units in the army into melee and wiping them out. However, Ironjawz are pretty tanky, especially when properly supported, so charging them head on is asking for trouble.

The Kharadron players need to play tactically, using their ranged fire to weaken the enemy or target their buffing heroes first before going for the hit, all while avoiding the big WAAAGH! charge of the Ironjawz. It creates for a dynamic experience where the first to pull the trigger risks winning it all or losing big.

Archetype

Ironjawz Infantry

Thoughts

Since Scourge of Aqshy brutes dropped, they've been one of the best boons to the army. A really solid anvil who can punch back with +1 to hit and wound, they are made even stronger with Zoggrok handing out Crit Mortals and the 3 Priests boosting damage and healing lost models makes them even harder to deal with.

While the Brutez and Ardboyz hang back on objectives, the Ragerz can run ahead and do some serious damage to take out crucial units or pin them in place.

Thoughts

The hardest part of dealing with Cities of Sigmar is the Cavaliers, you can try and charge them but they will make it very difficult to hurt them, but charging to another target and forcing attacks onto nearby Steelhelms.

So what do Helsmiths do? Don't engage, shoot them. While they can charge you instead, that's less ideal as it forces them to stretch their forces out. By bombing them from afar it presses the issue and makes them leave their comfort zone, and with the new updated Castigation Battery, those ranged attacks are even better.

Archetype

Helsmiths Mid-Range

Thoughts

Helsmiths were one of the bigger beneficiaries of the last battlescroll, getting their genuinely awful battle formations rewritten. It was unclear if Taar's Grand Forgehost would be replaced or not, but people seem to be feeling it out.

With the Castigation Battery, the Rocket Batteries benefit big time by getting Crit 2 Hits with 2 Demonic Power, which helms make up for their dicey 4+ to hit. Once the dust settles from ranged fire, the Bane Mace Dominator engines can close the gap, alongside the Bull Centaurs. The Engines incredibly tough with 2+ saves and a ward, enemies without proper tools to deal with them can find themselves stuck in combat with them and nowhere to run while the artillery keeps hitting them and the Centaurs charge..
