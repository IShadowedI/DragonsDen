[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-09-30

September 30th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

It's the weekend before LVO and so we got a few mid-sized events to show off before the one of the biggest show of the year!

This Week's Events

  • London Grand Tournament 2026 - England
  • Hammerfest GT 2026 - United States
  • Garden City Slaughter 2: Slaughter of Burlington - Canada
  • Welsh Open 2026 - Wales

This is interesting for the decision to drop Kairos Fateweaver and not run 20 Tzaangors blocks. Thoughts

Pre-September Battlescroll, this is basically the optimum cities list. Cavaliers are there to charge into threats (and if they're charged first, charge into them anyway) while Steelhelms suck hits away from them to keep them protected. Fusiliers offer covering fire to those who are stuck in combat, and the War Hydras create a very annoying to kill combat beat stick.

This is liable to change with the points hikes, and effective neutering of the War Hydra, but hard to say just yet how that will go down.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Fyreslayers infantry

Thoughts

Fyreslayers are, right before they got nerfed, top of the pack and this list takes advantage of exactly why. The Scourge of Ghyran prayer lore granted the prayer Blazing Impetus which (until the recent nerf not in this event) allowed a unit to make a free move even if it had deep struck that turn. And this list has a lot of ways to deep strike between the runesmiters and the KO RoR, and you turn a 9" charge into a 5" charge.

Add in the Fury of Vulcatrix for +1 to hit and +1 attacks, along with the Berzerkers already very respectable attack profile and 5+ Ward when near Heroes, you got a unit that kicks ass and takes the knockback too.

The List

Archetype

Prince Vhordrai and Vampires

Thoughts

This list is a bit of a throwback to the last seasons meta, with Prince Vhordrai leading alot of Askurgran Trueblades. He hands out permanent buffs to them whenever they kill stuff, leading to a snowball effect as they just keep getting stronger. The Vengorian Lord is a natural inclusion in just about any soulblight list, especially (say it with me now) before the nerf.

Kritza is a cute addition, letting units retreat without taking damage so they don't have to waste time on trash mobs, and the Fell Bats make ideal cheap screens.

Archetype

Daughters good stuff

Thoughts

Nothing to say that hasn't been said before. Despite her constantly rising price, Morathi remains one of the scariest giant beat sticks in the game right now and Blood Sisters pose a significant threat as well, giving Strike Last to high wound units and hitting with some really strong Crit Mortal attacks.

This list uses a lot more Khinerai than is normal, they're not great fighters but excellent tech pieces with movement abilities to steal objectives and fulfil battle tactics. Very solid good stuff list.
