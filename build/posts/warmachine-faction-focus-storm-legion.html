Warmachine Faction Focus: Storm Legion

July 30th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to our Warmachine Faction Focus series here on Tabletop Battles! In these articles, we will be taking a deep dive into each of the Prime factions available in Warmachine, writing about their rules, their casters and units, how they play, and give some examples of competitive lists. Whether you’re new to the game and looking for advice, or a Warmachine veteran looking to get more insight into the various armies of the Iron Kingdoms, we’ve got something for you here.

The mighty kingdom of Cygnar suffered much through the Infernals conflict but rebuilt with the help of many survivors and remnants of other factions, especially the Convergence of Cyriss, whose less fanatical followers helped perfect the galvanic technology that was formerly Cygnar’s experimental edge. Newly outfitted and eager to prove their grit, they stand tall against the Orgoth threat in the name of king and country.

Why Should I Play Storm Legion?

Credit: Steamforged Games

Do you want to play the “good” guys? Do you believe in the power of ingenuity and cooperation to develop new elite technologies to fight against your enemies? Do you want to shoot lightning at people and be as fast as it too!? Welcome to Cygnar’s premier standing army. You’re about to be equipped with a better arsenal than everyone else; it’s time to act like it.

Strengths and Weaknesses

Strengths

  • Plentiful tools to deal with waves of enemy infantry.
  • High DEF across both Units and Cohorts.
  • Amazing access to mobility tools and threat extension.
  • Solid suite of Heavy Cohort options.

Weaknesses

  • Issues with ways to consistently deal with high ARM targets.
  • High cost for elite infantry and utility tools.
  • Lack of caster spell support such as Spell Slave or Arcane Assist causes more strain on your Focus stacks.

Army Abilities and Playstyle

Fast as lightning and just as dangerous, there is no mechanic exclusive to Storm Legion, but there is a heavy prevalence of Damage Type: Electricity and spells that increase mobility. Running on newly-upgraded galvanic technology means shooting lighting, going fast, and being hard to hit due to lightning fast reflexes.

Casters

Captain Athena di Barro

Credit: Steamforged Games

Immediately bucking the trend I opened with, Athena differs from her fellow officers in relying less on speeding up her own forces and more on slowing down her enemies. Her feat is one of the most powerful control feats in the game, granting her own forces Reposition 3” and causing every enemy currently in her CTRL Area to suffer -2 DEF and SPD (so she can use it then reposition at the end of her activation; nifty). Di Barro has changed quite a lot over the course of MK4; she is now in her religious revival phase, showing off her faith in Morrow through several Menoth-style control spells in Blinding Light to hit fix for her forces turn over turn and Avenging Force to counter punch an enemy advance. Her three upkeep spells are backed up by Resourceful, letting her keep them up for free on her battle group, marking her uniquely as one of the least focus starved casters in Storm Legion despite her moderate ARC of 6. Building for Athena means playing more cautiously, and with Defense Formation you can keep your key pieces protected and ready to bring the holy smackdown on your enemies.

Captain Madison Calder

Credit: Steamforged Games

Oh Captain my Captain; Calder rides into battle as the pinnacle of what it means to be an elite Cygnaran military fighter. She does it all: Speed with Crusader’s Call, Damage for your units with Fury’s Strength, mobility with Rhythm of War, Anti Shooting with Counter Measure, and unparalleled optionality for her jacks with Bird’s Eye. She's here to position your forces for victory and launch them at your enemies with the speed of lightning and twice the destruction. Then she either enables that approach or defends from retribution with her feat Ride the Storm, granting her forces +2 DEF and her battle group Riposte to get that extra damage in. A premier caster for the faction, her only weakness is she can't do it all at the same time. The damage buff she grants is for non-cohorts so her jacks, while versatile and mobile, are only as powerful as they are printed, making heavily armored targets priority for elimination. Like her, you must play tactically and tactfully allocating her focus precisely for maximum impact. A consistent staple of top tournament performers, she's excellent for all skill levels.

Captain Raef Huxley

Painted by: Count David Pomeranz the beautiful, tamer of shrimp and protector of the realm

Why even develop galvanic technology if you aren't going to use it to make your warcaster armor a Mech Suit? Calculating and disciplined, Huxley brings his brand of Cygnaran excellence to every encounter favoring a large battlegroup. Battle Configuration lets him swap between Sky Stormer, granting his Cohorts SPD and Reposition 3”, and Ground Assault, granting his cohorts Steady and Sturdy, gaining Resourceful to conserve his focus, and making his already scary Heavy Mag-Bolter even more lethal with better stats and Arcane Precision to see through clouds. This already-hefty suite of abilities is further bolstered by incredible spells, with a single model super buff in Storm Rager and a feat-level super spell in Full Throttle that allows his Warjacks to run or power attack for free AND gain Boosted Melee Attack Rolls for all Melee Attacks. So what’s the catch? Well, Huxley boasts some of the most middling caster stats around with ARC 6 and CTRL 12, which strangles how much he can do in a turn and where he can move to do it. This is somewhat mitigated by his Feat Initiate Full Auxiliary Power which fully refills his focus pool, letting you go all out or bunker down with plenty of focus to keep from being assassinated. Another Tournament mainstay, the risk of being focus strapped is more than made up for by the might of his ferocious warjacks.

Major Allister Caine

Credit: Steamforged Games

Caine is certainly Major. In rank, in the lore, and as a pain in the butts of many players. One of the most venerated characters currently still active in the setting, there's a lot of history to Caine as a true gunslinger warmage who is singularly terrifying. Unlike most gun mages who have the Reload (1) ability to make use of spare focus for extra damage, this aetherpunk Cowboy has Reload (Infinity) on both his Spellstorm Pistols! A variety of powerful attack types that vary between extra damage, spell accuracy, and enemy movement that can switch modes between each shot give the Major some major versatility. Hard to hit with a glass jaw; Caine embodies the fantasy of an old west style death machine. With his feat increasing his damage output with every gunshot, he's even an assassination machine that can one round most enemy casters or get sneaky kills with his Magic Bullet spell. All that said, while he's a superhuman workhorse, he does little to assist his army. Blur helps get one or two key pieces up board against quality shooting, but often he will be hogging the spell for himself, leaving Calamity as his major other support tool which is hit or miss depending on the target’s spell defense. Caine’s armies want to be self-sufficient, since he doesn't do much to bolster them; he prefers taking the best high quality tools to give your opponent a staggering number of threats to contend with, until a gap opens up to let your pistol pro blow a hole through their carefully laid plans.

Major Anson Wolfe

Credit: Steamforged Games/Privateer PRess

Armed to the teeth with one of the best spell lists in the game, Wolfe is the almost direct opposite of Caine. Built to take a hit and deal much of it back, with tools like Side Step and Beat Back he can carve a bloody path through enemy lines and to otherwise well protected enemy casters. His own personal output is secondary, though, to his frankly absurd list of support spells. He can increase gun range with Snipe, boost ARM with Arcane Shield, crank up damage with Positive Charge, and increase threat ranges with Energizer. Every one of these is a caster-defining spell on their own and for him it's just another in a long line of powerful tools. Unfortunately, the lack of spell support in Storm Legion strikes again, as Wolfe cannot project his own threat very well, having to constantly pay for these powerful upkeeps on top of trying to find places to cast a potentially back breaking Positive Charge. Even Snipe isn't as absurd as it sounds since he has no ranged weapons himself, so it relies on good friendly pieces to use. Then there is the feat Give ‘Em Hell which seems to punish your enemies for crashing into your lines by enabling his cohorts to move 3” and make an attack for each friendly model destroyed. Sounds insane with a good balanced mix of infantry and cohorts? Like one big punishing bear trap? Well unfortunately for Wolfe, "destroyed" isn't the catch all it used to be. Any ability that Removes From Play gets around the feat entirely, and some armies have it stapled to everything, leaving wide swaths of matchups where there might as well be no feat at all. When it works it works though, and Wolfe continues to be one of the most versatile casters in the roster with a unique kind of personal output that can be mastered with enough hard won battles.

Mechanik Adept Sparkhammer

Credit: Steamforged Games

Last but only because he was charging up his galvanic hammer, we have the only modern Rhul caster in Mk4. Assistant to the "late" father of galvanic technology, Nemo, Sparkhammer continues his legacy of shocking research that he brings to every battlefield. Arcane Machinery helps adjust to the battlefield on the fly allowing you to increase his paltry 12” CTRL range, become the widest area of Ionization in the game by being his CTRL range, or making every one of his spells cost one less during his activation. Sometimes it can be hard to pick just one and that's where his feat comes in: Cloudburst let's you use all 3 Arcane Machinery effects for the turn as well as handing out free rerolls to every Electrical attack in range (storm legion has a few good ones). Couple that with a damage boost in Electrify, an incredible threat extender in Mobility, a jack efficiency enabler in Jackhammer, and a ranged slam spell in Force Hammer and you have a well balanced package ready for most foes. It's not all rainbows after the rain however, it's often found that too much of Sparkhammer’s kit fights with itself for attention leaving you having to make tough choices turn over turn between efficiency and tech. Overall an incredible starting caster for most up and coming Storm Legion recruits.

Warjacks

Non-Character Cohorts

Stryker

Credit: B0bTh3BuiIder

A marvel of modern engineering, this chassis is just as heroic and reliable as its namesake (a famed caster from previous editions). Currently one of the best positioned Heavies in the game at the time of writing due to a buff to its DEF stat bringing it up to a very relevant break point of 13, and its powerful use of Accumulator: [Storm Legion Warrior] to almost always start with a second free focus to reduce the need for support units to let it do work into the enemy. For its cost and defensive positioning, a Halberd-wielding Stryker is a mainstay in practically every single Storm Legion list, often in multiples. Unfortunately there's little variation from there; the Halberd is at a good POW break point of 18 and is the easy access to RNG 2”, and with Set Defense to raise its DEF to 15 against charge attacks you're very rarely exploring many more options for that arm. On top of that, the rest of the chassis prioritizes the cheapest loadouts to squeeze every ounce of list building but there is at least some variety, especially when looking at its very useful True Sight head to charge through enemy clouds. The backbone of the Storm Legion Front line; you can't go wrong with a few Strykers.

Courser

Painted by: Count David Pomeranz the beautiful, tamer of shrimp and protector of the realm

If DEF 13 is defining for the Stryker, the DEF 14 of the Courser from the same update is functionally insane. Raw defensive stats alone aren't enough of a reason to be used in battle, however, which is where the head options come in. As the only native option for Shield Guard and Arc Node in the faction, you will find one or two Coursers creeping into your builds to leverage these opposing but often critical tools at a premium, very unlike the cost effectiveness of the Stryker. While not currently in the best of spots to affect the battlefield with damage due to awkward costs and stats as well as missing out on Accumulator, they nevertheless are critical and thankfully resilient tools to practically any army list.

Borealis

Credit: Mike from Collateral Damage

Left out of the Defense update that blessed the Stryker and Courser, the Borealis is unfortunately in a bit of a rough spot. Simply put, there is only one rule of note on the card that is wholly unique to it in faction, and that's Repulser Field. When hit by a melee attack, the field immediately pushes the attacker back 1” after damage, which can deny certain cohorts additional attacks after a few hits or deny scoring on points if one model from a unit is out of range. This potentially powerful tool is unfortunately hindered by the rest of the comparison to the Stryker, who for multiple points cheaper is much harder to hit, has the same melee threat, and can have more versatile rules. Storm Breaker as a weapon does have some potentially backbreaking implications, with d3 additional POW 14s into an enemy that can get to POW 16s with ionization. The resulting package is relegated to very specific builds that want to capitalize on that gun and the field as control/zoning tools; it's not recommended for lists that don't have a specific carved out plan in mind for it, especially since its Accumulator:[Storm Forge Warrior] is far less ubiquitous than the Stryker counterpart.

Character Cohorts

Deuce

Spiritual successor to Caine's previous partner Warjack: Ace, if you want a toy that's the selling point for playing a Cygnar faction then look no further. With the premium DEF of 13 and the highest RAT of any Cohort in Mk4, Deuce has stats to move. Native Reposition 3” and True Sight bolstering his RNG 12 Spellstorm Cannon help to get him into range of his targets and then get out of danger allowing for the application of damage turn over turn. The combination of Reload:[1] and Powerful Attack means you get 2 fully boosted shots with 3 focus and the shot types all can hammer your opponent further between Mobility with Beat Back, Extra damage into opposing Warjacks with Decrepitation, and Blast where even the blast damage is boosted! At 17pts not every list will play him but every list that plays him will show you the true might of Cygnar.

The General

Where Deuce has premier RAT, The General has premier MAT at 7, but unfortunately, like the Borealis, The General suffers from the buffs to Strykers. While still sporting the premier DEF 13, RNG 2 POW 18 melee, and same Accumulator: [Storm Legion Warrior] as its generic counterpart, the remaining benefits are often seen as not as worth it for the drastically increased cost. Built-in Righteous Vengeance to punish your opponent’s aggression with threat extension is undeniably powerful but Overtake is fairly limited in power without a way to get free attacks to fuel it. Having a Buckler for +1 ARM is solid while still having a decent Gun on the sword, but that is minimal in terms of increased survivability for the same 17pts as Deuce. Clever players will find limited uses for this Stryker upgrade, but this is one character waiting for a January makeover.

Super Heavies

Thunderhead, Heart of the Storm

Credit: Mike from Collateral Damage

A triumphant return for an old Cygnarian favorite, Thunderhead sports some of the most ludicrous mobility and threat packages the game has ever seen on a cohort. While starting at an aggressively average SPD stat of 5, the servitors it summons unlock the real tech. Summoning one a turn, Thunderhead II can: Out of activation move towards it, target it with a spray to get perfect angles on enemies, slam it into opposing models, and use it as a bomb by dealing damage to everything around it. In addition, the summon provides an aura of +2 to hit for all your Electricity damage attacks in your army. Flying High helps to keep from being sniped on the approach but baseline this is a very killable heavy Warjack with a bigger base. That said, when you position right, you find those angles, and you use those other buffs from your Casters, Thunderhead is a one-model natural disaster for your opponent.

Colossal

Maelstrom

Credit: Steamforged Games

There are a lot of good Stats going for the largest of the Storm Legion super weapons. Its main melee options are at a massive 3” reach, all of its guns are powerful with good ranges and can get to silly numbers with Ionization, and it has one of the highest base DEF stats of any colossal at 11 (trust me for something the size of a building that's agile), but overall it is in a weird spot in the faction. It is what it says on the tin, a colossal with the power and number of attacks to back it up, but with how wide scenarios are in the 2026 tournament packet this massive mix of magnetic metal misses the mark by being not quite strong or defensive enough on its own to make up for its 30pt base line cost. Power is context dependent, though, so as the context changes in the game, be prepared for this potentially deadly oncoming storm.

Units

Stormblade Legionnaires

Credit: Steamforged Games

This is your bread and butter front line. With tools like Shield Wall to ward off all comers and Polarity Field to blunt charges by constructs, these knightly conscripts are emblematic of the advancements in galvanic technology. Sporting two attacks between sword and shield as well as Electro Leap from the sword make a charging unit of Stormblades truly terrifying for many similar infantry units due to just raw number of attack and damage rolls. Solid all around many infantry builds bring multiple units.

Stormguard Legionnaires

If the Stormblades are your defensive front line then Stormguard are your offensive spear. Hard to charge due to Set Defense these elite spearmen are often your second line behind your Stormblades and Warjacks ready to charge. Natural predators of cohorts of all kinds, Big Game Hunter gives them a particularly brutal 4 damage dice on the charge but at 7pts they need to be a deliberate part of your offensive plan.

Stormthrower Legionnaires

The ranged option of the galvanic knights, they got hit hard with the lethality nerfs from the 2026 update. Middling RAT and short ranged sprays, while Stormthrowers have an equal charge range to Stormguard and assault makes enemy infantry nervous, the 8pt price tag is rarely worth the price compared to other unit options.

Legionnaire Standard Bearer

Credit: Steamforged Games

For one point you get a Stormthrower without a gun that has a 5” aura of Ionization and has Take Up to stick around. Attaches to any of the on-foot legionnaire units but will see the least play with Stormguard. Definitely has its uses to keep your Electricity attacks juiced but is a very deliberate addition to your army. Played by preference.

Arcane Mechanics

Credit: Steamforge Games

A storm warrior unit that quite invaluably provides Repair and Guidance while having decent armor and 5 Boxes of health each. Their damage is nothing to write home about but at least one unit is essential to any 100pt list.

Storm Callers

Credit: Steamforge Games

Very high risk for pretty decent reward, these shock jockeys are so close to being good. The powerful Force Barrier ability built in along with Reposition 3” makes killing them with shooting difficult, but unfortunately not impossible. While Stormcall makes their targeting easy, an 11/6 starting profile, even empowered with Power Multiplier to 14, would take Ionization to get to relevant numbers into heavier targets, and at that point it's a lot of investment for a 5pt unit.

Storm Vanes

Painted by: Count David Pomeranz the beautiful, tamer of shrimp and protector of the realm

Talk about a wacky unit. Another cheap unit with health boxes, this is your anti-shooting tech. This unit has 3 abilities and each model can only pick to use one per turn and there's no doubling up. 99.9% of the time the one you're prioritizing is Wind Weaver for Warping Winds to reduce enemy shooting range in a 3” bubble. Stacks with Flying High on Thunderhead but not Huxley’s Windstorm. After that you hand out electro leaps with Lightning Wreath and lastly you use Polarity Field to protect from construct charges. If you need the tech you bring them, if you don't they stay on the shelf it's as simple as that.

Storm Lance Legionnaires

Credit: Mike from Collateral Damage

I promise this is that last “Storm” unit but it's a doozy. At a Staggering 10pts the Storm Lance Legionnaires are some of the best Calvary in the game. They have 3 Attacks each thanks to assault on the charge with the Calvary ability for boosted to hit on the charge attacks with Brutal Charge to get them to POW 16 then Unyielding and Reposition 3” to keep from getting dealt with easily. They take buffs well and are the bane of many players thanks to their native 13” charge threat. At least one unit will show up in your pairing and some years Storm Legion army lists started with two and built from there.

Tempest Thunderers

Credit: Steamforge Games

Going into the heavy infantry now, we start with the big guns. Decent RAT and good range with an absolutely solid gun profile of 14/8 AOE 2 at a respectable range whose damage is further bolstered by Ionization means that they're…only okay; 9pts is a lot to spend on these guys and even though they're a pretty respectable melee backup plan with Brutal Charge, you will find it harder to fit these guys in compared to other options.

Tempest Assailers

Credit: Mike from Collateral Damage

Slams in Warmachine are very good. They can affect multiple models, move them out of contesting position, and grant the invaluable boon of knocking down targets to trivialize hitting them with other models. These absolute paragons of Cygnaran excellence can do it on command with their Smite star action bolstered by their impressive native MAT 7. Backed by 8 health boxes each, Shield Wall to keep them armored up and the Borealis’s covered Repulsor Field ability means this unit is one you will happily spend 9pts on across many lists and casters.

The Black 13th

Credit: Steamforged Games

Warmachine’s premier gun toting character unit, whose only competition is Khador’s Hounds, these sharpshooting heroes of the battlefield return from editions past to carve out a new future for Cygnar. While not quite the menaces they were in the previous year, they're reliable and hard to pin down thanks to Reposition 3” and have ubiquitous access to an extra die on POW 12 damage rolls that can always be delivered with their True Sight. Momentum on a RAT 7 body is worth the 9pt price in some armies as is let alone the 2 shots just Gover has out of the trio. Watts brings Blast for AoE and Black Penny to contribute to melee scuffles penalty free and Ryan adds Thunderbolt to each of her guns to keep going with the enemy shoving. Excellent but frail scenario bullies, the Black 13th are like Deuce in that at 9pts they aren't in absolutely every list anymore, but any list they do join makes them a constant concern for the opponent.

Solos

Captain Bastian Falk

Captain Bastian Falk, from Warmachine. Credit: Rich Nutter

A returning Gun Mage from the past to teach a new generation, this is Storm Legion’s only Ambush option. At 4pts for one model, it's a steep ask for the sake of messing with enemy flanks, but he absolutely can with his two shot RAT 7 spraying shotgun that on kill lets him run up to contest objectives. Not most players' first choice for the role, but it's the only one the faction has so if you need the niche filled you add him begrudgingly.

Legionnaire Officer

Credit: Steamforged Games

This is your easy access to the best time-saving ability in Warmachine: Precision Strike letting your models ignore friendly models for movement and Line of Sight. For 3 points this is an auto include for most lists, but also granting Grievous Wounds and Pathfinder for units means there's a little more utility to be had and every Storm Legion player is glad they are there to bring it.

Lieutenant Sara Brisbane

One of the very rare Jr. Warcasters in MK3, this up and coming battle field commander is a neat utility piece to many Storm Legion armies. Granting an extra shot for certain cohorts with Open Fire while granting armor with Fail-Safe you can create an interesting flank piece to help cover where your Warcaster cannot.

Sharpshooter

It is bad. I hate to say that because a Reposition 3” solo with an Armor Piercing gun at RAT 7 should be a staple addition to any army, but that is all it is. If they are able to stand still and hunker down they sometimes get a second shot with Dual shot at a surprising distance thanks to Ballistic Correction but without True Sight or another way to ignore shooting defenses the puny POW 6 on the Mag-Bolter often can't find the right targets, and you're better off with a different use of 3pts.

Specialist Tyson Vas

An absolute mad bomber of a solo, Tyson is the most accurate way to reduce enemy DEF thanks to RAT 7, Strafing Run giving his shots an extra dice to hit but dropping the lowest and Arching Fire on all 3 shots to really make sure you're on target. While not a great range at 8”, his speed 7 helps get him where he needs to in order to make sure your army can scalpel out problem opposing pieces.

Weather Station

Credit: B0bTh3BuiIder

Did you want a less reliable but much much safer-to-position Tyson Vas? Well here's the solution. Storm Call ignores almost every form of common shooting protection and RNG 13 helps really keep this solo contributing but not disrupting your more melee oriented plans. Often more popular of the two for that safety, ability to shove enemies with Thunderbolt, ability to ground pesky opposing Flying enemies with Sky Rumbler, and reliability of targeting it is a gamblers choice with d3 shots. This is also an excellent support piece that sometimes makes its way multiple times into aggressive infantry lists.

Zephyr

Credit: B0bTh3BuiIder

One of the best 80mm Solos in the game due to SPD 8, Carapace to make it a bullet sponge with +4 ARM against guns, and a fantastic 10” aura of Ionization. It even punches well with a bunch of attacks and the Calvary ability to deliver Crit Knockdown on the charge with its Trampling Hooves or high damage with Brutal Charge on the Voltaic Javelin. A must-buy for any Storm Legion collection and an absolute staple for any and every competitive pairing.

Mercenaries

Alexia, Queen of the Damned

Credit: Steamforged Games

The fourth incarnation of this queen of the undead is Storm Legion's easy access to model recursion. Best used alongside the variety of models with boxes in Storm Legion, especially the Tempest units and Storm Vanes. Sporting the defensive profile of a warcaster and Sucker! to pass off damage to the same models she can bring back, Alexia is a great supporting backbone to any contested quadrant of the board.

Eilish Garrity, the Dark Traitor

One of the more notorious mages in the Iron Kingdoms, this venerated obsessive is Storm Legion’s access to the always helpful Puppet Master ability. Very few other models grant this powerful boon and can often be pivotal in landing your high quality attacks into the right targets.

Eiryss, Shadow of Retribution

Credit: Steamforged Games

Alexia has been in the setting for a long time. Eiryss has been around for a LONG time. On her fifth iteration, this elven assassin sports the absolutely ludicrous RAT 9 for her shooting alongside her already beefy MAT 7 for her two weapon master melee weapons one of which with Freeze to automatically make whoever she hits Stationary. Her big selling point is the game defining Disruptor Bolt option on her crossbow that immediately strips any model she hits of all Focus or Fury, even Warcasters/Warlocks. For 5pts and without many ways to increase her threat in faction, you'll find this highly deadly tool to be in your army by preference but she'll always top of mind for your opponent when placed onto the battlefield.

Kruger, Wrath of the Blighterghast

Credit: Steamforge Games

One of the best flanking solos in the game, this manifestation of fiery draconic fury has been nerfed several times and is still army defining. Being able to teleport 8” and hit all models within 2” automatically with a POW 13 fire attack is only the start of his onslaught as Gunfighter and Eyeless sight help his two shots of Dragon’s Fury (usually two sprays) always aim at what he wants to maximize output. Able to take damage to boost hits and damage, even at 6pts this is one of the most terrifying damage dealers in any Storm Legion arsenal.

Gorman Di Wolfe

Often only included next to models like Krugar, this alchemical madman is easy access to Grievous Wounds and Corrosion to deal with hard to pin targets, but usually wants to make best use of Spontaneous Combustion next to something like Krugar’s ability to light things on fire to deal enormous damage out of nowhere. Not essential but situationally powerful, this model is taken at preference.

Carver Ultimus/War Boar MMD47

Bringing home the bacon per usual, Carver and his trusty partner are excellent back line gun support but expensive to sit on 50mm objectives. This duo often finds their way into lists for Caine to pile on the shooting that ignores clouds thanks to War Boar’s Eyeless Sight.

Constance Blaize, Radiance of Morrow/Gallent

Credit: Steam Forge Games

The latest of the Super Jr. duos, this all-melee package aims to anchor a section of the board with gallant and threaten with Constance. Able to take on high ARM targets and waves of infantry alike, this duo wants to support an Infantry heavy force to pile on Requiem tokens for free buffs and spells, as well as Caine to give the gunslinger some melee output next to his Calamity spell to take on beefier targets.

Exulon Nostilla/The Aberration

Credit: ProfessorGrimm1 on Twitch (Grim1 on Discord)

If you prefer Brain over Brawn this combo brings both along with the most utility of the three Super Jrs. Between a Grievous Wound gun, multiple Shadow Bind sprays, and an arc-able befuddle for threat extension, Nostilla and her pet can be seen with Athena in more controlling lists making best use of enemy DEF reduction.

Magnus the Unstoppable/Invictus

The 2026 combo of the year. Invictus is universally strong with the best offensive output and the best threat range. Magnus is no slouch damage wise himself making the pair the highest impact option to include in your force and is the primary choice in Storm Legion to hold a flank without much support, freeing up your caster to focus on the rest of the battle.

Skybomber/Sky Raider

Similar enough to be included together, battle engines can score 50mm objectives but are odd to maneuver. The Storm Legion versions sport some hefty Electricity Damage weapons to synergize where needed but you will rarely find either of these bodacious blimps in a list pairing without very specific reasons.

Listbuilding with Storm Legion

Painted by: Count David Pomeranz the beautiful, tamer of shrimp and protector of the realm

When building for storm legion you have to keep note of the core fundamentals of Warmachine more than many newer armies. Your casters that care about Cohorts like Huxley, Sparkhammer, and Wolfe really need you to lean into a load bearing and often expensive battlegroup where as your more infantry focused casters like Calder and Athena need to treat their cohort options more as role fillers than the backbone of your force. Foundational to Storm Legion is a mixed arms force and leaning too far into guns or melee will often leave you lacking as the stats for your options are solid all around but rarely overwhelmingly so for your opponents. You want to make sure every gun and every blade is at the ready when you need it. Combos like the Storm Rager spell from Huxley onto the Zephyr and abusing the Bird’s Eye ability on Calder to allow for your melee jacks to aggressively position behind forests are the types of edges you look for to cover the gaps your caster’s primary focus leaves open.

What to Watch Out For

Storm Legion can handle most enemies but heavily find themselves disadvantaged into high singular armor values and wide melee onslaughts with shooting protection. Due to being a well oiled machine that wants to use all aspects of your army to take out the right parts of your opponents army, enemies that can gum up the works and be hard to shoot or melee away can cause issues as the ability for your army to function at its peak relies on a combination of attacks and attack types so make sure you use your repositions and charges to the fullest to dull or muddle enemy assaults.

These lists came from a 4-0 IGQ finish by B0bTh3BuiIder on Discord.

Calder saw play into the trickier Grymkin match-up, where the spread of infantry under Calder’s feat allowed him to gum up his opponent's tech options that are typically designed to pick off specific high value targets. Being a Caster who focuses on defense and units, you can often blunt an assault on feat turn then maximize the use of Fury's strength to dig your way out again. Magnus and Invictus help to take some of the CTRL range and focus cost pressure off of Calder to focus on delivering her best spells efficiently and safely.

Leaning heavily on the raw power and utility of Huxley for his generalist list, this style of build cares a lot about maximizing the most efficient and valuable tools in Storm Legion and ensuring their high quality of attacks connect with the help of the double Weather Station. Lord Carver and Warboar help to back up the deadly melee threat Huxley's cohorts bring to bare and can act as a kind of second wave to bulldoze and bully the remaining enemies.

Final Thoughts

Immoren has always been a crucible of conflicting ideas and morals fighting it out for regional dominance. The sacrifices of the Cygnaran people left the country unbowed by the Infernal threat and instead they forged a new future of speed, might, and honor. To play Storm Legion is to ride lighting into the eye of a hurricane and harness its power to hit foes fast and at all angles. Tactical and economical, if you want to win with this front line of galvanic might you must show an appreciation for all turbulent aspects of war.
