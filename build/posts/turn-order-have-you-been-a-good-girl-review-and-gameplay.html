Turn Order: Have You Been a Good Girl Review and Gameplay

August 4th, 2026 via Goonhammer
It's 2006. I'm leaning close to the monitor of the family PC, eyes locked upon an ongoing conversation in MSN Messenger. I don't write as myself, I'm someone else. A professor at a wizarding school (whose walls would later turn out to be lousy with black mold). She's tall, well-spoken, and unabashedly inspired by whatever anime was my latest obsession, though I am a gangly 17 year old teen who won't interrogate my feelings on gender for another 10 years at least. The other person in the chat isn't writing as themselves either; instead portraying the character opposite my own. They flirt, they fight, and in this messenger chat, they live a life nothing like my own.

It's 2026. I'm leaning close to the monitor of my personal PC, eyes locked upon an ongoing email thread. I don't write as myself. I'm someone else. A GIRL, in all caps. Each letter stands for another word generated by the toss of four six-sided dice. She's a sharp and unkind thing, and I write her like an Eversor assassin writ more butch than I feel on even my best days. I pick my words carefully as the attacker, as my counterpart does the very same as defender. My GIRL charges ever forward with each sentence into her waiting jaws, in the hopes that she might claim my prize before they snap shut around her.

As will undoubtedly come as no surprise to any regular reader of the TTRPG ESSENTIALS or the ongoing ADVANCED series, I'm a huge fan of games that skew towards the weird. As much as I can, and do, enjoy TTRPGs in the mainstream such as those previously recommended within this column, I love a game that's willing to pull me out of my comfort zone, and teach me a whole new tongue to grapple with. Have You Been a Good Girl (HYBAGG going forward), is a perfect example of this.

Commander/Mother/Creator. Your reason for being, and the sole target of your many foes.

Overview

HYBAGG is a collaborative storytelling game for two players who engage in play by messaging one another in Discord, email, etc. The individual to initiate play takes on the Role of the attacker, a GIRL attempting to access/circumvent/damage the other player's THRONE. The other player therefore takes on the role of defender.

The world of HYBAGG is whatever you make of it through gameplay, but there are still some constants. THRONE will always be the creator/mother/commander of your GIRL; who she will always wield as a vicious instrument against other THRONEs and their GIRLs. Where, how, and when these sorties occur is left to attacker and defender to define in their replies.

Mechanics

The rules for HYBAGG live within each message sent between players. There's a minimum word count, a maximum time limit between messages, advice on safety tools, demands on how messages end, and most importantly: Definitions of words and their interpretations.

See, words are how you interact with your opponent.

What is a GIRL? A curated d66 word table, of course.

The Attacker rolls six times on their GIRL's d66 table to determine their KEYS. Each message they send must include one of these KEYS, and once they have successfully utilized five of their six, the game is over in the Attacker's favor.

The Defender rolls seven times on their GIRL's d66 table, and the first six represent their LOCKS. If the Attacker uses any of these words in a message, the Defender informs them. If three locks are tripped, the game is over. The seventh word/term is the Defender's FATALITY, something so lethal, that if utilized by the attacker at any point, ends the game in the Defender's favor.

The gameplay loop created and demanded by this system encourages not merely evocative writing; to set and describe the scene at hand and the action(s) involved, but also manipulative writing. If you as Defender have chain(s) as your FATALITY, how is it that you ensure the attacker falls for it? You might invoke metal bonds as a threat for the attacker to contend with, or perhaps include them as a banal constant of the world surrounding your GIRLs, or invoke poetic or metaphorical imagery to make such a mundane word seem safe by comparison.

When a game ends; typically accompanied by florid description provided by the victor or their prey, I personally found that the momentum achieved by the gameplay experience propelled me forward into another game. Each past experience informing the next as I learned and established a shared language with my counterpart.

The Book

The rulebook itself is beautiful, with its visual style and layout invoking the language and world of Bungie's recent MARATHON. Learning how to play is as simple as reading 14 pages, each laid out in such a manner as to be evocative without sacrificing readability.

Described by its game designer December as a coming-out game, HYBAGG is unapologetically queer, sapphic, and violent, and if you enjoy writing with those themes at all, I encourage you to check it out in either the free Basic-B or paid premium form HERE.

Additionally, HYBAGG has recently finished crowdfunding for additional content to be added to the book like additional GIRLs designed around specific themes or content, Multiplayer rules for 3 or more players, campaign play, and more.

Have you?

Intersections

HYBAGG, like much queer art, is the sort that refuses to sit within the confines of one singular thing. It asks anyone that interacts with it to fall into the slipstream between emotions and impulses that are otherwise left outside of the bigger, more corporate TTRPGs. I hesitate to even directly connect it to parlor games, as the gameplay experience feels so much more intimate. This game, for me, feels closer to those intimate, messy, exploratory sessions of roleplaying I have enjoyed in the past through play by post roleplaying.

A big reason for this is the unabashed sexuality inherent to HYBAGG, where even the name itself invokes kink and queerness, and descriptions of gameplay within the book suggest at the possibility of eroticism between the two players involved. For all the miasma about D&D being about “flirting with your friends”, that game (mechanically or otherwise) doesn’t care about that. Any identity or sexuality you bring to D&D is, in fact, in spite of the framework it provides.

HYBAGG is a game that for me, lives counter to this. It is an exploration of self, kink, conflict, and collaborative worldbuilding. All of which lives and breathes within the actual gameplay and framework of the system. HYBAGG is asking you to try something new with someone you trust, and see what grows in the spaces between your writing.

Personally, this is the sort of game that I could see myself playing forever. I encourage you to try it, to see if it speaks to you similarly.

Final Thoughts

Thank you very much for reading this article! If you would like to read more TTRPG content on TTB, you need only look to the Turn Order column for a wide breadth of reviews, overviews, and other writing covering topics both introductory and advanced.

Additionally, if you're new to the hobby and this game has piqued your interest, I would direct your attention to our TTRPG Essentials coverage. These article are written with accessibility in mind, with no expectation of the reader to have any pre-established base of knowledge pertaining to the hobby, with the intent of welcoming you into it with ease and care.

Example of Play

My first gameplay experiences with HYBAGG were actually with December, where through two games I got the chance to experience the roles of both Defender and Attacker.

Typically, and despite enjoying written roleplay, I make a rule of not sharing this writing with anyone other than my writing partners. In the interest of offering you; the reader, an additional pitch for a game I really love and want to see more people try, I'm going to break this rule. Included below, and with the expressed permission of December, I've chosen to include written transcripts of our games.

Content Warnings: Death, Dismemberment. Recommended Listening: Nine Inch Noize by Nine Inch Nails

Game 1: Defender

December:

The ship is burning.

I taste soot and ash in my mouth as smoke escapes my chrome lips. My body, lithe and glistening in the metal formed of your THRONE, reflects the light of the fire a thousand times. I smile as internal diagnostics reveal a flamethrower built into my chest and larynx. I guess I was made partially from a forge? A lab? R&D? Something explodes. I turn to look across the hall as burning weapons lockers detonate from internal caches of bullets.

“Mmm. An armory,” I say to myself, grounding me in a new body, blinking away my last [dream] with new eyes. The sharp sounds of my metal heels precede me as I slowly make my way down your corridors.

What signs do I pick up about your GIRL?

Keys - 1/5
Petra:

My THRONE has been aware of you for some time. Yet, such is their nature to greet such impudence with patience, so that it might be better understood.

I lack her grace. I am a crude, inevitable thing that envelopes all that I pass with the chill kiss of void. Bulkheads I have passed are frosted over, translucent materials made opaque, and metal surfaces rendered so cold as to sting to the touch. Such are the signs of my going, as I beg and coax you inwards, to see if you might survive what I leave behind as gifts. Door, bulkhead, and hallway; each connected, but all left open for you.

Tell me, GIRL, how do you survive my wake?

December:

I spot you down a long hallway moments before I think you see me. I press my back to the wall. I see my artificial breath in the air, feel the heat in my chest soften as the cold reaches out.

“Damn she make you out of a fridge or something? Just my luck.”

A pistol pops out of a recess in my thigh. I take it in my hands and pull back the receiver to check if it is loaded, a nervous tick from when I had muscles and skin and–

“Time to come in from the cold, girl…”

It’s a tick, but it helps. Helps remind me I am armed. Helps remind me I am a thing that has a weight and history because when my waveform mind leaves my GIRL behind, hacking and ricocheting around in your ship’s routers and networks to collide with your quantum brain I can get kind of lost. My GIRL sleeve freezes up, inert, empty. How the hell else was I gonna get close to you.

Set the scene. What is {dreamt} when our minds collide?

Keys 2/5
Petra:
[Locks 1/3 Hot/Heat]

I am distant, darling.

You know that though, for how you seek me, amongst the invisible skein tangled across the dull mundanity of physical space. Now here, as our hidden truths meet, you see me. You see slender limbs tied in wire and links of cold metal. You see a chestcase more glass than ice, holding within it a single ember refusing to give out despite the overwhelming and omnipresent void that lives within the gaps of our shared reality. I stare at you with crystalline eyes choked with shadow so dense that it seems to swirl against itself like churning oil.

The frosted links of metal lash out at you more than I, perhaps seeking out your inherent warmth. They seek to bind, to connect, to ensure that there is no recall from this place, not without transformation.

What will you become to escape?

December:

I am a wolf.

I run bounding through the drifts of accumulated bits and bytes, permutating into non-essential data clouds of white noise that fill the dark as my subroutines sniff you out.

RUNNING… PLEASE WAIT

RUNNING… PLEASE WAIT

RUNNING… PLEASE WAIT

My teeth are ICE breakers, nipping at the boundaries of you, wanting to take hold of these metal links and rip and tear and shake and–

TARGET ACQUIRED

There it is. I bank sideways hard, throwing my vulpine form against your encasement, code and exploits trying to reach you as chains wrap around us both, the constriction bringing me closer to your core, like an old play about [gods], Fenrir to your Skadi, bound forever until–

“Fuck!” I shout, back in my GIRL, slumped against a bulkhead. I blink a few times even though I don’t need to.

What secret did I see that I very, very much want to steal?

Keys: 3/5
Petra:

You saw me, dearest. How do you die?

[Lock 2/3: God] [Fatality: Chain]

December:

I can’t move. My GIRL is frozen to the bulkhead, joints cut through with ice crystals. My UI screams in red neon before all power is rerouted to keeping my waveform conscious alive.

Ah hell.

She would have given me so much for you. Maybe it’s your hand on my cheek when I die. A caress so cold it feels like living. I die inert, dark and cold, like the universe will. I always was keen to get things done ahead of time.

[bravo]

Game 2: Attacker

Petra:

One upon a time, a hand reached for a heart unexposed but not invulnerable. The heart was something worth having, and so attached to that hand was a GIRL. Piercing your THRONE like a [metal] harpoon through fusiform blubber, her body a sharp and unkind thing stuffed with all the rapacious hunger that would be required of her incipient hunt.

I am that GIRL.

Tell me dear heart, does your THRONE bleed?

December:

WARNING. WAVEFORM PRESENCE DETECTED. WARNING. FIRE IN LEVEL 25 SECTION C. WARNING. DECOMPRESSION IN LEVELS 23. 24. 25. 26. 27. WARNING. AMBIENT TEMPERATURE ABERATION. WARNING. HUMIDITY AT 72 PERCENT. WARNING. CRYO SYSTEMS CRITICAL. WARNING. NAVIGATION OFFLINE. WARNING. WAVEFORM PRESENCE DETECTED.

It's been bleeding. Oil, grease, once potable water, some sort of grey sludge of decomposing bodies and biological material. This THRONE is sick, flooded, broken, neglected. No wonder you got in, it's a kilometre long corpse. A tired, synthetic voice announces a variety of impending disasters over various loudspeakers, echoing through dim and humid halls and cathedrals of broken fuselage.

Paint me a picture of your journey, please?

Petra:

I thought myself a predator, but the sight of this place makes me feel more like carrion. I find myself stalking through chambers upon limbs thickly corded by dark metal and pale flesh, where my strength works against me with each powerful step. Bulkheads screech, floor grating squeals, and I find myself tearing up more and more of this environment simply by existing within it.

For the first time, I feel uncomfortable in this form. My limbs too heavy, my weaponry too expansive, my GIRL too lethal.

"Fuck ." I find myself cursing, not simply because I have yet to find or even see you, but because the longer you remain elusive, the longer I must remain aware of my comparatively crude design.

How far from me do you remain?

[2/5]
December:
WARNING [TRAP 1/3  - FUCK]

You fall into the hangar bay.

Several vessels are all arrayed in a messy pile, shunted to one side of the metal cathedral by a worrying gravitational event centuries past. Landers, cargo ships, tools for the long abandoned colony. Among them is one that does not belong. Bulbous, sinewy, covered in chiton, a worm atop a haystack bleeding black ichor. Something lost that was found. The last strata of whatever happened here.

I’m not far now. The thing that killed me is waiting. Hunting. Moving like a readied bowstring, all potential until it isn’t. Do you go inside the ship?

Petra:

The impact is a familiar thing, and grants me the glorious return of my closest companion. Pain can drive me forward; it can make sense of a frame that previous felt ill-fit, and grant me the unrivaled bliss of purpose. For the first time, I find myself thankful for the state of your THRONE.

Pushing forward, I greet the [wet] traveler before me as though its alien nature was instead anticipated bounty, striding towards it with a smile on my lips and death in my eyes. Instead of turning to seek any designed point of ingress, I do what comes natural and begin to tear at its bulk with the blades I call hands. I pierce it with both, pressed together close enough to simulate prayer and then I wrench them apart to reveal the prize within. Ave, that you might hold what I seek.

Do you fear my conviction?

[3/5 Getting close now....]
December:

A messy pile of corpses is revealed to you inside the alien ship. GIRLs, a hundred of them at a guess. It made a fun game of it, for a while. Our THRONE hurled everything she had until we were all spent. Tried to make the air poison, tried to crush itself and all its insides riding too close to gravity wells. Nothing could kill this fucking thing. So she limped for long trajectories, hoping one her sisters could do the job, or at least rid us of this meddlesome thing, a lone missionary from who knows where tending a dead abbey of her own creation.

I look at you from where my cortical stack is caught in the thing’s teeth, one synthetic eye dangling from jaws that leak gossamer fluid. I fear your conviction, for I fear you may overstep like we did, or you will be too successful and doom me with the light drinking alien. But I am excited for one last show.

When it finally leaps at you, how do you fight it?

[Oh, I’m not worried…]
Petra:

This is not my target. This is no worthy foe. Yet, I see that you still exist within it, which grants me hope that my purpose might not yet be reduced to nothing. You are unembodied, but such a state can be temporary for GIRLs such as us.

As it hurtles towards me like a missile made of pitch, or perhaps a shadow of the harpoon I had become to meet the flesh of your THRONE, I remain still. I count and analyze its qualities in the scant seconds before impact:

  • [Pitch-black sinew and outer carapace // Acceptable Dermal replacement, if untorn.
  • Rigid spinal cord and tail // Design implies viable cortical splice
  • Claws/Teeth/Tail // Deadly weapons for a worthy foe to wield.]

My assessment is nearly complete by the time it hits me. By the time its claws meet my dense flesh, the data is clear. This will be an acceptable frame for you, my only worthy foe.

To accomplish this, I set to tearing out its current inhabitant.

May I grant you this gift?

[4/5 (A list of 3 or more things)]
December:

Goodness it’s messy.

I watch awestruck as much as I can: the body pinned to the side of the alien craft, your massive bulk struggling to reach a space in its spine, the viscera, and then the stillness. I wait, I’d hold my breath if I could.

Then the new body hits me like a freight train. New sensations, new spectrums of sight, sound, touch, god what is that taste. There is so much here, so much more than the broken memories of the woman I was and the bot I used to be and oh… oh my goodness babe, you really shouldn’t have. She’s beautiful.

You allow me one languid stretch to get my new nervous system online, and then you’re on me with startling precision. No fair, I only just got her and we’re plummeting out of the ship into the tangle of starship wrecks below, tail and talons and chrome flashing in the dying light of the hangar. I scream in frustration, in anger, in pure glorious apotheosis.

How do we try to kill each other?

[Okay… bit worried now]
Petra:

I'm kind, in the sort of way that only hands-on patience can be. The sort that allows you to feel your new body, to exult in every twist and curve and inch of lethal perfection. The kind where you can see your new expression, and the wounds you grant me (both shallow and deep) in the steel reflections across the spaceship hull.

It's a [zero] sum game, but I greet your throat with my hands at the same time I feel your tail go for my gut. [5/5  Darling!] Have any questions or feedback?
