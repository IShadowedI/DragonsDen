[40k] Competitive Innovations in 10th: Final Frontlines pt.1

June 17th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

It's last orders for 10th Edition, and indeed I suspect most of my audience is too busy building army lists with the shiny new 11th Edition points that arrived this morning to be that interested in goings on from the past. I also strongly suspect, on past precedent, that at least one event this coming weekend will adopt an instant update to 11th Edition, so we might be updating the column title as soon as next week. We shall find out.

If you haven't already checked out our coverage of the 11th Edition points, you can check it out below:

  • Overview
  • Space Marines
  • Imperium Factions
  • Chaos Factions
  • Xenos Factions

Check that out when you get a chance, and read on for some of your last sightings of the majestic lists of 10th Edition in their natural habitats.

This article gives me a chance to talk about how the updated points impact some existing lists, so thanks to Games Workshop for providing us with a review copy of the Munitorum Field Manual.

Archetype

Monster Mash

Thoughts

In second place we have monster mash Daemonic Incursion, another list that's going to need a re-think as we move into 11th. Daemons get hit both on points and by the Disruption Disposition being mandatory for Incursion, meaning that going this hard on big Monsters just isn't going to work any more. A second unit of Plaguebearers leaves Chrisptopher a bit more prepared for the change than some, but I still expect to see Daemons looking very different in the next few months, probably centred around Plague Legion and Shadow Legion. This build has, however, been an absolutely iconic part of 10th Edition, so congratulations to Christopher for giving it a final, undefeated runaroud.

Archetype

Board Control Invasion Fleet

Thoughts

Greg is keeping up the innovations right to the buzzer here with a very new spin on Invasion Fleet that focuses heavily on board control. Lots of Genestealers let you get in the opponent's face fast, lots of Hormagaunts let you push a huge wall of bodies and OC wherever needed on board, and loads of Raveners provide all the repositioning you could want to consolidate your position. The latter also provide something that can rip up a big target in a pinch, obviously important in the current metagame. Plan and units-wise, this list is closer to something 11th-ready compared to the others, since while Invasion Fleet also has a mandatory Disposition, it's Take and Hold which is pretty clearly already the plan here. However, as a list of all threes it does get bitten a bit by points steppers, so some refactoring still required. Well done to Greg for unleashing a new threat just when we all thought we were safe.

Archetype

Defiler-Killing Aeldari

Thoughts

Rounding out our top four, and the first list that goes down in price for the new edition, we have an Aeldari build utterly dedicated to being able to murder consecutive large targets. Your opponent can only have three Defilers, so consecutive turns of sending forward either the Dragons or the Spears with Battle Focus for no Overwatch gives you enough assets to blow them apart, and you also have Asurmen, Lhykhis and Jain Zar's units to fight for the board while you do that. This is a super cool plan, and Scott was able to carve through crabs in rounds five and six before finally falling to Mani. As for moving into 11th, this is another one where the Disposition bites a bit, as Disruption wants you to go wider rather than tall, but there's a bit of wiggle room that could be used here, and maybe with triple Defiler going up 250pts+ there's less pressure to have the second Dragon unit! Well done Scott.

Thoughts

AdMech are looking reasonably hot going into 11th, and one look at this build should tell you why (and that's before getting into what a steal Thulia now is at 180pts). Congratulations Justin.

Thoughts

Another 10th Edition classic to close us out, unleashing the powerful mix of shooting and mobility that Raven Guard enable. There is one mixup from the classic plan, which is taking Infiltrate on the second VanVet unit, giving the list an option to make early big plays if it finds it facing something that can outshoot it. 11th transition-wise, the points here are effectively untouched, but both the Detachment Rule and Coronal Susurrant take a hit, which might reduce the power. Don't count them out though - Shrike's full-unit Lone Operative is going to be ultra valuable, and bolter Inceptor alpha strikes get considerably nastier with the improvement to AP-1. Congratulations Jimmy for letting the Raven Guard fly one more time for 10th.

The Best of the Rest

There were 3 more players on 4-1 records. They were:

  • 2nd - Aaron Moat - Emperor's Children (Coterie of the Conceited): Triple Prince Coterie with a Defiler.
  • 3rd - Ethan Hauff - Adeptus Custodes (Talons of the Emperor): Land Raider/Custodian spam.
  • 4th - Adam Downs - Chaos Space Marines (Huron’s Marauders): Melee Marauders with a big Terminator brick.
