11th Edition Detachment Focus: Murdertalon Raiders

October 15th, 2026 via Goonhammer
In this series of articles we take a deep dive into a specific Detachment for a faction, covering the faction’s rules and upgrades and talking about how to build around that faction for competitive play. In this article, we’re covering the Murdertalon Raiders Detachment for Chaos Space Marines.

Introduced in the deluge of 1 DP Detachments at the outset of 11th edition, the Murdertalon Raiders Detachment acts as a 1 DP boost for your Jump Pack units, aka Raptors and Warp Talons. Initially released with the Purge the Foe Disposition, the Murdertalon Detachment has since been adjusted to be Recon, in order to prevent it from being a cheap way to get access to Purge.

Disposition: Reconnaissance

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

Unique: NIGHTMARE

This Detachment focuses squarely on buffing your flying infantry units, with rules very similar to the bonuses you get from Nightmare Hunt, only for just these specific units. For that reason perhaps, this is one of the few Detachments with a relevant unique tag: The NIGHTMARE tag for this Detachment prevents it from being used alongside Nightmare Hunt - but notably not Dread Talons, and we'll talk about the potential there in the "Playing this Detachment" section. Otherwise the bonuses here are fine but ultimately tied to units you're just not really using - CSM jump infantry are nothing to write home about; Warp Talons are good when they're costed efficiently and right now they're not. Likewise Raptors are more durable and stronger than before but still too expensive for a unit that lacks re-rolls in melee built into its profile.

Detachment Rule: Prey on the Weak

There are two parts to this:

  • Friendly INFANTRY FLY units’ attacks targeting a unit that’s either battle-shocked or below half-strength can re-roll hit rolls of 1.
  • When enemy units target a friendly INFANTRY FLY unit, if the attacking unit is battle-shocked or below half-strength, they get -1 to their hit rolls.

That -1 to hit rolls debuff is pretty good in 11th edition, since it combines with cover to put units on -2, which can be devastating when you’re relying on a smaller number of high-damage attacks. That said, battle-shock is and always has been an unreliable mechanic and it’ll be scant consolation if you can’t manage to finish off a unit and it lingers at the below half strength mark. This is just OK but don’t be shocked if you forget you have it. The big challenge is that CSM lack good ways to force additional tests on enemy units outside of Raptors getting into melee, and you really don't want to throw your units into melee with a unit they're not likely to kill.

A lot of the value in this Detachment comes from the Enhancements and the Stratagems, which do have some very good bonuses for your Warp Talons and Raptors to play with. That said, those are just not great units to begin with, and so building around them feels pretty bad. When 11th edition first released, this Detachment looked fairly attractive as an add-on to Veterans of the Long War; that was because at the time taking a unit of 10 Warp Talons was very common in CSM lists as a unit you could Ingress onto the table, then use to charge and kill an enemy unit before leaving at the end of your opponent's turn. However Warp Talons have since then gone up in price and become less useful offensively (staying S5 as marines have gone up to T5), and with the new update, the Cabal of Chaos Detachment has become much, much better. The net result is that most CSM players tended to focus on 3 DP Detachments or other options, and Warp Talons do just fine with the buffs you get from Pactbound Zealots, Creations of Bile, or Veterans of the Long War.

Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Enhancements

There are two options here, and they're both limited to Chaos Lords with Jump Packs.

  • Shadowcowl Talisman (20 points) - CHAOS LORD WITH JUMP PACK only. The unit has a 5+ Inv save. This is a neat upgrade for Raptors, who otherwise lack any kind of invulnerable save. That said, there’s a lot of AP-2 out there and not so much AP-3 so you may find the situations where you actually get to use this fairly rare. That said, it’s good enough when it does matter to be worth taking. If you're taking it, you want it with a unit of ten Raptors. Which you don't really want to take, and so you don't really want ot take this upgrade, tragically.
  • Pact of Cursed Pinions (20 points) - CHAOS LORD WITH JUMP PACK only. The model has the DAEMON keyword and gets +1 A on its melee attacks. This is fine. Chaos Lords can use the extra attack on their power fists. This model also gains the ability to join Warp Talons, which is listed in the MFM. That’s important, because it’s a great add-on to a unit of Warp Talons and without it, this enhancement is just worthless. 20 points is a lot for this, but getting the extra melee buff helps.

Stratagems

The Stratagems here are solid. They’re all priced at 1 CP, meaning you won’t have many issues around CP generation and you'll get real value out of bringing a Chaos Lord or two with you.

  • Plunging Talons (1 CP). Used in the Fight phase on an INFANTRY FLY unit that made a charge move this turn. They gain [LANCE] on their melee attacks. This is more or less the best thing about this Detachment. You’ve got a unit of 10 Warp Talons and now you can give them lance, letting them wound on a 2+ or 4+ against key targets and re-rolling all their wound rolls.
  • Raking Pass (1 CP). Used in your Movement phase, when a friendly INFANTRY FLY unit makes a Fall Back move. That unit can fall back and charge. This is also great - useful ability, great to have, re-triggers Plunging Talons. What’s not to love? In those rare times you get stuck in combat with a unit of Warp Talons, you can use this to fall back and find a newer, better target to assail.
  • Warp-Twisted Terrors (1 CP). Used in your Movement phase on a unit of Warp Talons finishing a move. Pick an enemy unit (excluding MONSTER/VEHICLE) within 9” of your unit. That unit has to take a battle-shock roll with -1 to that roll.  Out-of-phase testing is solid, but still unreliable. Being able to do this in the Movement phase is good however, and you can stack this with the -1 for a unit being within range of a unit of Warp Talons to put real pressure on enemy units.
Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Playing This Detachment

This is a tough Detachment to love. As a 1 DP add-on, it's competing with Cabal of Chaos, and now that the updates have hit and Terminators look way better, there just isn't much reason to take this one. That's also partly because the buff you actually get here on your Warp Talons and Raptors isn't very good, and if you're taking Warp Talons and playing them correctly, you're already spending 1 CP per round on them for Rapid Ingress, so even though the Plunging Talons Stratagem is good, it's not something you can spend on every single round.

So what are your options here? Well, if you're playing this it's for one of two reasons: Either you're trying to get more out of your Warp Talons as an add-on unit and don't want to run Cabal of Chaos, or it's because you're a Night Lords sicko trying to make them work as a full army.

Let's start with the first of those two scenarios. If you're running this to improve your Warp Talons, then chances are good you're running this Detachment alongside Veterans of the Long War because if you were taking Warpstrike Champions you'd be taking Cabal of Chaos instead for the Terminator buffs. Here you're going to be looking at a unit of Warp Talons led by a Chaos Lord with Pact of Cursed Pinions so you have the ability to drop the cost of your Rapid Ingress Stratagem with the Warp Talons, freeing them up to use Plunging Talons on a regular basis to kill harder targets. The math improves substantially when you add [LANCE] to the equation, and with full re-rolls to hit at 4+ it'll often make more sense to throw out [SUSTAINED HITS 1] instead of [LETHAL HITS] against those harder targets (4+ is the break-even point for lethal/sustained but sustained has higher potential upside; at 5+, stick to Lethals).

On the other hand if you're running Night Lords, well, you can't pair this with Nightmare Hunt so your option is basically Dread Talons. Dread Talons isn't a great Detachment, but it has some interesting things in it. Combining these two basically gives you a slight better(?) version of Nightmare Hunt, where you are forcing tests at units below Starting Strength, and at -1 to tests within 12". If you want to go as hard as possible, the play here becomes using Raptors, Dread Talons, and Noise Marines to stack -1 modifiers to Ld, then using the Warp-Twisted Terrors Stratagem to force tests at a whopping -4 in your Movement phase. These tend to be modifiers to the battle-shock roll, and so let you bypass that 9+ cap on Leadership modifiers - you're modifying the roll of the test, not the enemy Ld characteristic. Putting space marines on a 10+ is pretty great... but they're just going to un-battleshock on their turn. Go hard on Raptors - you want a unit of ten with a lord sporting Warp-Fuelled Thrusters and a second unit of ten sporting the Shadowcowl Talisman - then fill out the rest of your army with good units that get almost nothing out of being in an army with these Detachments.

Strengths

  • Warp Talons. You get some awesome buffs to your warp talons here - being able to attach Chaos Lords and have [LANCE] are both great benefits.

Weaknesses

  • Narrow benefits. This really only does anything for your Warp Talons, with a few benefits for Raptors. Those aren't units to build an entire army around, and Raptors have been living right on the edge of playability for a while now.

Final Thoughts

If you're taking this Detachment, the best place is with Veterans of the Long War, where you're running a generically strong Detachment with good rules and making one of your key pieces - Warp Talons - even better. It's a fun little Detachment, but losing Purge the Foe really hurts and it just can't combine with any of the other Detachments where you'd really want to take Warp Talons or Raptors, making it a tough sell.
