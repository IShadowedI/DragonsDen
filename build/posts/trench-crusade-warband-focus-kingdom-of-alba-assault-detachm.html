Trench Crusade Warband Focus: Kingdom of Alba Assault Detachment

July 24th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome, one and all, to another Warband Focus here at Tabletop Battles. Today we embrace the bitter cold of Alba, home to the ferocious Highland warriors. Their hatred of the Heretic forces knows no bounds, and their desire for revenge against them is all that is needed to fuel their pilgrimage to the front lines. 

The Lore

The men and women of the Scottish Highlands are fearsome beyond comparison. Their hatred for the Heretic forces has no equal, desiring little more than delivering God’s judgment, be it through Axe or Claymore. They are constantly needing to repel heretic invasions, due to the fleet of England caring only for its own territory, and they need to continually be on the lookout for valuable loot and treasure to send back to the homeland. Without it, the Kingdom’s impoverishment may worsen and spell its permanent downfall.

Credit: Raf Cordero

The Variant

Before we get into things, let’s quickly break down the Variant’s rules: 
  • Bagpipes: One Musical Instrument can be upgraded so it also grants Negate Fear within 8”. A nice upgrade for an item you are always going to take. 
  • Brave: +1 Dice for Moral checks. Not bad for those instances that end up being a bloodbath and you need all the help you can get to win.
  • Celtic Machine Armour: Models that have Machine armour charge with a D6” and not the usual D3”. Nice for your Lieutenant especially, as they’ll want to get into the thick of things more so than your Mechanized Heavy Infantry. 
  • Claymore Smiths: Great Swords / Axes are reduced in cost to 7 Ducats. A nice little boon for your units to get cheaper two-handed weapons. 
  • Cold Steel: The first time you buy a particular Melee Weapon, it is half the cost. E.g. A Sword / Axe would cost 2 Ducats instead of 4 the first time you buy it. Again, a great way to keep your early game costs down by diversifying your weapon purchases. 
  • Dum-Dum Ammunition: Dum-Dum Ammunition can be purchased for Alba for 5 Ducats. 
  • Highland Strength: Your Lieutenant and Shock Troopers get the Strong Keyword. Very welcome for these units, who will want to get into the thick of things and make the most of their Melee bonuses. As a bonus, your Lieutenant getting Strong means they can be one of your Satchel Charge carriers. As your MHI lose the +1 Dice to their Ranged Characteristic, you need to find an alternative SC thrower. 
  • Lightly-armoured: Only your Lieutenant and Mechanized Heavy Infantry can have Reinforced or Machine Armour. We’ve seen this with other variants, and like those, this is a fine “downside” to take. Most of your models will be lightly-armoured, if at all. 
  • Melee-focused: Your Mechanised Heavy Infantry models have a +1 Dice to their Melee Characteristic, instead of their Ranged Characteristic. Given the Melee theme of this variant so far, this makes perfect sense. Losing out on the Ranged Buff for your Heavy Shotgun and Satchel Charges will be rough, however. 
  • Rampant Charge: You gain the Ignore Defended Obstacle Keyword. A nice little buff that pairs nicely with Negate Fear. 
  • Strained Supply: Automatic Shotguns, Grenade Launchers, Machine Guns, Sniper Rifles and Submachine Guns are all limited to 1 per warband. 

Taking it all in, what we see is a very melee-focused Variant that relies on cheap two-handed weaponry that wants to get into the enemy lines, ensuring no debuffs can prevent the incoming onslaught. There’s no reduction to the cap for your MHIs, and with the cheaper Melee Weapons and higher charge potential, you may be eyeing more of them than you would see in something like a Prussian or Éire list.

New Antioch Shock Troopers. Credit: SRM

Unique Battlekit

Lochabar Axe - 20 Ducats

The quintessential weapon of the Albannaich, the Lochabar Axe combines the offensive might of a Two-handed Axe with the defensive prowess of a Polearm. Offensively, the Lochabar give you +2 Injury Dice as well as the Critical Keyword. Defensively, you get Block, giving you that sweet -1 Dice to enemy Melee Attacks. Lastly, there are the Cumbersome and Heavy Keywords, meaning that despite your Shock Troopers getting Strong, you’re still using two hands to attack with this. 

Dum-Dum Ammunition - 5 Ducats, Consumable 

A simple Consumable item that grants a single Ranged weapon the Critical Keyword. A nice addition for your Lieutenants and Sniper Priests that are paired with multi-shot weapons, enabling them to proc the Critical bonuses more often.

The Units

Lieutenant

New Antioch Lieutenant. Credit: SRM

Your Lieutenant has just one change, with just the added Strong keyword, but this small change has large consequences on how your Lieutenant is going to operate. As mentioned before, this change means that your Lieutenant is going to be one of your Satchel carriers, meaning less reliance on a Combat Engineer (although you will most likely need one if you want more Satchels). 

More so than many other variants, your Lieutenant is going to be doing a lot of heavy lifting. They need to be fitted out to set up and finish. That means enough weaponry to do both effectively and interchangeably, depending on the situation. This dual role means they will be taking a lot of fire, so be sure to equip them with enough defensive Battlekit. 

Loadout considerations: 

  • Automatic Pistol
  • Great Sword / Axe
  • Celtic Machine Armour
  • Satchel Charge
  • Martyrdom Pills

Mechanized Heavy Infantry 

Credit: Raf Cordero

The powerhouse of the New Antioch force has traditionally been your Heavy Shotgun user, as well as one of your valued Satchel Charge carriers. In an Alba variant, however, their focus is on getting into Melee, given their change to a +1 Dice to their Melee Characteristic. That's not to say shooting, or ranged in general, isn't important, as it is. It's still a New Antioch variant after all, so we have to ensure we maximise efficiency. So with that in mind, we want to make sure we're grabbing weapons with the Assault keyword, so we can butter up our enemies for the inevitable melee kill.

Loadout considerations: 

  • Grenades
  • Lochabar Axe or Great Sword / Axe
  • Celtic Machine Armour

Shock Trooper

Credit: Raf Cordero

The addition of Strong to your Shock Troopers means, like the MHI, you want them shooting and charging as much as you can. However, given the plethora of weapons with the Heavy keyword in the New Antioch Armoury, it's worth considering something like a Heavy Flamethrower as a ranged threat piece. On something like an MHI in a vanilla NA list, it was wasted, but here the lack of Ranged Characteristic bonus on the Shock Trooper means nothing, and you can punish enemies that are close together.

Loadout considerations: 

  • Grenades
  • Great Sword / Axe
  • Trench Club
  • Heavy Flamethrower

The List

Alba All the Way'

Faction: Kingdom of Alba Assault Detachment

Rating: 799 Ducats | 6 Glory

Patron: None

## Elites ##

Highland Lieutenant - 169D | 1G

Battlekit: Automatic Pistol, Greatsword / Axe, Combat Helmet, Dum-Dum Bullets, Martyrdom Pills, Celtic Machine Armour, Satchel Charge

Trench Cleric - 106D

Battlekit: Submachine Gun, Bayonet, Dum-Dum Bullets, Trench Shield

## Troops ##

Highland Mechanized Infantry - 112D

Upgrades & Choices: Celtic Machine Armour

Battlekit: Lochaber Axe, Celtic Machine Armour, Frag Grenades

Highland Mechanized Infantry - 122D

Upgrades & Choices: Celtic Machine Armour

Battlekit: Lochaber Axe, Celtic Machine Armour, Frag Grenades

Highland Shocktrooper - 61D

Battlekit: Greatsword / Axe, Sword / Axe, Frag Grenades

Highland Shocktrooper - 107D

Battlekit: Heavy Flamethrower, Great Hammer/Maul, Trench Club

Yeoman - 52D

Battlekit: Bagpipes, Frag Grenades

Yeoman - 70D

Upgrades & Choices: Infiltrator

Battlekit: Flamethrower

## Mercenaries ##

Communicant Anti-Tank Hunter - 5G

Battlekit: Anti-Materiel Rifle, Trench Club, Trench Club, Combat Helmet

So for this list, I wanted to go a little broader with a one-off list that shows off the general approach that you can achieve with Alba. We're making use of the cheaper Greatswords, but also reduced costs of melee weapons, enabling us to squeeze out some extra Ducats to use on things like Dum-Dum rounds. The Highland Mechanised Infantry and the Lieutenant act as a strong core, able to take quite a beating with the Celtic Machine Armour, whilst the Shocktroopers and Yeoman can hopefully get up the board unimpeded and add some disruption and armour-piercing capabilities.

Final Thoughts

I think Alba is a great consideration for those looking for a particularly Melee-focused New Antioch playstyle. You still have all the considerations of a New Antioch warband, namely working with a setup and finisher Fireteam consideration, but all within a melee framework. It certainly adds a good amount of variety to the standard formula that we know and love from NA. Your MHI especially will appreciate the freedom of not being pigeonholed into carrying a Heavy Shotgun and Satchel Charge only, but being able to take the fight to the enemy in a more direct and "in your face" manner.
