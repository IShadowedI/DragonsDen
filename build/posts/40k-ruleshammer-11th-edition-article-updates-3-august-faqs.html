[40k] Ruleshammer 11th Edition: Article Updates #3 (August FAQs)

August 28th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back to Ruleshammer! In this series we take a detailed look at the rules of key games; Warhammer 40k in particular and, right now, in specific. This week I’m covering more reader questions and looking at what’s changed with the August Update!

Shooting Phase

When is Damage modified to 0?

Q: My unit has access to a rule that changes the Damage characteristic of an incoming attack to 0. When such an attack is allocated to a model in that unit, is the Damage changed before or after save rolls?

A: After.

This FAQ follows a change in the way allocation works in 11th edition compared to previous editions. In 11th, while the order of allocation groups is decided before saves, the “thing” actually allocated to specific models is essentially a save roll, and they are allocated to the group from lowest to highest. This all occurs in Step 4 Inflict Damage 05.04.

The opposing player resolves the following sequence for each save roll, working from lowest result(s) to highest result(s),...

Another element of this scenario worth noting here, though, is that abilities that reduce attacks to 0 damage in 11th got a small buff due to how modifiers work in the new edition.

Note that rules that modify a characteristic in this step to a new value of ‘0’, ‘-’ or ‘*’ cannot be modified by other rules (in such cases, skip steps 2-5).

In 10th edition, whilst modifying damage to 0 did still happen before other modifiers it did not block further modification; weapons such as MELTA 2 previously could improve an attack reduced to 0 damage back up to 2. In 11th edition, that is no longer the case.

Critical Hits on X+ with or within Snap Shooting

The return of snap shooting as a concept in 11th has allowed GW to actually create a slight compromise to what these two FAQs now address.

Q: My attack has a rule that means it results in a critical hit on a hit roll of 4+, and I would otherwise need a 5+ to hit. Does a 4+ hit?

A: Yes, except when snap shooting: when snap shooting, each attack only hits on a roll of 6.

Q: My attack has a rule that means it results in a critical hit on a hit roll of 4+. Does this work when snap shooting?

A: Unless otherwise stated, no: when snap shooting, each attack only hits on a roll of 6.

In the past these abilities have tended to need a successful hit on the die i.e. if a 5+ was needed to hit then you needed a 5+ even with this ability, but that’s largely seemed to be because they specifically did not want these abilities to result in 4+ shooting when using Overwatch. Now that Overwatch has its own unique shooting rule in Snap Shooting, it’s allowed them to use that as an exclusion. So now Critical Hits on 4+ will always be Critical Hits on 4+ except when firing Overwatch.

Visibility Checks When Toeing Into Terrain

This is another one of those edge cases I’ve gotten the most questions about, and for many new players it can be confusing when “True Line of Sight” applies or doesn’t.

Charge Phase

Take to the Skies and Charges

These two FAQs are some of the more frustrating ones, as the timing of Take to The Skies just flatly contradicts the answer we’re given here.

Q: When do the effects of a FLYING unit taking to the skies apply?

A: When that unit is selected to make a normal/advance/fall-back/charge move. Note that for an advance/charge move, this means you must declare that a unit will take to the skies before rolling for that advance/charge move.

and

Q: How do modifiers to the charge roll (or to the maximum distance of the charge move, such as Take to the Skies) affect the selection of charge targets?

A: A charging unit may only select charge targets that are within the maximum distance of their charge roll, including any modifiers. If such a modifier occurs after selecting a charge target and causes that charge target to no longer be within the maximum distance, the charging unit would then select new targets (Target No Longer Eligible Or Viable, 04.03.03). Example: A unit's charge roll is 7", and that unit has a datasheet ability which adds 1 to charge rolls: that charge's maximum distance is 8", so you can select charge targets within 8". Example: A FLYING unit chooses to take to the skies and rolls a charge roll of 9"; that charge's maximum distance is 7" (9" minus the 2" from taking to the skies), so you can select charge targets within 7".

The intended ruling of these FAQs is clear: We have to decide to Take to the Skies BEFORE rolling advance or charge rolls. However, my complaint here is that the trigger in the Take to the Skies rules is making a charge move.

Each time a FLYING unit is selected to make a normal, advance, fall-back or charge move,

And you make Charge Moves after rolling the charge distance; that happens when you declare a charge. I’d really like to see this rule updated in the future to use that moment instead, swap it to “declare a charge” and the rule and FAQ align without issue.

Follow this link for more Ruleshammer on the Charge Phase.

Fight Phase

Fight on Death Models

This FAQ confirms something I’ve been meaning to make a diagram for for a while: Fight On Death models can block movement during the fight phase until they fight and are removed.

Q: Fight On Death (05.04.05) requires models awaiting such an ability to stay on the battlefield until their unit has attacked (or until the end of the phase). How do these models function while this is the case?

A: Models awaiting a Fight On Death ability are still present on the battlefield for all rules purposes. This includes (but is not limited to) the following regarding such models: They can be measured to by friendly/enemy abilities. They can be considered visible to friendly/enemy models where relevant. They can use their datasheet abilities. They can be targeted by stratagems. Enemy units can still be engaged with them (which may restrict the opposing player's consolidation/pile-In moves made as part of an overrun fight).

It’s entirely possible to not even be eligible to make an Overrun Fight in this situation.

Consolidation Move Changes

12.08 - Consolidation Move, After Moving Section, Ongoing Consolidation

Change to: Each model that started this move engaged with an enemy unit must still be engaged with that enemy unit. If one or more enemy units engaged with your unit have not been selected to fight this phase, your opponent must select each of those units, one at a time; when each is selected, it becomes eligible to fight and is selected to fight

This FAQ closes one of the edge cases we had from the Consolidation step. It was entirely possible to engage new units with an ongoing consolidation who hadn’t been eligible to fight earlier in the phase. and were now arguably still unable to fight. as the fight step was over and only Engaging Consolidation had a specific part that enabled newly engaged units to fight. This addition specifically allows those newly engaged units to fight after this consolidation move is resolved, before resolving any others.

Follow this link for more Ruleshammer on the Fight Phase.

Transports

New Disembark Moves

This update adds two new Disembark Moves to the roster, Assault Disembark Moves and Shock Disembark moves! These two moves and their associated FAQ resolve an issue with the current disembark move that I didn’t even notice. If your model has an ability such as the Devilfish’s Rapid Deployment, it didn’t have a valid Disembark move type.

Rapid Deployment: Units can disembark from this TRANSPORT after it has Advanced. Units that do so count as having made a Normal move that phase, and cannot declare a charge in the same turn, but can otherwise act normally in the remainder of the turn.

Or the Land Raiders’ Assault Ramp ability.

Assault Ramp: Each time a unit disembarks from this model after it has made a Normal move, that unit is still eligible to declare a charge this turn.

This also did not previously have a valid disembark move type to choose other than Combat Disembark; Rapid Disembark required the transport to have made a normal or ingress move, while Tactical Disembark required a transport to have remained stationary, meaning these abilities required you to make hazard rolls and the unit became battle-shocked. That's something the new Disembark moves show was clearly not intended.

There is however one small issue with the new moves that I hope is addressed quickly in the app:

The new Shock Disembark’s after moving section doesn’t really seem to make sense; in fact it appears to be the wording added to the Consolidation rules for Ongoing Consolidations (see above). I strongly suspect that this section should actually read:

"AFTER MOVING: Until the end of the turn your unit is not eligible to declare a charge."

As of right now, that section doesn’t make much sense; a unit embarked within a transport can’t start their disembark move engaged with anything, as it’s not on the battlefield. Fortunately the abilities themselves do preclude making a charge, so we can be pretty confident on the RAI here.

Abilities in Transports

Q: Do a unit's abilities continue to function while they are embarked within a TRANSPORT?

A: Yes, within the restrictions specified by the ability itself. Embarked units are Not On The Battlefield (01.02.04), so any ability requiring visibility or measurement to another unit will fail. Example: Azrael is attached to an Intercessor Squad unit and embarked within a TRANSPORT, and that TRANSPORT is making a shooting attack using the Firing Deck ability. While Azrael's Supreme Grand Master ability is still technically active, it will have no effect, as the requirements of the ability are not met (the ability affects 'weapons equipped by models' in Azrael's unit, and for the purposes of Firing Deck, the TRANSPORT itself is equipped with those weapons instead).

This FAQ confirms that weapons modified by an ability of an embarked unit don’t work when the weapon is then equipped by the transport. Why they couldn’t have in some way avoided this strange situation where transports ignore transitive properties and instead get versions of the weapons different to the ones their embarked unit is carrying, I’m not sure, but at least we know the intended ruling for this now.

Other New FAQs and Errata

Here’s a summary of other FAQs and Errata added in the August 2026 Update that are fairly self explanatory.

FAQs

Q: Can I use the Insane Bravery stratagem on a battle-shocked unit?

A: No, as a unit's controlling player cannot target that unit with stratagems (01.07).

Q: If a rule refers to an 'objective marker', does this mean an 'objective'?

A: Yes (excluding rules in the Core Rules).

Q: If both players have units with the Scouts ability, do they alternate resolving scout moves?

A: Yes, players alternate resolving any pre-battle rules units from their army may have, starting with the player who will take the first turn.

Q: When checking line of sight from one model to another, is this drawn between the bases of those models, or between any part of them?

A: Line of sight is drawn from any part of the observing model to any part of the model being observed.

Q: Units have all the keywords of all their models, and attached units have all the keywords of all of their component units. How are destroyed models taken into account for these purposes?

A: These statements are only true so long as the models possessing those keywords remain part of the unit; destroyed models do not contribute their keywords to their prior unit. Example: The Aspiring Sorcerer model in a unit of Rubric Marines is destroyed; as none of the remaining models in the unit possess the PSYKER keyword, that unit is no longer a PSYKER unit.

Errata

01.02.06 A unit must be split into two units, each containing as equal a number of models as possible (when splitting a unit in this way, make a note of which models form each of the two new units). If a rule states a specific starting strength of the split units, but this is not possible based on the number of models in the unit being split (e.g. due to attached Leader/Support models), split the unit as stated above.

24.08 Deadly Demise.

Change to: Each time a model with this ability is destroyed, after the … each unit within 6").

22.03.01 Psychic Abilities with a Psychic Level.

When using psychic abilities with a psychic level, a PSYKER unit cannot use the same psychic ability more than once per phase.
