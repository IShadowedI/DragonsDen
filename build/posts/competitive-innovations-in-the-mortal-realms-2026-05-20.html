Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-05-20

May 20th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

We have a big Competitive Innovations this week with 5 events all over the world, and all have radically different armies in the final championship. Which armies will come out on top? Read on, this week we'll be covering:
  • Wicked Dice Tournament 2026 | AoS | GW WM Qualifier - Germany
  • Realmshift Series 2026 - Chapter 1 - United Kingdom
  • Sigmar Summit @ Warpfire Minis: May 16th-17th - United States
  • Catch this Smoke - Marathon GT - United States
  • Sylvan Skirmish GT 2026 - United States

While it is nice to see KO in the final again in such a major event, the Soulblight list here has incredible advantages. It simply has more hammers to throw at the opponent and chaff to screen out their charging frigates with Skywardens.

The big challenge would be dealing with the guns brought to bear against them, but if Vhordrai or one of his stronger hammers can get into the Ironclad first, they likely can tear it apart rather quickly thanks to the high number of mortal wounds available to the army.

Archetype

Vampires and Deathrattle

Thoughts

This list makes a nice even split down the middle with Vampires and Deathrattle. Askurgan Trueblades and Blood Knights are supported by Vhordrai, who hands out permanent buffs whenever Vampires kill a unit, allowing them to snowball very rapidly into efficient killing machines.

Wights don't quite hit the same ceiling as Vampires, but they start with a better floor thanks to built in crit mortals. They're also much more numerous, letting them better function as chaff and objective holders that still have teeth. The Wight Lord on Skeletal Steed gives a +1 to charge to the Knights, and a nice Strike-after to seal the deal.

Archetype

Rat Cosmompolitans

Thoughts

A bit of every clan here. First we got the Moulder, Rat Ogors supported by the Brood Terror are one of the best hammers in the army, with 6 Rend 1 Damage 2 attacks per model and +1 to hit from the brood terror. The Plague Monks make an effective anvil, receiving tons of debuffs to hit and buffs to damage as needed by the Plague priest.

There's also the screaming bell and Stormfiends as support/tech pieces. The Screaming Bell is a wizard, casting the crucial Bell endless spell for -1 to wound their forces, and the Screaming Bell itself giving -1 to hit them. The Stormfiends are a decent combat piece but their value is more in the grinder fists, which can deep strike in even on the opponent's turn to snatch up objectives or sneaking behind to assassinate heroes.

Thoughts

Due to the high wounds on most of the units in the Slaves to Darkness, this puts them in a very precarious position going up against Blood Sisters, since it's guaranteed they'll be able to hit them with Strike Last. Mercifully this list is only running one unit of them, but it's still a lot to deal with it.

If they can avoid that, they still have to deal with the Blood Sisters pelting them, and the Khinerai able to ping pong around the field, guaranteeing much better board coverage than the Slaves to Darkness can muster with their few, low count units. This is before even getting into Morathi and Shadow Queen, one of the scariest beat sticks in the game right now.

Archetype

Morathi with Melusai and Khinerai

Thoughts

Leading the charge is of course Morathi and the Shadow Queen, who work in tandem as the Strongest Wizard in the army, and the Shadow Queen, the strongest hammer. She works with the Blood Sisters who are an incredibly strong hammer who can stop high wound models by hitting them with Strike last. Blood Stalkers aren't as fearsome as they once were, but are still decently effective shooters who can neuter charges by pulling a die out(and by bringing two MSU, increase the odds of the effect proccing).

The Khinerai round out the list by being able to charge and then immediately leave combat, making them capable of grabbing objectives very away and become very difficult to hunt down.

Thoughts

As far as I can tell this is not an adjusted score or anything, as the rest of the scores don't seem to look that way but I find it highly unlikely the Sons of Behemat player scored nothing.

The usual strategy for the Deathriders list is to use Deathrider Wedge to ping pong off of enemies and grab objectives while dealing some mortal wounds in the process. It's likely against giants they took a more direct approach by using Strike Last and hitting them with lots of Deathriders. While Deathriders do not innately have charge, enough Rend -1 attacks will add up to the giants and their poor saves. Supported by Double Shooting triaxes, picking one up each round is quite feasible.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Deathrider spam

Thoughts

The thrust of this list is wrapped up in the Liege-Kavalos and his Deathriders. Have the Mortek Crawler shoot to inflict strike last, then have the Liege Kavalos charge in with a bunch of Deathriders, each of which use their Deathrider Wedge ability to inflict D3 mortals and leave. If you need more damage for bigger targets, the Liege-Mortek and his Triaxes have you covered, able to shoot once in the shooting phase and then again in the combat phase thanks to the Liege.

The Teratic Cohort and Mortis Assassins exist to grab objectives and screen out the enemy.

Archetype

Ragerz and Wrekkaz

Thoughts

All gas no brakes. Ragerz get run and charge, and strike first if they charge. Wrekkaz aren't quite so flashy, but they hand out a +1 to hit to a nearby unit. Both have a ton of attacks so supporting them is Kragnos to give a 3D6 charge, and the Warboss to drop the almighty WAAAGH! For +1 attack and +1 to charge.

Kragnos, the Warboss and the Gougers also exist to crack tougher targets that the lower rend on the infantry might not be able to overcome, bringing some real heavy duty hammers to the list.

Thoughts

Both armies here want to bait the opponent into charging them first, as if they can do it in the ideal locations, they get a serious advantage (Sylvaneth near overgrowth, Alarith and Warden Lumineth near objectives). The Lumineth do want to play a little more on offense, with Severith and Light of Eltharion encouraging getting into melee, though that can be quite difficult when Overgrowth becomes an issue.

Drycha is a particular problem since she can warp behind enemy lines and avoid the more dangerous charge targets to focus on things like the sentinels, and gut them from within.

Archetype

Alarielle and Crew

Thoughts

Alarielle doesn't see as much play due to her 600 point price tag, but if you want to build around her it's understandable why. She is a 3 cast wizard, passed out powerful buffs, hits like a truck and regenerates 2D6 wounds each turn for free. If she is killed she gets to come back once per game, pretty brutal stuff all around.

The Kurnoth want to mostly hang out in Overgrown terrain to proc anti-charge, but they're pretty good if they have to go on the offense too. Belthanos helps their rather slow walking speed while also being another strong hammer himself. Drycha has become a favorite tech piece for the army for her ability to teleport and charge unsuspecting targets.
