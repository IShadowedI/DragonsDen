Tabletop Battles Hobby Round-Up: July 2026

August 10th, 2026 via Goonhammer

We're more than halfway through the year, when priming becomes impossible for most of us because it's too humid and vacations abound. Indeed, a bunch of us got to see everyone's recent projects in-person in Linthicum Heights at the 2026 Goonhammer Open, or in Tacoma for the Tacoma Warhammer Open where Rob won a Golden Ticket! So let's see what the crew has been up to in the hobby mines in between tearing the tournament circuit up from coast to coast!

Jack

With the Tacoma Open mid-month, I needed to update some of my long-untouched Blood Angels to avoid just running the same Imperial Fist army every single year/edition.

Blood Angels Sanguinary Guard. Credit: Jack Hunter

I started off with finally finishing six Sanguinary Guard. I started on these in 2024 when they released and never got past the primer stage, but now that I've finished them I really love them.

Blood Angels Death Company. Credit: Jack Hunter

After that was another five Death Company. I already had five done, but it seems like 10-man squads are the way to go here - so I slammed out another five to fill them up.

Chaplain Lemartes. Credit: Jack Hunter

And finally I had to get Lemartes done to go with them. I don't love what I did on his right shoulder pad, and may go back in to brighten it up some more, but he's a fun model (and horny with the spiked garter and ball gag). After Tacoma I took a few days off from painting to recover from cramming these out, and am very nearly done with another six Sanguinary Guard before I move on to the Armageddon box.

NotThatHenryC

Ok well it's true that I've made a start on some Armageddon stuff. I've been working on some reinforcements for the Crimson Fist army I started back in 8th edition, which I've done both with the Armageddon stuff and by attacking the vast pile of grey plastic shame that has accumulated.

The sensible thing would be to pick a unit and get it finished. I haven't quite done that. Here's what I'm working on at the moment. A couple of dreadnoughts and speeders are nearly done. I'm doing some details and markings, like the checks on the Ballistus and go-faster stripes on the speeders. There'll be more of that sort of thing to come, plus some transfers. I also need to figure out how I'm basing all this stuff.

Loading...

I'm also building some stuff. I'd done a Pedro Kantor conversion before but the Armageddon Captain is a bit closer to the old metal one. I have assembled a new version using that body as a base, with new arms and some different details - most of which are fists. There's still some Green Stuff work to do, like adding a scroll to the top of the banner.

WIP Pedro Kantor conversion. Credit: NotThatHenryC

I'm making a unit of Sternguard to go with Pedro. I wanted them to look less formal and more dynamic than the standard models so these are made from Assault Intercessor bodies with various bits stuck to them. These are my two Heavy Bolter guys, with guns from Devastators and shields from Inceptors.

WIP Sternguard. Credit: NotThatHenryC

Meanwhile I've been having a lot of fun making terrain for the new Exodite expansion for Kill Team. The expansion has templates to make various structures from plasticard, which is pretty unusual these days. Kill Team has provided us with all sorts of narrative terrain lately but all as models, not designs to scratch build yourself. It makes a change though, and is a nice alternative as a way to produce a low-cost board.

So far I've made these three structures from plasticard. They took a while to build and just a few minutes to paint, using Montana Marble spray over a Colour Forge green, then finished with a green glaze.

Maiden World terrain. Credit: NotThatHenryC

The slideshow below shows the steps to building them. It's pretty laborious but I think the result is very nice.

Loading...

I've managed to order a copy of Exodite, which was not guaranteed as numbers seem to have been very small. My Forest will be populated by Eldar, Dinosaurs and Orks before too long. For now, they've had some extremely old Rangers and Striking Scorpions facing up against some warbikes, which might show their scale at least.

Maiden World terrain. Credit: NotThatHenryC

A unit of Intercessors!  Easily my favorite Space Marine unit from the Armageddon box in everything but engineering (that would be the Land Speeder), these guys were a joy:

Intercessors: Credit, Contemptor Kevin Stillman

In an effort to not fall into a painting rut, I've decided to swap between my Orks and Space Marines in painting this box, after knocking out my favorite two models from the box (the Wartrakk and the Banner Nob).  So the next five were...five Ork Boyz!

Ork Boyz. Credit: Contemptor Kevin Stillman

Image credit: Jefferson Powers

Man-Thing in particular was pretty easy but surprisingly fun to do. Next up we have the big man himself, the Kingpin of Crime.

Image credit: Jefferson Powers

I painted the suit by drybrushing light grey and then white, then did all the details with Speedpaints -- it worked pretty well and went very quickly.

Image credit: Jefferson Powers

This maintenance crew is made by Crooked Dice for their excellent Colony 87 range. I have a fairly large pile of their models that I like to work on in between larger projects. Since I'm not trying to match established characters or game artwork, I can be a little more creative with the color choices.

Image credit: Jefferson Powers

Finally we have Solomon Kane himself, from Monolith's Solomon Kane RPG Vault, a bargain-priced repackaging of the models from their Solomon Kane board game. Since I don't have a particular purpose in mind for them, this is another set of models I'm doing a little at a time as the mood strikes me.

SRM

This was a light month for hobby, but a big month for games played. Fortunately you can catch up on that over on the 40k Badcast, where we recapped the Tacoma Open, our first GT of 11th edition 40k.

Ultramarines Librarian in Terminator Armor. Credit: SRM

I went hard on Librarians this month, as Librarius Conclave became the new hotness for a brief flash of time. First among these was the Terminator Librarian from Leviathan, and with him done, I can finally mark that box complete, some three years late. I found all the runic engraving to be kind of a pain to paint in and around, and if you look closely it probably isn't as clean as I'd like. It was nice working some different colors in there though, pivoting to an aqua color for the gems and force weapon, plus the little runnels of psychic energy on his armor. I probably should have done those in purple instead, but it's good enough for the studio paintjobs so it's probably good enough for mine.

Ultramarines Librarian. Credit: SRM

One big box down and we're on to the next! The Armageddon Librarian was a fun model to paint, with some lovely expanses of fabric to layer up. He had more of those engraved details that I don't super enjoy, plus the psychic doodaddery on his staff is kind of annoying, but I'm happy with the result. He's a pretty hard model to carry around in a case though, sticking out in seemingly every dimension. It's also strange batch painting a quartet of extremely detailed character models, but such is life in the Warhammer mines.

Ultramarines Librarian. Credit: SRM

I'd picked up the Ultramarines Combat Patrol with the intent of kitbashing Tigurius with a regular Librarian, and that's exactly what I ended up doing. A little shaving on the arm nubs made it an easy conversion, but I didn't go wild on more converting or the paintjob, as he's just got so much stuff on him. I like painting force swords and axes more than force staves, it just gives a better canvas for those big long blends and lends more color to the model. If you couldn't tell, I very much enjoy going hard with color. Unfortunately a smidge of black weathering powder wandered over from his back vents to the skull on his head then got sealed with varnish, so I'll have to paint over that someday.

Ultramarines Librarian. Credit: SRM

For the last of this batch of Librarians, I painted the other half of the Tigurius/vanilla Librarian kitbash process. Despite being made up of mostly leftover pieces, I still like this guy. Most importantly, he incorporates Li'l Baby Stabbums from the old plastic Librarian model. My buddy Mason got that model back in like 2013 and gave me the cherub, and I've just been waiting over a decade for the right model to use him on. I intended to stick him on the Armageddon Librarian (itself a soft remake of that older Librarian model) but the directions of the fabric didn't line up, so instead he's here on this more up-and-down space wizard.

Ultramarines Sternguard with Heavy Bolters. Credit: SRM

Part of what made Librarius so good was how well it paired with Sternguard, and my Veteran Intercessors from 8th served admirably as Sternguard stand-ins. That old unit didn't have access to any of the cool toys Sternguard have now that they've gotten the Primaris treatment, so I painted up a pair of Heavy Bolters to bring them up to speed. Naturally these were finished too late to bring to the Tacoma Open, but they're here now, and I've been told that late is better than never, though I can't find a source on that particular clai. These guys were an easy kitbash using the Heavy Bolter from the Space Marine Heroes Gravis Marine and Sicarius' spare head for one guy, and standard Sternguard bits for the other, given to me by my buddy Nik. Like the models you've seen in this article, we are kitbashes of all the bits we've been given in our lives, be they plastic or something more substantial.

With the brief period of Librarius dominance likely at an end, it's time to move on to other, likely still blue things. I'm giving Reclamation Force a try these days, and that'll mean painting more regular jamokes to flesh out my Assault Intercessor squads. There's some old army modernization to get to for sure, though I'll probably be distracted by something new and shiny before I can get all my squads up to speed. While I typically reject the "Dug from Up" characterization of having ADHD, that new XCOM game I got to interview Chris from Modiphius about sure has my interest, and Necromunda is getting some love, so we might be near the end of Ultramarines season.

dirtboi

My stuff consists of Star Wars: Legion minis! Been plugging away at the new Mandalorian Army box since the release and even finished some Initiates for a HTPE found here. Tried a "friends pick your colors" experiment with these fellas but wasn't too satisfied with some of the layering colors I selected. Still, I am quite please and looking forward to the next batch.

Picture credit: dirtboi

I also finished up my Armorer for another HTPE that releases next week, and I tried out some Vallejo True Metallic Metals, specifically the Forge Red and the Imperial Gold ranges. Quite loving these metallics, going to definitely pick up more. Here she is hanging out with some of her Children of the Watch Initiates.

Picture credit: dirtboi

Also plugged away at my "sizeable" Ohnaka Gang army, getting ever so closer to the finish line. By "sizeable", I mean 4 BIG tanks. I still need to oil wash the tanks, bases, and anything metallic, apply some weathering, and finish with a nice black base rim. It would help if I stopped going to small local tournaments and dedicated that 10 hours to crossing said finish line.

Andrew_N

Ive been bit bad by the Battletech bug this month - I finished a light company's worth of 2nd Free Worlds Guards just this week, and instead of focusing on terrain or models for Heresy or Old World next, all I want to do is paint more mechs!   I likely will be painting some Rasalhague Dominion next, or perhaps some Avalon Hussars from the Federated Sons. I should finish painting some more Solar Auxilia, though!
