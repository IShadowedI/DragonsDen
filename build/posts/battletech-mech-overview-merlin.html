BattleTech Mech Overview: Merlin

July 29th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Another day of grinding rust and settling dust…

Jeremiah sighed, Malificient’s actuators groaning and grinding as the Merlin MLN-1A’s black forearm rose against the glaring sun. The gleaming blue lens of the medium laser embedded behind the arm’s cowl glinted in the oppressive sun, the flash of light matching the radiating heat waves rippling away from the pitch colored paint. 

At some point we’ll get some real contracts. See the Sphere, get out of the Periphery. Do something big for once. Jeremiah’s mind wandered, cruising along at the same crawling pace of his ‘Mech. He relished the image of his Merlin soldiering through inbound fire as the battle pushed on alongside them, airstrikes and artillery calling down death like a valkyrie from on high.

“Jeremiah, you there?” Carmine’s voice called, crackling and sweet, over the poorly tuned Magestrix Alpha set as the equally pitch carapace of her Ostroc OSR-2R lumbered into the crisp, refined image of the newer Merlin’s cameras. “You’re not day-dreaming on the job again, are ya’ love?”

“No, Cammie, why I’d never!” He gasped, an admonished smile on his lips as he turned to peer out of Malificent’s transpex canopy. “You know me, Darlin’, always on the ball.”

“Depends on what you mean by ‘the ball’.” Cammie, laughed back, her Ostroc slewing around to tromp off into the southerly portion of our route. “Last time you said that you ended up lost on the live fire course for oh what was it, four days?”

“Three.” Jeremiah hissed back, his voice low as he did his best to keep the embarrassment from clawing too deeply at his throat, keeping his ‘Mech only a few strides behind hers. “A-And that’s entirely Hanson’s fault and you know it!” 

“Ah, yes, blame the range ‘Tech, Jer.” She giggled, her Ostroc’s shoulders bouncing in time with her own. “I’m sure it had nothing to do with a cute hotshot of a pilot feelin’ invincible in a shiny new ‘Mech.”

A flush of red that Jeremiah could only thank his neurohelm and the tinted transpex in front of it for hiding rushed across his cheeks as his partner complimented him. His mind ground and spun as he grasped at whatever words he could find to jab back at her with. Finally he seized on something to say, only to have his retort cut short before it ever even left his lips.

“Wizard One, this is Roundtable. Come in.” Rebecca’s voice zipped in and out on the banding static, chopping and chipping for a moment before I tuned the receiver up to cut past the interference. 

“Roundtable, Wizard One here. What’ve you got?” Jeremiah answered, slowing as Carmine turned to survey the horizon around them.

“Not exactly sure, Wizard. We’ve got some seismic hits about 40 clicks to your west. Something around two Lances or a full company. Moving close to mask their numbers. Can’t get a good count but they’re definitely bumping somewhere north of 70 KM/H.”

“Great.” Jeremiah grumbled, his ‘Mech’s myomer humming and straining as he hauled it around to peer out over the distant western hills. The telltale plume of dust hadn’t surfaced yet, obscured by the crests of the hills no doubt. Either way, this wasn’t going to be good. “Let’s get the rest of the Lance and whatever support assets are unloaded moving and up. Cammie and I will wait here.”  

Glory awaits.

— Mechwarrior Jermiah Oto, Unaffiliated Mercenary Command, Daylight Mercenary Company Merlin MLN-1A Pilot. Qualzi, Unaffiliated, June 12th 3038. 

—

Howdy ‘Mechwarriors and welcome back to the ‘Mech Overview! This week we ramble on out to the periphery to one of the first new machines to be built out in the far flung worlds after the succession wars, the Merlin! Introduced in 3010 with the original MLN-1A the Merlin rapidly spread throughout the Periphery and mercenary market while still kicking all about the place come the IlClan era.

—

Merlin. Credit: Rockfish

Chassis

The Merlin is a fine example of a reliable old 60 tonner. Every single one, that isn’t a special weirdo variant, has 84% of the possible armor, totaling out to 168 total pips. All of them go 4/6/4 and trundle along as they do their very best troopery duties.

Their BV’s range from 1,121 up to 1,870… well, one of them is up in the 1800s. The rest are sub 1400 but that’s besides the point.

Variants

These mechs have all been reviewed based on a standard F through S scale, which you can find described on our landing page here (along with all of our other ‘mech reviews, the name of the box you can buy to get any of the mechs we have covered, and our general methodology).

1st Canopian Cuirassiers Merlin. Credit: Valk

MLN-1A

The Outworlds Alliance sure is doin’ shit out there. Shit’s great. Well… except for the thing where you poke its belly and it explodes.

The old man rolls on, ready and willing to get beaten on and deal the beatings all the same. For a paltry 1,217 BV you get a 4/6/4 60 tonner with 84% armor. It’s packing a pair of Medium Lasers, one in each arm, a PPC in the Right Torso, a Flamer and a machine gun in the left torso, with a half ton of MG ammo and a full ton of LRM 5 ammo in the CT… and 18 single heat sinks.

Look, it was 3010, we can only expect so much from them here.

It goes up 3 + movement if it fires everything off. Manageable enough, considering the range bands you’ve got to work with here.

Liberty’s Rating: C-. It’s got the ‘explode like a grape’ CT issue but at this price for a brick of armor to push up, throw PPC shots at things, and do some skirmishing it's really pretty alright.

MLN-1B

Machine guns are lame. They should be heat sinks!

The 1B takes an A, drops the MG and its ammo, moves the LRM ammo to the Left torso, and puts two heat sinks in the CT… that’s it. For this you pay 1,228 BV instead of 1,217… Cool, I’ll take more survivable and better heat sinking for 11 more BV. sounds good to me.

Liberty’s Rating: C. Just a 1A but better! There’s still a flamer here, more than enough to keep the bugs off ya.

Lyran Commonwealth Merlin. Credit: SRM

MLN-1C

SUCH RANGE! SUCH SPEED! SUCH… oh no, wait, same speed, never mind.

Take a 1B… now make the Medium Lasers Extended Range… that’s it, that’s the change. 1,267 BV.

Cool. Fire off the ERMLs and PPC and you’ve got 20 heat for your 19 sinking which is 1+ movement and therefore fine.

Liberty’s Rating: C. If you want ERMLs instead of normal MLs take this one, I suppose?

MLN-1D

Oh, now we’re doin’ shit with new toys.

We’ve bounced some weight to save by switching from 19 singles to 13 double heat sinks. Packing a PPC, a light PPC in each arm, an LRM-5, a flamer, and a Heavy Machine Gun all in the usual places. The LRM and HMG ammo in the left torso is protected by CASE and padded with 2 DHS, an HMG, and a flamer.

Honestly, for 1,370 BV this thing slaps. I use it a lot for my marshals and I love the damned thing. It’s cheap, it’s beefy enough to take some time to kill. It’s got reach out and touch options, and it just kinda does stuff at you. Namely run, fire a PPC, two Light PPCs, and an LRM-5, at you. It does lack for close range power now that the MLs are gone but with careful positioning it makes for an alright mid-range plinker to poke and prod at the enemy with. A bit sad to have no close range options though.

Liberty’s Rating: C+. Like I said, I like him! Good ‘mech for junk bot ops, set him to a task and let him try and bully someone for their hubris.

MLN-1E

OH BOY, CLOSE RANGE OPTIONS!

The E is a D that dropped the HMG and a Heat Sink to swap the LRM to an MML-5 with two tons of ammo in a CASE II protected bay. This is somehow 1BV cheaper at 1,269 BV which… honestly I’m upset the Fronc Reaches doesn’t get this variant, especially considering the Taurians are building the damned thing right next door.

Liberty’s Rating: B-. God I love MMLs, and MMLs that are safe as shit and compliment the rest of the weapons load well make me very happy.

MLN-1P

The Fed Rats are… just doin’ things with the Merlins… for some reason?

Take a 1D, clad it in Heavy Ferro-Fibrous, replace the Light PPCs with Medium VSPs and the PPC with an LVSP. We’ve still got an LRM-5 with one ton of ammo, and a flamer, but we’ve also ditched the CASE… for some reason.

Firing just the VSPs makes you heat neutral on the run which is acceptable and workable. This… is a pretty alright screening heavy to keep lights and zippy mediums off the backs of your bigger boys, and for 1,182 BV that is… really enticing.

Liberty’s Rating: B+. Shit fucks, if only it still had CASE though so it wouldn’t just stop existing sometimes.

Mountain Wolves Merlin. Credit: Lynn C

C

UH OH, WE DOIN’ BIRD SHIT UP IN HERE!

So, the original Merlin was built by the Outworlds Alliance. Well, they had a whole thing with Clan Snow Raven and became the Raven Alliance… and made this thing.

Take a 1D, make it on a Clan Chassis, clad it in Ferro-Lamellor, and give it a pair of Improved Heavy Medium Lasers, a Streak LRM-5, a clan ERPPC, a Clan Flamer, and a Clan HMG. Has the same pips of armor, same movement profile, but now sinks such that it puts a brick on the firing stud and just goes. 

Every turn it fires everything and is heat neutral on the run. Ferro-Lam keeps it well protected, pissed off, and approaching. Slow but sure it closes until it’s on you and it gets the iHMLs talking.

For 1,870 BV this thing’s honestly a hoot. It could certainly be more for the price but it’s also the best I think the Merlin ever truly gets.

Liberty’s Rating: B+. The Device is approaching. The Device is angry. The Device speaks bird.

MLN-SX

Oh god… it’s doin’ weird shit. It’s a Jihad unique and it’s doing weird shit. Sigh… Where’s my rulebook?

The SX is a 1P with an endo composite structure, a light engine, and 88% armor, instead of 84. For 1,121 BV you trade away your LVSP, your LRM-5, and your flamer to mount a bombast laser, an extra heat sink, and a laser insulator on each gun. So, what do Laser Insulators do?

Simple: They Insulate your laser. Making the MVSP build 6 heat instead of the usual 7 and the Bombast… slightly less in each of its myriad fucked up and weird firing bands. Cool…

Liberty’s Rating: Uh… C? Look there’s a Bombast Laser here, backed up by MVSPs or not, how good could it really be?

MLN-1B (Porter)

Take a 1B, now replace the PPC with a large laser, drop the flamer, add a heat sink, and replace the LRM-5 with an SRM 4…Also it’s got full on 100% armor coverage now.

Sure, cool, it’s 1,233 BV and can fire everything and only build heat if it jumps the full 4 hexes.. Supposedly it’s from some guy named Porter Romero on Armington who salvaged the merlin during a pirate raid and then… patched it up with a busted up Bulldog… Sick!

Liberty’s Rating: C+. I dig it, kinda wish this was a mainline variant, would be cool.

Hastati Sentinels Merlin. Credit: Jack Hunter

Final Thoughts

Merlins are… consistent if nothing else. They largely do the same thing, which is fine, but I really wish there was something more. I’d love some good in close fighters, or a real long range hammer to pound on people with. Maybe a C2 to drive them both home at the same time?

Plenty of options, especially considering how many places make some variant of the Merlin.

Until next time! Fair weather, good seas and happy casting-and-conjuring-in-the-name-of-Arthur-and-the-roundtable!
