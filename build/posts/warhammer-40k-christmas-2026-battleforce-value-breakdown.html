Warhammer 40k Christmas 2026 Battleforce value breakdown

October 19th, 2026 via Wargamer

On Monday, Games Workshop revealed seven new Warhammer 40,000 battleforce box sets for Christmas 2026, each containing a sizeable chunk of a Warhammer 40k army. While GW hasn't yet revealed the prices for the sets, these are always bundle deals that offer a saving over buying the contents separately - but which stocking has been stuffed the most? We've worked out the prices to save you the hassle.

The last run of battleforces went on sale for $265 / £165, and if that price sticks then all of these sets will be sold at a discount of at least $75 / £43.50. With that said, sometimes one or two Warhammer 40k factions from a given run of battleforces costs a little more or less, so keep your eyes on the actual shop price.

Assuming the price is flat across all seven boxes - which is not necessarily the case - then the Chaos Space Marines have gotten far and away the best deal, with close to a full 1,000 point starter army and the most sprues of any of the sets. All of these sets break GW's normal army pricing conventions, and - assuming you want the contents - they're an effective way to get a jump starton an army. If you've got a proudly painted battleforce from last Christmas, come and share some pictures in the Wargamer Discord community!
