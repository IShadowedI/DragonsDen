Archetype

Brutal Pressure Blood Angels

Thoughts

A win for Blood Angels kicks us off today, following the pattern established by most lists in using lots of Sanguinary Guard, augmented by Tacticus Attack Force and Assault Brethen. As with some other builds we've seen they're backed up by Death Company Dreadnoughts, this time upgraded with the Artificer Sarcophagus to turn them into the ultimate wrecking balls. They're really good at that - the combination of damage reduction, a Feel No Pain and a Blood Surge makes handling them a high risk prospect for most opponents, and they hit like trucks when they connect. That really describes the entire army here, which is fully capable of steamrolling through many armies, in a very classic Blood Angels fashion. Congratulations Joe!

Archetype

Penitent Pressure

Thoughts

Jordan continues to explore ways to use Repentia, and also to gently try my patience with image-based army lists. We should have moved past that, as a culture. At least the Defiler one was funny.

Anyway - lots of Penitents, ready to slam into the foe at rocket speed, and with lots of Mortifiers providing a fair bit more backbone than Repentia alone would muster. Supporting them, two Exorcists with Symphonic Payload and the Triumph to enable double Acts of Faith, providing vasty more reliability than you'd expect from a couple of hulls. The big Triupmh unit also provides a very sticky way to contest positions - you choose to order the Triumph ahead of the Hospitaller for taking damage, and then until the foe has chewed through all 18W with a Feel No Pain, some Sisters can pop back up around it every turn. Very good for consolidating gains carved out by the more murderous nuns. Congratulations Jordan on taking undefeated second place with a very unique build!

Archetype

Terminator Warband

Thoughts

The World Eaters have calmed down a little after last weeks excesses, but they're still doing fine, and this list shows off another strong angle for them. S5 AP-1 combi-bolters are super good on WE Terminators thanks to their re-rolls and high shot count, and the Butchers detachment is really exceptional, augmenting them on a number of different angles, and making them credible competitors to loyalist Terminators. A big part of the draw is that in Warband, you can stack the +1A Stratagem on top of the detachment rule, providing a truly heroic amount of damage output that will flatten almost anything. Two units of those feels great, as you can start one on the table and Ingress the other, and I'm a fan of finding room for the Kharn unit alongside them, as to me any World Eaters list without his squad is leaving value on the table. Great to see another angle on Angron's finest, well done Theo.

Archetype

Snagga War Horde Goodstuff

Thoughts

Orks crush the competition at our next event, going for a fairly all-rounder War Horde setup centred on herohammer and Beast Snaggas. At their low price, they're a very efficient way of piling on the pressure, and they do especially well with War Horde's boosted output and Fight on Death. The Battle-shock ability is genuinely pretty real, too, and with Dreadnought looking solid for both Custodes and Marines, it provides a solid angle of attack. Elsewhere, this is all stuff you know and love - the Ork heavy herohammer suite is one of the best out there, and this list properly saturates the foe with must-kill targets. Super nasty stuff, congratulations Fred!

Thoughts

Loyalist on loyalist action for today's showdown, as aggro Templars try to stop the Ultrawatch menace. The Templar build takes advantage of all their best units being Tacticus, and thus benefitting from Attack Force, and also the fact that there are Scouts Enhancements in both that and Vow-Sworn. That means the Templars can go fast, threatening swift retribution against any foe that stands in their way.

Unfortunately, the Ultramarines here are pretty great at standing in the way, and a few factors are going to be very tough for the Templars to overcome. The killer here is that that the Ultrawatch have access to really good defensive Stratagems (Armour of Contempt, Tactic Dreadnought Fortitude) and the Templars do not. The Templars, of course, are on Purge, so they have to go on the front foot and try and fight the Ultramarines.

The confluence of these two factors augurs...poorly. I'm always a little hesitant to distance diagnose issues with other people's lists, but I think it's very risky to run this Templar build as Purge, and that it would do a lot better as T&H (which it can get via Tacticus). I still think it would be at a disadvantage in this game, but it would at least be able to leverage the OC advantage from the big Crusader brick to try and make something happen. As is, I think this is extraordinarily uphill for them, and the scoreline would appear to reflect that.
