[Star Wars Legion] Competitive Innovations in a Galaxy Far Far Away: September 2026

October 15th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our serialized feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the month’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Another month of GTs has passed, and with it, more players are finding the best ways to bring their favorite armies to the top. With September also being the month of LVO we have decided to make a separate article covering LVO since it deserves its own coverage for Star Wars Legion. Do we have any big meta shakeups this month? Are we still in the era of the Tauntaun brothers dominance? Let's get to the stats and find out.

Thoughts

Looking at these two lists, the Mandalorian list does seem to have some advantages over the Rebels, with all the Impact that can deal with those ATRTs before they can get into their preferred range and the entire list lacking armor for the ATRTs to target. The activation gap could definitely have caused some issues for the Mandalorians, especially since all those extra activations just mean more attacks that can stack suppression on an army that doesn't have access to the best suppression mitigation. On the Rebels' end we see a variety of strong, efficient units that have proven time and time again in this current meta that they are the cream of the crop. The Rebel list does have a lack of both Tauntaun brothers that we normally see, and opts for Operative Luke instead. Operative Luke can be a great control piece that the Mandalorians list has to work around since it can turn off the damage output for 2 units, on top of Change of Plans letting the Rebel list interrupt their opponent's command card for the turn. 

Result

The game ended on Round 5 14-8, with the Mandalorian List taking the win.

Thoughts

Richard of Crit2Block fame has won 2 GTs now with this exact list, a combination of the best Children of the Watch has to offer mixed with the Darksaber Bo for even more token generation. This kind of list has proven to be efficient since it can Infiltrate up to 4 units because of how Detachment works. The combination of Tank Hunter and Jetpack Rockets gives the Fire Support teams a lot of anti-vehicle potential, especially in this vehicle heavy meta. The Flame Projector Warriors are also a threat in close range that can delete squishier units. With the amount of tokens this list can generate as well as the damage that it can push out towards the opponent's list, it is no wonder Richard is doing so well with it.

Thoughts

This Rebel list has a lot of the usual suspects aside from a lightsaber-wielding, Tauntaun-riding commander of the Echo Base forces. Sniper teams and Commandos have proven to be more than worth their weight in points and activations, forcing characters or other more difficult to kill units to be properly positioned lest they take some unblockable damage, or even need to roll dice against multiple crits. Hantaun is a wrecking ball of a unit that has helped define the current Rebel list building, and it is clear with this list that he is here to stay. A unique inclusion is Operative Luke, who on his own is a powerhouse of a unit with the ability to jump from melee to melee with Disengage on his unit card, a rarity in Star Wars Legion. A tanky unit with a massive 4 courage with a suite of command cards that can control the battlefield around him, it is no wonder Brian Bear has been singing Operative Luke's praises for a while now. Triple ATRT with the Rotary Blasters brings some fast and efficient Anti-Armor to round out the list.

Best of the Rest

The rest of top 8 from top cut

3rd - Michael Lohse - Another Mandalorian list that brings almost the maximum amount of custom characters available, it is still a Kryze/Children of the Watch list with all the usual upgrades.

4th -Jonathan Griz Grizaniuk - A Rapid Interdiction Force with triple STAPS and Wheelbikes and Grievous on Wheelbike to head the list.

5th - Rob Mowgly Rowland - A 20 activation Empire list running Thrawn/Krennic and maxing out on Shoretroopers and Mortar Troopers.

6th - Brian LevityGames Labay - LevityGames - An empire list headed by Iden and Krennic with Operative Vader and 3 strike teams but no snipers, just the Sonic Charges.

7th - Daniel Avalos - An Ashoka and Yoda list with triple arcs and ATRT's for some anti-infantry and anti-armor.

8th - Isaac Pendley - A Rebel list with the Hauntaun brothers tripe snipers and ATRT's and Cassian fleet troops. A group of all the best Rebels have to offer.

Congratulations to all players who were able to make it and the top 8 for 3 long days of Star Wars Legion rounds. You all did a fantastic job!

Thoughts

A Poggle and Trench list with a custom Operative and Sun fac. This list brings cheap, and efficient characters that bring a lot to control the board and the opponents gameplan. Trench's Ploy Tokens can mess with opponents plans by adding suppression on unsuspecting units or even sending their orders back to the pool. Poggle also brings a cheap commander option that equips Fire Control well, especially since he is deceptively fast and can keep up with the other units around him. There are a host of different units in this list that show off how well CIS units can function with just a little support or even no commanders to support them.

Best of the Rest

The rest of top 8 from top cut.

3rd - An Ohnaka gang list with all 4 tanks, 6 hit the dirt Weequay squads and the man of the hour Hondo Ohnaka.

4th - A CIS list with Kalani and a custom Operative, an Ion Spider Droid and a Flamethrower Spider Droid and a Snail Tank bring heavy support to 4 B1's and some commandos.

5th - A Rebel list that forgoes any characters on Tauntaun and instead brings Han on foot, a custom Commander, Din, and a custom Mandalorian Operative. These support triple snipers, rebel troops, and a X-34 Landspeeder.

6th - A Mandalorian list that brings a lot of what we see with the Kryze/Children of the Watch lists that are floating around but opting to bring a custom commander and Operative.

7th - A Trench and Kalani list with a custom Operative and Aquas instead of Droidekas or Spider Droids. A nice spread of B1's and Commandos as well.

8th - A pure Clan Saxon list, with a custom Operative, Commander, and Rook as well. Triple Supercommandos and a host of warriors, with no Children of the Watch or Kryze to be seen.

Congratulations to all the players who made it to top 8; 5 rounds of Star Wars Legion over two days can test your endurance and everyone should be proud of their performance.

Thoughts

A Rebel vs GAR matchup, with the Rebel list opting to being a WLO and some Clan Wren Vets while the GAR player brought Commander Ahsoka who you don't see often. This match really comes down to positioning and proper target assessment, since both lists have a variety of anti-armor and anti-infantry firepower.

Thoughts

A GAR list that brings Yoda and Padme for some token support as well as Yodas entire kit. No republic special forces like Arc troopers to be found but in there place are some Clan Kryze Vets who can Infiltrate with Axe and a unit of Wookies with the newest character upgrade Chewbacca for some tanky hard hitting melee threats with some upgrading dice from their newly acquired character. 2 WLO tanks bring in some armored threats with decent firepower, and the decision to bring 2 of them has certainly made a tough to kill army even tougher to kill.

Best of the Rest

The rest of top 8 from top cut.

3rd - A Leia led Rebel list with double bus and a unit of Chewie led wookies, no Tauntauns or snipers in sight.

4th - A pure Children of the Watch List with only Din as the named character, letting Paz and the Armorer lead their own units with it all being led by 2 customs.

5th - A Tauntaun brothers list bringing a variety of mercs with Ewok Slingers and Skirmishers and Urda without her Veterans, and some Astromech troopers for the double landspeeders.

6th - A Galactic Republic list with Padme, Yoda, and a custom leading the charge. Flanked with an Ashoka maximum squad of clones, Cody led Marksmen, and a Plo led Arc squad.

7th - A Children of the Watch list with Bo added to spread the extra tokens around. 2 custom Operatives and a custom Commander lead the rest of the Warriors and Fire Support squads

8th - A CIS list led by Grievous on Wheelbike, Poggle, a custom and Sun Fac. This list brings Aquas, Spiders, and Geonosians but decides to avoid all the other droids we have been accustom to seeing.

Congratulations to everyone who made top 8, great job with keeping up your endurance for playing all 5 rounds and doing your best.

We have seen each faction start to gravitate towards some incredibly efficient units as well as seeing Mandalorians finding the lists that utilize the tricks they have available to them. Mercs, CIS, Republic, Rebel, and even some Empire lists have been squeaking their way into top 8 with some clear winners of the bunch. This is not without some players finding ways to utilize some interesting models such as Operative Luke or even an entire pure Clan Saxon list. Character upgrades have also seem to found their homes and in some cases have breathed some new life into units such as Wookies. With a developer update in November we may see some major shakeups coming soon to a galaxy far far away.
