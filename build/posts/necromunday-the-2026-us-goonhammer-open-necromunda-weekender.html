Necromunday: The 2026 US Goonhammer Open Necromunda Weekender!

June 22nd, 2026 via Goonhammer
It's GHO season, and once again Necromunda will be at the GHO US in Baltimore, MD! There are still some spaces left, and if you're interested grab a ticket here.

We love Necromunda. A lot. We love it so much that it's been a featured element of the last three Goonhammer Opens, and this year is no different. Weekender events like the GHO and the NOVA Open (shout out to Crimson Oracle) are a great way to rapidly get some games in, make friends, and have some good vibes. We're happy to say that every year things get a little better and a little smoother, and after the success of the 2025 event we're hoping to do even better.

Check Out the 2026 Primer Here

The link above is the draft copy of the primer. If you have any comments or suggestions then please share them (you can add comments to the document), as we would love your feedback.

Our primary goal for the Necromunda Weekender is that everyone has fun in every game. This means doing everything in our power to mitigate "feelsbad" moments where the player questions the point of playing. The biggest risk of that is snowballing, where the gangs that win (and thus get more credits) continue to win because they're just more powerful. That's not really fun in a weekender campaign where everyone paid to play for two days. We successfully addressed that last year using an Escalation style mechanic where the players could make whatever gang they wanted up to a credit limit. This let players keep their marque characters who did awesome stuff but prevented players who lost everything from feeling like they shouldn't play another game.

Prepare to be hunted. Credit: LordPancakes on Reddit

2026: Everything Wants to Kill You

In 2025 we had the players all fight for "shards of brisance" which were very shiny and very deadly crystals which could blow up if you got shot while carrying them. This year we're going with an even deadlier theme; Hive Baltimore is infested with monsters and they all want to kill you.

Back in 2020, Games Workshop released a fun little solo mission called The Hunt. While unfortunately the rules of that mission are no longer available from WarCom's website (GW if you're reading this could you put them back? Please?), the TTB team is full of sickos who hoard everything and we kept a copy of the rules. We're adapting them to a two-player game where each map contains a unique monster. Players will have two goals; acquire loot and kill monsters.

I don't know what this thing is but it's going to kill someone. Credit - Kevin Genson

Get Your Favorite Movie Quotes Ready

Acquiring loot is fun, but what about acquiring loot while beings of unspeakable terror are actively trying to slaughter you? The core theme of this year's GHO is that every map will feature a monster. Players are going to absolutely want to read the primer because these things will appear from nowhere and hunt the players, rapidly acting before disappearing into the shadows to strike again. Monsters have two states; Hiding and Active. When Hiding, the players attempt to search for the monster or set up for covering fire. At every End Phase a D6 is rolled to see if the monster appears; the more fighters that search the less likely that the monster will surprise them.

Once the monster appears it is Active and will begin to hunt the fighters. Each monster is unique and will follow their own chart based on the mission. Every monster has both melee and ranged options and ignores things like Blaze or the Webbed condition. The monster goes first before Priority is rolled and then activates again each time a player finishes an activation (or the last model in a group activation). They will charge or attack the nearest fighter, and should a hapless victim be Seriously Injured and alone then don't be surprised if they're dragged into the dark... never to be seen again.

The good news is that if you kill a monster, their head is worth a lot of loot!

The Trading Post and Loot Points

One change from last year is that the auction-style Trading Post is going away. As fun as it was the logistical burden on the sleep deprived Arbitrator was a bit much. Instead, each mission will contain objectives which will provide an opportunity for players to acquire Loot Points. These points provide two benefits. First, whoever accumulates the most Loot Points gets a trophy at the end. Second, accumulating points allows you to get rare stuff from the Trading Post. Your Loot Point total serves as a cap on the total Rarity of things that your gang can field. For example, if you have 10 Loot Points then you can bring one item of Rarity 10 or less. If you have 20 Loot Points then you could bring a Rarity 15 item or a pair of Rarity 10 items. The more Loot Points you acquire, the more things you get! You still have to pay for the value of the items out of your credit limit, but if you wanted every Slave Ogryn to have a pet grapplehawk then here's your chance.

Escalation

What about credits? The answer is simple; don't worry about it! None of the gangs will earn credits over the course of this campaign. Instead, the campaign will use a variant of the Escalation rules to represent the various contractors putting more and more money into achieving their goals. Players will start with a 1200 credit gang for the first mission, and then after each mission they will be able to add 200 credits to the gang rating until the final match has every gang featuring a 2000 credit gang. Fighters fully Recover between missions and the intent is for players to not have to worry about falling behind because their Leader overcharged a plasma cannon and exploded. If you've ever wondered wanted to take advantage of the fact that Ambots and Ash Wastes Arachni-rigs come in packages of two, now's your chance! Reputation will not be used this year which means you're free to take as many weirdos as you want.

What kind of monstrosity could appear in the middle of a race through the sewers of Hive Baltimore? Who knows! Credit: Kevin Genson

House Rules

The campaign will use a lot of the House rules that we previously discussed in our 2024 article about starting a campaign such as allowing shields to be equipped in addition to a suit of armour, only making one Injury Roll regardless of how many Out of Action results are produced, and making Melta bombs actually work. Instead of Tactics Cards, every player has access to Adrenaline Surge which grants a fighter an extra action on their Activation. Captured fighters are quickly interrogated and then freed, with the Interrogation representing an opposed Cool, Willpower, or Intelligence check to gain some XP for the participants.

Thanks for reading, and we hope to see you at the GHO in July! If you have any questions about the Weekender, you can either reach us at contact@goonhammer.com or contact Primaris Kevin on the Goonhammer Discord. The Primer is also set so that people can leave comments if they have questions or feedback, and we will be happy to take a look at ways to make things even better!
