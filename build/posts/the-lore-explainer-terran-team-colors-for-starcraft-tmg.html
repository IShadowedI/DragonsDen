The Lore Explainer: Terran Team Colors for StarCraft TMG

June 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer

In The Lore Explainer, we take a deep look at the lore behind our favorite games, movies, and books, and talk about the story behind them and sum up what you need to know and how you can find out more.

With the impending wide release of the StarCraft Tabletop Miniatures Game, many of us are starting to try to figure out how we're all going to make our armies fit into the wider StarCraft lore. While some people are content with just going with a scheme similar to the box art, or another similarly notable faction, there are plenty of gamers who want to represent a lesser known faction. This could be out of a sheer love for that particular band of space people, wanting to stand out at the tournament, or just the desire to have the best painted Antiga Prime Colonial Militia army in the world.

Antiga Primeheads went crazy in this cutscene. Credit: Blizzard Entertainment

Unfortunately for us tabletop gamers, the canon schemes of the StarCraft factions are not as readily available as the heraldry of any random 40k Space Marine chapter that only got a mention in a 1998 issue of White Dwarf magazine. In the original StarCraft, you couldn't even pick what color you wanted to play as in online matches, so there wasn't even really an opportunity to role play in that incredibly slight way. This resulted in this information not really being cataloged well on fan wikis, as there wasn't too much reason to care about it.

How the StarCraft Games Dealt With Team Color

While all of the various subfactions of the Koprulu did have team colors assigned to them, team color is treated as a game mechanic first and a lore fact as a distant second. Generally, if it is reasonable for your opponent to have the correct color, they will, but there are plenty of examples of blue Terrans being in the absolute wrong place. Part of the problem seems to be that the scenario editor didn't have a way to rename what faction was assigned to which color, so you will be fighting the Garm brood long after their cerebrate has been killed.

Outside of the game scenes, they also were not super consistent. Pretty much every FMV has Marines wearing whatever colors they like, with Zerg and Protoss units basically having no variety in their depictions.

A Marine in a unique scheme from the Battle on the Amerigo cinematic. Credit: Blizzard Entertainment

By the time StarCraft 2 comes along, they've gotten a whole lot better about having the subfactions be their correct colors, but there is a noticeable lack in variety of opponents - you're pretty much only ever fighting Dominion Terrans for example. Even any ally units you rescue get quickly repainted in your army's colors. Cinematics do match gameplay a lot more, at least.

StarCraft 1 had eight team colors to pick from: Blue, Red, Teal, Purple, Orange, Brown, White, and Yellow. Green was kind of a special case - it only existed to replace Brown on desert tilesets, and to replace White on snow ones. Brown itself is also kind of strange; it was supposed to be Black, but that caused problems with the mini map, so they changed it. There were also four NPC only colors, and 8 additional colors for multiplayer added in a patch about 15 years after StarCraft was released. Starcraft 2 keeps most of these colors, but replaces Brown and White with Green and Light Pink - sorry, Alpha Squadron fans.

Most of the lore for the subfactions themselves, especially the minor ones, comes from the original StarCraft manual. It's perfect for reading on in the backseat on the way home from Software, Etc, getting carsick while looking at sick ass Metzen art.

Terran Subfactions

Backwater Colonies and Insurgents

Credit: Contemptor Kevin Stillman

  • BLUE - Mar Sara Colonial Militia: The StarCraft campaign kicks off with the player controlling the Magistrate of the Mar Sara colony, and you have to contact Jim Raynor, and you all pretty quickly rebel against the Confederacy when it's clear they have no intention of keeping you safe from the Zerg threat. You get the colonists evacuated and they follow Jimmy throughout the stars, fighting for the freedom of not being stomped by boots, claws, or whatever Protoss wear on their feet. They later evolve into the:
  • BLUE - Raynor's Raiders: Well, at first they're the Raynor's Rangers, an elite team in the sons of Korhal. Disgusted with Mengsk's abandonment of Kerrigan, they strike out on their own and plot to overthrow the nascent dictator, kill/help the Queen of Blades, and generally anything they can do to save the galaxy from being eradicated. This is the player faction in StarCraft 2, and what you see on the StarCraft TMG box art. They're also one of the few factions with a bespoke faction symbol, two mirrored Rs with a tagger in the middle.
  • RED - The Sons of Korhal: Lead by the charismatic and definitely not megalomaniacal Arcturus Mengsk, the Sons were a major group of rebels and terrorists that sought to bring down the Confederacy and be enshrined as the human leaders of the Korpulu sector, or see everything burned to the ground trying. With the help of Jim Raynor and a few well placed Psi Emitters, they were successful, and forged the Terran Dominion. Their symbol is a beefy arm holding a whip in a circle around it.
  • PURPLE - Antiga Prime Colonial Militia: Antiga Prime was another colony like Mar Sara, on the outskirts of Confederate space. Their people rose up and successfully tore down their overseers, but were swiftly eliminated in a Confederate counterattack. Later, history repeats, and the next group of settlers on Antiga also revolt, and alongside the Sons of Korhal, are yet again successful, and don't get killed this time. They are only really seen in a single mission of the Terran campaign, the one where you first meet up with Kerrigan.

The Confederacy

The Confederacy itself has no distinct colors or iconography, aside from a very unfortunate flag you should not paint on your minis. You mainly fight against their military wings:

Alpha Squadron Goliath Online Credit: Mildnorman

  • WHITE - Alpha Squadron: AKA The Blood Hawks. lead by General Edmund Duke, these are the most elite of the forces that the Confederacy has to offer. They are your main human antagonists in the middle portion of the Terran campaign, and eventually join with the Sons of Korhal. They are most well known for the fact that they never leave their equipment behind, and certainly are not sloppy. Their symbol is a stylized alpha character with the Terran Armed Forces logo next to it.
  • BROWN - Omega Squadron: AKA The Death Head's Legion. There isn't a lot of in game lore for them, but the manual describes it as being "reknowned throughout Confederate space for its unrelenting savagry in battle". Their symbol is an upside down omega with a skull and crossbones next to it. Finally, you have something to do with all those Ultramarines decals.
  • PURPLE - Nova Squadron: The manual describes them as the squadron of "mystery and intrigue", they are tasked with intelligence gathering and inter-Confedrate policing, and are not well liked by the other squadrons. They do not appear in the StarCraft campaign, but are encountered in a beta mission found deep in the files on early copies of the StarCraft CD, Operation Silent Scream. Their symbol is an 8 pointed starburst.
  • ORANGE - Delta Squadron: Not mentioned in the manual, the only real purpose here is to give the player an additional player to fight against in anti-Confederate missions. You have plenty opportunity to spread your wings if you want to play as them. No symbol is given, but it's probably a stylized delta.
  • YELLOW - Epsilon Squadron: Slightly more mentioned in the manual, with a sole reference in the description of the Science Vessel. All we know about them is that they use those vessels for recon and study on the Confederate border. They are never seen in any StarCraft campaign, and we only know they're yellow because that's what the post game stats screen says.

Post-Confederate Governments

Terran Dominion Marines

  • RED - The Terran Dominion: Arcturus Mengsk's dreams become reality, and he transforms the dead Confederacy into an Empire with him at the helm. For the people of the Korpulu sector, life doesn't seem to get any better, really. There's plenty of sycophantic news coverage and endless golden statues of Mengsk and his dogs, all playing recording preaching Mengsk's greatness. This is the primary Terran antagonist of the latter StarCraft 1 campaigns and StarCraft 2's Wings of Liberty and Heart of the Swarm campaigns. Their symbol is a more angular version of the Sons of Korhal's arm and whip, arranged in a hexagonal red and black badge.
  • TEAL/GREEN - The Kel-Morian Combine: The Combine is an association of Terran occupational guilds that controlled a number of worlds outside of Confederate/Dominion space. They largely ignored the oncoming threats of the alien Zerg and Protoss, deferring instead to focus on making money, and lots of it. The Kel-Morians are only seen in one mission in Brood War's Zerg campaign. Despite having the teal color assigned to it, and the fact that there are 5 Terran AIs in that mission, the teal color is used as the hero color for Fenix, so they don't even get to use their team color in their solitary outing. Their symbol is a stylized K on top of an M in a box, with a drill to the right of it.
  • AUQA/WHITE - The Umojan Protectorate: Mostly seen early in StarCraft 2's Heart of the Swarm campaign, the Umojan Protectorate is a coalition of planets outside of Dominion space. They are known for developing high tech weapons and armors that are then sold to the Terrans fighting against the Dominion, though they did not really engage in war against the Dominion outright. Their troops have bright while, bulky armor, much sleeker than normal Terran gear, and you can purchase a ridiculously expensive skin set if you want to use it in StarCraft 2.

The United Earth Directorate Fleet

The United Earth Directorate

The UED sent a fleet of ships to conquer the Koprulu sector and capture a new Overmind. Despite being a few hundred years separated from the Koprulian Terrans, they had roughly exactly the same tech and weapons. What are the chances? There are two wings of the UED Fleet featured in Brood War.

  • BROWN - Atlas Wing: You play as this wing on the first mission of the UED campaign, probably because it is on the snow tileset of Braxis. I don't remember it coming up ever again.
  • WHITE - Cronos Wing: You play as the Cronos Wing in the rest of the campaign, and any time in the Kerrigan campaign that you fight the UED, it is generally the Cronos wing.

There are a few other UED forces you fight, like the yellow UED Defense Garrison in mission 6-2, or Stukov's blue Elite Guard, but they aren't given full subfaction names like the two wings. There unfortunately is not much to say about them, no real distinguishing characteristics are given. They were probably chosen as they wanted the UED to be white/black in scheme.

Mercenaries, Pirates, and Corporations

Mira's Marauders

  • LIGHT PINK - Mira's Marauders: Zooming ahead to StarCraft 2, Mira's Marauders are a group of mercenaries in girlypop pink with tons of cool spikes and skull motifs on their armor. Their leader, Mira Han, is married to Jim Raynor's first officer, but he's not really a huge fan of the arrangement. In the Wings of Liberty campaign, the player competes with the following group for Mira's aid.
  • ORANGE - Orlan's Mercenaries: Colonel Orlan lead a different, less cool band of mercenaries. Orlan is a former Confederate of the Alpha Squadron, who is seemly the sector's best hacker for some reason.
  • YELLOW - Moebius Foundation: Moebius is a research group founded by Dr Emil Narud, who wants to remind you to never read someone's name backwards. During Wings of Liberty, they command Tychus to team up with Raynor to find the pieces of the Xel'naga Keystone, and are antagonists during the middle portion of Heart of the Swarm.
  • SILVER/PURPLE - The Knights of Tyrador: The Knights are a group of mercenaries who are employed to protect and support the resort world of Tyrador. They are known for having extremely flashy armor and weapons. Their symbol is a chess knight with a unicorn horn.

StarCraft Terran Marines by Craig "MasterSlowPoke" Sniffen

There certainly are a wide variety of flavors of Terran for Zerglings to snack on. Did we miss your favorite band of humans? Let us know in the comments, or in the StarCraft channel on our Discord.
