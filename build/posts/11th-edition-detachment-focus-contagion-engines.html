11th Edition Detachment Focus: Contagion Engines

August 19th, 2026 via Goonhammer
In our Detachment Focus series we take a deep dive into an army’s Detachments, covering what’s in them, how they play, and how they’ll fit into the broader meta and your games. In this Detachment Focus we’re looking at the Contagion Engines Detachment for the Death Guard.

The Death Guard received three new 1 DP Detachments at the onset of 11th edition, each of which catered to a different unit type in the army. Of these, the Contagion Engines Detachment focuses on the faction's Bloat-Drones and Blight-Hauler units, giving them a solid boost.

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

Force Disposition: Reconnaissance

Death Guard armies have leaned heavily on Bloat-Drones and Blight-Haulers since the publication of their tenth edition Codex. They're fast, versatile units with decent durability and some solid damage output for their cost. The Contagion Engine Detachment gives them a much-needed boost by giving them the [ASSAULT] keyword on their ranged attacks.

In addition to this, there is one downside to the Detachment: Namely that it has the ENGINES tag, and can't be taken with another detachment with the ENGINES tag, which means you can't combine this with Mortarion's Hammer, blunting your ability to otherwise take a really sick vehicle Detachment. We're not sure that was necessary, given how Hammer tends to be less of a "vehicle horde" detachment normally and more of a "backdoor method of deep striking Deathshrouds" but it's something to think about.

Detachment Rule: Warped and Rusted Animus

Friendly Bloat-Drone, Helbrute, and Blight-Hauler units have the CONTAGION ENGINE keyword. Friendly CONTAGION ENGINE units have [ASSAULT] on their ranged attacks.

This is pretty good! It’s a nice mobility boost on your bloat-drones and blight-haulers and really helps the former make the most of the changes to the FLY rule, since now they can move through walls by taking a -2” penalty and negate that by simply getting a 2+ on their Advance roll. This is also great because these are units you just want to take - running a list with three Blight-Haulers and three Heavy Blight Launcher Bloat-Drones is more than reasonable, and having extra movement will make your opponent work harder to stay out of melta range.

Despite being among the fastest units in your army, Drones and Haulers only move 10" and getting an extra +3" or +4" is a really solid boost you can use to peak around ruins and close the gap on detection ranges. Every extra inch of movement in Death Guard counts. This is less interesting on Helbrutes, if only because their shooting is just not great.

Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Enhancements

Both of these go on vehicles, which is cool.

  • Parasitic Woe-Reaper (15 pts, Upgrade). CONTAGION ENGINE unit only. When this unit has fought, one model in the unit heals D3 wounds. This is a neat little bonus for your Fleshmowers, who are the most likely unit to end up with this upgrade. Healing tends to be better the tougher a unit is, and while T9 10 Wounds, 3+/5++ isn’t crazy, it’s also still solid enough to be annoying. It can also be fun on Blight-Haulers, whose bite attacks benefit from the +1 to hit/wound against MONSTER and VEHICLE targets.
  • Lancet of the Worldsore (15 pts, Upgrade). Goes on a Helbrute or Blight-Hauler. That unit gains MOBILE. This goes a long way to making Helbrutes playable, since being able to burst through a wall Kool-aid man style can help you stay hidden until it’s time to really do some damage. One of the better things about Helbrutes is their small model size - they’re just small enough to still hide. This can also go on a unit of two Blight-Haulers to get more out of the little engines, and combines well with the ASSAULT ability.

Stratagems

  • Fresh Vectors (1 CP). Used in your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when you pick a Contagion Engine to attack. It can re-roll wound rolls of 1 for the rest of the phase. This is neat, it’s a small but helpful effect for a nice buff when you need it. It's another ability that will do more work if you take units of two Blight-Haulers, since you get double the value out of the Stratagem.
  • Bloodrust Deluge (1 CP). Used in your Shooting phase, when a Contagion Engine shoots. You can pick a visible enemy unit and that unit is Afflicted until your unit has attacked. This one is essentially made with the understanding that you’re taking Rattlejoint Ague in your games, and it’s great for that. Dropping -1 T and -1 Sv on a target from a longe distance can be huge.
  • Soulrot Flux (1 CP). Used in your opponent’s movement phase when they make a Fall back move from an engaged Contagion Engine unit. Roll a D6, and on a 1, they take 1 mortal wound, on a 2-5 they take D3, and on a 6, they take 3 mortal wounds. This is situational but really useful for those times you charge a vehicle and it stays alive on 1-2 wounds.
Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Playing This Detachment

This Detachment incentivizes you to play with larger units of Blight-Haulers, pairing them up to get more out of the Lancet of the Worldsore upgrade and the Fresh Vectors Stratagem.

Your biggest problem with this Detachment is that it doesn't pair with anything good; in an ideal world you'd be able to pair it with Virulent Vectorium (already 3 DP) or Hammer of Mortarion (ENGINES tag), to build something that has real cross-unit synergy. So your real options are basically either Shamblerot Vectorium for a Recon strategy that goes wide with Poxwalkers to flood the board or pairing with something like Champions of Contagion to supplement a plague marine-heavy strategy.

The extra movement here on drones and MBHs makes them great, mobile units for scooting around the table, but the sauce here is Bloodrust Deluge - tagging an enemy unit from 36" away before you light it up with a Defiler is a solid value, though you can also get that out of a unit of Plague Marines as well. The thing about this Detachment is that it boosts units most lists were already taking - HBL Bloat Drones were already one of the game's better units, and the Blight-Haulers are likewise just incredibly efficient for their cost. Which is to say that these lists look just like Champions of Contagion or Shamblerot lists, only with stronger small vehicles.

Strengths

  • Great movement buffs for some of the army's best units
  • Decent Stratagems for offensive output and healing

Weaknesses

  • Doesn't pair with Hammer of Mortarion and so no natural pairing options

A Sample List

This list was run in mid July by Jensen Simmons, who finished 5-1 with it at the Hee Yaw event in mid July. It predates the late July balance update, and so will need to be reconfigured to accommodate the increased points cost on the Defiler, which probably ends up costing you one of the two Tallymen in terms of cuts you'll make.

As we mentioned earlier - this is just a regular Death Guard list, but with faster Contagion Engines. And that's more or less how it goes with this Detachment. You have more speed than before on your drones, and this packs a double unit of MBH instead of two single-model units to get more out of the Stratagems, but otherwise not much has changed.

Final Thoughts

If you're running Death Guard and you're not taking Vectorium, this is a solid option. That said, Paragons of Putrescence has been crowding it out recently - you just get more value out of the Terminator upgrades there than you do out of ASSAULT on your contagion engines here. But both detachments share a common factor, which is that they're improving units you just take in every list.
