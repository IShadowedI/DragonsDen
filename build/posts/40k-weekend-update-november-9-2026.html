[40k] Weekend Update: November 9, 2026

November 9th, 2026 via Goonhammer

In our Weekend Update series, we cover the big events and stories that are happening each week in wargames, so you can stay updated and informed every week. 

After a very light week for singles events it was back to business as usual over the weekend, with sixteen GT-sized events hitting our sample and a number of large Teams events. We’re now in the final month leading into the World Championships of Warhammer, and last week’s FAQs update crystallized what the meta will look like for 40k, Age of Sigmar, and Kill team heading into this year’s final event.

Alex Spathopoulos Wins the California Cup 2026

Green Banner Event Co.’s California Cup has become one of the most prestigious events on the West Coast over the last few years, and this year’s three-day event was the largest of the weekend, attracting 149 players competing over eight rounds. Alex Spathopoulos won the event with an 8-0 record piloting a Space Wolves list running Assault Brethren and Saga of the Great Wolf featuring Logan Grimnar at the head of a block of ten Terminators, a Wolf Priest and Ancient leading Bladeguard Veterans, and a unit of ten Furious Assault Wulfen. Alex bested fellow Xenos Petting Zoo team member Mike Helm (Tyranids) in the finals to win the event for a second time after winning in 2024.

Robin “RtheKnight” Lechevalier Wins OGRE 2026

The weekend’s second largest event was the French OGRE 2026 event, a two-day held in Saverne, France. Robin “RtheKnight” Lechevalier won the event running an Orks list on Purge the Foe featuring the Wreckas, Shoota Boyz, and Bully Boyz Detachments, besting Aurellen Auguste’s Adeptus

Robin (Lamar) Philippot and Jean-Loup GENIN also finished the event with undefeated records, each running different flavors of Adepta Sororitas.

Kalle Abrahamsson Wins Shuffle Wars IV

Kalle Abrahamsson won the SS& & MM Shuffle Wars IV: Makari’s hope event in Stockholm, Sweden over the weekend, winning the 79-player, two-day event with an undefeated Necrons list running Purge the Foe. Abrahamsson beat Patric Wreeth’s Space Marines in the finals to take the top spot. Axel Rydén and Martin Hultgren also finished with undefeated records after a fifth round tie, running Drukhari and Orks, respectively.

Crack Blow Gaming Win Kent Teams November 2026 Event

On the teams side of things, the Kent Teams November event was the largest teams event of the weekend, featuring 151 players on 5-man teams. Team Crack Blow Gaming won the event, going 4-0-1 and narrowly edging out X-Force after drawing against them 52-48 in the third round of the event. Congratulations to:

  • Ashley Brown (Space Marines)
  • Jonty Heilbrunn (Orks)
  • Josie Cartwright (World Eaters)
  • Paul Bridge (Leagues of Votann)
  • Toby Bennett (Chaos Space Marines)

Dumpster Fire Win Lehigh Valley Teams Tournament

The second largest teams event of the weekend was a four-round event featuring 8-man teams and four rounds presented by Green Dice Games. Congratulations to Team Dumpster Fire for winning the event:

  • Austin Warfel (World Eaters)
  • Dan Meloro (Space Marines)
  • Ian Power (Orks)
  • Jeffrey Kolodner (Adepta Sororitas)
  • Joshua Campbell (Death Guard)
  • Max Berlove (Necrons)
  • Quentin Chapman (Leagues of Votann)
  • TJ Lanigan (Chaos Space Marines)

The TOs: Snake in the Grass Win SASO

The TOs: Snake in the Grass won the San Antonio Shootout, another four-round, 5-man teams event held in San Antonio over the weekend. Congratulations to:

  • Ben Conner (Orks)
  • Benjamin Wood (Chaos Knights)
  • Erik Brody (Emperor’s Children)
  • Nicholas Williams (Leagues of Votann)
  • Peyton Beard (World Eaters)

Space Marines and Orks Dominate the Weekend, but Sisters Surprise

It was a Space Marine weekend, to no one’s surprise - between Dark Angels, Space Wolves, and standard marines, the faction took home 8 event wins and 33% of TiWP slots, with ten undefeated players in the mix. And those Dark Angels lists weren’t just a bunch of players desperately clinging to busted Deathwatch interactions, either - the majority were running Assault Brethren, Darkflight Pursuit, and Ironstorm Spearhead, showcasing how powerful Ironstorm lists are in the new book. The Space Wolves also continued to show off this past weekend as well, with lists running Assault Brethren and either Saga of the Great Wolf or Beastslayer + Stormlance Task Force.

The Orks were also out in force over the weekend with 5 TiWP players and two event wins, running a wide variety of lists. But the big surprise was the Adepta Sororitas, who snagged two event wins and a surprising 7 TiWP slots over the weekend. There appear to be two viable builds for Sisters right now: Hallowed Martyrs lists running Vahl and a host of Immolators and Castigators, and Sacred Champions+Penitent Host lists running the Triumph of St. Katherine and a host of sisters on foot.

We saw some interesting movement on win rates over the weekend, with Thousand Sons moving above 40%. Though over the past four weeks the losers in the current meta are clear: The Thousand Sons sit at a 39% true win rate, behind Imperial Agents (41%), Astra Militarum (41%), Chaos Space Marines (42%), and Chaos Knights (42%) in the bottom five. Of these, CSM appear to have some play with specific Detachments and in teams environments, while the others see considerably less play.

Games Workshop Releases 40k FAQ for the World Championships

Games Workshop launched its new events site last week and with it, a set of promised FAQs for the upcoming World Championships of Warhammer. Among these was the hotly anticipated FAQ for Warhammer 40k, which had a number of rulings and errata designed to hold players over until the December balance update. If you missed our coverage of the document, you can find it here. As an update, it's a bit light, but does cover some key balance issues, such as Deathwatch Terminators in Dark Angels lists and clarifying how rules about passing off mortal wounds and splitting units work.

Player Focus: Alex Spathopoulos

The player of the day this week is Alex "Spatho" Spathopoulos, a member of team Xenos Petting Zoo (XPZ) and the repeat winner of this weekend's 2026 California Cup - see above for details. Alex currently ranks 33rd in the WGR (formerly the ITC) this year, having had a strong year which included wins in January at the Palm Springs GW US Open, San Diego Classic GT in May, and the Riverside Classic in July. He's currently ranked 51st in Elo and is absolutely capable of making a strong showing at this year's World Championships - and has an outside chance of winning the WGR title. While those are both unlikely outcomes this year, it's worth keeping an eye on Spathopoulos next year as he continues to emerge as one of the game's top players.

Alongside our coverage of the what's-what of 40k each week, Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson joins us to provide some quick updates on the movers and shakers in the competitive scene of some of the other games we cover here at Tabletop Battles!

Sylvaneth Continue to Dominate the Mortal Realms

There is unrest in the forest in the Mortal Realms this weekend, as Sylvaneth continues to take top podium spots and cement itself as one of the strongest factions in the current metagame.

While the two largest singles events over the weekend, the PNW Masters held in Washington and Grudgefest 2026 held in Canada, were both fairly diverse in what armies snagged x-1 finishes, both of the undefeated winners of these events were running a very similar Sylvaneth list. This list leans on the biggest heroes of the faction, Alariealle, Belthanos, Drycha, a Warsong Revenant, and the Spirit of Druthu, to do the heaviest lifting for the list and provide damage, healing, and hit fixing. The lists vary only slightly in their composition, with Frederick Schmidt’s winning Sylvaneth list at PNW leaning into a reinforced unit of The Twistweald while Cole Winsor’s winning Grudgefest list instead opting to split the Twistweald into a minimum sized unit to add in a unit of Gossamid archers.

Congratulations are in order of course to Cole and Frederick for going undefeated in both of these events, as that does take plenty of player skill no matter the army choice. But with this weekend’s double win and a strong performance over the previous weeks, I wouldn’t be surprised to see even more Sylvaneth players at the top levels of the game.

Kill Team, Underworlds, and AoS Balance Updates for the WCW

The World Championships of Warhammer is just under a month away, and as promised Games Workshop released a big set of Balance updates for AoS, Underworlds, and Kill Team alongside one for 40k in preparation for Warhammer’s marquee event.

Like the 40k balance update, technically all three of these updates aren’t yet official, and instead are early versions of these updates that have been shared early in order to allow for WCW attendees to get practice prior to the main event. In previous years, this update would be shared secretly with WCW players and, like clockwork, would be nearly immediately leaked onto the wider internet. This year, GW has finally become wise to the fact that it’s nearly impossible to expect such a large group of people to not share something simply on the basis of “please be cool”, and opted to instead quietly make all three of these updates publicly available on the Warhammer Community events page.

Even while unofficial for now, all three of these balance updates include some pretty important changes to balance out their respective game prior to the WCW. For Kill Team, some of the most notable changes include a change to the Dominate Tac Op to make it less punishing for 12 wound teams, improved dice fixing capabilities for Deathwatch and Yaegirs, a nerf to Celestian’s just a scratch equipment, and a major buff to Murderwing’s Boost rule. The balance updates for AoS are less sweeping, but include some important changes to things such as adjusting the targets for Ossiarch Bonereaper abilities, a clarification on the transport abilities for the Khazalid Holds if they bring in Kharadron Skyvessels, and a points increase for Gotrek. Finally, the Underworlds update adds the Scourge tag to Realmstone Raiders and Wrack and Run cards, while updating the Forsaken and Restricted card list.

Warmachine’s Newest Army Wins the Iron Gauntlet

Warmachine hosted the North American leg of its official gaming convention, Lock and Load, over this past weekend in St. Louis, which included the capstone event of its competitive season, the Iron Gauntlet. Much like the World Championships of Warhammer, a spot at this event can only be won by winning a qualifying event, and sees the best of the best in North America battling it out for the chance to win the top trophy for the 2026 competitive season.

This year's podium winner made a particular splash (no pun intended) with their army choice. Dan “Golden Lion” Riker took the North American Iron Gauntlet top prize with Dread Armada, Warmachine’s newest action made available via 3D printing, most notably by leaning hard on Terminus’s tankiness and resistance to enemy control type abilities. Dread Armada’s rules became available in mid October, while their army was only made printable at the very end of October, making it an incredibly tight turnaround to bring Dread Armada at all. That being said, multiple players in the Iron Gauntlet finals brought Dread Armada to great effect, despite being hot off the presses.

Not to be totally outdone, Fane of Nyrro took both second and third place at the event, continuing Fane’s reign of vampiric terror on the larger metagame of Warmachine by leaning on the strength of the faction’s incredible Warlocks and powerful hunger abilities to win games.

Congratulations to Dan for winning the Iron Gauntlet with his undead irate pirates, and to the Warmachine team for the conclusion of a very exciting year for the Iron Gauntlet.

Warhammer World Hosts Throne of Skulls for Warhammer: The Old World

Things were old school in the old world over in Nottingham this weekend, as Warhammer World hosted a major Throne of Skulls organized play event for The Old World. 

This 44 player event was hosted over 5 rounds, and saw a pretty wide variety of armies duke it out to claim first prize. While it seems like the final placings on BCP remain hidden at time of writing, oldworldrankings.com gives us a better look at who was able to take top spot. It appears that when the dust settled, two players ended the event with 4-0-1 records, Marcus Robinson on Grand Cathay and Will Hodgson on Beastmen. Both of these players played to a draw in the final round against each other, and once tiebreaker points were factored in Marcus ended up taking the top spot for the event.

While both of these armies could not be more different to each other in the lore, Grand Cathay’s been one of the strongest factions in the current Old World Metagame, able to lean on its excellent suite of tools and characters to support a strong combined arms force. Congratulations to Marcus for leading Cathay to glorious victory in Nottingham.

Russia Holds Malifaux National Grand Tournament

Our last highlight for this week is over in Russia, who just wrapped up their National Tournament for Malifaux this past weekend. 

This rated tournament event is one of the largest of the year for Malifaux, bringing in 42 players from across the country to battle out between 5 rounds with their selected Crew. Going over the top podium spots shows a pretty healthy spread of crew selection choices, including Neverborn and Ressurectionists winning second and third place with 4-1 records. The only undefeated player and overall winner of the event was Danila Kanin, who selected the Ten Thunders as their crew of choice, leaning on the Thunders penchant for shadowy subterfuge to claim victory at all five rounds of the event. Congratulations to Danila for the win!
