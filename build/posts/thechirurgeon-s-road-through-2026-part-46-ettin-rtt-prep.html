TheChirurgeon's Road Through 2026, Part 46: Ettin RTT Prep

November 6th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive play progress through 2026. Last week I was plugging along with more hobby progress on my Red Corsairs, and slowly coming to terms with the idea that I'm going to have to run them at Worlds. This time around I'm mostly doing hobby work and running HeroQuest with the kids, plus I'm prepping for an RTT this weekend.

Hobby Progress

I'm still working on the Red Corsairs. Right now the goal is to add Valthex and Mutilators by the Grand Narrative, plus finish the Chosen if I can get that far. I'm making good time on the Mutilators, which despite being complex models are fun to paint and may be done by Saturday. I finished the first one last night:

I'm really happy with how this guy turned out - I think he looks great and I'm a big fan of how saturated the new Tone Pro reds look, though it does make him look markedly brighter than my existing Red Corsairs - I'm going to have to match the next batch of Terminators to the existing ones. I've started working on the other two, and I'm hoping to finish them faster now that I have a good idea of how I want the scheme on these to go.

The drills from the Assault Centurions just ended up being perfect on these and I'm so happy with the results. It was absolutely worth tracking those down and ordering a set so I could use the bits.

Prep for the Ettin RTT

I haven't been playing nearly enough games, and I'm getting rusty. I need to get some games in before the Grand Narrative, and as luck would have it, there's a 3-round RTT coming up this weekend at a local game shop. I went ahead and signed up for it, and my plan is to take the Red Corsairs, get in three relatively easy games, probably go 2-1, and win best painted. It's more about a refresher than anything else and I'm not planning to bring anything overly competitive at this point. In fact, I'm working off what I already have painted, and here's the general notion I'm working with:

It's not a great list, and it's based entirely on what I already have painted and can throw on the table. It's based on what I previously had, which was heavy on bodies and quite a few of my units got cheaper. I desperately need those mutilators and at least five more terminators and another Terminator Sorcerer to make this work, and that's not all getting painted by Saturday. Could be doable by the Grand Narrative, however. I need to work on this and get it all ready soon, and plan on using Thanksgiving break as my key painting time for the World Championships.

The weird piece here is the Master of Executions running Touched by the Warp - he gives Huron's unit PSYCHIC, and that's basically just to make their ranged attacks better. That's not very good, but it might be enough for some fun turnarounds here. The other value of running Veterans of the Long War here is getting full re-rolls to hit and having two very good reactive moves, so I can at least pull off some bullshit when I need to.

My general plan is to try and stall the board early and control the middle with that unit of Fabius + Chosen + the Masters, which is conveniently a PSYKER unit with T6 and the ability to shrug off some damage. We'll see how it pans out.

Final Thoughts

That does it for this week - I'm all in now on painting as prep for this weekend and while I don't expect to win three games, it'll be good practice and I'll at least have a good shot at best painted with this particular army. They look really good, and I'm happy with how the reds are turning out. I need to do a bit more work on the Legionaries and get them to a level I'm happy with but right now there's a lot to like.

See you next Thursday.
