40K Weekend Update: Battle for Salvation and Teams Results, Sam Pope Player Focus

October 12th, 2026 via Goonhammer
If you tuned in last week to see our Weekend Update article, welcome back. If you are just joining us, thanks for stopping by. This new series will cover the big events and stories that are happening each week in wargames, so you can stay updated and informed every week. The 40k side will be mostly covered by me, Isaac Terada, while Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson covers the non-40k side of event coverage and news.

John Lennon Wins Battle for Salvation

BFS was the largest event of the weekend with 131 players and 8 rounds, followed by Denver 40K Fight Club Oct. Open ‘26 with 104 players and 6 rounds. Events quickly drop off there in player size making this a pretty slow weekend for singles play. Both of the two large events were won by Space Marines; the first was taken by John with his combined Ultramarines and Deathwatch roster. Abusing the powerful melee threats in Assault Brethren combined with Calgar and the Deathwatch Terminators using their specialized Detachments, he was able to gun down the competition for a 7-1 finish. Notably, if you look at the event he was manually made #1. The head judge said that the X-1 and X-0 players going into day three agreed that whoever played on the top table round 8 would have spots 1 & 2 guaranteed to them even if they had a lower record. This was to make sure that even though there would be a single X-0 player after round 7 that anyone with a X-1 record could have a shot. That meant that while Sam Pope’s World Eaters went into round 7 as the only X-0 player, anyone who beat him would be able to take first, which John Lennon handily did, only losing to the Marines mirror versus Jack Murphy earlier in the event. 

Teams Back for a Big Weekend

Teams had a slow weekend last time we covered them, but this weekend they came back in a big way. Five teams events broke five rounds and over 100 players. The two largest were both six man teams, with an eight and two five-man roster events rounding up the top five. The rising number of events that aren’t just 8s and 5s is very interesting to see, and I am excited to see if 6s might become a new standard for smaller teams events outside of just European-run gigs. GT MADRID LETANIA DEL OSO was the largest event with 228 players split into six-man teams; congrats to Wargames Adictos for taking it down. Congratulations to Gonzalo "Raven" de La Moneda, Joker, Manu, Marco Esteban, Miguel Durá, & Terroxer El Rojo for the win with a 4-0-1 record. 

Deathwatch Terminators by Craig "MasterSlowPoke" Sniffen

First Codex Space Marines Results

The slate has finally taken over 40k events, no more stragglers with busted Orks. Now instead Space Marines have jumped into the lead. Funnily enough, Deathwatch has had a large influx of players, with 16 of them accounting for a 67% win rate. Does this have anything to do with any Marine army having access to 400 points of the best infantry shooting in the game if they bring Deathwatch terminator? Who can say. Orks still have a respectable 54% winrate, but no event wins, maybe they finally got hit enough to keep them out of top tables. 54% is still pretty high for a faction to hit this hard with the nerf bat so I would keep my eye on them. 

Most of the other event winners were spread across a decent selection of factions, but one to watch is World Eaters. They had a just-okay 51% winrate but took 8 players into a TiWP, which led to three undefeated finishes and a winner. They had the highest percentage of TiWP factions this weekend; those buffs on the last slate I think were just enough to push them into viability. If you want to learn how to deal with this new threat, look to Know Thy Foe: World Eaters, which we just published yesterday for our Warhound readers. 

I am excited to see where the meta goes, with a healthy side eye at Space Marine results. 

New Custodes First Thoughts

Unlike Marines and Orks which had a pretty good consensus on what was going to dominate, Custodes is still looking for a solid path. There are a lot of good datasheets and Detachments, but nothing as crazy as Dakka Rigs or Primarchs to really give theorycrafting a big push in a certain direction. Most top-level players see Custodes as a high B tier or low A tier book, with nothing that is necessarily broken to push it to the top of the meta, but with a lot of exploration to build and play with. Both of these are very good things especially after spending so much time living in either triple Land Raider or 18 Allarus land. We all need a break from Custodes dominating the field. 

The highlights from the book include Trajan, who is the most durable infantry character in the game and possibly the hardest hitting. The Dreadnaughts also look incredible with some great flamer access on incredibly hard hitting melee. The best Detachments seem to focus on increased mobility, with Solar Watch the big winner; turns out giving every unit access to +2 move is pretty good! We've given our initial thoughts on the Codex already, and if you want to dive more into Custodes, look for our beginner guide and Know Thy Foe focusing on them around the corner in just a few weeks. 

Sisters of Silence: Prosecutors - Credit: Pendulin

Player Focus: Sam Pope

If you haven’t been following teams 40k you probably have missed Sam Pope. He isn’t crushing the singles circuit, but he is a monster 40k player. He was part of the winning roster attending WTC in August with Team USA and is a Tyranid faction specialist. That doesn't mean he is stuck in a 'Nids box, however. He brought Chaos Space Marines to WTC for the winning run, and took 70 Khorne Berzerkers to a second place finish at BFS, only losing in the end to John Lennon. While he is not going to end up in ITC contention, landing currently at #759 on the ICT leaderboard and #65 in ELO, he is a force to be reckoned with and someone I am watching going back into teams season. (Sam Pope has his own YouTube channel called “Sam-Pope-40k;” it's very good, go watch it.)

Alongside our coverage of the what's-what of 40k each week, Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson joins us to provide some quick updates on the movers and shakers in the competitive scene of some of the other games we cover here at Tabletop Battles!

Khazalite Holds Announced for Preorder

The biggest reveal from the Big Autumn Preview from Games Workshop came in the form of Order’s tiniest defenders, the Khazalite Holds. Unlike their Khardoran cousins, whose steampunk airships are a far cry from what we normally expect out of Dwarves, the warriors of the Khazalite Hold are much more straightforward representations of fantasy Dwarf Archetypes. That’s not to say there isn’t some Age of Sigmar high fantasy flavor, of course: They do have a land boat with a big ol’ throne on it to sail you right towards the nearest bozo in need of clobbering.

With the preorder date set for this upcoming Saturday, we can expect to have our first look at the new army rules and unit profiles over the course of the week on the Warhammer Community website.

A Quieter Weekend for AoS and Kill Team 

After back to back gigantic events with the LGT and LVO, this week is much reduced in tournament activity for Kill Team and Age of Sigmar. That's not to say that there isn’t anything happening though, particularly in the world of Sigmar, where a fairly large team tournament was hosted in Spain. The event, LETANIA DEL OSO II AOS, hosted twenty teams of five battling it out over the course of five rounds. The winner of the event was team Requiem, finishing 4-1 with the highest overall score.

Things will start to pick up again for both game systems later this month, with a few larger 5 round events popping up at the end of October for Kill Team and another huge teams event next weekend for Age of Sigmar, as the format continues to rapidly increase in popularity.

Credit: Steamforged Games

Australia Wins the Warmachine World Team Championship

The biggest event in Warmachine, the World Team Championship (WTC), was held this past weekend in Poland, bringing 240 players from around the world a chance to compete for the chance to bring home the coveted WTC trophy for their home country. The event hosts any country able and willing to send players to compete in teams of at least five, and has changed venues from year to year to celebrate the diverse player base of Warmachine. This year, the winning team was team Charging Cassowaries from Australia, whose team makeup was notable for lacking any player piloting Fane of Nyrro, the current boogeyman of the Warmachine metagame. Second place was won by England’s Team Lancelot, and third was won by USA’s Team Death Valley.

Alongside these winners, the WTC announced the host for next year’s event is Dusseldorf, Germany, and the bidding process will soon open for the 2028 host country.

StarCraft TMG Reveals Wave 3 Alongside Release Roadmap

Archon Studios has been experiencing massive success with StarCraft: The Miniatures Game, and as Wave 2 is starting to hit store shelves and player hands Archon decided to pull the curtain back on what the next wave of the game looks like. 

Alongside a major patch to improve the balance of the races, Archon released the rules cards for the third wave expansion for SC:TMG. Each faction picks up a new hero and support style unit with this release, with the Ghost Nova and the flying Raven joining the Terran, the High Templars and Tassadar reinforcing the Zerg, and Zagara and the Defiler joining the Zerg roster. Preorders for Wave 3 open on October 12th, with an expected street date of early November. 

Alongside these releases, Archon gave us an updated roadmap look for what we can expect in late 2026 and in 2027, with a few very interesting tidbits to see. The most notable of these is the announcement of a new 2 player limited edition starter pack releasing this December, pitting the Zerg led by Infested Stukhov and Infested Terran forces against Alarak and his Tal’Darim Protoss. The release will also include bits to add on to the Terran Siege Tank to run as an Infested Siege Tank, so the Terran can no longer hog all of the fun.

Halo’s Next Expansion is Bringing the Covenant Proper to the Tabletop

It’s a pretty dreadful time to be a Halo fan right now -- unless you’re a fan of the Halo Tabletop Game from Mantic, in which case it is a very exciting time. 

On Friday, Mantic announced its next release of Halo: Judgement, which brings the original bad guys of Halo, the Covenant, properly on to the Tabletop for the first time. The expansion box includes the Prophet of Regret, one of the leaders of the fanatical Covenant cult hellbent on cleansing the universe, alongside  Elite Honor Guard and Rangers and the hulking Hunters. Joining the fight against the Covenant is one of the most iconic characters of the franchise, Sergeant Johnson, one of Master Chief's closest companions from the original trilogy of games and a fan favorite.

Alongside the Judgement expansion, Mantic is releasing the Halo Campaign Evolved board game, designed to be played by 1-2 players and focused on recreating some of the most iconic events from the Halo franchise, such as the escape from the Pillar of Autumn from the very first game. The game comes with standee miniatures for a wide array of covenant forces as well as a plastic Elite Zealot miniature, and a miniature for Master Chief himself to boot. You can’t expect to talk about the biggest moments from Halo without the Master Chief!

Wizards Reveals Next Marvel Crossover Set at New York Comic Con

New York Comic Con, one of the biggest conventions for general geek and pop culture in the world, was held this past weekend, and Wizards of the Coast brought some exciting reveals along to showcase to attendees. One of the most significant of these reveals was the announcement of the next Universes Beyond crossover set, Marvel: Darkhold Destiny. Unlike previous Universes Beyond sets, which focused on homage over storytelling, Darkhold is treading entirely new ground for Universes Beyond by telling its own original story with this set release. With this storytelling focus in mind, Marvel is releasing a tie-in comic introducing a brand new character from Darkhold to go along with the set, which will release digitally before the wider set release in April 2027, written by Jackson Lanzing and Collin Kelly. 

While it’s unlikely that every Universes Beyond set will be set up like this, it is very exciting to know that Wizards wants to push beyond just using these sets as fan service and are instead trying to push the boundary of collaboration by telling new stories with the unique storytelling stylings that so many players love about Magic.
