[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Green Tidings pt.2

July 17th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Editor's Note: Hello folks, this is Wings speaking, on behalf of the Tabletop Battles editorial team.

You will note below that one of the events we were due to cover today, the PATH TO GLORY - OPEN 2026 has been removed from the schedule, as currently there are no lists visible in BCP.

Bluntly - we saw the data for this event while doing our processing run at the start of the week, and the lists were there at that point, except for some lists belonging to players on the French WTC team. Earlier in the week the WTC put up a statement condemning the practice of hiding lists in public events (after the controversy of some players at an Austrian event doing it last week), and at some point after that, all lists have vanished from this event.

We overwhelmingly strive for this column to be purely positive, and normally avoid any callouts, but just this once we have to say it - this sucks.

There were nearly 200 players at this event, including four undefeated players who (originally) did not have hidden lists, and nearly 30 players who would have featured in the Best of the Rest. It is not fair on these other players for them to miss out on getting their accolades in order to, it would appear, help a small fraction of participants keep secrets.

We strongly implore the organisers of this event to un-hide the lists so we can cover the event (which we will, if that happens), and if that means a few people get some light mockery for doing toy soldier OpSec, then they should take that mockery like the champs they want to be.

Thoughts

A showdown that pits an established meta force (Librarius toting Marine Terminators) against one of the maligned forces in the meta (Astra Militarum). The specifics of the mission are high scoring here- both Priority and Recon can wrack up a fantastic score irrespective of the military engagement, and both lists have adequate scoring pieces to tick these off as they grapple.

The secret tech, and possible game decider here is the Siege Regiment package - rarely seen in 10th, it boasts two potent abilities- strip cover for a number of units outside of 12 inches, and the potential to make the foe -2 to move and charge. Terminators don't love either of these things, and the Guard list has solid Las Cannon Weapon Team and Scion shooting to take full advantage as they limp across the board. In the end, the surprise factor of the guard seems to have got it narrowly over the line, bravo!
