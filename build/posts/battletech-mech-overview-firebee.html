BattleTech Mech Overview: Firebee

June 3rd, 2026 via Goonhammer
Explosions and lancing lasers lit the distant night, their blitzing flashes casting sprawling shadows of a brawl that was rapidly getting out of hand as the storm above intensified. The blowing snow and billowing winds crushed the lights of the city and the auroras high above alongside any hope any sensors would ever have of gaining a lock against a small force coming in at breakneck from the wings. 

It was exactly what we were looking for.

“Marshals.” Marshal Kagan rumbled, the man’s Ti Ts’ang 9H turning to peer over our Lance for a moment before continuing. “Romer and his men are down there getting their asses kicked on our behalf. It’s time to get at it and put these Pirate punks down.”

“Aye Command Marshal!” I barked, my sentiment mirrored in the Lance’s roared shouts as the chorusing voices of our attached Tinstar escorts echoing us as we banked in and made a run at the Pirate’s back. 

“That’s what I like to hear, Marshals.” I could hear the smirk in the man’s voice as he led us in before calling ahead to summon the waiting tanks just inside the city’s snow-walls. “Captain Paulson, get your Lances on the move. We’re gonna need them.”

A pair of clicks answered him over the comm, the gesture followed shortly by a brilliant bolt of fire that lit the night from the edge of Kite’s limits. The fire fled from the city, its light chasing an arc across the plains over the backs of Romer and his men before coming down fast and hard on the silhouette of a teetering BattleAxe, bathing the machine and lighting the flurrying night in flickering explosions. 

Smokemaker was sluggish beneath me, the added weight of the attached Battle Armor forcing us to slow our approach. The old Firebee 3E was an early frame, to say the least, minted off the lines by Hellespont in the first run that had followed the original prototyping stage. The radio had never really worked right, the Plasma Rifle pulled a little to the left, and the MML feeds had to be knocked around when they switched from long range to short range packs.

But she was mine.

The static whisper of her ECM being fed back into the system filled my ears as it worked to keep our approach under wraps, shrouding our Lance in a pall of interference that meant the Pirates ahead of us would never know what hit them. With every step we closed in closer and closer, watching as Romer and Calen did what they could to guard the smoking heap of Rodney’s fallen Ymir. 

“Holman, shed and engage.” Kagan growled, waiting a moment for his attached squad of Battle armor to leap free of his ‘Mechs vast torso plates before letting the machine’s array of lasers burst to life. His ‘Mech rocketed forward, its triple strength myomer springing to life and sending a streak of steam chasing after him as he rocketed forward. He banked, picking a target and raising his hatchet high over his head before slamming it down into the cockpit of an unaware Bandersnatch, making the ‘Mech go rigid before it fell limp to the floor as he ripped the weapon free.

Time to get busy. 

— Marshal Abilene Langley, Fronc Reaches, Colonial Marshal Firebee FRB-3E Pilot. Rockwellawan, Fronc Reaches, April 4th 3131.  

—

Firebee. FRB-3E Variant. Credit: porble

Howdy Mechwarriors and welcome back to this week’s Mech Overview! This week we’ve got a quartet of… real fuckin’ weird Capellan ‘Mechs lumbering into the bay for our observation. That said I do love one of them and tolerate some of the others:

It’s the Waterwasp!

No, wait… Firebee, that’s right!

A 35 ton light ‘Mech of strange utility, incredibly low costs, and generally questionable design decisions! From a strange reliance on SRM-2s to a general lack of concern for the concept of survivability in some manners it sure is just… doin’ stuff out here.

Chassis

A 35 ton light ‘mech the Firebee is in good company with its many brothers in tonnage. Though things like Panthers, Venoms, Stilettos, Hollanders, Rokurokubis, and Wolfhounds, to name a few, are a strong competition the Firebee does its best with the tools its been given to stand tall among the competition and play its part.

From the primitive to the mid-end modern the Firebee is sure out here doin’ stuff and with that there is a lot of variance. The overall only consistency is that they largely go 5/8 and use some form of standard engine: primitive, standard, or fuel cell. Usually well armored and acceptably sunk they usually also have some usable firing patterns to get work out of.

Oh, and they’re cheap.

Variants

These mechs have all been reviewed based on a standard F through S scale, which you can find described on our landing page here (along with all of our other ‘mech reviews, the name of the box you can buy to get any of the mechs we have covered, and our general methodology).

FRB-1E (WAM-B)

I’m a primitive. I’m a Caveman. I’m a menace to polite society. I’ve been made aware of fire and am quite scared of it.

The 1E is a primitive, clad in primitive armor, with a primitive cockpit, powered by a primitive engine. This is heavy. As such it does not carry much, an LRM-5 with one ton of ammo in the right arm, and a trio of SRM-2s, one in each side torso and one in the left arm, with 2 tons of ammo in the left torso. It carries 10 single heat sinks so if it wants to it, for whatever reason, can just fire everything all the time with impunity. That’s cool, I guess. At a 5/8 movement profile and 58% armor it’s not the most survivable 35 tonner that’s ever existed but at a paltry 514 BV it could be worse.

I guess.

Liberty’s Rating: D. I mean when you’re that cheap and can move a reasonable enough speed you could certainly be worse… No, that comes later.

FRB-2E

Damn, that’s wild, we got light and shit now?

The 2E takes a 1E, makes all the primitive things not that and then uses the weight savings to buy an extra SRM 2, now in the CT, a Large Laser, and a fair bit more armor, now bringing the total up to 81%, and 5 jump jets.

For 808 BV this could be worse, its got alright movement, pretty good armor, a fair amount of padding around the ammo in the side torso, and a heat profile that gives it the capacity to rip off the LL or all the SRMs on a run and be heat neutral! With two tons of ammo it makes for an alright Inferno caddy where you’re likely to get something out of a salvo, even if it’s only a little bit of heat.

Honestly it’s also not the worst BA hunter for the price, it’s faster than them, has a good chunk hit to try and blow armor off of them, and the SRM salvo to follow it up! Add on top of that that it can come around and just drop an inferno volley on them to blow some troopers away that way and it, again, could certainly be worse.

I… kinda dig it? It’s like a fucked up and weird Panther. Shame it basically never exists.

Liberty: C. I like him, he’s cheap and weird and they should start building him again, especially now that the Capellans kinda need to rebuild that TO&E of theirs after tussling with the IlClan on Terra.

Firebee. Credit: Rockfish

FRB-3E

I am the funniest little Fireman.

The 3E takes a 2E and does funny and weird things with it. Mounting double heat sinks, a plasma rifle with 2 tons of ammo, an MML5 with 2 tons of CASE’d ammo, a Guardian ECM suite, a small cockpit, and an XL Gyro. It does have to ditch the jump jets to make weight but all that for 1,067 BV is a good steal! So why do I feel like I’m missing somethi-

AH! AGAIN! SNUCK UPON! HOW DOES THIS CONTINUE TO HAPPEN!

It’s got Stealth Armor! So if you don’t need that ECM suite for whatever reason you can turn on the Stealth and just run around lobbing plasma rifle shells at something for movement heat and if you do then you fire off the Plasma and the MML while running and be down a good 5 heat!

Honestly pretty good! I personally mount up a ton of SRM and a ton of SRM-I to get goofy with heat on people but beyond that do what you will! As well I am a big fan of the fact that offsetting that Small cockpit is not hard. Making the 3E 4/4 is only 1,174 BV which ain’t too bad!

Liberty’s Rating: C+. Not the best ‘Mech the Force Marshal ever did buy but certainly quite the useful one!

The Industrial ‘Mech in the Room

WI-WAM MilitiaMech

Yea… they made an Industrial ‘Mech version. With a stupid, if not funny, designation.

Clad in normal Industrial Armor, built on Industrial Structure, with nothing in the side torsos, and powered by a Fuel Cell engine this thing screams for death. It packs along a Thunderbolt 10 and a Rocket Launcher 10 as its armament which isn’t great as it only has 3 heat sinks so even just firing off the T-Bolt builds 2 heat, let alone anything else. Add to it the fact that Industrial armor has a BAR rating and the Industrial structure gives a bonus to the mere concept of a crit and you’ve got some real issues at hand to deal with.

You’d think only costing 456 BV would buy it some love but if you wanted a cheap MilitiaMech that you definitely have that does a 10 point hit you could just go get the PPC Buster for 475 BV, or the LB-10 Buster for 713… or the fucking Gauss one for 726.

Go get a different industrial ‘Mech.

Liberty’s Rating: F+.

Final Thoughts

The Firebee sure is a ‘Mech that sure does do things. The 3E is quite good and the 2E is quite usable. I’d like to see some further modernizations on the machine, as I do with most of the Periphery Pack ‘Mechs, but I’m not really sure what I’d want.

All said and done though if you get them then you may as well use them as kick in list filler that can just spot you when you need the hand! Every single one of these, except the MilitiaMech, fall into some category of The Little Bastard Zone and as such they’ve got some amount of value. Just uh… remember that they have a pretty fast path to death, yea?

Until next time! Fair weather, good seas and happy being-just-a-little-guy-ing!
