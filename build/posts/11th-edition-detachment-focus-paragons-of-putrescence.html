11th Edition Detachment Focus: Paragons of Putrescence

October 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer

In our Detachment Focus series we take a deep dive into an army’s Detachments, covering what’s in them, how they play, and how they’ll fit into the broader meta and your games. In this Detachment Focus we’re looking at the Contagion Engines Detachment for the Death Guard.

The Death Guard received three new 1 DP Detachments at the onset of 11th edition, each of which catered to a different unit type in the army. Of these, the Paragons of Putrescence focuses on the army's CHARACTER units, and as a result acts as the Detachment with the widest use among those 1 DP options.

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

Force Disposition: Priority Assets

This is a character-focused Detachment, and that means it can pair with a large number of builds and Detachments, but will primarily favor those running Plague Marines or Terminators. Notably, you can pair this one with Champions of Contagion, and that means a bit more when you have both extra Contagion Range and the ability to customize/do more with your Contagion. This is also the ideal paired Detachment for Shamblerot Vectorium, where you want to get more out of your large units of Poxwalkers with attached characters and Mortarion. Giving you the Priority Assets Disposition is an added bonus there, since it stops you from having to play Disruption.

Detachment Rule: Hypervirulent Strains

Friendly DEATH GUARD CHARACTER units have +3” to their Contagion Range (to a max of 12”).

This is just a great buff to have on all the time, and gives you 6” range in the first battle round - that’s a lot to work with, and makes your job tagging units in those early turns way easier. This also works on Mortarion, giving the big guy an extra menacing aura to work with as he floats around the table. If you combine this with Death Lord’s Chosen you can get some silly range on your mortal wounds ability, and it’s solid when you’re doubling upon on Contagions in Death Lord’s Chosen. It's also fine on Poxwalkers led by characters, though you'll lose the ability to Infiltrate when you attach to those units, so the benefits are somewhat limited in that regard. Having 9" contagion range on round 2 can really make some nasty tricks possible, such as daisy chaining deep striking units of Deathshrouds.

Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Enhancements

  • Rejuvenating Swarm (20 points). Goes on a non-TERMINATOR INFANTRY model only. Attacks targeting this unit with S greater than their T have -1 to wound rolls. This is great for improving the durability of Plague Marines, where being T6 and having -1 to wound from bigger attacks is just a really solid buff on a unit that can be surprisingly fragile. You know where it's even better, though? On a unit of 20 Poxwalkers, where you can make them even more of a pain in the ass to pick up. -1 to wound is even better when it's on T4 models who are more likely to eat 3+ rolls.
  • Host of the Hybridised Pox (40 points). INFANTRY model only. Once per battle, per army, in your Command phase, you can use this ability. If you do, you pick one of the plagues in Nurgle’s Gift and until the end of the battle your unit gets that plague in addition to the one you chose. This is also pretty neat, and a fun way to get -1 to be hit on top of the -1 to saves from Rattlejoint Ague. It’s cute, but probably not worth spending points. That said, you can combine this with Cornucophagus in Champions of Contagion to have all three effects in play at once. For 40 points, this is just a bit too much to spend, and the reality is that the -1 to saves plague is really all you need.

Stratagems

  • Territorial Infection (1 CP). Used at the start of the Command phase on a CHARACTER unit to give that unit +1 OC until the end of the turn. This is a really great way to steal an objective, and particularly useful on Deathshroud Terminators, where a unit going from 4 OC to 8 can make a big difference, but can also be useful for getting Plague Marines up to 3 OC each. It’s a big brain play that can cause a lot of problems if your opponent isn’t ready for it and it can be used in your opponent’s turn. It's also amazing when you can throw it on a blob of 20 Poxwalkers to suddenly hold a crapload of table space and steal both middle objectives.
  • Aggravus Spasms (1 CP). Used at the start of your Shooting phase on a friendly Death Guard CHARACTER unit. Pick one visible enemy unit within Contagion Range of your unit and that unit gets +6” Detection range. This is interesting - it means you can tag a unit within 9” on round 2 and give it 21” Detection Range, but if you’re within 9” you’re already just going to light that unit up and/or charge it. It’s an interesting ability but I’m not sure how much you’ll actually use it. Helpful for those times when Poxwalkers are outkicking your coverage, usually when you're being supported by Defilers and/or a Knight.
  • Simultaneous Contamination (1 CP). Used in your Shooting phase, when a CHARACTER unit starts an Action. The unit can still shoot while it does the action. This is nifty, and handy if your plan is to play Priority Assets or for those rare turns you draw Cleanse or Plunder. Pretty situational otherwise, though. It only lets you shoot, so the value is going to be more around taking extra shots with your Malignant Plaguecasters or units of Plague Marines, or for when Mortarion needs to hang back.
Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

Playing This Detachment

This is just a really versatile Detachment, and your go-to if you're running one of the other 2 DP options, as there's no world where you aren't taking at least a few characters. As we've mentioned, the most competitive way to use this Detachment is alongside Shamblerot Vectorium, where you get access to both Priority Assets - fine for an army running 7+ units of Poxwalkers - and the Rejuvenating Swarm Enhancement, which usually goes on a Noxious Blightbringer attached to a 20-model Poxwalkers unit alongside Typhus to create a nasty blob with extra movement, better healing, and added durability against incoming attacks. This is the current competitive list for Death Guard, and whether you play Priority Assets or Disruption will largely depend on whether you're playing in a Teams event (Teams events favor Disruption as this army does an OK job running it and someone has to jump on that particular grenade).

After that, you can get mileage out of Aggravus Spasms if you're being supported by Defilers or a Knight Despoiler, since it's about debuffing an enemy unit near one of your CHARACTER units - increasing a unit's detection range by 6" is quite a bit, and you can really open up on opponents who otherwise thought they were safe. Simultaneous Contamination is one that has limited utility given Poxwalkers can't shoot, but it's good to have in your back pocket for those times Mortarion wants to do an action and shoot and isn't in charge range of anything.

Strengths

  • Rejuvenating Swarm. This is a crazy good Enhancement and makes big units of Poxwalkers insanely annoying to try and take down.
  • Versatile. Buffing characters with extra contagion range is just always good, and you're always bringing characters.

Weaknesses

  • No real offensive buffs. Extra contagion range is nice but also not necessarily something you need, especially after round 3, and the real buff here is just a defensive buff you pay for on a single unit.

Final Thoughts

If you're running Death Guard competitively, chances are you're taking this one. It pairs wonderfully with Shamblerot Vectorium and the characters you want to take there, and gives you a much better disposition option. The Stratagems here are fine, the abilities solid, and most of your non-vehicle units are either characters or able to have attached characters, making this one that most of your army can use effectively.
