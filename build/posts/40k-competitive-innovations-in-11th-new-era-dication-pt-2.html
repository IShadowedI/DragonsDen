June 28th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Archetype

Custodes Bike Spam

Thoughts

The Praetors have landed! This build has been doing the rounds in early 11th testing and it excels at Purge the Foe - a limited unit count that all punch up exceptionally hard thanks to the lone wolf damage boost of Lions, it can also play a slow game, with Bikes popping out, killing something then returning to safety. An intervention from one of these units can also turn an entire flank, making it exceptionally hard to push into, and there is just enough Ingress threat via the Allarus to pick at the holes in the enemy lines. A real contender and it's AWESOME to see a different variant of Custodes take an event win so new into the edition!!

The Best of the Rest

There were 4 more players on 4-1 records. They were:

  • 2nd - Logan Prager - Adepta Sororitas (Army of Faith): Gunline Army of Faith with lots of Castigators, Vahl and some Mortifier MSU.
  • 3rd - Brad Ledbetter - Blood Angels (Stormlance Task Force): Massed Death Company exploiting the go-fast of Storm Lance and the new Death Company plug in.
  • 4th - Adam Major - Drukhari (Skysplinter Assault): Proper Venom spam Skysplinter using the new Wych detachment to juice up the output in Recon. This has been my go to detachment combo of the new edition, glad to see it works!
  • 5th - Kirk Roettinger - Tyranids (Warrior Bioform Onslaught): Crusher and Warrior Bioform combined with bricks of Warriors out ahead of a Norn and some Tfexes.

Thoughts

Thoughts

The shootiest of the Ad Mech builds takes a GT here, freed of the shackles of scoring outside of just bodying the enemy with Purge the Foe. Cawl and Thulia juice up the army whilst being difficult to kill (they can push objectives in the mid-late game), and units like the Autocannon chickens are far more dangerous in a world of new cover where their AP goes further and they become real all-rounders damage wise. It has plenty of infantry for running the centre and fast gun for the sides-  a strong model for early 11th! Well done on the event win.
