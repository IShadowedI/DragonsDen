40K Weekend Update: The 2026 Las Vegas Open

October 5th, 2026 via Goonhammer
If you tuned in last week to see our Weekend Update article, welcome back. If you are just joining us, thanks for stopping by. This new series will cover the big events and stories that are happening each week in wargames, so you can stay updated and informed every week. The 40k side will be mostly covered by me, Isaac Terada, while Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson covers the non-40k side of event coverage and news. This week, Rob "TheChirurgeon" Jones was on location to take pictures and provide additional reporting from the Las Vegas Open 2026.

Kit Smith Hanna Wins the 2026 Las Vegas Open

Kit Smith Hanna took gold at the 2026 Las Vegas Open over the weekend, winning the the Finals against Innes Wilson. The win marks the first LVO win for the rising star, capping off a stellar run over the past two years which have seen Smith-Hanna become one of the game’s top players. The win at LVO is actually only Kit’s second singles event win at a GT-sized or larger event this year, the first having been at the RCM Summer Skirmish in August. This win is likely to catapult Smith Hannah into the top 10 for Global Rankings after replacing his worst score.

Kit Smith Hanna (green jersey) and Jeff Cope (black shirt). Credit: Rob "TheChirurgeon" Jones.

Kit ran Orks at this year’s event and battled through nine rounds undefeated, plus an additional shadow round, to claim top honors. In the finals against Innes Wilson, he took an early lead after managing to destroy several key vehicle units while dominating the board. Kit’s list will be featured in Competitive Innovations this week. A stream of Kit’s games in the Semi-finals and Final Round can be found on the Games Workshop official Twitch channel. His list is projected to increase by around 145 points following the latest September Balance Update.

Innes Wilson Takes Top ITC Ranking, on Track to Win Circuit

Kit’s finals opponent, Innes Wilson, took second place at the event after losing in the Finals. Innes has been chasing gold at the Las Vegas Open for several years now as part of an ongoing quest to win the ITC. Despite finishing second, his score at the 720-person super major easily gives him a new top 6 score, placing him ahead of English player Mani Cheema atop the 2026 global ITC rankings. Wilson currently leads Cheema by 55 points and third place hopeful Jack Murphy by 77 points. Murphy finished fourth at LVO after losing to Innes Wilson in the semifinals.

Innes Wilson (white shirt) and Jack Murphy (USA shirt). Credit: Rob "TheChirurgeon" Jones.

Wilson’s Adeptus Mechanicus list is based on recent successful Adeptus Mechanicus lists and will need to find 5 points somewhere following the recent September Balance Update.

The race to determine this year’s ITC champion is likely to come down to the 2026 World Championships, where Wilson will need a good showing to retain the top spot in the circuit.

Event Coverage

It was a relatively slow week for 40K, with LVO being the only event to break other a hundred players, and only a single teams event of a meaningful size. LVO obviously took a lot of the focus this weekend, but I would expect events to start ramping up again next weekend, with BFS being the next large event on the radar. For teams the largest event of note was Grand Fanatic V - 40k, with 120 player over five rounds. This was a unique four player teams event, which is supported in the GW teams packet, but is relatively uncommon. The format basically comes down to a single pairing process, but allows players to not have to run Disruption which is always a plus. Team Ä.D.E.L.O.S.T. took it down with a 5-0 record. Congrats to Axel Rydéd, Emil Söderholm, Jesper Unander-Scharin, and Joel Larsson.

Kit Smith Hanna (green jersey) and Jeff Cope (black shirt). Credit: Rob "TheChirurgeon" Jones.

Orks Continue to Dominate Event Results

Orks continued to dominate the event results over the weekend, capturing a whopping seven event wins and more than half of our tracked GT+ sized events, while capturing 17% of all TiWP results (Dark Angels were next closest with 7.9% of TiWP results). Kit Smith Hanna finished the weekend as the top Orks player, but we also saw event wins from Jakub Klinsky, Mark Morrow, Damien Cooper, Jonah Nevin, Mark Pilkington, Beranger Guichardon, and Emil Söderholm. Those September Balance Update changes can’t come fast enough, though notably this week’s Ork winners ran a variety of different Detachments and dispositions.

New Marines First Thoughts  

With Marines being in the public eye for a few weeks, now we have a better idea of what will turn out to be the best builds. Ultramarines and Space Wolves appear to be the front runners, taking advantage of the increased durability and flexibility Marine Doctrines provide. Ravenguard also looks really good, with long range shooting threats that teleport being one of the oldest tricks in the book for a reason. The version of Dark Angels that existed in our heads for that first weekend got hit hard by the immediate Lion points adjustment, but they still have a number of very interesting builds to look at. Finally, Deathwatch looks great attached to any of the above builds, with two squads of Deathwatch Terminators with Plasma Cannons making an easy starting point for any infantry-focused Marine list. Besides them, the other big winners of the book include Victrix Guard, Terminators of all types, Eradicators inside of Impulsors, Heavy Bolter Eradicators, and Eliminators. There are a ton of cool and flexible builds available to try and I am excited to see more than just Dark Angels speeders winning for Marines. 

Player Focus: Jack Murphy 

Last week we covered Liam VSL, but now we get to cover the man who beat him at LGT. Jack Murphy is one of the youngest players on the circuit, and not as well known as members of Star Check or Art of War, but he has been on a terror this year. Over the past two months he has won a major, podiumed at Nova, and took fourth at LGT & LVO. I talked to him personally and he said he has qualified for nine golden tickets this season alone. Perhaps most impressively in recent times, and what will put him on the map for many players, is him taking down Liam VSL in the Semi-Finals of LGT. Liam is not by any means unbeatable, but anyone able to take him out is worth keeping an eye on. Jack is one to watch going into WCW and I am excited to see where he will go. With him at ITC rank #3 and a StatCheck elo of #9, I can definitely see him taking down WCW, or winning ITC outright with the right run. 

Innes Wilson (white shirt) and Jack Murphy (USA shirt). Credit: Rob "TheChirurgeon" Jones.

Alongside our coverage of the what's-what of 40k each week, Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson joins us to provide some quick updates on the movers and shakers in the competitive scene of some of the other games we cover here at Tabletop Battles!

Gundam Assemble Makes a Splash at LVO

Bandai Namco made an impressive splash this weekend at the 2026 Las Vegas Open, showing off their new miniatures game, Gundam Assemble. The event featured a scavenger hunt, demo tables, painting demos and tutorials, and an invitation-only event for creators to experience the game and see upcoming releases early. Among the announced releases were new expansions featuring mobile suits from Mobile Suit Zeta Gundam, Mobile Suit Gundam 00, Mobile Suit Gundam: Iron-Blooded Orphans, and the original Gundam series.  (Isaac: I tried it out and I think it has some potential, but want to see a more full release before I settle on my final thoughts.)

Gundam Assemble display from the LVO show floor. Credit: Rob "TheChirurgeon" Jones.

Kill Team and Age of Sigmar at LVO

Age of Sigmar and Kill Team hosted their own events this weekend at LVO, alongside the truly gigantic 40k supermajor. 

In the Age of Sigmar sphere, one hundred and 128 fought for supremacy over the course of three days, with five rounds between Friday and Saturday, and the final three rounds on Sunday to determine a final winner. The number one spot would go to Christopher Parten and his Ogor Mawtribes, an outstanding showcase of the power found in the new Battletome. The second place player, Jacob Brandon, was also undefeated, and piloted a Skaven list that leaned hard into Night Runners and Clanrats alongside some beefier big rats. Congratulations to both players for their excellent showings!

Meanwhile, the Kill Team event hosted by Squad Games saw 161 players battle for the LVO Kill Team Crown, with nine games played over the course of three days. The groups were split into an A group and a B group, who played only against members of their group on days 1 and 2, before the pods were mixed together on day 3 to determine the overall winner of the event. James Loomis would take the gold at this year’s LVO as the only undefeated player at the event, going 7-0-2 for the weekend, followed closely by Just Another Kill Team Podcast host and Tabletop Battles contributor Travis Cheng, going 7-1-1 with Kasrkin. Keeping such strong records over such a long stretch of games is extremely commendable, and congratulations to both Travis and James.

While both AoS and Kill Team had some of the largest competitive events of the year for both game systems at LVO, both systems also hosted a Narrative event as well, for players who wanted to focus on telling a story with their gaming over the course of the weekend. The AoS narrative event had 24 players this year, and the Kill Team Narrative event hosted again by the folks at Squad Games had 41 players. These don’t get as much spotlight as the competitive events, but we love to see narrative gaming in action when we can!

The Las Vegas Open 2026 show floor. Credit: Rob "TheChirurgeon" Jones.

StarCraft: The Miniatures Game Makes LVO Debut

There was a new kid on the block this year at LVO, who was actually the old kid from the nineties dressed up in miniatures game clothing insisting that no, it's young, it's hip, it's cool to be here. StarCraft: The Miniatures Game, which finally saw its wide release in August of this year, hosted an organized play event this year at LVO run by none other than Tabletop Battles very own MildNorman! The 13 player event saw Terrans take first prize at the inaugural outing for Starcraft at LVO, piloted by Dean Bilz. Congratulations to Dean for winning the event and to Norman for running it, and we hope this is just the beginning of many more Starcraft TMG LVO tournaments to come.

Reality Fracture Releases Worldwide for Magic: The Gathering

Magic the Gatherings newest set, Reality Fracture, released worldwide on October 2nd following a week of prerelease events held around the world. This newest set takes us to not just one plane, but across an entire multiverse as the Planeswalker Jace attempts to order all of reality according to his master plan. The set, which we reviewed last week here at Tabletop Battles, offers a plethora of new cards and interesting “what if?” takes on some of the setting’s most popular characters, and includes new set mechanics such as Empower and Heartwood Tokens.   

Warmachine Preps for the WTC Next Weekend

The biggest event in Warmachine is just around the corner, as the World Team Championship, or WTC, will be hosted in Poland this upcoming weekend. Held Between October 10th and 11th, the WTC sees 240 players across 48 teams of 5 from around the globe play some of the most intense matches of the year to decide what team and country will win Competitive Warmachine’s most prestigious event. One of our Warmachine Contributors, Blake, will be taking his Orgoth to the WTC to represent the USA, and wrote a handy guide breaking down the teams format as well as the WTC itself for those who want to learn more. We wish Blake, and all of the players at the WTC, safe travels and good luck in the upcoming event!
