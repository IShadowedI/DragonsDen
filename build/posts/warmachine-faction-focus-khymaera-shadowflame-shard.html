Warmachine Faction Focus: Khymaera Shadowflame Shard

September 24th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Khymaera are among the fastest and hardest hitting factions in MK4 Warmachine.  They have access to a suite of great casters, as well as potent threat extenders.  They also possess the ability to play a wide variety of styles, everything from gun line with Lylyth to a melee focused glass cannon army with Nyxyan and Quick Fangs.  Khymaera also has access to a wide variety of problem-solving tools with universal pathfinder and easy access to guidance.

Changelog

  • Published: 2026-09-24

Strengths

  • Among the fastest factions in Warmachine
  • High Defense

  • Universal Pathfinder or Flight
  • Access to a wide variety of utility tools

Weaknesses

  • Lack of Durability
  • High Point Cost
  • Ranged damage is limited high point models

Army and Playstyle

Leaders

Shyryss

Shyryss is the squishiest of the Khymaera casters with only 14 ARM and 15 Boxes; as a result, she can die to blasters more than any other leader.  With that limitation in mind, she is one of my go two casters.  She has the same speed as other Quick Fnags, making her among the faster casters in the game at SPD 7. She also has the ability to jump for free letting her move 12” before taking her combat action.  Sadly, she does not have a melee weapon so she cannot charge.  Lastly she has Repo 3” letting her move to safety after contributing.  She also brings field marshal prowl, giving stealth to her battle group when they have concealment.

Shyryss’s individual contribution with her gun is amazing.  It is a RAT 7, RNG8, POW10 gun - pretty weak at first glance.  There are several factors that turn it around, the first of which is ROF 3.  This lets her leverage multiple of her shot types. Second, all her thrown daggers have witchmark – not as a shot type, they just have witchmark.  Most leaders with multiple shot types must use a shot just to apply witchmark, Shyryss does it for free.  On top of that she has three different shot types; Shadowbind (Not crit shadowbind), Poison (extra damage dice against living) and Snipe (+3 RNG).

Credit - Trish Ross

Her spell list is also fantastic.  Everyone one of her spells can be a defining spell for a caster.  First distraction, -2 DEF, -2 MAT, and the target cannot make ranged attacks.  This spell shuts down units, and if you are aggressive enough, some casters. (Sorry Caine, no guns for you).  Mirage allows for a 2” place during the control phase – it can be used as a basic threat extender, or as a way to allow for a unit to reposition prior to charging, letting them position around a building or into a forest.  Banishing Ward protects high value targets from enemy spells or upkeeps. Hand of Destruction is signs and portents for her battle group, always useful.  Finally she has Host of Shadows - which makes her battlegroup ghostly – letting them pass through terrain.  A huge deal for models that struggle with impassible terrain like Triks.

Shyryss feat is -3 SPD on charges and slams against models in her control range, concealment (procc'ing prowl on her battle group) and dodge.  The -3 SPD on charges can be punishing – in many ways it is equivalent to +3 SPD when determining which player will get the alpha strike.  An important perk to note, only your models need to be in your control range, not the enemies.  A savvy opponent will notice that her feat does not apply trample power attacks – opponents can trample their full distance and start buying attacks.  Usually this is not a good idea, but at times it is so keep that option in mind.

When trying to shut down a key warbeast my standard procedure is move within 8” and throw a shadowbind shot at a model.  I will typically boost to hit with this shot but not boost damage.  I will then cast Hand of Destruction (auto hitting because of witchmark).  I will then throw two more poison daggers boosting damage.  Even against ARM18 these shoots typically do about 8 damage each, with potential for some really nasty spikes. Once that is done Shyryss will reposition backwards 3”, and commonly feat while the rest of your army moves forward and does work.

Kyrrax

Kyrrax’s defining trait is Alpha Hunter – an ability that gives his entire battlegroup +2 MAT and SPD.  It is triggered off  him killing a model, as well providing free runs or charges to entire battlegroup.  That ability alone is something you can build a list around.  Kyrrax will typically leverage an arc node in order to cast Battering Ram at a weak single wound infantry model just to trigger Alpha Hunter.  He is fairly tanky at 13 DEF, 20 ARM (counting the shield), so he is prepared to position further forward than most casters.  That is required if he is going to get any value out of Vortex of Destruction – which gives boosted damage rolls against enemies in his melee range.

While Alpha Hunter encourages players to build a beast heavy list, his feat encourages the opposite.  It provides friendly faction warrior models +2 to hit.  Additionally, when a model in his control range destroys or removes from play an enemy model, a warrior model in his control range can make a 2” advance followed by an attack.

Nyxyan

Nyxyan, while one of my favorite casters in Khymaera, is in a bit of an odd state.  Her kit is focused on supporting infantry, while also being ARC 8 -- meaning she needs multiple beasts to provide her with the fury she needs to cast spells.

She brings Dash, mix that with Hunters Mark from a Vypex and infantry that originally threatened 11” now threaten 14” – which will out threats most other units.  She also has Breath Stealer, a -2 SPD, -2 DEF debuff.  It only costs 2, mixed with her ARC of 8 means she can cast it twice boosting to hit both times.  A -2 DEF debuff is not to be underestimated.

Credit - Trish Ross - Nyxyan

Her feat, which only affects friendly faction warrior models, lets them share the benefits of Prey for a +2 to Hit and +2 to damage (and also change Prey's target). Interestingly, the only part of her feat that is restricted to her control range is the selection of a new Prey target, letting her gain the benefit all over the field.

She also has Revelations of the Outer Darkness, letting her cast a spell when a friendly warrior model is killed while within 12” of her.  I find the three most useful spells to cast with Revelations of the Outer Darkness are: Blackest Night, for a -1 on enemy attack rolls while within 10” of her, Admonition creating a new challenge for an enemy to crack, or Caustic Mist from Aklyss disrupting my opponents line of sight mid turn for forcing them to replan their turn.

Lastly, Nyxyan is sitting on a strangely effective spell assassination.  Using her feat and blessing of the gods for an extra focus, Nyxyan is able to cast Howling Flames 3 times, putting out AAT 9 POW 15 magic sprays.  Very few things in the game are able to negate magical sprays, and if a caster is low on resources the assassination is there for the taking.

Rassyk

Rassyk brings an interesting toolkit to Khymaera.  He has significant personal output with a MAT 8, POW 17 weapon and access to flashing blade meaning he can kill a heavy on his own if he wants to.  His battle plans allow him to build a defensive brick combining battle formation and mortal fear from Ryvyn for an effective +4 ARM swing.

His feat provides reposition 3” along with defensive strike.  This can be combined with models like the Drakyon which have potent on hit effects that can shut down opponents charges.  If faced with a mountain of tough models, Deathbringers gives grievous wounds to all the melee attacks of members of his battle group.  Warpath allows for a cohort model in his battle group to move 3” outside of activation working as a threat extender, a reposition move, or a laundry list of other tricks.  Lastly Disintegration provides some damage output while also acting as an ARM debuff opening the door for further damage.

Lylyth

Lylyth is the meta leader for Khymeara and is a staple in a lot of pairs.  She brings a strong gun with option of 3 POW 14 shots, or a single Armor Piercing POW 10 shot with Crit Stagger.  Stagger causes the model hit to loose ALL of it’s initial attacks.  On a colossal this can be 7+ attacks – crippling players plans.  Players will lean on her to crit stagger key enemy pieces which becomes very reliable with Deadeye adding an additional dice, boosting, and puppet strings for a re-roll of the crit doesn’t connect.  The down side of that tactic is she will typically not one shot the key piece meaning run and gun won’t trigger.  Lylyth players will then play careful reconnaissance on her to allow her to reposition 3” back to safety.

Deadeye can be used as a RAT fixer for any model in the faction, making normally unreliable pieces very effective.  While beasts are the traditional target, Quick Fang Masters throwing blind bombs have ruined more than one players day.  (See the bit on the quickfang master lateron)  Slipstream is a powerful threat extender that does not require rolling to hit like hunters mark does.  Lastly Silence of Death boosts the power of any unit it is cast on.

In terms of playstyle, Lylyth could best be described as sandpaper.  Her army will move up, remove a layer of models at the front, before triggering run and gun and moving back to safety.  Her feat is typically used to disrupt your opponents scenario position.  Traditionally gunlines like Lylyth typically runs struggle on scenario but Lylyth’s feat solves this problem.

Vallyx

Vallyx brings a wonderful bucket of tools to build around.  His feat, Blood Moon, makes every attack against enemies in his control range a critical hit.  Automatically critically hitting enemies opens some potent doors.  First, he can trigger his own weapons crit effects to automatically knockdown enemies that he charges, it also opens the door on two other key pieces in Khymaera.  Both the Trik and the Hydrix both have heads that are Crit Armor Piercing.  Armor Piercing cuts an opponent’s base ARM in half.  The Trik will swing at dice plus 9 in most case, with the Hydrix swinging at dice plus 7.  Few things can survive 4 or more attacks hitting that hard.

Credit - Trish Ross - Vallyx

Vallyx has a base 12” melee threat, but that can be extended 3” with vanish.  If you bring a hunters mark Vypex, that threat can be extended to 17”.  That alone allows Vallyx to bully the board.  His two weapons are both weapon master and carry major crit effects – knockdown and weaken respectively – which when combined with his feat allow him to kill the majority of leaders in Warmachine.  The final major part of Vallyx kit is Well of Hate, providing Murderous to models in his battle group (including himself) letting his army much away at high defense enemies.

Beasts

Aklyss

Aklyss brings two key tools.  First, Master of Ruin, decreasing the ARM of all OTHER models within 5” (including your own!).  The ARM debuff is then able to be heavily leveraged by the rest of the army.  The other key tool Aklyss brings is Caustic Mist.  This animus places a 3” cloud within his control range.  When combined with castings from your leader, this allows a ~9” cloud wall to be placed – causing a massive headache for many opponents.  If you leverage terrain, you can often block between half and a third of the board.

Credit - Trish Ross

Drakyon

The Drakyon the most durable beast in Khymaera per point; its ARM 19 and unyielding animus easily bring it up to ARM 21.  The flip side is it only POW 15 and POW 13 on its weapons – sure the POW 13 will usually gain the benefit of Trash since the Gore attack knocks models over, but the equivalent of POW 16.5 is nothing to write home about.  Smite on the Gore attack can be used in cute ways such as defensive strikes under Rassyks feat – but it struggles to find a home in many lists.

Hydrix

The Hydrix is one of the most dynamic beasts in Warmachine.  It can be built in a wide variety of ways.  It can be built as a traditional melee beatstick with the Toothy Maw head.  It can also be built as a gun beast leveraging the Flame Spitter head, and the Venom spitter heads on the smaller heads.  It can also be built for a more supporting role with the Diviner head to hand out Future Sight, letting you boost after rolling, then two Venom Spitter heads to contribute from safety.  If paired with Vallyx one head will usually be dedicated to the crit armor piercing head – which when combined with his feat makes it one of the hardest hitting tools in the game.

Credit - Trish Ross

Shadow Seraph

While the jury is still out on the Shadow Seraph, signs right now point to it being underpowered.  It boasts one incredibly powerful attack (POW 14 Armor Piercing is amazing), but otherwise it is pillow fisted with all followup attacks coming in at POW 15.  Coming in at 21 points, a heavy that can not kill most other heavies struggles to find a role in most lists.  The Shadow Seraph’s gimmick is the ability to cast one of it’s 3!!!! animi after being hit.  All of these animi increase it’s defense which in theory make the Shadow Seraph very challenging to deal with.  Unfortunately there are quite a few tools that just shut it down.  Things like silence, mage static, or even casting guidance on the attacking model cripple the Shadow Seraphs defensive tech leaving you with a beast that struggles to deliver value.  The flip side is, if your opponent does not have the tools to counter the Shadow Seraph it can be nigh unkillable.  Most of the games top lists have the right tools to shut it down, so it struggles to find a place.

Skylla

Skylla is the character Hydrix in Khymaera.  She boasts two unique things.  First, Rejuvenating Miasma – which is a cloud that heals friendly models within 3” of her.  This can be combined with Shyryss to trigger field marshal prowl providing stealth all the times.  Skylla’s other tool is Transfiguration on her spray and Vorpal Maw.  These attacks allow you to return grunts to various units when she kills enemy models – those returned models then healed by Rejuvenation Miasma assuming they activate after Skylla.

Trikhymaerax

The Trikhymaerax, or Trik is a beast stick of a beast.  Coming in a Def 11 ARM 19 with Rapid healing, it is deceptively durable.  Its defense is just high enough that opponents will miss with some attacks given the volume of attacks required to kill it.  Rapid healing almost always being in place makes it pseudo-ARM 21 since it will heal an average of two points of damage from every damaging attack.  As a colossal it is immune to the Anti Heal aspect of Torment and Grievous Wounds, so its main fears are Entropic Force or Mortality.

There are many different loadouts for the Trikhymaerax, but the staple upgrade is the Arcane Negation tail.  The negation tail allows you to force the Trikhymaerax to negate a spell targeting it, or a model within 3”.  This allows you to shut down spells that would otherwise cripple a colossal such as Mortality, Stranglehold, Deadweight, or Crippling Grasp.

When running with Vallyx, the armor piercing head is acceptable, but if run with another leader the Trik needs either the Gulper head or the Arcane Many-Eyed Head in for damage output.  The Many-Eyed head swings at POW 19, enough to destroy most non-buffed targets, and because of Purgation, it ends up hitting buffed targets even harder.  The downside of the Purgation head is you lose the Eyeless head, which, while expensive, gives you access to two powerful tools.  First, Force wall as an Animus often used to provide +2 Def and blast resist to Quick Fangs or the Shades makes the staggeringly durable.  The Trik itself is also a great target for Force Wall.  Force Wall will shift it from  DEF 11 to DEF 13, a critical swing in defense.  The Eyeless head also offers a slam ranged attack.  Crucially, the slam attack lets you roll the distance slammed, then choose the direction the model will move.  That knowledge allows for pinpoint shenanigans.

In terms of ranged weapons, it is generally a choice between more effective shots, or weapons with pistol if you plan on brawling with your Trik.  Both are solid choices.

Vypex

Vypex tend to fall into two categories.  The first is the 10 point version with Asp/Hunter’s Mark/Cleavers.  This version is basically just there to provide hunter’s mark.  10 points is pricey for it, but getting the alpha strike off is often a key factor in victory.  If points are available the Cleavers will usually be upgraded to Impalers to shift the Vypex from just being a hunters mark tool, to a solid melee piece.  The downside of the Vypex is at only ARM 16 and 26 boxes, you are paying the same points as a heavy for the defensive stats of a light.  Players will also occasionally run the Viper head, to attempt to leverage Side Step and it’s 3 initial attacks to Pac-man through troops.

Credit - Trish Ross - Warmachine Vypex

Wyvern

Clocking it at 19 points a Wyvern is a pricy beast.  It brings solid damage output at POW 18 base.  The Wyvern natively threatens 13”; however, that is only with the low POW tail.  Truth be told is only threatens 12”.  Combined with Bellighul, and the Wyvern his hitting at POW 20, enough to remove most heavies.  It’s animus, Draconic Aura increases the POW of all fire damage rolls by 2, while also preventing continuous fire from expiring.  It’s potent if built around.  The Wyvern’s spray, Nyxyan, Wyrmspine Cinderbacks, and Trik all love the effect.  Frustratingly, the Wyvern’s spray does not have pistol, limiting its effectiveness on a beast that needs to be in melee to earn its keep.

Credit Trish Ross

Solos

Incarnate Conjuror

Incarnate Conjurors are generally considered more cute than good, but they can still be fun.  They bring an aura of -2 to hit for enemy models that are on fire.  This, when combined with a Wyvern can create some interesting situations.  Additionally, the Conjuror can cast Firestarter making a friendly faction models attacks always do continuous fire.  This can be combined with AoE attacks to set a broad swath of your opponent’s force on fire.

Credit - Trish Ross - With such an amazing model I wish the Incarnate Conjuror were good

Pythia

Pythia is a staple in Khymaera lists.  She brings a free upkeep, as well as being an expendable arc node.  While she no longer has auto hit on her weapon, it is often worth charging her to dispel key enemy spells increasing armor.  She is then able to reposition 3” out of melee, leaving her free to function as a channeler.

Regulus

Regulus is a high defense contesting piece.  Espionage, while powerful, will rarely be impactful since you must be quite far forward to successfully trigger it.  He can be frustrating to kill, but boosted attacks will still do it.  Currently most players leave him on the shelf.

Ryvyn

Ryvyn brings two key things to Khymaera.  The first is mortal fear, making all living enemy models within 8” suffer -2 to damage rolls.  A key thing to note, this is not just melee, but also ranged attacks and spells.  Ryvyn also has a weapon master thresher attack – in the right position this attack can be devastating.  He can move forward 6”, jump another 5”, then thresher everything within 2” of him.

Credit - Trish Ross - Talk about a model with style

Shadowmancer Scion

The Shadowmancer Scion is brought for one reason – puppet strings.  If you absolutely need an ability to go off, puppet strings lets you reroll that result.  Commonly a player will put puppet strings on a Vypex before attempting to caster hunters mark, or Lylyth uses puppet strings to ensure she triggers crit stagger on her full draw attack.

The Scion is not a one trick pony and can be used for other aspects – but they are situation rather than central.  Grip of Shadows boosts increases the odds of spells hitting key targets which Nyxyan and Wyrmspine Shadowmancer’s appreciate.  DEF 15 with stealth is also a durable defensive stat line.  Lastly, if the opportunity arises, the Scion can charge in and utilize its two blessed attacks to attempt to trigger battle wizard then cast Stygian Abyss to put some damage out.  Its rarely plan A, but it is a solid plan B – well, maybe plan C.

Spinner

The Spinner is the only 2 point solo in Khymaera and is often used to fill in leftover points.  The Spinner can be used to cast Grim Returns to return a friendly trooper model.  This will most often be used on Incarnate Knights since they are the most durable warriors Khymaera has access to. The other interesting aspect of the Spinner is the Acid Bomb – notably the fact that the blast is POW 12/10.  The high strength of the blast damage allows you to remove obnoxious high DEF models without needing to succeed the roll to hit.

Wraithwing Paragon

The Wraithwing paragon is the premier anti shooting tech in Khymaera.  The Paragon offers blast resistance and enemies suffer -3” RNG if they are targeting friendly models within 3”.  The -3” range aspect is crucial for keeping our beasts and high value pieces safe.  A weapon going from RNG 14 to RNG 11 means if you are getting shot, you can charge them.  The blast resistance aspect is crucial for keeping Quick Fangs alive.  Their ARM 12 will die in droves even to POW 7 blasts, but the Paragon keeps them safe.

The Paragon’s personal damage output is unreliable.  MAT 7, POW 15 with crit decapitation is pretty potent, but famously it will always miss – it’s a meme at this point.  When it does hit, it can hit like a ton of bricks, and sprint away to continue to add value.

Credit - Trish Ross - Wraithwing Paragon - Not to be confused with a beast

Bellighul

Bellighul is technically a mercenary, but he only works for one faction - Khymaera.  He brings a handful of tools, but the reason he is taken is Enrage.  It is a +2 damage swing on every attack on a key model makes.  This can be used to crank a Trik or a Wyvern up to 11 in terms of damage.

Credit - Trish Ross

Units

Incarnate Knights

Incarnate Knights are unique within Khymaera, they are the only unit in the faction that can really take a beating.  They struggle in terms of damage output only having 3 attacks, but they make up for it by having ARM 20 (counting +2 for Battle Driven) and 30 boxes across all 3 of them.  This puts their durability on par with most heavies.  They are commonly run to contest enemy objectives or to jam an opponent.  If you are running Kyrrax they are the ideal Dauntless Resolve target.  That will take them to ARM 23, tough, and they natively have steady meaning they are quite a tough thorn to remove.

Quick Fang Stalkers

Quick Fang Stalkers are my single favorite unit in Khymaera.  They threaten 11” base, but with access to hunter’s mark, or another tool like slipstream or dash it can easy move to 13” to 15”.  They also possess Acrobatics combined with pathfinder which basically means they ignore half the rules in the game.  Acrobatics lets them move over models, but just as importantly it ignores intervening models when declaring a charge target.  This means you can charge models behind a jamming wall with no trouble.  They also have ambush and advanced deploy letting them get to key places fast.

They pack two attacks, but I would say about 60% of the time, I end up using the combo strike.  Pow 13 is enough to start to apply damage to ARM 19 on the charge.  The choice between combo strikes or single swing usually comes down to the opponents stat line.  If the target only has one wound and are living, 99% of the time, you want single swings.  Anatomical Precision means that you no longer even have to roll for damage and will simply remove the models on hit.  Anatomical Prevision also prevents tough regardless of the targets living or unliving state.  With infantry units 98% (source – trust me bro) of the time you want to charge with infantry models, Anatomical Precision means you are often part of the 2%.  If the target unit has set defense, you will often NOT want to charge.  The extra damage die isn’t important and suffering -2 on to hit rolls isn’t worth it.  If you don’t need the speed – don’t charge.

Credit - Trish Ross - Quickfang Stalkers approach a Cryx Stalker.

The final amazing thing about Quick Fang Stalkers is their resilience.  They are like cockroaches.  DEF 14 is a break point, 5 models with DEF 14 who have charged in and positioned as spread out as possible become very hard for a lot of players to deal with.  Mix in stealth and there are few things that can reliably remove them.  The two things that DO terrify Stalkers are blasts, which just pick them up in droves, and E-leaps.  Avoid those two things and your Quick Fangs will have long happy lives.

Quick Fang Wind Strikers

Quick Fang Wind Strikers are the premier ambush unit in the army.  They can ambush in and move to contest key scenario elements while also being Def 14 and stealth.  They have crit shadow bind on their shots which can be useful in niche cases.  When used with Vallyx that niche case becomes something you can plan around, shadow binding cavalry and other models that don’t have easy access to shake.  Wind Strikers also do an automatic D3 points of damage to the target completely bypassing armor.  This is useful into High ARM single wound infantry.  Other ideal targets are Kithguard Guard Towers, a 5 man unit has a very solid chance of removing one.  The other thing to keep in mind is that the D3 damage is bypasses ancient shroud on models like Vorony in Old Umbry and Blood Sirens in Fane of Nyrro letting the Wind Strikers cripple pieces that usually take way more resources to remove.

Shades

The Shades deserve an entire article on their own.  Their ability to scalpel out key pieces is second to none. Their speed and Unpredictable Movement, letting them place within 4" instead of 2", allows for some insane placement tricks.  Additionally, Fortune Hunter, gives them an additional dice to hit and damage against characters makes them a serious assassination threat at all times.  One not so direct trick is to move forward, and place the moving shade ~11” from a scenario element.  From there, you place a second shade 4” away towards that element.  The Shades then reposition 3", with the forward Shade moving to contest, and the originally placed model moving further away into safety.  This maneuver lets you contest with one frustrating to deal with model at a time.

Credit - Trish Ross

Talon Death Dealers

Talon Death Dealers are the second most durable unit it Khymeara. ARM 15 with unyielding and 8 boxes is still not that durable. They are one of the few MAT 7 units in Khymeara letting them operate relatively support free. Their POW 13 finisher attacks let them hit harder than most units, charging into a damaged ARM 19 model, a unit of them will do an average of 24 points of damage. I have found them most useful as a second wave unit, letting other units like Quick Fang stalkers charge in first and inflict a little damage before the Death Dealers come though as second wave – this ensure that Finisher is always active.

Credit - Trish Ross

Talon Lashers

Talon Lashers bring a unique state line, DEF 14 ARM 14 and 8 boxes. The DEF 14 is a major trait for survivability.  In a world where MAT 6 is standard, DEF 14 can be a hurdle. They also bring a unique combination of offensive traits. RNG 3 on melee weapons combined with reposition lets the unit charge in, place 2 inches away, reposition, then engage a line of models ~18.5 inches long.  They can also leverage their two attacks in an attempt trigger crit knockdown.  They are also able to drag key enemy models out of position, opening doors for others to charge in and position more advantageously.

Credit - Trish Ross - Talon Lashers

Wyrmspine Cinderbacks

Cinderbacks have been shunned for the most of the last year but folks are starting to come around to them.  8 points for three blasts helps a lot in a world where there are lots of cheap infantry running around.  When combined with either Lylyth for dead eye, or Nyxyan for prey, they become very consistent.  When they throw their bombs they generate a cloud where they were standing which can be quite useful blocking line of sight to models behind them – the big downside is that the Cinderbacks will still be on the cloud after throwing their bombs and Khymaera does not have an easy way to reposition the unit back – only Rassyk’s feat will do that.

Wyrmspine Shadowmancers

Wyrmspine Shadowmancers are among the first units added to most Khymaera armies. They bring two key traits - Guidance and Condition.  Guidance offers units eyeless sight to target model.  As much as our opponents assume everything in Khymaera has eyeless sight, only two models actually have it. Having eyeless sight on a stick is almost as good. The other utility aspect of the Wyrmspine Shadowmancers is condition, removing excess fury allows for Khymaera players to run more beasts than they otherwise would. (It also lets you put extra fury on a beast in the event that you didn’t put enough on them during their activation – a mistake I have NEVER made) Lastly they have access to Flesh Eater, a pow 10 arcane attack. It is almost never used, usually when you are desperate to kill a random contesting model.

Credit - Trish Ross - Khymaera Wyrmspine Shadowmancers

Quick Fang Master

The Quick Fang Master is the only attachment in Khymaera.  It clocks in at 3 points, which is quite steep for an attachment; however, they bring a staggering amount of utility. Most often taken with Quick Fang Stalkers their key tool is their Blight Bomb.  The Blight Bomb has pistol and can be used in melee and will frequently be the first attack made as part of a charge.  The two attack types that are used most are Rust, reducing the ARM of a key construct by -2.  This can be stacked with other debuffs Khymaera have access to in order to make Quick Fang Stalkers hit well above their weight class.  The other useful option is Black Oil, which blinds the target and the closest model with 1”.  This brings a -4 def swing that will cripple most targets.  The grenade is AoE 1, so if you are sloppy you can hit your own models with the blast.  It also opens another opportunity.  If your opponent has placed a low DEF model within 1” of a high def model, lob the grenade at the low defense model while positioning your models such that the AoE will hit the high DEF true target.

List Building with Khymaera

List building starts with a unit of Wyrmspine Shadowmancers then expands from there.  Most of the leaders in Khymaera are looking for a mix of beasts and infantry.  Each leader supports different aspects of the army and needs to be built accordingly.

Kyrrax: Mixed force to leverage his feat and alpha hunter.

Lylyth: Leans heavily into ranged beasts to maximize the power of run and gun.

Nyxyan: Her entire kit supports units.

Rassyk: Lists are built to be durable and leverage defensive strike.

Shyryss: Leans into a combined arms play style while shutting down charges.

Vallyx: Leans into beasts, particularly those with strong crit effects.

What to Watch Out For

The Shades and other insanely fast assassination attempts.  The Shades alone open so many doors with what they can do with Ricochet.  Units like Quick Fang Stalkers combined with speed buffs allow for units to get where most players did not expect them.  Finally, Lylyth, like in the last three editions of Warmachine, is always sitting on a ranged assassination if you don’t respect it.

Final Thoughts

Khymaera bring a wonderful mixtures of playstyles to the game.  They are able to run a melee focused army, a gunline, or a combined arms playstyle.  They have a wonderful selection of tools that let them solve problems.
