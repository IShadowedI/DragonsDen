[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-06-03

June 3rd, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

After a very busy week last week, we're slowing it down with 3 smaller events located in the US.

This Week's Events

  • Aetherforce Age of Sigmar GT

  • River City Rumble 2026

Thoughts

This is a pretty interesting battle. Slaanesh is very agile and flexible, can even take a good charge with their Twinsouls, but well, Ironjawz are really good at charging, especially with Kragnos.

The secret likely lies in their ability to pelt enemies from afar with Blissbarbs, charge in with Painbringers and the Lord of Hubris while waiting back with the Twinsouls to defend the point.

Hedonites of Slaanesh Victory - 64 - 25

Credit: Silks

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Slaanesh Mortals

Thoughts

Syll'esske is an interesting addition here since while the Warscroll really implies you want a mixed force of Daemons and Mortals, and there are no Daemons here, it's still a solid Warrior-Wizard, much more budget conscious than a Keeper of Secrets. In the right match up their spell allowing friendly units to subtract 1 from Enemy Ward saves is a nice perk, too.

The main hammers of this list are the Twinsouls and Painbringers. Painbringers are better at pushing up into enemy territory, while Twinsouls hang on objectives and wait for enemies to charge them. The Seekers hang back and weaken targets first, while the Fiendbloods wait in deep strike until its time to jump out and grab an objective or charge an unsuspecting target.

Thoughts

Given how well DOK have been doing this is a very surprising upset. It's hard to gauge what happened, since the Skaven's Primary murder weapon here is the Doom-Flayers, which would tear up the Blood Sisters if it wasn't for the Flayer's high wounds making them very vulnerable to the Sisters Strike Last.

I would assume that the Night Runners were able to help Crixxit get close to Morathi or Krethusa and remove them from the board early, which would heavily weaken the army while supported by the Jezzails to do damage to the Sisters from range.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Doomflayers

Thoughts

This is a nifty little list. Doom-flayers are a solid sort of Cav but not actually unit, they get 2D6 Rend 1 Damage 2 attacks when they charge (with Anti-Infantry too) which means they absolutely shred through infantry but can box with a lot of other things if they need to.

Supporting them is the usual Suspect of the Scourge of Ghyran Screaming Bell. Both a powerful Caster and also offers +2 to move and -1 to be hit depending on the round. Jezzails give some solid ranged fire, away from the danger of combat.

Night Runners with Deathmaster Crixxit is a nice bit of Tech, the Night Runners have a lot of movement tech to get across the field and sneak past Chaff with smoke bombs to get closer to the enemy. Then Deathmaster Crixxit can pop out and shank them, and he is an excellent Hero Assassin, carving out the piece that is keeping the opponent's list operating..

Thoughts

This is an interesting matchup since if the FEC list was more skewed toward the Terrorgheist spam that is still reasonably popular, it probably would have more of an issue here. Instead it only goes about half in, but that almost means it can't really overwhelm the Monsta-Killaz quite as easily.

Swamphorde bullies makes tackling large groups of Gutrippaz and Monsta-Killaz difficult for just about any unit, while on the FEC side Cryptguard are fantastic at digging in and waiting for a charge, so such a game is going to be largely about baiting the other into needing to charge them to gain an advantage.

Result

Kruleboyz Victory - 52 - 40

Eli Sweatt - Kruleboyz - 1st Place

Kruleboyz - Credit: RichyP

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Kruleboyz Good Stuff

Thoughts

Swamphorde Bullies remains top of the charts for Kruleboyz and it's easy to see why. With how relatively cheap the units are, its quite easy to stack units of Gutrippaz next to other units like Monsta-Killaz, give them an extra attack. Assuming the Dirty Tricks fired correctly, they're getting Crit Mortals on a 5+ and going before you by giving Strike Last to a unit. They can be pretty tanky, thanks to the buffs and debuffs from the spells cast by the shaman.

The Corpse-Rippa Vulcha is a great Hammer when you have to crack something a bit too tough for the Gutrippaz, and you still got tons of disposable chaff left over to grab and hold objectives.
