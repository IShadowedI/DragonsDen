[40k] Competitive Innovations in 10th: Shell By Date pt.2

June 12th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Thoughts

BLAP! Soulforged, once described to me by a friend as 'like putting a gun to their head and yours at the same time and just seeing what happens'. Defilers tilt those odds more than a bit, as they can take a decent chunk of mortal wounds whilst deleting god from existence compared to the Forgefiends and such this build used to rely on. Fantastic to see this bonkers detachment take a GT win, it's very unlikely you'd see more than one of it at an event...

  • 3rd - Jose Antonio Costa - Drukhari (Spectacle of Spite): Wych and Hellion spam Spectacle.
  • 4th - Nacho Jhinbe - Thousand Sons (Grand Coven): Grand Coven goodstuff with a Defiler.

Wrap Up

I don't have to pretend to be excited about 10th edition at this point, and neither do you. It's been a really fun three years, but it's time for something fresh! I hope you're enjoying the reveals as much as I am :)

Lowest of Men, out.
