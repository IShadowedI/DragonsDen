Gencon 2026 Infinity Seminar - The Tabletop Battles Hot Take

August 7th, 2026 via Goonhammer

As is their custom, Corvus Belli have held a preview seminar at GenCon where they showed off some upcoming stuff for Warcrow, Hexadome Tactics, and Infinity. It's the latter which is our special interest, so we're going to talk about some of the main points. If you would like to watch the seminar video in full, it's available here.

Changes to Aerial Vehicles Incoming

In a very welcome acknowledgement of a major issue affecting competitive events, CB have said they will be taking some kind of action to rein in Vehicles. There's no indication at all what that might be yet.

  • Musterkrux: I really just want to see a light touch from CB here. It'll only take a nudge here and there for Flyers to be balanced appropriately. Too much and they'll become entirely non-viable. Also, while there is data to be collected in listening to your community and taking measured action, turning Infinity into a game where Round Zero is whinging hard enough that the company nerfs your opponent's faction harder than yours gets nerfed is a mistake the war-gaming community has already made at least once before (Warmachine's Community Integrated Development, if you're wondering).
  • Genghis: I'm not too worried about that. CB have made it clear that they do gather data wherever possible, looking at factions' win rates and the inclusion of different profiles within tournament army lists. That isn't to say collecting that data is without challenges, but they do try. I'm more interested to see whether they go purely with a points-based solution, or walk back some of Vehicles' unique strengths. I personally think the upper limits of their current mobility need to be examined. 11" of true flight movement in a single Order while firing, combined with the advantages of Silhouette 7 size when vaulting terrain, is just too hard to balance against conventional units.

Forthcoming Releases

One of September's releases, this lovely new Gecko TAG for Nomads/Corregidor will be in Unicool, like all their new bigger models, and interestingly it's the first Nomad TAG to be packaged with the Robbybot Peripheral Ancillary.

  • Genghis: I'm in the awkward position of being someone who never really runs more than 1 Gecko in a list, and has a painted metal one I'm happy with. But this sculpt is a real step up and I am certainly planning to pick one up.
  • Musterkrux: I like it, though I think those massive shoulder-pads are selling it as a much bigger, meaner TAG than it actually is. It's a very cool sculpt and all the other factions should be jealous that Nomads keep getting absolute bangers. Though, I question who decided to mount the gun under the Chain rifle and not the other way around. Nomad engineers are weird like that.

Another September arrival, the fearsome Tarkshyas is (currently, at least) one of the real powerhouses behind OSS. As you'd expect for a unit with Transmutation, it comes with a separate Operator model.

  • Musterkrux: CB, why do you only release such an awesome model, for such a great profile only after you announce a pending balance pass for Flyers? Such cruelty. I think there may have been an opportunity in designing the visuals of this two-element flyer (Pilot and vehicle) to depart a little from the 'person piloting a machine' aesthetic and CB missed it. Imagine how cool it would be to have the pilot-remote strapped to the under-side like a missile. I mean, why does the robot need a pilot seat and controls if they plug into each other like Voltron? 'I'll form the head' but for a Flyer?
  • Genghis: I have seldom been so conflicted; rules-wise I think the (current) Tarkshya is an abomination which fills me with bitterness, envy and hate. But it looks really cool. Seething.
  • Musterkrux: Seethe and Mald, old man. Go enjoy your collection of amazing Nomad TAGs or something. :)

One of the most hotly anticipated concepts previewed earlier this year is Ioann Bann, a mercenary who will be available to a few factions. Now despite being available for September pre-orders, we have not yet seen any profile for this fine fellow. As a dog warrior type with Transmutation, we expect him to be at least 3VITA, and like long-term dog person McMurrough, we expect him to be a holy terror on the battlefield. One to watch out for in Infinity Army updates.

  • Genghis: Werewolves aren't my favourite part of the Infinity aesthetic, although I really like his body armour. The katanas caused him to be unkindly, but funnily, described as a 'werewolf mall ninja'. We'll see if he walks the walk - I'm probably more interested in the rules than the model.
  • Musterkrux: A werewolf dual-wielding katanas is the perfect combination of cringe, Edge Lord and leftover D&D character templates that I am 100% here for. I will be very interested to see how he differentiates himself from McMurrough.

As the ITS Exclusive sculpt for the next season of ITS, we have Armand Le Muet. Who is a fantastic piece on the table in a game that is slowly circling back to allowing hard AROs to exist once again (Nature is healing). There may be a general release version, so people who desperately need him can either try their luck at winning an event, buying an ITS pack for themselves or just waiting.

  • Musterkrux: That's definitely a model of Armand Le Muet.
  • Genghis: ha, it is fairly straightforward, but it's an impactful unit which I think has been OOP for some time. Pretty decent sniper pose. If I win one I might swap out the head and make it a general-use proxy sniper.

Then there's also Tao Wu, who despite having a fairly modern sculpt gets another one.

  • Musterkrux: I've never really been one for Tao Wu as a character, I don't think CB has ever quite gotten his execution right when it comes to putting a 'mastermind and schemer' on the table. That said, I'm willing to give it another go. Just look at his majestic mustache.
  • Genghis: I guess if I end up with one of these, I can always use the other version as an HVT.

New Book: Hyperthermal

For those who aren't familiar with CB's rules model, this book will not itself contain any new rules. Typically it will release alongside some new units, but even that is not an exact 'release day' thing, the main associated drop of new rules might have been the recent Mazebreaker update, or it might be the Spec-Ops models (see below). But all Infinity rules are free via PDF downloads or the Infinity Army app. The paper books are purely for art and fluff.

What we do know about that fluff is that the book will provide detail on Corregidor, OSS, and Haqqislam.

  • Genghis: I'm often in two minds about the books. The artwork is always gorgeous, and I do find Infinity's universe to be conceptually interesting. However I'm quite critical of how CB have written their narrative in the last few. They tended towards quite dry catalogues of military campaigns, which on fictional planets can blur into a tedious melange. That said there are often little flashes of wit or evocative fiction.

New Spec-Ops Models

The Hyperthermal book (see above) will outline the context or circumstances that warrant the deployment of Spec-Ops across all of the Infinity factions. There may or may not be a McGuffin involved. (There's always a McGuffin)

It looks like each Spec Ops set will contain three models from your chosen faction: The Spec-Ops as Light Infantry, a Heavy Infantry variant, and then an S1 Support Remote buddy.

  • Musterkrux: CB has me dialled in perfectly. I know the whole 'Model Doggo' meme has gone full circle and is a little cringe now but I remain an unashamed Good Boi Enjoyer. I've only had Men Tong for a day and a half and if anything ever happened to him I'd put my opponent into Loss of Lieutenant and then myself.
  • Genghis: I too am willing to be pandered to with regards to miniature pets. I'm reminded of the critical debate around the recent film adaptation of the Odyssey - fundamentally, people have been suckers for a dog for thousands of years.
  • Musterkrux: Look at this guy. He's having a fantastic time. Though, that cod-piece is absolutely medieval. Suspiciously so, though.
  • Genghis: something about the Yu Jing spec ops (pictures of a LI version and a concept art dossier are available online, we haven't included everything) just makes me think he's a huge d-bag. Something about the shades and the jauntily-angled cigar.
  • Musterkrux: My body is ready for the return of Spec-Ops. My read is that they're going to return to the classic box contents where a Spec-Ops comes with multiple weapon options, which is an absolute dream for people who want to weapon-swap other models in their faction. I'm not even sure who is my favorite sculpt there, on one hand I adore Big-Hair PanO but that cheeky grin and sunglasses on the Gui Feng is giving me strong Aura Farming vibes. Overall, I am very excited to see Spec-Ops get more support, the modelling opportunities are massive and communicating the RPG-like progression of a character from their LI incarnation to their Heavy Infantry load-out just sings to me. Bravo, CB.
  • Genghis: god I would love a little sprue of weapons like the old Spec Ops blisters came with. Within the game, though, it's very difficult for them to balance all weapon profiles on units if they're allowed to freely mix-and-match though. I would be fine with a selection of bespoke profiles, like a paramedic with SMG and some utility kit, a plain Specialist Operative with an SWC gun, that sort of thing.

We're also expecting a Combined Army version (Nexus Spec Ops), which was also previewed with LI/HI versions and Remote buddies:

  • Musterkrux: What a happy guy, just living his best life flaying the skin from the upstart sapiens of the Human Sphere.

Additionally, those of you with the wallet-power to pre-order the Hyperthermal book as well as a Spec Ops set will score the Rumbler NA2 Spec Ops. If the Rumbler is the NA2 Spec-Ops, there's no indication whether it comes as a LI/HI pairing or not (or if it gets a Buddy-bot).

  • Musterkrux: I, for one, believe in Cyberpunk Cowboy Batman. You can't just start by perching on gargoyles, you gotta start at the bottom and work your way up, doing all of your brooding on a...Keg of Beer?
  • Genghis: yeah I'm a bit mystified by that as well. Seems like a detail I'd totally lean into for Necromunda (I have some kegs of victory beers as one of my Gang Relic markers) but is out of place for Infinity's futuristic black ops. I might file the letters off and leave it as a generic sci-fi doohickey.

Conclusion

Beyond the immediate releases over the next couple of months (release the Ioann Bann rules now, CB!) this seminar mainly whets our appetite for Hyperthermal and associated releases, notably Spec Ops. We are also keen to see Vehicle re-balancing. There's no surety yet on when that will be, although at an educated guess, we expect something towards the end of this year.

  • Musterkrux: I believe they mentioned it previously that it had encountered some difficulties in securing a production partner but I just want it on the record that I am still deadly keen on that movie/TV-series they previewed a year or two ago. I haven't forgotten, CB.
  • Genghis: Ooh, well remembered. Personally I really want to see the Nomad Spec-Ops models now. I love that they went with HI variants as a standard inclusion. There are a lot of releases in that Spec Ops category though - Infinity has 9 full factions, plus Tohaa (OOP and I wouldn't expect anything) and NA2 (presumably just getting the single Rumbler, not a full set). That is 6 more Spec Ops packs beyond what we've seen previewed here.

Look forward to more coverage from us as and when we get further Infinity news. Tabletop Battles is also looking to expand its WarCrow coverage in the future.
