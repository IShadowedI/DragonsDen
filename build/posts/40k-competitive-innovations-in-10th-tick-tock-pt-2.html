Thoughts

A fitting indictment of the current meta, as a fairly established Defiler list (the CSM Raiders variant, with added speed and AP) runs into a novel Death Guard Daemons AND triple Defiler variant (utilising the Daemon shenanigans of summoners to support the crabs), and produces an absolute blowout victory for the CSM. What a time to be alive.

Raiders can struggle for hard target removal, being excellently tooled to blast infantry, but Noise Marines disembarking with full rerolls can put the hurt onto any unit that dares to stand on the objectives, and that is very much Tallyband's game. Raiders will be able to pick the heavy hitter engagements because it simply has so much more chaff and activation play ahead of their Defilers, with the multiple Rhinos giving the DG something of an activation issue. Defiler mirrors are rarely long or stable games, and it looks like this one ran away hard from the more creative of the two variants. Lovely stuff.

Thoughts

As alluded to above, the real strength of this Renegade Defilers list is that it has a lot more strings to it's bow than the Defilers alone, as potent as those are. It has mission play for days and lots of transports - the recent success of Drukhari against Defilers has highlighted that activation locking the greedier builds out of the game is a valid route to vicotry, and this Chaos list applies a bit of that logic itself whilst retaining the incredibly output of the Defilers behind the lines. Fantastic work on the event win!

Really nice to see some techier chaos lists coming through! Great work on the top placing.

Archetype

Sub Assault Mixed Arms

Thoughts

A GT win for Sub Assault, which has stoically plugged away as the most technical and versatile Tyranid detachment for what feels like the last 6 months. Being able to apply a truckload of Zoan shooting to one location, then relocate it, is huge in a meta of big but slow crabs shuffling about. If you play this to a high level, you can pick apart some of the greedy meta lists, and the win here is vindication for fighting brawn with big bug brains! Amazing work.
