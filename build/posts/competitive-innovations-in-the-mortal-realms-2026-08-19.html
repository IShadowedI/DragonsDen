Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-08-19

August 19th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Since the release of the Aqshy General's Handbook, response has been very positive. Events are up, and attendance is way up. This has been great to see, and really feels like a revitalization in the life of the game as people are responding positively to the scourge of Aqshy.

Little Guys

Thoughts

Both lists are the same, just moving the enhancements from one hero to another.

Mike forsakes the large model of most lists (most try and include the Glottkin or Rotigus) in order to stuff it to the gills with more bodies. Archetype

Be'lakor and his legions

Thoughts

Both lists utilize Be'lakor as their leader, but change the composition. List 1 goes for a more elite, with a big brick of Chosen alongside the Warriors and Knights. This is the list to take if your opponent doesn't have a ton of stuff on the other side and you need something to hit real hard, to crack an opponent with an iron shell.

The second list is much more mobile, with Darkoaths being much more flexible and disposable, since they can come back from the dead and fight again. It works well against Hordes that you need to outmaneuver and avoid direct combat with.

Both use Be'lakor and his powerful Dark Master ability to neuter the most powerful threat on the other side of the board before dismantling the rest of the list.

Archetype

Severith and Teclis

Thoughts

Within 1 faction, these lists couldn't play any more differently. The Severith list is very mobile, with Windchargers and Severith they can dart around the board, plink away at enemies from safety before charging in for the kill. Gotrek is a fun addition for when you need a real hammer to get in there and cause chaos.

The Teclis list leans on Stoneguard who can move onto objectives and just stand there. By guarding Teclis with Stoneguard, it becomes difficult for opponents to claim objectives back from you while getting pelted by spells and arrows from the rest of the list.

Thoughts

The lack of Magmadroths here is surprising, but it means more infantry can fit in.

Thoughts

Cities of Sigmar are in a really strong position to handle Sylvaneth, especially post-Sylvaneth nerf. Sylvaneth generally try and play cagey, creating denial zones the enemy doesn't want to tread (And slowly expanding this across the board) while Cities castles up, charging only if it has to.

This puts Sylvaneth in a position where their usual strike and fade tactics don't work. If they're charged, Cavaliers will simply charge out of combat. While they can't leave combat, they can position themselves so it's very difficult to maximize damage against them. This leaves Sylvaneth stranded and easy to be picked apart.

Archetype

Freeguild Good Stuff

Thoughts

This is pretty much the platonic ideal for Cities of Sigmar right now. Cavaliers want to be charged, if the enemy charges them the Steelhelms pull all the attacks onto them and the Cavaliers can charge even in combat, meaning their damage isn't blunted by being charged. Even better if there's a better target to pick on nearby.

If they aren't charged, they can still take the initiative. That's fine. Supporting them is shooting units like the Fusiliers and Tahlia who can lead from the front with some nasty damage output and support.

Thoughts

Well, it's a Sons of Behemat list so (at least before we see a Battletome) it's pretty straightforward. King Brodd is an all around good Gargant, a Priest who can fight when it comes down to it. The Scourge of Aqshy Gatebreakers picked up a fun trick where they can bypass enemy screens that might try and slow them down (one of the biggest weaknesses of Gargants) and improve their number of shooting attacks to let them be more effective at range. Still a bit random, but if they roll hot it can be quite effective.
