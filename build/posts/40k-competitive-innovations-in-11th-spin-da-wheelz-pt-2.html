Guard Prio

Thoughts

Well well well! A Guard event win! It's tough times out there for the Astra Militarum but this list dials back time and shoves masses of Scions and infantry bricks. You get the drop antics of Bridgehead and the bring back a unit from Combined Arms, which suits the Prio scoring really nicely. Plenty of units here to run up a passive score early and retaliate brutally from the skies. You're a damned hero for winning an event with the Guard in this environment; well done.

Archetype

Zombie DG

Thoughts

The Hauler/Mortarion/Defiler and Zombies build takes another placing. It scores like a dream, you can't really push into it without getting violated, and it has a great many ways to sneak into unexpected spots to score secondaries with resurrecting Poxwalkers. This is one of my favourite innovations of early 11th edition, and I love to see it continuing to perform!

Allarus Spam

Thoughts

Another podium for the golden bricks, as Allarus see in the last weeks before the big rules change for Custodes. A brilliant and obnoxious list, well done.

Archetype

World Eaters Pressure

Thoughts

Good old fashioned Berzerker Warband takes an event win. This is before their new buffs kick in as well, so all the more impressive! This version is quite mech heavy, with Helbrutes Maulerfiends and Angron offering some real chunk and the means to smash up big targets in one on ones. Other than that it's all about seizing the right moment and launching a huge go-turn. Glorious work winning with the World Eaters in this meta!
