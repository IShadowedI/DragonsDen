Tabletop Battles Reviews: Horus Heresy Combi-Weapons & Shotguns Upgrade Set

July 25th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Behold, a box of guns to hand out to our little 30k Space Marines! This box contains lots of combi-weapons (though not all) and some shotguns. In this article, we’ll look at building these guns and also have a think about what to do with them.

Thanks to Games Workshop for sending us a box of these guns to play with!

Credit: Meltabombed

Credit: Meltabombed

The box contains two of the same sprue, giving you (as the WarCom preview article stated) 2 each one-handed guns and then 10 each of the two-handed ones, as well as a bunch of little gubbins. Gubbins are cool, who doesn’t like extra ammo pouches and whatnot? This seems like pretty good value since it gives you plenty of options for a mix and match squad or 5 full squads of mono-builds.

Meltabombed: This seems like the only real 'miss' of this set in that it seems lame to have buy 5 boxes to fit out of a full unit of Veteran Breachers with shotguns, but I concede this could be a solely 'me' problem. At least it's possible, albeit expensive. If you don't have use for your one-hand shotguns, hit ya boy up! 

The muzzle break holes on the bolter portions of all the guns are also already drilled (i.e. cast that way), which is a really nice touch and shows that GW's casting technology is ever-expanding. Even the really small holes in the shotgun barrels only require a quick twirl of the ol' hobby knife to open them up; nice.

Notably missing from the set are the basic combi-bolter, combi-grav, combi-grenade launcher, and combi-disintegrator. We theorize that there might be some kind of box in the future to fill in the rest of the combi-weapons, possibly nemesis and kraken bolters as well; a man can dream. We’ll just have to wait to see! Don’t forget; combi-disintegrators already have their own sprue, though it’s always possible we could see them expanded in the future to include more in a box; right now you only get 4 in the full disintegrator box.

Meltabombed: I’m a fan of these combi-weapons, I love the choice to make the bolters top-fed. Not only is it a retro, somewhat esoteric choice that fits the Heresy aesthetic well, it also feels sleek and military. The old blocky Tigrus combi-weapons always felt so clunky and awkward looking in the hands of the legionnaires holding them. Great upgrade! And the shotguns, oh MAN! What a sexy piece of hardware! These are absolutely gorgeous and I love them. Absolutely a huge upgrade from the old resin, ‘Deliverance’ pattern, ‘trench gun’ style shotguns that FW sold as a set and with the old resin Recon marines. I have been a long-time Astartes shotgun stan, and the fact that Veteran Breachers can all take them now fills me with great joy. 

We also wanted to point out that the single-handed weapons, while absolutely usable for breachers, could also be used on terminator units. As you can see, it would take a bit of trimming, but the hands are the same size as those of the new Cataphractii terminators; that didn’t used to be the case. I did not have the chance to put this into practice, but figured you all would appreciate the observation and size comparison. 

New Combi-weapon on top, New Cataphractii Volkite Charger beneath Credit: Meltabombed

The Build

The build for these guns is about as straight forward as they could be, as the only gun that takes multiple parts is the combi-volkite where you have to glue the other side of the little volkite muzzle onto the bottom. Otherwise, all the guns are a single piece which is really nice. 

Since these combi-weapons are essentially just a 1:1 swap-in for the bolters or special weapons the base models would otherwise be carrying, adding them onto your existing models is pretty easy. The instructions do a good job of breaking down each pose per armor Mk and what part goes where, which is a nice quality of life update. 

Meltabombed: Seeing these gorgeous weapons, I decided to get a start on a unit I’d been meaning to make for my Thousand Sons for a while, an Ammitara Cabal. Yes, I know this model is in power armor and the Ammitara are in scout/recon armor, don’t @ me. I want this unit to be able to fill multiple battlefield roles since I intended to put a decent amount of conversion work into it. Ammitara, Veterans, Scouts, Recons, Seekers, etc.

Credit: Meltabombed

To that end, I made the last minute decision to magnetise his hands to be able to swap between shotguns, combi-weapons, or possibly even future proofing for kraken or nemesis bolters. This was a pretty easy prospect using 1mm round magnets in both sides of both wrists, but you could probably get away with one if you’re short on magnet money. I will say though, the double magnet bond is really solid and feels very satisfying to swap. For more on how to magnetize your miniatures, check out the Hobby 102 article and video here.  

Credit: Meltabombed

Credit: Meltabombed

Credit: Meltabombed

Painting

Meltabombed: When I got the box, I was going to paint both this Ammitara and maybe one or two other models. But I was a younger man back then; full of ambition, hopes, dreams. Alas, I was only able to get the Ammitara primed after sculpting his demi-cloak and magnetizing his hands; behold. 

Credit: Meltabombed

Credit: Meltabombed

Instead, I decided to paint up a Veteran Breacher for my Traitor Ultramarines as their scheme is dead easy and I can easily crank one out in a single evening. I also absolutely had to use the one-handed shotgun somewhere, right? Plus, who doesn’t love a gold-plated shotgun? Where my early-2010’s Call of Duty boys at? Anyway… 

Ultramarines 216th Company Veteran Breacher with Shotgun Credit: Meltabombed

The shotgun looks suitably menacing and looks fantastic paired with the breacher shield! Would love to have at least a 5 man unit of these guys running around. And no, he's not a Warhound; realsnice already has those covered. I didn't have the chance to get all of his transfers one there; it's a time-consuming process, okay?

Mini-Tactica: Combi-flamers, Combi-meltas, Combi-volkites, and Shotguns

Just to add a bit more to this read than “new guns cool”, a few of us had a chat about these guns from a tactics POV, and we came up with some ways to make use of this box set in your own armies!

Combi-weapons 

We agreed there are a few categories for them, with many of these guns working best for just one or two models in a unit rather than the whole lot. You can have something on a Sergeant or character to cause statuses, for example. You can also look at giving whole units of Veterans some kind of gun but that gets expensive quickly. E.g. 10 points for a combi-volkite vs 2 for a volkite charger - so you're paying 8 points for the bolter attachment, gaining a couple more shots but losing assault.

For veterans, 10 points for a combi-melta is an interesting comparison with 15 points for a melta. After all, how often do you get a second shot with a melta squad anyway? 5 vets with combi-meltas are 135 compared to 115 for a tactical support squad with proper melta guns. The Vets have BS5, 2W and Line 1, which are all pretty awesome. The choice may come down to whether it’s easier to unlock an Elite or a Support slot for your army.

Sadly, we didn’t think the same applied for other combi-weapons. 135 for 5 combi-flamers or plasma guns is a poor trade with just 65 for a Tactical Support Squad with flamers or 90 for plasma guns.

The best use for the combi-flamer or grav is probably on Sergeants and Characters. Having your Tactical Squad Sergeant inflict a Panic test on an enemy unit to chase them off an objective (assuming they haven’t already reacted away to avoid Vanguard) could make a lot of difference to your points - if it works. Grav only inflicts Pinning but has much better range and it still prevents the enemy contesting.

The Combi-grav is interesting because we don’t have a unit that can take all that many grav guns, usually. There might be a scenario where firing a load of breaching blasts with a built in status could be game-winning but that would be pretty rare. It’s a fun option though, and one that you’ll sadly need to wait for.

Usually a combi-weapon only gets one shot, but Volkite and the Grenade Launchers are not Limited and can fire all game. Neither is great though. Combi-Volkite don't have assault, and krak grenades don't have many obvious targets with only S6 and AP4, though are at least D2.

Shotguns

Just for your reference and as far as we (let's be honest, I, meltabombed, the shotgun stan) could find, the list of generic units that have access to shotguns as a choice are thus: 

  • Recon Marines
  • Scouts
  • Outriders
  • Veteran Tactical Marines 
  • Veteran Breachers
  • Praetorian and Centurion Command squads
  • Praetors, Centurions, Optaes, Primus Medicae, Delegatus, Warmongers, and Pathfinders

Shotguns are kind of worse than volkite chargers, except that can Stun. Both are 2ppm and Assault. Shotguns are S4 with Stun (0) while Volkite Chargers are S5 and Deflagrate (5). The ideal might be a mix of the two so you apply statuses but don’t lose out on too much output. 

Shotguns are also an interesting choice for characters, where available. Now your character is a status machine. Similarly, a few shotguns sprinkled into a veteran unit or on a character are nice because you only need to hit to inflict the Stun check. One possible combo could be a praetorian command squad with jump packs deep striking in with combi-weapons and an attached telepathy librarian and just statusing the snot out of whatever is around them, following up with some charges the next turn. 

Special Cases

Word Bearers get more mileage out of shotguns and combi-weapons that inflict statuses because of the Fear (1) they often bring to the table. It’s no fun to be on the receiving end of a Gal Vorbak squad with flamers, and this would be very similar. Throwing a combi-grav or flamer on a tactical sergeant nearby one of their native Fear(X) toting units could be gnarly to deny scoring, or a nice one-two punch to soften something up for a charge. Veterans or Veteran Breachers piling out of a spartan with bolter sponsons, a hull heavy flamer, and a pintle havoc launcher nearby a Gal Vorbak unit, Mhara Gal, or Overseer would be a pretty effective (though expensive) status machine.

Shotguns and combi-weapons could get more mileage in Zone Mortalis due to the shorter ranges, more protracted melees, and Blind Panic. Obviously, the tight quarters and shorter sight lines make getting shotguns into range a bit easier, and the units carrying them (Veteran Breachers and maybe Command Squads) will have more sticking power after launching a volley with them (though, admittedly, won’t be doing much damage afterwards.) Blind Panic makes it so that any unit that Falls Back to within 2” of a friendly unit that has a status forces both units to take a Leadership check or gain the Routed Status. So, if you’re handing out potential Stun statuses with shotguns, this could potentially cascade in spectacular fashion. 

Final Thoughts

These weapons are super cool, if only from a modeling perspective. Hopefully, we’ve shown that there are some tactical uses for them, but for the most part they are long-missing collection of equipment to make your Heresy forces look more interesting and utilitarian!
