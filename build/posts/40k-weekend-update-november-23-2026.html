[40k] Weekend Update: November 23, 2026

November 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome to another 40K weekend update put together by Rob and Isaac. This week we had a drop in teams events as singles finally took top billing again as we lead into the generally slower holiday months for 40K, but at least for this week there were plenty of events to look at and break down for our readers. 

Jack Murphy Wins the Games Workshop Atlanta Open GT

The weekend’s largest singles event was the GW US Open Major in Atlanta, with 175 players competing to win top honors and a golden ticket to the 2027 World Championships of Warhammer. Jack pulled off a staggeringly dominant performance, going 8-0 with his Darkflight Pursuit Dark Angels army and scoring a perfect 800 battle points in the process, including a 100-42 win in the finals over Justin Mooneyham.

Felix Forsmark Takes Down Fantasia Fanatic L 40k 

Based in Sweden this event had a whopping 160 players over only five round leading to an insane five players with a 5-0 record! The winner on tie breaker was Felix Forsmark who brought it home with a Dark Angels list just like Jack Murphy, but with a slightly different twist. Taking Assualt Brethren instead of Iron Storm this list leans into the melee side of Dark Angels a bit more, bringing the Lion and another big Outrider brick instead of the plethora of speeders found in the Murphy list. Bellow we will make sure to mention all the other lists snubbed on tie breakers (fun fact they all chose to play Take and Hold, maybe that disposition is good or something).

  • Felix Forsmark - Dark Angels
  • Rickard Wallin - Adeptus Mechanicus
  • Sami Sjolander - T'au Empire
  • Mattias Östling - Space Marines (Astartes)
  • Benjamin Hyvönen - Space Marines (Astartes)

Broadsword Wargaming 40K Seasonal Major X Won by Mark Morrow

This 99 player event out of Ireland was won by Mark Morrow on a Purge the Foe Orks list built out of Blitz Brigade, Bully Boyz and Wreckas. Reminiscent of some of the early powerhouse Ork lists, but with a splash of Nazdrag and Mega Nobz, he crushed the competition on tie breakers. Both Mateusz Oswiecinsk with Take and Hold Space Marines, and Shane Porter’s Chaos Knights in Reconnaissance also came in with 5-0 records for an underaged podium sweep.

MIDEA TIBICENAS Dominate GT GIPUZKOA 2026 

The largest teams event of the weekend after a smaller showing was a four man team event hosted over five rounds in Spain. With a 112 players it is just a medium sized teams event, especially in comparison to some of the recent teams outings. The winning team MIDEA TIBICENAS took it down with a 5-0 record. Congrats to:

  • Alberto Rodríguez Guerra
  • Eduardo Cortell
  • Jorge Hoyos Aleman
  • Nelson WiRm

Throwdown at the Campground 5 Crushed by Cascadia Free Company

Found in the PNW of the US this 80 player four round event was the largest eight man teams event this weekend. Taken down by the Cascadia Free Company with a perfect 4-0 record. No other team even touched an undefeated record, making them the undisputed champions. Congrats to:

  • Alex Pina
  • Chris Pina
  • Ian Swallow
  • Joshua Howard
  • Ken Bush
  • Lukas Troller
  • Nicolas Ohlsen-Johnson
  • Tanner Hebert

Red Waaagh! Snatch Victory at I Asturian Team Championship 

This second Spanish event was only sixty players over five round, but came in as the third largest teams outing of the weekend. There were two undefeated teams, Red Waaagh! and Crisis de los 40k, but Red Waaagh! edged it out with a 4-0-1 record compare to second places 3-0-2 record. Congrats to:

  • Alberto Rey
  • Daniel Velasco
  • David "Daweed"
  • Eduardo Garmilla
  • Leon Lodbrok
Credit: Greg Chiasson

Stats: The Haves and Have Nots

Statistically this week was very strange. Only three factions -- Marines, Custodes, and Dark Angels -- broke a 50% win rate. Everything else was falling below that middle ground. Notably Custodes were able to grab an event win which we predicted last week when pointing to their climbing win rate and TiWP. While generic Marines “only” won two events they had a socking 17 TiWP players and 7 undefeated. It’s really a marine world and we are just living in it.

Everything in the marine book has turned out to really just overtuned. With the wider marine book seeing much more play after the nerf to Deathwatch, it has really become clear how deep the book is in comparison to 10th Codexes and even Orks and Custodes. There is a busted build in basically every corner of the codex, marine players just get to pick how to break it based on personal preference.

The bottom of the barrel is most of the barrel with basically every faction dropping below 50% and nine of them being below 45%. Since basically every faction loses against marines and everyone is playing them, it’s pretty much a dead meta. Drukahri took the bottom spot this week with a horrid 35% win rate. We see Emperor's Children, CSM, Chaos Knights, and Imperial Agents rounding out the bottom five, none of which are huge surprises. We are in a desperate need of a new slate to salvage this marine apocalypse, which I hope will take drastic action.

GW’s 2026 Grand Narrative Concludes

The 2026 Grand Narrative came to a close on Sunday, capping off a three-day, six-game event with more than 350 players meeting up - often in costume - to determine the fate of the planet Ferrum’s Wake and its would-be tyrant, Kalen Voss. The story of the planet was that Voss was a marine who defected from the Red Corsairs to take a planet for himself (Ferrum’s Wake), attempting to usher in radical freedom for its people with a lot of “Space Ron Paul” vibes. Voss was hunted by Admiral Morn of the Imperium, a daemonic entity named The Warrant claiming to serve Huron Blackheart, and a Corsair named Arathiel. The campaign’s ultimate conclusion saw Voss kill the Warrant, then be killed by Arathiel as he reclaimed the soulstone of a departed friend. Meanwhile Morn and Imperial players ended up teaming up to help a young woman named Mara Venn escape the planet with her refugee people, delivering them from the planet’s increasingly worse conditions. Venn ended up being a Genestealer operative, and is now on a ship bound for Terra.

Nassim Fouchane, from our previous profile

Player Focus: Nassim Fouchane

If you were following the Art of War UK event this weekend you probably saw Innes and Nassim play a great game in the finals on stream. While Nas did take the loss he is not someone to be taken lightly. Currently number #6 on the WGR track and #9 on Stat Check ELO, he is someone to watch at WCW this year. While known primarily as a Imperial Guard player Nas has been recently been making a name as a Space Marine menace, taking down two separate super majors with them this year. I wouldn’t be shocked to see him perform very well with his Iron Hands at WCW with a shot at number one WGR with the right draw.

Alongside our coverage of the whats-what of 40k each week, Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson joins us to provide some quick updates on the movers and shakers in the competitive scene of some of the other games we cover here at Tabletop Battles!

Seraphon Battletome on the Horizon

The long-awaited Battletome for the Seraphon was announced for preview this Sunday on Warhammer Community, rounding out one of the final Battletome releases for the current edition of Age of Sigmar.

The new release comes with a new hero model for the Seraphon, Blessed Tetraxi, Prophet of Tepok, a Skink oracle mounted atop a great Pterasaur. Aside from Tetraxi, the battletome release doesnt include any new models, but does include a new Separhead, the Skink-focused Claws of Tepok, a separate release of the Sunblood Pack from Underworlds, and a new Regiment of renown featuring two Stegadons for use by other Order armies.

Alongside the new Seraphon book, a new Path to Glory book was also announced for Sundays preview, titled The Grymmpeaks. The book centers on a conflict between the Duardin in the dark halls under the mountains of the mortal realms, and includes new rules for fighting in these underground lairs.

Both the new Seraphon Battletome and The Grymmpeaks are set to go live for preorders this upcoming Saturday.

Age of Sigmar Battlescroll Codifies Worlds Changes

Age of Sigmar and Underworlds both received balance updates last week, in line with the changes we saw from the Worlds Balance update released earlier this month.

For Sigmar, this update is overall fairly light handed, handing out only a handful of changes for a handful of factions. The Ossiarch Bonereapers get some tweaks to their army rules so that the costs of Relentless Discipline stay the same when used on reinforced units, Gotrek’s Regiment of Reknown was adjusted in points cost following the new Khazalite holds release, and Some clarifications were made on some frequently asked questions, such as with the Kharadron Airships in the new Khazalite book.

For Underworlds, we see some updates to the restricted card list, focused on cards that were turbo-boosting a fighter’s damage output into the stratosphere. Ideally, restricting these cards would reduce the offensive spikes early in matches to even out the experience. Alongside this, Bladecatcher was sent to the forsaken list, which effectively bans this very powerful card from matched play.

Changes to both of these systems reflect the changes we saw from the Worlds balance update, which at the time it was published was only official for Worlds. Now that these balance updates have been posted, they can be used for all events for AoS and Underworlds, with no confusion on whether they’re official or not.

Sylvaneth Take Top Prize at Renegade Open

With worlds on the horizon, things are fairly quiet in the mortal realms this past weekend, with only a handful of large GT events to take note of.

The largest of the weekend was the Renegade Open GT, hosted in Minneapolis. This 5 round event had 50 players, and had one true undefeated player at the end of the event. Daniel Dowell took the top prize home, continuing the Sylvaneth big heroes list’s dominant run in the Sigmar metagame. Dowel’s list does differentiate itself from its peers by relying on less heroes than other lists, eschewing favorites like Drycha for more units and a brick of Kurnoth Hunters, but Alarielle remains an incredible anchor for the army as a whole, providing both top tier support and damage output when needed.

Other large events over the weekend included the Realmshift Series 2026- Chapter 3, a GT as a part of a larger GT league in the UK, which was won by Tavi Gane piloting a wide Seraphon list supported by strong casting. Alongside this event was Fantasia Fanatic in Sweden, a 30 player event won by Marcus Persson and his Daughters of Khaine list that combined Morathi-Khaine and Krethusa for some Khainite herohammer.

It's an interesting peek into what we can expect at worlds, and while several factions have strong showings and potential, it appears that Sylvaneth is a clear favorite coming into the largest event of the year.

Star Wars Legion November Update Introduces Flyers

Not to be outdone by Warhammer, Star Wars Legion released a large-scale balance update last week, providing rules updates for various factions in the game alongside new rules.

The most notable new rules added for Legion is the addition of how Flying Vehicles work in the game, in order to prepare for the release of the new flyer models teased earlier this year like the TIE Fighter and X-Wing. There are plenty of other important additions alongside these flyer rules though, including to how priority is determined, Cunning and Interrogate rules tweaks, and the overhaul to bounty hunter units like Cad Bane and IG 88.

As far as balance changes go, the Galactic Empire received some big points changes across the board to improve the faction’s internal balance, and the Separatist Alliance and Grand Army of the Republic both received some shots in the arm to boost their performance against their peers.

Overall, between the balance changes and new updates, Star Wars Legion looks to be in a great place for the end of the year and going into 2027, continuing the exciting momentum of the game its received since its new edition.
