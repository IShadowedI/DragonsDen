[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-08-05 (Part 1)

August 5th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Welcome back, this week is a bit more Jam packed than usual, with 6 events. So today we'll hit the American events and come back tomorrow for the European events.

Thoughts

Seraphon haven't been having a good go of things lately and here seems no different. Troggs have the ability to tank a lot of damage which works really well going into the mortal wounds that Seraphon can push out. Even their cav just will struggle to crack the damage threshold for them, which likely made it much easier for the troggs to just sit on objectives and chill.

Archetype

Troggs

Thoughts

Troggs are tough, that remains true no matter what. With the new season, they're also a great candidate for Unleash The Fury with their Damage 3 weapons (and they got the wounds to shake it off if they roll some unlucky 1s). So the central pillar here is the Troggs, under the right phases of the moon they reduce Rend by 1 and have a 5+ ward built in. Throw in the shooters which can inflict -1 to hit in combat and all the annoying debuffs Gitz have access to and it becomes a real pain in the ass to actually do enough damage to these guys. Then they clap back with the full force of their clubs and just sit on objectives.

Archetype

Midrange

Thoughts

Honestly this list has got a bit of everything. Looking at the new Aqshy stuff, the Namarti Reavers can help set up for the charge by shooting a target and if they can remove even one model they can inflict -1 to wards or ignore positive modifiers to saves. Even if they don't, 3+ isn't bad odds either. Then the Thralls can charge in with support from the tidecaster, giving Crit 2 hits. Coming up behind the Allopexes can offer some fire to weaken targets from afar and then charge in with the Ishalaen Guard if they need something with a bit of a stronger hammer.

The remaining Heroes and Leviadon offer strong buffs, while Callis and Toll drop in to offer support to deal with Heroes by dropping in between enemy lines.

Thoughts

(Since the Ogor Mawtribes book isn't available outside the box set right now, this is the old rules.)

Getting this far with Beastclaw Raiders and putting up a good fight into a solidly stacked Soulblight list is quite impressive. The Vengorian Lords are basically designed to fight big monsters like this and the Zombies and Askurgan Trueblades pose a challenge for being huge tarpits and monster killers, respectively.

That said, a strong Beastclaw Charge is still quite scary and if they can properly time it that can give them a lot of early momentum. Even if they can't hold it for long, this is a solid scoring game.

Result

Soulblight Gravelords Victory - 61 - 41

Neferata, the Mortarch of Blood. Credit: chimp

See Showdown

Archetype

Nefereta and the Vengorian Lords

Thoughts

The emphasis on Monsters here largely plays into the Unleash Your Fury with some strong damage attacks. Nefereta leads by inflicting a -1 to hit on enemies she's in combat with and lets attacks targeting a non-Monster ignore modifiers to save. The twin Vengorian lords work well together, with Lauka Vai adding bonus attacks and the generic one in its SoA form can redirect infantry units to lock them down into combat.

Another piece of tech that's become popular for Deadwalker Zombies is to give them the Never-dead buff. This effectively gives them a 5+ Invulnerable save and so for 120 pts for 20 that is pound for pound your best tarpit available. Even if they die they might do some mortal wounds back so you kinda win in the end anyway.

Thoughts

Genuinely an impressive fight here. Boingrot Bounders and Squig Hoppers can Fly and do a lot of Mortals on impact, which allows them  to bypass the shieldwall from the Hashutite spears to deal with the ranged weapons, and make a solid impact on the Dominators Engines (who with their 2+ Save and fairly low wound count, fear mortal wound spam).

Given the gap it seems like the Gitz got an early lead and charged ahead. Congrats to Fredrick on this surprising win.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Squigs

Thoughts

Go fast and don't stop. Basically this list is a glass cannon, it charges in and hopes to do a ton of mortal wounds before it gets blown up. Under the right moon sign, they can substitute a 4 for their random rolls which gives them a respectable 10" (12" for Hoppers) move with fly. Accompanying them is Droggz who gives a -1 to hit aura that helps keep them alive and the Spider is a decent Combat wizard that can inflict strike last on troublesome enemy heroes.

Genuinely a surprising hodge-podge of a list. Seems fun to play though.
