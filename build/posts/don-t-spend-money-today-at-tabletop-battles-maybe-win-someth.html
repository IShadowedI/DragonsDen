Don't Spend Money Today at Tabletop Battles, Maybe Win Something Anyway

November 27th, 2026 via Goonhammer

It's Black Friday, and we'd love if you subscribe to the website at any tier, but what we're offering you today isn't gonna cost you any money at all.

We're starting monthly giveaways at the Tabletop Battles Patreon! How that works is this: You sign up for the Patreon at any tier, including the free membership -- no purchase necessary! -- as a resident of the United States or the United Kingdom (sorry to our Canadian, Australian, European, and worldwide friends). I'll announce a deadline, and a couple days later, I'll pull the patron list from the site, do a random draw, and one lucky qualified patron will win something of fairly significant value! To give you an idea of the kind of item we're giving away, here's the first one:

A 2026 Warhammer 40K Combat Patrol of your choice!

That's a $177 value that you could be getting shipped to you for free just for being a patron at any level of Tabletop Battles, including the free membership. We're going to be doing the draw for this on Friday, December 4th -- I will be pulling the patron list on that morning -- so there's even an outside chance we can get this to you in time for Christmas, though no guarantees on that especially if you're a UK winner.

In addition to being put on the list for great prizes every month, you get the benefits of whatever tier you join on Patreon -- that's Administratum support that scales across your pledge, that's three subscriber-only competitive 40K articles a week; we offer a great value for your money! But to be eligible for that Combat Patrol, and all our future giveaways, you don't have to spend any at all. Happy Black Friday.
