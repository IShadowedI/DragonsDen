11th Edition Detachment Focus: Blood Brothers Auxilia

October 1st, 2026 via Goonhammer
In this series of articles we take a deep dive into a specific detachment for a faction, covering the faction’s rules and upgrades and talking about how to build around that faction for competitive play. In this article, we’re covering the Brood Brothers Auxilia Detachment for Genestealer Cults. 

Brood Brothers is one of the most talked about lore sub factions for Genestealer Cults, but unfortunately their recent rules set in the GSC Codex hasn’t lived up to that hype.

Detachment Cost: 2 DP

Force Disposition: Take and Hold

While I’m not the biggest fan of Brood Brothers as a Detachment in this Codex, I will say I think that Take and Hold is one of the better dispositions you could run for this army. Between two armies you have some of the cheapest 20 man units you could find so just having a list that is an Objective Control stat check with a bunch of bodies that just sit and hold points then T&H is your spot to be.

Detachment Rule: Brood Brothers

To short hand the allies part, you can include up to 1000 points of Astra Militarum units in the list but none of them can be your Warlord and you can’t include Aircraft, Commissar, Epic Hero, Militarum Tempstus, Ogryn, Ratling, Tech-Priest Enginseer, and Ministorum Priest units. It is also worth noting that your army rule is still Cult Ambush not Voice of Command so you don’t get access to orders outside of units that can specifically order themselves like Kasrkin. This is a fine part and basically what we’ve seen for most “soup” Detachments in the 10th edition codices. I normally wouldn’t recommend 1000 points of Guard as GSC really has a lot of internal synergies that need to work to make the army function and it’s hard to slot in Guard into that, but there are certainly pieces to use here.

Detachment Rule: Integrated Tactics

Continuing on to the second part of the rule, in the Shooting phase each time an Astra Miliatrum unit is selected to shoot it can select an enemy unit within 18” and visible to it. It can only target that unit with its attacks this phase but that enemy is ‘caught in overlapping fire’ until the end of the phase. While a unit is caught in overlapping fire, each time a Genestealer Cults model from your army targets that enemy unit with a ranged attack, add 1 to the hit roll. This is a lot of words to say if a Guard model shoots one thing within 18” then GSC gets +1 to hit in the shooting phase. Even then it’s a lackluster rule as GSC is already an army that needs a lot of stacking buffs through multiple activations to get a strong output and adding another layer for that is rough especially since it will only be active for ranged attacks. As well, the ranged units that can take advantage of this are exactly Neophyte Hybrids and Achilles Ridgerunners, which isn’t the deepest range of threats. Overall it’s another hoop to jump through, but it does exist so it’s something we can use.

Enhancements

I’m doing my best here to be positive about this Detachment, and while some of these rules have in fact gotten a bit better in the new edition it’s still a pile of oddities that I don’t know if I would go out of my way to take.

  • Fire-Point Commander (10pts): GSC Infantry model only. Each time you target the bearer’s unit with the Fire Overwatch Stratagem, while resolving that Stratagem, hits are scored on an unmodified hit roll of a 5+. This is pretty decent as it basically affords you a secondish shooting activation with your heavy weapons that normally hit on 5s anyways. That being said, while Neophytes with a Benefictus are the ideal target for this, I’m not super sold; I would usually prefer to overwatch with the bounty of flamers we can still field, so I think it is list dependent.
  • Adaptive Reprisal (15pts): GSC Infantry model only. When you target this unit with the Heroic Intervention Stratagem, that use is -1CP. This would be great if this Detachment did anything in melee. I can see it going on an Iconward Neophyte squad or maybe an Abominant with Aberrants, with either unit just there to help bog down attacks, but it is still something I’m not super excited for. That being said, it does allow you to do the 6” heroic on turn 1 if your opponent goes first, which can help deter some very aggressive turn 1 move blocks.
  • Martial Espionage (25pts): GSC Infantry model only. Once per turn when a friendly Astra Militarum Infantry or Mounted unit within 9” of the bearer is selected to shoot, the bearer can use this enhancement and give those ranged attacks +1 AP until the end of the phase. Actually pretty good, I won’t lie; especially with the cover changes in 11th edition no longer giving an AP buff, you actually can make a large volume of attacks functionally hurt a lot more this edition, which is nice. I’ve had this in a few lists I’ve run and a wounded Krieg squad that revives its meltas and plasmas to then shoot an enemy with +1 to hit, wound, and AP is a randomly scary threat that can catch people off guard.
  • The Hero Returned (20pts): GSC Infantry model only. Improve the Leadership and Objective Control characteristics of models in the bearer’s unit by 1. Actually a crazy good strat when paired with extra healing from the Xenocult Detachment and an Iconward for a 5+ Feel No Pain. Now you can bog down the center objective with a load of 3OC Neophytes that have a Feel No Pain and regenerate 6-9 models. I think this is a steal.

Stratagems

Two of these don’t currently work and one only works on a 3+ dice roll. I am not a fan but I must tell you about them against the better judgement of my soul.

  • A Dark Network (1CP): During the reinforcement step of your opponent's movement phase, just after an enemy unit is set up on the battlefield, one friendly GSC or Astra Militarum unit within 12”of that enemy unit can make a normal move of 6”. Well, the reinforcement step no longer exists, thusly this stratagem does not have a legal timing window. You can house rule it to ignore that, but RAW this doesn’t work. That being said, when it did work it was pretty decent in some matchups and irrelevant into others.
  • Symbiotic Destruction (1CP): One Astra Militarum and one GSC unit get re-roll 1s to wound but must shoot the same visible target that they are both within range of. This is actually really good and something I would say is worth using a lot to handle the tougher bricks of your opponent’s army. Giving the aforementioned Kriegers reroll 1s to wound while they might have their +1 to hit/wound active is good, and Neos have enough volume that this is going to be affecting a lot of dice. That being said, it does lock these units into only shooting the one unit, so there is no split firing and if one unit kills it by themselves then the other unit cannot shoot anything else that phase.
  • Acceptable Losses (2CP): In your shooting phase, one Astra Militarum unit in your army can shoot at an enemy unit that is within engagement range of a GSC unit from your army. After resolving those attacks, roll one d6 for each GSC unit that was in engagement range of that enemy unit: on a 5+, that GSC unit takes D3+1 mortal wounds. This is pretty costly at D3+1 mortals, but I think overall this is more than fine and can even help free up a unit that is being tagged up by just a few chaff models from an opponent’s army. So, situational but not something that is a waste of a Stratagem slot.
  • Regimental Reinforcements (1CP): During your opponent’s Shooting phase or the fight phase when an Astra Militarum Infantry Regiment unit is destroyed. Roll a d6: on a 3+ add a new unit to roster that is identical to the destroyed unit at Starting Strength and place it into Cult Ambush. You can only use this stratagem once per battle. I trimmed a bit of the fat explaining it but essentially when they kill one of your Guard units, spend a CP to roll a 2 to never be able to waste a CP on this stratagem for the game. That’s right, even if you fail the 3+ you cannot use this stratagem again later on to try and get back at least one unit during the game which is a really big let down. When it does work, however, it’s still pretty good and can really help with horde style lists that want to run as many bodies as possible as bringing back 20 Krieg with this is akin to bringing 20 Neos and that’s 6 whole Resurgence Points. I’m torn on this strat because I would like it honestly to just be 2CP and automatically go off but we are here. Use it if you’re playing swarm or want more Kasrkin for more mortals.
  • Suppress and Overwhelm (1CP): In your shooting phase just after an Astra Militarum unit has shot an enemy unit. Select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks and until the end of the turn that enemy unit cannot be the target of the Fire Overwatch Stratagem and each time that unit is charged by a GSC unit, the GSC unit gets to re-roll the Charge roll. That’s a lot of words to say, “Your GSC units get to re-roll charges against something a Guard unit shot” because the first half of this stratagem doesn’t work anymore, although it didn’t really work in 10th either, but I digress. Yeah this is bad because you also have no benefits in the fight phase from this Detachment, so charging enemy units is just doing so with unsupported GSC melee units and I’m not the biggest fan of that.
  • In the Shadows of Iron (1CP): When an enemy unit ends a move within 8” of a cult ambush marker you can move that marker to be 8” away from enemy models and within 6” of an Astra Militarum Vehicle unit from your army. I almost always feel nothing when I think about the ambush marker stratagems from GSC Detachments. You don’t really need to do this but I guess it can be cute in the perfect scenarios.

After playing several games with this Detachment, I will let you know the most used stratagems were: Explosives, Rapid Ingress, and Go To Ground Heroic Intervention. I hope that illustrates just how impactful these six stratagems are. Small sidenote: It’s been three dataslates since the new edition dropped; how are there two entire stratagems that just do not work still? Is it genuinely acceptable to just not care so much about a faction that you can’t be bothered to update rules that try to function with incorrect timings or in phases that no longer exist? Can GW stop fumbling GSC and actually support the faction in a meaningful way instead of just giving us scraps and nerfing our points whenever someone finally does well with them? What would happen if Gladius went through three dataslates and a third of its rules still just didn’t work? I’ll end it there because I don’t want to come off as unprofessional but I genuinely care about this faction and even though Brood Brothers isn’t the sub-faction for me, the people who do enjoy it should have their time, money, and energy that they put into the hobby respected.

Playing This Detachment

Brood Brothers is a bit of a mess and its rules aren’t the cleanest, but this faction holds a very cherished spot in the hearts of a lot of GSC and Guard players, figuring into everything from fun creative builds that feature Aberrants running alongside a Baneblade at your local game store to the sweatiest of games where I’m dropping 250 bodies onto the table and making my opponent experience actual trench warfare. The Detachment doesn’t do enough, but the changes to 11th and the access it has to Detachments like Xenocult do give it a bit of a facelift and can actually solve its problem of zero damage. That being said, I think that what we have here needs a fundamental rewrite as the lists you make in this are most often just better off as either GSC or Astra Militarum and that doesn’t feel good.

Strengths

  • Diversity. Kind of scraping the bottom of the barrel here but the fact that this detachment gives you 2 army ranges to pull from does mean that the styles of lists you can make varies greatly and I’ve see a lot of different lists that are teched to handle different archetypes.
  • Objective Control. When I ran this list to a 5-0 finish it included 120 Death Korps of Krieg and 120 Neophyte Hybrids that flooded the board and stopped my opponents from ever holding the point. I think that you can still very much do that today, but it’s going to be a challenge for sure

Weaknesses

  • Rules. The Detachment brings you 1000 points of Guard and two broken stratagems to support your shooting side of GSC. There are no melee buffs here and you can’t even run Ogryn next to Aberrants to make a thick bruiser style list. 

A Sample List

I’m sure you will be shocked to discover that Brood Brothers Auxilia doesn’t have the biggest showing of results. I will say that there is still some room to be played with a variant I affectionately call “Brood Bolt,” where you focus on bringing units that focus on dealing Mortal Wounds through Melta Mines and Demolition Charges to whittle your opponents down to let the Xenocult side of your army finish them off. It’s a very fun list and if you do want to get into trying out Brood Brothers I’d say give it a try!

Final Thoughts

I’ll be very honest and open here, for a long time I have really talked down on this Detachment saying it wasn’t good and should be avoided. I think those statements were misguided and unproductive. When this Detachment works together it does feel really cool and it genuinely can be a lot of fun and that should be encouraged. One of the most deeply ingrained parts about the Astra Militarum and Genestealer Cults is their complicated history with one another and I think a Detachment that represents that coalition should have the actual support and respect for its rules like every other Detachment and army does. However, at time of writing I don’t think that is accomplished and it’s not the failure of the player for it being in this state. I'd urge the team that works on the next GSC Codex to really take time and understand how and why this version of the Detachment failed so that you can better learn from it and succeed next time. I want to sing your praises, so please give me something worth doing so.
