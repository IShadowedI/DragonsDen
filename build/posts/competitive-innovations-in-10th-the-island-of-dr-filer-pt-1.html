Thoughts

Our first winner of the week is very much riding the end of the Edition vibes, combining Defilers with what is very much a greatest hits of Chaos up till now. Archetype

Crisis Toolbox

Thoughts

Retaliation T'au take second place, going for a Crisis toolbox build with very flexible output, and some strong trading plays into the Defiler menace. The Sunforge Coldstar unit projects a pretty large area that's unsafe for crab-themed robots, while Farsight's squad can rock in from free Ingress to line up solid angles, and both units run you less than the price tag of their favoured targets. Definitely a unit whose time has come - big centrepieces with 5+ Invulnerables and an achievable number of wounds are the perfect prey. The rest of what's here is all the T'au hits - Riptides, Stealths, you know the stuff - it points at people and kills them. Well done to Mike.

Starshatter C'tan

Thoughts

Rounding out our top four - the previous metagame monster, still somewhat kicking in the new metagame. Four big -1D threats with massive damage output sweep the table, with great utility and Character support, it's still pretty good! Defiler builds tend to beat this because they get way more other stuff, but it's not a completely unwinnable game either, and Matthew did slay a triple crab CSM build early in the tournament only to eventually fall to Clay in the final. Well done on 4th place!

Prep time: 10 mins Cook time: 20-25 mins Temp: 400°F

Thoughts
