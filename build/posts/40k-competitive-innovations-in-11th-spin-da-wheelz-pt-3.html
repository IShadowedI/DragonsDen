[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Spin Da Wheelz pt.3

October 10th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

It's mostly out with the old today, as we've got lots of pre-update superstars, but there's one post-update event to sink our teeth into, and some interesting dynamics in the showdown, so let's get to it.

Thoughts

Not a huge amount to say on this one because it's a pre-balance list and we all know 135pts Dakkarigs was, shall we say, generous. Congratulations Jonah.

Archetype

Mauler Coterie

Thoughts

Coterie takes undefeated second place here, cutting out any Princes for double Maulerfiend. That plus two Defilers gives this a solid amount of threat saturation, and ensures it has good melee answers to larger targets if the Infractors get dunked. This needs to find 50pts for the nu-Marine tax, which it can do most easily by dropping a Maulerfiend to a third Chaos Spawn squad, but that might impinge on the threat saturation plan, so you could see some more involved tinkering to make it work. Guess we'll see soon enough, and congratulations to Grant on the undefeated finish!

Archetype

Shamblerot Tarpit

Thoughts

Defilers and zombie hordes take second place, and that's not hugely surprising - this build was extremely good pre-update, doesn't really change at all (in fact gains 10pts, which Haydn has made the power move of simply not spending), and benefits from the departure of old Oath of Moment, as it reduces the rate at which some builds can handle the big Pox unit. Things should only get better with the upcoming departure of big Allarus units, so this is a real one to watch. Congratulations Haydn on the undefeated run.

Archetype

All-in Kan Dakka

Thoughts

More pre-balance shooty Orks, here on the double Kan plan. My impression from chatting to players is that doubling up on these is probably one of the out-and-out casualties of the changes, as you get diminishing returns from the second unit, so they're the easiest place to harvest a bunch of points to pay for nerfs and re-allocate. In addition, the arrival of new Custodes is painful for them, because suddenly there's an entire army where every meaningful unit hs T7+, which cuts their output a lot. We'll see though - they are really good, and if Marines end up running lots of 3W stuff, it could go the other way. Congratulations Beranger.

Thoughts

Old Dark Angels close off their incredible early 11th run with one final showdown, facing off against a very hard-hitting Genestealer build in a Recon mirror. Gather Intel has some fairly unusual scoring dynamics, and both sides have some angles on exploiting that. The good news for Cults is that you can score very flexibly here - front-loading points onto turns two and three is very doable, and if the Dark Angels ever let them sneak onto their home and Action, that locks in a bunch of points. The bad news is that the reverse is also true - if the Dark Angels play a cautious, grindy game, they can rack up points later once most of the Cults are dead. That is a bit riskier into Cults than almost anyone else, because it's quite unlikely you'll manage to fully eliminate them, and this is a mission where the right unit in the wrong place can create a massive point swing.

I think what ultimately pushes this one towards the Dark Angels (apart from the sheer brutality of the army) is how difficult it is for the Cults to deny them access to central objectives with split centres. As long as the Dark Angels get their 6VP turn one, they have so many routes to 100 and such a killing advantage that it's hard to see the Cults managing to make sufficient headway. The Angels have to somewhat overcommit to protecting their home to avoid a slip up, but otherwise focusing on carving up the Genestealers while the Lion and Samael's units sweep key pieces off the table should be more than enough for the win, which does seem to be how this played out. Interesting one to analyse - this shows how big a difference the change to split the centres on this mission made, helping grindier Recon armies win the mirror.
