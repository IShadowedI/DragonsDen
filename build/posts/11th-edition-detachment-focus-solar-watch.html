11th Edition Detachment Focus: Solar Watch

October 3rd, 2026 via Goonhammer
Following hot on the heels of the Emperor’s Angels come his Praetorian Guard. The Adeptus Custodes get an early 11th edition Codex, and with it a slew of new rules and Detachments. In this article, we’re taking a look at the Solar Watch Detachment.

Swift death awaits any who dare to breach the sanctity of Terra, with the Solar Watch standing vigilant ready to intercept any threat to the throneworld. What does that look like in-game? Let's find out.

Thanks to Games Workshop for providing us with a review copy of this Codex and a provisional version of the Munitorum Field Manual. Any rules or points costs we reference are subject to change until they are published on the Warhammer 40K App.

Solar Watch

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

Force Disposition: Reconnaissance

Unique: SHIELD HOST

So you wanna go fast but hate thinking about playing models that might actually die like the Aeldari? There is no better way to bring your fight to (or perhaps run away from) your foes than by running the Shield Host with the job of protecting the Sol System. The Solar Watch is a 1 DP Shield Host Detachment that focuses on maintaining and manipulating movement on the battlefield. As the key to clinching so many battles often comes down to being in the right place at the right time, the Solar Watch is a great way to establish board control.

Custodes Vertus Praetors. Credit: Jack Hunter

Detachment Rule: Talon Sortie

Adeptus Custodes units that elect to make a fall-back move are still eligible to charge. With the big bumps to speed and endurance that Custodes have seen across the board, any ability that keeps them from being bogged down in the wrong fights or that lets them make the most of their limited activations is a good one to have. Given that every model in the Custodes army is a stone cold killer this ability can make solving the meat mountain problem a really difficult one for your opponents

Favoured Ka’tah: Calistus

Each time a unit activates the Calistus Ka’tah they gain +2" to their movement characteristic. Calistus is one of the least flashy Ka’tahs in the Custodes arsenal but in lists built around the Solar Watch it is easily the most powerful and you will frequently find yourself activating it for at least half the units in your army round to round as it lets you move through all types of models when making normal/fall-back/charge moves while also pushing your standard foot custodes to a blistering 10" of speed.

With access to so many ways to get advance and charge or assault on your units from the myriad other Detachments, you quickly end up with an army of T7 jetbikes without the need for the bike itself (though there’s no reason not to slot a few into your list anyway since we’re going full Talladega Nights over here). 

Enhancements

  • Sally Forth (30pts): This enhancement is for Shield-Captains only and allows them to select a friendly Adeptus Custodes Infantry unit within 6" at the start of your Charge Phase and give them advance and charge. Just a straight up powerful enhancement to have at your fingertips, which a lot of factions would love to have in their back pocket.

    Wings: You're pretty much always taking this if you're playing this Detachment (unless you're going all bikes), it's one of the big draws.

  • Auric Eagle (15pts, Upgrade): This unit upgrade gives a single Adeptus Custodes Infantry or Mounted unit (but not terminators, they’re not allowed to have fun) +1 to their advance and charge rolls. Nothing to see here really but if you have the 15 to spend, as you sometimes will with Custodes there’s nothing wrong with going even faster. This also works well with a Blade Champion thanks to their built-in re-rolls to these.

Stratagems

  • Inexorable (1CP): During your movement phase, when an Adeptus Custodes unit is selected to move it may ignore modifiers to your unit’s movement, advance and charge rolls. Just a solid situationally useful Stratagem though keep in mind it also shuts down your modifier from Calistus.
  • At Spear’s Length (1CP): When an Adeptus Custodes unit is selected to make a fall-back move it can still shoot. Great for getting absolutely every bit of power out of your units as they careen around the board like my kids at the trampoline park, but with ancient archaeotech weaponry at their disposal instead of just ADHD and a lack of impulse control.
  • Gravimetric Grenade (1CP): Oh how an old favorite has fallen…At the start of your opponent’s charge phase an unengaged Adeptus Custodes unit may target a visible enemy unit within 12" and reduce any attempted charge rolls it makes by 1. This isn’t the old Allarus grenade of old that would send Death Guard players into apoplectic fits when their movement was reduced to -2 but it still has its place as a deterrent in the off chance your opponent actually wants to engage you.

Detachment Combos

Solar Watch goes well with just about any combination of Detachments in the book. It is easy to lean on Jet Bikes in a Detachment with this much speed but with how much Custodes want to be able to freely move about the board there really aren’t any bad choices for it. I’m personally a big fan of combining this with Might of the Moritoi for even more devastating dreadnoughts that absolutely will never be locked down but there are pretty strong arguments for anything outside of the Grav Assault Force. Venetari Lances are particularly brutal here as you can set up some really deep assassination runs by jacking their movement up to 14, hitting them with Auric Eagle and then launching them halfway across the board with ease to land their Precision strikes.

Is It Good?

Allarus Custodes from the Solar Watch Shield Host painted by Crab-stuffed Mushrooms

While I’d argue that the stratagems available to the Solar Watch aren’t the flashiest (though Fall-back and shoot is always welcome), the rest of the package is great for anyone looking to make the most out of their limited model count these days and those stratagems can easily be sublimated by the fact that this baby is only 1 DP.

Final Thoughts

If you ain’t first you’re last and I see no better way to take up as much space as possible if even the dice fail you than by running the Solar Watch. And yes, White is a pain in the ass to paint, but Custodes models take to it really well.
