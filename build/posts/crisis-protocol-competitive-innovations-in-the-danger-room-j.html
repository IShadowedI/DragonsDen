[Crisis Protocol] Competitive Innovations in the Danger Room, July 2026

July 9th, 2026 via Goonhammer
The Earth's Mightiest Heroes Assemble to battle it out Credit - Alfred_Pharius

Greetings, True Believers!

Atomic Mass Games has announced an imminent shake-up to Marvel: Crisis Protocol, with most characters in the game supposedly receiving at least minor adjustments. While we wait for everything to settle, it's a good time to look at the results from the last three months of competitive play to see what's working—and what isn't.

For this article, we'll be looking at results from rated events held between April and June inclusive, and only from events with more than 10 players.

It's worth remembering that the Standard Challenger missions went live in June, so this dataset includes games played using both the previous mission set and the new one.Top Affiliations By Win Rate

SHIELD

The agents of SHIELD posted the highest win rate during this period, winning 64% of their 523 recorded games.

Many of SHIELD's core characters received health updates ahead of the broader health changes we know are on the way. Nick Fury Jr., Hawkeye, and Black Widow, Agent of SHIELD all received increases to their health pools.

The main SHIELD game plan is often referred to as "double bubble," where players stack rerolls from Namor's Charismatic Leader and Baron Zemo's Strategic Genius. Add the Invaders leadership into the mix to reroll skulls, and suddenly you're rerolling your way out of almost anything.

Convocation

The Mystical Convocation for Marvel Crisis Protocol - Credit Alfred_Pharius

The mystical menace of Convocation claimed the second-highest win rate, posting 62% across 174 games. Convocation remains a relatively uncommon affiliation, as reflected by its lower number of games played. However, for players willing to invest the time to learn it, the affiliation offers a deep toolbox of tricks that can catch opponents off guard.

Their leadership, The Bar With No Doors, has two distinct components. One helps them gain an early lead on extracts by granting free movement when their characters take damage. This allows the wizards to snatch up risky extracts with far greater safety. The other ramps up their attrition game by providing additional rerolls for Mystic attacks.

Servants of the Apocalypse

credit: Brushwizard

The Servants of the Apocalypse continue their reign of terror with a 56% win rate across 370 games.

While this remains comfortably above average, it represents fewer Apocalypse games than we've seen in previous competitive periods, despite the affiliation not yet receiving any balance adjustments.

This may simply be the result of spending too long at the top. Players have had time to learn the matchup and develop strategies to counter the things Apocalypse does best.

It's likely that some balance changes are coming for the Servants before long, so it will be interesting to see how they perform for the remainder of the year.

Dark Dimension

Photo courtesy of Atomic Mass Games

Dark Dimension comes in with a 54% win rate across 281 games.

Under Dormammu's leadership, models can begin turn one with additional power. Several characters in the game benefit enormously from that extra point of power, whether that's enabling a charge, allowing them to safely pick up extracts, or activating tactics cards that would otherwise be unavailable.

Dormammu himself received some love relatively recently, allowing him to count skulls on his attack rolls rather than his opponent's defence rolls. Since he's usually rolling more attack dice than his opponents are rolling defence dice, it's a welcome increase to his offensive output.

Hellfire Club

STRANGER THINGS. Joseph Quinn as Eddie Munson in STRANGER THINGS. Cr. Courtesy of Netflix © 2022

Rounding out the top five is the Hellfire Club, sitting at a 54% win rate across 78 rated games.

Once again, this strong win rate is likely influenced by a relatively small number of dedicated players mastering the affiliation. The Hellfire Club was once a terror in the meta when Emma Frost could recruit virtually anyone into her ranks. Since moving to a more traditional affiliation structure with a few less-popular characters, its presence has declined slightly. Even so, the ability to hand out power exactly where it's needed remains incredibly strong.

On top of that, Emma herself remains one of the game's premier 4-threat characters, capable of repositioning enemies and controlling the battlefield while proving surprisingly difficult to take down thanks to her Diamond Form.

Best of the Rest

A further eight affiliations sit just above the 50% win-rate mark.

The Web Warriors clock in at a healthy 54% win rate. They remain the premier crisis-focused affiliation, preferring to outscore opponents rather than simply outfight them. Recent character releases have helped keep them firmly relevant in the current meta.

The Inhumans continue to perform strongly with a 53% win rate. The loss of the Great Refuge has hurt them somewhat, but they remain a significant competitive threat.

Wakanda also sits at 53%. King T'Challa continues to be a dominant presence, rotating around the table with ease while delivering some serious damage.

Asgard has seen a modest increase in effectiveness, reaching a 53% win rate across 487 games. The addition of Valkyrie and Elendil as a strong affiliated 4-threat option has helped solidify a powerful core roster.

Rounding out the top ten, the Guardians of the Galaxy post a 52% win rate across 981 games. Adam Warlock, Moondragon, and Quasar have provided the affiliation with a much-needed boost.

Final Thoughts

With the announced changes just around the corner, the next Competitive Innovations report could look very different.

With the highest win rate sitting at 64% and most affiliations clustering around the 50% mark, the game is currently in a fairly healthy place. Time will tell how the upcoming character updates reshape the competitive landscape.
