11th Edition Detachment Focus: Cabal of Chaos (Updated October 13, 2026)

October 13th, 2026 via Goonhammer
In this series of articles we take a deep dive into a specific Detachment for a faction, covering the faction’s rules and upgrades and talking about how to build around that faction for competitive play. In this article, we’re covering the Cabal of Chaos Detachment for Chaos Space Marines.

Introduced initially in mid 2025 as part of a quarterly balance update and updated with the initial deluge of 11th edition Detachments, the Cabal of Chaos focuses primarily on the faction's psykers and Daemon Prince units, buffing them in interesting ways and giving you added buffs when playing them.

Changelog

  • 10-13-2026 (Current): Updated for 11th edition and the September Balance Update
  • 6-4-2025: Initial Publication
Disposition: Disruption

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

The Cabal of Chaos Detachment gives Chaos Space Marines a new way to build armies around PSYKER and DAEMON PRINCE units, giving units additional bonuses when they make a Dark Pact near one of those units. This can either be additional strength on their ranged attacks near Psykers or Extra AP on melee attacks near a Daemon Prince. Additionally, there are some solid Stratagems and Enhancements here to buff units and reward you for taking Psykers in your army.

Although initially not much of a factor in 11th edition, as CSM armies have moved toward taking 2 DP Detachments, the Cabal of Chaos has seen more play, primarily because it's the only 1 DP Detachment offering much of value without forcing you to take units you might not want to take and the new update

Detachment Rule: Empyric Wellspring

There are two parts to this:

  • In your Shooting phase, when a friendly HERETIC ASTARTES PSYKER unit (excluding DAEMON units) is selected to shoot, if that unit makes a Dark Pact, its ranged attacks have +1 S.
  • In the Fight phase, when a friendly HERETIC ASTARTES DAEMON PRINCE unit (excluding KHORNE units)  is selected to fight, if that unit makes a Dark Pact, it gets +2 S and +1 AP on its melee attacks.

This is a pretty limited rule, and shooting buffs on psyker units are kind of limited in their utility, though we'll look at where they provide the most value. At a quick glance, your non-Daemon Psyker Units in the Chaos Space Marines army are:

  • Sorcerer
  • Sorcerer in Terminator Armour
  • Dark Commune
  • Masters of the Maelstrom
  • Master of Possession
  • Nemesis Claw

At first glance, that's not a great selection of units to give +1 Strength on their Shooting attacks. That said, the recent September 2026 balance update pushing combi-weapons and combi-bolters to S5 AP-1 suddenly makes +1 Strength a lot more interesting, as it means firing at S6 with combi-bolters and combi-weapons when you pact, and that means you can effectively punch up into a lot more targets. There's a very real play now in taking units of 10 terminators with a Terminator Sorcerer in Warpstrike Champions, where your units can go back into deep strike and get built-in re-rolls to Advance and Charge rolls from the Sorcerer, plus you can use Death Hex for Extra AP. A unit of ten Terminators dropping S6 Bolter shots at AP-2 with Lethal or Sustained Hits and full re-rolls to hit is a real threat - just ask any Thousand Sons player.

The Nemesis Claw are also interesting here as they come with the PSYKER keyword natively and so just have built-in +1S on their shooting attacks when making a Dark Pact. Their shooting isn't particularly great, but you can give them a Heavy Bolter if you take ten models, and having a S10 meltagun shot is pretty funny. Legionaries can also be interesting now with a bunch of S5 AP-1 bolters, but generally speaking these are both melee units, so the benefit is ore like a fun add-on than something you'd actually build around.

The other big option here is Daemon Princes, where getting +2S and +1AP in melee helps make the Winged Daemon Prince legitimately interesting as a threat, sending his hellforged weapons to S10 AP-3 on the strike and S8 AP-1 on the sweep. That +2 Strength gets you over some solid breakpoints and ensures you're still wounding basic marines on a 2+, and the extra AP is basically necessary to ensure you're not whiffing against key targets. Add in Death Hex from a Terminator Sorcerer and your Daemon Prince can actually put in work.

Terminator Sorcerer. Credits: That Gobbo

Enhancements

These enhancements are fine. There's not a ton to take them over other options. Touched by the Warp is more cute than useful, though Conduit is great on a Daemon Prince.

  • Touched by the Warp (10 points) - Heretic Astartes model only, can’t go on a Khorne model. The model has PSYKER and its weapons have [PSYCHIC]. Because this only applies to one model, it’s tough to make it work. The problem here is that you just don’t really have characters who are good enough at shooting to make this viable. The only one which comes to mind is the Lord Discordant, who can carry an autocannon and might want to avoid catching modifiers to hit. The other place you’d theoretically use this is on a Warpsmith joining Havocs to give them the PSYKER keyword but they already ignore modifiers to hit so you’re at best just trying to get them +1 Strength, which can be helpful on Autocannons. The actual value of this is more in the ability to trigger some of the Stratagems here, like Infernal Vigour and Wreathed in Warpflame.
  • Conduit of Chaos (20 points) - Daemon model only (excluding KHORNE models). The model’s melee attacks have [LANCE]. Oh hell yeah. This one is huge when you pair it with the +2 Strength from the Dark Pact. S10 and Lance will wound most things on a 2+ and can punch up effectively into tougher threats on a 4+, helping shore up one of the big problems with the Daemon Prince’s melee.

Stratagems

The Stratagems here are solid. They’re all priced at 1 CP, meaning you won’t have many issues around CP generation. Your bigger challenge will be wanting to use all of them all the time.

  • Infernal Vigour (1 CP) - Used in your Command phase on a friendly PSYKER or DAEMON unit (not KHORNE). That unit heals D3+1 wounds. This is really great for Terminators, as it means more often than not you can get back a full 3-wound model when you throw this out. It's also great on Chosen or Possessed if you're going that route, but regenerating Terminators for your big units is the real value.
  • Fleshy Curse (1 CP) - Used at the start of your Shooting phase. Pick a Psyker unit and a visible enemy unit within 12” of them. Roll a D6. On a 1 they take 1 mortal wound. On a 24 they take D3, and on a 5-6 they take 2D3 mortal wounds. This is all done by a psychic attack. This is great - it’s a solid grenade-like substitute, and can combine with a grenade just as easily for extra mortal wounds.
  • Wreathed in Warpflame (1 CP) - Used in your Shooting phase, when a PSYKER unit is picked to shoot. That unit’s attacks gain [IGNORES COVER]. Given that Psychic attacks already ignore modifiers to hit, this is purely a benefit for the non-psyker models in the unit. Which means it's great on your Terminators when you need to push through extra hits against a horde unit. Don't bother with it if you're going into something that requires [LETHAL HITS] for punching up, though - just let the full re-rolls do the work as you fish for lethals.
Credit: Dan "Swiftblade" Richardson

Playing This Detachment

We didn't think much of this Detachment when it originally released, and that's partly because at the time, it focused on units you just weren't taking in your CSM lists. However the Cabal of Chaos slowly became a more common pick for CSM armies, primarily because it was often  the only 1 DP Detachment offering anything of value to your army and because it was an easy way to get the Disruption disposition in teams environments. With the release of the new September Balance update, things have shifted dramatically.

Essentially, giving Chaos Terminators S5 AP-1 bolters changes the entire calculus on their utility. The Thousand Sons built an entire strategy around having AP-2 bolters with re-rolls to hit and Lethal hits firing en masse at major threats and now CSM armies can just do the same by pairing a Terminator Sorcerer (and Death Hex) with a unit of Terminators, who gain full re-rolls to hit whenever they make a Dark Pact. The result is a deluge of S5 AP-2 shooting with either LETHAL or SUSTAINED HITS 1 and full re-rolls to hit firing into a target. The obvious pairing here becomes the Warpstrike Champions Detachment, which lets said Terminator unit go back up into Deep Strike each round, then drop in to repeat the process, staying out of trouble. Because volume is key here and hitting on a 4+ before modifiers sucks, you'll want to kit your terminators out now with Combi-bolters and not combi-weapons. Losing the dev wounds isn't amazing, but you'll make up for it with the extra volume.

If you don't want to go the terminator route, you have some other options. Cabal pairs nicely with Chaos Cult, which has its own built-in psykers (and loves being able to grenade targets with Fleshy Curse), and benefits from adding in lone Terminator Sorcerers and Winged Daemon Princes. If you're going heavy on regular marine bodies, then pairing this Detachment with something like Veterans of the Long War can work as well - a unit of Chosen with Masters of the Maelstrom and Fabius Bile can be really nasty, and will makes for an amazing target when it comes to healing via the Infernal Vigour Stratagem (especially because you can double heal with Garreon reviving one model and the Stratagem giving you another, or use your free CP to immediately heal).

Strengths

  • Terminator Sorcerers. Hands-down the best PSYKER option in the army, Terminator Sorcerers now give your units a ton of value, and make 20 terminator strategies viable. Being able to heal them via Stratagem or give your unit [IGNORES COVER] is also huge, and add a lot to the mix.
  • Daemon Princes. Chaos Daemon Princes can be a bit underwhelming at times but here the extra AP really helps them push through the one extra wound they need to be respectable. The walking Daemon Princes’ upgrade to gain a conditional Lone Operative ability synergizes well with their role to hang out and buff units, and the flying Daemon Princes hit quite hard here.

Weaknesses

  • Narrow benefits. Outside of Terminator Sorcerers and Daemon Princes, the buffs here just aren't that useful. +1 Strength on ranged attacks is only good on specific units, and it really only started mattering to Terminators when they got +1 AP on their bolters.
Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

This is nasty - your three big hammer units are the Terminator bricks and Kravek + Mutilators, and you can pickup two each per turn. One of them gets a turn 1 Deep strike while the other gets beefier shooting, and as an added bonus you have a nasty flying threat in the form of the Winged Daemon Prince. Your biggest challenges here are going to be finding the CP to do everything you want and the fact that you are stuck playing Disruption - in particular your matchup into Purge is a nightmare. So just focus on tabling your opponent and using the Bikers/Raiders/Cultists to do your actions as needed and using the big bricks of terminators to eliminate key threats.

I'll readily admit this list has more legs in teams than singles right now, but the core threat of two big terminator bricks ready to throw down at T6 and spitting out insane amounts of AP-2 firepower is very solid. You're basically pulling the same trick Scarabs did, only at a discount, and with more reliable access to full re-rolls to hit.

Final Thoughts

What a glow-up. The change to bolters has had a dramatic impact on this Detachment's utility, turning it from a nothingburger you take as your 1 DP filler into something you can legitimately build around in Warpstrike Champions. There are a number of interesting ways to build around this Detachment now, and it figures to be a very competitive option moving forward.
