Competitive Innovations in 11th: Up To Eleven pt.1

July 8th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

11th Edition continues to ramp up, and this is probably our last week before things get wild, because it's a decent crop despite a complete lack of American events (celebrating some sort of birthday, I hear). We've got our first 11th singles supermajor from the UKTC in birmingham, and a bunch of healthily-sized majors and GTs from around the world on top of that, so let's dive in.

Archetype

Robots, Terminators and a Crab

Thoughts

Thousand Sons continue to be, for my money, the faction to beat right now, taking down both the biggest event in Sam's hands here, and also putting in the strongest win rate across the events, narrowly hitting the 60% threshold. Sekhetar Cohort is one of the very strongest 1DP detachments available, turning the robots into all-round all-stars in combination with either Hexwarp or Soulforged. Here, Hexwarp is the play, significantly boosting the bots lethality, and making their free heavy flamer Overwatch particularly nasty. They're backed up by a big Scarab unit, which Hexwarp turns into an extremely flexible bully piece, cheap Tzaangors for scoring early (especially the Shamans, who can be Hidden), and some Rubricae units for big extra volume punch on key turns via the Sorcerers. Oh, a Defiler, also. Those are still great, apparently. This build does everything, and Sam is a fantastic player, so it's no massive surprise to see him take down the tournament, big congratulations!

Archetype

Triple Defiler

Thoughts

We are not, apparently, free from the crab menace. I saw this in the roster when prepping for the 6+++ show last week and felt a sinking sense of dread, and sure enough here we are. +270pts on the Defilers and we're still kicking. Unfortunately, while the price has gone up on them, 11th's missions and terrain help them a bunch, as they're well placed to benefit from toe-on terrain, can pick Purge so that there's no Action requirements on the Primary, and can back-load Secondary scoring till they're done rolling up the enemy army, allowing them to leverage their lethality to the max. Running Venomcrawlers shotgun with them is smart too, as it keeps the activation count up without diluting the defensive setup too badly. Well done to Theo, I guess lets see how high we can push the price step on Deilers.

Archetype

Mawloc Assault

Thoughts

In third place, Florence goes Dune mode and unleashes the full fury of the Tyranid's giant worm contingent. Mawlocs get appreciably easier to use to maximum effect in 11th thanks to the default Ingress distance dropping to 8", squeezing additional value out of their Mortals. They're also nicely broad in what they can engage with up close - high attack volume at S8 AP-2 means they're just as happy munching on Marines or Orks. Having three in the list and the constant pressure of the Red Terror is going to force serious caution from the foe, really leaning into the Disruption theme, and with the increased threat that Shadow presents in 11th, an overly slow start can be fatal if the mid game mind crush lands hard. Zoanthropes also provide a big shooting punish into any high-value targets that can be lined up, really helping close off the builds final gaps. I've always had a soft spot for Mawlocs and I'm thrilled to see them getting serious play, and major props to Florence on a fantastic finish.

Archetype

Recon Retaliation Acquisition

Thoughts

T'au continue to do well in fourth place, and this week most of the really top performances have come courtesy of the Retaliation/Advanced Acquisition combo seen here, providing a bit more all-rounder game-long lethality compared to Mont'ka, and depending less on vulnerable pieces that can be liabilities in Purge. The new tech we're seeing this week is going even further on avoiding Purge races, with both Jack and an event winner elsewhere swapping the build to use Recon. This works pretty well with Retaliation Cadre, which has some great mobility pieces between a full move Fire and Fade and a 6" Deep Strike stratagem, really helping it lock in points. Starflare Ignition is very nice for that as well, helping reset the Sunforges when there's lots of big targets that need to die. That wasn't enough to defeat Theo's defilers, but Jack had a great run through to the top cut ahead of that, well played.

Thoughts

More Daemons here, this time launching a lighting fast Slaaneshi assault on foes with a mixture of Daemonettes with Characters and big Tormentbringer/Hellflayer units. This setup gets massively better in almost every way in 11th. Daemonette/Character units are pretty nasty for their price once they have always on Advance/Charge, and having multiple Epitome's around is particularly good. Hidden makes it far more likely that you can have one of these visible to a friendly unit but not your foes shooting, allowing you to live the Thieves of Pain dream and thwart your foes killing plans. The list also has access to a straight -1 to Hit stratagem, which stacks delightfully with new Cover to further reduce the ability of foes to kill stuff. Hellflayer units also enjoy the lighter tables, and still have access to Phantasmal Longing if they need it. Finally, the changes to Fight ordering means that if this list only wants to pick a single fight in a given turn, there's (usually) no longer any downside to activating Seductive Gambit, because unless the foe has Fight First, you'll still get to pick the first unit. Daemons seem to have a wealth of options available to them in 11th, and it's cool to see Slaanesh joining the party once more, congratulations Jorj.
