Looking Ahead to June in Umamusume Pretty Derby

June 4th, 2026 via Goonhammer
June may be the somewhat traditional first month of Summer for many folks, but in Umamusume: Pretty Derby, there isn’t a whole lot to get excited about for fun in the sun. Although there are some events and new releases this month, there’s very little in here that would be considered a must have or a want to have; that said, it is important to at least lay out what is coming down the pipe as June rolls on, as well as briefly touching on the single new Champions Meeting we have to look forward to in June, the second running of the Cancer Cup. If you’re a long hauler digging in for the next scenario or just playing safe with your economy, June is a good month to focus on building up your carat collection and not spending unless a trainee is your absolutely most favorite of all time trainee.

Trainees for June

We have four new Trainees in June’s schedule, as well as a rerun banner at the end of the month. The biggest problem with this month’s batch of trainees is that while some of them are pretty good, there are very, very good trainees and cards that will hit the game in July, and unless you have the disposable income to just pull for whatever you want at all times (in which case, I’m glad you like my articles but why are you here?), you’ll very likely want to save your carats to help guarantee the trainee and card copies you’ll be looking for when the Summer sets start coming out in full force.

The first new trainee is magic witch Sweep Tosho, and she’s a pretty good pick if you want to run an End Closer who could Debuff OR Ace, and there aren’t a ton of options for End Closer Debuffers in the first place. However, if you have trainees like Narita Taishin or a very well raised Gold Ship, you probably don’t need Sweep Tosho, and she doesn’t particularly do super well as a parent enough to bother grabbing her for that particular purpose. She is cute though, and we do love girls with giant hats, but her alts later are a lot better if this is your favorite design and you are willing to be patient.

Taiki is just Good, so you can't go wrong.

The event units this month are the Camping themed girls, Camping Mejiro Dober and Camping Taiki Shuttle. Both of these are actually very good options, with Camping Dober being a straight up upgrade from her original form with the only downside being that she can’t be her own parent to inherit OG Dober’s unique skill easily. She’s got some great skills and is a very strong competitor in the Late Surger category, which although is often a position many trainees can run, not many of those who can do so well or effectively. Camping Taiki Shuttle is not quite as much of an upgrade from her OG form, but she is very good, especially if you want to lock down Mile races. She excels at Miles much like OG Taiki does, and comes with Mile Maven, Head On, and a new unique that works (mostly) better than the original Taiki’s ult. About the biggest downside to Camping Taiki is that OG Taiki is ALSO just very good, so there’s not quite as much of a huge return in performance from one version to the next; that said, Taiki is almost always a great unit in any of her forms (past and future), so if you want her, she’ll do a great job.

Why do you hate me, Cygames? Why is she so BAD?

And now we come to heartbreak hotel: Air Shakur. I love Air Shakur. She is a great design, one of the coolest personalities in the game and honestly one of the best examples of “looks rough, is smart and kind” that you’ll see in media, but… she’s just not very good. Why is she not good, you ask? Well… because her only stat growth is 30% Wit. And you might think that’s great, because that’s such a big number, but Wit is already a dump stat that is exceptionally easy to raise, and 30% growth in Wit doesn’t benefit any of the other stats that are actually important to winning races, like Speed, Stamina, or even Power. Running a campaign with Air Shakur and attempting to make her your Ace is a nightmare because her margins are exceptionally low, meaning you often need EXCEPTIONAL luck to get constant high rolls off your Support Cards and bonuses to make up for the fact that she gets literally 0% growth in any other stat but Wit. If that wasn’t bad enough, her unique, “Trigger:BEAT”, is a little uneven, as it requires her to be on the inner side of the pack to activate. She ALSO has a Negative Skill that she can get in non-Trackblazer scenarios called 99 Problems that she gets for winning the Japanese Derby that gives her -40 to Wit and Guts if she’s in a bad mood. While I can appreciate the flavor they were going for, Air Shakur just feels exceptionally bad to train, and ironically, Trackblazer at least makes it easier due to removing 99 Problems from occurring. The other problem? She’s a Late Surger, which as we mentioned, is a pretty weak position at the moment.

As for this months reruns, Seiun Sky and Narita Brian both remain really strong competitors. Seiun Sky also remains an eternally strong Parent and Grandparent in the game due to Angling x Scheming, so if you never managed to get her in the initial run and don’t mind the chance of also getting Narita Brian, this is a pretty okay banner to attempt pulling on. It is often a bit better to work off of single target banners unless you want both units, but if you have the carats to pursue a guaranteed pull of one of them and get the other, you’ll be even better off. I still caution waiting on July’s banners and possibly even holding off till August, but if you really need Seiun to help round out your parentage roster for Front Runners (or just want a Front Runner), this could be the banner worth considering this month.

New Support Cards

June also has a few new support cards coming, and none of these are must haves. You’ll probably want to look to borrow some of them, like the SSR Taiki Shuttle card, but unless you’re committing to being the person everyone else borrows from, you can probably hold off on trying to get a copy of the card. It is very useful for training Milers though, so whenever you do go to train your next Mile Ace, consider looking for a friend’s Taiki card to borrow. The SSR Oguri Cap card is actually not great compared to her other versions, and the most notable thing about it is that it provides an easier way of getting Wet Conditions, but at the cost of providing nothing else of great value.

The next four cards in the month are Wit Mambo (Matikanetannhauser), Guts El Condor Pasa, Power Air Groove, and Wit Gold City. All of these cards are skippable, and unless you are a brand new player who has literally no cards at all to your name, none of these cards are worth spending your Carats on over saving for another month for better banners. I know that this can be a boring suggestion in a game that feels fun when you pull something new, but the goal of a guide like this is to help F2P or limited budget players, and I am going to level with you: none of these cards are, or ever will, be meta. Even the Air Groove card is “good” but in a way that has no lifespan, making your investment into it very meaningless.

A great card if it didn't have an expiration date 30 days from this article.

The final card of this month is… an odd one. The card is titled “The Throne’s Assemblage”, and is often shortened to just “Throne” by fans, so if you happen to look at other content creators or guides, you may see them talking about “Throne”. If you look at the card’s events and skills on paper, it does seem like a very good card, and it IS an exceptionally good card… for Trackblazer. At MLB, it gives 10% race bonus, bonus skill points every time you activate a training with it, a big Friendship Gauge boost, lots of Energy recovery and effectiveness, and even has Wit and Speed bonuses. So why would I tell you to avoid this card? Because it is only beneficial in Trackblazer, which Global is about to move out of. Keeping in mind that Global moves at an exceptionally fast rate compared to the JPN release, that means that your investment in this card will only give you returns for about 4 to 6 weeks at best, before the card is immediately overshadowed and useless. Compare this to Riko, who remains useful months after her debut, and even Kitasan Black, who is just now starting to enter the downswing of dominating Support Decks a literal year after the game launched. If you want a specific REASON why this card will become useless, that’s because it operates in the same space as Trainer/Friend cards, and the new scenario will introduce the card that replaces it: Light Hello, who you are going to want in order to make training in the next scenario more effective.

Champions Meetings: Cancer Cup Returns



June only has a single new Champions Meeting Race (with the caveat that Gemini Cup’s rerun is already happening by the time the calendar changed), the rerunning of the Cancer Cup. Much like how the Gemini Cup changed things up, though, the rerun of the Cancer Cup shifts from a 1600m Mile race to a 2200m Medium Race with a far more difficult set of parameters to compete with. We’ll talk more about Cancer Cup in full detail next week, but just to put some of the basics on the line, this version of Cancer Cup is 2200m, which means you need NON-Standard Distance, and is also run on Cloudy Weather in Good Conditions, rather than the far more common Sunny / Firm. What this primarily means is that you’re going to need to look into a brand new Ace to train for this one rather than hoping to run someone you’ve already trained, and there’s no better time than to start than right now!

Looking Towards July

Hard to believe that we all suffered through Trackblazer era and its already leaving us before Summer ends, considering how much people have talked about it. Never fear, Grand Live (or whatever it will be called) is about a month and a half (or so) away, with it new stat caps, mechanics, and also the end of the eternal racing schedule of Trackblazer. If you’ve been slowing how much you played the game due to how unpalatable Trackblazer is, don’t worry, you’ve got lots to look forward to! July also promises to bring with it a lot of the beloved Summer units and cards, such as Summer Maruzensky’s card and Summer Gold Ship, so if you’re saving your carats and being patient, you will be very shortly rewarded! Until then, please leave us comments down below, drop by the TTB Discord if you’re a Patron, or even email me at marcy@tabletopbattles.com! Until then, see you out on the track!
