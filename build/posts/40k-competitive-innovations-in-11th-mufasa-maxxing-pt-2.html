Thoughts

Votann continue their solid recent performance in undefeated second place, with Alex going for the extra violent Hearthband/Needgaard setup, using lots of Hearthguard. The big value Votann builds like this bring to the table right now is the fact that essentially anything is in scopre for them to alpha strike - if they line up their buffs and key units, even the very toughest foes will fall. Archetype

Stormraven Devastator Salamanders

Thoughts

More sendoffs here, because in third place Michael has mixed up the Salamanders formula considerably. The obvious centrepieces are soon-to-be-legends Devastators and a Stormraven, which can tag team to unleash catastrophic close-range melta damage. The very cheap extra bodies a full squad gives you combine nicely with the Librarian's invulnerable save, and being able to flip a six in shooting also plays very nicely with Fusillade. Elsewhere, we have Sternguard Veterans with the unusual choice of Prescience, which can allow them to cycle into one of the two big transports, potentially setting them up for a brutal Burning Vengeance shortly afterwards. Finally, I really like the small Bladeguard/Adrax unit, feels like a really solid troubleshooting piece for the price you pay. Well done to Michael for mixing things up on a well established build.

Thoughts

Khorne's clearly on a robot wars kick here, as Kastellans face a mixture of Daemon Engines and Possessed. The AdMech have brought two full Kastelan units to the battlefield, which creates a daunting Take and Hold piece, and also have plenty of grindy damage to cut through the Chaos forces. The World Eaters are far from helpless though - their setup has lots of fast units that can reach out and pick off the AdMech, keeping their scoring flowing, and disrupting objectives that the Robots aren't controlling.

The problem, of course, is that the Robots will be controlling the objectives, and unless that can be dealt with, a methodical AdMech victory is inevitable. So, can they? This, unfortunately, is where the World Eaters list is narrowly tuned wrong for this exact matchup, which is that the Slaughterbound are all with the regular Eightbound, rather than having one with an Exalted unit. With the Daemonic Strength stratagem, you can push Exalted (and a Slaughterbound with them) to D4, at which point with the 9A from the Slaughterbound factored in, the AdMech would have to be seriously worried about this squad Ingressing and then picking up an entire robot unit by itself. That would make positioning for the AdMech a nightmare, as this is quite hard to screen, and would potentially allow the World Eaters to take the initiative. As is, I simply don't think there's too much chance of the AdMech getting badly punished if they push a Kastelan unit forward, especially as they have so much D3 in their list to clear out the various Eightbound even if they do lose a squad. Feels like there are just too many crunchy targets, and too much agency on the AdMech's side for World Eaters to get there. The best shot for them is probably to try and box the AdMech in, and hope that the Secondary draws betray them, but that clearly didn't happen here, as AdMech rocked a a 100VP finish.

Archetype

Double Robot Rampage

Thoughts

Kastelans slam onto the battlefield here, benefitting massively from synergy with Lords of the Forge. Access to an Armour of Contempt when on an objective really pushes them over the top at their current price, and as the showdown demonstrated, most people are teching for 3-4W elite infantry, and can be stymied by something that's a weird halfway house between those and true heavy vehicles (especially if you stick in the T10 mode for a few extra turns). There's also a phenomenal amount of killing power backing them up here, and it's going to be rough for foes to both clear the robots and have enough left to push through extra-tough Thulia/Cawl into the tanks. I was pretty impressed when I saw robots on the table recently, and this does nothing to dissuade me of that opinion, congratulations Simon!
