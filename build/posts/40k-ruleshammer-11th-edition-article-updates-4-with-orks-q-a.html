[40k] Ruleshammer 11th Edition: Article Updates #4 with Orks Q&A

October 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back to Ruleshammer! In this series we take a detailed look at the rules of key games, but Warhammer 40k in particular. This week I’m covering a few of your questions you’ve submitted via the Ruleshammer form or via Discord.

Cover and Visibility Q&A

How does cover work when some INFANTRY models are fully visible but within a terrain area, and some models are not fully visible but not within a terrain area?

The key thing to remember in these scenarios is that the cover penalty applies to attacking models and is checked per attacking model when declaring targets; this means you can have some situations where some models in a unit have the penalty apply, and some don’t. This particular scenario, though, is better explained with the diagram below.

For more guides and Q&A on cover and visibility rules check out my Terrain Guide.

Orks Q&A

Riled Up Questions

Ork Boyz Part 2. Credit: Kevin Stillman

If a unit makes an advance move and then gets riled up, can that unit declare charge?

While a unit is riled up:

      • <some other abilities>
      • When that unit is selected to make an advance move, that advance move does not prevent that unit from being eligible to declare a charge.

No. Specifically the unit needs to be riled up because this effect only causes advance moves made whilst riled up to not prevent a unit from being eligible to declare a charge. The important element is that it’s “that advance move” made “while riled up” that has this benefit. It’s not a blanket “riled up units can charge even if they advanced this turn” ability.

Can a Warboss use the Intimidating Motivation ability to rile up himself and advance and charge?

Warboss. Credit: Rockfish

Intimidating Motivation (Once per battle round, per army): In your Movement phase, at the start or end of this unit’s move, you can select one friendly ORKS unit within 6" of this unit. That unit:

    • Is no longer battle-shocked.
    • Is riled up until the start of your next turn.

Yes. Though, wow is this wording a bit tricky here. The ability is very much geared towards the Warboss being able to select another unit either within 6” of where it is now or within 6” of a position it has moved to. However the unit is definitely within 6” of itself and it’s a friendly Orks unit, so it can be used on itself.

The other question though is if “starts its move” is the same timing as “when selected to make an advance move.” The consensus here at Tabletop Battles was yes, that “starts its move” is the moment a unit is selected to move next and therefore the unit was riled up at the time it was selected to make an advance move. So the unit is riled up when selecting the advance move and that advance move wouldn’t prevent the unit from advancing and charging.

Can a Warboss use the Intimidating Motivation ability if they remain stationary?

No. Despite being a "move type" the effect of Remain Stationary means it doesn't allow this ability to be triggered.

EFFECT: No models are moved (either in straight lines or rotated). Units that remain stationary do not trigger any rules that are triggered when a unit starts or ends a move.

Which does mean that a Warboss in combat that doesn't fall back can't use this ability to rile up their unit or another nearby unit.

If I do Tactical Disembark with my Warboss unit, can I use his Intimidating Motivation ability before he makes an Advance move and Charge on the same turn?

Yes. For the same reasoning as the other question though with some Transport flavour thrown in. First of all the ability would be used at the start of the move, which in this case is when you declare the unit is disembarking. Some players at this point might ask a few questions such as “does the ability work when the unit isn’t on the battlefield?” Yes it does.

Q: Do a unit's abilities continue to function while they are embarked within a TRANSPORT?

A: Yes, within the restrictions specified by the ability itself. Embarked units are Not On The Battlefield (01.02.04), so any ability requiring visibility or measurement to another unit will fail.

Some might then say, “But this ability requires measurement, so does that prevent it from working?” No, it does not, because of the rule mentioned in that FAQ answer:

01.02.04 Not On The Battlefield

    • …
    • Players cannot measure distances to or from that unit (units are within range of their own abilities).

So a unit's abilities still function and the unit is in range of its own abilities even while embarked. So we can declare it is being used as the Disembark begins, and it will be riled up before it advances, allowing it to charge later on in that turn.

Note you can’t actually select it after disembarking but before advancing though despite this ability being usable at the end of a move. This is because Tactical Disembark doesn’t end until the Advance move has been made based on the rules for moving units.

Ending a Move

After you have finished setting up all of the models in a unit and/or moving all of the models in a unit that you want to move, check that all of the following apply:

    • If that unit is on the battlefield, it is in coherency (03.03).
    • No models in that unit are on another model or partway through a surface of a terrain feature (e.g. a wall or ceiling).
    • All stated ‘After Moving’ conditions are met.

The advance move is an after moving condition of the Tactical Disembark move, and I’m not sure we can argue it’s been met until that advance move has been completed.

Can the Grab It Stratagem be used to embark a unit into a Trukk?

Credit: Contemptor Kevin Stillman

Yes, with some limitations. The Grab It stratagem requires a unit to end its move within range of an objective:

Which it will do, but that condition only applies to the end of the Normal move. It doesn’t need to end the turn within range of the objective, just the move. Units need to meet certain conditions to embark though:

Once the first battle round has started, a friendly unit can embark within a friendly TRANSPORT model after making a normal, advance or fall-back move, if all of the following conditions apply:

    • Each model in that unit is within 3" of that TRANSPORT.
    • That unit was not set up on the battlefield this turn.
    • That unit is eligible to embark within that TRANSPORT, as described on that TRANSPORT’s datasheet.
    • That TRANSPORT has sufficient remaining transport capacity for each model in that unit.

Some are obvious, like the Transport needs to have capacity for them to embark. Some are more subtle, though, such as this combination of rules not working if the unit targeted with the Grab It Stratagem was set up on the battlefield this turn, either by having disembarked or arrived from reserves.

Can a Weirdboy leading a Beast Snagga Boyz unit embark within a Kill Rig?

Ork Kill Rig. Credit: Magos Sockbert

No. Whilst the unit would have the BEAST SNAGGA BOYZ keyword, the required keyword to embark within a Kill Rig the Weirdboy model itself would not gain this keyword from leading the unit.

19.03 Keywords in Attached Units

An attached unit has all of the keywords of all of its component units. As such, an attached unit is affected by any rule that applies to units with any of those keywords. Note that models in an attached unit do not gain the keywords of other models in that unit that they do not already have. Remember that attacks target units, not models.

Transport capacities are checking model keywords not unit keywords.

These questions and answers will be added to Ruleshammer 11th Edition: Orks.
