Thoughts

There has been much talk about Disruption being one of the struggling dispositions out of the gate- Kult of Speed demonstrate that this need not always be the case! The list shoves a tonne of OC 2 chunky models into the opposing grill, able to shut off access to the parts of the board that many dispositions need to score. A standout build for Disruption, taking a big event win here! Well done.
