BattleTech Mech Overview: Assassin

September 30th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Like rain on an ol’ tin roof. 

Missiles blotted the sun as they fell around the fleeing Assassin, the humming whine of the old ‘Mech’s myomer howling in Cochran’s ears as he was pelted and battered by successive falling salvos coming in from the Catapult down in the valley. The command couch in the old ‘Mech was cramped and stiff while the air was searingly hot and dessicatingly dry; a set of conditions made all the worse by the raining fire that shook and shuddered the walls around him. Gray-Red rocks and dust zipped past as he poured the speed on, ducking Alley-Cat around a boulder to cut the incoming flight off before the sputtering pop of her jump jets split the air and sent them skywards on roiling plumes of plasma.

She was an old frame, and he knew it better than anyone. A ‘Mech long past her prime in the service of his family, his company, and currently his employers. Unfortunately it seemed someone had told the garrison, and the ground, that as well.

Another flight sailed in, a small one, 5 angry bees popping and swirling from the Merlin’s chest launcher to clip Alley Cat’s left leg, boring a hole through an old patch and directly into the actuator. The ‘Mech, and Cochran with it, came down with a crash as the leg crumpled underneath of it and sent them tumbling through the dirt. 

“You alright, Major?” Hoshi’s voice called out, the woman’s Wolfhound bolting through the miasma of heat and dust, the rest of her lance only a few hundred meters behind.

“Well enough, Lieutenant.” Cochran grumbled back, the grinding squeal of the Assassin's leg actuators punctuating the statement as he shoved the ‘Mech to its feet. “But I could be better.”

“Not wrong, we could be back on New Vandenberg having a beer.” She laughed in return, watching as the ‘Mech jogged to meet her. “But that’s gonna have to wait, now isn’t it?”

“That it is.” The Major’s agreement came, low and annoyed as he flexed his ‘Mech’s left hand. “Let’s get to it and get home already.”

— Major Preston Cochran, Taurian Employed Mercenary Command, The Bull’s Head Mercenary Company Assassin ASN-21 Pilot. Qalzi, Unaffiliated, June 12th 3038.  

—

Howdy ‘Mechwarriors and welcome back to another weekly Mech Overview! This week we’re taking a look at the ‘venerable’- really stretching the definition there -old IS scout and interdictor ‘Mech: The Assassin!

I’ll be honest: I have a generally poor opinion of Assassins as a whole and am largely convinced that the only thing that makes it capable of winning a fight against say a watermelon is the fact that it has guns. Even then I might still give it to the watermelon if it was moving fast enough… or was like a really tough watermelon.

The Assassin is a seemingly constant presence on the field of the IS’ wars, from the periphery to Terra and on out to the early dawn of the Clan’s the Assassin has been there, done that, and gotten its shit pushed in for it somewhere along the way.

When a Marshal sees an Assassin we know that the fight isn’t gonna be hard, it’s just gonna be annoying.

Lyran Commonwealth Assassin. Credit: SRM

Chassis

The Assassin is a 40 ton medium that has exactly one thing in common across all of its many variants: it goes 7/11/7- except for the one that goes 7/11/5 -and they carry a mixed load of guns. Usually some energy weapon in the arm and some LRMs or SRMs in the torso.

This is… well it sure is.

They’ve got variable armor loads but in the end they are still largely just 40 tonners… and usually 40 tonners with ammo in them. Not even new Core ammo rules can save that from a bin popping.

Variants

These mechs have all been reviewed based on a standard F through S scale, which you can find described on our landing page here (along with all of our other ‘mech reviews, the name of the box you can buy to get any of the mechs we have covered, and our general methodology).

ASN-21

He’s a man, not a man with a plan mind you; just a man.

Welcome to the year of our lord 2676. We stand in the showroom of the Maltex Corporation. We are Star League acquisition officers. We are being shown a genuine piece of shit… and enough money to set us up for the rest of our lives.

This model of Assassin is pitched as a few things in its time, amongst the grift and graft, most notably that of a scout, light hunter, and as a replacement for the venerable Stinger and Wasp. Note: we have been shown lies. The data is wrong, the performance statistics are overblown, and the Battle ROMs were probably forged.

So what do we actually have here?

Well, a 40 ton 7/11/7 ‘Mech that packs on a whopping 72 pips of armor, this is 52% of the armor load. This paltry amount of armor is 24 points more than a Wasp 1A’s 48 pips. It’s certainly more durable but IDK that I’d call it that much more durable.

So, what is it protecting?

Well, other than a bad cockpit, shite QC, and a shit load of lies about performance? Well, an LRM-5 with 1 ton of ammo, a Medium Laser, and an SRM 2 with one ton of ammo.

Cool. Great use of this chassis.

How much? 749 BV? Oh, so 19 BV less than 2 Wasp 1As. Also of note for the whole ‘in universe bit’: That’s also around 500,000 C-Bills more expensive than 2 Wasp 1As. Cool… why? Like it’s kinda in the little bastard zone but a little bastard whose long range claim to fame is a possible 5 pointer for under 1K should be like 500 BV and named ‘Stinger 3P’.

Liberty’s Rating: D. It’s… I guess it’d be fine for objectives? At least it’s sub 1K BV but if I wanted a 7/11/7 sub 1K BV normal Medium Laser I’d get a Stinger 6G and it’d at least be an MXPL to boot. Honestly that thing might very well win that fight anyhow.

ASN-23

Hey kids? Wanna see an upgrade package?

Remove the SRM 2, you’ve done this? Very good… make the ML an MPL and give the LRM-5 Artemis. Don’t change the armor, keep the same 10 single heat sinks, and retain the same ability to just pop like a grape from the LRM ammo going off.

What? That’s not that much better,  you say? 740 BV for an MPL and an LRM-5 is still kinda too much, you say? Well…

Yea, yea it is.

I know that not every ‘Mech can be a brow beating world beater that can just go toe to toe with everything for the most optimal exchange of BV ever devised but like… even from an in universe perspective who the fuck went ‘Yea, I think a single LRM-5 and an MPL should be enough’? What design genius at defiant got a call from the Lyran High Com. and did this to answer the contract?

Did he at least get bullied for it? Chastised? Fired???

Who am I kidding, knowing the idiots at Defiance I bet he got a fuckin’ promotion.

Liberty’s Rating: D. Man that sure is the same thing but with a blinky light and a weird computer instead of a solid one and some SRMs.

ASN-30

Somehow being more expensive makes a better Little Bastard?

So, the 30 takes a 21, adds 32 pips of armor, rips all the guns out, and powers it with an LFE loaded up with 10 double heat sinks all mounted to an Endo-Steel frame. So, what does it put in all that extra space it has now?

Easy: an ERML and an LB 5-X with one ton of ammo. This is 925 BV and is peak Little Bastard energy. Load it with cluster shells and use it to crit fish enemies that have gotten their armor hammered open while otherwise being a general nuisance for a while anyhow.

Liberty’s Rating: C. This is better than the last ones at least, and at least it’s got some vaguely real armor on it!

ASN-99

Well that’s a big number jump! I hope it’s a big quality jump too!

I… don’t hate this? It’s got a bit more armor than the 30, 98% of possible load at 128 pips, an XL engine, and an ECM suite to power its stealth armor. Armament wise we’ve got a Light PPC, a pair of SRM 2s, a TAG, and… a sword? Sure, why not. All of this is cooled by a 10 pack of double heat sinks.

With a total heat build, with the Stealth on, of 19 heat and you’ve got a pretty workable heat profile of movement -1. At 1,116 BV it’s not the toughest 40 tonners that’s ever existed but with the stealth on and the LPPC firing it’d be an absolute pain in the ass to put down earning it yet another spot in Little Bastard land… even if it is a bit of an expensive one for its damage output.

Liberty’s Rating: C. Honestly kinda like this: Of note, in the OTP Fronc Reaches book the Reaches are attributed as having these in the RAT… I still don’t have them though, annoyingly.

Star League Defense Force Assassin. Credit: Jack Hunter

ASN-101

The year is 3024, and we’re finding ways to save weight.

The 101 is a 21 that has dropped down to 7/11/5 and packs on a meager 64 pips of armor, 47% coverage. It does this so it can add a trio of small lasers to its loadout… For a somewhat familiar 757 BV.

Cool?

It builds movement heat rippling everything off but just dropping the LRM makes it heat neutral on a run and at least gives it a touch more close range damage… even if it is just a trio of small lasers.

Liberty’s Rating: C-. Extra damage and shit is cool, I guess, though going 7/11/5 isn’t great.

ASN-109

Yea, I guess that’s one way to do an upgrade package for the modern era.

The 109 is a rec-guide variant meaning it matches the art. To do this it carries the armor of a 99 with the overall weapons load of the OG 21… or at least something approximating it.

A Light PPC, an MML 5 with 2 tons of ammo in a CASE II bay, and an improved one shot SRM 2. It has stealth armor which is being powered by an Angel ECM suite. The weapons load has a 1 hit pull of 20 heat if it pops the I-OS SRM 2 off with everything else and the stealth armor on. When that’s already fired then you’ve got a clean heat neutral on the run ‘Mech that fires an MML 5 and an LPPC at something with stealth on every turn. For 1,158 BV this is another entry in the ‘stealth armor is annoying, stop doing shit at me’ category of the Little Bastard zone.

Liberty’s Rating: C+. The versatility of the MML is doing a lot here. Having the ability to pitch in in a skirmish before blitzing on someone with some good crit seek is a longstanding favorite archetype of mine and this one certainly can do that. Of the extant main-line Assassins this is certainly the best of them… but what about named ones?

ASN-30 Alice

Who is Alice? What does she want? Is it my kidneys? I bet it’s my kidneys again.

This is the personal ‘Mech of one ‘Mechwarrior Alice Humterderby, helluva name, of the 24th Lyran Guards. It packs the heaviest armor of any Assassin at 134 pips and does something I really wish a normal one would: it boats medium lasers.

Cooled by 10 double heat sinks the Alice carries a C3 Slave, a Beagle Active Probe, a Guardian ECM suite, and 4 IS ERMLs backed up by a Targeting Computer. Powered by a light engine and fully devoid of any and all explosives this thing makes for one hell of an e-war platform that can cover itself while spotting the enemy force for its C3 integrated lance mates. Tack on the movement profile, light engine, and lack of explosives and you’ve got a surprisingly tough little shit head that can hammer some alright damage into someone and only build movement heat.

At 1,507 BV it’s a bit expensive for its damage output but is still workable enough as a movement asset and a real light hunter… like the Assassin always wanted to be.

Liberty’s Rating: B-. a PSR’s worth of TC’d ERMLs backed up by all the EWAR goodies is a fun time and at a relatively affordable price point it’s a pretty alright light whacker/backstabber. I dig it.

Servitor

That’s an absolutely fucking metal name for a ‘Mech that’s basically just a weirdo Solaris jock’s shitbucket.

This ‘Mech started life as an ASN-99 that shed its stealth armor and ECM suite. The TAG, thoroughly not needed in the land of the Game World, has been removed and its spot filled in by a small laser. The sword has been replaced by a small vibroblade. The rest of the armament is rounded out by a Medium X Pulse Laser, and a pair of Small X-Pulse Lasers.

20 heat generated with everything firing/on for 20 heat dissipation makes for a movement heat ‘Mech that can run around with the vibro blade off at Movement-1 until it gets close enough and decides to get to choppin’.

At 1,019 BV this isn’t too bad and could certainly make for a very fun weirdo skirmisher that tries to get after people and chases light ‘Mechs around for most of the game.

Liberty’s Rating: C+. Solaris ‘Mechs are always so weird, I love them.

Final Thoughts

Man, Assassins sure are… something. They run around, do stuff at you, and then explode terribly, or just like fall over at some point. I wish we got a clan grade one, and maybe a few more of the alluded to succ war refits. I’d take a Medium laser boat, maybe something that leans into a more ‘fast support boat’ role with a bigger launcher, perhaps an SRM carrier weirdo. Some variety from the usual would be very cool.

Until next time! Fair weather, good seas and happy guarantee-you’ll-not-go-hungry-ing!
