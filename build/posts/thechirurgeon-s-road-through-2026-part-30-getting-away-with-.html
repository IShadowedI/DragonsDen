TheChirurgeon's Road Through 2026, Part 30: Getting Away With Some Bullshit in Tacoma

July 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive play progress through 2026. Last week I talked about helping run the GHO Baltimore and getting in some more games of Warhammer 40k at the narrative, plus some final prep before the GW US Open in Tacoma.

As you may or may not recall from last time, I uh, didn't take building my list for this event super seriously. In fact, I intentionally built my list for Tacoma around 1. Not requiring that I paint any more models, and 2. Fitting in a small GW minis case. Specifically, this one:

That's primarily because I had to fly to NYC directly from SeaTac on the following Monday for business purposes, and wouldn't be returning home until the following Friday. That's a long trip when you are obstinate about not checking bags, and so I needed something which would fit easily into my larger, overhead stowing rollerbag. When all was said and done, this was the list:

This is not a good list. There's no redundancy on the Forgefiend; I have no idea what I'm doing with the Rhino, and the bolter Rubrics are just bad, especially without an Infernal Master to give them [SUSTAINED HITS 1] or  Sorcerer to give them [LETHAL HITS]. Hexwarp also does very little here - its primary value is 1. Fall back and shoot/charge via Stratagem. Meanwhile Ritual does more for me but can be a bit unreliable. The key parts here are reviving Terminators and the pseudo-grenade strat you can use in close combat.

The General Plan: Put the Rubrics on the table with the ten-man in the Rhino, and put all the Terminators into Deep Strike Reserves. Avoid getting stuck in melee, especially on turn 1, then drop the Terminators in, usually one unit at a time unless there are *very* juicy targets to take out - the Terminators really want re-rolls to hit to buff their shooting; they tend to be much less fun when you can't fish for Lethal Hits against bigger targets. This is also generally why Magnus is better than a second unit of Terminators - even with regeneration, you only need the damage output from one unit, because the second won't have re-rolls unless you're focus firing down a friggin warhound.

And that's basically it. Move up, drop both units of Terminators, cripple my opponent by taking out their biggest threats, then hopefully they bounce off the Terminators and I do it again the following turn. I'm on Purge so I can just do that bullshit. The opponent can't score if they're dead, so my plan is to rack up games where I score very few points turns 1 and 2, then max coring on 3-5.

Thursday

I arrived Thursday night around 9:30pm and took an Uber from SeaTac into downtown Tacoma. One of the worst parts of the Tacoma location is that there's no mass transit to Tacoma from the airport (only to Seattle), meaning if you want to get to the venue you're looking to pay for a $90 Uber to get there. I end up skipping dinner and just crashing in the room more or less immediately - I'm still kind of tired and worn out from the GHO.

Friday

Well, I slept like shit. Maybe four hours of sleep, in what will be a recurring theme for this event. I rise, shower, and make my way downstairs to the hotel lobby bar, which has pretty decent food, all things considered. There I meet up with Jack, Campbell, and Dan for breakfast, and end up getting an insanely large stack of pancakes which will absolutely carry me through lunch - that's generally the goal, since the venue tends to be light on good lunch options. Jack lends me his red lunch tray for my army and I head up to the venue hall.

One thing I will say: they had the pre-painted terrain out for every table and it looked fantastic. I very much have no desire to paint my unpainted set now. I don't love every part of the new prepainted stuff, and it needs a little touch-up - covering some of the sprue gate spots and maybe a spray of matte varnish to remove the gloss - but it looks really good.

Surprisingly, AJ isn't on Green Tide - he's running Freebooter Crew and More Dakka!, which gives him some surprisingly solid shooting, especially into rubrics. Hidden is a problem for me on this, and AJ's "Go" turn is still plenty deadly into my Rubrics and with Wazdakka.

The Mission: Unstoppable Force vs. Immovable Object

We're on Dawn of War deployment on this one - I think I ended up playing Dawn or Sweeping Engagement on like 6 out of 8 missions over the weekend. I end up going second, which is good - I want the Orks to come out where I can see them and be able to respond, especially since I'm not dealing with an army full of Green Tide Da Jump Boyz. AJ pulls Forward Position and No Prisoners round 1 and scores neither but does get a central objective. I snag Display of Might and Centre ground and use a double move to get there.

The game effectively turns into a brawl at midfield - Wazdakka joins in and I've lost all of my Rubrics, my Tzaangors, and my vehicles, but what I have left is two full units of Scarab Terminators. This is more or less a recurring theme in many of my games - I'd often end games with only scarabs left roaming the board, and if my opponents weren't careful to screen me out, I'd take their home and use it for extra scoring.

That's what happens here - I split one unit of Scarabs to the middle, while the other took AJ's home objective, and while I never quite finished off his whole army, I killed Ghaz and scored more on primary late while scoring more secondary points to pull ahead. We tied on primary - 41-41 and I won on Secondary, 39-28.

Result: 90-79, Win

That was a closer game than I'd have liked, but it's always good to start round 1 with a win. I put the two 5-man units of flamers in the Rhino that round and I think that was a mistake - in future games I'll do the 10man in there. I also wildly underestimated tankbusta shooting and paid the price.

Necrons are really a game of going all-in; you have to completely kill those units or they'll slowly regenerate and become a problem. This makes splitting fire really fraught; keep that in mind later for when I make that mistake. The Wraiths are also interesting in that I need to more or less avoid them early and kill everything else. If I can just tag wraiths with -1 to hit from the Forgefiend, they become nearly worthless.

The Lokhusts are also a big problem, since they're tuned to kill Rubrics. My hope is that Ryan tries to kill the Exalted Rubrics with them, as a 4+ invulnerable save and extra regen may make that hard to do in one or even two turns.

The Mission: Meatgrinder

I played Meatgrinder a lot over the weekend - I think it was half of my games. The big thing about this one is protecting your home and taking the opponent's. And in that sense, Ryan handed me a gift: He put the Void Dragon in reserves and left his home undefended as he pushed to the middle of the table. He caught me off guard turn 1 with his big unit of auto-advancing/advance and charge Lychguard - this was more a mistake on my part than a lack of info from him - and that caused me problems I didn't want, but I dropped both units of Scarabs behind him on turn 2, shot him off his home objective, and spent the rest of the game owning his backfield.

I ended up only +2 on primary here as Ryan was able to take my home with the Void Dragon late but I stayed consistently ahead on secondary scoring all game and prevented him from getting Centre Ground, Display of Might, and Plunder all game. I think Ryan needed to be aggressive early, putting the Void Dragon on the table and going to mid, while screening me off his home.

Result: 96-70, Win

That puts me up at 2-0 and I go into the next game pretty confident.

The Wardens are the big worry here - their ability to get a once per game 4+ Feel No Pain makes them a nightmare to remove with Scarabs, since they can tank the damage pretty easily and then just charge in and kill the Terminators. Games against Custodes - especially Terminators vs. Custodes - always end up being a battle of who can roll the most 4+ invulnerable saves, making them a crapshoot.

The Mission: Meatgrinder

My plan is to just scrap at the middle of the table. If Ryan gives me his home - and he will since he has to spread out with very elite units - I'll take that with Scarabs and use it to outscore him on primary. To that end, I need to take out the bikes early so the rest of the army is stuck footslogging, but the six Terminators are an issue as well.

This is one where I mostly end up outscoring Ryan on Primary, keeping him off Kill More pretty effectively. Ryan had some good luck early on with saves but that kind of flipped midgame when he needed to pass his 4+ saves and just didn't, tilting things in my favor. It wasn't pure luck, but it definitely accelerated things when his Trajan failed something like nine out of fourteen saves to just keel over dead in the face of Rubric shooting.

Result: 89-45, Win

This is an insane result - I shouldn't be 3-0. The good news is that I can't make it into the top bracket without going 4-0, and there's absolutely no chance of that happening.

Meanwhile at the next table over was this absolute Chad, Ben Williams, who was running a Warhound Titan with his Sisters of Battle. I've played Ben before at Tacoma - check out my write-up from 2023 to see the recap of my round 1 game against him - and he's an absolute delight. That night I ended up hanging out with some of the GW staff, playing card games and grabbing a hotel lobby bar burger - the Marriott's hotel bar is expensive but they don't skimp on the food so I'm usually satisified with the order. Saturday

I grab breakfast with Jack Hunter in the hotel lobby bar. They've solved the problem of being insanely understaffed by just putting out a buffet, which isn't amazing but means way better service and faster food. Jack's struggling this morning after last night. I'm feeling good but tired - I didn't sleep much, but I didn't overdo it on drinking, either - a few beers and a solid Painkiller at the McMenamin's tiki bar.

I'm 3-0 today and like, I definitely shouldn't be. This army sucks. The bolter Rubrics haven't done anything for me but die, and barely any slower than the rest of the army. The Exalted Sorcerer has rolled a 1 on his revive rolls every single game. The Forgefiend has yet to actually tag anything and really give -2 to hit. The flamer rubrics are the only unit which actually do anything besides the Terminators.

This time it's Green Tide, and we're on Purge vs. Take and Hold again. The only consolation here is that my list theoretically matches up well into Noah's - I can kill three units of 20 boyz in a turn with the right setup. But if I get stuck in melee, I'm more or less fucked.

The Mission: Unstoppable Force vs. Immovable Object

I'm going second. Noah goes full send early and dumps Stormboyz and one unit of boyz into my army, but crucially fails one of his boyz charges after Da Jump. I'm able to kill one unit of Stormboyz by grenading them via strat in melee to kill three models, then the other three go down to fists and staves. I largely think this is correct: Noah needs to play aggressively early on to put me on the back foot, but where he messes up is not aggressively screening me out of the backfield, allowing me to drop in behind him, take his home, and whittle him down while also taking the middle objective.

That big gap in the middle means that even after I lose half my army to those early charges, I'm able to stabilize and more or less with the game with two big Terminator units. This one came down to the wire - All Noah had left was Ghaz, a weirdboy, a unit of boyz on my home, and Kommandos coming in from reserves. He drew Engage on all fronts and Secure No Man's land on the final turn, and was holding Cleanse. He maxed primary while I was at 37, but I was going to max primary on my turn. He brought his Kommandos in on his Expansion to Cleanse twice, but couldn't score Secure thanks to my Terminators on the middle objective. This mean the had to score Engage.

Noah rolls a 1 on his Da Jump roll. He can't score engage this turn. That puts him at 91 VP. At the end of his turn I score 5 VP for Burden of Trust, then I draw Assassination and Centre Ground. Without having to kill Ghaz, that puts me at 92 VP, good enough to win. I might still have been able to pull this off if he hadn't failed Da Jump, but ultimately I won by 1 VP because Noah failed the roll.

Result: 92-91, Win

Holy shit, I am 4-0 for the first time at a GW event. And with a terrible fucking list. HOW DID THIS HAPPEN? I CAN'T KEEP GETTING AWAY WITH IT!

I grab lunch with Craig Sniffen, our weirdest guy and one of our key backend devs as well as just being an amazing painter with lovely Rainbow Warriors.

We have quick, shitty event space concessions lunch and then I'm off to get my ass beat in by whoever I'm facing in the top bracket. I check in on the rest of the Tabletop Battles crew and it seems like everyone's doing OK and having a good time. I'm the only top cut player of our core group, though Floof is also in the top cut. I'm hoping to play them at some point but if I don't I can just lord it over them forever that I am still 1-0 against them after winning in the LVO teams tournament against their admech list.

The Mission: Unstoppable Force vs. Immovable Object

I relieved some of the pressure with my Tzaangors but this would ultimately lock me in my Deployment Zone most of the game and make it impossible to get out of the Rhino for two turns with the 10man squad, a brutal outcome.

The big thing here was just space - Glyn locked out all game, forcing the Scarabs to come down in bad positions where I wasn't able to meaningfully challenge. He got some nasty charges off early that made this easier, but he also was just really smart about not leetting me get the angles I wanted. I needed to go first in this matchup and I didn't get it. I was the first player to up more than 37 points against Glyn in the event and even that was a result of desperate late-game clawing.

Reuslt: 52-100, Loss

All of my scoring came from Pimary. I think I pulled 8 on Secondary, and I only pulled 15 on Primary in the final turn by going second. Any time I play Glyn I usually get a barrage of messages that are like "how was it?" Glyn has a reputation for being an intense guy on the table, but I am happy to report that since I first met him in January at LVO TT and Palm Springs he's improved steadily and was very pleasant to play. As always the real test is how someone plays when they're losing but I enjoyed our game and it was much more comfortable. This is also likely due to how Orks are pretty straightfoward; against T'au it's much easier to feel like you're getting cheated or tricked somehow just because of how much bullshit they can do. When Orks charge into me and beat my face in sideways, I'm never confused about how we got there.

So anyways, Glyn: B+, would play again. Big improvement, didn't scream "Waaagh!"

I more or less expected to eat shit in the top cut but I'm still hoping to start 5-1; only the first six rounds count for Best Overall scoring and while this isn't the best version of my Thousand Sons list from a paint standpoint, it's good enough for best overall, maybe. Also, I'm not in the Shadow Round, which is huge - I do not want to be playing warhammer until midnight; I want to be eating dinner with my friends.

Result: 93-57, Win

OK I know how I won this won, and it was by basically by doing the same thing, only more and without a Land Raider. This means I'm 5-1 and in a really good spot to win Best Overall - my competition is basically Scott Ketchum and Brian Jones, and so I decide to spend some time over at the hobby challenge station fixing my army.

Basically the big thing I have to address is the bases - I originally based them with Agrellan Earth texture paint, which creates a cracked, dry earth look. Unfortunately it flakes off all the goddamn time, leaving patches of black base showing through. So I bought a brush and a pot of Karak Stone and spent about half an hour painting over the black spots missing texture paint with just dirt-colored paint. This did the job pretty well and helped ensure I didn't drop a shitload of paint points - I needed all the help I could get.

Credit: Robert "TheChirurgeon" Jones

I set up for Showcase and then went to dinner with the crew. One of the big frustrations about these days having three-hour rounds and starting at 9:30 is that you don't get out for dinner until 8:30 at the earliest, so we had to beeline somewhere nearby with a still-open kitchen, as our usual pizza place - The Rock - closed after last year. Tragic.

We ended up at the biergarten down the street, where we've had food and drinks before. It's German cuisine styled with big tables and benches like a beer hall and it's legitimately a great time. We ate way too much and had some good beer and good hangouts overall. I ended up getting the Steak Frites, which was very solid, and we split some cheese Spatzle, Corn Dogs, and a pretzel with the table. We also turned in relatively early that night, which was fine by me - I was exhausted.

Sunday

Ah, day three. I can't win the event and these games don't matter. I played like absolute ass, making decisions that were high risk and likely to end the game sooner, win or lose. which isn't to say my opponents weren't good or didn't play well; but I was not as buttoned up as I could have been, especially in game 8.

That wraps up a surprisingly successful campaign at Tacoma. I feel pretty good about my shot at Best Overall given the 5-1 start, but then I learn Scott Ketchum started 6-0 and lost in the shadow round. My Thousand Sons may look better than his sisters (I don't know if this is true - I didn't see his sisters), but they almost certainly aren't an entire win better. That's a bit of a bummer as we head up to watch the awards ceremony.

Of course, here's the thing: Scott already has a golden ticket, which means he gets the free plane ticket to Barcelona, but the ticket itself passes to the second place player for Best Overall which was... oh shit, it was me.

I've just won a golden ticket, giving me an official invite to the World Championships in Barcelona.

ARE YOU FUCKING KIDDING ME??

How did I pull this off with this list, of all lists? I basically threw this in my case for the GHO Narrative two weeks ago as a way to sandbag games and here it is somehow pulling off a 4-0/5-1 start and Best Overall. I'm immediately very fucking glad I did the touch-up work on my bases before showcase now, and completely blown away by the fact that somehow my best start ever at one of these events was facilitated by a list I felt was just dumb bullshit. Like honestly even having that ten-man unit be flamers would have been a huge step up. Or putting an Infernal Master with the bolters to give them Sustained Hits. Hell the Rhino might have even been a mistake. Pulling off a TiWP with this list is criminal.

And as an enduring credit to the list, it barely was touched by this week's balance pass. Sure, I'd have to drop Purge with Ritual of Regeneration but I honestly think the list would have just been better off running Rubricae Phalanx.

We end up grabbing dinner in the hotel lobby with Brian Jones and Nick Nanavati before they head off to catch their flight. Hanging out with Nick is always fun and I end up talking to him at pretty much every event. And I think that he is, this time, forced to begrudgingly admit I might be a better player than he gave me credit for, given my ability to pilot a terrible list to a top cut finish.

Final Thoughts

This is so dumb. This stupid game. Of course the one time I just say "fuck it, let's take a bad list that'll fit in my suitcase easy" I end up having my best event ever and winning a golden fucking ticket.

Anyways, I got a ride back to SeaTac from Jack and flew straight to New York at 7am on Monday morning. I'll talk more about that and prep for the Lone Star Open next week, though.

See you next Thursday.
