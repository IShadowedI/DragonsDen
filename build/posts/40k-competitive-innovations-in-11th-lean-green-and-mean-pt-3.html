Thoughts

The Dark Angels are going fast and furious at our first event, combining deadly Advance/Charge melee threats like the Lion, Azrael's unit and a newly improved Ravenwing hammer with lots of Speeder shooting in support. Up against them, the new extra chunky Take and Hold spin on Lions, using walls of Allarus and Custodian Guard to lock down objectives and murder anything that comes too close. To maximise the value of sitting on objectives, the Custodian units have cheap inquisitors with them, who are very much no joke if they're double shooting with wound re-rolls and the Lions buff up.

The headline here is that the Dark Angels absolutely can table the Custodes if they're methodical about it. There are enough 4W models on the Custodes side that it isn't just instant once the Vengeances open up, but the combination of those and some powerful melee units that absolutely can lift a decent chunk of a Custodes brick is going to add up. The question then turns to whether there's anything the Custodes can do with scoring dynamics to try and flip that script.

Maybe? I kind of feel like they need to either go really hard or extremely slow. The good news for them is that the Ravenwing's scoring does require them to move out into all quarters, which will inevitably set up some targets or counter-charge options, and naturally splits up the army a bit. Going in all guns blazing and trying to ram as many Allarus up the board as possible could catch enough of these units and overwhelm the guns.

I think I'd prefer a really slow-rolled plan though. Put two big threats in the sky for Ingress (probably the Captain Allarus and one big Allarus squad), hide the army as best as possible, then just push up onto the expansion turn 1 with the other Allarus unit. Hidden should be enough to stop them getting killed. The Ravenwing don't actually want to take the central objective, as it doesn't change their scoring, so move onto that on turn 2 (or Ingress just off it, if going second) to keep the new objective points coming, and try to pick off units the Ravenwing are playing quarters with. Then on turn three go loud, unleash your other Ingress tool (and the Venatari too) and try and hit hard enough and with enough units still live that it cascades through to a win. Still a tough game to win, because those melee nasties will really tell here, but I think realistically there are too many options for the Dark Angels to punish anything else. As it was, the Unforgiven seem to have gotten the jump on the Custodes and quickly dispatched them, taking a comfortable victory. Part of the reason I prefer the slower plan for Custodes here is that the Recon side can easily cruise to 100 with a swift tabling, so if you go for an all-in plan, they can spend the first couple turns holding back and maximising methodical destruction without it really costing them.

Thoughts

Covered in the showdown, this build highlights many of the best tools Dark Angels have available to them, and proves they're not just the Deathwing Knights army. The new ability to double attach with Ravenwing Command squads can create some very nasty threats, and this setup where both Samael and a Ravenwing Champion will be smashing for three damage on the charge seems especially good, particularly as it ticks the box for being a RAVENWING FLY unit and thus unlocks all the Stratagems (including the option to up detection range, which can be helpful for the Speeders). Upgraded Vengeances support that with incredible all-purpose shooting, and the Lion and ICCs are just generally great. Very strong build, congratulations Josh!

Thoughts

A bit of an academic one to finish, as this list gets properly carved apart by the Dataslate. I quite like the concept of running this setup as Recon rather than Purge, as the Stratagems and enhancements of Retaliation Cadre support it surprisingly well, and the units selected here strike a good balance between leaning in and maintaining enough killing power. Can't do that any more though, so congratulations to Jonathan and sorry your list was too beautiful for this world.

The Best of the Rest

There were 4 more players on 4-1 records. They were:

  • 2nd (4.5-0.5) - Justin Yun - Orks (Taktikal Brigade/Da Big Hunt) - Disruption: Go wide board control Orks.
  • 3rd - Paul Reynolds - Orks (Taktikal Brigade/Green Tide) - Take and Hold: Boyz horde and some Dakkarigs.
  • 4th - Mike Sanchez - Chaos Space Marines (Pactbound Zealots) - Priority Assets: Abaddon with double Defiler, double Destructor.
  • 5th - Logan Funderburk - Chaos Daemons (Cavalcade of Chaos/Blood Legion) - Purge the Foe: Bloodcrusher spam with a Bloodthirster and Skarbrand supervising.
