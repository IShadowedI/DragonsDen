TheChirurgeon's Road Through 2026, Part 47: The November Ettin RTT

November 12th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back, Dear Reader, to my ongoing blog of hobby and competitive play progress through 2026. Last week I was doing some hobby work prepping for the Ettin RTT, hoping to get in a few practice games, both to refresh myself on the rules I'd be playing and to just get in some more Warhammer before the World Championships in December. I started the year off hot games-wise, but I've been very lax since July or so, mostly due to a lot more travel and work requirements.

My List

Look at all those boltguns. What a time to be alive.

Ok so first thing to note: This is a bad list. It's not good. Vindicators aren't great in 11th, and Land Raiders are also bad. But I have them, they're painted well, and I can work with them. In an ideal world I'd have at least five more Terminators, and a trio of Mutilators + Kravek Morne to round the group out. Huron and Terminators would be a fine combo if I could get another five but at the moment he's really here because he's painted. This is probably what I'm going to play at most of the Grand Narrative, because it's playably good but bad enough that I'm not liable to be accused of going too hard in the paint at an event where many people just don't play that much Warhammer.

As far as the units here go, there are three big unit threats: The Terminators with the Terminator Sorcerer, who are here to do a lot of bolter shots and drop Death Hex on an enemy unit, Bile with the unit of Chosen, Huron, and the Masters of the Maelstrom, and Huron with his unit of Legionaries, plus the Master of Executions with Touched by the Warp so they get the PSYKER keyword and better bolters. That's really the way I'm working here: I'm throwing out an assload of bolter shots at S6 AP-2 and sometimes Lethal Hits or Sustained Hits to take out targets. It's better than it looks, but it can't compete with Space Marines, who can just do that more efficiently and with better movement.

Cabal of Chaos here gets me +1 Strength on my ranged attacks with PSYKER units when I do a Dark Pact. There are also some great Stratagems in the Detachment to do mortals and heal dead models.

Veterans of the Long War gets me a second reactive move, plus that Focus of Hatred/Oath of moment ability helps make the Land Raider and the Vindicator actually playable.

My expectations were 2-1 and a best painted win. So I packed up and took my army over to Ettin games, which is a short enough drive that I can do an RTT and easily be home in time for dinner. For some reason the event was running 10-7 instead of 9-6, which was a bit annoying on the back end but I did appreciate the ability to sleep in a bit more than usual.

Round 1: vs. Connor's Ultramarines

Connor was playing Ultramarines and it's important to note that this list was not using the WCW FAQ, so this is also running Deathwatch Terminators with no nerfs. Good stuff - I'm cooked. Lists weren't made public for this event and I'm not going to go back and ask for them, so the key elements here are:

  • Guilliman
  • Calgar + Wardens + Bladeguard Veterans
  • Two Impulsors full of Eradicators
  • Incursors
  • Two units of Deathwatch Terminators
  • One unit of Heavy Intercessors
  • Phobos Librarian
  • Reivers

The Mission: Take and Hold vs. Take and Hold

This list is faster and meaner than mine. Its terminators are better by far, its Eradicators will kill my vehicles easily, and its big brick of Bladeguard Veterans are a nightmare. I had the first turn, so used the opportunity to push into Connor's Deployment Zone with Raiders on the left, charging his Heavy Intercessors and Impulsor. He was then able to intervene into them with his Deathwatch Terminators, but not with full attacks. My goal here was to try and tie him up an extra turn and block his movement on the right side, getting a good shot into his Calgar Brick by isolating them, hoping to kill them before anything else.

It kind of worked - The Raiders survived and pulled the Terminators back into Connor's deployment zone for a turn, but the Rhino full of Chosen got one-shot killed by the Impulsor full of melta, and the Chosen inside were charged and mostly wiped. I definitely misjudged the distance on those Bladeguard, but they also rolled a 6 to advance so I should have played it safer.

On my turn I pushed to the middle to try and wipe the Bladeguard and my Terminator Sorcerer promptly shit the bed and failed his Death Hex, which would be a brutal blow that meant the unit survived my all-out assault. I needed a decisive kill here and didn't get it, and as a result had to deal with being tied up at midtable while Guilliman pushed forward alive. I was sitting on about 45 VP at this point and ahead but as turn 3 rolled around it was clear I didn't have the gas left to keep it up and the second unit of Deathwatch Terminators hit my deployment zone.

The end result was just getting mostly tabled by the bottom of 3 by the insane amount of shooting left as I'd been forced to overcommit to killing the Calgar brick and didn't have the output I needed to kill anything else. The Eradicator Impulsors went more or less untouched and I didn't have the output I needed to kill them.

Result: 47-100, Loss

That was a brutal loss, and one I probably take even with the updates to Deathwatch Terminators. I just don't have the juice to compete with an army that's basically "mine, but way better on a per-unit basis." The good news is that now I'm in the loser bracket and liable to avoid nasty meta space marine lists.

I end up grabbing lunch with my on-again, off-again teammate, Chris Johnson, who's here playing a fun list that's substantially better than mine. We end up grabbing Portillo's for lunch - they just opened across the street and it's a solid lunch. Then it's back to the game mines for round 2.

Round 2: vs. Clay Sinclair's Chaos Space Marines

It's a mirror match! Well, kind of. Clay is running Veterans and Cabal, but with 20 terminators, two terminator sorcerers, Kravek Morne and a unit of Mutilators, Obliterators, and a Defiler. It's a more elite unit than mine, and one that'll struggle to spread out and do things. He's running one unit of Traitor Guardsmen and a unit of Beastmen, which give him some cool flexibility and forward OC but cost a bit more and offer no sticky objective shenanigans.

The Mission: Take and Hold Mirror

I'm going second on this one so I wait for Clay to move out on his first turn - he puts his Terminators too far forward and holds his Defiler back, while moving his Obliterators up. Deploying the Obliterators isn't the best move here, but he just didn't realize they only move 4" and that's a mistake I've seen before. They're just too slow to walk anywhere.

So I do what I do best and crash into him - I advance and Charge Bile and his unit of Chosen and Huron and his unit, killing the entire unit of Terminators and the Sorcerer without breaking a sweat. Meanwhile, the Nurglings move into position to help with the Defiler - the plan is for them to intervene and give it -1 to hit - and the Raiders make short work of the Traitor Guardsmen and get stuck in with the Obliterators. The Oblits can punch hard, but they'd rather be shooting and so keeping them fighting is a win.

Clay brings out his Defiler and loses it on the following turn, but makes a good push from my right flank with the Beastmen and Kravek Morne and his guys, making a scoring push  but failing their charge attempts. I had put my Vindicator back into reserves during deployment and used the opportunity to bring it in and shoot them, whittling the unit down so I could finish it off the following turn.

The Mutilators did manage to kill the Land Raider, but by the the time the final Terminator unit dropped Clay didn't have many options left and I threw Terminators + Legionaries + bikers at it to finish it off.

Result: 97-20, Win

Clay was a fun opponent and relatively new to the game - it's always rough to body someone in games like this but we had a good time and I think he learned a lot. I gave him some feedback after the game about using his reactive moves more and being more aggressive with the Defiler - he really put himself in a position to be alpha struck and it cost him.

Round 3: vs. Jeff Murphy's Tyranids

The final round was into Jeff's Tyranids, running Reconnaissance with the Ambush Predators and Vanguard Onslaught Detachments. His army was a Swarmlord, Tyrannofex, Winged Hive Tyrant, Biovore, Norn Assimilator, Genestealers + Broodlord, Hormagaunts, Raveners + Ravener Prime, two Neurolictors, Deathleaper, and a unit of Zoanthropes with a Neurotyrant.

The Mission: Purge and Secure vs. Reconnaissance Sweep

I started this one by using my Nurglings and Raiders' Infiltrators deployment to box Jeff in his deployment zone, then pulled them back using my Redeploy from the Masters of the Maelstrom. That kept Jeff from pushing too far forward, too fast. I was going second, which meant I could let him step forward tentatively and punish him.

This game was really about how nasty bolters are now and how annoying Nurglings can be. I pulled my Raiders forward with bikes on turn 1 and killed most of the Genestealers with shooting and a charge from the Raiders, and I used the Nurglings to tie up the Genestealers and keep the Raiders alive with the -1 to hit. The Norn intervened into the Genestealers and well, it spent two turns not killing them before my Vindicator and Land Raider shot it to death before it could ever reach the middle objective for its feel no pain.

I got super lucky in this one with my charges on round 2, making a series of 7/8-inch charges to tie up Jeff's units and keep things in my favor. I ended up pushing him back all game and using bolters to just blow large holes in a number of his units. S6 shooting from bolters is very nasty, and the ability to just plow forward and drop 20-30 shots at a time on enemy units with Letha/Sustained hits and full re-rolls made chewing through even bigger targets easy.

Jeff surveying the damage

This was another fun one but it was a bloodbath. At one point Jeff dropped his Raveners down near my Chosen brick and asked aloud "how much damage could they take in shooting?" and I could practically hear the Curb Your Enthusiasm theme start playing in my head. We had some fun moments with it despite the bloodbath however.

Result: 100-30, Win

Well, that set me to 2-1 and I did in fact win best painted. Jeff and Clay were great sports and I appreciated them both playing brutal games and keeping good spirits throughout. That's never easy.

Jeff had a lovely army, by the way - if you want to check out his work, he has an Instagram page where he shows off his painting and does commissions. Cool stuff.

Final Thoughts

That does it for this week - it was great to get in some practice games and I feel good about this as a Grand Narrative list, at least - walloping inexperienced players and medium lists while getting bodied by top meta lists feels like the area I want to shoot for most of the time, and I'll have some extra treats to add so I can scale up or down depending on need. Now it's just time to get a bunch of painting done.

See you next Thursday.
