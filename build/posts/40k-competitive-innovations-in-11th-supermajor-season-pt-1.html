[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Supermajor Season pt.1

September 30th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Supermajor season has officially kicked off, with the 2026 LGT entered into the books. Due to the fact this event didn't wrap up until Monday night (after I kicked off data work for this), and the arrival of the September Update today, the LGT is going into part 2, which this week we'll be running on Thursday, followed by your normal Lowest of Men-driven edition on Friday. Got it? Hope so. If you haven't already,  check out our review of the balance updates (including all the shiny toughness and bolter upgrades across the game), otherwise it's on to some events.

Some of my commentary in this is informed by the September update, so thanks to Games Workshop for providing us with a review copy.

Archetype

Elite Melee Marauders

Thoughts

Huron's finest take our first trophy today, going for a full send melee setup with a mixture of Terminators, Chosen, Possessed and Mutilators, the latter of which seems to have finally found a correct price point. That's even more true with the new Marine updates - Mutilators don't see any changes in capabilities or costs, while some of the other stuff here climbs a fair bit. It all gets better (and in particular, a big Terminator brick with Kravek gets real mean), but you'll have to find some cuts, so a relatively inexpensive trade/troubleshooting piece seems really good. Congratulations to Chris!

Thoughts

I think I've come within a few points of putting this exact list on the table when I needed to fill a slot at a team event in a hurry. Two C'tan and two Wraiths is a heck of a lot of heft to try and carve through, and I'm expecting something of a resurgence in this kind of build going forward - Wraiths are at the exact right breakpoints on S and T where hardly any of the Marine changes affect them (they weren't killing Terminators without Hungry Void for the AP anyway), and the departure of Oaths removes one of the best counters. I'm certainly planning to give mine a go, and congratulations to Peter for showcasing Canptek capabilities with an undefeated run.

Thoughts

Robots vs. zombies for our first Showdown of the week, pitting a different (and sadly fleeting) spin on Kastelans and Lords against the current Death Guard hotness. The Death Guard are looking to use a wall of Poxwalkers to hold fast while their Defilers do their work, with Mortarion to swoop in and clear out anything especially threatening. The AdMech have a surprisingly similar plan, aiming to grind the foe out with the Rad-Zone detachment rule, Skitarii and the tanks, with Characters and Kastelans to deploy to handle threats. Less pure chaff, but an overlapping ethos.

Who comes out on top? I think the Death Guard have some challenges here. Their biggest one is that they really want two of their Contagions active, but are going to have to pick one and go with it. If they don't pick the boosted AP, they're going to really struggle to put the robots down, as Scriptural Prognosis is exceptional for no-selling a Defiler unless they get very lucky with Devastating Wounds. However, without the Stealth/-1 to hit, AdMech might just be able to cleanly wipe them off the table, especially as that Vanguard/Marshal/Manipulus unit out of a Dunerider threatens to cleanly lift the big Poxwalker unit. This being a game of Actions also makes the AdMech's detachment rule really shine, as unless the foe pushes forward (into the jaws of guns/robots) they could have their plans disrupted by a badly timed Battle-shock. Between a Callidus and good Battleline, the AdMech are also better set up to force Actions through when needed. Finally - the Robots actually can just one-punch Mortarion, if Cawl's got him Oathed. Not a sure thing, but within the bounds of possibility, so has to be accounted for.

What's the plan from the Death Guard's side? Perhaps be a bit less pushy with the Poxwalkers than normal - use one unit to block the AdMech from double tapping Sabotage turn one, but otherwise get them out of the deployment zone (no Radial Suffusion, mercifully), but don't just throw them away, and stage for pushing up with critical mass in the mid-game, ideally with the Defilers in tow. The AdMech aren't that great at being super proactive, so if you can keep them from scoring early, then try to grind them mid-late, that could plausibly work, though there is a risk that their better options for forcing scoring will prove a problem. Whether that or just a battle-shock riot, the AdMech did comfortably take the day.
