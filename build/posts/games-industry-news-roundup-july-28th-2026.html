Games Industry News Roundup- July 28th, 2026

July 28th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Here at Tabletop Battles, we know that it’s hard to keep track of all the news happening all the time in the games industry. So much is always going on, all over the place, all the time. With games of all sorts, and their related media, being an informed gamer can feel impossible some days. 

That's why every week, we help you stay in the know by rounding up five of the biggest recent stories in the gaming and gaming-related pop culture sphere in the Games Industry News Roundup. Our trusty news boy, Dan “Swiftblade” Richardson, is right here at the very real, and cool TTB newsdesk with the scoop.

Amazon’s Warhammer 40k TV Project Brings in Veteran Horror Director

Image credit: Amazon

Mum has been the word to the public when it comes to any details regarding the in-development Warhammer 40K TV show project from Amazon studios, but there’s one group that’s important enough for Games Workshop to ease up its secrecy and provide a few details: The shareholders.

After months of silence on the collaboration between Games Workshop, Amazon, and Henry Cavill, Games Workshop provided a brief update on the project in their 2025-2026 Annual report regarding the project, as well as a few other projects they have cooking with the streaming giant.

After an entire year of negotiations, Amazon and Games Workshop were able to decide on terms for Warhammer screen adaptations through Amazon, and at the time only shared that Henry Cavill would be executive producer on projects set in the universe. The two companies have been silent since then regarding their updates, but Games Workshop did confirm in its annual report that veteran horror director Mike Flanagan is attached to the premium live action show set in the 40k universe.

“The project continues in line with our contractual agreement with Amazon,” Games Workshop writes in the report. “This same contract prohibits us from sharing certain specific details or commercial terms. What we can share is that Amazon has brought onboard United Artists (UA) and Mike Flanagan. Vertigo and Henry Cavill remain involved as they have been for some time. Having completed initial outlines, Mike should soon be moving on to script. Meanwhile, UA have been decisive and brought their renowned pace and quality to the project.”

Flanagan is one of the most critically acclaimed filmmakers in modern horror, with projects like The Haunting of Hill House, Midnight Mass, and Doctor Sleep. His most recent projects include writing the first draft of the upcoming Clayface film for DC, as well as a new adaptation of Stephen King’s Carrie for Amazon.

Alongside this update, Games Workshop also shared details about two other upcoming projects. The first is a new Warhammer Secret Level episode for the anthology series second season, this time set in the Age of Sigmar universe. Finally, while we wait for Flanagan and Cavill's big premium project, Games Workshop confirmed that work on a new animated show, Deathwatch, is currently underway at Amazon, with John Orloff writing the script and animation being done by Blur studios, who were also responsible for animation Secret Level. Orloff’s most notable work includes writing two episodes of Band of Brothers and being lead writer for its follow up series, Masters of the Air. 

No updates regarding release date have been provided for any of these projects.

CMON Cuts Ties with NFT Company After Multi-Million Dollar Planned Acquisition

Credit: CMON Games

CMON just absolutely cannot get a win, at all, ever. This time feels like their own fault though. After announcing plans to invest over $2 million in the NFT game company Blissful Link 3 months ago, CMON has apparently pulled out from the agreement. 

According to a motion filed with the Hong Kong Stock exchange, the deal between CMON and Blissful Link for CMON to acquire the company for $2.1 million fell through due to Blissful Link failing to meet the conditions of the initial agreement. Since the conditions of this agreement were not met, CMON has pulled out from the contract. Fortunately for CMON, this does mean that they aren’t once again out of several million dollars.

CMON doesn’t elaborate on the precise conditions Blissful Link failed to meet as per their agreement, but it is likely due to financial concerns. In the initial agreement, CMON stated that the acquisition would only go through following a thorough examination of Blissful Link’s finances and assets, to ensure that they were properly valued and nothing was being hidden from CMON. Were CMON to discover an issue with Blissful Link’s books, the deal would quickly terminate.

Blissful Link is responsible for creating and championing games based on NFT blockchain technology, most notably its Capverse series of games. The company’s last posted financials at the time of the deal were from FY 2024, where it reported a loss of around $197,000 dollars, and it did not share any information regarding its 2025 finances.

Despite these shady figures, CMON was ready to bet big on NFT technology as the future of several of it’s titles back in April when the deal was announced, writing “The company believes that in order to continue to be relevant in the games industry and to expand the group’s revenue stream, the group would need to conduct digital transitioning and venture into video game development and Web3 projects. Digital transitioning would have the benefits of enhanced visual effects, have apps that handle scoring, timing etc [sic], would enable a diversified number of players and are more accommodating to players not within the same vicinity.”

CMON has not publicly announced if it still plans to pursue NFT and blockchain based technology following the cancellation of their deal with Blissful Link. 

World of Warcraft Is Crossing Over with Dungeons & Dragons

Credit: Blizzard Entertainment

An accidental leak has spoiled a big surprise for fans of World of Warcraft and Dungeons & Dragons, as a collaboration between the two universes is coming to a shelf near you. 

The initial leak came from the digital tabletop website Roll20, who posted an article stating that pre-orders were live for a new crossover between World of Warcraft and Dungeons & Dragons. This was apparently posted early and quickly taken down, but the cat can’t be put back in the bag once it’s out. 

From what could be gathered from the post, the new book would be a crossover setting book for players to go on Dungeons & Dragons adventures in Azeroth, the fantasy setting of World of Warcraft. The book would include background information for players to use for their games, as well as classes, races, abilities, and monsters from the setting. This would put the book in line with other crossover D&D books released by Wizards in the past, including Magic the Gathering settings like Ravnica and pop culture franchises like Rick and Morty. 

While there’s been no official word from Blizzard or Wizards of the Coast acknowledging the leak, it’s likely that the World of Warcraft crossover is planned to be officially announced at Wizard’s upcoming Gen Con keynote show, hosted by Baldur’s Gate 3 actor Theo Solomon.

PlayStation Network Outage Temporarily Locks Players Out of Their Digital Games 

Credit: Sony Entertainment

PlayStation’s been under fire from gamers all over the world following its announcement that it plans to cease physical disc production in 2028, and a massive outage of the PlayStation Network (PSN) online servers is only intensifying the criticism. 

Last Thursday, PSN experienced a major service outage that lasted most of the day. During the time the servers were down, PlayStation users were unable to access their accounts or play any of their digital games. The outage also caused the delay in a major FIFA 26 Esports championship and coincided with the release of the highly anticipated fighting game Marvel TOKON.. Sony was able to have all online servers and systems working by Friday morning, but hasn’t provided any comment as to the source of the outage.

The PSN servers going down for an extended time has redoubled the criticism leveled at Sony for its announcement to cease production on physical disks, as the outage highlighted the fragility of digital ownership relying wholly on online access, which could be prone to interruptions or outages outside of a player’s control.

Massive server outages locking them out of games they paid money for isn’t an unreasonable thing to be concerned about for PlayStation owners, as a 2011 incident left the PlayStation servers offline for 24 days.

Wizards of the Coast President Stepping Down

Credit: Wizards of the Coast

A major shakeup at Wizards of the Coast is coming soon, as the company’s president, John Hight, is stepping down from his role as company president on September 1st, 2026.

Hight joined the Wizards of the Coast team as president in 2024, following 13 years at Blizzard, where he was the longtime general manager for Blizzard’s hit Warcraft franchise. During his tenure at Wizards of the Coast, Hight oversaw the massive expansion of IP crossover content for both Magic: The Gathering and Dungeons & Dragons, as well as a push for Wizards to push harder into the world of video games with its IPs following the breakout success of Baldur’s Gate 3. 

While the crossovers, such as Magic’s Universes Beyond line, have been a runaway financial success for Wizards and its parent company, Hasbro, its digital forays have stymied. It looks like Hasbro and Wizards is walking their video game plans significantly following this failed bet, as the company last week noted a $56 million impairment charge in its annual financial report on cancelled video game projects that had yet to be announced.

Regarding Hight’s departure, Wizards of the Coast told Gamesindustry.biz, “After two years as President of Wizards of the Coast, John Hight is transitioning from the role, effective September 1. We're deeply grateful for his leadership and the contributions he's made to our teams and our games during his tenure. John will continue to support Wizards in an advisory capacity while pursuing other creative and professional opportunities he's passionate about.”

“There is no change to the strength of the Wizards business. Magic: The Gathering and Dungeons & Dragons continue to perform incredibly well, consistent with our most recent earnings release, and our upcoming 2027 video game slate, including Exodus and Warlock remains on track.”

Wizards has not made any statement regarding Hight’s successor aside from noting that the company is actively looking for a candidate both internally and externally. Hight hasn’t made any comment at the time of writing for the reason behind his departure, or what his future plans are.
