Lore Explainer: Trench Crusade Court of the Seven Headed Serpent

June 19th, 2026 via Goonhammer

In truth, the Trench Crusade is a proxy war between the forces of the Tyrant-God and those who lost the War in Heaven. None fully expected the Ultimate Heresy to open the Gate in Jerusalem, or at least none who had the power to fully exploit such a thing. But even so, on occasion the denizens of Hell must sometimes take as direct of action as possible. The Covenant of Hell prevents the Fallen from outright setting foot on Earth, but as with any contract the devil is in the details.

The Court of the Seven Headed Serpent is the closest thing to a standing military of the Damned as the fractious nature of Hell allows. It is a war council, each Mortal Sin getting a uniquely-flavored Naughty Chair from which whatever Devil most embodies each Sin reigns, arguing with the others about how the War should proceed. You can think of it as a never ending board meeting about which particular department should contribute what to the overall effort of overthrowing all creation.

As one can imagine, disagreements are incredibly common among the Court as to how the War should unfold. Any effort that is to have the full backing of Hell must pass a simple majority vote, which is about as easy to get as herding rabid cats. Even while the seats are in their usual occupation (things like squabbling, politicking, backstabbing, and being overthrown by another devil before their slave-fiends have even finished carving those statues of themselves that they ordered can throw a wrench in things) large scale offensives are very difficult to manage. So most of the time the Court and its members must rely on more personal hunting parties, lead by a begrudgingly trusted not-quite devil  sent to Earth in service to their Patron to try and ensure that things unfold as they wish. These are the warbands that will take to the table in a Campaign of Trench Crusade!

The Lords of Damnation

Court Praetor Credit: Darius Minis

When a hunting party of the Court exits the Brass Gate of Jerusalem it is a thing of great ceremony and celebration. Being allowed out of Hell for an extended period of time is rightly seen as an immense privilege for the lesser creatures that are not fully bound by the Covenant of Hell. A vacation away from the worst boss imaginable, with plenty of squishy, fleshy humans to really vent those infernal frustrations on. Those unholy beings that lead such warbands are usually the mighty Praetor. A Praetor is the single most terrifying being that can walk through the Gate and not trigger a reprisal. They are Half-Breeds of the most horrifying pedigree, the spawn of a beast of the earth, usually a predator like a bear or hyena, and a devil. A Praetor could be virtually anything, from a slavering and almost mindless beast of murder, unchained in the vague direction of what needs to be destroyed, to a sorcerous master with a mind as incomprehensibly complicated and twisted as its infernal progenitor. Only three things about them are certain: They bear three faces in mockery of the Divine Trinity, they fly on wings of drooling gore and they are one of the most powerful things to stalk the trenches of No Man's Land in the Great War.

The other option for entities that can lead a hunting party of the Court is a Sorcerer. A sorcerer is a fragment of an angel that died during the Fall, one of a thousand pieces of a corpse locked at the moment of its unmaking, neither alive nor dead. Being such a complicated piece of work, they are excellent at channeling the unholy magic that Court can bring to the table while lacking the raw killing potential of a Praetor. Both have excellent maneuverability, as both are blessed with flight.

Slavering Hordes

Yoke Fiends Credit: Darius Minis

The vast majority of the forces lead by the Court are made up of Yoke Fiends and Wretched. Wretched are little more than living batteries of mortal suffering herded into combat purely to suffer and die to ensure that human blood is always on hand to be spilled to power the Court's Goetic Blood Magics. They are always looking for more of these sorry souls, so consider signing up today!

A Yoke Fiend is a heaving mountain of shivering muscle and fat, a broken and shattered stillbirth of endless infernal inbreeding. So debased and twisted that they no longer qualify as a demon, tormented by the barest fragment of tortured divinity that still coldly burns at the very center of their being. They will never forget the dizzying heights from which their origins fell, and will take a vicious glee in tormenting or destroying any portion of Creation they can get within reach of their bestial limbs and barbed weapons.

Other Elites

Hunter of the Left Hand Path Credit: Darius Minis

While not able to lead the Court in its hunts, the undisputed master of such pursuits is the dreaded Hunter of the Left-Hand Path: One of the mysterious beings that hale from the unmapped and shifting hinterlands of Hell, who hunt nameless beasts that were old when the first Fallen was banished. The Hunters are loners who will take orders from Arch-devils as suggestions at best, and only join the Court on Earth when the unknowable stars and omens deem it correct to do so. In No Man's Land they are virtually impossible to pin down, stepping onto the path of their namesake and slipping between realities and ley lines of Hell and Creation to teleport at will. Their own left arms are mutated in blasphemous rituals to forgotten beings, allowing them to fire endless arrows of their own corrupted blood.

Hellknights meanwhile are an enduring monument to the price of failure in the Court. Either lesser fallen angels, mighty mortal champions raised to devilhood or praetors that succeeded in having their mortality shorn from themselves, no matter what the Hellknight once was it has been cursed eternally with its current form. Folded impossibly in on themselves, compacted and twisted until their once-glorious armor is now their eternal and inviolable prison. Crushed into a space barely a tenth of their original stature, the Hellknight is still a mighty and imposing warrior locked in eternal servitude to the masters whose sigils they bear upon their infernal armor. Armed with ancient and brutal weapons, Hellknights rejoice in the bringing their still formidable skill at arms to bear on any mortal foolish enough to challenge the nobility of the Court.

Trophies and Hounds

Desecrated Saint
Credit: Darius Minis

A hunting party of the Court also has a few more specialized denizens of the underworld that they can bring with them to stalk the Earth. When the Gate first opened, before the Court could organize itself or decide what armies would march forth to invade the Levant, something was released in a horrifying swarm. The Pit Locust is an infernal creature that is an iron-clad horse-sized amalgamation of bestial and insect shapes. In great plagues they descended on the first armies of Man in the Levant that refused to bow before the nobility of Hell. In reward for the carnage they inflected in those first days of the war, they are now allowed to hunt with the Court.

A Desecrated Saint is both a trophy of an archdemons skill at leading even the best of humanity astray, and a potent altar of unholy power. At the core of these strange beings is the immortal remains of one who was destined to be a saint, prophet or other great person in the eyes of the Tyrant but was tempted into Damnation. Still enshrined in the reliquary that they were buried in, the Desecrated Saint is carried to war by an abomination formed from the bodies of those who swore to follow them in life, the oaths of loyalty sworn binding them together for eternity. As a beacon of unholy magic, reality is thin near these great unhallowed relics, the laws of God inverted or simply gone when one gets near to them.
