Tabletop Battles Reviews: Adeptus Custodes Legends Datasheets

November 12th, 2026 via Goonhammer

With the release of the Adeptus Custodes Codex, we've also seen what was left of the old Forgeworld Upgrade Kits, Aleya/Valerian along with the Land Raider, and Venerable Contemptor sent to the annals of Legends. Unfortunate to see most of these go, especially when Sisters of Silence lack a proper Leader character and the Custodes Units that did go benefit so much from the new Codex.

But that said, these units do still have a place at narrative games, casual play, and... are unusually well designed and appear to have some foresight put into them.

I'll break this down by categories, saving the best for last.

Vehicles

Venerable Land Raider

A theme I've noticed with a lot of these datasheets is "The Legends get the last laugh," as they have tools a lot of Custodes lists will want. For example, D3+3 Damage anti-tank weapons, ranged firepower in general, and a solid transport. It's annoying that it did not get a second ability like so many of the other Land Raiders in the Space Marine Codex, but the assault disembark is something a lot of Custodes lists want; a way to safely carry a couple of squads of troops is huge. The issue, however, is this will become the ultimate priority target on the field. The transport capacity of 6 means Leaders in their squads put this unit in a very awkward position. As you'll either have two empty seats, or just shove a couple squads of Terminators/Custodian Guard, etc. (This also adds a very hilarious option of just shoving a little under two-thirds of your entire list into a single transport by putting two Blade Champions, one Shield Captain, one Allarus Shield Captain, Trajann, and Valerian into a single transport. Don't do this unless you're going for a really insane narrative mission.) Also, it's well-pointed at 230. Very good decision to go lower since Custodes appear to have launched overcosted.

Venerable Contemptor Dreadnought

Lots of small improvements on the old VenCon that keep it a pretty decent choice in the right match-up. Keeping the normal upgrades from the other Contemptor Pattern dreadnoughts like the Achillus/Galatus, the VenCon went to M9, T10, 2+/4++ (WHOA BABY!) Kheres Pattern Assault Cannon is now AP-2, Damage 2, even has Sustained Hits 1 built in (and it is not conditional; it just works against everything!), and it's a good Strength 7. I could see this being absolutely devastating in Might of the Morotoi and Aquilan Shield, as it can get a lot out of these detachments, rerolls from Might, fallback shoot, and charge, along with +1S from Aquilan Shield. Also, in a nice change of pace and consistency, the Contemptor Fist has been upped to AP-3 D3+3 damage, matching the Redemptor/Brutalis Fists, meaning it can punch up and hit about as hard as it's Marine Contemporaries. The Unyielding Ancient ability to bring back has been nerfed, but it trades the potential extra wounds for consistency in that you'll always get back up with 3W, not great, but it could be a lot worse... Maybe. Currently pointed at 220 points, it's a good beasty; it can help if you need something to fight infantry or bully Space Marines in general.

Credit: Jack Hunter

Infantry

Custodian Guard Sodality with Adrasite Spears

What a GLOW UP on the Gun. 2 shots a piece, now S8 AP-2 and 3D; that's just nasty. Combo this with Aquilan Shield for the fallback shoot and charge, and now the new unit has the ability to reroll wounds of 1 or reroll all wounds on an objective. (Note: You don't have to control it either.) Extra shots were badly needed, but going from S5 to S8 means you have a unit that can punch up HARD now. You've got dedicated Terminator hunters with all this 3D, and even take the fight to vehicles with the fishing via full wound rerolls. You can even put the fear of the Emperor in a Custodes Mirror Match with the +1S from Aquilan Shield so you can punch into even Allarus Terminators, or annoy all those Space Marine Land Speeders, or some of the smaller Daemon Engines and Tau Vehicles with this. Also 200 points for 3. That's amazing, a full 40-point drop from rank-and-file Custodian Guard and even better is they still get the Trusted Sentinel keyword, so the unit benefits from the new rules from Honoured Companions and Guardians of the Throne, immensely, along with Salvus Stance ignoring those silly things like cover.

Custodian Guard Sodality with Pyrithite Spears

Now we get to something beautiful, an infantry anti-tank weapon. Still only one shot, but was raised to match a lot of the other Melta Weapon profiles at S10 and AP-3 with D3+2 Damage, but the big winner is if you run Aquilan Shield and pop +6" range so even coming in relatively safe can still get you the Melta Damage Boost, what's more while the unit keeps the old 10E ability of getting Lethal Hits and Ignores Cover Once Per Battle, they also are Trusted Sentinels which means you can take Honoured Companions and give the squad wound-rerolls of 1. Then it gets even better: there's a reason to run a Pyrithite Spear Captain beyond just the -1 Damage shield. 4 Melta Shots with wound rerolls and potentially much more depending on detachment choice means that this unit has an immense amount of flexibility. Also, 215 points for 3 is great for Custodian Units and especially infantry. After testing this unit, my advice is using this to fight things you don't want to immediately charge; rapid ingress your Captain and Pyrithite Guard somewhere safe, jump out next turn and melta blast, if the target isn't dead? Charge in and finish the job!

But the big winner of the Infantry? Oh, here we go!

Sagittarum Guard by Crab-stuffed Mushrooms

Sagittarum Custodians Sodality Squad

Try saying that three times fast! Gone but never forgotten, you glorious Rifleman Custodes! (I hope for a resculpt!) And what a glow-up these boys have gotten. Gone are their rifles being a sad mess of AP-1 and situational buffs. No, now they fight like true Gunstodes! Flat Sustained Hits 1 is already great, but now a statline of 3A S7 AP-2 2D and are only 185 points per 3?! W H A T?! This is glorious! I could see narrative lists fielding a lot of these units as backline gun support, slapping on the unit's Debuff of the suppression -1 to hit left and right while filling in awkward holes in the game, and yet again, someone was awake when they wrote this unit and made sure to give the Trusted Sentinel keyword to the unit, so now the unit can reroll wounds of 1 if you take Honoured Companions, pop this with the Once Per Game Devastating Wounds? You can really bring the hurt if you need it. Awesome work all around.

Characters

Aleya

Aleya is basically unchanged except gaining Scouts... 6"? Why? Every other unit scouts 7" that can for the Sisters of Silence. Also, for some reason, she missed the memo, as Somnus still has regular Devastating Wounds. Aleya's unit ability is the same; it has the same keywords, mostly. I advise taking her if you want to throw down a surgical strike assault of the Vigilators; otherwise, don't really bother. However, Aleya is priced at 55 points, so if you're one of the dozen or so Sisters of Silence fans that own enough to make the idea work, Aleya's a great take as she hits hard and moves quickly; her ability is unchanged from 10th edition, right down to the "+1 to wound when below half strength" part that always stings. Then there's the funny thing with Aleya: she's a Leader, and the Knight-Centura is a Support, so you could have a giant 12 model blob consisting of Aleya, a Knight-Centura, and 10 Vigilators all riding out from an Anathema Psykana Rhino. Pretty cheap mid-board skirmisher combo, all things considered.

Credit: Daniel "Skails" Rodenberg

Valerian

(BROKEN COMBO WARNING! BROKEN COMBO WARNING! ABANDON ALL HOPE YE WHO FIELD THIS COMBO!) Are you nostalgic for the days when armor saves were an all-or-nothing situation? Do you long for the days of 7th edition, when AP was more a suggestion to Custodes, and nothing ever died? Do you crave to see your chosen squad caked in the only material more durable than Aurumite, or a named character without a helmet, as they stride across an open battlefield soaking up insane amounts of damage? Well then, you need Valerian and a squad of Wardens! Because whoever was writing the Legends rules did not get the giant slip of paper saying WARDENS HAVE ALWAYS ON ARMOUR OF CONTEMPT! Now Valerian has always-active Armour of Contempt! I hoped you enjoyed it, folks, but we're too late now! We now have a four Custodian-sized, unkillable mob that refuses to die to conventional firearms, and even makes Melta Weapons struggle! Unlike most of the other units, this combo in particular wants to go into Guardians of the Throne. How does ignoring all AP-2 or lower weapons, with -1 to hit and wound via Shield of Honour, sound? Because to me I punch that into my Mathhammer and it makes a happy face! Oh, wait, someone's shooting Ap-3 or 4 weapons at you? Yeah, pop In Auramite Clad! Here, let's just ignore all AP-3! It'll be fine!" [Additional warning: Valerian and Wardens is a combo that should be used cautiously, as this unit is punishing as can be and warrants a careful response from the opponent that not all players can counteract. Use this combo respectfully, if someone in your Crusade Campaign is being a power-gaming maniac, or you really, really, really REALLY HATE your opponent.]

Final Thoughts

To summate: Custodes have some amazing legends units that deserve your attention and consideration, the combos the infantry alone have potential here are worth everyone's attention and for narrative, casual play, and anything else? It's a good sign. I also hope that with how well build so much of this is, it's a sign of a possible resculpt for the fancy spears and the Saagitarum Custodes. (LET ME HUFF MY COPIUM!) None of these besides Valerian are broken, they're just good, and in the world of 40K, especially in Narrative play? Good, is fantastic. I hope this is the standard a lot of Legends rules are in the future.

Overall rating, A-.
