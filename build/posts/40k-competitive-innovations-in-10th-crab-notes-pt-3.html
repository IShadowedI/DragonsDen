Archetype

Corsair Coterie

Thoughts

An impressive showing for the Corsair Coterie in fourth place here, with Pau assembling a build that looks designed to pull the classic Aeldari move of annoying opponents to death. It's got enough good, high reach threats that opponents have to play cautiously against it, lest Jain Zar, Lhykhis or a Rapid Ingress Yriel bomb ruin their day, and they may also find themselves pinned and move blocked by a big Striking Scorpion unit that's threatening a Blood Surge. If they try and push back, they'll hit Mortal Wound booby traps on objectives, have shooting lines thwarted by 18" Lone Operative, all that good stuff.

I think in the current metagame, you're always going to be on a knife edge with this kind of build, because eventually you'll hit something that can just rush you and body you off the board (especially on Dawn of War-style maps) and that did eventually happen with the Space Wolves list above here, but what a ride to take Corsairs that far, huge congratulations Pau!
