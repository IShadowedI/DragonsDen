[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Dis Iz Rigged pt.1

September 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

We're back in record time, shaking off the Space Marine-related exhaustion and back into the fray. Shooty Orks are the big news this week, pushing to the top of the win rate pile, though with a fair bit of build variety, and they're not the only ones having a good time. Stalwarts like Custodes continue strong, but Votann are making serious waves, and this week we also have a surprising (blood) surge from World Eaters. Still plenty going on out in the world, and of course next week we'll have the results from the biggest event of the year, the LGT.

Make sure to tune back in for that, and finally if you somehow haven't seen all the hot new Space marine details yet, you can check them out here.

Thoughts

Votann's very strong recent performance continues into another week, and at this point I'm just putting my hands up and saying this is a goodstuff build. Look. Stuff. That's good. You know I'm right. This version leans a bit more go-wide via extra Kaprici, and also brings melee Steeljacks as an extra close-quarters threat. Also interesting is the choice to take bolt Thunderkyn instead of beams, perhaps gunning for enemy power armour, or just wanting to dump strats on the Hearthguard instead. Ultimately, this list just outdoes many foes on sheer quality, with a nice balance of big threats and efficient scoring. Congratulations Jasper!

Thoughts

I, for one, am pressing F to pay my respects. Who would have guessed that the Predator Destructor would be one of the defining vehicles of Warhammer 40K 10th Edition? Sad to see it trundle off into Legends, but what a final three years it's had. This build uses it (and its Annihilator friends) very effectively, providing cheap, grindy threats that can be pushed to the max with super Oaths, and a big Ingress-able Victrix unit to threaten anyone who gets too close. A respectful salute to some classic tanks and a great 10th Edition strategy, well done Alessandro.

Thoughts

Shooty Orks continue to make the most noise out of the new Codex, just as they like it really. This build shows off one of the options, which is using the very potent Targetin' Gizmos upgrade on a Battlewagon to mega charge the power of Tankbustas and big Meks, unleashing extreme drive by demolition. It's supported by the big Kans unit we've seen in lots of build, and also the Kill Rig, which gets plenty of benefit from the Blitz Brigade, and is also just plain good in the new book, which I'm glad someone seems to have picked up on. Extreme violence and surprisingly solid defences to the crack back, potent stuff from Thomas!

Thoughts

The popular Slaanesh Daemonkin reap some souls here, going for a slightly different setup than normal focused on holding positions. Congratulations Jean-Luc.
