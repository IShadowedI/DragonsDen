How to Paint Everything: Marvel Crisis Protocol - Bastion

July 30th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Hey everybody, I'm back with a new How to Paint Everything: Marvel Crisis Protocol, and today we are taking a look at how I painted Bastion! This model can be found in the new Sentinels Affiliated Character Pack, featuring Bastion, Nimrod, and Omega Sentinel.

Bastion comes with a bunch of options to customize him to fit your tastes. He features a human and cyborg head, and options for both arms as well. For mine, I decided to go full 'borg and gave him the mechanical arms and cyborg head options.

This time we'll be going over how I paint black super suits and the purple accents of his suit, pink glowing effects as well as some super low effort NMM silver.

Credit - Brushwizard

Black Suit

Basecoat - AK Interactive Black, Citadel Incubi Darkness

Layer - Citadel Dark Sea Blue

Highlight - Citadel Dark Sea Blue, AK Interactive Ice Yellow

Highlight - Previous Mix, AK Interactive Ice Yellow

Highlight - AK Interactive Ice Yellow

I started with the basecoat for the black. I started with a smooth layer of Black mixed with Dark Sea Blue; this will give a blue-green tint to the black, and let us play around with the highlights a bit more. Next, I painted a layer of Dark Sea Blue to start creating the effect of light reflecting from his suit. Another layer, this time using a mix of Dark Sea Blue and a small amount of Ice Yellow, was used to reinforce the highlighting. More Ice Yellow was then added to the mix and used to highlight it further. Finally, I finish it off with a highlight of Ice Yellow on its own.

Purple Upper Chest Area

Basecoat - AK Interactive Purple

Layer - AK Interactive Purple, Army Painter Spellbound Fuchsia

Highlight - Army Painter Spellbound Fuchsia

Highlight - Army Painter Spellbound Fuchsia, AK Interactive White

The purple sections started with a layer of Purple as the basecoat. I then mixed a roughly 1:1 mix of Purple and Spellbound Fuchsia together and added that as my 2nd layer. The highlights were done with Spellbound Fuchsia on its own, followed by another highlight of Spellbound Fuchsia and a tiny amount of White.

Cybernetics and Tentacles

Basecoat - AK Interactive Dark Sea Blue

Drybrush - AK Interactive French Blue

Drybrush - AK Interactive Snow Blue

Drybrush - AK Interactive White

Wash - Citadel Nuln Oil

Highlight - AK Interactive White

Dark Lining - AK Interactive Black

This looks like a lot of steps; in reality it's actually a pretty quick paint job since I was going to be drybrushing most of it with our non-metallic metal colors.

To start, I basecoated all of the areas that were going to be metal with Deep Sea Blue. Next, I drybrushed layers of French Blue, Snow Blue and White. I wanted these drybrushed layers to be successively lighter than the last, starting with a heavy drybrushing and finishing with a much lighter drybrushing of White to act as a reflective highlight.

To reinforce shadows, all of the metal areas were given a wash of Nuln Oil. Once the wash was dry, I reinforced the highlights with White and then deepened the shadows by painting Black into the recesses as needed.

Gold Metal Accents

Basecoat - AK Sahara Yellow

Layer - AK Sand Yellow

Highlight - AK Ice Yellow

All the little gold connectors and accents started with a basecoat of Sahara Yellow. Then they were given simple highlights using Sand Yellow and Ice Yellow.

Skin Tone

Basecoat - Citadel Bugman's Glow, Reaper Fair Skin

Layer - Previous Mix, AK Interactive Snow Blue

Highlight - Reaper Fair Skin

Since I went with all-cyborg body parts, I decided that his skin ought to have a sickly pallor. I started by mixing Bugman's Glow with Fair Skin as a basecoat. I then mixed Snow Blue into the previous mix and gave it a highlight. The nose, cheekbones and forehead were highlighted with Fair Skin.

Hair

Basecoat - AK Interactive French Blue

Layer - AK Interactive French Blue, Citadel Ushabti Bone

Highlight - Previous Mix, AK Interactive White

Bastion's hair is white/blonde in most of the reference materials I found. I decided to start with a basecoat of French Blue, then layered on a mix of French Blue and Ushabti Bone. It was then finished off with a highlight using the previous mix with a bit of White added in to get the whitish look to his hair color.

Corrugated Hose

Basecoat - AK Interactive Black

Layer - AK Interactive French Blue

Drybrush - AK Snow Blue

The corrugated hose holding him aloft started with a basecoat of Black. It was then given a quick drybrush of French Blue, followed by Snow Blue. I then painted Black into the recesses to break up the surface.

Glowing Details

Basecoat - AK Interactive White

Layer - Army Painter Spellbound Fuchsia

Layer - Lethal Shadows Telemagenta

Highlight - AK Interactive White

The glowing details on Bastion started with a White basecoat, to achieve as much brightness from the pink and fluorescent colors as possible. I then painted on a thin layer of Spellbound Fuchsia, followed by a layer of Telemagenta fluorescent pink. To crank up the brightness a bit more, I added White back into the center of his eyes and chest light.

Concrete Basing

Basecoat - Citadel Stormvermin Fur

Stipple - Army Painter Ash Grey

Stipple - Army Painter Brigade Grey

Wash - Citadel Nuln Oil

As usual, the concrete sections of the base were painted using a basecoat of Stormvermin Fur, stippled with Ash Grey and Brigade Grey for texture, then tied together and shaded with a wash of Nuln Oil.

Dirt Basing

Basecoat - Citadel Gorthor Brown

Wash - Citadel Agrax Earthshade

Probably the simplest thing to paint on any of my Crisis Protocol models. A basecoat of Gorthor Brown, washed with Agrax Earthshade.

Credit - Brushwizard

Bastion is now ready to eliminate mutantkind! As a bonus, the Prime Sentinels were painted using the same color scheme. So you can follow these same steps for those two as well. You can find Bastion, alongside Nimrod and Omega Sentinel, in the MCP09 Bastion, Nimrod & Omega Sentinel Character Pack, which is now available for purchase at your FLGS or online. As always, a big thank you goes out to Atomic Mass Games and Asmodee for sending this character pack my way. It's very much appreciated!
