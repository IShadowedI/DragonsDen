How to Paint Everything: Marvel Crisis Protocol - Sentinel Prime MK4

September 10th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Hey everybody, I have returned with a new How to Paint Everything: Marvel Crisis Protocol! This time, I'll be covering how I painted my MK4 Sentinels, which can be found in the new repackaged CP236 Sentinels Affiliation Pack.

For this one, I'll be using the MK4 Sentinel Prime as an example; the standard Sentinel MK4s are still in progress at the time of this writing. But since they are all painted using the same steps, that's perfectly ok. I had painted these big boys once before, in a blue and purple color scheme. But, thanks to Atomic Mass Games sending this pack my way, I get to redo them in a new red and purple scheme that is closer in line to the 90s animated series color scheme.

So, let's get rolling. We have a lot to cover, and this model is basically like painting an action figure from scratch. I'll be going over how I painted the non-metallic metal red and purple effects, as well as a simple drybrushed NMM silver. Finally, I'll cover the glow effects I used for their eyes, chest lights and hand repulsor blasters.

As usual, you'll find a fancy paint chart below. There you'll find all of the colors that were used to paint the Sentinel MK4 miniatures. Let's get to it!

Credit - Brushwizard

Silver Metal Bits

Basecoat - Citadel Chaos Black Spray Primer

Drybrush - AK Interactive French Blue

Drybrush - AK Interactive Snow Blue

Wash - Citadel Nuln Oil

Drybrush - AK Interactive Ivory

Before painting anything else, I started with a primer coat of Chaos Black spray paint. All of the metallic bits were then drybrushed using a large round Army Painter drybrush. I started with a drybrushing of French Blue and another drybrush of Snow Blue. After that, I washed everything with Nuln Oil shade to reinforce the shadows and recesses. Once that was all dry, I gave it all one final drybrushing of Ivory.

Face Plate

Basecoat - Citadel Bugman's Glow

Shadow - Citadel Rhinox Hide

Layer - Citadel Bugman's Glow, AK Interactive Pastel Pink

Highlight - AK Interactive Pastel Pink

The faceplate starts with a basecoat of Bugman's Glow. I then shaded it with a layer of Rhinox Hide, painting this color into all of the recesses. It was also painted on the panels of the face itself, leaving some Bugman's Glow showing. I then mixed Bugman's Glow and Pastel Pink together and painted this mix on top of the other Bugman's Glow areas, to brighten them up. Finally, the panel edges were edge highlighted using Pastel Pink.

Red Armor

Basecoat - Army Painter Basilisk Red

Shadow - Citadel Rhinox Hide

Layer - Army Painter Wyvern Fury

Layer - Army Painter Dragon Red

Layer - Army Painter Dragon Red, Army Painter Angelic Red

Layer - Army Painter Angelic Red

Highlight - Army Painter Angelic Red, AK Interactive Ivory

Highlight - AK Interactive Ivory

Dark Lining - AK Interactive Black

The red armor sections began with a basecoat of Basilisk Red. Shadows were added to the panels by painting Rhinox Hide in the recesses and areas that don't catch direct light. Highlighting started by painting Wyvern Fury on top of the Basilisk Red areas, making sure to leave Basilisk Red showing and blending the two together where they meet. This was repeated using Dragon Red over the Wyvern Fury areas. I followed this up with another layer of Dragon Red mixed with Angelic Red and then a final layer of Angelic Red on its own. Next, I edge highlighted all of the red armor panels with Angelic Red mixed with Ivory, followed by Ivory on its own. The recesses of panel lines were then dark-lined using Black.

Purple Armor

Basecoat - Army Painter Magecast Magenta

Shadows - Army Painter Triumphant Navy

Layer - Army Painter Warlock Magenta

Layer - Army Painter Warlock Magenta, Army Painter Spellbound Fuchsia

Highlight - Army Painter Spellbound Fuchsia

Highlight - Army Painter Spellbound Fuchsia: AK Interactive Ivory

Highlight - AK Interactive Ivory

Dark Lining - AK Interactive Black

The purple armor sections start with a basecoat of Magecast Magenta. Shadows were then added by painting all of the shaded and recessed areas with Triumphant Navy. Next, the I began the highlights by painting a layer of Warlock Magenta, blending it into the Magecast Magenta. Another layer was painted using a mix of Warlock Magenta and Spellbound Fuchsia, followed by a highlight of Spellbound Fuchsia. All of the armor panels were then edge highlighted using a mix of Spellbound Fuchsia and Ivory, and a 2nd edge highlight using Ivory on its own. The recesses were also dark lined with Black.

Chest Light and Eyes

Basecoat - AK Interactive White

Layer - Lethal Shadows Fluorescent Red

Highlight - AK Interactive White

The eyes of the MK4 Sentinels and the light on their chests were first given a base layer of White. They were then given a layer of Fluorescent Red, followed by a highlight of White.

Hand Blaster Lights

Basecoat - AK Interactive White

Layer - Lethal Shadows Fluorescent Blue

Highlight - AK Interactive White

Wash - Citadel Pylar Glacier

The repulsors in their hands were painted White. I then thinned Fluorescent Blue and painted that in the recess, trying to left some white showing in the center. I highlighted the center further using White, and then it was given a wash using Pylar Glacier Contrast paint.

Concrete Basing

Basecoat - Citadel Stormvermin Fur

Stipple - Army Painter Ash Grey

Stipple - Army Painter Brigade Grey

Wash - Citadel Nuln Oil

Like always, my concrete bases start with a basecoat of Stormvermin Fur. Its then stippled with Ash Grey and Brigade Grey. The base is then given a wash with Nuln Oil to tie it all together and shade any recesses in the base.

Rusty Metal Bits

Basecoat - Citadel Mechanicus Standard Grey

Layer - Citadel Doombull Brown

Wash - Citadel Agrax Earthshade

There's a few pieces of wrecked metal on his base. These were given a basecoat of Mechanicus Standard Grey. I then painted thinned down Doombull Brown randomly on them to give the look of rust discoloration. It was finished off with a wash of Agrax Earthshade to tinge the surface brown as well.

credit - Brushwizard

That's the Sentinel Prime MK4 all ready to go! I'm hoping to have the two smaller ones finished up soon, to go along with him. You can find this guy and the other MK4 Sentinels alongside Cassandra Nova in the CP236 Sentinels Affilation Pack, which is now available for purchase at your FLGS or online. As always, a big thank you goes out to Atomic Mass Games and Asmodee for sending this Affliation pack my way. It's very much appreciated!

Are you joining in the Sentinels resurgence and painting up the models? Join us in the Marvel: Crisis Protocol channel of the Tabletop Battles Discord server! Or you can find me on Instagram or Bluesky as well. I am always interested in seeing what the community has going on with MCP.
