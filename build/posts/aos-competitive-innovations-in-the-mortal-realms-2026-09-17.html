[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-09-17

September 17th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Apologies for the wait folks, it's a pretty packed week this week! As we move toward the Las Vegas Open, the big one, people are getting as many games in as they can before the big event. This week we're hitting 5 events from all over the world.

Another positive note is the size of these events continue to climb, which shows a huge shift from Ghyran. The people seem to love Aqshy, and that's great news!

Thoughts

Given this is one of the earliest weeks we've gotten to see the Ogor book in action as they've been in a bit of purgatory waiting for the official release and I can see how a skilled KO player with the right list could manage a strong win.

They have great abilities for being able to engage at long distance. Ogors want to be able to charge, or force their opponents into a less than ideal charge against them. If they can't do that, a lot of their abilities are neutered pretty hard and KO can keep their distance to dog the Ogors with their pretty mediocre saves by gunning them down with strong ranged weapons.

Thoughts

It seems despite the nerfs, KO still have some gas to work with. The primary power here is still the Frigate with Skywardens. It can charge in, bringing the Skywardens and Brokk with them and and drop them down with Strike First. Thanks to the new Scourge of Aqshy upgrade they're even stronger, with a +1 to hit or wound depending on the enemy type.

The Ironclad hangs back to give strong firing support and cover the support heroes that make up much of the list. The Akanauts and Vongrim round things out as objective grabbers and battle tactic-fulfillers.

Archetype

Daughters of Khaine Good Stuff

Thoughts

This is more or less the template for Daughters right now, a bit more focused on raw damage with 2 units of reinforced Blood Sisters. Thoughts

Seraphon have been in a bit of ascendancy since their points have just been consistently dropped until people were happy with them. Combined with the rules changes of Asterisms and that Aqshy is very kind to giant Monsters with 3 or more damage attacks, that's a huge gain for them. Still, this is Daughters of Khaine, one of the strongest armies out there and Morathi can box better than pretty much any other Monster in the game except for maybe Kragnos. Blood Sisters also get in on the fun,  high wound models make it very easy for them to  get their first strike and take them down quickly. Still's its a pretty good fight here with "only" a 24 pt difference.

Archetype

Gluttons

Thoughts

As above, we're seeing people break in the new Ogor Mawtribes and seeing a bit of a pattern here. Gluttons are economical for the number of wounds and attacks they get. Supported by Redd the Maw they get Hero phase moves from his prayer, and a Ward from his Aura. Good stuff to shore up some of their weaknesses of being slow.

The Hunters and Cav are much faster, not as good in combat but great for running out for battle tactics and objectives.

Archetype

Idoneth Good Stuff

Thoughts

The drop in points and the introduction of Aqshy Reavers have gone a long way for Idoneth. Aqshy Reavers can pelt a target from range and then shut off positive modifiers to save. This makes charging with their cav much easier as the enemy won't just turtle out of it. Speaking of Turtles, the Leviadon exists not only to make the army more durable but also to charge in and deal with big threats as needed.

Archetype

Thanquol and his machines

Thoughts

I'm not even sure what I'd call this list, but it's pretty damn brilliant. Thanquol is your primary Wizard, casting skitterleap to teleport units and Warpgale to hit enemy units with Strike Last.

The list comes with a bunch of decently heavy hitters to take advantage of that. The Warbringer hits like a truck, as do the Doomwheels if allowed to get into their targets. Stormfiends can be gnarly as well, and have a fringe benefit of being able to deep strike even on the opponent's turn, grabbing up objectives if they foolishly leave them open.
