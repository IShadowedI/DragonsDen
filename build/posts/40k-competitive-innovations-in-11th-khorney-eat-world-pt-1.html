[40k] Competitive Innovations in 11th: Khorney Eat World pt.1

October 21st, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Another big weekend is in the books, and this time it's the mighty World Eaters who are really making some noise. Terrible, gruesome noise. They're near the front on most metrics but they're not alone - AdMech builds using Cohort Cybernetica at 1DP are excelling, Tyranids had a great weekend, and Necrons saw a bit of a surge, especially in France. Orks, also, are still very good.

The ood news is that although there are standouts, balance at the top is looking healthier than it has done for a while, with no faction quite beating the 55% benchmark, and most weaker performers still taking some top finishes. Let us peruse the landscape, here's the schedule.

Thoughts

Aeldari try their hands at playing a defensive game here, unleashing lots of Wraiths to lock down objectives and repulse whatever the foe can throw at them. On the other side of the table, Orks, looking to smash stuff. Big guns, big Meganobz units, Big Squigs, big Ghaz in charge of everything. Brutal, not especially kunnin', if we're honest.

Can it smash the Aeldari? Oh my yes. There are probably worlds where something extremely strange happens and the Aeldari win (mostly involving every bright lance shot going through for a whole turn), but in general this should be a bloodbath. The tools the Orks have are very good at killing Wraithguard/Blades, they've got lots of them, and the Aeldari don't have the speed or objectives to avoid that becoming a terminal issue. Dakkarigs blow them to bits, there's a bunch of reliable damage 3 in melee, and a lot of it takes serious killing to put down. The Aeldari can focus on one thing a turn and probably drop it, but that's not going to be anywhere near enough. I don't think there's too much more to say on this one, it's a horrible mismatch and the scoreline reflects that.

Engines Warband

Thoughts

Our first World Eaters build of the week rounds out this top four, running what is pretty much the default formula of units and putting Brazen Engines on top. You can basically stack either Vessels or Engines on the standard Warband list and have them perform, with this option giving you the ability to punch a Forgefiend's shooting through modifiers at a critical moment, at a cost of slightly less deadly melee Characters. I'd guess how prevalent 4W Terminators end up being is going to be critical for deciding which dominates; boosting Forgefiends against those doesn't move the dial much, but into lots of the rest of the metagame any Character unit in this list is plenty deadly already. Ultimately - both good, but this one is maybe a bit rarer, so well done Enes for making it work.
