Everything You Need to Know From Warhammer Skulls

May 23rd, 2026 via Goonhammer
Games Workshop rolled out a pretty huge list of announcements and updates for upcoming Warhammer video games and collaborations at this years Warhammer Skulls, marking a 10th anniversary of the event in style. We've got the full rundown and trailers from what was announced and what you need to know about the upcoming games in the Warhammer universes, with a pretty heavy focus on 40k but also some fantasy to round things out, yes yes.

Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV



The return of Dawn of War is nearly upon us, and the Skulls trailer gave us the most important news any upcoming game could ask for: A release date. Warhammer 40,000 Dawn of War IV will release on September 17th, and the trailer also included notes of upcoming updates for the first few months of release as well. Players can look forward to the return of Crusade, a Mission Editor, some new Commanders to spice things up, and two DLCs that seemed to be stand-alone and paid, notably one for Blood Ravens and the other named "Aftermath" that promises a new faction to compliment the launch roster of Space Marines, Orks, Adeptus Mechanicus, and Necrons.

Warhammer 40,000: Chaos Gate Deathwatch



Kind of a mouthful of a name, Chaos Gate Deathwatch puts players in the role of, well, the Deathwatch, following up on the previously rleased Daemonhunters by the same team, so if you enjoyed Daemmonhunters, or just really like Deathwatch, then this one might be up your alley. It also is one of the titles coming to the most platforms, hitting PC, PS5, and Xbox Series systems sometime in the future, as there was no date to accompany the announcement. But what it did show is that the action may not be limited to just Space Marines directly, with a Dreadnaught and even a tank taking center stage a bit.

Warhammer Age of Sigmar: Deathmaster



A surprise in the announcements to me was this 2D action platformer, according to the publisher. I'll hesitate to bestow it with the infamous "Metroidvania" tag, but... it seems pretty likely. The most surprising thing to me for this trailer was that players will take on the role of one of those filthy little rascals, the Skaven, rather than fighting against the horde of rats. This one is another pretty far off title with a slated release of 2027, but very little other details to accompany it. Deathmaster will be available on PC, Nintendo Switch 2, Playstation 5, and Xbox Series systems.

Total War: Warhammer 40,000



Probably the most anticipated title here other than Dawn of War IV, there hasn't been a ton of news for Total War 40k, but this trailer gave us some new tidbits and featured the ever popular Yarrick and that some portion of the game's campaign will take place in or around Armaggedon. Sadly there wasn't much else shown with the trailer in terms of gameplay, and we're still lacking a release date, but such is the way of drip marketing in these times; when we find out more about Total War 40k, we'll be sure to let you all know!

Helldivers 2 x Warhammer 40,000



This one was either a headscratcher or a "hell yeah" moment depending on your tastes and interests, and the trailer did very little except announce the collaboration. I am going to hedge my bets that the core Helldivers 2 game is not going to suddenly change into a Warhammer themed game with Warhammer worlds and characters, so my gut instinct tells me to expect to see Warhammer themed cosmetic items in Helldivers 2. The trailer mostly touches on the developers love of the Warhammer 40,000 franchise, showing the requisite model being painted segment, and then ends with the announcement of an announcement for later this year.

Warhammer 40,000 Darktide: Skitarii



The next update from Fatshark games for Darktide is fast approaching, and Skulls showed off a trailer for the new incoming Skitarii class. The update is slated to be released on June 23rd, and will cost 11.99 USD, so if you and your friends are still playing, or looking for a reason to dive back in, take a look at the trailer and see if the Skitarii are right for you.

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 Purgation



If you hadn't already seen the news yourself, surprise! The Space Marine 2 Purgation update is now live, having pulled the old favorite of "released right now!" after a trailer gimmick (honestly one of my favorites). The new mission provides an expanded PvE experience as well as some updates to Siege Mode, a Practice Arena, Iron Hands, and some other updates.

Warhammer 40,000: Mechanicus II



Another "released as you watch", Mechanicus II is officially out! If you were on the fence, this trailer is hoping to hype you up, but if anything, the fun and pretty engaging strategy gameplay of the original should be enough to get you interested if you like tactical games; so far, Mechanicus II has been pretty solid, and is available on PC, Playstation 5, and Xbox Series systems.

Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresey and Rogue Trader



Owlcat returned to Skulls with the beta trailer and announcement for Dark Heresy, which looks to unsurprisingly follow up with the success of their Rogue Trader release; there's no hard date yet for the game but you can certainly wishlist it and check out when beta access might open for PC players, with the game also slated to eventually release on PS5 and Xbox Series. No word yet on which character Owlcat will be totally confused by player attraction to, but I'm going to take a guess it will be the Kroot or Aeldari.

Meanwhile, over in Rogue Trader, the next character to spin up player attraction arrives when everyone's favorite collector Trazyn the Infinite arrives as part of the Infinite Museion (try getting your spellchecker to accept that word) expansion arrives on June 11 for PC, Playstation 5, and Xbox Series systems. Rogue Trader has garnered a pretty big fan following and player support, so expect more of what people love in the expansion; plus, who doesn't love Trazyn?

Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2



The Boomer Shooter in the 40k Universe returns with Boltgun 2, and if you've never tried the first one, you can give this one a try via a demo released on Steam. I personally bounced off of the original Boltgun, but I know many people loved the interpretation of the 40k universe through the Boomer Shooter genre. Boltgun 2 allows you to pick your poison between Space Marine Malum Caedo or Sister of Battle Nyra Veyrath, and although no hard date was revealed, the game is slated to hit PC, Playstation 5, and Xbox Series systems by the end of the year. There was also the announcement of a mobile port of Boltgun, called Boltgun Boom, from Nitro Games, slated for release in 2026.

Warhammer Survivors



Auroch Digital rolled out another game, Warhammer Survivors, for basically every possible platform on the market: Mobile via Android and iOS, and PC, Playstation 5, Xbox Series, and Nintendo Switch (1 and 2). Survivors also has a demo on Steam if you want to play it; while Auroch lists the game as a "roguelite" pixel action game, it really is easiest to just say that this is a Warhammer skinned Vampire Survivor. The interesting part is you can play both 40k and Age of Sigmar, rather than it being specifically dedicate to one or the other. If you like Vampire Survivor or similar style games and love Warhammer, this might be up your alley. I didn't exactly find the demo to knock my socks off and make me want to play it over Vampire Survivor, Holocure, or Megabonk, but I'll withold that full judgement until the game is fully released.

Total Warhammer III Updates



Creative Assembly had more news for Total Warhammer III fans, namely that Bhashiva & The Tiger Warriors (not to be confused with the Cheetah Men) released on the day of the event for PC across all major platforms. There's also an 8.0 update that gave some new changes to Grand Cathay and Taoyan the Merciless for players to try out. \

Final Bits

Double Eleven, publishers of Rust, announced that players on Rust Console Edition will be getting the Warhammer 40,000 DLC that came to PC last year, so you can finally dress up your Rust character like a Death Corps of Krieg character from the comfort of your couch. Battlesector also announced DLC for the Ultramarines, a Crusade Update, and is currently Free to Play until May 28th, so if you want to give it a shot, you've got a few days left to try it out without having to pay.
