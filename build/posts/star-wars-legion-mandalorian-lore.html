Star Wars Legion: Mandalorian Lore

August 7th, 2026 via Goonhammer

The new Mandalorian army for Star Wars Legion has been incredibly popular and a huge draw for people who really love the look of the Mandalorian armor or even love the TV show but Mandalorians have existed in Star Wars media since we first saw Boba Fett in his iconic green armor. Their history, much like other Star Wars media, has had rewrites throughout the years and changed, but there is a timeline we can follow for them that explains their warrior heritage, the rise and fall of their home, and just why exactly they are so splintered in the modern Star Wars stories.

Thank you to Asmodee for sponsoring a series of articles on Star Wars: Legion.

What Is a Mandalorian?

The question "what is a Mandalorian" is one without an objectively correct answer, and is a massive part of their lore. In the early days of the Mandalorian history they were multiple tribes of warriors that held together through an unstable peace. These tribes would take in anyone who would join, and held to a warrior creed that became known as the "Old Ways". This creed would define their society, no matter how splintered it was. They would not remove their armor in front of others, they would hone their martial prowess with multiple weapons and fighting styles, and above all others the young fledgling Mandalorians (otherwise known as foundlings) would take precedence above all others until they had come of age. There would come a time during the Clone Wars era where this creed would be known as more of an ancient way of thinking and be relegated as a story for children. During this time, a Mandalorian would no longer be an outsider coming in, but those born on the planet of Mandalore itself.

Credit: Mandalorian Sleuth

The Ancient Ways

During the days of the old Mandalorian clans, there was a man only known as Mandalore the Great who had slain an ancient beast in the mines of Mandalore known as the Mythosaur. Mandalore the Great would eventually become legend, after having fought against the Jedi Order in multiple battles, and establish the Mythosaur as the symbol of the Mandalorians. Some time after this, there was a famed weapon known as the Darksaber. This was the creation of a Mandalorian child who had gone to the jedi temple during the Old Republic era and became one of the only known Mandalorian jedi. This child was Tarre Vizsla and not much else is known about him. This weapon would become the signifier of who was the leader of the Mandalorian people, otherwise known as the "Mand`alor". The only way to select a new Man`dalor would be to defeat the current one in combat and claim the Darksaber for your own. Tarre would found House Vizsla, which would become a massive player in the different clans power struggles as well as the Mandalorian history to come. After Tarre's death the Mandalorians would raid the jedi temple in order to take the Darksaber back, which would start a war between the Jedi Order and the Mandalorian clans. This conflict would ultimately make the planet Mandalore into an arid desert, and require cities to be made within domes that had their own ecosystems in order for the Mandalorian race to survive. This would splinter the Mandalorian clans and result in years of civil wars throughout their home planet, causing them to not be a prominent force in the galaxy as a whole. This splintering would also cause some Mandalorians to make their own claim as bounty hunters and mercenaries.

Peace

All of these civil wars would cause a pacifist movement to begin, known as the "New Mandalorians." They would unite as much of the Mandalorian clans as they could and create a unified society opposed to the old warrior ways. This would cause a group known as the "Old Mandalorians" who would not let go of their warrior heritage to fight in a large scale civil war that would destroy their capital city. The Old Mandalorians would inevitably fail and the New Mandalorians would create a peaceful society led by Dutchess Sabine of Clan Kryze. This peaceful time would last throughout much of the Clone Wars era and the Mandalorians would maintain neutrality throughout much of the war.

Credit: Mandalorian Sleuth

The Fall

In the backround a splinter cell of Mandalorians who still believed in their militaristic heritage would be founded, known as the Death Watch they would gain power and attempt a coup to overtake Mandalore from Dutchess Sabine. The Deathwatch would be comprised of multiple Mandalorian clans such as members of Clan Wren and Clan Kryze. The Deathwatch was led by a man known as Pre Vizsla, who had control of the Darksaber at the time to establish his control of this group. This initial coup would fail and the Deathwatch would spend some time rebuilding in secret, eventually allying with Darth Maul and his Shadow Collective. Their next coup would succeed, leading to the death of Pre Vizsla and the subsequent takeover of their society by Maul. This prompted the Republic to respond, sending a battalion of clones. This war would be known as the Siege of Mandalore. This Siege would end up in a Republic victory, and also conclude at the same time Palpatine would take over as galactic emperor and establish the Galactic Empire. This battle would not just consist of the Clone army but also a Mandalorian Resistance led by Bo Katan Kryze, who wanted to take control of their home back from Maul and his Shadow Collective.

Empire Control

Bo Katan would refuse to submit to the Empire's rules. This prompted the Empire, who had a full occupation of the planet, to instill one of Maul's former lieutenants as the governor. Gar Saxon would then create a group of Mandalorian soldiers for the Empire known as the Imperial Super Commandos. This would cause Clan Saxon to be backed up by Imperial forces and rule over the other Mandalorian clans for multiple years. A young Mandalorian student by the name of Sabine Wren would join a Rebel cell known as the Spectres, claim the Darksaber from Maul, and with the help of Clan Kryze unite their clans and take back control of Mandalore. This would ultimately end up prompting the Empire to punish the entire Mandalorian race and bombed the entire planets surface in a night known as the Night of a Thousand Tears or otherwise known as the Great Mandalorian Purge to others. This would render the planet uninhabitable and ultimately splinter the Mandalorian clans again. Mandalorians would create secret coverts, some converting back to the old ways. With Bo Katan trying and failing to unify the Mandalorians on multiple occasions even after the fall of the Empire.

Mandalorians! Credit: Colin (Legalized Mischief)

Reuinification

Thanks to the effort of the Mandalorian Din Djarin and the foundling Grogu, the Darksaber would be retrieved from the remnants of the Empire's grasp. The covert known as The Children of the Watch and Clan Kryze would reunite under Bo Katan, who now wielded the Darksaber again, and take back their home planet from the Empire remnants. From this point on the tale of the Mandalorians has yet to be completed and has ushered in a new era of unity among this small but ultimately influential group of Mandalorians as they take back their home and create a new society.

Mercenaries

Due to all the splintering and civil wars, many Mandalorians throughout their history have gone off to become Bounty Hunters and Mercenaries in their own rights. Influential Bounty Hunters such as Jango Fett who would be the template the Kaminoans utilized to create the Republics Clone Army, or his clone son Boba Fett who would become infamous and even capture the Republic General Han Solo. Mandalorian mercenaries would be known as the best of the best, and be highly sought after by all sides of conflicts or by major crime syndicates for their skills. Even Din Djarin was a famous Bounth Hunter in Nevarro before meeting Grogu and would make a name for himself across the sector he operated in.

Final Thoughts

Mandalorians have had a tumultuous history, with the constant infighting and change in regime. They have multiple prominent characters and there is no question why people love them. This would include me, if you couldn't guess by my pen name. They have been a part of Star Wars lore for as long as Star Wars has existed, and throughout the Cannon and legends timelines there is something for everyone to find and love about their ultimately messy history. If you find yourself wanting to dive in yourself the Clone Wars TV show as well as Star Wars Rebels will provide you with the majority of what you will need to know. The Mandalorian TV show and movie will catch you up to our present day and who knows what else there is in store for the Mandalorian culture in the future.
