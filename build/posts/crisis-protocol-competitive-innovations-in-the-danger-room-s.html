[Crisis Protocol] Competitive Innovations in the Danger Room, September 2026

October 8th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our monthly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each month our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the month’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

Greetings, True Believers!

We finally got the biggest update in all of the history of MCP with hundreds of characters getting changes to their health and some key affiliations getting major overhauls.

For these competitive innovation articles usually I continue on from where the last article stopped but this time we're only looking at results from events since the August update to take a look at how it has shaken up the meta. As a reminder of some of the changes in that update take a look at our article here. 

I've restricted the data to only include rated events with more than 15 people in case anyone wanted to look into more detail on Longshanks.

Affiliations by Winrate

Hellfire Club

Hellfire Club Credit - Alfred_Pharius

The Hellfire Club sit at the top of the competitive meta with a very healthy 63% win rate across 219 games.

Since her first update earlier in the year Emma Frost has become a dominant force and one of the premier 4 threat characters in the game. With even more health now, she is an anchor to the Hellfire Club and brings a toolbox of useful abilities as well as a strong leadership. On top of that, key characters like Omega Red and Mr Sinister received improvements to their output and mobility giving them a durable 12 threat core.

The Hellfire Club bring some tough characters on top of a leadership that heals them, if you don't manage to kill them quickly they can easily snowball out of control.

If you are struggling to beat the Hellfire club some potential counters could be characters like Ultron Metal Tyrant who has strong mystic defenses and can't be displaced by most of the Hellfire Club's tricks.

Wakanda

Erik Killmonger for Marvel Crisis Protocol MCP painted by Crab-stuffed Mushrooms

Wakanda are proving strong in this new world and despite the nerfs to King T'Challa they achieved a 62% win rate over 187 games.

Earlier in the year M'Baku was the preferred Wakanda leader but since the update Killmonger Usurper appears to have taken the throne from him. Usurper received some nice quality of life improvements gaining more health, better timing on his leadership and the ability to use his covert armaments attack in retaliation when he is attacked.

Wakanda have always been a strong affiliation and Wakanda forever remains one of the strongest cards in the game. The ability to suddenly make additional attacks with all of your affiliated characters can swing you from a losing position to a winning one very quickly.

New Mutants

credit: Brushwizard

The New Mutants are up next with a very respectable 58% win rate across 55 rated games. This one will need to come with a pinch of salt as the number of games is comparatively lower than the rest of the list but that doesn't mean we need to count them out.

The biggest change to the New Mutants post update was the addition of Honey Badger to the affiliation list. She gets a lot of benefit from Magik's Cry for Help leadership placing next to whichever ally is being attacked and taunting the attacker preventing them making any follow up attacks. She additionally gained the ability to pick up extracts meaning she can now contribute to the scenario as well as attrition.

X-23 and Honey Badger both being affiliated mean that any attack an opponent makes to either of them can lead to a huge amount of movement for the New Mutants player.

We've now seen what Mirage and her leadership might bring and although the leadership itself is underwhelming Mirage as a character seems strong.

The New Mutants do have one key weakness which prevents them ever being on top and that is that both of their leaderships require the opponent to interact with them. Though counterintuitive the best thing to do when facing the New Mutants is often to ignore them preventing them from gaining any leadership benefit.

SHIELD

The agents of SHIELD remained strong, winning 57% of their 151 recorded games.

During the previous competitive innovations period, SHIELD were at the top of the meta but some recent changes have brought them down a couple of rungs. The biggest change was the update to Baron Zemo's Strategic Genius rerolls. Previously people would take this alongside rerolls from Namor's Charismatic Leader ability to create a "Double Bubble" of stacking rerolls. Although this is still possible it isn't nearly as strong as it once was now that Zemo has to pay for his rerolls and that they are once per turn.

Despite these changes SHIELD still has some of the best tactics cards in the game such as Battlefield Medicine and The Shieldmobile. On top of that they have multiple ways to safely pick up and manipulate extracts and some excellent defensive abilities. With these things combined if SHIELD get ahead on points it can become very difficult to claw things back from them.

Web Warriors

Web Warriors. Credit: head58

The Web Warriors round out the top 5 with a 56% win rate across 323 games.

The Web Warriors had an interesting time with the latest update as many of their most popular characters had no changes while their underused characters all got better. We can see from which leaders were used that Miles Morales is now the least popular leader with the lowest win rate leadership which we never would have said last year.

The addition of the very strong character Ultimate Spider-Man may be another reason that the 3 threat Miles Morales has lost popularity as they share an alter ego and cannot be taken together.

Best of the Rest

A further nine affiliations sit just above the 50% win-rate mark.

The Convocation have a healthy 54% in rate across 171 games. The biggest update they received in August was the addition of the Scarlet Witch to the roster. She brings some much needed punch to the affiliation playing a similar role to Blue Marvel in the Defenders.

The Black Order also performed above average with a 52% win rate also helped by the addition of Nebula to their affiliation list. Corvus Glaive and Proxima Midnight have become a terror in the meta after their updates and the Black Order seem to have benefited from their changes compared to where they were earlier in the year.

The Midnight Sons, Guardians of the Galaxy and Mighty Avengers rounded out the top 10 affiliations in this period all with a healthy 52% win rate.

Final Thoughts

The August update has certainly shaken up the competitive scene with dominant affiliations such as the Servants of the Apocalypse no longer making the top 10. And underplayed affiliations like the Hellfire Club now creeping into the top tables. The best lists still aren't solved and there are some big events to come later in the year. It will be interesting to see how things shake up!
