Tabletop Battles Reviews: Star Wars Legion Mandalorian Custom Characters

October 8th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Welcome back to the end of our Mandalorian unit reviews. Now that some time has passed and the Mandalorians have found their place in Legion it is time for us to go over the customizable characters and the custom clan rules, with some light touches on how to utilize your own custom Mandalorian leaders in the other clans as well. These are some of the most exciting models to come with the army release and it has been a joy to paint and play them on the tabletop, and with all that said let's get to the review.

What are Customizable Characters?

For those who are a bit newer, Star Wars Legion has a Commander and Operative for every army that is a customizable character with a plethora of upgrades available to them. These characters can be tailored towards your personal play style as well as coming with bits for you to customize their looks as well. These characters help players bring out their personal play styles in their lists, and the customizable Mandalorians keep this theme going.

Custom Mandalorian Leader

Unlike most custom commanders the Mandalorian Leader has multiple built in keywords. Jump 2 gives the unit the ability to make a move ignoring or landing on terrain of the same height. Impervious reduces the Pierce value of weapons it is defending against but 1, this can be to a minimum of 0 so it is essentially immune to Pierce 1 weapons. The final keyword on the units card is Retinue: Mandalorian Warriors which allows you to give the unit stated (in this case Mandalorian Warriors) 1 Aim or Dodge token. As a base this is already a fairly supportive commander unit before upgrades.

Equip is a keyword that forces you to take upgrades on a unit, for the Mandalorian Leader it needs to equip an Armament upgrade and a Doctrine upgrade. Doctrines are a unique upgrade for the custom characters that drastically change how the character functions, but enough about the unit card the real interesting part of the Mandalorian Commander comes from the upgrades unique to it.

These 2 Doctrines are able to be taken by both the Leader and Hunter. Battlemaster provides the unit the ability to use an expendable upgrade as an exhaustable upgrade instead the first time you use it. This can allow you to use a whipcord twice, or a flamethrower twice, rockets, and so on. The upgrade also adds the keyword Death From Above which removes the defending units cover when you attack them from terrain that is a higher elevation than your attack target. This makes the Mandalorian Leader a mobile attacker that can hunt units effectively across the battlefield. Deadeye is a Doctrine that gives the Unit Target 1 and Sharpshooter 1. Target gives the unit an Aim token if it is issued an order and Sharpshooter reduces cover of units defending from this units attacks. This is a generically strong combination of aggressive keywords that support any weapon on the model, short or long range. This does not improve the damage of melee weapons since in melee cover does not apply.

The Mandalorian Leader has a unique Doctrine only it can equip. War Party Leader gives the unit Direct: Mercenary Trooper, Inspire 1, and it increases the courage value of the unit by 1. This gives the Mandalorian Leader a courage bubble of 3 for a mostly courage 2 army which makes your units harder to panic in general. Direct lets this unit order a unit to the unit the Direct keyword is referencing, which in this case is any unit that is both a Mercenary and a Trooper. For instance with the upgrade Underworld Connections you can order any unit with any affiliation or if you use the Mandalorian Clan upgrade of your choice you can order units of those affiliations. Inspire 1 removes a suppression token from a unit at the end of the Mandalorian Leaders activation.

The armament upgrades are available to both units, there are 2 melee weapons and 3 ranged weapons. The Dual Armaments add the keyword Charge which lest the unit make a melee attack action when it moves into melee with a unit and Gunslinger which lets the unit make a ranged attack towards another unit in range after it makes a ranged attack. The Vibroknives have High Velocity which disable your opponents Dodge tokens when you attack them and Pierce 1 which cancels 1 successful defense roll. The 3 white dice and 3 black dice aren't the most damage but it does have some strong keywords. The EE-3 Carbine has Overwhelm which adds an extra suppression on the defending unit when you use an Aim token to re-roll dice. This along with the 1 red and 3 black dice pool combos well with Deadeye wince you will commonly have an Aim token. The GALAAR-15 Carbine is 4 black dice at range 1-3 which is boring with a lack of extra keywords but it is a free way to increase the units range. The GALAAR-90 Sniper Rifle adds Sharpshooter 1 (which can stack with Sharpshooter 1 to become Sharpshooter 2 and essentially negate heavy cover) and has the old sniper upgrades dicepool, High Velocity, and Pierce 1. It isn't a lot of damage but it can be a consistent way to push damage through to units. The Vibrosword provides the unit with Charge and Duelist, which is a complicated keyword but in essence gives Pierce 1 when you spend an Aim to re-roll dice and provides Immune: Pierce when you spend a Dodge both in melee. This can make the Vibrosword Pierce 2 if you spent an Aim and with the dicepool it can do a decent amount of damage to units while also being defensible in melee. The interesting part of the Vibrosword is that it allows you to use Shield tokens in melee which you normally are not able to do.

The Mandalorian Leader comes with 1 command card. Honor and Strength is a 3 pip that issues any 3 units a faceup order token. During this round as long as your Mandalorian Leader is not suppressed or panicked Mandalorian Troopers within range 2 upgrade 2 of their dice. In most cases this will bring black dice to red dice which can really increase the effectiveness of your Mandalorian Warriors or even other characters. This command card also downgrades enemy attack dice within range 1 of the Mandalorian Leader unit which can reduce the effectiveness of attack, especially if they already have Pierce which is reduced by the Mandalorian units Impervious.

Mandalorian Hunter

The Mandalorian hunter is the other half of the box. The Mandalorian Hunter comes with the same 2 keywords as the Leader but also comes with Mercenary: all factions which greatly increases the use cases for the unit, and adds something to the Republic that they do not have but we will get there when we get to the unique Doctrine for this unit. Unlike the Leader counterpart the Hunter does not have surge to defend which causes it to be a little easier to remove than the Leader but it is cheaper by a large margin to compensate for that.

The Hunter has access to Battlemaster and Deadeye and they add the same effectiveness to this unit like they do to the Commander. The Mandalorian Hunter being an Operative does make it easier to justify it not babysitting units and moving off on its own to deal with other characters on the battlefield. The Mandalorian Hunter does have a unique Doctrine.

Guild Member lets you create your own custom bounty hunter that can be used across all the factions. This is one of the most exciting parts of the Mandalorin Hunter, and provides the Republic with the bounty hunter they have never had. This also makes the Mandalorian Hunter more efficient and defensive at fighting its Bounty target (or any Bounty target for that matter) since it provides an Aim and a Dodge if you are fighting against your Bounty target. With all this said, what is Bounty? Bounty makes you choose an opponents Operative or Commander unit at the beginning of the game during setup and give it a Bounty token. This token cannot be removed by any normal means except if that Bounty target is defeated. If a Bounty a unit with the Bounty keyword defeats a unit with a Bounty token that Bounty token becomes 1 VP for the player controlling the unit with Bounty. Any unit with Bounty can claim the VP (Victory Point), they do not have to target specifically the unit they assigned the token to.

There is 1 other unique upgrade for the Mandalorian Hunter that puts it on par with its Mandalorian Leader counterpart, and that is Full Beskar Armor.

This provides the Mandalorian Hunter with surge to defend which makes the Mandalorian Hunter just as survivable as the Mandalorian Leader.

The 2 units share the same weapons, but the Mandalorian Hunter can utilize the Pierce weapons better since the Bounty Hunter keyword requires it to defeat its Bounty target to collect the VP. Melee is usually a preferable range for bounty hunters so they can be protected from being shot by other units. This makes the Vibrosword a fantastic choice for the Hunter.

The Mandalorian has 1 command card that is a 1 pip. No Escape only gives an order to the Mandalorian Hunter but it prevents units engaged with or being attacked by the Mandalorian Hunter from spending Dodge, Aim, or Surge tokens. This makes the already deadly Mandalorian Hunter that can be attacking with Pierce 2 even deadlier since it can prevent Surge tokens from being used as well.

Each of these units can be taken by the different Mandalorian Clans since they have the Mandalorian Clan upgrade slot. This lets you provide them with the different clan keywords which only add to the customizability of these characters. You can take a more aggressive Mandalorian Hunter with the Vibrosword and Victory or Death from the Clan Saxon upgrade so you can get free tokens or a defensive Mandalorian Leader with the Sniper, War Party Leader and We Fight for our Family so you can spread dodge tokens along with orders to other units. These units add a force multiplier to your army's and can be tailored for exactly what your army list needs.

Custom Mandalorian Clan

The Mandalorian Battleforce comes with a fifth clan that we have yet to talk about. The Custom Clan is unique from the other clans because you get a choice of 2 special rules from a list of 6.

In order to get this benefit you lock yourself out of all of the named characters and all veteran units as well. You also cannot bring any clan upgrades on your other units so you are locked out of all the clan upgrades. Between the 6 options some of the more interesting ones are Guns for Hire which let you bring a vehicle into your list, which the most interesting seems to be the AA-5 Speeder Truck because it can transport units and has more open upgrade slots. Another interesting option is Rapid Deployment which lets you set aside 2 units to be deployed onto the board not only in your deployment zone like Infiltrate, but anywhere range 2 away from all enemy units. This can be fun to combine with other options such as Guns for Hire so you can deploy your transport anywhere on the board and deliver a unit of Mandalorian Warriors behind the deploying vehicle. There are so many options available within these 6 choices for you to tailor a list completely for yourself and play Star Wars Legion uniquely from other players. You will need to decide if giving up the other clans is worth it for you but that is the fun part of list building in general is choosing which options work better for your personal style of play.
