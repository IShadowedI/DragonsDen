11th Edition Detachment Focus: Shoota Boyz

August 22nd, 2026 via Goonhammer
The Ork Codex arrives with a staggering 15 Detachments to choose from. We’re digging into all of them across a mammoth review series.

Thanks to Games Workshop for providing us with a preview copy of these rules. Any rules or points referenced are subject to change until published on the Warhammer 40K App.

Ork dakka is looking better than ever with the new Codex, and if you're want to squeeze the most out of that, the new Shoota Boyz Detachment provides you with great shooting boosts.

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

Disposition: Purge the Foe

Shoota Boyz is the spiritual successor to More Dakka!, and while it’s seen a number of changes to be brought into line from its previous incarnation, the core toolkit remains the same. It’s still all about giving your ORK INFANTRY models better reach, providing [Assault] across a ton of your army, but has leaned away from strict lethality and towards flexibility, with a variety of strats aimed at making your shooting better in specific scenarios. In a world of souping together 1 DP Detachments, dedicating a bit to massed Assault is going to feel pretty good.

Tankbustas. Credit: Rockfish

Detachment Rule: Dakka! Dakka! Dakka!

Your Ork Infantry units’ ranged attacks have [Assault], and an additional +3” range while Riled Up.

First, the good news: Assault is still here, and still fairly unconditional. All of your normal greenskins will appreciate the capacity to fire off a few rounds while charging up the board, and in any list taking bricks of things like Flash Gitz, Tankbustas, or even Weirdboy-led Boyz you’ll thoroughly appreciate the keyword. As for the rider while Riled, it’s worse than before, providing extra range as opposed to some number of Sustained Hits. This second component of the Detachment rule is more accessible, with Riling being pretty easy, but extra range isn’t especially helpful when you want to close that distance anyways, outside of a few specific profiles.

Enhancements

  • Supa Glowy Fing (25pts): ORKS INFANTRY model (excluding Weirdboy) only. The bearer’s ranged attacks have [Anti-Infantry 4+], [Devastating Wounds], & [Supa-Hazardous], the latter of which is just Hazardous that requires you make two hazard rolls instead of one. There’s not a ton of outstanding combos for this, beyond just being a solid upgrade to middling shooting, save for one: The Big Mek in Mega Armor, taking the Tellyport Blasta. While you will owe 4 hazard rolls in the aftermath, pairing this with a Kustom Mega-Blasta gives you 9 4+/S9/-2/3 attacks, ignoring cover, and seasoned to taste with Sustained Hits 1 & a bit of Blast. That’s going to carve up TEQs like nobody’s business, and with a Grot Oiler you can even ignore the worst of the initial self-inflicted damage. This feels like an intended (and very strong) combo, with the extra +3” range even helping you ensure the 12” Tellyport Blasta hits its mark.
  • Targetin’ Squig (20pts): BIG MEK/BIGBOSS/WARBOSS model only. Ranged attacks from the bearer’s unit have +3” range. A bit of extra reach for something like the new Nobz isn’t bad, but just unnecessary. This doesn’t help you clear any key range thresholds you couldn’t already with the second rider on the Detachment rule.

Stratagems

Big Mek in Mega Armour. Credit: Rockfish
  • Kustom Dakka (1 CP): During your Shooting phase, when you select an ORKS INFANTRY unit to shoot, that unit’s ranged attacks have +1 to wound against non-MONSTER/VEHICLE units. Not too much to say, this is a great tool to have in your kit, and oftentimes brings your above-average profiles to wounding on 3s or 2s. Nobz like this, Flash Gitz really like this, and even the humble Boyz might use it in a pinch.
  • Glowin’ Dakka (1 CP): During your Shooting phase, when you select an ORKS INFANTRY unit to shoot, that unit’s ranged attacks which target a unit within 9” have +1 AP. I want to very specifically call out this in stratagem’s use in concert with a Weirdboy, especially alongside the Wurrband Detachment. When you arrive from Deep Strike with 20 Boyz, you can do so more than 8” away…but certainly within 9”; this means you have a comical amount of AP1 Lethal Hit shots to dole out to basically anything on the board, and with a strength-boosted Weirdboy’s vomit and/or their own Da Krunch enhancement (another chunk of D1 Lethal Hits) to boot. There’s no easier way to get within 9” than to shunt yourself through the warp, directly into your opponent’s face.
  • Never Enough Dakka (1 CP): During your Shooting phase, when you select an ORKS INFANTRY unit to shoot, that unit’s ranged attacks have [Sustained Hits 1]. While this is quite a bit worse as a stratagem than a rider on the Detachment rule, it’s nice to still have access to massed Sustained + Lethal in this new More Dakka! analogue. The fact it’s the most universal of the stratagems in Shoota Boyz is likewise appreciated.

Detachment Combos

Based on the overall rise in power for Ork shooting in this codex, the better question regarding Shoota Boyz is what it can’t combo with. Any Ork list running key bricks of Boyz, Nobz, Tankbustas, or Flash Gitz is going to want this, and it similarly has play with the more Leader-focused Detachments, with a Big Mek in Mega Armor combo that can kill god, and accessible AP for Weirdboys. Green Tide, Bully Boyz, Wreckas, Taktikal Brigade, Madcap Meks, Wurrband—you can make a solid case to slot Shoota Boyz in as the glue to hold any of these disparate Detachments together. Personally, I’m most excited for Wurrband/Shoota Boyz/Blitz Brigade, but that also goes to show that the relative flexibility of this Detachment can tie together odd skew lists like that Psyker-focused option.

Is It Good?

Absolutely, and while it may not provide a clear direction, Shoota Boyz perhaps sits as the strongest option to splash with any other Detachment combination. It gives you the tools to answer specific threats your list may otherwise struggle with, and we already know how strong massed Assault on Infantry can be, with More Dakka! being played extensively in 11th’s pre-codex Orks. Unless points are egregious, you should expect to see Orks at top tables, and by extension, Shoota Boyz.

Final Thoughts

The new Detachments in the 11th Ork Codex run the gamut of being locked to as few as ~2 specific models, all the way to Shoota Boyz, which conceivably buffs your entire army. It’s an odd precedent to set, but with how detached from one another these build paths can get, there’s some sense in having a couple options be mechanical glue for the rest of the faction. This is a deep book, and it’s chock full of combos to explore…many of which wouldn’t mind some fairly-generic shooting buffs as a fallback, once the game gets scrappy.
