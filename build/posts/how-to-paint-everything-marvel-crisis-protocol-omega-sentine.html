How to Paint Everything: Marvel Crisis Protocol - Omega Sentinel

September 24th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Hey everybody, I'm back again with How to Paint Everything: Marvel Crisis Protocol, and today we are taking a look at Omega Sentinel from the new Sentinels affiliated Character Pack, featuring Bastion, Nimrod, and Omega Sentinel.

Omega Sentinel boasts a few different configurations for you to customize her look. Since I was first introduced to her in the House of X/Powers of X comic series, I decided to go with the Mega Buster style gun arm, back spikes and her bald head. But you have the choice for a masked head with hair and a few other arm options too.

Like Nimrod, Omega Sentinel's armor is mostly white, but this time we also have some red and black accents too. Her bald head has the markings on it, so you can paint it either with them or without them. I decided to keep it looking like her character card art and added them to it. We'll also go over some quick and dirty non-metallic metal as well as the customary Sentinel energy glow effects.

Below you'll find the handy paint chart, as usual. Featuring all of the colors that were used to paint her up. So, let's get rolling on it!

credit - Brushwizard

Head Skin Tone

Basecoat - Army Painter Dragon Red

Shadows - Army Painter Magecast Magenta

Layer - Army Painter Dragon Red, Army Painter Angelic Red

Highlight - Previous Mix, Reaper Rosy Skin

Highlight - Previous Mix, More Reaper Rosy Skin

Omega Sentinel's head started with a basecoat of Dragon Red. The recesses of her eye sockets, under her chin and any other shaded areas were then painted Magecast Magenta. Highlights started with a layer of Dragon Red mixed with Angelic Red. I then added Rosy Skin to the mix to brighten it up a bit, followed by another highlight layer with a bit more Rosy Skin added to the mix.

White Head Markings

Basecoat - AK Interactive Black

Layer - AK Interactive White

Shadow - AK Interactive Pastel Blue

The white markings on her head started with a base of Black to define the shapes. Then I painted White inside the shapes, leaving a black line around the perimeter. Shaded areas were given a layer of Pastel Blue to darken them up a bit and added color variation.

White Armor

Basecoat - Citadel Grey Seer Spray Primer

Layer - AK Interactive Pastel Blue

Layer - AK Interactive Ivory

Dark Lining - AK Interactive French Blue

Highlight - AK Interactive White

As with most of my recent white armors, I start with a basecoat of Grey Seer primer. The non-metallic metal effect started with a layer of Pastel Blue, making sure to leave some of the Grey Seer visible on the surface. Next, the reflective surface highlights continue with a layer of Ivory on top of the Pastel Blue layer, leaving some of the previous layer showing as well. The armor panel lines were dark lined with French Blue, and then all of the armor panels were edge highlighted with White.

Red Armor Sections

Basecoat - Army Painter Dragon Red

Shadows - Army Painter Dragon Red, Army Painter Angel Green

Layer - Army Painter Angelic Red

Highlight - Army Painter Flickering Flame

Highlight - AK Interactive White

Like her head, the red sections of her armor started with Dragon Red. This time, shadows were painted by mixing Angel Green into Dragon Red to create a darker red; this was painted into any areas that would be darker or in shadow. Highlights started by painting a layer of Angelic Red over the Dragon Red areas, leaving some Dragon Red visible. It was then highlighted and edge highlighted with Flickering Flame and White.

Black Striping

Basecoat - AK Interactive Black

Layer - AK Interactive Black: AK Interactive French Blue

Highlight - AK Interactive French Blue

The black striping of her suit started with a basecoat of Black. It was then highlighted with a mix of Black and French Blue, followed by a simple final highlight of French Blue on its own.

Metals

Basecoat - AK Interactive French Blue

Shadows - Thinned AK Interactive Dark Sea Blue

Highlight - AK Interactive French Blue

Highlight - AK Interactive White

Dark Lining - AK Interactive Black

All of the metal areas of this model were painted with a base coat of French Blue. Next, I thinned Dark Sea Blue and painted all of the metal with it to get that color into any recesses in the bionics. It was then highlighted using French Blue and then White. Shadows were reinforced by painting Black into any recesses that I felt needed a bit more oomph.

Glowing Armor Lights

Basecoat - AK Interactive White

Layer - Lethal Shadows Fluorescent Red

All of her light effects were painted White, followed by Fluorescent Red.

Gun Arm Glow

Basecoat - AK Interactive White

Layer - Lethal Shadows Fluorescent Light Orange

Layer - Lethal Shadows Fluorescent Red

The glow inside the barrel of her gun was given a basecoat of White. I then used Fluorescent Light Orange inside the entire area and then Fluorescent Red around the perimeter.

Hoses

Basecoat - AK Interactive Black

Highlight - AK Interactive Dark Sea Blue

Highlight - AK Interactive Dark Sea Blue: AK Interactive Ivory

The hoses that are holding her up off of her base started with a basecoat of Black. It was given a layer of Dark Sea Blue to make them look a bit rubbery, and then highlighted with a mix of Dark Sea Blue and Ivory.

Concrete Basing

Basecoat - Citadel Stormvermin Fur

Stipple - Army Painter Ash Grey

Stipple - Army Painter Brigade Grey

Wash - Citadel Nuln Oil

As usual, the concrete basing started with a base layer of Stormvermin Fur, followed by stippling of Ash Grey and Brigade Grey. All of the concrete sections were then washed with Nuln Oil to tie it together and add some shadows to the recesses in the base.

Dirt Basing

Basecoat - Citadel Gorthor Brown

Wash - Citadel Agrax Earthshade

Any dirt on the base was painted Gorthor Brown and then washed with Agrax Earthshade.

credit - Brushwizard

With that, Omega Sentinel is now ready to blast her way onto the tabletop! You can find Omega Sentinel alongside Bastion and Nimrod, the Hunter in the MCP09 Bastion, Nimrod & Omega Sentinel Character Pack, which is now available for purchase at your FLGS or online. As always, a big thank you goes out to Atomic Mass Games and Asmodee for sending this character pack my way. It's very much appreciated!
