Detachment Focus: Darkflight Pursuit

August 4th, 2026 via Goonhammer
In our Detachment Focus series we take a deep dive into an army’s Detachments, covering what’s in them, how they play, and how they’ll fit into the broader meta and your games. In this Detachment Focus we’re looking at the Darkflight Pursuit Detachment for the Dark Angels.

The Dark Angels were among the big winners of the new detachment lottery with the release of 11th edition, getting a massive number of new Detachments. Competitively the most important of these is the Darkflight Pursuit, which has made the d

Detachment Cost: 1 DP

Force Disposition: Reconnaissance

The Ravenwing continue their eternal hunt for the Fallen and all other enemies of the Chapter. Darkflight Pursuit is all about enabling the speeders and aircraft of the chapter to maximize their output and reveal the enemy for the rest of the Chapter to mete out the Lion’s judgement. It directly benefits your army's fastest units, and directly plays with the new Land Speeder datasheet released in July. The result is a Detachment which lets Dark Angels armies more or less zip around the table with impunity.

Detachment Rule - Black-Winged Vigilance

Friendly Ravenwing FLY units’ ranged attacks have [IGNORES COVER].

This is an insanely powerful Detachment rule for a 1 DP Detachment. This means ignoring the -1 BS penalty to your hit rolls and ensuring you're never losing accuracy with your key units.

For reference, the Ravenwing FLY units in the supplement are the Land Speeder Vengeance, the Ravenwing Darkshroud, the Nephilim Jetfighter, Sammael, and the Ravenwing Dark Talon -- however keep in mind that all VEHICLE FLY units in Codex: Space Marine get the Ravenwing keyword in Dark Angels. This means all of the Storm Speeder variants, the new Land Speeder, and the ever-present Stormraven Gunship all benefit from this Detachment rule.

Black Templars Storm Speeder Thunderstrike. Credit: SRM

Enhancements

  • Thundercowl Turbines (Upgrade, 15pts). Ravenwing FLY unit; allows you to make an ingress move in your first Movement Phase. Pretty interesting - there’s a very silly combination with this on three Land Speeder Vengeances and then as many other Speeder units as you can shove into the Fulguris Task Force for maximum T1 deep strike plays. Also allows your Aircraft to actually participate in all five turns of the game.
  • Nightforged Battery (Upgrade, 15pts). Land Speeder Vengeance only. This unit can re-roll the dice for the number of attacks with its weapons and can re-roll Hazard rolls. Very solid, makes the Land Speeder Vengeance considerably more reliable as the d6+1 shot count has a lot of variance and with only 10 wounds it has a tendency to cook itself during the game. This more or less ups your expected shout count from 4.5 to 5.5, assuming you're re-rolling any time you get a result of 1-3.

Stratagems

  • Skyborne Surveillance (1 CP). Used after a Ravenwing FLY unit from your army has shot - gives +3” detection range to visible enemy units within 6” of that Ravenwing FLY unit. Somewhat interesting - as you don’t have to have shot at those units to affect them, just for them to be visible to the shooting unit. Fun way to counter units that are benefitting from Go To Ground or if you just can’t quite reach a Hidden unit on that key objective.
  • Wings of Shadow (1 CP). Gives a friendly Ravenwing FLY unit stealth when they’re targeted in your opponent’s Shooting phase. This is a nice little buff, though with how common cover is, this is really for those moments when you're caught completely out in the open and need the extra boost.
  • We Are Vengeance (1 CP). In your opponent’s Shooting phase this allows you to make a normal move of up to d3+3” after one of your Ravenwing FLY units has been shot. There's an interesting interaction here - Sammael has FLY and Ravenwing so this can be used to set up a Heroic Intervention play with his unit. It's probably not that common an occurrence, but in a game where movement is king this stratagem will shine. The main use will be to limit activations into your key units as you can move out of range / visibility of the other threats after the first enemy unit has shot.

We Are Vengeance is the real sauce here, giving you a ton of extra movement to either get out of line of sight or steal objectives. Suddenly dumping an OC land speeder on an objective and stealing it will make an opponent think twice about taking pot shots at your T8, 9W vehicle.

Credit: Greg Chiasson

Playing This Detachment

The name of the game here is speed. Land Speeders are already fast units - moving 14" with FLY is huge - but having the new Land Speeder makes things even better. You can use the Purgation Run ability to move, shoot, then make a normal move of D6", and if you get shot by the opponent after that, you can still make another D3+3" move with We Are Vengeance. The competitive play is to pair this Detachment with Masters of Maoeuvre, which gives your entire army the ability to shoot after they Advance or Fall Back, giving you even more mobility and making it possible to have turns where you trivially move 18"+ before ducking back behind cover. The other Stratagems you get here - Hunters' Trail to sticky objectives, High-Speed Focus to stack another -1 to be hit on one of your units, and Rapid Reappraisal to go back into reserves give you a ton of flexibility.

While you have options for your units, your best options are going to be Speeders, and lots of them. The new Land Speeder Datasheet is very, very good, and the Land Speeder Vengeance and Storm Speeder Thunderstrike are both very good options as well. Ravenwing units also benefit here, and Black Knights are also a solid play. Sammael is also a powerful force here, and having him setting around with a 21 OC brick can be incredibly tough to handle. Oh, and because he gives them the FLY keyword, if he leads a unit of Ravenwing Black Knights they gain the [IGNORES COVER] rule. Fun stuff.

With Company of Hunters you'll have access to Disruption as well. Don't. Stick to Reconnaissance - your army is fast and capable of quickly pushing into key positions for scoring as you need.

Strengths

  • Makes Land Speeders and Storm Speeders absolutely menacing on the table
  • You just kind of ignore a lot of the game's rules in a way that can be frustrating for opponents.

Weaknesses

  • You are basically playing Eldar.

A Sample List

If you want to play Ravenwing, this is the list for you. There are a few different ways to run this, but you're going to be leaning heavily on Speeders and mixing in other units to taste - Ravenwing Black Knights are a solid add, but the Lion, Azrael, and Inner Circle marines all have a place here as well.

Jeremy Atkinson took this list to a 6-1, 2nd place finish at the Salt City GT in early August, losing to only Emperor's Children in the final round after scoring 100 VP in five of his six rounds prior.

It's still an incredibly powerful list and more or less lets you put all of your favorite Dark Angels pieces on the table. Speeders aren't good in combat but are durable enough to survive bad combats, and because of Company of Hunters they can't be locked down in melee - you can just leave and go wherever you want and shoot on the following turn. The key with this list is to control the tempo and prevent your opponent from interacting with anything meaningfully on the game's early turns while whittling them down and picking off key threats.

Final Thoughts

The Darkflight Pursuit Detachment is incredibly good and currently helps position Dark Angels as one of the game's true melee terrors. When combined with Company of Hunters it gives players an insane amount of speed and flexibility without sacrificing damage output.
