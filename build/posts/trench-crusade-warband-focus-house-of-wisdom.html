Trench Crusade Warband Focus: House of Wisdom

July 17th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Personalization is one of the largest draws within the wargaming hobby. The ability to paint, customize, and make our models unique to our own vision is a unique option that many individuals pride themselves with. When it comes to rules, sometimes rigidity makes this personalization reliant primarily on the visuals we create and narrative we tell. However, the Iron Sultanate has the ability to hold its head high in this regard, specifically with its variant House of Wisdom. We are going to dive into what makes these book nerds unique and how they offer an astonishing realm of choice to truly personalize your warband.

The Dynamic Duo

Painted by John Shields

Losing access to our noble Yüzbasi Captain, instead we have no model with the keyword leader. Rather, our joint efforts are the mad scientist duo of not one… but TWO Jabirean Alchemists! Alchemists are a flexible powerhouse within core IS, and that doesn’t disappear here. With the ability to grant a Blood Marker generating Keyword to all their weapons, they are excellent at priming targets for bloodbaths. With lucky skill rolls, a gunslinger alchemist can single handedly take down some of the largest threats on the field as well!

Each of these mad lads can also take a Secrets of the House of Wisdom, aka another cool ability for a nominal fee. Every option is situationally phenomenal and has a place within varying lists. Medicine allows +1 dice medical checks that can remove 2 Blood Markers (1 Infection Marker) or stand up a downed model within 1”. This is one of 3 abilities in the game that heals infection markers, which makes it very useful in matchups against Black Grail. The flat 2 Blood Markers over 1d3 standard of med kits also means a higher base rate of healing vs RNG of rolling. 

Cartography allows us to grant 2 models on 32mm bases or less the INFILTRATOR Keyword. This allows us to have early movement for mission congestion or flamethrowers to secure surprise kills vs primed targets. Secrets of Tawkin allows a Homonculus to gain the bodyguard ability from the Communicant, but only taking blows for their specific Alchemist. This can situationally be very useful with an aggressive gunslinger alchemist, more often than not the other options do tend to overshadow this one.

Chemistry allows us to get weird and manipulate the board by placing a 2x6 inch piece of Dangerous/Difficult terrain with our choice of FIRE, GAS, or SHRAPNEL. This can cut off movement routes, access to mission objectives, and much much more. This option allows a lot of control on game flow right out the gates before dice are even rolled. Lastly we have Philosophy. By not having a Leader model, we are at flat morale checks without a troop flag. This option grants us a +1 DICE to morale checks to mitigate our lack of a leader as long as that alchemist isn’t Down or Out of Action. It also grants the added benefits of no risky rolls after breaking morale and gives that alchemist the Negate Fear keyword.

Favorite Flesh Heaps

Painted by John Shields

Alongside each alchemist is their favorite flesh heap, your friendly neighborhood Tawkin Homonculus. For each alchemist we gain one, allowing a whopping two build-a-bear boys. This is where we start to get into the creative creation we gain in this variant. Some players may be aware of Homonculus due to the exploration table even without playing House of Wisdom previously. Imagine the fun you had building them, and double it.

Unlike the exploration Homonculus, the House of Wisdom sets no perimeters to how much we can bling out our chaps with mutations. The balance is baked INTO the mutations; some don’t permit mixing with others. I’m not gonna break down every mutation and combination… there's a lot. But if you can think of it, it's doable. Want a flying sniper that is mobile and hard to kill? Bam, you got your own Sniper Elite. Want a flamethrower holder that you could infiltrate with Cartography and hold his own once charged? Welcome to the jungle. How about a SECOND BULL (I did not in fact stutter, we call that beautiful chap a Bullmonculus)? The possibilities are as endless as your pockets.

Speaking of your pockets, yes these fleshy chaps are expensive to build out. But, the risk is withdrawn when fielding them. When they inevitably perish horribly within the trenches or city streets you find yourself in, you can simply recreate them for 40 ducats. They lose NOTHING, no additional costs needed. Simply reshape that lump of meat and kick 'em back out to the warzone to rinse and repeat.

Lab Assistants / Specimens

Painted by John Shields

Our spoon loving Janissaries can step to the side, we have our strong armed Fāris there to carry all our heavy shipments (and horrific weapons). Having a diminished number available, only 2, they instead gain the ELITE keyword and can start gaining XP (and injuries) right out the gate! Besides building out Homonculus specifically (technically Bull too, but they have their own armory), these are our only Strong individuals in the army. We have to be smart about what we bring (or steal) with the Heavy keyword due to this limitation.

Our weak armed Azebs are replaced by their bane juice loving siblings, Kavasses. They can be persuaded to hit the gym, going to a flat +0 Dice for melee instead of their siblings -1. That gym membership is not free however and does cost 5 ducats additional. The gym membership also makes them ego trip and never back out of a street fight, causing them to lose the Light Skirmisher ability.

Do you consider yourself a cat person? Do you want… more cats? Well I’m happy to say House of Wisdom gets to take up to THREE Lions of Jabir. With the update lions received in the April Rules review, buying CLEAVE 2 makes them particularly angry kitties. The movement, lockdown, and damage they offer for their price can’t be beat and we welcome the extra body happily here.

Opening the Pantry

Not only do we get some specialty Battle-Kit, we get to borrow sugar from the neighbors. That's right, we get to take 1 item each from New Antioch and Trench Pilgrims. If you thought the homunculus offered customization, you're eating the cake now. There are an assortment of options, but some of the best are Automatic Rifles, Heavy Shotguns, Punt-guns, and Anti-Tank Hammers to name a few. Automatic Rifles make an Alchemist into a Blood Marker machine well before they ever get Gunslinger. Heavy Shotguns and Punt-guns are great for our Faris or specialized Homonculus. And a melee-built Homonculus with an Anti-Tank Hammer… *chefs kiss*.

None of our elites have the tough keyword within House of Wisdom. Instead, we gain access to a unique battle-kit; Elixir of Al-Khindr. This consumable allows us to give a non-artificial model the keyword tough for a battle. Gunslinger Alchemist and an aggressive Faris are prime targets for this upgrade, allowing them to survive on the frontlines a bit longer. At only 10 ducats, this is some cheap additional survivability to slap on your most important model.

Famously, the Iron Sultanate both loves and hates on fire. With alchemists burning everything to the ground and their armor negating it, it makes sense that the maddest of the mad would cook something else up. Introducing the Fire Shield, we gain a shield that is double the cost but gains Negate Fire AND -1 Injury Dice to injury rolls from a source with the Fire Keyword. Those pesky flamethrowers ruining your day? This means that even though they ignore armor, they still roll at -2 Dice for the injury.

Last, and most certainly not least, we journey back to a battle-kit piece available to all of Iron Sultanate as of the April Rules Review; the Al-inbiq Kit. If you haven’t noticed, the House of Wisdom loves fake things (or as Trench Crusade calls them; Artificial). Our lions? A lie. Our famous Homonculous? Never existed. The Bull themselves? I didn't see them. Totaling our Artificial count to 6 models potentially. That means our alchemist can heal or stand up any of these 6 models as an action within 6” of themselves. Combined with the Medicine Secret and a potential stolen med-kit, these Alchemists can bring any of our fake lads back from the brink of death continually.

Open Door Policy

Whether you're new to Iron Sultanate or a lover of its other variants or base; House of Wisdom has everything you could want. The flexibility to push your playstyle as you see fit, to truly sculpt your models to the image you hold within your own mind. This is THE variant for customization without a doubt. The doors to the House of Wisdom are always open, new faces are always welcome within the cabal; either as specimen or soldiers.
