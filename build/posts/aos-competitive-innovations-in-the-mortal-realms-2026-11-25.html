[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-11-25

November 25th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

After a couple weeks of 1-2 events, we got a solid 4 events week of mid-size, from the US, to the UK and even up to Sweden.

This Week's Events

  • Renegade Open 2026 - AoS GT - United States
  • Realmshift Series 2026 - Chapter 3 - United Kingdom
  • The Standoff 2026 AoS - United States
  • Fantasia Fanatic L Age of Sigmar - Sweden

Thoughts

Awakened Wyldwoods are going to be a serious pain in the ass for a Pyregheist, hiding in the woods until the time is right to strike. While Nighthaunt have way more guys to capture objectives with, Sylvaneth get to play the long game. Strike out with Kurnoths, Alarielle and Spiteriders, then hide back in the trees until they are sufficiently weakened to secure victory.

Nighthaunt usually win by playing objectives or battle tactics, not brute strength, as they lack some serious hard hitting units like other armies do. But Sylvaneth can almost patch them pound for pound in movement and that's going to be a real tough challenge to keep up with.

Sylvaneth Victory - 68 - 46

Branchwych. Credit: Rockfish

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Alarielle and Kurnoths

Thoughts

With this list you have some decent flexibility with being able to remain back in your trees and hold your ground while also being able to strike out. Sylvaneth is an army that plays the long game, playing conservatively as their Overgrowth spreads out so they can better control the board in the second half of the game. With Spiteriders, you can fly out, charge and destroy a unit and then leave at the end of turn, allowing you to weaken the enemy before they come into your treeline where the Kurnoths and Alarielle beat them senseless. Spite-revenants are annoying enough, and cheap enough, to put them on objectives to capture them. Even if they die shortly after, they did their job.

Thoughts

Tzeentch tends to excel against armies that can't cover much of the board, due to their very high mobility and ability to deep strike in from reserves or swap out with other units to secure objectives. Ossiarchs, meanwhile, tend to excel best at being able to turtle up and not expand too far outside their castle, which puts them at a real disadvantage here. Tzeentch can outplay them at every turn and even if they do set up on a necessary objective, the Daemon Prince and Tzaangors are waiting to jump them and put in some actual work in the combat phase.

The List

Archetype

Tzeentch Good Stuff

Thoughts

Tzeentch generally is an army that's trying to play to objectives rather than raw strength. Their ability to teleport, swap out units, and generally move rapidly around the battlefield while miring their opponents in tarpits while they chuck mortal wounds at them makes them very frustrating.

Pretty much everything in the list works to serve this goal, Horrors are a tarpit with a Lord of Change making them -1 to be hit, slinging out mortal wounds with spells and even bringing back more to do it all over again. If you do need a hammer to swing into things, you got the Tzaangor Enlightened and Daemon Prince, swap them out of reserve and surprise your foes!

Thoughts

The Daughters of Khaine here get to function basically as a blunt instrument, with very strong hammers than can cleave through a lot of different units on the opposing side with very little resistance. Soulblight definitely has a lot more stuff, and some very strong board control with deep strike and Mannfred's movement shenanigans, but given the opportunity Morathi and her Blood Sisters (plus buffed up Blood Hags) will tear through the enemy and deal with the reinforcements just the same.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Morathi and Krethusa

Thoughts

Including both Morathi and Krethusa is a very expensive choice that forces you to cut a lot of infantry and makes the units you bring that much more valuable, but on the flip side Krethusa is a very solid Priest with a versatile spell and she becomes near impossible to hit once your blood rites get online.

Between your usual Shadow Queen and the Blood Sisters combo, you have your hard hitting hammer. The blood hags when properly supported can hold the line while being rather strong in their own right, and the Khinerai grab objectives/fulfill battle tactics.
