Mayday, Miss Marcy! That One Northernlion Clip, Reselling a Gift, Cutetastrophe

July 30th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome back once again lovely readers to the last Mayday, Miss Marcy of July! Hard to believe how it seemed like 2026 would be a year that could never end, and yet here we are passing well beyond the halfway point of a year! Summer has been particularly brutal on most of us, but even so, we sally on to take on new hobby tasks and projects; as you may have heard, I even started taking up model kit building again! As Summer starts to slowly wane into Autumn, we've still got a few weeks left for any real burning questions that need some answers.

If you're curious about how you can have your own questions answered in this very column, then please read the blurb below:

Have a question you want answered on just about anything you’d need advice on in this grand hobby space of ours? Board games, 40k, AOS, TTRPGs, MTG, anything and everything that we cover, we can help with!

For some examples, take a look at previous editions of our column at this link with a handy tag to see all previous questions and answers!

ALSO, we're heading OUT of Summer, so if you have some Back to School, Fall, or similar turn of the seasons questions, let us hear it!

If you want to submit questions, we have a few ways you can do it. Patrons can use the Discord bot command to ask questions, which will be received anonymously!

If you’re not a Patron, or just don’t want extra steps, you can comment on this article, or e-mail me directly at marcy@tabletopbattles.com!

Last week, we dealt with mismatched rulers and more, and this week, I have another collection of 3 questions for your perusal!

They Call Him The Wokeler

Dear Ms Marcy,
I am, as much as possible, Very Woke. However, while I do have the extreme fortune of having been raised relatively politically aware, I also have a tendency to slip up and say or do something Decidedly Unwoke that I picked up somewhere. This has tipped into my hobbying world, where occasionally I’ll lean into an aesthetic that I think about and go “oh crap I screwed that up”.
 Some examples of times I’ve screwed up: the night lord I painted dark blue, with a prominent distorted skull logo, accidentally making something that looked quite a bit like a Neo-nazi space marine. Or, for example, the Armageddon steel legion logo I painted on the leg of a different marine, which I then realized may or may not be a Nazi logo, which also made me realize I don’t know a lot of nazi logos. Said marine also had a camo pattern that made me think “oh god, did I pick that up off a Nazi tank somewhere?”. And now, a project I just started involving a tzeentchian army based on the Islamic golden age, which I really really don’t want to turn into an “Islamic people are demons” army.
Anyways, how do I up my Woke Meter, in terms of hobbying? Specifically, what are some good ways to spot and avoid Neo-nazi imagery and aesthetics? Also, how do I represent a non-European culture faithfully while still being fantastical and still being fun to make?
Gratefully yours,
The Woker, Baby
Have you ever seen the Northernlion clip? Yes, THAT one. Because that is what this question sounds like, assuming this is not some sort of elaborate ruse question to see if I'll give you The Most Woke Answer of All Time and then giggling about how TTB is CringeWoke or something similar. I'm trying not to be totally dismissive of the question entirely, so I just wanted to say that these were the things that came to mind when reading the question, because at best it sounds like you are just comically overthinking things by accident.
I don't think there's an easy way around the fact that many wargames have a fascism adjacent problem. 40k basically uses it as window dressing, historicals are full of "just asking questions" types or those who are a little TOO happy to paint WW2 Germans, companies with questionable painting dioramas, etc. But in your case, it sounds like you're just overthinking it and worrying that people will see what you've done and accuse you of being secretly anti-woke or whatever you want to call it. And I'm going to let you in on a little secret:
Sometimes its fine to just not overthink things like this. You don't have to be Performative Woke all the time and it can be exhausting (to yourself and others) to try and do that. If you are actually a person with strong character who would not and does not support those things, no one is going to suddenly think you do because you painted a weird little spaceman (especially if he's evil). As for the the other question, that one is harder: not doing cultural appropriation can be pretty hard, frankly, because if you are not from the culture you're borrowing from, almost anything might read as orientalist or othering. I think the key is to just focus on not making it demonizing or flagrantly stereotypical, like putting little wicker hats on all of your units to make them "Asian", or making sure everyone knows your Islamic themed army is Islamic by having models in Burkas or talking about them killing infidels all the time or something similar.
Basically, it's great that you are aware of things, or trying to be, but don't stress yourself out over not knowing every single Neo-nazi icon in the world (that would be, frankly, weird). If someone does say something about a model you painted (which they likely never will), you can just be honest and say you didn't know. I think there's also a lot of course over correction these days with media that includes "problematic" elements, whether it be complaining that fiction needs to always have good morals or no conflict or etc., and that does also go to your armies: if the army you're making are meant to be Neo-fascists, then that's fine, just don't paint the literal swastika on them.

Regifting Is So 90s, Jerry

Dear Marcy,

My friends recently (like, last year) gave me some models as gifts for the holidays. i appreciated the gift at the time but i have since kind of shifted gears away from playing the game the models were for to a different game. is it okay for me to sell them, and should i tell them before i do so? I'm worried they'll think i didn't appreciate the gift or something worse.

the gifted

Dear Gifted,

Well, appreciate is an interesting word. You said you appreciated it at the time, and certainly they seem to have believed you would have liked them. If you even had asked for them, it would make sense that they got the items for you as part of what you asked for. But people change, and so do their tastes, so this isn't unheard of. Also, you didn't immediately turn around and start listing them on eBay or something after receiving them, you just simply had a change in priorities. There's nothing wrong with making better use of the gift by selling it and getting something you ARE into from that, especially after nearly 8 months of not using them (or longer). I don't think you need to tell your friends, either, that you intend to sell them, unless the items were specific and very hard to come by. But if this is simply a box of Space Marines or something, your friends likely got them on sale from an LGS or Amazon and the price was worth the thought of getting you a gift. What you do with that gift is your own business. If you were going to throw the gift in the trash instead of sell it, would you tell them? No, right? So, why would you tell them here? They would likely be happy to know that you appreciated the thought and effort at the time, and that you are using that gift to get yourself something else you really enjoy now. Go forth and resell that gift, Gifted.

No Longer Cute

Dear Marcy,

A player at my LGS is the type that runs "CuteMarines", as in they are painted in extremely bright pastel colors, lots of white and pink and glitter. i know i am going to sound like a hater, but the problem i have is not with the models, it is with the player's attitude.

whenever this player loses units, loses games, loses anything, they start complaining that it is because of their models and that people are being mean to them. i think the intent is to be cutesy about this, but it instead is incredibly annoying and a turn off of playing with this person at all. i feel like i should just say knock it off, but then i feel like i'll be accused of, well, being mean to them because of their models, which is just a cycle.

not-anti-cute, just anti-cringe

Dear NACJAC,

You'd be surprised at how much I actually understand the question and know that you are trying to express something that sounds like if you said it out loud you'd be ostracized. You aren't calling this persons' models ugly or cringe, but instead their behavior, which they are using the models to mask or excuse. I think that sucks, and I don't blame you for being fed up with it. I actually think that if they are trying to be "cute", they're doing a very bad job of it, and instead this sounds like a very long-game type of passive-aggressive acting that is looking to get someone to explode at them over it. Much like the first question this week, there's nothing wrong with being annoyed by this person's antics, and it wouldn't matter what their army looked like or how they had it painted; they're simply using it as a shield to dissuade people from criticizing them or even making up for bad playing, which is infuriating.

I would say that the next time it happens, specifically about losing, is that you should simply speak up about it: "No, I'm not doing this because of how your models look, this is a war game and someone has to win and someone has to lose." If they continue, tell them you don't appreciate being accused of maliciously targeting them and that it is making you uncomfortable. That should get them to stop, and if it doesn't, I think that's when you call a judge/LGS person and file some form of complaint. Because the issue is that either no one will, or the next person that does this instead of you might make it the type of explosive event this person is obviously trying to goad them into. I know the joke of clear communication is one we make often about this column, but I actually respect your hesitation to consider it at first, because there are a lot of ways this could make you seem like a jerk.
