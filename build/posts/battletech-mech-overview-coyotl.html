BattleTech Mech Overview: Coyotl

May 20th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Everything went silent as the Sergeant's salvo took its hold. The Marshals stopped for a moment to glance over their shoulders, confusion evident in the slack posture of their ‘Mechs as they watched the building wall of snow bear down on their position.

“Good God, Kelvin…” I whispered, watching the carnage that was about to unfold upon the unwitting Lance. “Remind me to promote you when we get back to the Broken Tooth.”

Roaring snow billowed and churned as the avalanche swept down the mountain, slamming into the back of the advancing Marshals like a wave bowling over a toddler on the beach. That quartet of lumbering old cans tumbled, swept up in the tide of snow as it crashed down upon them. 

“I’ll hold you to it, Major.” The man sneered, his BattleAxe hefting its lasers up towards the sky as the last of the steam from his strike bled away. 

I turned, slowly striding towards the pile of white powder that marked the Marshal’s tomb. “This only changes so much. We’ve still got to take the ta-”

My voice broke as the snow in front of me shifted, rolling opposed to the rest of the pile. The berm exploded, cut open by a shear beam of light that scraped gold paint from my left torso, followed close behind by a small-bore cannon shell that skipped off my cockpit glass, setting my ears ringing like I’d been cold-clocked by a heavy-weight boxer. 

We stumbled at the hit, my ‘Mech threatening to lose its balance as I fought a rim of black in my vision. The pain cleared quickly, though the ringing certainly didn’t, taking with it the pressing darkness just in time to watch as the full lance, cracked armor plates and bent gun barrels and all slammed forth from the piled snow.

“SHIT!” I shouted, scrambling at the controls as the new Coyotl under me turned on a dime, its myomer groaning with exertion, the screaming slugs skating past just left of our hull.

The cannons from the ever advancing bulk of the brown and black Mackie barked again, the recoil shuffling off a deluge of snow from its panels and barrels while the heat of its PPCs flashed even more snow into a pall of steam. 

A barking crack belted on my right, heralding a bolt of blue and white that ripped through the pelting snow. Streaming particles shot on, leaving a wake of steam trailing after it as it slammed into the torso of the Ymir beside the Mackie, blasting a pyrotechnic spray of molten armor into the flashing night as the growling flash of our pulse laser went wide into a distant snow drift. A trilling wail sounded through the cockpit as the low-light target lock flashed crimson, the streak bin to my left having finally found a lock. I keyed the ignition stud, letting the missile flight fly with the rattling pop of kicker charges throwing the SRMs flying into the night on a hard track into the hulking old ‘Mech.

Explosions blossomed all across the ‘Mech as we passed, a small press of heat washing into the cockpit before being rapidly eaten away by the oppressive cold beyond the transpex. The Marshal Ymir stumbled at the hits, coming down hard on its left as I banked back around, keying the comm to get the rest Apple Lance’s attention. “Apple, we’re in the clear, keep pressing past these fools. Blue, stick it to them and keep on hammering. We’ll be back when the work is done.”

“Understood, Major.” Sergeant Bingham responded, his BattleAxe hauling over and hammering a heavy salvo into the stricken Ymir as it struggled to rise to its feet. 

This Marshal lance was pitiful, aside from the Mackie at its front, a mashing of ancient and tired primitive designs that were no more than an inch from being forced to settle down and die. Were they new graduates? Old-timers on their way out? Disillusioned warriors just looking for a last stand before they died?

Lucky for them we were all too happy to oblige. 
  • Major Josiah Collan, Dog Head  Mercenary Company Pirate Band, Coyotl Pilot, Prime Config. Rockwellawan, Fronc Reaches, April 4th 3131.   
—

Howdy Mechwarriors and welcome back to another weekly ‘Mech Overview! This week we’re continuing with the ever progressing tide of the ‘Mech’s development! As such the old shit bucket we’re looking at in bay four is…

A Coyotl?

Is… Is that a fucking Omni-Mech?

God Dammit.

Coyotl. Credit: Rockfish

Chassis

The Coyotl BEING AN OMNI-MECH has a common shared baseline that all the units must contend with for their design space. So, what is a Coyotl? What makes this 40 ton Coyote get drunk on the moon?
  • A 7/11 movement profile provided by a 280 XL engine.
  • A 99% (136 Pip) load of standard armor.
  • An Endo-Steel structure.
  • 10 Double Heatsinks.
  • 15.5 Tons.
  •  12 Armor on the Arms, 20 on the legs, 15 on the side torsos, 16 on the CT, 9 on the head. This complements a 5/7/5 rear arc.
  • A base unloaded cost of 680 BV
  • No hard mounted equipment.
Honestly this is an awesome base for a medium weight striker that can scream in, hammer something’s rear arc, and then zip right back out. Or a pair of them, one for opening, one for crit seeking. Man I hope there’s some cool configs in here!

Variants

These mechs have all been reviewed based on a standard F through S scale, which you can find described on our landing page here (along with all of our other ‘mech reviews, the name of the box you can buy to get any of the mechs we have covered, and our general methodology).

Prime

The Coyote’s a Survivor. Reckon he’s got to be.

The Prime is a ‘man you sure are a Clan fast Medium ‘Mech’, aren’t you?’ loadout. A quintessential one, even. Packing an ERPPC in the Right Arm, an LRM 10 with one(1) ton of ammo in the Left Torso, an MPL in the Right Torso, and a Streak 4 in the left arm with one(1) ton of ammo. Supported by 1 extra Double Heat Sink the Prime goes up 4+ movement if the Streak manages to catch a lock and 1+ movement if not.

For 1,974 BV this isn’t exactly in my ‘man I’m excited to pay this price!’ band. Good guns and fast movement can only get you so far if you’re too expensive to reasonably field without worry that you’re gonna get bullied by something like a Wraith. I like the concept here but at the same time it’s also trying to do way too many things at once. That cost is one thing if you’re a monster buzzsaw or horrid long range ‘can’t touch me’ machine but as a ‘Mech that’s just doin’ weird fast Centurion Trooper shit I really do not like that price.

Liberty’s Rating: D+. Holy shit that is expensive for what is essentially a fast juiced up 40 ton Centurion. One especially stiff breeze and you’re losing an appreciable portion of your force clean off the field. I feel like this chassis is ripe for making a pair of configs that work great together in a can opener crit seeker pair but this ain’t it.

A

No, not like that.

The A, with its poor listening comprehension, attempts to do both the can opening and the crit seeking that I had requested on its own. Unfortunately its escapades after this goal have landed it at 1,753 BV. So, what’re you getting for this Clan Spec Cost?
  • One(1) Large Pulse Laser in the Right Arm
  • One(1) Small Pulse Laser in the Center Torso
  • Two Streak SRM-4s, one in each side torso, with one ton of ammo in the Left Torso.
  • Seven Jump Jets.
That’s it, that’s the bag. This has an opening idea of being this horrid striker that lands behind you, ripples off the LPL and SPL to see what they can’t gnaw open before blasting you with the Streak boxes, which is cool and would be awesome… if it wasn’t still really expensive for what you’re getting with that 40 ton frame. It’s a REALLY good frame but this just feels like it isn’t how it ought be used. 18 possible gun heat with 20 sinking means that on the run if both boxes lock you’re neutral and on a jump you’d be up 5. This is quite manageable and usable but man that price.

Note: This is the Second Cheapest Coyotl.

Liberty’s Rating: C-. The price is a bit more reasonable and the loadout takes a good use of the Coyot’s speed to get in on the knife fight but it’s still north of 1500 for a cLPL, some good movement, and some misc. Streak boxes because we both know that SPL isn’t all that real when it all boils down to it. Honestly it’s a lot for what you’re getting. Being able to do it all every turn on the run is cool but you’re paying for those jump jets and if you’re not gonna be using them I kinda just wish their weight plus that small pulse was like… bigger streak boxes and maybe a couple MPLs?

Davion Heavy Guards Coyotl. Credit: Jack Hunter

B

The Coyote, much like the Cowboy, also carries a six-shooter.

This is the, in my opinion, best Coyotl. Eschewing the A’s frivolous jumping and the Prime’s abhorrent cost the B goes for raw efficiency in the way that any good trooper actually should. Costing a reasonable and quite useful 1,447 BV the B is the cheapest of all the Coyotls by about 300 BV. So, what’re you getting at that price point?

Well, easy: A big gun.

The B mounts up the device that reins in all Omni-Mech costs at some point: A UAC/10 with 2 tons of ammo in the Right arm. The old iron is backed up by an ER Medium Laser in the Right Torso that mostly matches range bands with the UAC and an SRM 6 with one(1) ton of ammo in the left arm. This thing is -3 on a running alpha and just kinda keeps going until the bins are empty. I love this thing, I use it all the time. Shit’s hilarious and always ends up being a distracting problem when it appears behind someone’s trundling assault and simply states ‘Ultra, on 7s’ before burying two 10 pointers and an SRM flight into the rear arc.

Liberty’s Rating: B+. Look I’m a sucker for efficient UAC trucks and this thing is great, truly about as close to a Clan Blitzkrieg as I think we’re really allowed to get. God bless this thing.

C

God I love ATMs and Plasma Weapons… OH GOD, NOT AT THE SAME TIME, OH N-

The C is 1,851 BV to bring in an ATM 6, a Plasma Cannon, an ER Large Laser, and an ECM suite. This is a lot. But at least we can fire it all off at once, right? I mean you can but you go up 3+ movement which isn’t really great. It’s workable but it isn’t the best option. I’m not entirely sure what this wants to do. Does it want to peck someone with the ERLL and the ATM? Annoy them with the ATM and the Plasma cannon? Or maybe make an end run on them with all of it and hopefully turn something important off in the process with the ECM?

I, quite simply, don’t know. This is an eclectic mix of weapons where two of them are full tilt ‘ah fuck it’s expensive time’ bait and the third is certainly there with its ammo. I don’t like this. I would’ve loved a fat stack ATM carrier or something built on this chassis but good god this is not it.

Liberty’s Rating: D. I… Get away from me, you’ve squandered how funny you could’ve been as two separate machines.

D

Oh hey the ‘it matches the art for IlClan stuff!’ one!

The D is the most expensive Coyotl at 1,980 BV. It gets this monstrous price tag by packing in a CT mounted TAG, a Streak LRM-10 with one(1) ton of ammo in the Left Arm, an Improved Heavy Large Laser in the Right Arm, and a random APGR with one ton of ammo in the Right Torso.

Also there is a TComp here… and a Super Charger.

I mean a really fast blink and you’ll miss it TCd IHLL is incredibly funny. Like ‘I’d use the shit out of this in meme lists’ kinds of funny. But it’s not good. Or, well, it’s not 1,980 BV good.

If everything here locks up and goes down range then you build 1+ movement. If the SLRM fails to find a solution then you’re at Movement -1. Manageable enough, every now and then you just don’t fire the SLRM or the APGR and call it good, I suppose.

Liberty’s Rating: D-. Fucking Christ do any of you Clanners know any restraint?

Final Thoughts

It’s such a cool looking ‘Mech man. It’s this really fun idea in the later eras of a readily available all about the place fast striker scout weirdo that’s the kind of shit I love in fiction… and yet all of the configs let it down. I’d love to see a few true blue dedicated to purpose configs here. A real ‘I’m a scout and E-WAR package. A good ATM striker. A good light hunter, doesn’t have to be pulse mind you, I’ll accept some weirdo TC abominations! Or maybe even just another good trooper loadout that keeps it in its pants.

Either way, hopefully we get more Coyotls as time goes on. Until next time air weather, good seas and happy getting-high-on-a-bottle-of-rye-while-the-coyote-gets-drunk-on-the-moon-ing!
