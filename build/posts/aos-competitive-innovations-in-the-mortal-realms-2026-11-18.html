[AoS] Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-11-18

November 18th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

  • Turicum Bash der vierte - Grand Tournament - Switzerland

Thoughts

Rather unfortunately, there are a lot of things stacked against the Seraphon here. Morathi is arguably the best monster beat-stick in the game right now, easily going to toe to toe with the Stegadon Heavy list here. Not to mention, the Blood Sisters are very strong against high wound models, which is this entire list.

If there is a balance here, it's that Seraphon Monsters are quite tough, there's a fair number of them and the Solar Engine gives a good ranged attack with the Daughters lack. But if Morathi and her Blood Sisters can get up the field it's basically going to be over very quickly.

Archetype

Morathi and Blood Sisters

Thoughts

As always, The Shadow Queen and the Sisters here steal the show as the most terrifying parts of the list. The Shadow Queen, even with her points hikes, is one of the most fearsome beat sticks in the game. Supported by the Blood Sisters which hit really hard in their own right, and can inflict Strike Last on most anything with a lot of wounds (i.e. most things that would try and challenge Morathi).

The rest of the list exists in volume, to hold objectives and fulfill battle tactics. The Witch Aelves, when properly buffed by Priests and Wizards can be quite a terrifying force as well, so while they require a bit more support, they're no slouches.

Mixed Infantry

Thoughts

Our first Khazalite list for CI, it's hard to tell exactly where the army meta is going to shake out but this sets a strong first impression. The Valayans and Clan Shields stand up front to hold the line, while the Oathkeeper Cannon and the Wizards fire from the back.

Then once the opposition is locked up tight, send in the Gholems with Gottrek like a missile to tear through anything the infantry couldn't handle on their own. Straightforward and with little fuss nor muss.

Thoughts

This is a tough fight for both sides, as both are walking in with distinct advantages.

Lumineth are more fragile, but when played correctly are very good at picking out a specific threat and disposing of it, before getting out before things get too hot. This plays well into Soulblight, who have some key heroes that when eliminated can cause the rest of the list to topple.

The advantage of the Soulblight is even with those crucial heroes, they have so many guys and they can keep coming back. They can cover a lot of the board in ways Lumineth will struggle with. Lumineth are much faster, but when the Soulblight are just everywhere it can be difficult for that to mean much.

This ends in a nailbiter of a finale with only a 3 point difference.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Lumineth Good Stuff

Thoughts

Pretty much the predominant list for Lumineth, this list favors units that are fast and make precision strikes against key units like Heroes and Big Monsters and then hightailing it out of there before getting hit back. Severith is fast, has a strong ranged attack and can make the Windchargers and Hurakan Spirit even faster. Once he gets tangled up into an enemy unit they become -1 to wound and become basically unable to do serious damage before he just takes off and leaves.

Backing him up are the Light of Eltharion, one of the strongest beat sticks in the army that you can risk sending out, since he can come back from the dead. Riverblades which can deep strike onto an objective, and leap over enemy infantry to assassinate key heroes, and Ellania and Ellathor, who are a strong Warrrior or Wizard, depending on what you need most in that moment.
