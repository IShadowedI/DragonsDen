The Tabletop Battles 2026 Gift Guide

November 25th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Looking for that perfect gift for someone on your list this year, even if that person is you? In our 2026 gift guide, we've got a handful of suggestions to help you make your shopping easier ahead of the big shopping season. In our list you'll find items for most of our core games, as well as some other featured things we just like and think you might like too!

Warhammer 40,000

Warhammer 40,000 Starter Set: USD 255.00

With the release of 11th Edition, now is the perfect time to take the plunge on a Starter Set if you've been considering getting into the game, or know someone who has been. The starter set comes with terrain and a pretty robust set of models to get started with, and when we reviewed it back when it released, we noted that this is one of the most beginner friendly starter kits for newer players.

Warhammer 40,000 Battleforces: USD 255.00 

If you're already established in 40k and looking to expand your army, or want to try a new one, the seasonal Battleforce boxes always make a great option. This year there's sets for Chaos Knights, Necrons, Orcs, Chaos Space Marines, Space Wolves, Space Marines, and Blood Angels, so there's a decent amount of options; if you're really looking to spoil someone (including yourself) a starter set and a Battleforce is a great combo. Campbell goes into more detail on the 40k offerings here!

Age of Sigmar

Warhammer Age of Sigmar: Spearhead Starter Set

Much like with 40k, sometimes one of the best holiday gifts tends to be a starter set, and we think the Spearhead set from 2024 remains one of the best places to start the game from. It is important to note this is not the most recent released set, and we prefer the 2024 set because it makes it very easy to understand the game, get started, and know where to go from here.

Warhammer Age of Sigmar: Battleforces: USD 255.00

And also like 40k, if you know or are someone who already has a pretty solid established set of miniatures and interest in AoS, the Battleforce boxes are always going to do you well. The AoS sets include Knights of Hammerhal, Grunta Stampede, Annihilation Host, Plaugesworn Host, Legion of Damnation, and Court of the Undying King which includes Nagash himself. Campbell has the breakdown of what's what in each box here, if your giftee is a specific fan.

BattleTech

BattleTech Core Box: USD 69.99

If you or someone you know has been Battletech curious for a while now, then the new Core Box from Catalyst is the best possible place to start. It also makes for a pretty affordable gift regardless of the occasion, coming in under 100 USD. If you're on the fence, we reviewed it and think it is one of the best places to start, but also benefits existing players by providing updated materials and models.

Boardgames

Kinfire Delve: USD 20~ and Up

We really enjoyed Kinfire Delve, and it remains a very solid pick for board gaming. If you missed out on it or know a board gamer who might like to give it a shot, the game is very much available and fairly inexpensive compared to other board game offerings on the market at the moment. We also reviewed it, if you'd like to see what Jake had to say about it before hitting buy!

Lord of the Rings: Battle in Balin's Tomb: USD 52.00

I did want to bring attention to the fact that Barnes and Noble lists the game as "Ballin's Tomb", which is very funny, but also, this game is pretty great. It is a Games Workshop product, much like the 40k and Blitz Bowl games you might have seen in Barnes and Nobles before; it may require a little work to find it if you don't have a Barnes and Noble near you, but if you can find it, this is a very solid pick up.

Trench Crusade

Canvas Map: USD 75.00

If you're looking for a gift that's a little off the beaten path of a figure or game piece, this canvas map from Jared Blando of Trench Crusade's world is a really amazing art piece to place up on your gaming or hobby room. there's also a poster variant if you're not that into a canvas map, or if you need to consider budget but still love the illustration.

Trench Crusade: Trench Ghost Bust: USD 70.00

Similarly, Mind Works studios has a pretty impressive collection of busts if you or someone you know is looking for a challenging paint journey. The Trench Ghost bust is an excellent piece that gives a lot of fun options to try bust painting with, and there are a few other Trench Crusade options from the studio (as well as just some general busts that are also pretty cool to consider).

Miscellaneous Gifts

Warhammer Age of Sigmar Gloomspite Gitz Mega Hoodie: USD 99.99

Do I even have to write up why you want one of these? You want one. You know someone else who wants one. You know simply owning this would make your life better, complete, and overall just improved. You can still order it from the GW website, and some stores reportedly still have them, so let me just say that you should run, not walk, to your browser and gift this to yourself and or someone else in your life.

Wr0ng Play! Umamusume Tees: USD 20-33

Do you know an Umamusume fan in your life, or are you one yourself, and you have asked, "I wish that someone would make shirts that go insanely hard and also have them be Umamusume themed"? Well, may I present you with Wr0ng Play's custom tee-shirts that do exactly that. Whether you're getting them as a gag gift, as new fashion statement, or just because they go pretty goddamn hard, the shirts all fit great and are high quality.

Affiliates

As a heads up, the following listings are from our Affiliated partners! If you happen to pick something up from any of them, we do get an amount back, so we wanted to provide this heads up before linking to these partners for the sake of editorial clarity. 

Frontline Gaming

We're big fans of Frontline Gaming's terrain and mats, and we use them at our own events and at home. If you're looking for something to help build up your gaming space or table and you've already got all of the models you could ever need, investing in a solid gaming mat is a great way to ensure that your models, dice, and terrain all last a lot longer, as well as just improving the overall look of your table.

The Army Painter

There are a lot of painting options on the market right now, and that makes it kind of complicated to suggest any specific set, product, or type of paint; you probably know what works best for you, or the person you're shopping for, but if you're looking for something that can make shopping easy for the beginner hobbyist to the expert, Army Painter is a solid choice for many options and skill levels.

Elegoo

While this isn't exactly an endorsement of 3D printing or suggesting that 3D printing is for you, we've worked with and reviewed the Elegoo products for a while now, and stand by that they are a quality product that we don't mind being affiliated with. If anything, these are one of the bigger investments on the list, so absolutely make sure this is something you, or your giftee, wants and needs!

Victrix

If you or your giftee are into Historicals, then our partnership with Victrix is perhaps one of the best options for a wide variety of Historical miniatures, and also for picking up the ruleset for Pillage, which we really pretty high on. Their range includes medieval, Napoleonic, WW2, and more, and Victrix has long been one of the most reliable names in 28mm wargaming.

Bandua

Finally, we are pretty happy with the terrain offerings from Bandua, and if you're looking for terrain for your table, consider whether or not Bandua is right for you. We also quite like their carrying cases, and frankly have always been happy with the products we've reviewed and received from them; again if you're looking for something other than just miniatures or rulesets, Bandua has a lot of options for a lot of various gaming systems.
