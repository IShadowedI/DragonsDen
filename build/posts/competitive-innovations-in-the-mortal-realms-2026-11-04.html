Competitive Innovations in the Mortal Realms: 2026-11-04

November 4th, 2026 via Goonhammer

Welcome to Competitive Innovations, our weekly feature for a number of game lines across the tabletop wargame space. Each week our army list and tactics experts canvass the top tournaments for their game of specialty, looking at the week’s top match-ups and how they played out on the tabletop to let you know what’s hot, what’s not, and what might be coming next as the competitive meta evolves year-round.

A bit smaller this week, but a nice mix-up from our usual countries in comp innovations, as we head to Scandinavia and Central America for two GTs.

This Week's Events

  • Warhammex GT Open 2 - Mexico

Thoughts

SCE Annihilators lists are a bit of a mixed bag. They drop down, and try and make their charges. If they make it, they will probably pick up whatever they went into, if they don't, they just stand there impotently while the enemy either picks them off or just keeps their distance.

The score seems to imply it was a bit mixed. not a complete blow out, with Praetors and Reclusians to back them up they're not completely without damage options if they fail, but the mobility of this kind of Lumineth list means that they are very difficult to lock down for Stormcast, with their slower walk speed once they've hit the ground.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Hurakan with Vanari hero support

Thoughts

Severith and and the Hurakan Spirit of the Wind make a great synergy with the wind chargers. Thanks to Severith's ability to buff other Hurakan units, they can get -1 to be hit, and extra 6" of movement and +5 control out of the gate. This means these guys are fast and hard to lock down. If they get into combat, Severith can inflict -1 to wound and the generic spirit Strike Last, making them east pickings for some of the stronger beat sticks in the army.

Light of Eltharion and Ellania and Elathor (When Elathor is in charge) are brutal combat pieces who can swing in and pick up a lot of impressive threats. The Riverblades hang back as objective grabbers and assassin pieces, while the Wardens guard the Heroes from a frontal attack.

The List

See Showdown

Archetype

Darkoaths

Thoughts

This time I don't really need to give my thoughts because I was able to get it directly from Matt himself how the list works:

"Hey everyone! My name is Matt "Noog" Nguyen and I'm the pilot of this Darkoath STD list. As an ex-BOC player, I wanted to find a playstyle that was similar to the 2nd or 3rd edition book. Thankfully, I've found that with the Darkoath half of STD. The core identity of the Darkoath list is to overwhelm the opponent with MSU waves of trash, combat pieces that can trade up, and being able to play a 5 turn game where the opponent will struggle to stop me from dominating primary + achieving most of my battle tactics. What makes this list work starting off is the subfaction Darkoath Horde. For one CP during my movement phase, I can revive any non-hero Darkoath unit like Marauders, Savagers, and even Wilderfiends to come wholly within 6 of a board edge outside of 3 of enemy units. This ensures that no back objectives or foot heroes are safe from being assailed.

The bread and butter of the list lies in Darkoath Marauders and Wilderfiends. Having 5 units of MSU marauders not only lets me have layers of screens for my army and roll 50+ dice on death to do mortals on a 5+, but they also serve as batteries for the Wilderfiends. If any models die within 12 of a Wilderfiend, they can charge up sacrifice points. With up to 6 sacrifice points, they can use it to turn off commands on a 5+, do mortals on a 3+, or heal themselves. The amount of utility, tankiness, and damage for a 120 point unit makes bringing 3 worthwhile as they are an MSU threat that cannot be ignored by your opponent.

Leading these vast hordes are seven heroes in the list. The Darkoath Chieftain can chain-fight to give +1 attacks to efficient combat units like Savagers while the Darkoath Warqueen can potentially give out a 5+ ward on an objective she's contesting to any Darkoath on it. Gunnar brand and Singri are used in the list to help score Burning for Vengeance 1 and Siege of Ashes 1 or farm places of power. The Oathsworn Kin are my 1 cast in the list whose job is to play behind the army to get ready to cast 3D6 charge or fight last. If need be, they can often be sent in as a trading piece. The Chaos Lord sits by the Scourge of Asqhy Warriors to chain fight them for +1 to wound. Finally, we have the Ogroid Myrmidon with both of my command trait and artifact. For an 120 point unit, he can get up to 12-15 attacks with Rune of Murder on 3s and 2s rend 1 3 damage each and be able to fight twice with Deathmonger. This creates an insane combat hero that will always uptrade for his cost. I've had him kill the Shadow Queen, Redd, 10 gluttons, and other expensive units in one go. All of my heroes are just layers to this onion that is my list.

The mainline hammers of the list are my reinforced block of 20 Savagers and my Scourge of Aqshy Chaos Warriors. Now that they have allowed most Warcry units to become reinforceable, a block of reinforced Savagers at 180 points is an insane potential hammer. Starting off at 2 attacks each at 4s 3s crit mortals 1 damage each, they can be given Mark of Khorne for +1 attacks and if the Darkoath Chieftain chain-fights them, then they go up to 81 attacks with crit mortals. The most critical part of my list however is my reinforced Scourge of Aqshy Chaos Warriors. While everything else is a 4-5+ save in the army, they are my anvil and hammer in this list. Equipping them with Dread Banner lets them stop commands with any foe they are in combat with which stops all out defense and power through so your opponent's units are forced to stay in the mud with them. The Warriors also become the strongest combat piece in the army when they get to 7 fury which gives them +1 damage to their melee attacks and strike first as long as they don't charge. 40 wounds on a 3+ save and Mark of Khorne to get them to 3 attacks each at potentially damage 2 has turned the tide in my practice and GT games. I've watched them solo Ogor, DOK, and other strong combat armies.

All of my screens, trading pieces, MSU units, and hammers in the list lets the army be at 190+ wounds. The wound density lets me absorb double turns from my opponents and not care about turn priority as most other armies would. Every single warscroll in this list has a job to do whenever it is to die for the glory of the Chaos Gods to help me waylay my opponents or embark on the Path of Glory to earn their recognition of their dark deities by slaying the most powerful of my opponent's units. Every single unit is expendable and I have often sacrificed my heroes and deadliest hammers in order to dominate primary and tactics. At its heart, the list is a board control army that has built in recursion, lots of MSU to cause confusion for my opponents on what units they should target, and cheap combat units that will always trade up into expensive reinforced hammers or god monsters while letting me play a 5 turn game of Age of Sigmar."
