Content We Liked: July 11, 2026

July 12th, 2026 via Goonhammer
Although it’s a big internet out there, the Tabletop digital universe is pretty small and spread out. In Content We Liked, we take a look at the articles, videos, podcasts, and products that caught our eyes or were noteworthy during the prior week you might have missed.

Last Week on Tabletop Battles (June 29-July 4)

Since we had a busy holiday weekend and some other things going on, we didn't have a CWL last week, so this week you're getting a double dose of CWL instead of our usual posts around town.

Age of Sigmar

There was a huge update dropped the first week of July for Age of Sigmar, and our dutiful team worked hard to ensure that everything got covered and discussed, and if you didn't see or catch some of them, then here are the articles you missed!

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/tabletop-battles-hot-take-scourge-of-aqshy-order

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/tabletop-battles-hot-take-scourge-of-aqshy-chaos

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/tabletop-battles-hot-take-scourge-of-aqshy-death

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/tabletop-battles-hot-take-scourge-of-aqshy-destruction

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/tabletop-battles-reviews-the-june-2026-battlescroll

40k

The video team put out a Disposition Guide for Normies, and whether you consider yourself one or not, check it out!

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/warhammer-40k-dispositions-for-normies

We also had a great Ruleshammer from Vrekais that can help if you're looking for clarification on shooting.

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/ruleshammer-40k-11th-shooting-range-line-of-sight-and-visibility

Battletech

There was also a lot of big releases and updates for Battletech, so if you happened to miss out or wanted a quick recap from the first week of July, here you go!

If you were looking to get started with Battletech, Jack has a great collection of reviews of all the new big releases that make for an excellent entry into the game:

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/battletech-starter-box-the-tabletop-battles-review

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/battletech-mech-overview-the-starter-and-core-boxes

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/battletech-core-rules-updates-for-existing-players

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/battletech-core-rules-the-tabletop-battles-review

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/battletech-core-box-the-tabletop-battles-review

This Week on Tabletop Battles (July 6-July 11)

This week, the hits keep on coming, but at least some of the big updates and new releases have slowed a little bit! Still, we had a lot of great stuff for you to catch up on, so let's get to it.

Battletech

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/battletech-mech-overview-updates-axman-dragon-and-falconer

We had even more udpdates to Battletech, and Jack covered a few mech updates and overviews.

Magic the Gathering

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/magic-the-gathering-marvel-super-heroes-commander-review-monocolor-cards

The MTG crew had some notes on Monocolor cards to round out over coverage of the new Marvel Super Heroes set!

Starcraft

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/how-to-paint-everything-starcraft-the-miniatures-game-zerglings

Over in Starcraft territory, Dan has a handy HTPE on Zerglings.

Warhammer 40k

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/tabletop-battles-reviews-new-warhammer-40000-starter-sets

We also had a chance to look at the new starter sets thanks to Games Workshop, and the guys have some high praise for these.

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/40k-ruleshammer-11th-edition-fight-phase

Also, another banger Ruleshammer from Vrekais regarding the Fight Phase!

Turn Order

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/one-page-endless-stories-one-page-ttrpg-overview-previews

We've been working hard on a set of TTRPG guides for new and advanced players, and Petra has just put out one of our first guides to Advanced play.

Come See Us at the Tabletop Battles Open in the UK!

https://www.tabletopbattles.com/uk-tabletop-battles-open-september-2026

And finally this week, an annoucement for the Tabletop Battles Open in September! We'd love to see you there!

That's all for this weeks CWL! Have any other questions or feedback? Drop us a note in the comments below or email us at contact@tabletopbattles.com.
