Marvel Crisis Protocol: What Am I Playing Now? Mighty Avengers Edition

September 3rd, 2026 via Goonhammer

Greetings, True Believers! The most recent character update has made Marvel Crisis Protocol really feel like a brand-new edition, and as such has made me completely re-think my choices for my Mighty Avengers roster! Overall, my roster feels much better because so many of my characters got increased Stamina, which means they can stick around even longer to score points! Without further ado, let’s dive into what I’m playing now!

Core Affiliated Models

Sam Wilson, Captain America for Marvel Crisis Protocol MCP painted by Crab-stuffed Mushrooms

For the most part, the affiliated models in my Roster haven’t changed a ton.  Spectrum is still the only leader in the Affiliation, so she is an auto-include, as is her boxmate Blue Marvel.  I was honestly very pleasantly surprised that Blue Marvel didn’t receive some kind of downgrade in this update, but I am not going to complain.  He is one of the punchiest models in affiliation (if not the punchiest model in affiliation), so he can really fill a gap that the rest of the Affiliation is a little bit lacking in. 

Several Affiliated models also got significantly better through this update.  Black Panther, Chosen of Bast felt more than a little fragile when he only had 5 Stamina, but feels great now that he has gotten a Stamina boost.  One of my favorite updates, though, has to be Ant-Man.  He got a Stamina boost to 6/6, which feels great paired with the Stealth and defensive re-rolls he has in his Tiny form.  Most importantly, however, he can now carry Asset tokens when he’s Tiny!  Is he an every game inclusion? No. But if we have an Asset Extract, he can actually play the game and contribute to the Extract scoring, which is a huge addition to the character.  He is still incredibly mobile and can help with scoring Secures, and with more Stamina, he should stick around longer to score those points. 

One of the biggest weaknesses of Mighty Avengers was only having one affiliated 4-threat model, Steve Rogers, Captain America.  Before the update, his kit just didn’t work.  His power generation was off, he could only use his re-rolls once per turn, and if you were carrying an Extract token you couldn’t use his On Your Left superpower at all.  Additionally, he felt paper-thin at only 6/5 Stamina.  Now that he can move M three times in an activation and carry an Extract, he can get away from potential threats, and his likelihood of being taken out in one attack has gone down significantly.  I’ve included him in my roster now so that I can have an affiliated 4-threat when I need it for squad building, and he can play a vital role on the team when he is in the game. 

One affiliated model that I have removed since the update is 2-threat Black Widow.  It has been difficult to find a situation where she brought enough utility beyond “just making the math work”.  She’s an L mover, but in most situations I would rather bring Chosen of Bast.  Having Stealth and Martial Artist can only go so far when you have 4/4 Stamina, and boy does she look lacking when compared to her 3- and 4-threat versions post-update.  Sam Wilson continues to be a stalwart of the affiliation, and now has an extra Stamina on his injured size. There is also enough cross-affiliation synergy that I can choose him as my leader if I need to have a lower-threat leader based on the scenario.  With all of the changes to the affiliated characters, I am currently running 5 affiliated models, which leaves room for 5 splash characters.  This is where the real spice is.

Non-Affiliated Characters

Hulkbuster Iron Man for Marvel Crisis Protocol MCP painted by Crab-stuffed Mushrooms

After the update, a whole new world of choice has opened up for which non-affiliated models fit in with this Roster.  Nick Fury, Jr is still a staple for this Roster, and he didn’t see any changes since he was last updated. Guess what? He’s still solid.  Omega Sentinel has also cemented the spot she had in my roster, even though she didn’t see any updates.  Honestly, I would’ve been surprised if AMG had updated this character at all considering how new she is.  Beyond these two, the other spots in my Roster have been a revolving door of experimentation just to see how new models might play and complement the rest of the roster. 

One of my favorite new additions has been Ultimate Spider-Man.  He got a Stamina increase (which I did not see coming) and was already a very durable model with his defensive re-rolls.  He was already incredibly mobile with his R3 place and L move, and could also punch way above his Threat Value and has an insanely cheap Spender attack for what it does.  However, the biggest upgrade that Miles got may have gone unnoticed if you were only looking at his character card.  The main rules were errata'd to include a change to how tokens are applied to characters.  Essentially, even if you can’t apply a token to a character during an attack, you can still do the second part of the clause that included the token application.  For Miles, this means that even if you can’t apply the Slow condition to an opponent, you can still pull them S, which gives him very reliable displacement in addition to everything else he could already do. All of this wrapped up in a 4-threat model no less! 

Another exciting model I have been trying out in my Mighty Avengers roster is Hulkbuster.  He essentially gained Indomitable on his card, and his Damage Reduction got changed to an innate superpower instead of having to pay for it before dice were rolled.  He also has Hit and Run which gives him some much-needed action compression so he can always be covering ground and attacking.  He also has a sizeless Push on his Builder attack Heavy Repulsor Blast, which guarantees at least 1 Power, even if you deal no damage to your target.  Both sides of his card gained a Stamina, and his smaller version gained defensive re-rolls, increasing his survivability chances.  He is also one of the few 6-threat models in the game now without the Peerless keyword, too, which allows me some more flexibility with squad selection. 

The last character I want to discuss that has caught my attention post-update is Angela.  Attrition is an area where my Roster can really struggle, and Angela can do that in spades.  She got a Stamina bump to 7/8, which is the smallest change she got.  The timing on her Angelic Assassin had previously been changed so that she could use power she gained on her attack to fund it, which is fantastic.  In this update, though, she gained so much more.  Now, she can use her Heven’s Wrath spender and add dice based on the defender’s threat instead of size.  This means for 4 power she can make an 8 or 9 dice attack against the majority of models in the game, and now has an easier Cleave trigger which, importantly, grants her an extra 0 zero cost attack.  It doesn’t specify which of her two 0 cost attacks it has to be, and it also doesn’t require her to attack the same character that she made her Heven’s Wrath attack into.  With correct placement and attack choice, she can bounce around the board and make 5 attacks in an activation.  This is bonkers to me.  Add in to that one of those attacks should be boosted by Spectrum and have two additional dice.  She is so much fun to play now!

How have your rosters changed post-update? Let us know in the comments!
