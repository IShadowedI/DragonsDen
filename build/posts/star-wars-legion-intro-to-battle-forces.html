Star Wars Legion: Intro to Battle Forces

July 21st, 2026 via Goonhammer

So you have gotten started in Star Wars Legion, have gotten a few games in and want to expand your knowledge of how to build an army list with new options you have not tried before. You may have heard or even played against an army that was using a Battle Force and want to give one a go yourself. Maybe you bought the Mandalorian army box as your first purchase and want to understand how to use them. If these apply to you or you are just curious, look no further while I explain in depth what a Battle Force is, why you would want to use them, and go in depth about all 15 Battle Forces available at the time of writing.

Thank you to Asmodee for sponsoring this series of articles on Star Wars: Legion.

What Is a Battle Force?

A Battle Force is a unique way of building your army that you can choose to use instead of the normal army list building rules. Battle Forces come with built in restrictions on the type of units you can use, commonly have different rank requirements, and are also limited on what unique upgrades you are able to take. Battle Forces do need to adhere to the rules of requiring at least 1 Commander rank unit as well as needing to be either 600 points or 1000 points, but most will come with a special rank requirement chart for 600 points. A Battle Force will also say what Faction it belongs to.

Some armies are limited to Battle Forces if you wanted to take them such as the Shadow Collective or the Mandalorians. These armies are composed entirely of Mercenary units. A Mercenary unit in Star Wars Legion is a unit that does not belong to one of the four Factions (Rebels, Galactic Republic, Empire, or CIS). Examples include the Pyke Syndicate, Mandalorians, Ewoks, and many others. Some of these units have the Mercenary keyword which allows them to be taken in the main factions, but some don’t and Battle Forces are where those units are allowed to be taken, or in some cases become your only options.

Why Would I Want to Use a Battle Force?

With all these list building restrictions you must be wondering why you would even take one. When choosing a Battle Force they come with inherent restrictions, but they also come with all manner of new units, extra army rules, and sometimes completely new command cards or units limited to only that Battle Force. Some Battle Forces may even allow you to bring more than the limited amount of Mercenaries you are normally allowed to take. It varies between each Battle Force, but they all bring something unique for your army, such as the Echo Base Defenders Battle Force which has three unique Command Cards you can bring if you decide to use it, as well as having an expanded number of Operatives and Commanders you can bring.  These boons are a serious consideration for deciding to work within the restrictions of a Battle Force.

Each Battle Force also changes the playstyle of the army it is tied to, or even introduces a completely new playstyle into the game. These new playstyles can provide a new way of playing your favorite armies. In some cases a Battle Force will change up how a unit works or even come with rules to support a specific unit.  For example, you have the Rapid Interdiction Force which allows CIS to bring an army mostly comprised of the TSMEU-6 Wheel Bikes and has a new type of Geonosian unit that can repair and  support your wheel bikes opening up the opportunity for you to Mad Max your way across the battlefield and get rewarded for it. These unique playstyles are also a large draw for wanting to bring a Battle Force instead of utilizing the normal army list construction rules.

What Battle Forces Are Available?

Each faction has a number of Battle Forces available, and that number will only increase with time. As of writing there are 15 Battle Forces spread across each Faction, and three of them are Mercenary-only Battle Forces. They are as follows:

  • Echo Base Defenders
  • Bright Tree Village

Empire

  • Stormtrooper Battalion
  • Blizzard Force
  • Imperial Remnant
  • Tempest Force

Galactic Republic

  • 212th Attack Battalion
  • 501st Legion
  • Wookie Defenders

CIS

  • Separatist Invasion Force
  • Rapid Interdiction Force
  • Experimental Droids

Mercenary

  • Shadow Collective
  • The Onaka Gang
  • Mandalorian Clans

Imperial Remnant

The Imperial Remnant has a host of rules. For starters, all your Death Troopers and Scout Troopers count as Corp rank for the purpose of army building, and you cannot take more than 1 of each Corp rank unit until you have taken at least one of each as well as being unable to take detachments. When you are issuing orders you can only issue orders to units within range 2 and all units outside of range 2 of a Commander unit gain the keyword Independent: Aim 1 or Dodge 1, and finally you cannot use the courage of a Commander outside of range 2 of a unit when checking to see if you are panicked. The final rule of this Battle Force is that all of your non-droid units in this army can take a Heavy Weapon upgrade that has Stormtrooper only, Death Trooper only, Scout Trooper only, Shoretrooper only. For example your Death Troopers can take the Sonic Charge Saboteur that normally only the Scout Troopers could take.

Commander: Moff Gideon, Imperial Officer

Operative: Imperial Agent

Corp: 1-2 Stormtroopers, 1-2 Shoretroopers

Special Forces: Scout Troopers, Imperial Death Troopers

Support: 74-Z Speeder Bikes, E-Webb Heavy Blaster Team

Heavy: Imperial Dark Troopers

Thoughts: Imperial Remnants is a unique Battle Force that breaks the rules of the game in an interesting way. This Battle Force lets your units share their Heavy Weapon upgrades to any of your units listed in the special rules, which creates unique combinations of units as well as creates an interesting challenge in deckbuilding. On the table you have the Independent keyword on most of your units as long as you forgo being close enough to your Commander for the courage comparison. This can create multiple small and large decisions you need to think about while playing, since your units might be more susceptible to losing actions and even panicking from suppression. Thematically this Battle Force is perfect, it truly feels like the remnants of an army trying to make do with what is available to them, even if it means leaving their rank structure to utilize weapons leftover from war.

Tempest Force

This Battle Force has a few special rules: All your Vehicles gain the Scout 2 keyword. Units that are not within range 3 of an allied commander that also do not have a coms upgrade equipped remove one less Suppression during the Remove Tokens step. During Setup, you can set aside a Special Forces or Support unit that is not holding an Asset Objective token and mark them with an Advantage token. The first time that unit would activate this game, instead of moving onto the board normally you must deploy that unit anywhere on the battlefield outside of range 2 of all enemy units if able. The unit must be in cohesion and is treated as having activated and you flip their order token facedown. You then remove that unit's Advantage token.

Rapid Interdiction Force

This Battle Force has a decent amount of special rules. At the start of the first Command Phase, place 1 Advantage token on each allied Heavy Droid Trooper or Vehicle units card. Additionally if any allied Heavy Droid Trooper units or Vehicle units make a third move during their activation you can put an Advantage token on their unit card if they do not have one already.

During the Resolve Command Card Effects step, after revealing Command Cards but before resolving Command Card effects, if you played a Command Card with fewer pips than your opponent you may choose up to 3 allied Heavy Droid Trooper or Vehicle units without an Advantage token on their unit card and place one on their unit cards. If you played a Command Card with an equal number of pips or more pips than your opponent, only choose 1 unit instead.

At the start of an allied Heavy Droid Trooper or Vehicles activation it may spend the advantage token on its unit card to either increase the speed of their move actions during their turn by 1 or they can gain a dodge token.

On top of all this, BX Series Commando Droids in this army count as Corp units for the purpose of army building and Battle Cards and they gain the keyword Infiltrate.

Mercenaries

Mercenary Battle Forces are Unique in that they cannot use any of the main Factions units. They all come with the rule that your mercenaries count towards your minimum number of required units and you’re not limited on the number of Mercenaries you can take.They each act almost like a miniature faction of their own.They do not however come with the ability to issue orders across Affiliations army wide so do keep that in mind when you start mixing Affiliations.

Clan Saxon mandalorians, credit Mandalorian Sleuth

Outside of being entirely composed of Mercenaries the only other special rule is that Transports can issue orders to the unit they are transporting regardless of affiliation.

Thoughts: Mauls seedy criminal empire, complete with bounty hunters and new to Star Wars Legion at the time of writing Savage Oppress. This group of mercenaries may have their own interests in mind, but they combine to make something truly scary on the tabletop. The entire army has the Independent keyword, which can give free actions or tokens if you do not get given an order. This makes the army efficient at generating tokens on all of their units. This Battle Force is expansive and has so many options they deserve their own article that will be a part of our Faction Focus articles, but if you want to play with Maul, His Eminence or want to play an army completely composed of the worst criminals in the Star Wars universe, this Battle Force is for you.

Onaka Gang

This Battle Force comes with a bevy of special rules as well. While Onaka Gang does follow the same rule of mercs counting towards your minimum and being unrestricted it does have the caveat that all units in your army must have the raiders affiliation.

This Army cannot contain more WLO-5 Speeder Tanks than it has Weequay Pirates, and each WLO-5 Speeder tank must be transporting a Weequay Pirate unit.

When Hondo Onaka deploys, instead of deploying normally he may instead make a speed 1 move measured from the base of a WLO-5 Speeder tank with both prongs of the movement tool touching the base.

Trooper units in the army gain the keyword Eyes on the Prize: Relentless and when Raider Trooper units in the army are within range 3 of an allied Hondo Onaka they may use his courage instead of their own when checking for panic. Hondo also gains a command upgrade slot and can be nominated as Commander during the Command Phase.

Thoughts: The legendary (or infamous depending on who you ask) pirate Hondo Onaka comes with his own gang to Star Wars Legion. If you love the fast talking, silver tongued pirate or want to show your opponent the overwhelming firepower of 4 tanks all filled to the brim with murderous pirates ready to get their prize, this Battle Force is for you. Eyes on the Prize gives your unit a keyword if you are holding an Objective token or contesting an Objective, after all profit is the name of the game. Relentless gives your units the ability to shoot or make a melee attack after a move which makes your pirates that much deadlier to your opponent. The Command Cards in this army provide a host of supportive abilities from free moves to the defensive abilities Low Profile and Nimble which make your pirates even more difficult for your opponent to remove from the field. Combined with the might of your many tanks, you can control the battlefield with your superior firepower and overwhelming numbers of pirate units and claim your opponents riches for yourself, just make sure to deploy Hondo from a tank that will provide him with the maximum amount of profit he can gain on the battlefield, he doesn’t take well to being put in the line of fire himself.

Final Thoughts

Battle Forces can be a fun and thematic way of building your Star Wars Legion army. They each bring something unique to the table and can allow you to fulfill the fantasy of defending the Rebel Hoth Base or even allow you to lead your favorite jedi's clone army. With the release of each Battle Force, units are given new ways to be played and sometimes take on an entire new life like the prototype droids. I personally have enjoyed trying out all sorts of Battle Forces, and enjoy the challenge they bring to list building with their limited options. The ones listed in this article are also not the last, with confirmations that we are getting a Mimban Battle Force for the empire and what we can assume are many more to come. I would highly recommend playing a Battle Force from time to time to keep your games of Star Wars Legion fresh and to spread your wings to other units you may not have tried out yet.
