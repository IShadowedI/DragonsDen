Thoughts

As always when Innes wins events, we've got the details of his final in his own words:

Innes: The finals of Edinburgh saw the mighty "Stack all the Defensive Buffs" go up against "The Concept of the Battle-shock Step" - and it created some interesting tension. With myself of Priority Assets, I felt like I had the easier passive game plan - I have an easy route to 10-12 points on Primary a turn, while the Chaos Daemons has a more casual 9. This combined with my generally easier access to secondaries (due to having much more durable units) meant Blair had to come to me to start killing things and get into the game.

I was able to use the durability of the Deathwing Knights to lock down his Great Unclean Ones and start wailing on them with Sternguard and Azrael's Intercessors, eventually breaking through the front lines and near maxxing my score. Blair was able to keep his scoring on Primary high, but couldn't convert on many of his secondaries with the defensive wall of the Dark Angels in front of him. Some clutch Battle-shocks kept me off the perfect score, but it will ended 98-84. List design aiming to deny kills with Fire and Fading speeders and durable bricks working just as intended!

Result

Dark Angels (Librarius Conclave/Inner Circle Task Force) Victory - 98 - 84

Thoughts

Innes continues to set the gold standard in UK 40k, with another triumph here. Librarius was a known entity going in but seeing the combinations with Inner Circle and Deathwing Knights here is very intriguing - you get around the traditional DWK weakness (speed) via relocation and the movement boosts in the detachment, and the overall defensive and offensive package in Inner Circle Task Force seems to work much, much better on the new maps and missions! Scouts and Land Speeders for early scamming as our boy dials it back to 9th edition. Sensational work.
